## Teaching corrections

English blocks **4, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 18, 20, 21** changed, with corresponding isiZulu draft reconciliation. The four lesson bodies, relevant quiz answers/rationales and the rain-observation assignment checklist were updated.

- Walk after a storm when the site is safe. Keep away from lightning, fast water and unstable banks. Excess water needs a safe route out; retaining every drop is not appropriate on every site.
- Add A-frame calibration: mark both feet, take a plumb-line mark, swap the legs onto those same positions, and use the midpoint of the two marks. Mark a contour only after calibration. Remove the unsupported two-hectare/morning promise and the suggestion that the tool is sufficient to design a dam or establish earthwork safety.
- Make infiltration conditional on soil, stability and site suitability, with planned overflow.
- Explain winter midday sun in South Africa without claiming that the sun is always north. Shadow duration depends on height, distance and the structure; a wall cannot guarantee frost protection.
- Replace blanket provincial wind directions with observation and local records.
- Cold air can collect in low ground; frost does not flow like a liquid. Ice crystals are useful evidence, while mist alone does not prove frost. Remove unsupported distance and week claims. Use the learner's local frost season.
- Airflow and morning sun can help leaves dry; they are not a cure for late blight.
- Label paced distances as estimates. Weed presence alone cannot diagnose compaction, fertility or a seep; inspect and compare the soil.

No new plant names, engineering dimensions, treatment intervals or rainfall rates were added. The A-frame remains one narration block with reading continuations; it was not split into new recording identities.

## Sources

- [ECHO — A-Frame Level](https://www.echocommunity.org/en/resources/1e97162c-712a-43ef-a21c-86e65c8d2925) and [Contour Cultivation and Live Barriers](https://www.echocommunity.org/en/resources/9faca1c2-bda1-42be-9aba-dbae1af62f8a): calibration by reversing the legs on fixed foot positions and marking the midpoint; contour tracing.
- [FAO — Manual on small earth dams](https://www.fao.org/4/i1531e/i1531e.pdf): engineering, investigation and design requirements; a simple contour tool is not a substitute for dam design.
- [US National Weather Service — Lightning safety](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-safety): avoid outdoor exposure during thunderstorms.
- [University of Minnesota Extension — Escaping spring frost](https://blog-fruit-vegetable-ipm.extension.umn.edu/2024/03/escaping-spring-frost-in-upper-midwest.html): cold-air drainage and low ground. Northern Hemisphere aspect advice was not transferred to South Africa.

The other edits remove unsupported guarantees and encourage observation; they do not introduce new regional weather, disease-treatment or weed-diagnostic claims.
