# F2 practice sheet — follow the produce and money

Reserve English learner draft. All quantities and amounts are invented for practice, not prices, yields or recommended fees. Work in a notebook, with counters or aloud. Use the event cards supplied by your facilitator; worked answers are in `f2-records.md`.

## 1. Follow one product

Begin with 4 kg in store. Add H01: 16 kg picked. Follow these events in order: D01 sells 6 kg; HOME01 uses 2 kg at home; D02 sells 5 kg; GIFT01 gives away 1 kg; H02 adds 8 kg picked; LOSS01 records 1 kg spoiled; D03 sells 4 kg.

Write the stock remaining after each event. Keep the source reference beside the number.

The separate closing count is 9 kg. Does your book agree? Which references explain the food that was not sold? What other checks are needed even if the total agrees?

**New example:** There were 10 kg available. The records show 3 kg sold and 2 kg used at home. You count 4 kg left. How much is unexplained? What evidence would you seek? Do not guess its destination.

## 2. Follow the buyer's payment

S02 records 5 kg delivered on Day 2 for R100.00. PAY02 records R60.00 received on Day 4 for that sale.

## 3. Follow money between places

Before MOVE01, cash is R607.50 and the wallet R100.00. R100.00 is moved from cash to the wallet, with both sides completed.

Cash afterwards: ______  Wallet afterwards: ______  Combined: ______

What new produce sale occurred? ______  What new expense occurred? ______

Later, FEE01 separately shows a R2.50 wallet charge. Which account changes? What document supports the change?

**New example:** Move R20.00 between two accounts owned by the same practice farm. Does combined money change? If evidence separately shows a R1.00 fee, what then changes?
