# Farm Finance — proposed taught-course delivery

Rory asked on 21 September how the separate course would run on consecutive days. This is a proposed facilitation timetable, not a duration already established through learner trials. The course remains eight units and 24 English lesson drafts; practice and assessment days do not add to that lesson count.

## Recommended starting timetable

Plan ten teaching days: one day for each of F1–F8, then a supported integration day and a practical assessment/revision day. Allow approximately four to five contact hours per day, excluding breaks (40–50 contact hours overall). Translation, learner confidence with numbers, device sharing and additional practice may require longer. Pilot the first unit with the intended learners and revise the schedule from observed time; do not advertise these estimates as validated course hours.

| Day | Unit or session | What learners work on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | F1: Understand the farm's money | Household versus farm money; cash, sales, amounts owed and profit; supporting documents |
| 2 | F2: Keep useful records | Harvest destinations and stock; sales, receipts and costs; reconcile and correct mistakes |
| 3 | F3: Costs and prices | Direct/shared costs and unpaid work; unit cost and break-even; compare buyers and agreed prices |
| 4 | F4: Budgets and cash timing | Enterprise and farm budgets; dated cash forecast; delayed receipts and adverse cases |
| 5 | F5: Invoices and payments | Order/delivery/invoice/receipt; new, past and paper sales; deposits and outstanding balances |
| 6 | F6: Equipment and commitments | Buy/hire/share; understand borrowing documents; maintenance and reserves |
| 7 | F7: Read results | Cash versus profit and position; stock/equipment/period boundaries; prepare records and questions for current tax guidance |
| 8 | F8: Plan and improve | Market evidence; enterprise/business plan; actual-versus-planned season review |
| 9 | Supported project | Assemble the connected practice pack; trace every figure; relevant app tasks or workbook alternatives; correct mistakes with support |
| 10 | Practical assessment and revision | Independent case, explanation of decisions, corrective feedback and a fresh retry; agree the learner's next real-farm record task |

Each teaching day combines explanation, demonstration, guided practice and an independent attempt. Include paper/card/calculator routes and oral explanations, not a day of watching videos. Introduce the needed app control beside the concept rather than requiring learners to complete every app guide first.

The assessed pack includes source-linked records, a cost/price comparison, budget, cash forecast, checked invoice/payment reconciliation and a simple evidence-backed business plan. Use supplied synthetic cases for the consecutive-day programme; do not pretend a real growing season, harvest or farm profit has been observed during the course. The existing unit practice packs have separate opening balances and scopes: do not merge their numbers into one continuous ledger. A future connected capstone must explicitly provide its own reconciled source dataset.

A later review of the learner's own records should be scheduled by the facilitator for a relevant trading or production cycle. It is separate from these classroom days and must not be counted as completed during the intensive course.

## Delivery alternatives

An eight-day intensive covers one unit per day but needs assessment and extra practice time accounted for explicitly. For beginners or bilingual facilitation, the ten-day starting plan provides more room. Self-paced app learners take the same 24 lessons at their own pace, with audio, transcripts, exercises and the relevant app guides. The timetable does not make the finance course dependent on completing the whole permaculture course.

## App location and boundaries

Farm Finance is intended as a separate course in My Studies, alongside the permaculture pathway and Using ImbewuField help. Its own lesson sequence, progress and final assessment remain production work; the 24 drafts are not yet registered as published lessons. Existing app guides are live and narration is in production. Unsupported bookkeeping tasks use their workbook alternative; do not invent app capabilities. This timetable does not claim accreditation or an accounting qualification. Animations remain held for Rory's joint review.
