# F8 source and boundary record

Checked 21 September 2026. All example values and records in f8-practice.json are original, explicitly synthetic exercise inputs. The source documents below supply topic structure and evidence questions, not the case's prices, quantities, dates, hours or answers.

- [FAO Farm Business School manual, 2011](https://www.fao.org/4/i2136e/i2136e00.htm): its contents connect market enquiry, enterprise choice, business planning, records, risk and end-of-season review. Used for the learn-by-doing sequence only. This is a South Asia manual; no regional recommendations, exercises or financial examples are copied.
- [Department of Agriculture, Forestry and Fisheries: Agricultural Business Plan Guidelines](https://old.dalrrd.gov.za/doaDev/sideMenu/cooperativeandenterprisedevelopment/docs/Business%20Plan%20Guidelines_WEB.pdf): source for documenting market, resources, assumptions, risks and linked financial planning. Search-indexed primary-document excerpts inspected; direct fetch of the current nda.gov.za mirror returned502. Treat as historic guidance, not current programme eligibility or a prescribed application form. No old prices, livestock rates, tax figures, ratios or forecast horizons are adopted. Its broad statement that negative cash flow is always bad is not reproduced: timing, opening funds, financing and investment context matter; an unfunded projected payment is the issue in this exercise.
- Existing source-checked F3-F7 packs in this directory provide the prerequisite treatment of cost scope, dated cash, settlement schedules, equipment and records. F8 does not introduce new tax thresholds, finance products or legal rights.
- App source/released guide baseline b314136: `/student/guides/mapping`, `/student/guides/charts`, `/student/guides/invoices`, `/student/guides/sales`. These are working readable companions; the F8 workbook does not claim a full business-plan builder or a partial-payment ledger exists in the app.

Verification: integer-cent sums, dated cash lines, independent category totals, complete-case closing reconciliation, sales/cash bridge and separate items exercise. Fifteen deliberate answer faults must be rejected. Real profit remains unknown where stock, equipment, overheads or unpaid work are not fully supplied. The missing-cost case also removes closing reconciliation so that no displayed known total silently supplies the missing amount.

Open reviews: local bookkeeping review of explanations and exercise assumptions; learner trial; fluent isiZulu; narration; app/field teaching integration. A plan is not evidence of title, compliance, profitability, creditworthiness or an approved grant/loan. No records were sent or funds moved to make these teaching materials.
