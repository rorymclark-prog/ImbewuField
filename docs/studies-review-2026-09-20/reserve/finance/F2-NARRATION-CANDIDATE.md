## F2.1 — Harvest, stock and destinations

Start with what was already here. Add what you picked. Record what left and why. A sale, food for home and a gift are different uses. Keep the same unit. At the end, count or weigh what is still here. Compare that with the book. If they differ, look for the cause. A difference is a question to investigate. It is not proof that someone stole food.

## F2.2 — Sales, payments and expenses

In this exercise, write the sale when the agreed produce is delivered. Keep the payment beside that sale, with the date it arrived. If only some money arrives, show what is still owed. Moving your own money from the cash tin to the wallet does not create another sale. It changes where the money is kept. A fee is a separate payment when the evidence shows one. Keep the amount and cents from the source.

## F2.3 — Reconcile the book and correct mistakes

Reconcile means compare and explain. Count the cash. Read the wallet statement. Match deliveries, sales, payments and receipts by their references. Check the dates and units as well as the totals. When you find a mistake, keep the original reference. Write what changed and why. If you cannot explain a difference yet, say so. Do not add a guessed entry just to make the totals agree.
