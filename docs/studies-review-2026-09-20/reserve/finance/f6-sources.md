# F6 sources, model boundaries and review

Primary pages reviewed 21 September 2026. All exercise amounts, charges, dates and assumptions are original synthetic inputs. No market quote, equipment recommendation, interest rate, legal maximum, guaranteed affordability threshold or reserve percentage is supplied.

- [FAO: Hire services](https://www.fao.org/sustainable-agricultural-mechanization/hire-services-as-a-business/hire-services/en/) supports considering access to a service rather than assuming every farmer must own equipment. F6 uses this broad ownership/access distinction; it does not import regional prices, hectare generalisations, machinery specifications or prescriptions for tillage/spraying.
- [National Credit Regulator: Form 20, pre-agreement statement and quotation](https://www.ncr.org.za/index.php/act/list-of-forms/category/123-english?download=480:english) provides a source-reading structure for advance, instalments, interest, fees, insurance, security and payment arrangements. We do not reproduce the form, assert that every business arrangement falls within it, quote legal deadlines or make a compliance claim. Its dated legal references require current qualified applicability review when used for an actual agreement.

The nominal charge arithmetic is original and independently checked, not a calculation of APR, effective interest, regulatory compliance or complete affordability. Loan receipts are distinguished from sales and principal from financing charges. A withheld fee is deducted once; a fee financed into principal would require a different supplied schedule. Future interest and charges are not silently included in the label “remaining principal”. Missing compulsory insurance leaves complete instalments and charges unknown.

Equipment figures are dated cash movements within a stated horizon. They do not establish depreciation, lifetime ownership cost or full profit. Retained asset/share values remain unknown. Hire's full deposit refund and package inclusions are explicit exercise assumptions, not general promises. Shared ownership has an unresolved booking; it is not represented as proven equivalent access. The borrowing case is separate from the equipment case; R570.00 net funds are not silently added to the equipment's R500.00 opening cash.

Reserve allocation conserves the defined total cash. An actual maintenance payment reduces it. Earmarked funds in the model have no legal restriction; no conclusion is drawn about real restricted grants or bank access. The repair case's negative projection is an unfunded shortage, never a payment that is assumed possible. A non-cash cost estimate and an unapproved application do not create or move cash.

The shared-tools hero is reused unchanged from `docs/media/studies-illustrated-release/art/market-community/shared-tools.jpg`. Visually inspected: lifelike neighbours with tools in a warm rural homestead setting, matching the established course style. It is an illustration, not evidence of ownership or a safe-operating demonstration. Numeric labels and balances are authored and derived from the verified pack; no Flow credits are spent on generated financial text.

`verify-f6.py` reconciles three equipment options, three repayment schedules and two reserve projections. It rejects 15 deliberate errors, including omitted/doubled fees, missing insurance treated as zero, refunded deposit counted twice, an unknown asset value forced to zero, allocation treated as an expense and a clipped cash shortage. These checks protect model arithmetic and displayed answers; they do not substitute for bookkeeping or legal review.
