Checked 21 September 2026 against main `6fb8284369e7aaa00129b95d3d0fb32f4977b0f2`. Original synthetic cases, not real farm records or South African price, seasonal, yield, wage, lending or tax advice. Reserve teaching draft; qualified bookkeeping, learner and fluent isiZulu review remain open.

## Primary sources

- [FAO, Basic Finance for Marketers, Chapter 4](https://www.fao.org/4/w4343e/w4343e05.htm), cash-budget section and monthly format: planned receipts and payments, carried-forward balances, timing of customer collections and supplier payments. Its operating/cash-budget distinction supports linking activity plans to dated funding needs. No historical worked inputs are copied. F4 adds its own within-month counterexample because positive monthly closing balances alone do not establish continuous cash availability.
- [University of Wisconsin Extension, Understanding the Statement of Cash Flows](https://farms.extension.wisc.edu/articles/understanding-the-statement-of-cash-flows/): a historical statement and a future cash budget answer different questions; opening cash plus inflows minus outflows reconciles to closing cash. Cash movement is distinct from profitability. The article's US reporting conventions are not presented as South African accounting requirements. F4 uses a simple combined-funds teaching schedule, not a compliant statutory statement.
- [FAO community action planning guidelines, cash-flow exercise](https://www.fao.org/4/ad741e/ad741e0p.htm), opened during preparation: **do not copy its sign instruction**. It defines net cash flow as inflows minus outflows but then tells facilitators to subtract that net amount from opening cash. This is inconsistent with the definition. F4 explicitly adds net inflow and verifies the identity with independent arithmetic. Its assertion that everyone has a positive opening amount is also not a valid universal assumption.

## Checks and scope

The practice pack is the source for authored tables and interactive media. Exact integer cents and rational allocations avoid hidden rounding. The verifier checks the printed facilitator answers, shared-pool conservation, operating-budget/cash-source reconciliation, monthly carry-forward, actual event order, horizon exclusion, unknown propagation and independent transfer answer. Deliberate mutations must fail, including a fake funding receipt, double-counted internal transfer and a month-end-only gap claim.

The schedule can become negative. No available overdraft is implied, and no automatic plug receipt is inserted. Later positive numbers remain an unfunded projection. Model deficit depth is not a recommended borrowing amount. The proposed equipment deferral is an alternative requiring feasibility/agreement, not a default instruction to delay a liability. Reduced sales is separate from partial settlement of an existing invoice.

Combined funds assume the provided opening cash tin and bank are available and unrestricted. The model does not prove account-level settlement, simultaneous event order, credit access, production feasibility or buyer reliability. Unknown costs stay unknown. Opening receivable/payable settlements are kept separate from the current activity budget; equipment cash purchase is separate from its unknown period-use cost. Full profit remains unknown.

`lib/finance-series.ts` describes recorded event series, and `lib/finance-plan-source.ts` identifies mapped planning inputs. Neither establishes this exercise's dated available-cash forecast. F4 uses a standalone reserve workbook/timeline. No hypothetical data are written to the app. Existing expense/invoice companion links are reused; no unsupported forecasting, partial-payment or bank-balance control is invented.
