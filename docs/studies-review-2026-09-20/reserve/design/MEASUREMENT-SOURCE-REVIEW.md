## Specific arithmetic exclusions from the historical report

Page 19 says 362 square metres less an assumed one third for paths leaves 253 square metres. The arithmetic instead gives about 241.33. It then multiplies the stated 253 by R10 and reports R3213; the multiplication would be R2530. It calls 362 plus an extended 721 a combined 1093; those source quantities add to 1083, before any consistent treatment of paths. These are arithmetic checks of a historical source, not corrected recommendations. Even correct multiplication would not establish profit, suitable access, staffing or yield. None of these figures is being taught as a prescription or published as the worked case.

## Other previously inspected candidates

The Momahau Garden aerial PDF has a visible 40-metre image scale bar. This may support approximate image-scale reading after checking the original resolution and context. It is not a measured boundary survey and is not a source for the invented busy-yard household. The earlier Smallo/Ubhejane/RVCC drawings remain visual references with the limitations recorded in COURSE-INTEGRATION.md and external source review notes.

No real-site aerial, personal survey data or historical species list has been published in the new learning folder. The next measured example still needs a coherent source pack with reliable dimensions, units, feature references and a stated measurement basis.
