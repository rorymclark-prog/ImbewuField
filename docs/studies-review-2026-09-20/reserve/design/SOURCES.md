## Current primary design references

- [Permaculture Association: Design methods](https://www.permaculture.org.uk/knowledgebase/design-methods), accessed 21 September 2026: distinguishes survey of people/place, analysis, design, implementation, maintenance and evaluation. Used as a short cross-check of the pathway structure, not copied as lesson text.
- [Permaculture Association: Project design](https://www.permaculture.org.uk/iact/project-design), accessed 21 September 2026: implementation requires resources, responsibilities and dependencies as well as a vision. This corroborates the original outlines' implementation sequence and the RVCC planning approach.
- [David Holmgren: Essence of Permaculture](https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf), accessed 21 September 2026, English version 7.1 ©2013: supports distinguishing the design framework from individual gardening techniques and identifies the twelve design principles as Holmgren's formulation. No energy-future predictions or historical statistics are imported.

The eighteen learner texts and worked case are newly written from Rory's teaching brief and the documented source scope. External references corroborate the named concepts; they do not supply local measurements or certify the example.

## Source claims deliberately excluded from these drafts

These are review leads, not a finding that every related technique is invalid. Do not publish them as settled facts without a targeted primary-source check.

- Handbook §2: a universal slope threshold that leads to forestry/earthworks; fixed aspect productivity rules; plant presence presented as proof of water or disturbance.
- Handbook §2 slope planning: conflicting above/below-contour road rules; universal dam/tank/greywater placement without site assessment and use-specific checks.
- Handbook §3: blanket fire strategy and firebreak/planting prescriptions; universal windbreak performance figures; fixed temperature differences; a blanket statement about southern walls always being shaded.
- Outline: automatic international/professional authority from a course certificate. Delivery formats and historical certificate language do not establish present accreditation or permission to perform specialist work.
- Existing core introduction: attribution of the specific twelve-principle formulation should be reconciled with Holmgren and Rory's handbook. This branch does not change the existing lesson, quiz, translation or audio.
- Existing Reading the Landscape: numerical A-frame productivity and broad “enough for earthworks” wording need targeted review across written text, quizzes and narration. No replacement engineering threshold is supplied here.

No new species, rates, spacings, yields, dimensions, costs or technical construction instructions are introduced. The invented busy-yard case is prominently labelled, supplies no quantitative farm advice, and is not represented as field evidence. The source outlines' practical emphasis is retained without requiring unsupervised earthworks or buying inputs to pass.
