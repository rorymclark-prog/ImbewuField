# From observation to a working homestead plan

Status: additional design pathway in reserve. Six stages and eighteen English lesson drafts authored. The worked case covers the early reasoning, but its measured source pack, completed plan set and final visual demonstrations are still missing. An English app reading preview is implemented on the development branch; it is not merged to production, assessed, translated or narrated yet. Do not add these figures to the public course count.

This pathway answers the design-process gap in the August course audit. It connects the existing technical modules rather than replacing them. Each learner develops a design folder across the pathway: household brief, evidence map, analysis, compared alternatives, developed plan, implementation/care plan and a justified revision. Use paper, supported oral explanation or the app. Drawing quality alone does not demonstrate design competence.

These are teaching stages, not eighteen promises of one-day competence. Pacing needs a facilitated trial. Classroom attendance cannot replace seasonal observation or a supervised practical skill. The original outlines' five- and ten-day schedules are references for delivery planning, not evidence that this new pathway takes the same time or grants a professional qualification.

## Teaching pattern

Begin with a visible problem. Give a short explanation. Show the worked household case. Let the learner make the next part of their own folder. Check the decision and its evidence. Offer specific feedback and a fresh attempt. Use the same case consistently, revealing new evidence rather than quietly changing earlier facts.

The worked case in CASEBOOK.md is explicitly invented classroom material. It is a concept example, not a measured property, planting prescription or construction plan. The early stages deliberately show what cannot be decided without measurement. A later D4 source pack must supply internally checked dimensions before a scale-layout exercise is released; a schematic must never silently become a measured plan.

## Visual direction

Use Rory's approved warm, lifelike homestead imagery to introduce the people and setting. Spatial explanations need uncluttered maps with readable labels, a consistent legend and separate existing/proposed layers. A beautiful scene must not conceal a missing path or imply a surveyed boundary. Use still-image pairs and diagrams now. Animation creation and publication remain on hold.

Complete the remaining teaching and case material, then review it against the technical sources, the actual app and the reserve capstone rubric.
