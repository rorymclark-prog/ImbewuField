# C08–C10: make an invoice and keep the sale together

Status: first companion teaching draft. Current field labels and routes checked against source and the live sample invoice screen on 20 September 2026. A complete practice save, PDF review and payment reconciliation still need to be recorded and tested before publication. Linked finance unit: F5 in `../financial-course.md`.

## What you will be able to do

Make a clear invoice, find it again, explain whether it has been paid, and keep it connected to the sale. Begin in the app's sample tour with a facilitator's practice card. Have the agreed buyer, produce, quantity, unit, price, issue date and payment terms ready. Use actual payment evidence when working on your own farm. Never put real bank details or customer contact information into a group demonstration.

## Open the right record first

Open **My Records**, then **Invoice**. If the document already exists, choose **Saved** and open that invoice. Do not choose New invoice just to print an existing one.

For a new document, read **What are you recording?** Choose **A new invoice**, **Produce already sold**, or **An invoice already written on paper** according to what happened. A past sale or paper copy also asks **Is this sale already in My Records?** If yes, choose **Yes — link the existing sale** and select the exact available sale. If no, use the option to record it with this invoice. A paper copy must retain its original reference and issue date. Do not enter today's date simply because today is when you typed it.

Existing-sale linking preserves its crop, kilograms, total and payment date. If the existing record is wrong, investigate the source rather than inventing a second invoice to cancel the difference. Do not link a different sale just because the amount looks similar.

## Fill and check the document

Check the seller details and the buyer. Add each product as a line item. Check quantity, unit and price per unit together; the total is not the price of one unit. Use **Add line item** for another product. A suggested guide price is not the price agreed with your customer; check and change it when needed.

Choose the agreed **Payment due** terms and include the buyer's reference if required. Review the invoice preview: seller, buyer, document reference, issue date, due date, descriptions, units and total. Do not put a tax reference into the field unless it belongs to the relevant business. The current app does not calculate VAT from that field; use appropriate reviewed invoicing arrangements where tax-invoice requirements apply.

## Say what really happened to the payment

Under **Has the buyer paid?**, select **Not yet — payment outstanding** if payment is still due. Select **Yes — paid in full** only when the full payment has been received and checked. Then check **Payment received on** and, when useful, **Payment method**. The issue date and the payment date may be different.

The current screen has no partial-payment status. A deposit does not make the whole invoice paid. Keep a separate checked customer balance record for partial payments and ask the facilitator how to reconcile it with the app without double counting; this companion does not pretend that a full instalment ledger exists.

## Save, reopen and verify

Choose **Save invoice**. Read the result. If the app says it saved on this device and needs reconnection to update the crop sale book, keep the source document and follow that message. Do not repeatedly create new invoices while waiting for one to synchronise.

Open **Saved**, find the same invoice and check its details. When a previously unpaid invoice is paid in full, use **Review payment** or reopen that same invoice, check the receipt date and save the corrected status. Paid invoice kilogram lines can appear in the crop sale book; other units are not converted into kilograms. Look for the linked record before typing another sale.

## Preview before sharing

Use **Share PDF** or **Print** on the correct saved invoice. Inspect the output before giving it to anyone: buyer, dates, quantities, units, prices, total, payment status and payment details. A PDF saved to your device has not necessarily been sent to the buyer. In the lesson, stop at the preview or download; a real send belongs to the learner. Reopen the saved invoice for another copy rather than creating a new sale.

## Practise and explain

The facilitator supplies a practice order and payment record. Create its invoice, reopen it, point to the issue and payment dates, and explain where the money appears. Repeat with an unpaid order and a paper invoice whose sale is already recorded. The correct choice for the last case is to link the existing sale, not add the income again. Use a fresh example for reassessment.

Check: a buyer paid a deposit only. Which status says that accurately? Neither current option records the partial balance completely. Do not choose paid in full. Keep the deposit and outstanding balance in the agreed supporting record and flag the app limitation.

Check: a buyer wants another copy of yesterday's invoice. Open the existing invoice from Saved and use its output controls. The request is for another document copy, not another sale.

Check: the invoice records produce in bags. Does the crop book know their kilograms? No. The unit does not establish weight. Preserve the actual unit and use measured evidence where a kilogram record is needed.
