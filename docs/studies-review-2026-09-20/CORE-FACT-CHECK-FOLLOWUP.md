## Attribution of the twelve principles

`lib/course-modules.ts:103` and `lib/course-transcripts.ts:468` attribute the
specific twelve-principle formulation jointly to Mollison and Holmgren.
[Holmgren's own Essence of Permaculture](https://holmgren.com.au/downloads/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf)
distinguishes his set of twelve from other teachers' formulations and from the
joint origin of the term permaculture. Attribute this specific formulation to
David Holmgren while preserving the explanation's plain language.

Coordinated assets: the introduction's English slide-09 recording and full track,
with their manifest/size entries and any transcript used by the player. The
corresponding slide image says Twelve Principles and does not itself make the
attribution. The focused review found no quiz repeating the attribution.

## A-frame observation versus construction design

`lib/course-modules.ts:186,189,195-203` and `lib/course-transcripts.ts:581-583`
include an unverified two-hectares-in-a-morning claim, “No surveyor needed”, and
quiz language claiming suitability for farm earthworks.
[FAO's A-frame guidance](https://www.fao.org/fishery/static/FAO_Training/FAO_Training/General/x6707e/x6707e06.htm)
supports a calibrated simple contouring tool with accuracy and construction
conditions; it does not establish that universal speed claim.
[South African conservation-structure research](https://www.scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S1816-79502019000100016&script=sci_arttext)
describes site-specific hydraulic, runoff, soil, slope and structural design needs.

The smallest correction removes the unsupported productivity claim and blanket
survey/earthworks assurances. Retain preliminary contour observation and distinguish
it from designing or digging earthworks, which requires site assessment and suitable
local technical advice. Align the quiz question, answer and rationale with that
distinction rather than leaving audio or assessment to contradict the written text.

Coordinated assets: Reading the Landscape English slide-06 recording and full
track, manifest/size entries, transcript, and the existing linked A-frame clip's
teaching claims. The slide image itself has no time, area or sufficiency claim.
The clip has not been newly reviewed here; animation work remains held.
