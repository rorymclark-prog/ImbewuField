## What happens next

The intended order is one complete lesson bundle at a time. A still can be the
finished medium when motion adds no clear teaching value; a held slot is not an
automatic Flow order. No further x1 prompt is currently selected from the
remaining 24 slots after the latest source and phone-view review.

1. Choose a candidate whose farming claim and action can be shown safely in one
   continuous x1 shot.
2. Use one Flow prompt, inspect the actual whole clip and phone-scale frames, then
   integrate the video, poster, transcript/narration fit and still fallback.
3. Run the course checks, push one coherent batch, inspect both CI jobs and verify
   the preview route.
4. Keep a factual or visual hold when the clip cannot prove the lesson claim. Rory's
   explicit clearance is still required for any held SVG/code-drawn animation.
5. Develop Plant Propagation and a Small Nursery with the other reserve, Design
   and app media in reviewable bundles. Farm Finance is excluded from this
   completion pass at Rory's request. Seeds is not part of the replacement queue.
6. Develop Teach the Teachers and Mentorship as the last two courses. Their scope,
   lesson count, teaching staff, mentor capacity and delivery format still need a
   separate brief and review before they can be counted as built lessons.
