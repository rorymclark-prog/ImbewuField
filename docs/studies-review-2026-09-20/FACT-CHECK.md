## Sources checked or identified

- DWS, [Water use authorisation](https://www.dws.gov.za/ewulaas/WUA.aspx), and [National Water Act chapter 4](https://www.dws.gov.za/iwqs/nwa/tmp_Chapter_4.html). Do not use old gazette search snippets to infer current universal thresholds.
- ARC, [Rangeland management](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-api/Pages/Animal%20Recording%20and%20Improvement/Rangeland-Management-.aspx). Local assessment and forage planning support the reserve module direction.
- University of Minnesota Extension, [Multi-species grazing](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/animals-and-livestock/livestock-operations/multi-species-grazing). Sheep and goats share parasites; mixing species is not a substitute for parasite knowledge.
- American Consortium for Small Ruminant Parasite Control, [Grazing and parasite planning](https://www.wormx.info/growworms). Do not transplant US time/height recommendations into a South African universal rule.
- Rain Harvesting, [manufacturer handbook](https://rainharvesting.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Rain-Harvesting-Handbook-AU.pdf), first-flush ball-and-seat mechanism. Product-specific dimensions are not course defaults.

## Source handling

The user's manuals and decks supply the intended teaching scope and practical approach. They are not automatically current factual authority. Historical audit findings are checked against current code and assets before being called unresolved. Model-generated illustrations are checked for physical teaching errors as well as appearance.

## Corrections implemented in the illustrated release

- Removed the chicken stocking/fertility guarantee and the claim that chickens replace goat worm control. Added feed, water, shelter, observation and animal-health planning.
- Removed reliable tick-control and universal beehive-yield promises. Pollination is crop- and context-dependent; bird presence does not replace disease prevention.
- Distinguished nutrient cycling from complete closure: bought feed and products leaving still matter.
- Cold-air drainage: corrected the frost-as-flow explanation, removed unsupported fixed distances/timings and distinguished mist from frost. [FAO frost protection, energy transfer](https://www.fao.org/4/y7223e/y7223e09.htm) describes cold air moving downhill; [site selection](https://www.fao.org/4/y7223e/y7223e0c.htm) covers accumulation in low ground.
- [ARC pollination research](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-ppri/Pages/Insect%20Ecology/Pollination-Research.aspx) supports insect pollen transfer and crop-specific effects; no historical trial yield is promoted as a household guarantee.
- First-flush replacement v3 visually shows the ball against its upper seat; earlier ambiguous versions were rejected. This remains a conceptual cutaway, not construction dimensions or a drinking-water guarantee.
- Planting-in-rain and winter-shadow replacements inspected: corrected saturation/planting depth and inconsistent light direction.
- Flow roof scene v1 rejected because it invented water through a closed tank wall. v2 restricts motion to light drizzle and the farmer; all tank geometry remains stable in sampled frames.

## Still open before accuracy sign-off

The whole course is not yet fact-checked. Water slides contain unsourced universal sizing/slope/first-flush examples; Market contains unsourced loss and transport-price figures; Food Forest contains fixed succession times and an indigenous percentage target without attached evidence. Review these against the actual source or remove/qualify them across narration, slides, quizzes and audio. Soil compost-temperature advice needs time/process context before claiming pathogen control. Do not mistake a green software test suite for resolution of these claims.

[Grassland Biome, SANBI](https://pza.sanbi.org/vegetation/grassland-biome) explains the role of frost, fire and grazing in maintaining grass-dominated ecosystems. Reserve ecology material must not imply every South African landscape should become forest.

## 20 September correction pass: water, soil, forests, markets and vegetables

The earlier open items above are now corrected in the English lesson bodies, related quiz claims, narration, titles, generated slides and replacement audio. This supersedes their pending status, but does not certify untouched material or fluent pronunciation.

- **Water:** removed universal slope cut-offs, first-flush litres, province-based tank sizes, duck-cleaning claims and filter-as-potable guarantees. Added site assessment, designed overflow, intended-use water testing/treatment and water-authority checks. Greywater teaching now keeps untreated water away from edible crops and human contact; mulch is not disinfection. Three concept animations were re-rendered with corrected captions.
- **Soil:** removed colour/worm count as a diagnosis, an unsettled jar as proof of sandy texture, fixed regional compost readiness, core heat alone as sanitation, universal mulch cooling and worm-bin leachate as safe root feed. The soil comparison is explicitly an example requiring several observations. Compost and mulch actions remain practical, with process and contamination limits.
- **Food forest:** removed universal height bands, fixed establishment/harvest years, the unsupported 30% indigenous target and automatic non-competition. Existing species names remain unchanged and are explicitly examples requiring local identity, climate and legal checks. A useful tree is not automatically edible. Ground protection starts early; management responds to observed conditions. The sequence animation is now labelled one example, not a universal calendar.
- **Markets:** retained R18/R15 only as an explicitly labelled teaching example. Removed assumed channel prices, customer-count income guarantees, invented seed-diversity arithmetic, universal loss percentages and transport savings. Costs, losses, demand and payment terms must be checked. A food-gap date is worked backwards using the local crop calendar.
- **Vegetables:** reconciled the written lesson with safer pest-management narration, replacing the homemade spray recipe with registered-product/label guidance. Corrected Three Sisters attribution and immediate nitrogen-transfer assumptions. Removed blanket claims about seed packets, competition-free intercropping and guaranteed harvest overlap. A ten-day assignment checkpoint does not defer daily care.

Primary sources used for this pass (accessed 20 September 2026):

- [CDC: rainwater and health](https://www.cdc.gov/drinking-water/about/collecting-rainwater-and-your-health-an-overview.html) and [treatment systems](https://www.cdc.gov/drinking-water/about/about-home-water-treatment-systems.html): first-flush limits, contamination and treatment appropriate to the hazard/use.
- [WRC greywater guidance](https://wrc.org.za/?mdocs-file=65954): avoid edible crops, contact, spraying and pooling; local rules matter. The universal 24-hour figure was not introduced as a substitute for system-specific advice.
- [FAO small earth dams](https://www.fao.org/4/i1531e/i1531e00.htm): site, design, spillway and maintenance context; this course provides no construction dimensions.
- [NRCS soil health assessment](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health/soil-health-assessment) and [earthworm indicators](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/Earthworms.pdf): use several physical, chemical and biological indicators; conditions affect worm activity.
- [Clemson jar exercise](https://hgic.clemson.edu/factsheet/soil-texture-analysis-the-jar-test/): settling sequence and sufficient observation, with a simplified course exercise explicitly distinguished from laboratory texture analysis.
- [EPA home composting](https://www.epa.gov/recycle/composting-home) and [composting process](https://www.epa.gov/sustainable-management-food/composting): materials, air, moisture and maturity; no claim that one hot centre sanitises all material.
- [UC Master Gardeners worm composting](https://ucanr.edu/sites/default/files/2024-04/396102.pdf), leachate panel: liquid drainage differs from worm tea and may carry pathogens or phytotoxic substances; keep off edible plants. A dilution ratio was deliberately not promoted as disinfection.
- [NRCS fungi note](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/state-offices/illinois/soil-tech-note-7a-fungi) and [legume residues](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2023-04/nrcs142p2_053276.pdf): soil biology and nutrient release during decomposition.
- [UMN mulch guidance](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/mulching-for-soil-and-garden-health): protect soil while leaving stems clear; no universal temperature reduction copied.
- [World Agroforestry tree management](https://apps.worldagroforestry.org/Units/Library/Books/Book%2006/html/7.3_tree_mngment.htm?n=80): pruning/thinning manage competition; no regional calendar or cutting dimensions copied.
- [FAO postharvest handling](https://www.fao.org/4/ae075e/ae075e03.htm) and [shade/cooling](https://www.fao.org/4/ae075e/ae075e13.htm): careful handling and appropriate storage reduce losses; no universal loss fraction or local price assumed.
- [Maryland succession/intercropping](https://www.extension.umd.edu/resource/vegetable-succession-and-companion-planting): Three Sisters origin and complementary cropping, without claiming no resource competition.
- [South African agricultural-remedy registration](https://www.gov.za/services/fertilizers-farm-feeds-agricultural-remedies/agricultural-remedy) and [Department guidelines](https://www.nda.gov.za/index.php/publication/581-guidelines): use the local regulatory context; no homemade pesticide dose prescribed.
