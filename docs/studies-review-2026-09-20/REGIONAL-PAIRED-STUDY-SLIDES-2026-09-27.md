All audio remains English and requires an explicit English source narration choice. Unauthored frames fall back to the complete English slide. The paired JSON files in `docs/narration/` are the source audit records; `scripts/make-lesson-slides.mjs --validate-only` checks all headings and paragraphs against the current English narration before any image is written. The six registered WebP files are derived from those records. Changing a held paragraph to a draft needs a separate semantic review and new render.

Standard written Xitsonga is a provisional starting point for the Shangani-speaking group. Its comprehension has not been established by a speaker trial. Sandile advised that uncertain technical terms may stay in English for facilitators to explain; that does not certify these translations.
