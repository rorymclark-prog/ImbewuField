Farm Finance is excluded. Seeds and Seed Sovereignty (3 lessons; 8 registered video slots) is the completed baseline and is not in this rework pass. The current review register has 51 media slots: **27 video registrations and 24 still-only slots**. Counts below use the live `COURSE_DECKS` registrations plus the 51 rows in `PROGRAMME-MEDIA-MATRIX.md`; held candidate files are not registrations.

**Media boundaries:** depict Black African people in South African smallholder contexts in human media. Generate one x1 Flow output per prompt. Root checks each candidate for motion and farming accuracy and may continue without per-video Rory approval, while providing Rory a direct preview. Code-drawn/SVG motion stays held for Rory's visual clearance. A held slot is a still-first state, not an automatic request for another clip.

**Duplicate check, 23 September:** all 27 registered English video paths exist, match the offline byte manifest, and have distinct SHA-256 hashes. All 41 MP4 files under the public course-animation tree are also byte-distinct. Different files can repeat a teaching action: older unregistered hen-foraging, wide sheet-mulching and seed-or-seedling clips overlap the current foraging, close-up sheet-mulching and transplant slots. The old Seeds wet-processing duplicate is no longer registered. Those actions are excluded from new Flow prompts unless a separate lesson need is demonstrated. This is an asset/action inventory, not a full motion-quality approval.

**Evidence rule:** titles/IDs come from `lib/course-modules.ts`; registered videos from `lib/course-deck.ts`; still-only counts from the media matrix and `COVERAGE.md`; technical claims from the latest dated lesson check or topmost applicable continuation entry. A technical/player pass is not learner, Rory, practitioner, legal, fluent-language or farming approval. Historical playback notes do not establish current registration. “No source hold recorded” is not a claim that teaching is cleared.
