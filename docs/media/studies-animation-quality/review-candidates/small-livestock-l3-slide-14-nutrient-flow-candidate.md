## Concept and claim boundaries

A purchased-feed input enters the animal stage. Manure is collected and managed safely as compost. The graphic shows a partial return of some nutrients to the growing area, while food and other products leave the farm. It uses no amounts, percentages, processing duration, or application timing, and does not depict a sealed nutrient cycle. The static still does not retain the old “Watch” heading.

## Visual inspection

Inspected at full 1920 × 1080 resolution and simulated at 390 px wide (390 × 219). At full size, all three stages and the external feed/product flows are distinct. At phone fit, the title and stage headings can be read, but the supporting copy and flow labels are too small for a learner slide. Revise the layout for phone readability before considering registration. This remains a candidate for Rory to assess for design quality; it is not approved media.
