The English body qualifies the operations: leave the pruned support plant
healthy, keep mulch off the trunk, use the correct Bocking 14 cultivar and
avoid spreading root pieces, then observe insects and actual crop damage
rather than promising that flowers or wild garlic cure pests. The
[Royal Horticultural Society Bocking 14 profile](https://www.rhs.org.uk/plants/238189/symphytum-ibericum-bocking-14/details)
identifies the cultivar as sterile and propagated by root cuttings.
[Penn State Extension's tree mulching guide](https://extension.psu.edu/mulching-landscape-trees)
supports keeping mulch off the trunk, and its
[beneficial-insect guide](https://extension.psu.edu/let-beneficial-insects-work-for-you)
supports monitoring pests and natural enemies. No new species, dose,
spacing, yield, cure or guaranteed outcome was added.
