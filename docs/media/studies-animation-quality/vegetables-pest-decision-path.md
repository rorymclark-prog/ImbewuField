# Vegetables lesson 4, slide 16 — pest decision path

**Held review record:** Rory has not accepted this code-drawn animation. Its
MP4 and poster are no longer public assets or registered in the learner player.
The builder and hashes remain here only to make the earlier preview recoverable.
Slide 16 currently uses its existing still and protected narration.

The existing slide-16 still shows a damaged leaf, a helpful insect, a barrier
and hand removal. In the 390px learner player those options remain visible, but
the still cannot show the **order** of the protected English narration. The
ten-second silent authored diagram adds that order to the slide's optional
Watch control. It does not replace the still, speech, transcript or quiz.

The stages are: observe the damage, check water/roots/spacing/nutrition/drainage
before assuming a pest cause, protect beneficial insects, choose a suitable
light action and monitor, then apply the final conditional safeguard **if a
treatment is needed**. That last held frame asks for a product registered for
the crop and pest and instructs the learner to follow the label, protection
and harvest waiting instructions. It does not name a crop, pest species,
product, mixture, dose, diagnosis or promised result. The final frame holds
once instead of looping back to an earlier stage during narration.

Render with `python3
docs/media/studies-animation-quality/render-vegetables-pest-decision.py`.
The checked-in Python/Pillow and ffmpeg builder produced the MP4 and poster
twice with identical SHA-256 hashes, without a generation service or Google
Flow credits. The drawings are explanatory vector-like symbols; they are not
photographs of an observed field response. The first, middle and final frames
and 390px contact view were inspected. A first trial failed because its
registration text overlapped the badge; the corrected panel has separate
readable rows. Stage changes now crossfade briefly and the active dot moves
through the five panels. A second in-player review showed that native video
controls covered the lower half of the held frame at 390px, so all essential
registration, label, protection, harvest waiting and no-improvisation text was
moved into the upper portion before this candidate was kept.

The [FAO's integrated pest management practice guide](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/spi/scpi-home/managing-ecosystems/integrated-pest-management/ipm-how/en/)
supports observation, local diagnosis, beneficial organisms, non-chemical
options and monitoring; it also explicitly cautions that crop-protection
choices are site-specific and that pre-harvest intervals matter. The
[South African Government's agricultural remedy registration page](https://www.gov.za/services/fertilizers-farm-feeds-agricultural-remedies/agricultural-remedy)
explains national product registration and label requirements. Neither source
licenses a particular product or proves that any intervention works in a
learner's field.

The module remains English-only in the public deck/audio. The unpublished
isiZulu draft for slide 16 makes stronger outcome claims and omits crop-and-pest
registration and harvest waiting instructions. Fluent review against the
protected English script is required before any localized release. Human,
practitioner and learner acceptance of this new visual also remain open.
