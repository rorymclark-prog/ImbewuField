## Teaching fit and limits

The candidate visibly shows hens pecking and stepping among dry stems on bare
ground with continuous real motion. The birds remain identifiable in the 390px
contact view. This supports the slide's observation sentence. The image does
not show a moveable enclosure, prove that a bed was already harvested, or show
insects, weed seeds, feed, water, shelter or manure management. It must not be
used as evidence for those other statements in the lesson. The scene's location
is not established as South Africa, so no geographic claim is made for it.
