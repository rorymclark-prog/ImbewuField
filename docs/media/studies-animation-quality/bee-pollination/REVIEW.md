## Teaching distinction

A visit to one flower is observable behaviour. It does not, by itself, show pollen arriving on a receptive stigma or establish fertilisation, fruit set or yield. Do not keep the old narration about bees leaving a hive when this shot contains no hive. Do not title this footage a complete pollen-transfer demonstration unless a separate accurate explanation is produced.

For a complete replacement, pair the inspected flower visit with a clearly labelled explanatory view: anther makes pollen; loose pollen can adhere to hairs; suitable pollen reaching a receptive stigma is pollination. The explanatory view must remain distinguishable from the generated observation. The result must show the mechanism legibly, not merely add unrelated moving dots. Crop compatibility and later fruit development are not guaranteed by a depicted visit.
