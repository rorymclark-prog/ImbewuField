## Exact English narration proposal

**Slide 3 — Swales Slow Water on the Slope**

> A level contour swale is one design for slowing runoff. Its trench follows
> a level contour so water can spread and soak into suitable soil.
>
> Some swales are deliberately given a slight grade, with a designed safe
> outlet. Soil, slope, drainage and storm flow decide which approach is
> suitable. Ask a trained local adviser to assess the site before digging.

**Slide 4 — Watch: A Swale Sinks Water**

> This picture shows a level contour swale as a concept. Water may spread
> along the trench and soak in where the soil allows it.
>
> It does not show how deep moisture reaches on your land. Check the soil,
> slope and overflow route before building.

**Slide 5 — Plant on the Downhill Berm**

> The excavated soil forms a berm on the downhill side of a swale.
>
> Trees can be planted there when the site design is suitable. They may draw
> on moisture stored in nearby soil after rain; the result varies by site.

**Slide 7 — Watch: The Overflow Point**

> A swale needs a safe overflow route for heavy rain. Plan it before digging.
>
> Water may go to another swale or a dam only when a site assessment shows
> that the route, outlet and receiver can take the flow without damage.

## Primary source basis and coordinated work

- [ARC Agricultural Engineering](https://arc.agric.za/Agricultural%20Sector%20News/Simple%20solutions%20to%20combat%20soil%20erosion.pdf)
  describes a contour swale and an alternative with a slight slope that can
  lead water to a reservoir or stream.
- [Department of Water and Sanitation's rainwater-harvesting study](https://www.dws.gov.za/Documents/Other/WMA/12/RainwaterHarvesting.pdf)
  describes off-contour swales with a slight angle, controlled downslope
  conveyance and a safe discharge location. It warns that breached or poorly
  maintained contours can form severe gullies.
- [Department of Human Settlements stormwater guide](https://www.dhs.gov.za/sites/default/files/documents/Redbook/REDBOOK_Section_L_Stormwater_v1.pdf)
  sets the site, drainage, receiver, erosion and runoff questions behind a
  safe stormwater route. A conceptual animation cannot prove those conditions.
