The English lesson requires observation and checking water, roots, spacing,
nutrition and drainage before assuming pest cause. It protects beneficial
insects, favours a suitable light action, then asks the learner to monitor.
If a product is needed, the existing text/quiz require crop-and-pest
registration and label compliance, including protection and harvest waiting.
The [FAO integrated pest management guide](https://www.fao.org/agriculture/crops/thematic-sitemap/theme/spi/scpi-home/managing-ecosystems/integrated-pest-management/ipm-how/en/)
supports observation, local decisions, beneficial organisms, non-chemical
options and monitoring. The
[South African Government remedy registration page](https://www.gov.za/services/fertilizers-farm-feeds-agricultural-remedies/agricultural-remedy)
supports the product registration and labelling requirement. No pest species,
product, dose, cure, harvest or field outcome is inferred from the diagram.
