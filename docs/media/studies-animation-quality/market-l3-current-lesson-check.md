## Seed-sharing legal and teaching boundary

The lesson rightly requires crop/variety identity, source, collection date,
crop-specific isolation, selection, drying, storage and germination checks.
It currently says neighbours can share different varieties and its field
action invites a seed swap without a qualification about protected varieties.
South Africa's [Plant Breeders' Rights Act 12 of 2018, sections 7 and
10](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201903/4234729-3-2019plantbreedersact12of2018.pdf)
provides rights over protected varieties and exceptions, including private
non-commercial use and a farmer exception conditional on prescribed farmer
categories, plant categories, uses and, where applicable, royalties or
labelling. The [Department of Agriculture says the Act and regulations took
effect on 1 June 2025](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/agriculture-commencement-plant-breeders-rights-act-2018-and-regulations-23).
The lesson should not imply that every saved variety can be exchanged in
every circumstance. This is a scope warning, not a conclusion that any
particular seed lot in the film is protected or that all farmer exchange is
forbidden.
