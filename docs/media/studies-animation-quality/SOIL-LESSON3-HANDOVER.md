## Slide 14: conditional authored visual brief

Use this only if the comparative review above proves no existing candidate can meet the gate. Build a controlled, wordless comparison from the source above. It may keep the currently registered 14-second slot, but the teaching action must be unmistakable by the final English word at 7.443 seconds. A shorter replacement changes the current slot contract and must be reflected consistently in the deck and offline metadata; there is no reason to re-record English merely to accommodate the visual.

1. Establish the complete matched context: bare left top surface, loose leaf/straw cover on the right, fixed split line, fixed camera and unchanged cutaway faces.
2. Move into a deliberate macro inset or crop of the two *top surfaces* while retaining enough paired context to identify bare versus covered ground. This is a diagrammatic close-up, not an invented physical measurement.
3. Apply visibly equal, controlled raindrop inputs. On bare soil, show a few readable low splash arcs and a few brown grains detached by impact, all falling back with gravity. On covered soil, show the same drops intercepted by loose, stable cover; a few droplets may bead and pass through gaps.
4. Return to the full comparison. Surface wetting may be subtle on both sides. Keep possible water entry and limited downslope movement on both; do not show an impermeable mulch lid or universal runoff prevention.

Reject the replacement if rain or a water trail appears on a vertical cutaway face; rain differs between panels; water/grains travel uphill; soil layers, roots, plants or terrain transform; cover fuses into a mat or moves as a lid; the macro action is too small at 390 CSS pixels; or the film adds labels, rates, arrows, numbers, animal figures, voices, music, or a claim of instant improvement. Do not introduce microscopic soil life or toy/primitive animals: neither belongs to this mechanism.

The factual boundary is already recorded in [soil-rain/STORYBOARD.md](soil-rain/STORYBOARD.md) and [FACT-CHECK.md](FACT-CHECK.md): surface cover dissipates raindrop energy. It does **not** establish a South African mulch depth, storm intensity, infiltration/runoff percentage, sediment quantity, time-to-improvement, or nutrient/fertility result.
