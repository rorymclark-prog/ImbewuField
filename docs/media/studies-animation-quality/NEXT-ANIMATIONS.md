# Animation replacement order

Rory’s benchmark is the warm, lifelike Food Forest / Guilds artwork. Working playback and small files do not establish visual quality.

Visual screening: 57 on-disk posters reviewed in five contact sheets (includes English/isiZulu variants and existing Guilds / Seeds clips). This is a composition/style screen, not a new full-motion audit of every clip.

1. Chicken scene — replace tiny shape-based birds and gliding cage with detailed foraging film. Published in PR #456; frame review and live player verification completed. Rory’s motion assessment remains open.
2. Bee pollination — replace tiny schematic bees with a detailed close view of a bee contacting a flower’s anthers and stigma. Behaviour and morphology must be checked; no pretend pollen transport through the hive. A controlled explanatory overlay should make the transfer legible.
3. Forest layers and establishment — use detailed matched vegetation assets, depth and deliberate reveal, while preserving exact layer labels. Do not depict instant growth or invent plants. The seven-layer guided cutaway is implemented on `codex/forest-layers-quality`; establishment remains to rebuild. Ground-to-canopy and establishment are authored timelines, not generic generated scenery.
4. Living soil and mulch protection — build textured soil sections with legible channels, ground cover, water entry and surface runoff. Clearly conceptual; no diagnostic certainty or invented flow rates.
5. Water, sun, cold-air and windbreak explanations — keep the useful mechanism, improve terrain/materials, camera framing, labels and motion. Preserve correct direction, northern sun orientation and water level relationships. Mzomoyethu is the preferred contextual reference.
6. Nutrient loops, markets and farm records — premium animated information graphics on top of relevant homestead scenes; simplify the sequence, keep readable labels, avoid tiny moving icons and text walls. These need legibility and causal clarity rather than photorealism everywhere.
7. Existing Flow/Guilds/Seeds footage — judge full motion shot by shot for handling, anatomy, object continuity and actual lesson fit. Do not replace useful existing footage just to increase the clip count.

Acceptance gate for every replacement: art direction matches reference; subject is legible on a phone; natural or physically coherent motion; stable anatomy/assets; narration describes what is shown; no invented agronomic outcomes; source traceability; complete playback plus frame review; honest download size and offline replacement; record remaining limitations. No passing a flat placeholder because it moves.

For authored 3D, use properly modelled textured assets and rigged movement when animals are shown. Smooth camera motion over spheres and cylinders is not an adequate substitute. Use Flow for lifelike observable behaviour and authored scenes for exact mechanisms.
