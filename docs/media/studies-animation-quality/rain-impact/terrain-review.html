Review limits

Inspect the first contact, splash and late frames. The model has no soil absorption or detached soil grains. It cannot demonstrate infiltration, an erosion rate or a whole storm. Repeated leaf shapes and the CG appearance remain quality concerns. No narration is recorded for this study.

Motion contains every simulated frame from 20 to 56 at 24 fps. Slow playback is a review control, not a claim about real time. Playback completion alone does not establish visual quality.
