# Held locally authored study animations — 22 September 2026

Rory rejected the visual quality of the code-drawn/SVG-style lesson animations
and requires his explicit clearance before any such animation appears in the
learner player. The registered set was audited from `lib/course-deck.ts`, the
renderer scripts and git provenance.

Twenty-two learner registrations were withdrawn. Twenty now continue to use
their existing lesson stills; Soil Health slide 10 and Small Livestock slide 9
have since received separately reviewed Flow candidates. Protected narration,
transcripts, quizzes and rationales remain unchanged.
Six older unregistered code-drawn movies were also removed from the public
course payload so offline packs do not spend learner data downloading media the
player cannot use. In total, 28 movies and 28 posters were removed from
`public/course-animations/` and from saved course caches.

The genuine Flow and source-footage registrations remain: roof rain, the two
Mzomoyethu water extracts, Earth Care mulch, A-frame use, compost materials,
the new compost-build shot, sheet mulching, hens, ducks, the new
bee-between-blossoms shot and seed sharing.
The Seeds and Plant Guilds media have no identified local renderer provenance
and were left in place pending their normal visual review.

The renderer scripts and review documents remain as a recoverable historical
record. They are not learner media and their existence is not approval. Any
future SVG or code-drawn replacement must be shown to Rory before registration.
Flow replacements are made one prompt at a time, x1, and only after the specific
lesson gap is confirmed.

This is a technical withdrawal and cache migration. It is not human, farmer,
practitioner, learner or fluent isiZulu acceptance of the retained media.
