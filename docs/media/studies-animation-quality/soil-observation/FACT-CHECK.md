# Soil observation: factual scope

Checked 20 September 2026 against primary sources:

- USDA NRCS, [Soil Health Assessment](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health/soil-health-assessment): use several physical, chemical and biological indicators; no single outcome diagnoses health. Physical indicators include structure, pores, infiltration and water holding. Observe patterns across a field and over time.
- USDA NRCS, [Soil Health and Small-scale Agriculture](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health/soil-health-and-small-scale-agriculture): surface residues protect soil; roots and soil cover are relevant observations.

The two matched cutaways are illustrations, not measured samples or a before/after result. Their similar colour and root density deliberately avoid implying that cover instantly changes the soil underneath. No good/bad label is assigned. The close-ups direct attention to structure, roots and surface cover. Feeling a moist sample is a field observation, not something the video claims to demonstrate. Colour alone is insufficient; the final narration prompts several further checks. No numerical thresholds, application rates, named species or US-specific prescriptions are introduced. The rainfall comparison remains separate and unaccepted.
