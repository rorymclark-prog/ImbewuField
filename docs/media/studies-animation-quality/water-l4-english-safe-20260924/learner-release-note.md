# Water Harvesting L4 learner update — 24 September 2026

**Based on core English branch starting at `origin/main` `6d0c39b2`.**

We updated the greywater lesson to explain why household water sources and risks vary. Before considering any reuse, check the source, household services, intended use and site with your municipality and a qualified sanitation adviser. If you cannot get clear advice, do not reuse the water. The lesson does not provide a farm reuse design or say reuse is automatically permitted. Slides 19–22 now use static safety cards with matching English narration. Slide 21 remains a still. Previously saved English copies are cleared once so learners can choose to download the updated lesson.
