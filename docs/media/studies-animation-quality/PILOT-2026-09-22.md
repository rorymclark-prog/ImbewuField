Make a few foreground raindrop impacts clearly legible: on the left, droplets hit exposed earth and lift small brown grains in short low arcs which fall back under gravity; on the right, droplets hit leaves and straw, bead and drip through gaps while the cover remains in place. ## Review and decision

Inspected the source, browser playback with progress observed through 7.56 seconds, and all 16 review frames covering the eight-second output. This is not a claim of a human full-motion or audio-listening sign-off.

The broad source composition, roots and mulch remain recognisable; impacts and water movement are more visible than in the old authored prototype. However, the bare side develops conspicuous concentrated splash and pale surface flow with vertical drips down the cutaway edge. The detachment of individual soil grains is not clearly legible in the phone-sized comparison. Visible rain on each side does not establish equal rainfall forcing. The motion therefore does not yet justify a clean controlled comparison or a claim about infiltration. An attractive, technically valid film has not completed the teaching objective.
