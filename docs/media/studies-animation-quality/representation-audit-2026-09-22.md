# Studies visual representation direction and core media audit

**Date:** 22 September 2026
**Direction from Rory:** when people appear in Studies images or animations, depict Black African people in the South African smallholder setting. Person-free diagrams need no added figures. Apply this to every new media brief and review existing assets before replacing them.

## Current core audit

Two lower-cost audits inspected all 33 core lesson images, the English slide stills for the 51 animation review slots, and three sampled frames from each of the 28 registered core videos. The supervising review opened the flagged full-resolution video frames and ambiguous lesson images. The current Reading the Landscape L4 slide 17 is a person-free still; its rejected mapmaking Flow clip is not registered.

No definite portrayal contrary to the requested direction was found in the current core media. Several initial video flags were caused by bright daylight and distance: full-resolution frames of Soil Health L2 slide 11, Reading the Landscape L1 slide 6, and Plant Guilds L2 slide 27 visually depict Black farmers. The same check found the people in Small Livestock L1 slide 7, Plant Guilds L3 slides 41 and 45, and Water Harvesting L3 slide 14 visually aligned with the direction. The market and vegetable flat illustrations use brown-skinned human figures; some other diagrams contain no people.

This is a visual consistency check, not verification of any depicted person's identity, nationality, consent, farming role or community acceptance. Hands-only shots and tiny distant figures cannot establish identity from appearance. Rory's visual approval and the wider learner/practitioner reviews remain open.

## Production rule for remaining media

- Specify Black African adults or community members in the prompt or source brief whenever people are needed. Keep clothes, setting and action appropriate to the South African smallholder lesson without inventing a named person or place.
- Review the actual whole video and phone-sized frames. Do not treat prompt wording as proof of what Flow produced.
- Keep a still when one film cannot show the lesson action accurately. Reading the Landscape L4 slide 17 is still-only by Rory's decision.
- Extend this visual check to reserve courses, Finance, Design and app-guide media as those assets are prepared; the present audit covers the current core media listed above.
