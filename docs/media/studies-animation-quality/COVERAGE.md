# Studies media coverage register

Prepared from `lib/course-deck.ts`, `lib/course-audio.ts`, the reserve curriculum
index and lesson files, `lib/course-finance-content.json`,
`lib/course-design-content.json`, and `reserve/app-companions/README.md`.

This register has **158 coverage records**: 51 core animation slots +
51 reserve draft lessons + 24 Finance lessons + 18 Design lessons + 14 app
companions. A record is a review and production-planning unit. It is **not** a
video, a completed lesson, an accepted shot, or an order to make media. In
particular, a `src` registration is not acceptance: inspect the complete asset,
the first and last frames, its phone view, transcript, narration fit and offline
fallback before it can be accepted.

## Treatment key and shared limits

- **Retain and review** means inspect the existing asset before deciding it can
  remain. It does not spend credits. A replacement is conditional on a failed
  review and the completed lesson bundle.
- **Authored precise diagram** means controlled illustration, geometry, masks,
  arrows, labels, calculations or interface annotation; it is the default for
  relationships, quantities, dates, flows and decisions. Rory requires his
  explicit visual clearance before any SVG or code-drawn **animation** is
  accepted or released; a diagram treatment in this register is only a draft
  option until that clearance.
- **Conditional Flow treatment** means one candidate only after the listed
  preconditions; it is never an automatic shot order. Use an inspected reference,
  one continuous eight-second action, locked camera by default, x1, no text,
  figures, interfaces, narration, music, cuts, time lapse or invented outcome.

Keep each lesson unit together: lesson body, slides, quiz/rationale, EN and
isiZulu narration, transcript, still/reduced-motion alternative, offline
replacement and phone review. Do not add species or figures. Dynamic controls
need a dated check; practitioner holds remain human decisions. Animal media must
preserve anatomy, welfare, stable fences/feet/tools and real-world continuity.
Finance visuals use only checked practice values and retain missing values as
missing; they never invent prices, tax, interest, forecasts or conversions.
Physical mechanisms (water, soil, plant handling, manure and animal movement)
must be visibly plausible and must not claim instant recovery, safety or a
guaranteed outcome.

### Existing-media reconciliation comes first

Every registered `src` in the core table is **existing and review-first**. It is
unreviewed until reconciled against the local asset, its poster/source frame, the
published/player registration and its lesson narration; it is not missing and it
does not justify a replacement request. Before any future shot order, search and
compare the relevant existing Flow project, local source, published asset and
this register. Record the result as retain, a controlled authored replacement, or
a specific escalation. Do not regenerate a clip simply because another candidate
exists.

Known review evidence to reconcile, rather than regenerate: the existing Flow
asset **Honeybee walking on apple blossom**, `bee-pollination-source.png`, the
old Fast **Rain falls on split-screen soil**, the new Quality **Rainfall striking
soil comparison** trial, **Three hens foraging in enclosure**,
`chickens-source.png`, **Woman lowering hand in drizzle**, and **Community
members sharing dry seeds**. The relevant existing project locations are
**Studies**, **Mzomoyethu** (visible water capture), and **PlantGuilds**. The
soil trial and older Fast candidate require direct side-by-side pedagogical and
physical-mechanism review; neither is a reason to place another order.
