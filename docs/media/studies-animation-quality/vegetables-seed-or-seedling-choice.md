# Vegetables lesson 1, slide 6 — seed or seedling choice

**Held review record:** Rory has not accepted this code-drawn animation. Its
MP4 and poster are no longer public assets or registered in the learner player.
The builder and hashes remain here only to make the earlier preview recoverable.
Slide 6 currently uses its existing still.

The existing eight-second Flow derivative shows a person handling a nursery
seedling, but its final root plug is still above the bed. It cannot teach a
finished transplant. The slide's English narration asks the learner to choose
between direct sowing and a protected nursery start for the particular crop.
The replacement is therefore an **authored schematic choice**, not a claim that
the filmed action was completed. The original movie and poster remain on disk
as source evidence, but the learner's Watch button points to the diagram.

The diagram has two generic panels. A seed enters the bed and is covered; an
intact nursery plug moves into a schematic hole and surrounding soil returns.
The last frame holds so the comparison remains visible during longer speech.
It shows no named species, planting depth, spacing, timing, fertiliser, survival
or yield. The slide's existing advice to check the seed packet and local grower
guidance still governs crop-specific decisions.

Render locally with `python3
docs/media/studies-animation-quality/render-seed-or-seedling-choice.py`.
The checked-in Python/Pillow and ffmpeg builder produces the MP4 and JPEG under
`public/course-animations/vegetables-staples/`, without a generation service
or Google Flow credits. It is a controlled illustration rather than a photo or
footage of a real South African planting outcome.

[South Africa's Agricultural Research Council](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-vopi/Newsletter%20Library/ARC-VOP%20Newsletter%20No%202%2C%20June%202018.pdf)
describes a planting hole and soil covering the nursery root plug. The
[University of Minnesota Extension's vegetable planting guide](https://extension.umn.edu/garden-and-home/yard-and-garden/gardening-in-minnesota/planting-the-vegetable-garden)
distinguishes direct sowing from transplanting and cautions that some crops do
not tolerate transplanting. These sources support the limited relationship
shown; neither supplies a universal spacing or depth rule for the app.

playback and source checks. The lesson's existing isiZulu draft remains outside
the English-only public deck/audio pending fluent editorial review.
