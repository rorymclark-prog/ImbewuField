## Source basis and coordinated work

- [FAO frost guidance](https://www.fao.org/4/y7223e/y7223e0c.htm) explains
  cold-air drainage and site-specific effects of slope, aspect, soil and
  obstacles. It does not promise that every valley frosts first and last.
- [ARC climate monitoring](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/Pages/Climate-Monitoring-Services.aspx)
  provides site-specific wind observations; it does not support one damaging
  wind direction for every Highveld or KZN escarpment farm.
- [KZN DARD tomato guidance](https://www.kzndard.gov.za/images/Documents/Horticulture/Veg_prod/tomato.pdf)
  identifies prolonged cool, damp spells as favourable to late blight.
  [University of Minnesota Extension](https://extension.umn.edu/agriculture/specialty-crops/vegetable-farming/disease-management/late-blight)
  lists several controls, including drying leaves and allowing air between
  plants; bed relocation alone is not a complete control.
