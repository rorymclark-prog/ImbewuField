## Playback issue found

The compact audio list could advance to the next spoken slide while the learner
opened the silent Earth Care video. The result was a slide 4 picture alongside
slide 5 speech. The player now pauses the compact list when a deck video starts,
and pauses a running deck video when another audio clip starts, including when
the deck's own audio is already paused. Confirm both directions on the deployed
preview after this correction is pushed; local typecheck and suite alone cannot
verify browser media events.

## Source hold before content clearance

The current slide 7 diagram and lesson quiz present sharing borehole access while
monitoring the water table as the right decision without first checking the
permitted use or whether the supply can support it. South Africa's National
Water Act §22 makes water use conditional on Schedule 1, an existing lawful use,
a general authorisation, a licence or other listed authority. Monitoring is not
itself permission or evidence of sustainable supply. Review the narrow English
lesson, quiz, slide 7 still and narration correction before clearing this lesson
for teaching. AGENTS.md protects lesson text and Rory's permission for a
programme-wide narrow correction is pending. No isiZulu approval is implied.

Primary source: [National Water Act 36 of 1998](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a36-98.pdf),
§22 and Schedule 1.
