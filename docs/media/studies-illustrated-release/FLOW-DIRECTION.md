# Google Flow treatment

Project: https://flow.google.com/project/7d35cc98-916e-4c7b-89b0-86365cac5442

Veo 3.1 Quality, 16:9, one eight-second generation at a time. Nine generations used 900 credits; eight accepted scenes are in flow-clips.json. The A-frame is trimmed to six seconds. No further purchase or remaining-balance claim.

Direction used warm rural South African source photographs, a fixed camera, stable people/objects and one restrained teaching action. No generated writing, speech, music, instant growth or invented equipment. Scene-specific actions:

- Earth care: place mulch around plants without burying stems.
- A-frame: hold feet still, observe a small settling plumb-line swing. Late leg lifting was excluded; this is not a complete contour-setting demonstration.
- Compost: tip recognisable green plant material onto dry brown material; no instant decomposition or heat effect.
- Seedling: lower an intact root plug into a prepared hole; keep the stem upright and avoid burying it.
- Sheet mulch: wet plain overlapping cardboard, then place leaves above it; avoid trunk burial.
- Ducks: natural bill-foraging among established plants; no scratching or promised complete pest control.
- Seed sharing: transfer existing dry seed into a paper envelope; stable seed identity, hands and containers.
- Roof rain: the first generation invented a leak through the tank wall and was rejected. The accepted retry explicitly requested very light drizzle, a stationary intact closed tank, no external flow/overflow/filling effect and only a small lowering of the woman's hand. The exact mechanism is taught separately with authored diagrams.

Review used two-frame-per-second contact sheets across the source clips, individual frames where needed, full decoding and actual Watch-button playback. This is the recorded review scope, not a claim of independent agronomic certification. Generated footage is an illustration, never evidence of an actual measured outcome at a farm.
