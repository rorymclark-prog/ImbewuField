# ACT / SEF mentor review — 8 September 2026

Rory requested a mentor experience weighted towards African Conservation Trust's
SEF food-security delivery, while retaining enough flexibility for other garden
programmes. The branch includes the training-evidence work from PR #433 and the
invoice / visit-follow-up work now on main (#428).

## Programme fit

- Field teams can choose **ACT · SEF food security** or **General garden support**.
  The tour uses the ACT focus. Existing live teams retain general support until
  their organisation selects the appropriate focus; no live client is relabelled.
- Priorities put due actions and participant coverage before the directory.
  The visit guide covers garden upkeep, water, soil, seedlings, harvest use and
  practical coaching. Searchable participant cards open the shared visit form.
- Visits retain support areas, observations, agreed action, owner, follow-up,
  photographs, optional GPS and a practical skill observation. Action completion
  requires a date and evidence of what changed.
- Attendance, certificates of attendance and demonstrated skills remain distinct.
  These records support programme delivery; they do not certify employment
  attendance, payroll, contractual compliance or independently verified outcomes.
- No current ACT targets, headcounts or delivery agreement were available. Historical
  website figures are not imported as current programme defaults.

## Correctness changes

The old learner-card visit button wrote nothing in the tour and used a separate
collection live. It now opens FieldTeams with that participant selected. Visit
saving returns the confirmed record and keeps it visible, including when a long
history is loaded. Existing legacy records are not deleted or migrated.

One scoped calculation supplies visit figures, actions, charts and exported report
content. Participant coverage deduplicates repeat visits and does not claim to count
distinct sites. A support-area bar chart may count one visit under several areas;
it is deliberately not a pie chart. Action status is the latest recorded status,
not a reconstructed historical snapshot. Report photography states loaded versus
recorded availability.

The former 500-visit hard stop is replaced with cursor pages. Full coverage figures
and the report wait until available history has been loaded. Existing organisation,
assignment, own-visit, photo and concurrent-edit checks remain in the API.

Learning query failures no longer become zero progress or absent enrolments. Late
loads are discarded after an account, organisation or role change. Funders are
directed to their published-evidence workspace; mentor notes remain internal.
Changing the visit participant clears coordinates and cancels pending location capture.

## Presentation route

1. Open the mentor tour: show the priorities and food-security guide.
2. Open **People & gardens**, find a participant, then **Record visit**.
3. Capture an observation, skill result and next action with owner and date.
4. Save, reopen the visit and show its report. Complete an action only with evidence.
5. Open **Training** for attendance, signatures, photographs, venue and feedback;
   open **Learning** for individual module progress.
6. Show published programme outcomes from the funder workspace. Use the mentor
   field report for internal coordination because it contains private notes.

## Public sources and interpretation

- [ACT food security](https://projectafrica.com/food-security/) describes ongoing
  garden mentoring and agroecological support.
- [ACT Social Employment Fund](https://projectafrica.com/social-employment-fund/)
  describes food security alongside wider environmental and community activities.
- [SEF Food Security and Nutrition learning brief](https://www.socialemploymentfund.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Social-Employment-Fund_Learning-Brief_Food-Security-and-Nutrition-1.pdf)
  supports practical training, food distribution and mentorship around water,
  fencing, pests, inputs and market access.
- [IDC strategic implementing partner terms, 2022](https://www.idc.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2022/10/Terms-of-Reference-Strategic-Implementing-Partners-Open-Call-2022.pdf)
  describe SEF reporting and its payment / attendance system. This historical
  source does not establish ACT's current contractual requirements.
- [SEF: beyond employment outcomes](https://www.socialemploymentfund.co.za/practice-note-beyond-employment-outcomes/)
  supports recording community benefit and learning alongside work delivered.

The screen priorities and prompts are product interpretations of these sources,
not a reproduction of an ACT-approved reporting form. The separately supplied
two-page report visual proposal uses only figures from Rory's uploaded PDF.
