# ACT Teach the Teachers two-day refresher — source and delivery note

The proposed app course is at `/student/teach-the-teachers`. It prepares ACT's agroecology and permaculture facilitators to teach from the existing core lessons. It is a **two-day practice refresher**, not a compressed version of the five-day course and not a facilitator qualification. The 09:00–16:00 agenda is a planning template; ACT has not confirmed hours, participant count, trainers, venue, device access, language mix, assessment policy or certificate wording.

## What informed the course

- Rory asked in this task for a Teach the Teachers course after the core programme, then on 25 September specified a two-day ACT refresher next week for the agroecological/permaculture course, with app practice.
- `docs/studies-review-2026-09-20/reserve/facilitator-and-capstone.md` contributes observable teach-back, accessible responses, source/unknown labels, distinct attendance and performance evidence, privacy, and a retry after feedback.
- Unmerged PR #509 (`courses/teach-the-teachers/`) is a **five-day draft**. Its useful structure is a model lesson, `Look · Try · Talk · Take home`, small-group micro-teaching, observer feedback, app/paper alternatives and co-facilitator planning. Its fixed 20-person/two-trainer schedule, five rounds, certificate rules and farming demonstrations are not imported as ACT facts.
- Rory's `Imbewu_Course_Outlines.docx` in the local training archive supports practical and outdoor teaching but does not specify this two-day format. `docs/ACT-SEF-MENTOR-READINESS-2026-09-08.md` says ACT targets, headcounts and delivery agreement were not available when written.

## Boundaries before delivery

1. ACT should confirm the room, hours, group size, language support, available trainers, safe practice site, devices and paper materials. Parallel teach-backs need enough observers for everyone to get a turn.
2. Use an existing core lesson only after checking its exact source, current lesson media and local limits. Avoid the old draft's uncited ratios, chemical/greywater recipes, crop spacing or planting windows. A field observation is not a soil diagnosis or construction design.
3. Use demo accounts and fictional/practice sites for app exercises. A real participant's site or photo requires the programme's actual consent and data-handling process. Photo refusal must not bar attendance.
4. Written lesson translations, slides and narration can be at different stages. Mark machine drafts and English fallbacks; arrange fluent/local interpretation rather than implying an audio track is translated.
5. Attendance, self-report, quiz answers, submitted photos and observed practical performance are different evidence. The observation guide is formative. ACT must decide any certificate wording and may choose attendance-only wording; this draft grants no professional or accredited status.

## App check before the session

On a trainer phone, open the new page from My Studies, follow one core lesson link, play a slide and audio, switch language, download an available lesson, briefly disconnect, and reopen it. If any step fails, use the printed still/transcript and log the failed step rather than improvising a claim. Recheck the live app near delivery because lessons and translations are changing in batches.
