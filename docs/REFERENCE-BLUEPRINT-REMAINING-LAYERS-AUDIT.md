## Reusable visual rules

1. **Geometry first:** never move, resize, reshape or recolor a saved polygon, line, footprint or source-derived house/driveway. Any new visual is a stroke, fill, badge or label composited from that record.
2. **Data-gated content:** a legend row, callout, level, patio, vetiver bank or phase appears only when its corresponding saved feature/item/line exists. Satellite appearance is not evidence.
3. **One owner per mark:** zones own zone fills and badges; water owns water routes/symbols; planting owns growing footprints; structures own built footprints; the whole sheet composes them once.
4. **Panel order is semantic:** use fixed section headings and stable category order, but omit empty sections. Do not let alphabetical or placement order define the reading order.
5. **Local labels only:** prefer a contained centroid or nearest valid point, then a short leader to the margin. Keep leaders on the map side, avoid the panel, and de-conflict vertically without changing the anchor.
6. **Context recedes:** existing ground, house and driveway provide orientation on layer sheets but must use quieter alpha than the active layer. Hard surfaces use hatch; tar remains a solid dark access mark.
7. **Print-scale legibility:** preserve the current fixed type size and fit by removing least-important rows/bullets, never by shrinking labels until they become unreadable.
8. **Stable identity:** sheet number, title, cache key and gallery label must use one plan-set numbering table. For this benchmark comparison, masterplan is `06` and phasing is `07`.

## Deliberately not recommended

- Do not infer terrace contours, elevations, patio extents, gates, swales, tanks, species or phase work from the benchmark image.
- Do not ask the image model to repaint zone geometry, write labels, or create the legend for these exact sheets.
- Do not add extra callouts merely to match benchmark density; the saved design may contain fewer systems.
