208 | Bees: Pollination, Honey and System Ecology
209 | Honeybees and other insects carry pollen between flowers. This helps many fruit and vegetable crops, including avocado. Different crops and varieties have different pollination needs. A hive does not guarantee higher yields everywhere: weather, water, plant health and other pollinators also matter.
210 | South Africa has two native honeybee subspecies. The Cape honeybee occurs in the Western Cape and parts of the Eastern Cape. The African honeybee is native to central and most of southern Africa. These broad natural distribution ranges are not a guide for moving bees. The Department's control measures set a demarcation line for the moving of bees. Check the current rules on moving with the Department and an experienced local beekeeper before you move bees or hives.
211 | Learn from an experienced local beekeeper before you get a hive. Keep hives away from busy paths, houses and places where children play. Morning sun can help; a safe place comes first. Provide flowering plants through the seasons and avoid bees being exposed to pesticides. Active bees do not prove that the farm is free of chemicals or diseases. The national control measures for honeybees require registration for defined beekeeping activities, including managed hives for bee products, queen rearing, commercial pollination, and the removal, eradication or relocation of colonies. Check with the Department if you are not sure whether the rules apply to your activity. If a colony swarms repeatedly, ask a trained beekeeper to inspect it. Overcrowding is one possible cause, not a diagnosis.
