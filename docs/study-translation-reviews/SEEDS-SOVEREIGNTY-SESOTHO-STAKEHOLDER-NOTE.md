# Sesotho Seeds and Seed Sovereignty: stakeholder note

On 26 September 2026, Sandile's voice note asked that unfamiliar or key farming terms remain in English for now because translations can change their meaning. Facilitators can explain them in local languages during training. This is stakeholder guidance, not fluent-speaker or farming-review approval.

Keep **Seed Sovereignty**, **open-pollinated**, **F1 hybrid**, and **Dry and Wet Methods** in English. The module title and description, and the L2 title, are held in exact English. Only the general L1 and L3 headings are Sesotho machine drafts paired with their exact English sources. Lesson bodies, key points, quizzes, image descriptions, slides, and narration remain English.
