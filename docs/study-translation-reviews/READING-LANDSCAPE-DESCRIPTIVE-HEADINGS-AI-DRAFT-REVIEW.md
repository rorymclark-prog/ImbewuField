- **Field-instruction hold:** Sesotho slide 1's existing translation of “Read the land before you change it. Find where water moves, where sunlight falls, where wind travels, and where cold air settles.” was also restored to an exact-English hold. This was not counted as one of the ten new heading candidates.

Per language: Sesotho 4/3, Tshivenda 3/4, Xitsonga 5/2 (draft/hold). These counts include prior drafts, not just this screen's candidates.
