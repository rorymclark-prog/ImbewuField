## Points for the reviewer

- For Vegetables and Staple Crops, check whether the local term for staple food or crop has the same scope as the English phrase. “Succession planting” and “pest management” are also technical farming concepts; keep them in English if a clear local equivalent is not available.
- For Seeds and Seed Sovereignty, check the distinction between saving, storing and sharing seed. “Seed sovereignty” describes control and choice over seed, not simply seed storage; preserve the English term if the Tshivenda candidate would narrow that idea.
- For Plant Selection & Guilds, check “plant partners” and “guild” as a designed group of plants with interacting roles. Do not let a candidate imply that any nearby plants automatically form a beneficial guild.
- For Market Gardening & Community, check whether “market gardening” is understood as growing produce for sale. Preserve the distinction between surplus after household needs, selling, record-keeping and local food networks.
