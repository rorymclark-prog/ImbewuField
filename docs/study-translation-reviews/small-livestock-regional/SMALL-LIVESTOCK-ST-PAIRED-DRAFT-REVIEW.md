## Back-translation notes

The back-translations retain the source meaning at a plain descriptive level. In slides 1 and 2, `liphoofolo tse ruuoang tse nyenyane` literally describes small domesticated animals; a fluent speaker should check whether it is natural for “small livestock” in this teaching context. Slide 1 uses `ho kopanya` (“bringing together/combining”) for “integration,” which may be narrower than the systems meaning. Slide 17 is a simple observation question. These checks do not establish fluency or local farming approval.
