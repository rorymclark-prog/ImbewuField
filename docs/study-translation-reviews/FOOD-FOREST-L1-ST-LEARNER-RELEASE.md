# Food Forest L1 Sesotho draft release

26 September 2026. This source-paired machine draft is not a fluent-speaker or local farming
approval.

## Learner scope

Food Forest L1 in Sesotho shows five marked descriptive sentences: the forest from branches to
roots, light and moisture at each plant's level, the original Highveld example, the example's
second plant group, and how shade and leaf remains change conditions below growing plants.

The species and crop names in the Highveld examples remain exactly as written in English. The
following warning against treating the example as permission to plant every species remains in
English, along with the title, diagram description, remaining body paragraphs, key points and quiz.
The full English body is shown alongside the draft. Slides and narration remain English.

## Wording held back

The paragraphs about fixed planting layers, suitable supports, spacing, establishment care,
competition and pruning stay in English because they give practical design guidance. A proposed
Tshivenda Market L2 batch was not released: back-checking found that its wording could change
"retain more of the sale price" into earning more, "regular selection" into an all-the-time food
selection, and the warning that area or customer count alone is not a forecast into a forecast.

These Sesotho sentences are provisional machine drafts and still need review by a fluent Sesotho
speaker before they are treated as approved teaching language.
