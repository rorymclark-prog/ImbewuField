# Seeds and Seed Sovereignty — regional orientation and reflection pilot

**Status:** source-paired review packet only; not connected to learner Study. The
module title, technical teaching, quizzes, slide bodies, and narration remain in
exact English. No fluent-language, learner, seed-saving, farmer, or ACT cohort
approval is recorded.

This bounded pilot covers four general section labels from the English Seeds
and Seed Sovereignty narration. Reflection questions remain in exact English
because the available candidates did not pass the narrow semantic screen. The
pilot does not translate seed genetics, pollination, seed selection,
processing, drying, storage, viability, crop names, numbers, or safety
guidance. The technical term **Seed Sovereignty** remains in English.

Each target is an unreviewed machine draft paired with its exact English source.
“Keep candidate” below records only a second low-effort machine semantic screen;
it is not a language or local-use approval. Sesotho and Tshivenda candidates
still need fluent-speaker review. Xitsonga is provisional for a Shangani-speaking
cohort and needs review for dialect fit as well as meaning.

## Machine review record

Gemini 3.8 Flash Low produced the candidate translations from the English
strings. A separate Gemini 3.7 Flash Low pass compared each candidate against
its source and flagged uncertain meaning or agreement. The screen marked the
four labels and “What will you do next?” as keep-candidates in all three
languages. It marked Xitsonga seed questions for uncertain noun
agreement/meaning and the Tshivenda exchange question for an uncertain verb;
the full seed reflection above therefore stays in exact English. Machine
review does not establish orthographic correctness, natural register,
comprehension, or farming safety.

## Review needed before learner use

- A fluent Sesotho and Tshivenda speaker should review every draft for meaning,
  natural wording, and local register.
- A fluent standard written Xitsonga reviewer should review its four label
  drafts and the suitability of this written variety for the intended
  Shangani-speaking learners.
- A facilitator should check that labels and reflection questions are clear in
  the actual Study screen. No lesson instruction or assessment wording is
  included in this pilot.
