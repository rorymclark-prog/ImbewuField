## App and output behaviour

The real page supplies both displayed site conditions and an independent current
store reader to the existing guarded workflow. A marked proposal shows canonical
source limits, selected inputs, assumptions, country, region and original/new review
dates. An explicit unchecked declaration gates acceptance. Unchecking/rechecking
while a save is pending retires the captured declaration; it cannot revive a stale
attempt. Whole-book occupancy, private generation and final raw-writer checks remain.

Saved history is separate from operational approval. A current country/source hold
keeps the accepted evidence readable without supplying replacement catalogue dates.
Historical HTML validates the actual admitted saved row and rechecks actor/base
through the asynchronous preparation and final file click. It labels estimates as
historical, planned and unobserved. Structural actor, source-key/revision and policy
fingerprints are omitted from the public evidence view. Entered text and public
source titles/URLs remain intentional content, not a general privacy guarantee.

Operational discovery compares actual source identity/revision, country/policy and
review day. Independent reads may differ in milliseconds without becoming stale;
UTC rollover still retires the action. Each row validates its own receipt so an
older neighbour cannot hide an independently valid ordinary/current cycle.

Nursery forecasts, preparation groups, member details and CSV/PDF exports retain
source evidence per member. A legacy member gets no borrowed evidence. Canonical
replay validates marked member evidence; unreadable, missing, changed, non-JSON or
invalid/duplicate members cannot silently export ordinary-looking marked quantities.
Existing all-legacy fields and calculations are retained. PDF evidence uses word
wrapping and member-labelled continuation headings; entered references retain their
existing exact-text/glyph checks.
