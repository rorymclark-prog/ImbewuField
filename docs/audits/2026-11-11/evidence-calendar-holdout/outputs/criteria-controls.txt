== Part 1. Answers that ignore the place, scored on the four criteria ==
answer given to every place          56 held-out: pass    44 development: pass
always Winter rainfall                        56 of 56                44 of 44
always Winter frost                           35 of 56                27 of 44
always Light frost at most                    31 of 56                22 of 44
always Hot area, no frost                     25 of 56                18 of 44
the app (for comparison)                      54 of 56                44 of 44

"Always Winter rainfall" passes everything: H-A and H-B only constrain the hot-area and light-frost
answers, H-C allows the winter calendar at any frost count, and H-D forbids the hard-frost calendar only.
So 54 of 56 is compliance with four one-sided frost constraints. It is not a share of places given the
right calendar, and it says nothing about sowing months, crops or yields.

== Part 2. POST HOC, not pre-registered: does the calendar agree with the rain timing of the independent record? ==
The winter-rainfall calendar is meant for places whose rain falls mainly in winter. The app decides that from
NASA POWER rain: summer half-year (October to March) share of annual rain at or below 0.35. Here the same share
is taken from the independent record's own rain, and compared.

place                    calendar               summer share, app  summer share, independent   agreement
Paarl                    Winter rainfall                     0.21                       0.22   same side of 0.35
Malmesbury               Winter rainfall                     0.21                       0.21   same side of 0.35
Hermanus                 Light frost at most                 0.39                       0.35   same side of 0.35
Clanwilliam              Winter rainfall                     0.17                       0.24   same side of 0.35
Caledon                  Winter frost                        0.38                       0.39   same side of 0.35
Calvinia                 Winter rainfall                     0.29                       0.39   DIFFERENT side of 0.35

(only places on the winter calendar, or with an independent share of 0.40 or less, or that disagree, are listed)
the app's winter / not-winter answer is on the same side as the independent record at 55 of 56 places
an 'always Winter rainfall' answer would be on the wrong side at 53 of 56 places

This check would reject the always-winter answer. It was chosen after the results were known, on places already
seen, and it uses the app's own 0.35 line, so it is a description of this run and a candidate for the NEXT
pre-registered validation. It is not evidence that the winter calendar's months are right for these places.
