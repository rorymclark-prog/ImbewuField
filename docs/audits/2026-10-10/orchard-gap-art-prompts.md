# Product artwork for research gaps

Created 11 October 2026 using the built-in ImageGen tool with transparent background. These extend the existing painted vegetable and fruit picture system. Each output was visually inspected before mechanically resizing to 128×128 RGBA PNG. Large outputs were colour-quantized without changing the composition; final assets remain under the print icon byte budget with empty transparent corners.

Illustrations represent products, not botanical identification, cultivar recommendations, suitability evidence or yield. The species catalogue and saved map identities were unchanged. Product/calendar lookup is maintained in `lib/species-art.ts`.

Nineteen assets were generated. Fifteen exact prompts were retained below. The first four prompts were not retained verbatim; their subject descriptions below are reproduction guidance, rather than a claim to recover the original text. All source output filenames are listed for local provenance; the committed deliverable is the corresponding `public/fruit-art/<id>.png`.
