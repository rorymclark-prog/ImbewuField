# Additional production artwork — 10 October 2026

Mode: built-in `image_gen`, one generation per product; no CLI/API-key workflow.

Project assets are saved under `public/` at the paths below. Original generated files remain under Codex's generated-image directory. Selected images were visually inspected, reduced to 128×128 RGBA, and compressed with alpha preserved. Each final image is at most 25 KB. Transparent-corner, visible-pixel and distinct-product checks exercise the deployed bytes and shared app/PDF URL resolver.

The following retained prompt briefs describe the common style and each requested subject. They are briefs, not a claim that these are verbatim transcripts of all earlier calls.

## Common style brief

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: a tiny transparent production-calendar illustration for South African farmers. Softly shaded natural painted food illustration matching existing crop artwork, subtle realistic textures, light from upper left, compact centered cluster filling about 80–90% of a square, clear silhouette readable at 18px. Transparent background, all corners empty; no plate, ground, cast shadow, text, border or watermark.

### Pigeon pea — public/fruit-art/cajanus-cajan.png

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: tiny transparent food-production calendar illustration for South African farmers. Subject: pigeon pea (Cajanus cajan) edible produce, two short fresh green slightly hairy pigeon-pea pods, one pod open showing several small round olive-green peas, with three small dried tan pigeon-pea seeds beside it. Match the existing softly shaded natural painted vegetable and fruit calendar illustrations: rounded modeled forms, subtle realistic texture, warm natural colors, light from upper left, clean distinct silhouette readable at 18 px. Centered compact cluster fills about 82 percent of a square image. Actual transparent background, isolated cutout with all corners empty. No tree, whole plant, bowl, plate, ground, cast shadow, lettering, labels, border or watermark. Botanical correctness: pigeon pea pods short with visible pea bulges, not long green beans. One illustration only.

### Apple — public/fruit-art/malus-domestica.png

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: tiny transparent farm production-calendar illustration. Subject: edible apple fruit (Malus domestica), one round red-green apple with natural stem and one small leaf, plus a half apple showing pale cream flesh, five-point core and dark brown pips. Match soft-shaded natural painted vegetable icons: subtle textured skin, rounded modeled forms, gentle light from upper left, no hard outlines, clean distinct silhouette legible at 18px. Centered compact cluster fills 82 percent of square image. Actual transparent background and all corners empty. No tree, whole plant, plate, basket, ground, cast shadow, lettering, labels, border or watermark. Red-green apple coloration is a generic product illustration, not an implied named cultivar or recommendation.

An illustration identifies a food. It does not establish a harvest window, climate suitability, meat dressing yield, preservation recipe or safe storage time. The source ingredient stays unchanged when a recorded stock form uses another picture. Different breeds producing the same food can share its image; meat from chicken, duck, rabbit, goat, pig, sheep and cattle uses distinct food images.
