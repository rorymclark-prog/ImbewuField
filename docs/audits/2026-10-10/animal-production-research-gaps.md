# Animal production research closure — 11 October 2026

## Result and authority

All 15 existing animal enterprises have retained source dossiers. No animal research dossier was found lost. The authority is `research/animal-sources/*.json` → `scripts/build-animal-enterprises.mjs` → `lib/animal-enterprises-data.ts`; `lib/animal-enterprises.ts` supplies context and housing choices. A mapped structure counts housing, never animals. Prior audits are [the 10 October source checks](evidence-production-reaudit/animal-source-checks.md), [4 October product audit](../2026-10-04/production-products-codex.md) and `research/manual/factcheck/05-animal-systems.md`. Older summary claims were not copied as quantitative authority.

The bounded source review added 13 separately cited production reference points to 11 existing dossiers. It corrected the Boer-goat denominator, the rabbit output stage, the Saanen parity and season-description errors, and the geographic description of the Polish duck trial. No numerical default, harvest-month list, botanical name or map geometry was changed. These are source references, not farmer production records or yield curves. Root owns generator/type and app/PDF presentation wiring.

Every enterprise was examined for output basis, onset/stage, regional season/breed evidence and the remaining farm information needed. The closure verdict is **all 15 research records reviewed; usable references retained; incompatible or absent fields explicitly withheld**. It is not a claim that 15 universal South African yield forecasts exist.

## Newly verified primary references

Exact short quotes are stored in each `productionReferences[].evidence` record. The following gives the source URL, page and applicable scope without repeating long extracts.

1. **SA rabbit grower age and carcass basis.** [North et al. 2019 original PDF](https://www.scielo.org.za/pdf/sajas/v49n5/04.pdf), printed pp816 and 819, Table 2 (PDF pp2 and5). Grower-house Stellenbosch trial, April–May 2017; New Zealand White/Phendula, balanced pellets and water freely available. Slaughter was about 11 weeks from birth. Separate overall means: live 2.62 kg, hot carcass 1.55 kg, chilled 1.48 kg, reference carcass 1.26 kg. Last removes head/red offal but includes bones. Original table visually inspected. It closes a missing SA grower reference, not extensive-doe annual output or boneless food yield.

2. **SA Saanen parity and defined seasons.** [Malemela et al. 2024 original PDF](https://www.scielo.org.za/pdf/sajas/v54n5/09.pdf), printed p622 Table 1 / p625 Table 3 (PDF pp3 /6), both visually inspected. Table 1 defines spring August–October; the former “no defined months” gap was false. Table 3 gives parity-group means for standardised 305-day lactation, with final group ≥7th rather than HTML >7th. The complete row values are retained in the dossier. Kidding-category dates do not populate milk collection months.

3. **Extensive Limpopo dairy-goat stage.** [Norris et al. 2011 original article](https://scialert.net/fulltext/?doi=ajava.2011.274.281), Methods, Results and Table 2; 179 lactating does, Putukwane near Polokwane, 1996–2010, irregular supplementary feed, 210-day observation. Daily **kg** by breed and the early/mid/late lactation trend are retained separately from stud-herd totals. March–April versus October–November findings concern kidding in this cohort. No litres conversion, inferred national calendar or merged yield range.

4. **Current commercial poultry age/strain standards.** [SAPA Industry Profile 2024](https://www.sapoultry.co.za/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/SAPA-INDUSTRY-PROFILE-2024.pdf), printed p39 Table 4 / p82 §7.9 (PDF pp46 /89). Layer standards are cumulative to 90 weeks; broiler slaughter-age average is 30–31 days. The older 2022 references stay clearly dated. Table 4 visually inspected. These sources are commercial comparisons, not smallholder kg or an annual egg default.

5. **Named indigenous chicken trial recovered.** [Grobbelaar et al. 2010 FAO-hosted original](https://www.fao.org/4/i1353t/i1353t04.pdf), methods printed pp27–28, first-egg results p29, Table 4 p31 (PDF p7, visually inspected). Two 52-week cycles at ARC Irene with commercial feed, water and controlled lighting. Age/egg means are retained in one reference point, without replacing KZN scavenging-flock evidence. First-egg age differences between breeds were not significant. This corrects the prior claim that this paper's figures could not be checked.

6. **Tilapia geographical suitability requires pond temperature.** [ARC General Aquaculture FAQ](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-api/AquacultureProductionApp/Pages/FAQs.aspx), “Can I grow tilapia on the Highveld?”: water thresholds and tunnel context retained. Summer suitability is conditional on warm water; winter limits matter across South Africa. No air-temperature-to-harvest-date conversion or grow-out guarantee was added.

7. **Pig stages and climate.** [ARC Pig Production manual](https://www.arc.agric.za/arc-iscw/CSA-Toolbox/CSA%20Pig%20Production/Manual/10-Pig%20production.pdf), printed pp442–446, Table 1 p446. Pregnant/lactating sow water ranges are distinct; piglet warmth differs from sow cooling. Source's nursery prose/table conflict was not promoted into a ration. No sow-output or meat kg default added.

8. **Warm/humid KZN dairy stock and calf competition.** [KZN DARD Subsistence Dairying](https://www.kzndard.gov.za/subsistence-dairying-in-kzn), Makhathini trials. Nguni×Jersey discussion and calf/milking-system context retained. Stated targets or potential yields are not treated as this plan's observed defaults.

9. **Government goat manual now accessible.** [DAFF 2016 Goat Training Manual](https://www.nda.gov.za/images/Branches/AgricProducHealthFoodSafety/genetic-resources/goat-training-manual-book_a4-layout_.pdf), printed p2 breed-purpose examples, pp4–5 records, p7 §2.4.6 uncontrolled breeding. Manual covers birth/weaning/weight/sale records. All-year births do not establish all-year weaned kids or meat. Buck replacement intervals are not doe lifespan.

10. **Government sheep manual breeding context.** [DAFF Sheep General Information Manual, Final Draft](https://www.nda.gov.za/images/Branches/AgricProducHealthFoodSafety/genetic-resources/sheep-training-manual.pdf), printed p12 §3.4.6 (PDF p14). Mara June–July mating/November–December lambing retained as a station breeding example only. Housing Table 1 p5 was visually inspected but cites a generic ScienceDirect domain without a traceable underlying source, so its space numbers were not adopted as local standards.

11. **KZN goat feed season context.** [KZN DARD Goat Production](https://www.kzndard.gov.za/goat-production-in-kwazulu-natal), Highland Sourveld discussion, winter nutrition. Seasonal feed shortages interact with pregnancy/kidding; this is local care guidance rather than a generic output reduction or a national calendar.

## Presentation and farm-data closure

Surface the exact `outputUnit`, source note and first-product stage alongside each animal reference in app and printed appendix. Present `productionReferences` as cited examples, never as an automatically selected farm quantity. Root owns that shared presentation wiring.

Actual/expected production records need site, product, exact year/month, amount, unit, status and provenance. A deliberate zero is evidence of no production for that period, not a missing value. Meat entry must distinguish live animal, dressed carcass and food product; fish must distinguish whole fish from processed product. Eggs are counts unless their actual mass is recorded; milk stays in the recorded source/farmer unit. These references contain no source-backed universal conversion from count, litres or liveweight into edible kg.

Site survey inputs needed before animal recommendations become specific: enterprise/purpose, actual head count by stage, breed/strain, purchase/birth date, calving/kidding dates and lactation/parity, feeding system and seasonal feed availability, clean drinking water, shelter/heat/cold/drainage, pond water temperature/heating, forage/colony management and local adviser confirmation. Reference calendar climate may screen fit; it cannot supply missing animals, lactation dates or quantities.

Source retrieval limit: the fresh UFS Boer-goat endpoint returned HTML challenge, so that already-verified quote was retained and only its unsupported annual label removed. No new UFS quantity was claimed. Source PDFs were read from temporary files; no downloaded PDFs, credentials or logs were added to the repository.
