# Production age and product evidence recovery

Checked 11 October 2026 in the production re-audit worktree. This records the evidence recovery and final 25 research workstreams, rather than claiming a complete measured yield model for every plant.

## What was missing from the app

The initial 37 dossiers contained 13 non-null first-bearing ranges, seven full-bearing ranges and eight mature kg/tree ranges. Their non-null values had not been lost in generation. The earlier generator did discard citations attached to null fields and supplementary context. A null kg/tree field could therefore hide a useful hectare result, cultivar experiment or establishment reference. Bearing-condition notes were also absent from the editor's main references.

The current audit branch adds structured `supplementaryReferences`, `productionGuidance` and `householdFoodStatus`, preserves range/pollination conditions, and displays the references in the age editor and product guide. Source dossiers remain the authority. The generated data must be refreshed after edits. The projection continues to use the farmer's entered age/quantity schedule; a research citation does not automatically create an annual production curve.

A mature-orchard hectare yield, a single-season cultivar result, a commercial budget, wet nuts in shell and edible kernel kg answer different questions. They are retained in their own units and context. An intervening age value is not inferred from two distant observations. No tree density is chosen merely to make a per-tree number possible.

## How the plan should use this evidence

For a mapped planting, record the exact product, count or growing area, actual planting date/starting stock, applicable climate/cultivar and observed or checked expected weights. Use entered age benchmarks only where they apply to that cohort. Preserve a recorded zero separately from missing quantity. Tree establishment, repeatedly cut leaves, annual ice plant, seasonal tubers, pond flowers and tea cutting cycles need their actual production basis.

For processed food, record fresh inputs and recovered stored outputs separately. These sources do not establish household safety processes, drying conversion factors or storage durations for every product. Wet macadamia nuts, dried kernels, fresh ginger, dried spice and tea shoot inputs must not be added as if they were duplicate edible output.

The site report can retain regional nursery/cultivar candidates and research references. The production calendar should show the chosen compatible reference, explicit provisional assumptions, locally recorded picking and future age schedule. A held food-use verdict must remain visible in map inventory while being excluded from ordinary food availability and kg totals, even if a picking month is entered.

## Validation and remaining limits

The nine corrected existing dossiers and final 25 new dossiers parse as JSON. Peer source review checked fruit stage, food-use holds, Coleus/Basella crop-cycle evidence and ginger product/unit conditions; two fruit-appearance intervals were removed from harvest arrays. At final-25 write time the shared research directory contains 100 unique dossiers, covering the initial 37 plus the 13 major additions and both 25-workstream batches. `git diff --check` passed. Parent owns regeneration, integration tests and final app/PDF checks; this report does not claim those checks for ungenerated sources.

Remaining gaps are primarily measured age-by-age yields for the actual site, cultivar, rootstock and production system, plus local harvest records for many indigenous/leaf/tea products. The research verdict for each workstream is complete; unsupported kg, invented planting densities or guessed months have not been substituted for the missing measurements.
