# Kenya cowpea conditions in the provisional preview

Base: ad2cdefcf7126ece61c54f3ba7a9635fea1d7e74 (draft PR1344).

The grower can now select leaf production and a declared growth habit before
choosing the WorldVeg Kenya direct-sowing reference. Spreading varieties show
50–75 cm between rows and plants; erect/semi-erect varieties show 50 cm between
rows and 15–25 cm between plants. These are the original ranges, with no selected
point, reciprocal density or asserted final stand. The source's 2–3 seeds per
hill is explicitly distinguished from surviving plants. Density, yield and dates
remain unknown unless supported separately. An independently entered density is
still identified as the grower's estimate. The selected conditions and source
limits accompany the calculation and printable export.

The original manual is Publication 24-1077, June 2024 (day unknown), World
Vegetable Center, Kenya Country Office, printed pp21–22 / PDF pages25–26:
https://worldveg.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/24-1077_Agroecology-Kenya_compressed-82.pdf
