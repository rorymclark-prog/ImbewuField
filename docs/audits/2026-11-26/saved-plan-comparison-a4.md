Rendered Whole Design and Planting through the exact map compositor, then placed them on landscape A4 with 5 mm page margins. Inspected rasterised PDF pages before and after the changes. Whole Design retains the ditch and bank marks, dotted drip lines and overlapping crowns across the lower plots. The proof is a digital print check. No physical printer was used.
