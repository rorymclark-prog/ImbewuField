## What was already done

The 16 October audit left 160 open findings. They were split into seven tracks by file
ownership (the audit's "Swarm plan" section) and implemented in parallel on
`swarm/w13-*` branches, opened as draft PRs #1014–#1020. The integrator merged all
seven into `claude/zen-rubin-00ndhb` in that order, resolved the conflicts
(`lib/i18n.tsx`, `lib/locales/zu.ts`, `components/TabBar.tsx`, `app/records/page.tsx`,
`app/student/page.tsx` and the shared theme CSS) and then:

- **Study history layer for the dictionary changes.** The dated Study proofs pin
  `lib/i18n.tsx`, `lib/locales/zu.ts` and `app/student/page.tsx` byte-exact and require
  every pre-wave-11 dictionary line to survive in order. Wave 13 reworded and deleted
  lines, so a new dated layer `tests/wave-13-theme-history-checks.ts` (with the
  pre-wave-13 fixtures under `tests/fixtures/*-before-wave-13.*`) records the exact
  rewordings and removals, re-pins the live files and projects them back to their
  pre-wave-13 bytes for every older owner (`slides-webp` → `blind-reviewed` →
  `study-ui-due-unlock` → `study-remaining-controls-next`). The copy-12 rename inside
  `lib/i18n-pending.ts` (`mentorRoleNgo`) spreads into the Tshivenda dictionary, so
  `rewindWave13PendingDictionary` in `tests/study-ui-due-unlock-history-checks.ts`
  rewinds that one key too.
- **Carry-forward edits asked for in PR bodies:** the records CTA now reads
  'Try My Records' (copy-04); the journal page h1 is 'Field journal' / 'Ijenali
  Yasensimu' and the NGO dashboard back link is 'All gardens' / 'Zonke izingadi'
  (copy-06, copy-07).
