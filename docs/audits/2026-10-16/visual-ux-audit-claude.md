### The picture in one paragraph

Earth light on a phone is in good shape: 51 of 51 farmer routes render, nothing
crashes, and the core Home → Map → Records → Prices loop looks like one product. The
app stops looking professional in four places: (1) **dark theme** is only half
applied — the header and tab bar go dark while staff dashboards, the study area, the
network/atlas maps, partners, design pickers, updates, example and the records/exchange
controls stay light or become unreadable; (2) the **Lima launcher** and the **tour
pill** are fixed over content on every page without a bottom bar, covering the last
button or the primary CTA; (3) **desktop** is a stretched phone — no shared header, the
phone tab bar across 1280px, 448px columns on /vision and /example, dashboards that end
with 650–1700px of empty paper, and 70 sample invoices rendered inline; (4) a handful of
**first-contact states**: Account is a blank sprout in sample mode, maps have no
"cannot open on this phone" state, and the sign-in / contact / surveys / feedback screens
talk about backends and Firebase.
