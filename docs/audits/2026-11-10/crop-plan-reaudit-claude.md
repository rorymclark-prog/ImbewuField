## How to read the findings

Each row says what kind of finding it is:

- **WCAG**: a measured failure of a WCAG 2.2 AA criterion, named in the row.
- **House rule**: breaks a rule this repo already enforces elsewhere (44px taps, the 12px farmer type floor, theme tokens, dialog focus, one h1 per page).
- **Defect**: visibly wrong on screen; reproducible.
- **Decision**: released on purpose; the question is whether Rory wants it this way.
- **Proposal**: an improvement, not a fault.

Evidence is in [`evidence-crop-plan-reaudit/`](evidence-crop-plan-reaudit/): one complete JSON file per view in `measurements/`, 17 screenshots, the four scripts that produce them and a [README](evidence-crop-plan-reaudit/README.md) with the exact commands and limits. Contrast failures are counted from declared styles. The crop-bar ratios were also read from screenshot pixels on the labels the script could sample; a row says so where the two differ. The pixel reading is a spot check with stated limits, not a second complete count, and a label that could not be sampled is not counted as confirmed.

### Proposals

Each of these simplifies how something is shown. None may change what a figure means, and the rows say what has to stay distinct.

| ID | Improvement | Status | Evidence and next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| CP-068 | A weekly target tells the grower what matters in the hardest way: uncovered months as a comma list of up to twelve, and labels such as "Unallocated volume" and "Benchmark before allowance". | proposal | Show the target's months as twelve cells and put its figures in plain words, from what the panel already computes. Three things must stay apart. Area: "crop-cycle area needed" is summed across cycles and repeated sowings reuse ground, so it is not comparable with mapped bed area (44 m² of beds on the sample) or with area actually free in a given month, and no shortfall may be derived by subtracting one from another. Months: a month not yet assessed, a planning gap and a month with an observed harvest are different states and need different marks.
