## What was already done

A great deal, and most of it holds. The 6 August audit tied yields, durations and spacing to KZN Department of Agriculture tables and removed invented figures. The 27 September work made the calendar choice "frost first, rain second" and added a test that no frost-tender crop in the hard-frost calendar stands in the field from May to August. The September dossiers record a quote, URL and page for each newer crop.

What had not been done is test the result against real places. Every earlier check asks whether the code agrees with itself or with one province's table. None asks whether a farmer in Komatipoort, Worcester or Durban is shown months that match their own climate and their own region's published advice. That is what this audit does.

## What is right

These were checked and stand:

- **Arithmetic.** A 5 kg/week target for 365 days is 5 × 365 ÷ 7 = 260.7 kg; at the carrot benchmark of 2.0 kg/m² that is 130.4 m² of crop-cycle area; four 6 m² beds give 48 kg. The app shows exactly these. Yield is benchmark × bed area × share, with no hidden multiplier (`lib/crop-plan.ts:1813`, `lib/harvest-targets.ts:35–127`).
- **Frost.** By the app's own exposure rule, 42 of 44 South African sites have no frost-tender crop standing in a month that averages one air-frost day or more. No site on the light-frost, frost-free or Western Cape calendar is flagged at all.
- **The light-frost calendar** follows the KZN warm-area column: 23 of 24 comparable rows have no month outside the source, and 8 are narrower than it.
- **The interior.** On the Highveld, Middleveld, KZN Midlands and Karoo the app's sowing months agree with the published regional guide for 70–79% of the months it offers.
- **Yields are conservative**, as intended: carrots 20 t/ha against 30–40 for open-pollinated varieties and beetroot 14 t/ha against 15–25 in Starke Ayres' guidelines.
- **Outside South Africa** the app computes no catalogue calendar, date, yield or task unless the grower confirms South Africa as the country (`lib/international/site-context.ts:150`). There is nothing to test across the globe because nothing is offered there, by design.

## Findings and disposition

How the calendar is chosen, for reference: the site's coordinates go to NASA POWER for monthly mean temperature and rain. `rainPatternFor` (`lib/koppen-global.ts:263`) then picks one of four calendars: `summer` (hard frost, the interior), `mild-frost` (KZN "warm areas, light frost"), `all-year` (frost-free) or `winter` (Western Cape). Each crop has a list of sowing months per calendar (`lib/crop-catalog.ts`).

The regional guide used as an independent yardstick is the "General sowing guide (according to SANSOR)", eight regional columns, 27 of this catalogue's crops. It is a seed-industry table reproduced by a nursery, dated 2004: a secondary source, used here to compare, not to correct.

## Verification

- Location harness: the app's own `fetchNasaPower`, `siteClimateFromLocationData`, `classifyKoppen`, `rainPatternFor`, `harvestMonthForCrop` and the heat-check functions, run for 62 locations with no failed fetch. Independent record: ERA5 daily minimum, maximum and rain, 2001–2020, through Open-Meteo.
- Reproduction: [`evidence-crop-plan-accuracy/README.md`](evidence-crop-plan-accuracy/README.md) gives every command. The scripts, their outputs and the catalogue snapshot are committed; the 12 MB per-crop file and the downloaded PDFs are not.
- Typecheck and tests: not relevant to this record; nothing executable changed.
- Limits to keep in mind:
  - ERA5 is a gridded reanalysis. It under-counts frost in valley bottoms, so every frost figure is a lower bound.
  - The SANSOR guide is a secondary, seed-industry source from 2004. Which of its regions a site belongs to is my assignment; two ambiguous sites were left out and six are judgement calls (listed in the README).
  - Agreement percentages count months, so a wide window and a narrow one weigh differently. They compare regions well and should not be read as a pass mark.
  - The KZN table rows are transcriptions made by earlier research passes, not read from the document by me (CP-083).
  - No farm was visited and no agronomist has reviewed this. A grower's own experience outranks all of it.

This record changes no picture and no farming figure. `PLAN_VERSION` is untouched.
