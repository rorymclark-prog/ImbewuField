## Evidence behind the two new safety findings

### Weather wording

The actual production farmer screen displayed the reference value and watering instruction together. A screenshot was visually inspected after scrolling the overview panel; this record does not substitute a source-only claim for a visual check. The observation is of the public sample, not a measured irrigation requirement at a private farm.

[FAO's reference/crop evapotranspiration definitions](https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e04.htm) distinguish weather-only ETo from crop water use and irrigation requirements. [FAO's crop-coefficient method](https://www.fao.org/4/X0490E/x0490e0b.htm) further describes the required crop relationship. The app currently bypasses that distinction in its wording. This audit prescribes no irrigation amount or coefficient.

### Dependency maintenance

[Next.js's support policy](https://nextjs.org/support-policy) lists 14.x as unsupported. [Its official December 2025 security update](https://nextjs.org/blog/security-update-2025-12-11) documents an App Router denial-of-service problem affecting the app's release line and a later patch. That historical patch is evidence that 14.2.18 is behind security fixes, not a claim that the December patch is sufficient for all October 2026 advisories.

The registry report includes condition-dependent advisories for platforms/features this deployment may not use. No attack was sent to production, no data exposure was demonstrated, and no package or lockfile was changed. Triage the actual reachable paths while updating to supported releases.
