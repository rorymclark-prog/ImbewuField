# Izimbewu neLungelo kuZimbewu (Seeds and Seed Sovereignty) — Narration Script (isiZulu)

**Kuhloselwe:** Injini ye-Text-to-Speech noma Umhlanganisi (Facilitator)
**Amakhasi (Deck):** ImbewuField_Seeds_Facilitator_Deck.pptx (Amakhasi alishumi - 10 slides)
**Ulimi:** isiZulu esilula nesijwayelekile sabalimi

---

**Ikhasi 1 — Isihloko (Slide 1 — Title)**

Siyakwamukela. Le ngxenye ikhuluma ngeZimbewu neLungelo lakho legalo ekubeni neZimbewu Zakho. Ingenye yezifundo ezilishumi eziyingxenye yaMabhuku e-ImbewuField. Eminizini embalwa ezayo, sizoxoxa ngezinto ezintathu eziqakathekileyo. Kungani ukugcina imbewu kubalulekile. Ungakwenza kanjani lokho ngokuyikho. Siphinde sibheke ukuthi uyigcina kanjani nokuthi uyabelana kanjani nabanye ngale mbewu oyigcinile.

---

**Ikhasi 2 — Izinhlobo Ezimbili Zembewu (Slide 2 — Two Kinds of Seed)**

Ake siqale ngomcabango obaluleke ukudlula yonke kule ngxenye. Kunezinhlobo ezimbili zembewu.

Imbewu evulelekile ngokwemvelo. Lokho kusho ukuthi uma uyigcina uze uyitshala ngenkathi elandelayo, uthola isitshalo esifanayo kahle hle naleso obusivunile.

Imbewu ehlanganisiwe — sthandwa ukubizwa ngokuthi i-F1 — yona yehlukile. Ihlangene kanye nje phakathi kwezitshalo ezimbili ezahlukene. Uma ugcina imbewu ye-hybrid uze uyitshala, awusoze watshala isitshalo esifanayo. Uthola okuthile ongakwazi nokuthi kuyoba yini. Kanti imaningi amathuba ukuthi isivuno sibe sincane kakhulu.

Lokhu akwenzeki ngengozi. Yingakho abalimi bephoqeleka ukuba bathenge imbewu entsha esitolo zonke izikhathi ngezikhathi zesitshalo, uma betshala i-hybrid kuphela.

[pause]

Ilungelo lakho legalo ekubeni nezimbewu kusho into elula kakhulu: inkululeko yokutshala, yokugcina, nengxenye yokwabelana ngembewu yakho. Ungekho ngaphansi kwesitolo zonke izikhathi ngezikhathi ngenxa yento ongazilimela yona ngokwakho.

---

**Ikhasi 3 — Ukukhetha Isitshalo Esifanele Ukugcina Imbewu Kuso (Slide 3 — Choosing Which Plant to Save From)**

Kulokhu kutshala kwaleli hlobo, khetha isitshalo esisodwa, uzibophezele ukugcina imbewu esitshalweni sakho esihle kakhulu.

Uma ukhetha isitshalo oyothatha kuso imbewu, ungavele uthathe kuleso esithele kakhulu kuphela. Bheka isitshalo esiphilile kunazo zonke. Esingenasifo. Esibumbeke kahle. Esashes sacwile saba sivuthiwe ngaphambi kweminye. Yiso leso sitshalo esinezimpawu ezinhle ofuna ukuya nazo enkathini ezayo yokutshala.

---

**Ikhasi 4 — Izindlela Ezimbili Zokugcina Imbewu (Slide 4 — Two Ways to Save Seed)**

Kunezindlela ezimbili yokugcina imbewu, kanti indlela oyisebenzisayo iyahambisana nohlobo lwesitshalo.

Indlela eyomisiwe ilula kakhulu. Izimbotyi, ophizi, ummbila, nezinqubo zojobe — uvele uyeke imbewu yome alukho uhlobo lwesitshalo, ubesuyayibutha uyigcine. Ayinazinhlupho, ilula kakhulu.

Indlela eswakamile yenzelwe izitshalo ezifana namatamatisi, amakhukhamba, namaphuzi. Lapha imbewu izungezwe inyama yesithelo, kanti le nyama iyayivimbela imbewu ukuba ingamili ngokushesha kakhulu. Ngakho-ke kufanele uqale uyisuse le nyama yesithelo.

---

**Ikhasi 5 — Indlela Yamatamatisi Yekubilisa (Slide 5 — The Tomato Fermentation Method)**

Nanku impela ukuthi le ndlela eswakamile isebenza kanjani, sisebenzisa itamatisi njengesibonelo ngoba yiyo elula kakhulu ukuqala ngayo.

Khipha imbewu nenyama yesithelo uyibeke embizeni noma ejajeni enamanzana amancane. Iyeke ihlale lapho izinsuku ezimbili noma ezithathu. Uzobona uhlobo olumhlophe lwamakhaza noma ukhula oluncane phezulu — kwavamile lokho, yinto esiyifunayo impela.

Emuva kalokho, hlula kahle imbewu ngamanzi, uyiyeke yome kahle impela ngaphambi kokuba uyigcine.

Zama lokhu ngokwakho kulokhu kutshala. Yingxenye elula kakhulu yokuqala ngale ndlela eswakamile.

---

**Ikhasi 6 — Ukugcina Uhlobo Lwembewu Luhlanzekile (Slide 6 — Keeping a Variety Pure)**

Kukhona nenye into eyodwa ngaphambi kokuba ugcine imbewu ezinhlobeni ezingaphezu kweyodwa. Kufanele ucabange ngebanga lokuhlukanisa — ukuthi izinhlobo ezahlukene kufanele zibe kude kangakanani ukuba zingavubani.

Amatamatisi ayazivundisa ngokwawo amaningi awo. Awadingi ibanga elikhulu.

Ummbila wona wehlukile kakhulu. Ummbila uvundiswa ngumoya, kanti umoya uhambisa impova kude kakhulu. Uma utshala izinhlobo ezingaphezu kweyodwa zombila endaweni encane, udinga ibanga elide phakathi kwazo, noma kuze kufanele uzivundise ngesandla.

---

**Ikhasi 7 — Izitha Ezintathu Zembewu Egcinwe Enqolobane (Slide 7 — The Three Enemies of Stored Seed)**

Uma imbewu yakho isiyomile, indlela oyigcina ngayo yiyo enqumayo ukuthi izobe isaphila yini ngenkathi ezayo yokutshala.

Kunezitha ezintathu zembewu egciniwe: ukushisa, ukukhanya, neswakamisa. Gcina lezi zinto ezintathu ziphansi, kanti imbewu yakho ingahlala iminyaka eminingi.

Sebenzisa izimvilophu zamaphepha, ungalokothi usebenzise i-plastic. Zigcine endaweni enomthunzi, epholile, neyomile — isitsha esivaliwe kahle esinelayisi elincane noma impuphu yobisi eyomile ngaphakathi kwaso kuyasiza ukudonsa iswakamisa esisalile.

Futhi njalo bhalela phezu kweyodwa nento ngayinye imvilophu: isitshalo, uhlobo lwaso, nosuku oyigcine ngalo.

---

**Ikhasi 8 — Yihlolisise Ngaphambi Kokuba Uyitshale (Slide 8 — Test It Before You Plant)**

Ngaphambi kokuba kuqale inkathi entsha yokutshala, hlola ingxenye encane yembewu yakho oyigcinile ukubona ukuthi isamila yini.

Imbewu ingaphelelwa amandla ngokuthula ngenkathi ihlezi endaweni yokugcina, kanti into ongayifuni neze ukutshala imbewu yayo yonke inkathi kanti isifile kakade.

Ukuhlola okusheshayo manje kukusindisa yonke leyo nkathi yokutshala.

---

**Ikhasi 9 — Ukushintshisana Ngembewu (Slide 9 — Seed Swaps)**

Nansi into efanele ukwenziwa kulokhu kutshala: hlelaniphi ukushintshisana ngembewu komakhelwane bakho.

Uma imizimzi eyisihlanu ngayinye isebenzela ukugcina izinhlobo ezimbili noma ezithathu ngokufaneleyo, kunokuba umuzi ngamunye uzame ukugcina yonke into, iqoqo lonke ligcina selinezinhlobo eziningi kakhulu, nembewu engcono kakhulu kunokuba umuzi owodwa ubungakwazi ukukwenza wedwa.

Lokho umuzi owodwa okwazile ukukugcina kahle, wonke umuntu angabelana ngakho.

---

**Ikhasi 10 — Ukubuyekeza (Slide 10 — Recap)**

Ake sibuyekeze.

Okokuqala: imbewu evulelekile ngokwemvelo iyazala ifane nesitshalo esidala, imbewu ye-hybrid ayikwenzi lokho — yingakho ilungelo lakho legalo ekubeni nezimbewu liqakathekile.

Okwesibili: sebenzisa indlela eyomisiwe ezimbotyini nommbila, indlela eswakamile ematamatisini namaphuzi, uqaphele namabanga okuhlukanisa.

Okwesithathu: gcina imbewu endaweni epholile, emnyama, neyomile, yihlolisise ngaphambi kokuba uyitshale, ubelane nabanye ngalokho okugcinile.

Yiyona yonke-ke le ngxenye. Khetha isitshalo esisodwa kulokhu kutshala, ube usuqala-ke.
