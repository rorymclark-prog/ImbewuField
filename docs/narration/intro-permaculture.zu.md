**Ikhasi 1 — Isingeniso se-Permaculture (Slide 1 — Introduction to Permaculture)**

Ngaphambi kokumba noma yini, kuyasiza ukwazi ukuthi izinqumo zenziwa kanjani.

Le module iyisisekelo sakho konke okunye esifundweni. Iqukethe ama-ethics amathathu, ama-design principles, nendlela elula yokuhlela umhlaba wakho.

Akudingi mali. Kodwa kushintsha lapho ubeka khona izinto, nokuthi uhamba ibanga elingakanani nsuku zonke.

[pause]

Bheka indawo yakho. Iyiphi ingxenye oyivakashela kakhulu?

---

**Ikhasi 2 — Kungani Lokhu Kubalulekile (Slide 2 — Why This Matters)**

I-design enhle yonga umsebenzi ngaphambi kokuba uphakamise ifosholo.

Umsebenzi omningi epulazini elincane awukho ekutshaleni noma ekuvuneni kuphela. Usekuhambeni, ekuthwaleni, nasekulungiseni izinto ezibekwe endaweni engafanele.

I-Permaculture ikufundisa ukubeka izinto lapho umhlaba usebenza nawe, hhayi ngokumelene nawe.

[pause]

Cabanga ngomsebenzi owodwa epulazini lakho othatha isikhathi eside kunalokho okufanele. Isikhathi sihamba kuphi ngempela?

---

**Ikhasi 3 — Imiphumela Yokufunda (Slide 3 — Learning Outcomes)**

Ekupheleni kwale module uzokwazi ukwenza izinto ezintathu.

Usho ama-ethics amathathu, futhi uwasebenzise ekwenzeni isinqumo esingenayo impendulo ecacile.

Usho ama-design principles abaluleke kakhulu emhlabeni wakho, uchaze nokuthi kungani ebalulekile.

[pause]

Uzokwazi nokudweba ama-zones nama-sectors akho ephepheni elilodwa. Lowo mdwebo uyisisekelo sawo wonke amapulani ozowenza emva kwalokhu.

---

**Ikhasi 4 — Ukunakekela Umhlaba (Slide 4 — Earth Care)**

I-ethic yokuqala yi-Earth Care.

Isho ukuphatha umhlabathi, amanzi, izitshalo nezilwane njengezinhlelo eziphilayo okufanele zivikelwe, hhayi izinto ozisebenzisa uze uziqede.

Uhlelo oluqedwa ukusetshenziswa luyayeka ukukusiza. Uhlelo oluvikelwayo luyaqhubeka nokukusiza.

[pause]

Bheka indawo eyodwa yomhlabathi ongenalutho. Kukhona yini okuyivikelayo manje?

---

**Ikhasi 5 — Ukunakekela Abantu (Slide 5 — People Care)**

I-ethic yesibili yi-People Care.

Izidingo zomndeni wakho ziza kuqala, bese kulandela ezomphakathi wakho.

Umlimi othengisa wonke amaqanda nayo yonke imifino, angashiyi lutho etafuleni lomndeni, uyeqa i-People Care.

Ipulazi kufanele londle umndeni olisebenzayo, hhayi imakethe kuphela.

[pause]

Yini ephuma emhlabeni wakho kodwa umndeni wakho ungayidli?

---

**Ikhasi 6 — Ukwabelana Ngokulinganayo (Slide 6 — Fair Share)**

I-ethic yesithathu yi-Fair Share.

Thatha okudingayo kuphela, bese ubuyisela okusele ohlelweni — imbewu, ukudla, amanzi nolwazi.

Umlimi othengisa wonke ama-mielies asele, angashiyi lutho lwe-compost noma lokugcina imbewu, uyaphula le-ethic. Akukho okubuyela ohlelweni.

Umphakathi ovala umthombo wamanzi osetshenziswa yiwo wonke umuntu nawo uyayiphula.

[pause]

Umhlaba wakho ubuyelani kuwe ngemuva kwesizini ngayinye?

---

**Ikhasi 7 — Bheka: Isinqumo Esisodwa, Ama-Ethics Amathathu (Slide 7 — Watch: One Decision, Three Ethics)**

I-borehole ikhiqiza amanzi amaningi kunalawo adingwa umndeni wakho.

Yabelana ngamanzi nomakhelwane, kodwa uqaphe i-water table. Ukwabelana kusebenzela i-People Care ne-Fair Share; ukuqapha kuvikela i-Earth Care.

---

**Ikhasi 8 — Lapho Kungekho Rulebook (Slide 8 — When There Is No Rulebook)**

Yilapho ama-ethics esebenza khona ngempela.

Umakhelwane ucela ukwelusela izinkomo emva kwesomiso. Isikhukhula silimaza ama-swales akho. Akekho obhale impendulo.

Ama-ethics akusiza wenze isinqumo ngaphandle kokulinda imvume, futhi uchaze kamuva ukuthi kungani wenze kanjalo.

[pause]

Yakha la ma-ethics amathathu endleleni ocabanga ngayo ngaphambi kokwakha noma yini emhlabeni.

---

**Ikhasi 9 — Izimiso Eziyishumi Nambili (Slide 9 — Twelve Principles)**

UBill Mollison noDavid Holmgren bahlanganisa i-permaculture yaba ama-design principles ayishumi nambili.

Awudingi wonke ukuze uqale. Amathathu enza umsebenzi omningi epulazini elincane laseNingizimu Afrika.

Observe and interact. Catch and store energy. Use edges and value the marginal.

[pause]

Sizothatha la mathathu ngamunye ngamunye.

---

**Ikhasi 10 — Bheka Bese Uxhumana Nomhlaba Wakho (Slide 10 — Observe and Interact)**

Bheka umhlaba wakho isikhathi sonyaka esigcwele ngaphambi kokuzibophezela kuma-earthworks amakhulu.

Isiphepho esisodwa sikukhombisa umzuzu owodwa. Isizini yemvula egcwele ikukhombisa iphethini — lapho amanzi egeleza khona, lapho ehlala khona, nalapho engafiki khona.

Lelo phethini yilokho i-swale okufanele ihambisane nakho. Uma umba ungakaqondi, uhambisa umhlabathi omningi uwuyise endaweni engafanele.

[pause]

Amanzi aphuma ngakuphi emhlabeni wakho? Uyazi ngempela, noma uyaqagela?

---

**Ikhasi 11 — Bamba Ugcine Amandla (Slide 11 — Catch and Store Energy)**

Vuna imvula, ilanga ne-biomass ngaphambi kokuba kuphume emhlabeni wakho.

Konke lokhu kufika mahhala futhi kuhamba mahhala. Amanzi ayageleza, amaqabunga aphephukele kude, ilanga liwele emhlabathini ongenalutho.

Ukukubamba nokukugcina cishe kushibhile njalo kunokuthenga into efanayo kamuva.

[pause]

Yisho into eyodwa efika emhlabeni wakho mahhala kodwa iphinde ihambe ingasetshenziswanga.

---

**Ikhasi 12 — Sebenzisa Imiphetho Nezindawo Eziseceleni (Slide 12 — Use Edges and Value the Marginal)**

I-edge yilapho kuhlangana khona izinto ezimbili — ulayini wocingo, unqenqema lomfula, noma umugqa oseduze kwendlela.

Ama-edges avame ukuba umhlaba okhiqiza kakhulu, futhi imvamisa yiwo umhlaba ongakaze uhlelwe muntu.

Bheka lokho osekuvele kukhula kahle eceleni komngcele wakho. Umhlaba wakho ukutshela okuthile.

[pause]

Iyiphi i-edge epulazini lakho engasebenzi lutho okwamanje?

---

**Ikhasi 13 — Bheka: I-Diversity Ivikela Osukwini Olubi (Slide 13 — Watch: Diversity Against One Bad Day)**

Insimu eyodwa yama-mielies ingabhujiswa yisichotho esisodwa; ukutshala okuxubile akuvamile ukubhujiswa kanjalo.

Sebenzisa i-diversity, ungakhiqizi i-waste, futhi sebenzisa izixazululo ezincane nezihamba kancane: ama-scraps abe yi-compost, ibhakede linisele ngaphandle kukagesi.

---

**Ikhasi 14 — Hlanganisa, Ungahlukanisi (Slide 14 — Integrate Rather Than Segregate)**

Beka into ngayinye lapho isebenzela khona izinto ezingomakhelwane bayo.

Ingadi, izihlahla zezithelo ne-chicken run ehlelwe ngendlela yokuthi izinkukhu zijikeleze emibhedeni emva kokuvuna, lokho ukuhlanganisa.

Izinkukhu ziqeda izinambuzane futhi zengeza ukuvunda, esikhundleni sokuhlala zodwa esibayeni esinganyakazi.

[pause]

Khetha ama-principles amabili noma amathathu ahambisana nenkinga yakho enkulu, uwasebenzise ngokuqinile. Amanye azocaca njengoba uqhubeka.

---

**Ikhasi 15 — Ama-Zones: Ukuhlela Ngokuvakasha (Slide 15 — Zones: Organising by How Often You Visit)**

Ama-zones asuka ku-0 aye ku-5, futhi akhuluma ngezinyawo zakho, hhayi ngezicingo zakho.

I-Zone 0 yindlu. I-Zone 1, eseduze komnyango wasekhishini, iphethe lokho okukha nsuku zonke — ama-herbs namasaladi aluhlaza.

I-Zone 2 yingadi enkulu ne-chicken run, evakashelwa kanye noma kabili ngosuku. I-Zone 3 yinsimu enkulu, evakashelwa masonto onke.

[pause]

I-Zone 4 i-semi-wild — izihlahla zezithelo ne-fodder edinga ukunakekelwa ngezikhathi ezithile. I-Zone 5 ishiywa iyindle.

---

**Ikhasi 16 — Kungani I-Zone 1 Ingenakuxoxwa (Slide 16 — Why Zone 1 Is Not Negotiable)**

Ama-herbs adinga ukukhiwa nsuku zonke ukuze aqhubeke nokukhiqiza.

Uma uwatshala ku-Zone 3, ensimini enkulu, ukuhamba kuba kude kangangokuthi ukuvakasha kwansuku zonke kuyayeka. Ama-herbs ayashesha ukuya embewini, noma avele anganakekelwa.

Akukho okungalungile ngesitshalo noma ngomhlabathi. Isitshalo sibekwe lapho izinyawo zakho zingayi khona.

[pause]

Yini etshalwe kude nomnyango wakho kunalokho ekufanele?

---

**Ikhasi 17 — Ama-Zones Ahlela Umsebenzi Wakho (Slide 17 — Zones Plan Your Labour)**

Yilona iphuzu lalo msebenzi wonke.

Ama-zones awahleleli isikhala kuphela. Ahlela umzamo — ukuhamba, ukuthwala nokuhlola okugcwalisa usuku lwangempela.

Into ekudinga kaningi ihlala eduze kwakho. Lo mthetho owodwa usika amahora omsebenzi phakathi nesizini.

[pause]

Yima emnyango wasekhishini. Yini ongayifinyelela ngaphandle kokucabanga?

---

**Ikhasi 18 — Ama-Sectors: Amandla Afika Ngaphandle (Slide 18 — Sectors: The Energies Arriving From Outside)**

Ama-zones avela ngaphakathi komhlaba wakho. Ama-sectors avela ngaphandle kwawo.

Ilanga, umoya, imvula, isikhukhula nomlilo kufika kunqamule umngcele wakho, noma ungakuhlelelanga.

Ipulazi lase-Lowveld elibheke enyakatho-ntshonalanga lithola imimoya eshisayo neyomile ye-berg ngo-August. Ipulazi laKwaZulu-Natal line-sector yemvula yasehlobo evela enyakatho-mpumalanga.

[pause]

Isimo sezulu esikulimazayo sivela ngakuphi?

---

**Ikhasi 19 — Bheka: I-Windbreak Iba Ohlangothini Lomoya (Slide 19 — Watch: A Windbreak Belongs On The Wind Side)**

E-Highveld, umoya oshisayo nowomile ovela enyakatho-ntshonalanga ushaya ngo-August.

I-windbreak iba emngceleni osenyakatho-ntshonalanga, phakathi komoya nezitshalo.

**Ikhasi 20 — Yidwebe, Usunayo I-Design (Slide 20 — Sketch It And You Have A Design)**

Dweba umngcele womhlaba wakho. Maka indlu. Dweba ama-rings aphumele ngaphandle ngokuthi uvakashela kangaki.

Bese udweba imicibisholo engena ivela ngaphandle, emele ilanga, umoya, umlilo namanzi.

Lelo phepha liyisisekelo se-design yakho. Konke okunye okukulesi sifundo kuzokwakhela phezu kwalo.

[pause]

Akudingeki licoceke. Kudingeka libe yiqiniso.

---

**Ikhasi 21 — Umsebenzi Wasepulazini (Slide 21 — Field Assignment)**

Dweba ama-zones nama-sectors akho ephepheni elilodwa.

Maka i-Zone 0 ne-Zone 1 kuqala, bese uphumela ngaphandle kuze kufike lapho umhlaba wakho uphela khona.

Faka umcibisholo wamandla ngayinye evela ngaphandle, bese ubhala ukuthi ivela ngakuphi.

Thatha isithombe salo mdwebo ukuze ukwazi ukuwusebenzisa lapho uhlela khona.

---

**Ikhasi 22 — Isenzo Sasepulazini (Slide 22 — Field Action)**

Phuma uyohlola ukuthi umdwebo wakho uyahambisana yini nomhlaba wangempela.

Yima emnyango wasekhishini, ubale lokho okuse-Zone 1 namuhla. Bheka ukuthi kukhona yini okudinga ukuvakashelwa kaningi kodwa kutshalwe kude.

Thola into etshalwe kude kunendlela oyisebenzisa ngayo, bese ubhala ukuthi kufanele ithuthelwe kuphi.

Buza umakhelwane omdala ukuthi umoya omubi kakhulu uvela ngakuphi, uqhathanise impendulo nomcibisholo wakho.
