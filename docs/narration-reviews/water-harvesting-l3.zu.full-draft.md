## Sources and safety holds

- English lesson: `lib/course-modules.ts`, `water-harvesting-l3`; narration: `docs/narration/water-harvesting.en.md`, slides 14–18.
- Existing isiZulu narration is stale. Alignment issues are in `docs/narration-reviews/water-harvesting-l3.zu.review.md`; do not reuse its fixed roof yields, example tank sizes, first-flush volumes, or claims untreated water is safe.
- Roof yield depends on roof area, local rainfall and losses. Check whether roof material is suitable before connecting a tank; use local rainfall records and account for water missing gutters, diverted or overflowing. Annual total does not predict dry-spell availability.
- A first-flush diverter keeps **some** early runoff out. Required diversion depends on roof/system; follow supplier sizing and maintenance; there is no single volume for every roof. A diverter does not establish that the remaining water is safe to drink.
- Tank size depends on demand, rain, roof area and dry-period length. Estimate intended uses from local records and compare supply through seasons. A province name alone cannot set tank size.
- Keep tank covered, screen openings, maintain roof/gutters/diverter, and keep harvested rainwater separate from drinking-water pipes. Clear-looking water may contain germs/chemicals. Local health authority should advise on testing/treatment for intended use. A basic filter alone does not guarantee drinking safety; water used on food crops needs a safety assessment.
- WHO rainwater collection guidance supports appropriate contact-safe materials, roof/system-suited first-flush diversion, covered storage, and the possibility of contamination. It does not make first-flush diversion a potable-water guarantee. [WHO rainwater collection technical fact sheet](https://www.who.int/docs/default-source/wash-documents/sanitary-inspection-packages/2-tfs-rainwater-collection-storage-d.pdf), [WHO sanitary inspection package](https://www.who.int/publications/m/item/sanitary-inspection-package-%28drinking-water%29--rainwater-collection-and-storage).
- The KZN Department of Human Settlements policy addresses urban housing scope; do not turn it into a universal national rule for all smallholders. Water-use safety questions should be directed to local health authority and applicable local requirements. [KZN policy directive](https://www.kzndhs.gov.za/images/documents/Provincial%20Policies/Policy_Directive_Rainwater_Harvesting_System.pdf).

## Lesson body

### English meaning — current source

Your roof can collect rainwater. The amount depends on roof area, rainfall and losses.

Check whether the roof material is suitable for rainwater collection before connecting a tank.

Use the roof area seen from above and local rainfall records. Then allow for water that misses the gutter, is diverted or overflows a full tank.

An annual total does not tell you how much water will be available during a dry spell. Compare supply with the uses you plan.

Roof runoff can carry dust, droppings and other contamination. A first-flush diverter keeps some of the first runoff out of the tank.

The required diversion depends on the roof and system. Use the supplier's sizing and maintenance instructions; there is no single volume for every roof.

A diverter does not make the remaining water safe to drink.

Tank size depends on water demand, rain, roof area and the length of dry periods.

List the intended uses and estimate their demand from your own records. Compare that with supply through the seasons.

Plan what you will do when stored water runs low. A province name alone cannot tell you the tank size you need.

Keep the tank covered, screen openings against insects, and maintain the roof, gutters and diverter. Keep rainwater separate from drinking-water pipes.

Water that looks clear may still contain germs or chemicals. Ask the local health authority about testing and treatment suited to the intended use.

A basic filter alone is not a drinking-water guarantee. Water used on food crops also needs a safety assessment.

### isiZulu proposal

Uphahla lwakho lungaqoqa amanzi emvula. Inani lamanzi lincike ngobukhulu bophahla, emvuleni nasekulahlekeni kwamanzi.

Ngaphambi kokuxhuma ithangi, hlola ukuthi impahla okwakhiwe ngayo uphahla ikulungele yini ukuqoqwa kwamanzi emvula.

Sebenzisa indawo yophahla uma uyibuka phezulu, kanye namarekhodi emvula endawo. Bese ubala namanzi angangeni kuma-gutter, aphambukiswayo noma aphuphuma ethangini eligcwele.

Inani lemvula lonyaka alikutsheli ukuthi mangakanani amanzi azotholakala ngesikhathi esomile. Qhathanisa amanzi angatholakala nalokho ohlela ukuwasebenzisela kona.

Amanzi ageleza esuka ophahleni angaphatha uthuli, ubulongwe nokunye ukungcola. I-diverter yamanzi okuqala igcina amanye amanzi okuqala angangeni ethangini.

Inani lamanzi okufanele liphambukiswe lincike ophahleni nasohlelweni. Landela imiyalelo yomkhiqizi yokulinganisa nokunakekela i-diverter; alikho inani elilodwa elisebenza kuwo wonke amaphahla.

I-diverter ayenzi amanzi asele aphephe ukuwaphuza.

Usayizi wethangi uncike esidingweni samanzi, emvuleni, endaweni yophahla nobude bezikhathi ezomile.

Bhala izindlela ohlose ukusebenzisa ngazo amanzi, ulinganise isidingo sakho usebenzisa amarekhodi akho. Qhathanisa leso sidingo namanzi atholakalayo kuzo zonke izinkathi zonyaka.

Hlela ukuthi uzokwenzani lapho amanzi agciniwe esephela noma esencipha kakhulu. Igama lesifundazwe lodwa alikwazi ukukutshela usayizi wethangi olidingayo.

Vala ithangi, faka izisefo emigodini ukuze kungangeni izinambuzane, futhi unakekele uphahla, ama-gutter ne-diverter. Gcina amanzi emvula ehlukene namapayipi amanzi okuphuza.

Amanzi abonakala ecacile asengaba namagciwane noma amakhemikhali. Buza isiphathimandla sezempilo sendawo ngokuhlolwa nokwelashwa okufanele ukusetshenziswa okuhlosiwe.

Isihlungi esiyisisekelo sodwa asiqinisekisi ukuthi amanzi aphephile ukuwaphuza. Amanzi asetshenziswa ezitshalweni zokudla nawo adinga ukuhlolwa kokuphepha.

## Quiz

### Question 1 — information for tank choice

**English question:** What information is needed to choose a rainwater tank?

**isiZulu proposal:** Yiluphi ulwazi oludingekayo ukuze ukhethe ithangi lamanzi emvula?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Only the province where the farm is located | Isifundazwe ipulazi elikuso kuphela |
| 1 — correct | Roof area, rainfall pattern, water demand and collection losses | Indawo yophahla, iphethini yemvula, isidingo samanzi nokulahleka kwamanzi lapho eqoqwa |
| 2 | Only the amount of rain in one storm | Inani lemvula yesiphepho esisodwa kuphela |
| 3 | Only the price of the biggest available tank | Intengo yethangi elikhulu kunawo wonke elitholakalayo kuphela |

**English rationale:** Tank planning must compare usable supply with demand through wet and dry periods. One fixed regional size cannot do that.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** Ukuhlela ithangi kudinga ukuqhathanisa amanzi angasetshenziswa nesidingo samanzi ngezikhathi zemvula nezomile. Usayizi owodwa obekelwe isifunda sonke awukwazi ukukwenza lokho.

### Question 2 — first flush for irrigation water

**English question:** Why does a first-flush diverter matter even for irrigation-only tank water?

**isiZulu proposal:** Kungani kubalulekile ukuphambukisa amanzi okuqala, ngisho noma amanzi ethangi ezosetshenziselwa ukunisela kuphela?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | It doesn't matter for irrigation, only drinking water | Akubalulekile uma kuniselwa; kubalulekile emanzini okuphuza kuphela |
| 1 — correct | The first flush carries concentrated droppings, dust and pathogens that can contaminate edible crops | Amanzi okuqala athwala ubulongwe obunqwabelene, uthuli namagciwane abangela izifo angangcolisa izitshalo ezidliwayo |
| 2 | It's more acidic and changes soil pH over time | Ane-asidi eningi futhi aguqula i-pH yomhlabathi ngokuhamba kwesikhathi |
| 3 | It stops the tank overfilling in storms | Avimbela ithangi ukuba ligcwale ngokweqile ngesikhathi seziphepho |

**English rationale:** Diverting early runoff can reduce contamination, but it does not guarantee that later water is safe. Assess quality for the intended use.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** Ukuphambukisa amanzi okuqala kunganciphisa ukungcola, kodwa akuqinisekisi ukuthi amanzi alandelayo aphephile. Hlola ikhwalithi yamanzi ngokwalokho ozowasebenzisela kona.

## Reviewer questions and holds

1. Confirm locally familiar spoken terms for roof catchment, losses, roof material suitability, diverter, early/first runoff, sizing, supplier maintenance, tank demand, drinking-water pipes, germs/pathogens, chemicals, testing, treatment, food crops and safety assessment.
2. Does the translation keep “some” first runoff, rather than implying every first drop/flush is removed? Does it clearly say sizing depends on the roof/system and supplier directions, without adding any amount?
3. Does it avoid implying that the first-flush device, a basic filter, clear appearance or later runoff makes water potable or safe for every use?
4. Is the rainwater separation from drinking-water pipes understandable without becoming installation instructions? Keep plumbing standards and cross-connection design for appropriate local professional advice.
5. Does the edible-crop statement clearly require a safety assessment for intended use? Check the word for pathogens/germs so learners understand possible contamination without inventing a specific organism.
6. Are quiz answers still index 1? In Q2, does the translation preserve that early runoff can contaminate edible crops and that diversion reduces but does not guarantee safety of later water?
7. KZN's policy source concerns urban housing scope; do not present it as nationwide legal advice. Confirm that “local health authority” remains distinct from a water authority or supplier.
8. Record fluent reviewer and appropriate local water/public-health reviewer separately, including roles, region, date, wording changes and approval limits.
