This supports caution and the action, not a diagnosis from smell alone.
- [Oregon State Extension: Do the rot thing](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/em-9475-do-rot-thing-choosing-using-composting-system): too-wet or bad-smelling piles may need air and less moisture; turn and add dry browns. It describes exclusion of pet/human waste, meat, dairy, diseased plants and other unsuitable materials. Its numeric schedules and temperature guidance are not imported here.

### Quiz coverage

The current lesson has two questions; the isiZulu narration draft contains no matching quiz text or rationales. Keep each correct answer unchanged (index 1 in both cases).

1. **Wet, slimy heap with strong ammonia smell — correct action:** add dry carbon material such as straw and turn the heap. Current rationale: a wet/slimy heap may need more air and drier material; ammonia can also suggest excess nitrogen-rich material; keep it damp, not soggy. Do not translate the old slide 11 single-cause claim into the explanation.
2. **Why exclude wattle seed pods from an ordinary heap? — correct answer:** some seeds may survive and spread when compost is used. Current rationale: an ordinary heap may not expose every seed to conditions that make it non-viable; excluding pods avoids spreading them with the compost. Do not claim a specific seed-coat or heat-resistance mechanism.

## Proposed isiZulu draft — five slide blocks

**All text below is a draft for fluent review.** Borrowed compost terms are left visible so the reviewer can choose familiar, accurate wording. Confirm especially *i-compost*, *ama-browns*, *ama-greens*, *i-ammonia*, *ukwelashwa* and the best way to express “non-viable” in spoken isiZulu.

### Ikhasi 9 — I-compost Yondla Umhlabathi

I-compost yizinto zemvelo ezibolile ngaphansi kwezimo ezilawulwayo.

Uma isivuthiwe, i-compost ingathuthukisa ukwakheka komhlabathi futhi ifake izakhamzimba emhlabathini.

Isikhathi esithathayo siyashintsha kuye ngezinto ezisetshenzisiwe, umswakama, umoya nokushisa. Isifundazwe ohlala kuso noma inani elinqunyiwe lamasonto akusona isivivinyo sokuthi i-compost isilungile.

### Ikhasi 10 — Buka: Yakha Inqwaba Ye-compost

Yakha inqwaba usebenzisa izinto ezomile ezinsundu (*ama-browns*) nezinto ezintsha eziluhlaza (*ama-greens*).

Gcina izingqimba zinomswakama, zingagcwali amanzi, ukuze kungene umoya futhi izinto ezibolisa i-compost zikwazi ukusebenza.

### Ikhasi 11 — Linganisa Ama-browns Nama-greens

Hlanganisa ama-browns omile nama-greens amasha. Gwema izingqimba eziwugqinsi nezimanzi ezivimba umoya.

Uma inqwaba ishelela noma inuka kakhulu i-ammonia, engeza ama-browns omile bese uyiphendula.

Hlola umswakama nomoya njengoba inqwaba ishintsha. Uhlobo olulodwa lweresiphi alufaneli zonke izinhlobo zezinto.

### Ikhasi 12 — Ukushisa Kukodwa Akufakazeli Ukuphepha

Ukushisa phakathi nenqwaba akufakazeli ukuthi yonke ingxenye yayo ithole ukwelashwa okufanayo. Isikhathi, izinga lokushisa nendlela yokuphatha konke kubalulekile.

Kule ndlela elula yasekhaya, ungafaki inyama, ubisi, izitshalo ezigulayo, indle yezilwane ezifuywayo noma izinto ezingcolile.

Ungacabangi ukuthi i-compost yasekhaya ibulala yonke imbewu yokhula noma zonke izinto ezibangela izifo. Uma kudingeka indlela eqinisekisa ukuhlanzeka nokuphepha, sebenzisa inqubo eyaziwayo.

### Ikhasi 13 — Gcina Ama-pod Embewu Ye-wattle Ngaphandle

Gcina ama-pod embewu ye-wattle ngaphandle kwenqwaba ye-compost. Inqwaba evamile ingase ingayenzi yonke imbewu ingasakwazi ukuhluma.

Sebenzisa izinto ezihlanzekile nezingakaze zelashwe. Amagxolo abola kancane; igama lawo lodwa aliqinisekisi ukuthi awangcolisiwe.

Hlola inqwaba bese uyiphendula lapho idinga umoya owengeziwe noma ukuxutshwa. Yigcine inomswakama, ingagcwali amanzi.

## Proposed isiZulu quiz rationales

These are rationale drafts only. They do not change the question, options, or correct-answer index.

1. **Quiz 1:** Inqwaba emanzi futhi eshelela ingase idinge umoya owengeziwe nezinto ezomile. Faka ama-browns omile bese uphendula inqwaba ukuze kungene umoya. Iphunga le-ammonia lingabuye libonise ukuthi kunezinto eziningi ezine-nitrogen. Hlola ukuthi inqwaba ihlala imanzi kancane, ingacwili emanzini.
