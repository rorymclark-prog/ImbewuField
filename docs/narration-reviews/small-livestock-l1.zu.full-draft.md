## Lesson body — English meaning and proposal

**English (current):** Chickens can help an empty bed after harvest. They scratch through plant remains and eat some insects and weed seeds. Their manure and bedding can be composted and returned to the soil. Foraging does not replace a balanced diet, clean water, shelter or daily care.

A chicken tractor is a moveable, floorless pen. Move it before the ground becomes bare, muddy or heavily covered with manure. The right time depends on the birds, soil and weather. There is no single number of chickens that guarantees enough fertility for every plot.

Keep chickens away from seedlings and crops being harvested for food. Fresh manure can carry germs. Ask an extension adviser how to manage manure safely before the next crop. Ducks scratch less, but can still damage plants and make wet ground muddy. Watch the birds and move them when needed.

**IsiZulu proposal:** Izinkukhu zingasiza embhedeni ongenalutho ngemva kokuvuna. Ziklwebha izinsalela zezitshalo, zidle ezinye izinambuzane nembewu yokhula. Umquba wazo nezinto zokulala kungavundiswa kwenziwe i-compost bese kubuyiselwa enhlabathini. Ukuzifunela ukudla akuthathi indawo yokudla okunomsoco, amanzi ahlanzekile, indawo yokukhosela nokunakekelwa nsuku zonke.

I-chicken tractor yihhoko elihambayo elingenaphansi. Lihambise ngaphambi kokuba umhlabathi ungabi nazitshalo, ube nodaka noma umbozwe kakhulu umquba. Isikhathi esifanele sincike ezinyonini, enhlabathini nasesimweni sezulu. Alikho inani elilodwa lezinkukhu eliqinisekisa ukuvunda okwanele kuyo yonke indawo.

Gcina izinkukhu zikude nezithombo nezitshalo ezivunelwa ukudliwa. Umquba omusha ungathwala amagciwane. Buza umeluleki wezolimo ukuthi ungawuphatha kanjani ngokuphepha ngaphambi kokutshala isivuno esilandelayo. Amadada awaklwebhi kakhulu njengezinkukhu, kodwa nawo angalimaza izitshalo futhi enze umhlabathi omanzi ube nodaka. Wabheke, uwahambise uma kudingeka.

## Quiz

### Q1
**English:** You want chickens to prepare an empty bed for replanting. When's the right time to put them in?

| # | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Right after planting seedlings, so they loosen soil around them | Masinyane ngemva kokutshala izithombo, ukuze athambise umhlabathi ozizungezile |
| 1 — correct | After harvest, once the bed is cleared, before the next planting | Ngemva kokuvuna, lapho umbhede ususuliwe, ngaphambi kokutshala okulandelayo |
| 2 | During the growing season once the canopy can withstand scratching | Ngesikhathi sokukhula, lapho amaqabunga esengakwazi ukumelana nokuklwebha |
| 3 | Only in winter to avoid heat stress | Ebusika kuphela ukuze kugwenywe ukucindezeleka ngenxa yokushisa |

**Rationale (English):** Use an empty bed after harvest. Keep fresh manure away from food crops and plan safe manure handling before replanting.

**Rationale (isiZulu):** Sebenzisa umbhede ongenalutho ngemva kokuvuna. Gcina umquba omusha ukude nezitshalo zokudla, futhi uhlele ukuphathwa kwawo ngokuphepha ngaphambi kokutshala futhi.

### Q2
**English:** Why are ducks better suited than chickens to an established food forest understorey?

| # | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Ducks produce more manure per day | Amadada akhiqiza umquba omningi ngosuku |
| 1 — correct | Ducks eat slugs and snails without the heavy scratching that disturbs roots and mulch | Amadada adla ama-slug neminenke ngaphandle kokuklwebha kakhulu okuphazamisa izimpande nesembozo somhlabathi |
| 2 | Ducks are immune to Newcastle disease | Amadada awangenwa isifo se-Newcastle |
| 3 | Ducks roost in trees, reducing ground compaction | Amadada alala ezihlahleni, ngaleyo ndlela anciphise ukuqina komhlabathi |

**Rationale (English):** Ducks do not scratch like chickens. They can still trample or eat plants, so watch the ground and move them when needed.

**Rationale (isiZulu):** Amadada awaklwebhi njengezinkukhu. Asengazinyathela noma azidle izitshalo; ngakho qapha umhlabathi, uwahambise uma kudingeka.

### Slide 7 — Ducks Suit Established Understorey

**English:** Ducks eat slugs and snails without the heavy scratching of chickens. They may suit an established food forest understorey. They can still eat or trample plants and make wet ground muddy. Watch the birds and move them when needed. Provide clean water and suitable feed; foraging alone may not be enough.

**IsiZulu proposal:** Amadada adla ama-slug neminenke ngaphandle kokuklwebha kakhulu njengezinkukhu. Angase afaneleke ngaphansi kwehlathi lokudla elinezitshalo esezimile. Asengazidla noma azinyathele izitshalo, enze nomhlabathi omanzi ube nodaka. Wabheke, uwahambise uma kudingeka. Wahlinzeke ngamanzi ahlanzekile nokudla okufanele; ukuzifunela ukudla kuphela kungase kunganeli.

## Reviewer questions and holds

1. Does the isiZulu naturally distinguish a chicken tractor (moveable, floorless pen) from the clip, which shows no pen?
2. Is “before the next crop” unmistakably before planting, and is the extension adviser/manure wording actionable without implying a fixed wait or safe use of fresh manure?
3. Are slugs, snails, understorey, manure, bedding and compost understandable in spoken local farming isiZulu? Confirm borrowed terms without coining new vocabulary.
4. Does the quiz keep the correct options at indices 1 and 1 and preserve all distractor meanings?
5. Confirm foraging is only partial food/pest help; animal diet, water, shelter, care, crop protection, muddy-ground checks and conditional movement remain explicit.
