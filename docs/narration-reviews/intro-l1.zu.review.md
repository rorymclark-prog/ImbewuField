- Primary legal source: [National Water Act 36 of 1998](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201409/a36-98.pdf), §22(1) and Schedule 1. Section 22 lists the lawful bases for water use, including Schedule 1 uses, existing lawful use, general authorisation, licence, or a licence dispensation. Schedule 1 includes reasonable household domestic use where the person has lawful access. The lesson must not say that every borehole needs a licence, or imply that a household may grant another person a water-use entitlement. Check applicable permission and source capacity before agreeing to additional sharing.

## What changed and what remains protected

The current English scenario says the borehole serves the household and neighbours ask for water. The learner must first check whether sharing is allowed and whether the source can serve all users without taking too much. Only if sharing is allowed and enough water is available should users agree how to share fairly. Monitoring helps notice change; it does not itself grant permission or show that extra use is safe.

The existing isiZulu slide 7 instead says the borehole produces more water than the household needs, then instructs the learner to share and monitor the water table. The scenario's “surplus” was unsupported, and monitoring is not a substitute for permission or evidence of sustainable supply. The English quiz now uses the same check-first condition and retains answer index 2. Slide 8's old isiZulu says ethics allow a decision “without waiting for permission”; that is too broad. The corrected English says to check the rules and ask permission where needed.

The broader three-ethics teaching stays as written. The shared-spring example in the lesson body is an ethical example; it should not be translated into a claim that any particular source is legally open to all. This packet does not change the body, other quiz, or media.

### Ikhasi 7 — Buka: Isinqumo Esisodwa, Ama-Ethics Amathathu

I-borehole inikeza umndeni wakho amanzi. Omakhelwane bacela amanzi nabo.

Qala uthole ukuthi ukwabelana ngamanzi kuvumelekile yini. Hlola ukuthi lo mthombo ungakwazi yini ukusiza bonke abasebenzisi ngaphandle kokusebenzisa amanzi amaningi kakhulu. Uma ukwabelana kuvumelekile futhi kunamanzi anele, vumelanani ngokwabelana ngokulingana bese niqhubeka nihlola izinga lamanzi.

I-People Care ne-Fair Share kuqondisa isivumelwano. I-Earth Care isho ukuvikela umthombo wamanzi. Ukuqapha izinga lamanzi kukodwa akusho ukuthi ukusebenzisa amanzi engeziwe kuphephile noma kuvumelekile.

[pause]

Ubani ongakusiza uhlole imithetho nokuthi amanzi akhona anele yini?

## Proposed isiZulu borehole quiz draft

**Question:** I-borehole inikeza umndeni wakho amanzi. Omakhelwane nabo bacela amanzi. Iyiphi indlela ebonisa kangcono wonke ama-ethics amathathu?

**Options (keep current answer index 2):**

0. Thengisa ilungelo lokuthola amanzi kumuntu okhokha kakhulu.
1. Gcina wonke amanzi e-borehole ukuze wandise indawo yokunisela.
2. Thola ukuthi ukwabelana kuvumelekile yini nokuthi lo mthombo ungakwazi yini ukusiza bonke abasebenzisi. Yilapho kuphela eningavumelana ngokwabelana ngokulingana futhi niqhubeke nihlola izinga lamanzi.
3. Vala i-borehole ukuze uvikele amanzi angaphansi komhlaba kuphela.

**Rationale:** Qala uthole ukuthi yikuphi ukusetshenziswa kwamanzi okuvumelekile nokuthi umthombo ungakwazi yini ukusiza bonke abasebenzisi ngaphandle kokusebenzisa amanzi amaningi kakhulu. Uma ukwabelana kuvumelekile futhi kunamanzi anele, vumelanani ngokwabelana ngokulingana. Ukuqapha kukusiza ubone ushintsho; akukuniki imvume yokusebenzisa amanzi engeziwe.
