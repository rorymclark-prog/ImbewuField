# Vegetables and Staples L2 — isiZulu review packet

**Status: UNREVIEWED AI DRAFT. Do not publish or use with learners.** This
side-by-side review compares the current English lesson and narration with the
existing unpublished isiZulu draft. The suggested isiZulu wording below needs
review by a fluent isiZulu speaker familiar with South African smallholder
farming, including the terms for succession planting, bed access, the Three
Sisters, legumes and decomposition. No farmer, practitioner, learner or fluent
language approval is claimed.

## Scope and sources compared

- English lesson body, key points and quiz: `lib/course-modules.ts`,
  `vegetables-staples-l2`.
- English narration: `docs/narration/vegetables-staples.en.md`, slides 7–11.
- Existing isiZulu draft narration: `docs/narration/vegetables-staples.zu.md`,
  pages 7–11. It is already marked unpublished elsewhere in the source review.

English's worked bed example gives an exact width and length for one practice
bed. The isiZulu draft carries over the same values and says it is one practice
bed. Preserve those values only in that worked example; do not turn them into a
general spacing recommendation. Both languages give a sowing interval of two
to three weeks, and English explicitly calls it a starting rhythm that must be
adjusted to the crop, season and what the garden shows.

### Quiz comparison

The English quiz asks why to sow lettuce in small batches every two to three
weeks; its keyed answer says this gives a steady harvest instead of a glut and
gap. The body and narration elsewhere qualify this benefit: harvests may
overlap, intervals need adjustment, and a difficult season can still prevent a
harvest. The English quiz wording itself may therefore need a later editorial
decision before it is translated as a promise. The second quiz answer says
nitrogen in bean residues is released through decomposition; it matches the
English narration's limiting explanation. This packet does not alter either
quiz question, answer or rationale, and proposes no quiz translation for
publication.

## Suggested isiZulu wording — unreviewed AI draft only

These sentences are intended to preserve the existing English safeguards, not
to establish agronomic timing or promise a harvest. A fluent farmer or
practitioner reviewer should revise them before any learner use.

**Slide 8 — replace the heat/heavy-rain guarantee:**

> Ukuhlwanyela ngezikhathi ezihlukene kunganciphisa ingozi yokulahlekelwa yikho konke ngesikhathi esisodwa. Kodwa akukuqinisekisi ukuvuna uma izimo zokukhula ziqhubeka zinzima.

**Slide 9 — replace the fixed fourth-sowing harvest claim:**

> Uma isikhathi sokukhula kwesitshalo sikuvumela, ukuvuna kungaqala ukuhlangana. Isivuno sokuqala singase singakalungeli ngesikhathi sokuhlwanyela kwesine.
>
> Amaviki amabili kuya kwamathathu ayisiqalo sokuhlela, akuwona umthetho. Isitshalo samaqabunga senkathi epholile singathatha isikhathi eside. Ukushisa kungasheshisa ukukhula noma kubangele ukwehluleka. Bheka okwenzeka engadini yakho, ulungise izikhathi zokuhlwanyela ngokufanele.

**Slide 10 — replace the origin and “do not compete” claims:**

> Odade Abathathu (Three Sisters) bayisibonelo esivela emasikweni okulima abantu boMdabu baseMelika. Ummbila unikeza ukuphakama nesakhiwo. Izimbotyi zikhwela ummbila futhi ziyiprotheni egcinekayo. Amaphuzi amboza umhlabathi ngomthunzi futhi asiza ukuwugcina umswakama.
>
> Lezi zitshalo zisengancintisana. Zinike indawo, amanzi nokukhanya okufanele. Izimbotyi zibopha i-nitrogen ngosizo lwamagciwane asezimpandeni, kodwa ungacabangi ukuthi zondla ummbila ngokushesha. I-nitrogen esezinsaleleni itholakala lapho sezibola.

**Slide 11 — replace “every place has a hungry gap”:**

> Umuzi ungaba nesikhathi lapho ukudla okugcinwe khona sekuncipha, kanti isivuno esilandelayo asikakulungeli ukuvunwa.
>
> Okwenu kungase kufike lapho ummbila ogciniwe usuphela. Kungase kufike ngaphambi kokuba imifino yasebusika ilungele ukuvunwa. Kungase kufike nangesikhathi esomile lapho ukuntuleka kwamanzi kunciphisa lokho okungatshalwa engadini.
>
> Ningakopeli ikhalenda lomunye umuntu. Qalani ngezinyanga zenu. Zibhaleni phansi, bese nikhetha isitshalo nesikhathi sokuhlwanyela esingasiza ukuletha ukudla kulelo gebe.
