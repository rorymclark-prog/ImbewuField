## Terminology check

The Department of Sport, Arts and Culture's [Multilingual Natural Sciences and Technology Term List](https://www.dsac.gov.za/sites/default/files/2024-10/Multilingual%20Natural%20Sciences%20%26%20Technology%20Term%20List%20for%20Grade%204%20to%206.pdf) lists Sesotho `pollination` as `tulafatso` and `pollen` as `modula`; Tshivenda `pollination` as `nyanḓadza muvula` and `pollen` as `muvula`; and Xitsonga `pollination` as `nsindzahato` and `pollen` as `nsindza`. It lists Xitsonga `pollute` as `thyakisa`, confirming that `ku thyakisa hi phoyini` was unsafe wording. The Sesotho term list also independently gives `tulafatso` and `modula` ([DSAC Sotho-group dictionary](https://dsacevents.dsac.gov.za/NLTS/docs/natSciencesTech_gr46_Sotho2013.pdf)).

The correction uses the official Sesotho noun `tulafatso` with the listed `modula` gloss in a short explanation. Tshivenda and Xitsonga retain the English technical term `pollination` inside translated sentences because the local sentence construction has not been reviewed by a fluent speaker. `open-pollinated`, `stable variety` and `F1 hybrid` remain exact English in all three drafts; the earlier parenthetical claims describing open-pollinated seed as pollen coming from the same or other plants have been removed.

Back-translation of the corrected Xitsonga opening sentence: “Seed from an open-pollinated stable variety can produce similar plants when pollination is properly controlled.” This keeps the English source's condition and result without adding a specific pollen route.
