# Reading the Landscape orientation: regional AI draft screen

The first three silent slides now include ten additional bounded draft paragraphs: five Sesotho, three Tshivenda and two Xitsonga. Every slide retains its exact English source, identifies machine text as unreviewed and keeps English as the selected narration fallback only when the learner explicitly chooses it. No regional audio was added.

| Language | Draft fields | Exact-English holds kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sesotho | Slide 1 body 2; slide 2 bodies 1 and 3; slide 3 bodies 1 and 3 | Sun angles, wind and frost detail, map contents and other technical instructions |
| Tshivenda | Slide 1 body 2; slide 2 body 1; slide 3 body 3 | Farm context, ridges and hollows, water exit, map contents and other technical instructions |
| Xitsonga | Slide 2 body 1; slide 3 body 3 | Slide 1, water direction, frost, map contents and other technical instructions |

One lower-cost agent drafted candidate wording and a separate agent checked the candidates against the English meaning. The screen rejected alternatives that changed sun angles into sun paths, ridges into generic high places, hollows into low places, frost pockets into vague cold places, or the farm into a field. `A-frame level` and `contours` remain English technical terms for facilitator explanation. These machine checks are **not** fluent-speaker or local farming approval. A facilitator should check the three languages against the paired English, especially descriptions of water, cold air and landform shape, before teaching from a draft.

The slides were regenerated from the paired draft files and visually inspected at full image resolution. The reviewer must also inspect the deployed phone player, slide-only offline pack and narration selector. Slide 1 Tshivenda initially put its second English paragraph's draft in the first paragraph slot; that was corrected before publication and the regenerated image was checked again.
