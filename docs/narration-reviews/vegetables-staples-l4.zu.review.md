## Quiz coverage

The current L4 quiz has two questions; the isiZulu script contains no matching quiz text. Keep wording, options and keyed answers unchanged unless the English owner changes them.

1. **A treatment product is needed:** correct answer is a product registered for the crop and pest, used according to its label. Rationale says crop, pest, dose, protection and harvest waiting matter; natural origin does not make an improvised treatment safe or suitable. The isiZulu version must not reduce this to “follow the packet” or endorse a household mixture.
2. **Yellow brassica leaves:** check whether the cause may be soil nutrition or watering before assuming pests. Rationale says the causes need different responses; avoid pest-specific treatment until the cause is checked.

The current narration and generated transcript match the English lesson. The existing isiZulu narration weakens the label safeguards and adds an outcome assurance, so it is not ready for recording. Public course audio/decks currently expose English only.

## Human review questions

- Does the spoken wording preserve “may”, “if treatment is needed”, and “check first” without turning clues into a diagnosis?
- Are helpful insects clearly described as beneficial organisms rather than pests, and does the translation retain the observe/check/protect/act/monitor order?
- Does every treatment instruction say registration must match both the specific crop and the pest, then require following the full product label?
- Are personal protection and harvest waiting instructions explicit, including for any neem product, with no safe-by-default or “natural means safe” implication?
- Has the unsupported claim that hand removal or improved plant care solve most minor problems been removed?
- Does the still remain an illustration of the decision sequence, not proof that a treatment worked? The older video is not registered in the learner player.
