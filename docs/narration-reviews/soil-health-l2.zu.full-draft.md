## Lesson body

### English meaning (current source wording)

Compost is organic matter broken down under managed conditions.

Finished compost can improve soil structure and contribute nutrients.

Time to readiness varies with materials, moisture, air and temperature. A province name or a fixed number of weeks is not a readiness test.

Mix dry browns with fresh greens. Avoid thick, wet layers that keep air out.

If the heap becomes slimy or smells strongly of ammonia, add dry browns and turn it.

Check moisture and air as the heap changes; one recipe does not suit every mix of materials.

A hot centre does not prove that every part of a heap has been treated. Time, temperature and management all matter.

Keep meat, dairy, diseased plants, pet waste and contaminated materials out of this simple household system.

Do not assume home composting destroys every weed seed or disease organism. Use a recognised process where sanitation is required.

Keep wattle seed pods out of the compost heap. An ordinary heap may not make every seed non-viable.

Use only clean, untreated materials. Bark breaks down slowly; its name alone is not proof that it is free of contamination.

Check the heap and turn when it needs more air or mixing. Keep it moist rather than waterlogged.

I-compost esilungile ingathuthukisa ukwakheka komhlabathi futhi ifake izakhamzimba kuwo.

Isikhathi sokuthi i-compost ilunge siyashintsha kuye ngezinto ezifakiwe, umswakama, umoya nokushisa. Igama lesifundazwe noma inani elinqunyiwe lamasonto akusho ukuthi i-compost isilungile.

Hlanganisa ama-browns omile nama-greens amasha. Gwema izingqimba eziwugqinsi nezimanzi ezivimba umoya.

Uma inqwaba iba bushelelezi ngokushelela noma inuka kakhulu i-ammonia, faka ama-browns omile bese uyiphendula.

Hlola umswakama nomoya njengoba inqwaba ishintsha; iresiphi eyodwa ayifaneli zonke izingxube zezinto.

Ukuthi indawo emaphakathi nenqwaba iyashisa akufakazeli ukuthi yonke ingxenye yayo ithole ukwelashwa. Isikhathi, izinga lokushisa nendlela yokuphatha konke kubalulekile.

Ungafaki inyama, ubisi nemikhiqizo yobisi, izitshalo ezigulayo, indle yezilwane ezifuywayo noma izinto ezingcolile kule ndlela elula yasekhaya.

Ungacabangi ukuthi ukwenza i-compost ekhaya kuqeda yonke imbewu yokhula noma zonke izinto eziphilayo ezibangela izifo. Uma kudingeka ukuhlanzwa kwe-compost, sebenzisa inqubo eyamukelekile.

Sebenzisa izinto ezihlanzekile kuphela, ezingakaze zelashwe. Amagxolo abola kancane; igama lawo lodwa aliqinisekisi ukuthi awangcolisiwe.

Hlola inqwaba, uyiphendule lapho idinga umoya owengeziwe noma ukuxutshwa. Yigcine inomswakama, ingagcwali amanzi.

## Key points

### English meaning (current source wording)

1. Balance browns, greens, moisture and air.
2. A hot centre does not prove the whole heap is sanitised.
3. Keep seed pods and contaminated materials out.
4. Judge readiness from the compost condition, not a fixed regional timetable.

### Proposed isiZulu

1. Linganisa ama-browns, ama-greens, umswakama nomoya.
2. Ukushisa phakathi nenqwaba akufakazeli ukuthi yonke ingxenye yenqwaba ihlanzwe ngokwanele.
3. Gcina ama-pod embewu nezinto ezingcolile kungangeni enqwabeni.
4. Yahlulela ukuthi i-compost isilungile ngesimo sayo, hhayi ngohlelo lwesikhathi olumisiwe lwesifunda.

## Quiz

### Question 1 — wet, slimy heap with ammonia smell

**English question:** A farmer's compost heap smells strongly of ammonia and is wet and slimy. What's the fix?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Inqwaba ye-compost yomlimi inuka kakhulu i-ammonia, imanzi futhi iyashelela. Yini okufanele ayenze?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Add more nitrogen-rich green material | Faka izinto eziluhlaza ezengeziwe ezine-nitrogen eningi |
| B — correct | Add more dry carbon material like straw and turn the heap | Faka izinto ezomile ezine-carbon eningi njenge-straw bese uphendula inqwaba |
| C | Stop turning it and let it cool | Yeka ukuyiphendula uyiyeke iphole |
| D | Add more water — the smell means it's too dry | Faka amanzi engeziwe — iphunga lisho ukuthi yome kakhulu |

**English rationale:** A wet, slimy heap may need more air and drier material. Add dry browns and turn the heap to open it up. An ammonia smell can also suggest too much nitrogen-rich material. Check that the heap stays damp, not soggy.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Inqwaba emanzi nebushelelezi ngokushelela ingase idinge umoya owengeziwe nezinto ezomile. Faka ama-browns omile bese uphendula inqwaba ukuze kungene umoya. Iphunga le-ammonia lingase futhi libonise ukuthi kunezinto eziningi ezine-nitrogen. Hlola ukuthi inqwaba ihlala imanzi kancane, ingacwili emanzini.

### Question 2 — wattle seed pods

**English question:** Why keep wattle seed pods out of an ordinary compost heap?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Kungani kufanele ugcine ama-pod embewu ye-wattle ngaphandle kwenqwaba ye-compost evamile?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Bark makes every heap too hot | Amagxolo enza yonke inqwaba ishise kakhulu |
| B — correct | Some seeds may survive and spread when the compost is used | Enye imbewu ingasinda bese isabalala lapho kusetshenziswa i-compost |
| C | Pods always attract termites | Ama-pod ahlala eheha umuhlwa |
| D | Pods release a gas that kills every soil organism | Ama-pod akhipha igesi ebulala zonke izinto eziphilayo ezisemhlabathini |

**English rationale:** An ordinary heap may not expose every seed to conditions that make it non-viable. Excluding pods avoids spreading them with the compost.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Inqwaba evamile ingase ingayibeki yonke imbewu ezimweni eziyenza ingasakwazi ukuhluma. Ukukhipha ama-pod kunciphisa ingozi yokuthi imbewu isabalale ne-compost.

1. Are the borrowed terms `i-compost`, `ama-browns`, `ama-greens`, `i-ammonia`, `i-carbon`, `i-wattle`, `ama-pod` and `i-straw` natural and understood aloud? Please do not coin a technical term; identify familiar local usage.
2. Does the readiness wording make clear that materials, moisture, air and temperature affect the time, with no fixed regional timetable?
3. Does sliminess or ammonia smell remain a reason to add dry browns and turn, without claiming one certain cause? Does “can also suggest” remain tentative?
4. Is it clear that a hot centre does not prove the whole heap was treated, and that a recognised process is needed where sanitation is required? No temperature, time, recipe or sanitation promise should be added.
5. Are all exclusions limited to meat, dairy, diseased plants, pet waste and contaminated material for this simple household system? Do not add unmatched exclusions or imply every finished heap is unsafe.
6. Does `ingasakwazi ukuhluma` express “non-viable” in plain language without asserting a seed-coat or heat mechanism? Is the reason for excluding wattle pods kept at “some seeds may survive and spread”?
7. Does “clean, untreated” remain distinct from a claim that bark is automatically safe? Does the sentence say only that bark breaks down slowly and its name alone does not prove it is uncontaminated?
8. Do slides 10–11 and their short films avoid implying that the later checks, turning, readiness or sanitation are shown in the footage?
