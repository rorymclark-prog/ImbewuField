## Lesson body

### English meaning (current source wording)

In much of South Africa, especially in winter, the sun is to the north. Its path changes with the season and your location. North-facing slopes often receive more sun and can be warmer and drier. South-facing slopes are often cooler and moister. Frost can collect in low hollows where cold air settles. Watch your own site before choosing where to plant tender crops or place buildings.

Winter sun is lower and farther north than summer sun. A wall or shade cloth can shade a bed longer in winter than in summer. Before placing anything permanent, stand in the spot at 8am, midday, and 4pm on a winter's day and watch where the shade falls.

Pawpaw and young citrus are sensitive to frost. Keep tender plants out of known low frost pockets. Observe local frost before planting.

### Proposed isiZulu

Ezindaweni eziningi zaseNingizimu Afrika, ikakhulukazi ebusika, ilanga libonakala lisenyakatho. Indlela elihamba ngayo esibhakabhakeni iyashintsha ngokwenkathi nangendawo okuyo. Imithambeka ebheke enyakatho ivame ukuthola ilanga eliningi futhi ingafudumala yome kakhulu. Imithambeka ebheke eningizimu ivame ukuphola futhi ibe nomswakama omningi. I-frost ingaqoqana ezindaweni eziphansi eziyimigodi lapho kuhlala khona umoya obandayo. Bheka indawo yakho ngaphambi kokukhetha lapho uzotshala khona izitshalo ezizwela amakhaza noma ubeke khona izakhiwo.

Ilanga lasebusika libonakala liphansi futhi lisenyakatho kakhulu kunelasehlobo. Udonga noma i-shade cloth kungafaka umbhede emthunzini isikhathi eside ebusika kunasehlobo. Ngaphambi kokubeka into enganyakaziswa kalula, yima kuleyo ndawo ngo-8 ekuseni, emini, nango-4 ntambama ngosuku lwasebusika. Bheka lapho umthunzi uwela khona.

I-pawpaw nezihlahla ezisencane ze-citrus ziyazwela ku-frost. Gcina izitshalo ezizwela amakhaza zingekho ezindaweni eziphansi ezaziwayo eziqongelela i-frost. Bheka ukuthi i-frost ivela kuphi endaweni yangakini ngaphambi kokutshala.

**Terminology questions:** Please check whether “ilanga libonakala lisenyakatho,” “izindawo eziphansi eziyimigodi,” the borrowing `shade cloth`, and the borrowings `frost`, `pawpaw`, and `citrus` are clear in spoken isiZulu. Confirm the phrasing does not turn apparent sun position into a fixed direction rule or `low hollows` into south-facing slopes.

## Key points

### English meaning (current source wording)

1. North-facing slopes often get more direct sun; south-facing slopes are often cooler and moister.
2. Winter sun is lower and farther north; check local shade before building.
3. Cold air can collect in low hollows; aspect is only one site factor.
4. Check local frost before placing tender pawpaw or young citrus.

### Proposed isiZulu

1. Imithambeka ebheke enyakatho ivame ukuthola ilanga eliqondile eliningi; leyo ebheke eningizimu ivame ukuphola futhi ibe nomswakama omningi.
2. Ilanga lasebusika libonakala liphansi futhi lisenyakatho kakhulu; hlola umthunzi kuleyo ndawo ngaphambi kokwakha.
3. Umoya obandayo ungaqoqana ezindaweni eziphansi eziyimigodi; indlela umthambeka obheke ngayo ingesinye sezinto okufanele zibhekwe kuphela.
4. Hlola ukuthi i-frost ivela kuphi endaweni yangakini ngaphambi kokubeka i-pawpaw noma izihlahla ezisencane ze-citrus ezizwela amakhaza.

## Quiz

Correct answer positions below match the current English lesson. Quiz 1's correct answer is C (English index 2); Quiz 2's correct answer is A (English index 0). Keep every option and rationale aligned with the current source.

### Question 1 — first place to consider for a frost-tender pawpaw

**English question:** Where should a farmer first look for a frost-tender young pawpaw on a Highveld smallholding?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Umlimi wasepulazini elincane lase-Highveld kufanele aqale abheke kuphi uma efuna indawo yesihlahla se-pawpaw esincane nesizwela i-frost?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | The lowest point where cold air collects | Indawo ephansi kunazo zonke lapho kuqoqana khona umoya obandayo |
| B | A cold, shaded hollow | Umgodi obandayo nosethunzini |
| C — correct | A sunny spot outside a known frost hollow, after checking the site's frost pattern | Indawo enelanga engaphandle komgodi owaziwayo oqongelela i-frost, ngemva kokuhlola ukuthi i-frost ivela kuphi kuleyo ndawo |
| D | A position chosen without checking the site | Indawo ekhethwe ngaphandle kokuhlola indawo |

**English rationale:** Cold air can collect in low places. A sunnier site outside a known frost pocket may reduce risk, but local frost observations must guide the final position.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Umoya obandayo ungaqoqana ezindaweni eziphansi. Indawo enelanga engaphandle kwendawo eyaziwayo eqongelela i-frost inganciphisa ingozi, kodwa ukubona ukuthi i-frost ivela kuphi endaweni yangakini yikho okufanele kuqondise ekukhetheni indawo yokugcina.

### Question 2 — checking shade cloth before fixing it

**English question:** A farmer plans shade cloth on the north side of her garden. What should she check before fixing it in place?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Umlimi uhlela ukubeka i-shade cloth ohlangothini olusenyakatho lwengadi yakhe. Yini okufanele ayihlole ngaphambi kokuyiqinisa endaweni yayo?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — correct | Where its shadow falls on the bed in winter | Lapho umthunzi wayo uwela khona embhedeni ebusika |
| B | Whether it redirects frost away | Ukuthi iyayisusa yini i-frost iyiyise kwenye indawo |
| C | Whether the sun is always overhead at noon | Ukuthi ilanga lihlala liqonde ngqo phezulu yini emini |
| D | Only whether it reduces summer evaporation | Ukuthi inciphisa yini kuphela ukuhwamuka kwamanzi ehlobo |

**English rationale:** Winter sun is lower and farther north. Shade cloth can change the hours of sun on a bed. Check the actual shadows at 8am, midday, and 4pm before fixing it in place.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ilanga lasebusika libonakala liphansi futhi lisenyakatho kakhulu. I-shade cloth ingashintsha isikhathi ilanga elifika ngaso embhedeni. Ngaphambi kokuyiqinisa endaweni yayo, hlola umthunzi okhona ngempela ngo-8 ekuseni, emini, nango-4 ntambama.

## Questions for the first-language reviewer

1. Does the wording sound natural aloud for the intended South African isiZulu learners? Mark sentences that are too long or sound translated.
2. Does `ezindaweni eziningi ... ikakhulukazi ebusika` preserve “in much of South Africa, especially in winter”? Does the next sentence clearly say that the sun's path changes with season and location?
3. Are “often,” “can,” and “may reduce risk” still conditional throughout? Does any sentence imply all north-facing slopes are warm/dry, all south-facing slopes are cool/moist, or every hollow collects frost?
4. Is a low hollow where cold air settles clearly distinct from a slope facing south? What ordinary spoken terms should be used for low hollow and frost pocket?
5. Does the shade cloth sentence say it can create shade for longer in winter, without promising a particular number of hours, height, or all-day result? Are the 8am, midday and 4pm checks clearly observations for that spot on a winter day?
6. Does quiz 1 keep C as the best answer because it is sunny and outside a known frost hollow after local pattern checking, while the rationale still says this may reduce risk and local observations guide the final choice?
7. Does quiz 2 keep A as the correct observation? Do any distractors sound accidentally correct in isiZulu?
8. Are `pawpaw`, `young citrus`, and `frost` familiar borrowings in this context? Please retain the same plant references and add no species or frost guarantee.
