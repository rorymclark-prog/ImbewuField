to check in local speech.

**Slide 16 — replace the one-morning and paced-to-scale instructions:**

> I-site map idinga iphepha, i-tape measure, i-compass, nesikhathi sokuhamba emhlabeni wakho.

> Hamba emngceleni wenze umdwebo wokuqala. Maka umdwebo ngokuthi awukabi ngesikali kuze kube usuwahlolile amabanga. Maka inyakatho. Faka indlu, izihlahla, amanzi, imigwaqo nezicingo.
