isiZulu draft. It is a claim and copy review, not an isiZulu translation or

## Quiz and media comparison

The first L2 quiz in `lib/course-modules.ts` uses inconsistent weekly supply to
compare a fixed-supply market, a weekly box, a no-fixed-commitment informal or
neighbour sale, and a daily school delivery. Its key is the flexible channel
that does not require a fixed weekly amount; its rationale says to sell what is
actually available. That answer does not cancel slide 10's local trading and
food requirements. The second quiz says confirmed regular orders can help plan
around demand, while the rationale keeps the conditions of reliable supply,
payment and fulfilment costs. The isiZulu narration file does not contain quiz
translations; preserve both the flexible-supply point and those conditions if
the quiz is translated.

The deployed L2 check is
[`market-l2-current-lesson-check.md`](../media/studies-animation-quality/market-l2-current-lesson-check.md).
The revised slide 9 is a static diagram of the same three routes and has no

## Primary-source anchors

- The [National Department of Health's R638 food-premises regulations](https://www.gov.za/sites/default/files/gcis_document/201806/41730gon638.pdf)
  cover hygiene requirements for food premises, food transport and related
  matters. The current English lesson correctly directs learners to check the
  requirements relevant to their own activity; this packet does not determine
  whether a specific seller or route falls under a given provision.
- Local trading requirements depend on municipality and trading place. For
  example, [Knysna Municipality's trader-permit guidance](https://www.knysna.gov.za/how-do-i/apply-for-an-informal-trader-permit/)
  includes public-space and taxi-rank sites. It is an example of local rules,
  not a nationwide rule for every informal sale. Keep the instruction to check
  local requirements; do not translate the draft's claim that informal outlets
  require nothing.
- The current [L2 English lesson check](../media/studies-animation-quality/market-l2-current-lesson-check.md)
  records the static route diagram, conditional customer/order/cost teaching,
  and that no route promises income. No market-price schedule or universal
  box-scheme scale is supplied by the checked course sources.

## Review dependency

After reconciling the draft to the current English lesson and quiz, a fluent
isiZulu-speaking farmer or agricultural educator should check idiom and
clarity. Local market and food-handling requirements need advice from the
relevant municipality or competent authority for the learner's location and
product. This packet does not provide legal advice, establish a particular
seller's obligations, or claim language or market approval.
