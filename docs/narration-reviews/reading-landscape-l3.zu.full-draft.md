## Lesson body

### English meaning (current source)

Wind can damage crops on a smallholding. The direction and strength of damaging wind change with region, season and your site's ridges and gaps. Walk the land on windy days. Record where the wind comes from and what it affects. Check local weather records before deciding where shelter is needed.

On a clear, still night, cold air can flow downhill and collect in low places. These places can be colder than nearby slopes. Frost patterns also depend on the site. Compare candidate places through the local frost season. Check local minimum-temperature records where available. If records are not available, keep observing across cold nights and ask a local agriculture adviser before choosing a permanent home for tender seedlings.

Frost is ice that forms on a cold surface. Mist alone does not show that ice has formed, and frost damage can happen without visible ice. Look for ice and plant damage, compare low ground with slopes, and check minimum temperatures where you can. Mark places where cold or damage lasts longest. Keep sensitive plants away from the cold pockets you observe.

For tomatoes troubled by late blight, airflow and morning sun can help leaves dry. Late blight can still spread during prolonged cool, damp weather. Moving a bed alone will not control it; seek local crop-health guidance too.

### isiZulu proposal

Umoya ungalimaza izitshalo epulazini elincane. Indawo ovela kuyo namandla omoya olimazayo kuyashintsha kuye ngesifunda, inkathi yonyaka, amagquma nezikhala ezisemhlabeni wakho. Hamba uhlole umhlaba ngezinsuku ezinomoya. Bhala phansi ukuthi umoya olimazayo uvela ngakuphi nokuthi uthinta ini. Hlola amarekhodi esimo sezulu endawo ngaphambi kokunquma ukuthi kudingeka kuphi indawo yokukhosela emoyeni.

Ngobusuku obucacile nobungenamoya, umoya obandayo ungehla ngomthambeka uqoqane ezindaweni eziphansi. Lezi zindawo zingabanda kakhulu kunemithambeka eseduze. Indlela isithwathwa esenzeka ngayo nayo incike endaweni. Qhathanisa izindawo ongakhetha kuzo kuyo yonke inkathi yesithwathwa yasendaweni. Hlola amarekhodi endawo okushisa okuphansi, uma ekhona. Uma engekho, qhubeka ubheka okwenzeka ebusuku obubandayo futhi ubuze umeluleki wezolimo wendawo ngaphambi kokukhetha indawo ehlala njalo yezithombo ezizwela amakhaza.

Isithwathwa siyizinhlayiya zeqhwa ezakheka phezu kwendawo ebandayo. Inkungu iyodwa ayisho ukuthi sekwakheke lezo zinhlayiya zeqhwa, futhi isithwathwa singalimaza izitshalo kungabonakali iqhwa. Bheka iqhwa nomonakalo ezitshalweni, uqhathanise izindawo eziphansi nemithambeka, futhi uhlole amazinga okushisa aphansi lapho ukwazi khona. Maka izindawo lapho amakhaza noma umonakalo kuhlala khona isikhathi eside. Gcina izitshalo ezizwela amakhaza zikude nezindawo ezibandayo ozibonile.

Kumatamatisi ahlaselwe yi-late blight, ukuhamba kahle komoya nelanga lasekuseni kungasiza amaqabunga ome. I-late blight isengasakazeka uma kuqhubeka isikhathi eside kubanda futhi kunomswakama. Ukususa umbhede uwuyise kwenye indawo kukodwa ngeke kusilawule lesi sifo; funa iseluleko sendawo ngempilo yezitshalo.

**English question:** Where should a farmer first look when placing a frost-sensitive seedling nursery on a Highveld smallholding?

**isiZulu proposal:** Umlimi kufanele aqale abheke kuphi lapho ekhetha indawo yenkulisa yezithombo ezizwela isithwathwa epulazini elincane lase-Highveld?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | A known frost hollow at the valley bottom | Indawo ephansi eyaziwayo lapho kuqoqana khona isithwathwa |
| 1 | An exposed ridgeline without checking the wind | Umugqa ophakeme wegquma ovulekele umoya, ngaphandle kokuhlola umoya |
| 2 — correct | A sunny, sheltered place outside an observed frost hollow, after checking the site's cold-night pattern | Indawo enelanga nevikelekile emoyeni, engaphandle komgodi owubonile lapho kuqoqana khona isithwathwa, ngemva kokuhlola okwenzeka ebusuku obubandayo endaweni yakho |

## Reviewer questions and source holds

1. **Fluency and register:** Does this sound like ordinary spoken isiZulu used by smallholder farmers, including a learner studying alone on a phone? Mark long, translated or unclear sentences and suggest natural alternatives.
2. **Local terms:** Confirm plain local terms for windbreak, wind direction/pattern, low ground, frost pocket, seedling nursery, minimum temperature, crop-health adviser and late blight. Is *isithwathwa* the familiar term for surface frost in the intended region? Is *i-late blight* understood, or should it include a brief approved explanation?
3. **Conditional cold-air statement:** Does the translation preserve “can” and the clear, still-night condition? Does it avoid implying cold air drains downhill every night or that all low places have the same frost pattern?
4. **Records unavailable:** The lesson body says, if local temperature records are unavailable, keep observing across cold nights and ask an adviser before choosing a permanent nursery site. Slides 14–15 say to check records or ask an adviser, without that explicit no-records branch. Confirm whether the current English copy is final and whether this difference should remain; do not silently insert a new condition into narration without aligning the English source.
5. **Frost signs:** Is it clear that mist alone does not show ice, and frost damage can occur without visible frost? Does the translation distinguish the ice on a surface from cold air and from mist?
6. **Nursery safety:** Is it clear that observation, local records/advice and comparison through the local frost season guide a permanent position, and that no hillside location guarantees frost-free conditions? Do not restore a guaranteed north-facing or gentle-slope recipe.
7. **Late blight:** Does the translation say airflow and morning sun may help leaves dry while prolonged cool, damp weather can still favour spread, and changing the bed alone will not control the disease? It must not imply a cure, complete prevention, or guaranteed rapid drying.
8. **Quiz alignment:** Are the intended answers still option 2 for the nursery question and option 1 for the tomato question? Are all distractors clearly wrong and all conditions retained in the rationales?
9. **Source scope:** The late-blight drying support includes UMN Extension, a US source; KZN DARD supports the local cool/damp risk statement. Does any isiZulu phrasing accidentally elevate the limited drying observation into local treatment guidance? Keep local crop-health advice explicit.
10. **Review record:** Record reviewer name/role, isiZulu variety or region, date, exact amendments and the material reviewed. Language review is not agronomic approval or human listening approval.

## Release holds

- The older isiZulu slide 12 has fixed Highveld and KZN seasonal wind directions. The older slide 15 has a north-facing, gentle-slope nursery recipe and says moving a tomato bed treats late blight. Do not restore these claims.
- Do not imply all frost occurs only on clear, still nights. The English says cold air can flow downhill on such nights; it also says visible frost is not the only sign of frost damage.
- Do not claim morning sun or airflow cures or controls late blight. Preserve the “can help leaves dry,” prolonged cool/damp spread, bed movement alone does not control it, and local advice conditions.
- Do not add a temperature threshold, date, guaranteed frost-safe location, treatment recipe, waiting period, or other farming number.
