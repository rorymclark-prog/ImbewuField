## English lesson body

> Open-pollinated seed from a stable variety can produce similar plants when pollination is properly managed. F1 hybrids come from selected parents. Their saved seed can germinate, but the next generation varies; it may not keep the combination you wanted.
>
> Seed sovereignty includes the knowledge and choices needed to grow, save and share suitable seed. Keep the crop and variety identity with each batch.
>
> Choose healthy plants with useful traits. Start with a crop you know and ask a seed-saving mentor how to manage its pollination and selection.

### Proposed isiZulu body

Imbewu yohlobo oluzinzile oluvulekele impova ingaveza izitshalo ezifanayo uma impova ilawulwa ngendlela efanele. Ama-hybrid e-F1 avela kubazali abakhethwe ngokukhethekile. Imbewu egcinwe kulezi zitshalo ingahluma, kodwa izitshalo zesizukulwane esilandelayo ziyahlukahluka; zingase zingabi nazo zonke izici obuzifuna.

Ubukhosi bembewu buhlanganisa ulwazi nokukhetha okudingekayo ukuze utshale, ulondoloze futhi wabelane ngembewu efanele. Bhala igama lesitshalo nohlobo lwaso ngeqoqo ngalinye.

Khetha izitshalo ezinempilo nezinezici ozifunayo. Qala ngesitshalo osaziyo, bese ubuza umeluleki wokulondoloza imbewu ukuthi impova ingalawulwa kanjani nokuthi izitshalo zizokhethwa kanjani.

## English key points

1. Stable open-pollinated varieties need suitable pollination management.
2. Seed sovereignty means freedom from depending on a seed company every season.
3. Genetic diversity across many saved varieties is real protection against climate unpredictability.
4. Select healthy plants with useful traits; use crop-specific seed-saving guidance.

### Proposed isiZulu key points

1. Izinhlobo ezizinzile ezivulekele impova zidinga ukulawulwa okufanele kwempova.
2. Ubukhosi bembewu busho inkululeko yokunganciki enkampanini yembewu njalo ngesizini.
3. Ukwehlukahlukana kofuzo ezinhlotsheni eziningi zembewu egciniwe kuyisivikelo sangempela ekungaqinisekini kwesimo sezulu.
4. Khetha izitshalo ezinempilo nezinezici ozifunayo; landela isiqondiso sokulondoloza imbewu esiqondene nesitshalo ngasinye.

## Quiz 1

**English question:** Why won't seed saved from a hybrid (F1) tomato breed true next season?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Kungani imbewu egcinwe kutamatisi we-hybrid (F1) ingaqinisekisi ukuthi izitshalo zesizini elandelayo zizofana nesitshalo esingumzali?

| Index | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Hybrid seed is sterile and won't germinate at all | Imbewu ye-hybrid ayikwazi ukumila nhlobo. |
| 1 | Hybrid seed is a one-time genetic cross — its offspring vary unpredictably from the parent | I-hybrid ye-F1 iwumphumela wokuwela imigqa ethile yabazali; inzalo yayo ingahluka ngendlela engabikezeleki esitshalweni esingumzali. **Correct (index 1).** |
| 2 | Hybrids only grow in commercial greenhouses | Ama-hybrid akhula ezindlini zokukhulisela izitshalo zezentengiselwano kuphela. |
| 3 | Hybrid seed loses viability faster in storage | Imbewu ye-hybrid ilahlekelwa amandla okuhluma ngokushesha lapho igcinwa. |

**English rationale:** F1 hybrids are bred by crossing two specific parent lines — their seed carries a mixed, unpredictable genetic recombination, not a stable copy of the parent.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ama-hybrid e-F1 atholakala ngokuwela imigqa ethile yezitshalo ezimbili ezingabazali. Imbewu yawo inenhlanganisela yofuzo engabikezeleki, ayiyona ikhophi ezinzile yesitshalo esingumzali.

## Quiz 2

**English question:** When selecting a parent plant to save seed from, what should guide your choice?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Uma ukhetha isitshalo esingumzali ozogcina kuso imbewu, yini okufanele iqondise ukukhetha kwakho?

| Index | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Whichever plant produced the single largest fruit | Isitshalo esithele isithelo esisodwa esikhulu kunazo zonke. |
| 1 | The healthiest, most disease-free, well-shaped plant, even if not the biggest yielder | Isitshalo esinempilo kakhulu, esinezimpawu ezimbalwa kakhulu zesifo nesimo esihle, ngisho noma singakhiqizi isivuno esikhulu kunazo zonke. **Correct (index 1).** |
| 2 | Whichever plant matured first, regardless of health | Isitshalo esivuthwe kuqala, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi sinempilo yini. |
| 3 | Any plant — selection doesn't affect future seed quality | Noma yisiphi isitshalo — ukukhetha akuthinti ikhwalithi yembewu yesikhathi esizayo. |

**English rationale:** You're selecting for the traits you want to carry forward — health and vigour matter more long-term than one plant's single biggest harvest.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ukhetha izici ofuna ziqhubekele esizukulwaneni esilandelayo. Impilo namandla esitshalo kubalulekile esikhathini eside kunokuvuna isithelo esisodwa esikhulu kunazo zonke.

## Source-pair checks and reviewer questions

- Keep the condition attached to stable open-pollinated varieties: similar plants depend on suitable pollination management. Do not turn this into a promise that every open-pollinated seed will breed true.
- Keep the distinction that saved F1 seed can germinate but its next generation varies. Do not imply sterility, failure to germinate, or a guaranteed specific outcome.
- Check whether “ukuwela imigqa ethile yabazali” communicates the English idea of crossing two selected parent lines. “Inhlanganisela yofuzo” may need a more familiar oral explanation; do not weaken “unpredictable” into “different but predictable.”
- “Ubukhosi bembewu” appears in the current isiZulu narration and deck. Ask a fluent local reviewer whether it communicates “seed sovereignty” clearly to the intended learners, and whether another phrase should be used consistently.
- The English key point “real protection against climate unpredictability” is stronger than the lesson body's conditional discussion. Flag it to the content owner; do not independently change the protected English lesson or translate it as a guarantee beyond the source wording.
- Keep crop and variety identity with every batch. This packet does not establish permission to reproduce, save, or exchange a protected variety. Do not add a blanket permission claim.
- Quiz answer indices remain 1 and 1. Verify that the isiZulu distractors stay incorrect for the same reason as the English options, and that the rationale does not suggest saved hybrid seed fails to germinate.
- Existing slide 5 illustrates both seed types germinating and varied offspring from saved F1 seed. It is illustrative; it does not establish a measured yield, crop-specific result, or universal genetic outcome. Existing slide 8 illustrates selecting several healthy parent plants; no additional number or spacing is prescribed.
