## Lesson body

### English meaning — current source

An indigenous forest fills the space from the highest branches to the roots.

Different plants use the light and moisture available at their level.

A food forest copies this pattern with productive species.

The result is not one crop in one row, but many useful layers growing together.

Think of tall canopy, smaller trees, shrubs and herbaceous plants.

Ground cover protects the surface, root crops grow below it, and climbers use suitable supports.

The heights and spacing depend on the plants and site. These are planning layers, not fixed height bands.

The original Highveld example includes Wild Fig or pecan above lemon, naartjie and black mulberry.

It places Cape gooseberry and Wild Medlar with vegetables, wild garlic, sweet potato and granadilla.

Treat this as a layout example, not permission to plant every species. Check identity, frost tolerance, mature size and local restrictions first.

Young plants need establishment care: moisture checks, weed control and protection from damage.

As plants grow, shade and leaf litter change conditions below them.

Check competition and access. Prune, thin or adjust lower planting when observations call for it; the system does not become care-free on a fixed birthday.

Umphumela awusona isitshalo esisodwa emugqeni owodwa; kuba izingqimba eziningi eziwusizo ezikhula ndawonye.

Izitshalo ezimboza umhlabathi zivikela ingaphezulu lawo, izitshalo zezimpande zikhula ngaphansi kwalo, kanti izitshalo ezikhuphukayo zidinga izisekelo ezifanele.

Ukuphakama nezikhala kuncike ezitshalweni nasendaweni. Lezi yizingxenye zokuhlela, azizona izilinganiso zokuphakama ezimisiwe.

Thatha lokhu njengesibonelo sokuhlela kuphela, hhayi imvume yokutshala zonke lezi zitshalo. Qinisekisa ukuthi isitshalo siyini, siyakwazi yini ukumelana nesithwathwa, sizoba sikhulu kangakanani, nokuthi ayikho yini imingcele yendawo ngaphambi kokutshala.

## Quiz

Keep the English correct answer index: Question 1 = 2; Question 2 = 1.

### Question 1 — weeds competing with young lower-layer plants

**English question:** Weeds are competing strongly with young lower-layer plants. What should guide the next action?

**isiZulu proposal:** Ukhula luncintisana kakhulu nezitshalo ezisencane ezikhula ezingxenyeni ezingezansi. Yini okufanele iqondise isenzo esilandelayo?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Wait until the fifth year | Linda kuze kube unyaka wesihlanu |
| 1 | Add more plants regardless of water | Faka ezinye izitshalo ngaphandle kokubheka ukuthi akhona yini amanzi anele |
| 2 — correct | Check the affected plants and manage competition | Hlola izitshalo ezithintekayo bese ulawula ukuncintisana kwazo nokhula |
| 3 | Assume all seven layers take care of themselves | Cabanga ukuthi zonke izingxenye eziyisikhombisa ziyazinakekela |

**English rationale:** Observe actual competition and plant condition. A fixed establishment calendar cannot tell you which plants need care now.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** Bheka ukuncintisana okwenzekayo nesimo sezitshalo. Ikhalenda elimisiwe lesikhathi sokukhula alikwazi ukukutshela ukuthi yiziphi izitshalo ezidinga ukunakekelwa manje.

### Question 2 — shade, leaf litter and soil moisture

**English question:** How can leaf litter and shade help protect soil moisture?

**isiZulu proposal:** Amaqabunga awileyo nomthunzi kungasiza kanjani ukuvikela umswakama womhlabathi?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | They guarantee access to groundwater | Kuqinisekisa ukuthola amanzi angaphansi komhlaba |
| 1 — correct | They can reduce water loss from the soil surface | Kunganciphisa ukulahleka kwamanzi engaphezulu lomhlabathi |
| 2 | They guarantee higher yield per litre in every system | Kuqinisekisa isivuno esikhulu ngelitha ngalinye kuzo zonke izinhlelo |
| 3 | They remove the need to check watering | Kwenza kungasadingeki ukuhlola ukuthi izitshalo zidinga ukuniselwa yini |

**English rationale:** Shade and suitable mulch can reduce surface evaporation. Plant water demand and establishment needs still require attention.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** Umthunzi ne-mulch efanele kunganciphisa ukuhwamuka kwamanzi engaphezulu lomhlabathi. Isidingo samanzi sezitshalo nokunakekelwa kwazo zisamila kusadinga ukunakwa.
