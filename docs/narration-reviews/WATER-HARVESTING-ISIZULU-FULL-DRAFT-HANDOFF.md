## Reviewer return

experience to check each line, conditional, technical term, answer index,
distractor and rationale against the paired English. In particular check
*swale*, *contour*, *berm*, *spillway*, *first-flush diverter*, gutters, and
the distinction between harvesting water and declaring it fit for drinking or
food-crop use. Return exact edits and their English meaning with reviewer

accepted, revise or hold. Earthworks, dam design and water safety may need
