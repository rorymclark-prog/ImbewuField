### Quiz coverage

The current lesson has two questions. The isiZulu narration has no matching quiz rationales. Preserve each question, option and correct-answer index (1 for both) in `lib/course-modules.ts`.

1. **Bare Highveld field over winter:** correct answer separates wind erosion of dry topsoil from damage to soil structure from spring storm impact. Current rationale overstates the rain mechanism by saying both events directly strip topsoil. The L3 check proposes a coordinated English correction: rain impact damages the surface; water erosion carries loosened soil only under conditions such as runoff. Carry that distinction into isiZulu if the authorized English rationale is updated; until then mark this wording for coordinated review.
2. **Worm-bin liquid:** keep the correct answer that it can contain harmful organisms or substances and dilution is not a safety guarantee. Current rationale says “drainage from unfinished material”; that is narrower than the lesson because leachate is drainage from a worm bin and does not have to come only from unfinished material. The English check proposes correcting it. Do not translate the narrow wording as a fact.

## Proposed isiZulu draft — slides 14–18

Draft only. A reviewer should confirm natural spoken wording for “dissipate raindrop energy,” “runoff,” “legume,” “suitable bacteria,” “residue” and “leachate.”

### Ikhasi 14 — Buka: Umhlabathi Ongenalutho Ne-Mulch

Qhathanisa umhlabathi ongenalutho nomhlabathi ombozwe nge-mulch.

Uma imvula ishaya i-mulch esikhundleni sokushaya umhlabathi ongenalutho, ingxenye enkulu yamandla amaconsi emvula ingadamba. Lo mdwebo ukhombisa umbono kuphela; awuqinisekisi ukuthi amanzi azongena, azogeleza noma azogcinwa kanjani endaweni ngayinye.

### Ikhasi 15 — I-Mulch Ivikela Umhlabathi

Mboza umhlabathi ongenalutho nge-mulch ehlanzekile nefanele, njenge-straw, utshani obomile noma ama-wood chips.

I-mulch inganciphisa ukuhwamuka, ithambise ukushaya kwemvula futhi icindezele ukhula.

Yigcine ingathinti iziqu zezihlahla nezitshalo. Hlola umswakama ngaphansi kwayo bese ulungisa ungqimba. I-mulch eningi ayihlali ingcono.

### Ikhasi 16 — Izitshalo Zokumboza Umhlabathi Phakathi Kwezilimo

Izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi zingawuvikela phakathi kwezilimo eziyinhloko. Khetha ngokwesimo sezulu sendawo, amanzi atholakalayo nesitshalo esizolandela.

Izibonelo ezikule mfundo zifaka ama-oats, ama-lupins, i-sunn hemp ne-cowpea. Hlola ukuthi zifanele yini indawo yakho ngaphambi kokuzihlwanyela.

Izitshalo zomndeni wama-legume zidinga amagciwane afanele nezimo zokukhula ezifanele ukuze zibophe i-nitrogen. Izakhamzimba ezinsaleleni zazo zitholakala njengoba lezo zinsalela zibola.

### Ikhasi 17 — Ama-Worm Castings Noketshezi Oluphuma Emgqonyeni

Amapulazi ezibungu angaguqula izinsalela zokudla ezifanele nezinto zokulala kwezibungu zibe ama-castings. Hlola umgqomo; ungalindeli usuku oluqondile lokuvuna.

Uketshezi oluphuma emgqonyeni lubizwa nge-leachate. Alufani netiye lezibungu elilungiswe ngendlela ethile.

I-leachate ingaba nezinto eziphilayo noma ezinye izinto eziyingozi. Ungayisebenzisi ezitshalweni ezidliwayo futhi ungacabangi ukuthi ukuyixuba namanzi kuyenza iphephe.

### Ikhasi 18 — Vikela Umhlabathi Unyaka Wonke

Insimu yase-Highveld eshiywe ingenalutho ngemva kokuvuna ummbila ibhekana nezingozi ezimbili ezinkulu.

Umoya wasebusika ungathwala umhlabathi ongaphezulu owomile.

Isiphepho sokuqala esinamandla sentwasahlobo singashaya umhlabathi ongenalutho silimaze ukwakheka kwawo. Uma amanzi egeleza, angathwala umhlabathi osuxegisiwe.

Izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi, i-mulch nezinto eziphilayo zisiza ukubamba umhlabathi futhi ziwugcine uphila.
