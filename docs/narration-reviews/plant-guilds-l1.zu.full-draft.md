## Lesson body

### English source — current

These bacteria convert nitrogen from the air into forms the legume can use.

Find nodules on a spare legume plant. Nodulation and growth depend on the plant, suitable bacteria and growing conditions.

Return suitable leafy prunings, fallen leaves and crop residues as mulch. Soil organisms release nutrients during decomposition.

This takes time. A living legume is not an instant fertiliser pipe into the fruit tree.

It is indigenous to KwaZulu-Natal. In suitable warm conditions, manage this short-lived shrub or small tree as a support plant.

Allow room for its growth. Prune for mulch and reassess it when shade or water competition increases.

Sesbania punicea is the invasive red sesbania. Its pods have four lengthwise wings. Confirm identity using reliable botanical guidance.

Check the full name before planting. Respect the agreed project species list, including any restriction on Sesbania sesban.

Use a sunny, freely draining position. It is a short-lived support shrub; frost and waterlogging can limit it.

Decide whether each plant mainly supplies peas or leafy material. Frequent severe cutting can damage it and reduce the food harvest.

Cowpea covers sunny gaps. Pigeon pea provides food and leafy material. Managed Sesbania sesban can supply taller temporary support.

Add suitable flowering and mulch plants. Keep the mango trunk clear and manage light, water and access.

Count woody supports across the spaces between fruit trees. Sow suitable ground cover by area.

There is no universal number per fruit tree. Adjust density to water, soil, plant size and your ability to prune and thin.

### IsiZulu proposal

Ama-rhizobia aguqula i-nitrogen esemoyeni ibe yizinhlobo ezingasetshenziswa yi-legume.

Bheka ama-nodule esitshalweni se-legume esingasetshenziswa ukuhlola. Ukwakheka kwama-nodule nokukhula kuncike esitshalweni, emabhaktheriyeni afanele nasezimweni zokukhula.

Buyisela emhlabathini njenge-mulch amagatsha anamaqabunga afanele athenwe, amaqabunga awileyo nezinsalela zezitshalo. Izidalwa zomhlabathi zikhulula izakhamzimba ngesikhathi sokubola.

Lokhu kuthatha isikhathi. I-legume ephilayo ayilona ipayipi elifaka umanyolo ngokushesha emuthini wezithelo.

I-Sesbania sesban idabuka kwaZulu-Natali. Ezimweni ezifudumele ezifanele, ingaphathwa njengesihlahlana noma umuthi omncane wesikhashana osekelayo.

Yinike indawo yokukhula. Yithene ukuze uthole i-mulch, uphinde uyihlole uma umthunzi noma ukuncintisana ngamanzi kukhula.

I-Sesbania punicea yi-red sesbania, uhlobo oluhlaselayo. Izithelo zayo ezomile zinamaphiko amane ahamba ngobude. Qinisekisa ukuthi uhlobo luni usebenzisa umhlahlandlela othembekile wezitshalo.

Hlola igama eligcwele ngaphambi kokutshala. Landela uhlu lwezitshalo oluvunyelwene ngalo yiphrojekthi, kuhlanganise nanoma yimuphi umkhawulo nge-Sesbania sesban.

Yitshale endaweni enelanga nalapho amanzi ephuma kahle emhlabathini. I-pigeon pea iyisihlahlana esisekelayo sesikhashana; isithwathwa namanzi ame emhlabathini kungayiphazamisa.

Nquma ukuthi isitshalo ngasinye usikhulisela kakhulu uphizi noma amaqabunga. Ukusika kakhulu nangokuvamile kungasilimaza kunciphise nokudla okuvunwayo.

I-cowpea imboza izikhala ezinelanga. I-pigeon pea inikeza ukudla namaqabunga. I-Sesbania sesban elawulwayo ingakhula ibe yinde ibe isitshalo esisekelayo sesikhashana.

Faka izitshalo eziqhakazayo nezamaqabunga e-mulch ezifanele. Gcina isiqu sikamango singamboziwe, ulawule umthunzi, amanzi nendlela yokufinyelela.

Bala izihlahla nezihlahlana ezisekelayo ezindaweni eziphakathi kwezihlahla zezithelo. Hlwayela izitshalo ezifanele zokumboza umhlabathi ngokwendawo.

Alikho inani elilodwa elifanele zonke izihlahla zezithelo. Lungisa ukuminyana ngokwamanzi, umhlabathi, ubukhulu bezitshalo namandla akho okuthena nokunciphisa.

## Quiz

### Question 1 — nutrient release from cut legume material

**English source:** When does nitrogen in cut legume material become available to other plants?

**IsiZulu proposal:** I-nitrogen esezingxenyeni ze-legume ezisikiwe iqala nini ukutholakala kwezinye izitshalo?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Immediately when it is cut | Ngokushesha nje lapho isikwa |
| 1 — correct | As soil organisms decompose the material | Lapho izidalwa zomhlabathi sezibolisa lezo zinsalela |
| 2 | Only when the fruit tree touches the legume | Kuphela lapho umuthi wezithelo uthinta i-legume |
| 3 | It can never become available | Ayisoze yatholakala kwezinye izitshalo |

**English rationale:** Decomposition releases nutrients over time. The rate depends on the material and growing conditions.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Ukubola kukhulula izakhamzimba ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. Ukushesha kwako kuncike ohlotsheni lwezinsalela nasezimweni zokukhula.

### Question 2 — support-plant density

**English source:** What should decide how many support plants you establish?

**IsiZulu proposal:** Yini okufanele inqume inani lezitshalo ezisekelayo ozitshalayo?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | The same fixed number at every site | Inani elifanayo elimisiwe kuzo zonke izindawo |
| 1 — correct | Water, soil, plant size and the care you can provide | Amanzi, umhlabathi, ubukhulu bezitshalo nokunakekela okwaziyo ukukwenza |
| 2 | Always placing every support uphill | Ukubeka zonke izitshalo ezisekelayo endaweni ephakeme ngaso sonke isikhathi |
| 3 | Planting as many trees as will physically fit | Ukutshala izihlahla eziningi ngangokunokwenzeka endaweni ekhona |

**English rationale:** Support plants need resources and management too. Observe growth and competition, then adjust.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Izitshalo ezisekelayo nazo zidinga izinsiza nokunakekelwa. Bheka ukukhula nokuncintisana, bese ulungisa.
