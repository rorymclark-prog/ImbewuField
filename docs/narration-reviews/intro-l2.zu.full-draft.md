## Lesson body

### English meaning (source wording)

David Holmgren set out twelve design principles in *Essence of Permaculture*. Bill Mollison and David Holmgren co-originated the permaculture concept. Three useful starting points for this lesson are: observe and interact — watch your land through a full season before major earthworks; catch and store energy — notice rain, sun and biomass before they leave your property; and use edges and value the marginal — a fence line or strip beside a path can be a useful place to observe.

Others worth knowing: produce no waste (scraps become compost, compost becomes soil), use small and slow solutions (a bucket can irrigate a bed without electricity), and use and value diversity. Hail injury to maize depends on the storm and the crop’s growth stage.

Pick two or three principles that speak to your biggest problem and apply them hard. The rest become obvious as you go.

### Proposed isiZulu

UDavid Holmgren wabeka izimiso eziyishumi nambili zokuklama encwadini ethi *Essence of Permaculture*. UBill Mollison noDavid Holmgren basungula ndawonye umqondo we-permaculture. Kulesi sifundo, singaqala ngamaphuzu amathathu awusizo: bheka futhi ufunde ngokusebenzelana nomhlaba wakho — uwubheke isizini yonke ngaphambi kokwenza umsebenzi omkhulu wokumba noma wokushintsha umhlaba; bamba ugcine amandla — qaphela imvula, ilanga nezinto eziphilayo ezivela emhlabeni wakho ngaphambi kokuba zihambe; futhi sebenzisa imiphetho, wazise nezindawo eziseceleni — ulayini wocingo noma indawo eseceleni kwendlela kungaba indawo ewusizo yokubuka.

Kukhona nezinye izimiso okufanele uzazi: ungachithi lutho (izinsalela ziba umquba, umquba ube umhlabathi); khetha izixazululo ezincane nezihamba kancane (ibhakede linganisela amanzi embhedeni ngaphandle kukagesi); futhi sebenzisa ukwehlukahlukana kwezinto eziphilayo. Ukulimala kommbila yisichotho kuncike ekutheni isiphepho sinjani nokuthi ummbila ukhule kangakanani.

Khetha izimiso ezimbili noma ezintathu ezihambisana nenkinga yakho enkulu, uzisebenzise. Ezinye uzoziqonda njengoba uqhubeka.

**Drafting note:** “catch and store energy” is intentionally left close to existing wording but the lesson's examples are rain, sun and biomass. Confirm that “izinto eziphilayo ezivela emhlabeni wakho” conveys biomass clearly and simply. Confirm the isiZulu name-prefix spelling and grammar in “UBill”; see reviewer questions.

## Key points

### English meaning (source wording)

1. Observe your land for a full season before major earthworks.
2. Catch and store rain, sun, and biomass before they leave your property.
3. Edges and margins can be useful places to observe what grows well.
4. Hail injury to maize depends on the storm and the crop’s growth stage.

4. Ukulimala kommbila yisichotho kuncike esiphephweni nasezingeni lokukhula kommbila.

## Quiz

Correct answer positions below match the English source and must stay unchanged unless the source lesson itself is revised.

### Question 1 — swales

**English question:** A farmer wants to dig swales to harvest rainwater. What should she do first, following “observe and interact”?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Umlimi ufuna ukumba ama-swale ukuze abambe amanzi emvula. Yini okufanele ayenze kuqala, elandela isimiso esithi “bheka futhi ufunde ngokusebenzelana nomhlaba”?

| Position | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Dig immediately after the first good rain | Yimba masinyane ngemva kwemvula yokuqala enhle |
| B — correct | Watch where water flows and pools across at least one wet season | Bheka lapho amanzi egeleza futhi ehlala khona okungenani kuyo yonke isizini yemvula |
| C | Copy a neighbour's swale layout | Kopisha indlela umakhelwane ahlele ngayo ama-swale akhe |
| D | Assume the same swale design fits every site | Cabanga ukuthi uhlelo olulodwa lwama-swale lufanele yonke indawo |

**English rationale:** A wet season shows more than one storm, but observation is only a first step. Check the soil, slope, drainage and safe overflow route with a trained local adviser before digging.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Isizini yemvula ikukhombisa iziphepho ezingaphezu kwesisodwa, kodwa ukubuka kuyisinyathelo sokuqala kuphela. Ngaphambi kokumba, cela umeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe ahlole umhlabathi, umthambeka, ukugeleza kwamanzi nendlela ephephile amanzi angaphuma ngayo.

### Question 2 — integration and chicken access

**English question:** Which layout best applies “integrate rather than segregate”?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Yikuphi ukuhlelwa okusebenzisa kangcono isimiso esithi “hlanganisa kunokuhlukanisa”?

| Position | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Chickens penned far from the garden | Izinkukhu zivalelwe kude nengadi |
| B — correct | Garden, fruit trees and a chicken run arranged so chickens use an empty bed after harvest, then the farmer checks safe management before edible crops return | Ingadi, izihlahla zezithelo nendawo yezinkukhu kuhlelwe ukuze izinkukhu zingene embhedeni ongenalutho ngemva kokuvuna; umlimi ahlole indlela ephephile yokuphatha umbhede ngaphambi kokuba izitshalo ezidliwayo zibuyele kuwo |
| C | Separate paddocks for each crop | Isitshalo ngasinye sibekwe endaweni yaso ehlukile |
| D | All animals kept off the cultivated zone | Zonke izilwane zigcinwa zingangeni endaweni elinywayo |

**English rationale:** Integration puts each element to work for its neighbours — here, chickens clean up pests and add fertility instead of sitting idle in a fixed pen. Fresh manure can carry germs, so check safe management before edible crops return.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ukuhlanganisa kubeka into ngayinye lapho isiza khona ezinye izinto eziseduze nayo. Lapha, izinkukhu zingadla ezinye izinambuzane futhi zengeze umquba, esikhundleni sokuhlala zinganyakazi esibayeni esisodwa. Umquba omusha ungaba namagciwane; ngakho hlola indlela ephephile yokuphatha umbhede ngaphambi kokuba kutshalwe futhi ukudla okuzodliwa.

### Slide 10 — Observe and Interact

**English meaning (source):** Watch your land through a full season before committing to major earthworks. One storm is one moment; a full wet season shows a pattern of where water runs, pools or does not reach. This helps you understand the site, but does not establish that a swale is suitable or safe. Before digging, check soil, slope, drainage and a safe overflow route that will not cause erosion or send damaging water to neighbours; ask a trained local adviser to assess the site. Then ask whether you know where water leaves your land.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Bheka umhlaba wakho isizini yonke ngaphambi kokunquma ukwenza umsebenzi omkhulu wokumba noma wokushintsha umhlaba.

Isiphepho esisodwa sikukhombisa isikhathi esisodwa. Isizini yemvula yonke ikukhombisa iphethini — lapho amanzi egeleza khona, lapho ehlala khona, nalapho engafiki khona.

Lokhu kukusiza uqonde indawo yakho. Kodwa ukubuka kukodwa akusho ukuthi ukumba ama-swale kulungele noma kuphephile kuleyo ndawo. Ngaphambi kokumba, hlola umhlabathi, umthambeka, ukugeleza kwamanzi nendlela ephephile amanzi angaphuma ngayo, engeke ibangele ukuguguleka komhlaba noma ithumele amanzi alimazayo komakhelwane. Cela umeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe ahlole indawo.

[pause]

### Slide 11 — Catch and Store Energy

**English meaning (source):** Harvest rain, sun and biomass before they leave your property. Every one arrives free and leaves free: water runs off, leaves blow away, and sun falls on bare ground. Compare the cost and work with the benefit on your own farm. The related lesson body uses the softer verb “notice”; the spoken slide uses “harvest.” Neither wording supplies a capture method or cost guarantee.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Konke lokhu kufika mahhala futhi kungahamba kungasetshenziswanga.

Amanzi ayageleza aphume, amaqabunga aphephuka ahambe, nelanga likhanyise umhlabathi ongenalutho.

Qhathanisa izindleko nomsebenzi kanye nenzuzo ongayithola epulazini lakho.

### Slide 12 — Use Edges and Value the Marginal

**English meaning (source):** An edge is where two things meet, for example a fence line or a strip beside a path. These can be useful places to observe. Look at what already grows well along your edge. This does not say that every edge is suitable for planting or earthworks.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Lezi zingaba izindawo eziwusizo zokubuka. Bheka ukuthi yini esivele ikhula kahle ngasemngceleni wakho. Lokhu kungakufundisa okuthile ngomhlaba wakho.

### Slide 13 — Use and Value Diversity

**English meaning (source):** Use and value diversity. Hail injury to maize depends on the storm and crop growth stage; diversity does not promise that a crop survives every event. Two other principles: produce no waste, so scraps become compost and compost becomes soil; use small and slow solutions, such as a bucket irrigating a bed without electricity. Then ask what one bad day would cost.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Sebenzisa futhi wazise ukwehlukahlukana kwezinto eziphilayo. Ukulimala kommbila yisichotho kuncike ekutheni isiphepho sinjani nokuthi ummbila ukhule kangakanani. Lesi simiso asithembisi ukuthi isitshalo sizosinda kuzo zonke izenzakalo.

Ezinye izimiso ezimbili ongazazi: ungachithi lutho — izinsalela ziba umquba, umquba ube umhlabathi. Khetha izixazululo ezincane nezihamba kancane — ibhakede linganisela amanzi embhedeni ngaphandle kukagesi.

### Slide 14 — Integrate Rather Than Segregate

**English meaning (source):** Place each element where it works for its neighbours. A garden, fruit trees and chicken run can be arranged so chickens rotate through beds after harvest. Keep chickens away from crops being harvested for food. Fresh manure can carry germs. Ask an extension adviser how to manage the bed safely before edible crops return. Chickens can clean up pests and add fertility rather than sit idle in a fixed pen. The same elements fenced apart do only their own job. Ask learners to choose two or three relevant principles and apply them.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Beka into ngayinye lapho isiza khona izinto eziseduze nayo.

Ingadi, izihlahla zezithelo nendawo yezinkukhu kungahlelwa ukuze izinkukhu zijikeleze emibhedeni ngemva kokuvuna. Gcina izinkukhu zingasondeli ezitshalweni ezivunelwa ukudliwa. Umquba omusha ungaba namagciwane. Ngaphambi kokutshala futhi ukudla okuzodliwa, cela umeluleki wezolimo akutshele ukuthi umbhede ungaphathwa kanjani ngokuphepha.

Izinkukhu zingasiza ekudleni ezinye izinambuzane nasekufakeni umquba, esikhundleni sokuhlala zinganyakazi esibayeni esisodwa.

Lezi zinto ezintathu uma zibiyelwe zahlukaniswa, ngayinye yenza umsebenzi wayo kuphela.
