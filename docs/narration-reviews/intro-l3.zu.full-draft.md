# Introduction L3 isiZulu — full review draft

**UNREVIEWED AI DRAFT. Do not copy into the learner app, record, or publish.**
This is a complete proposed alignment for the current English L3 body, key
points, both quiz questions and slides 15–22 (including the module field task).
It is not a fluent-language or local farming review. A first-language isiZulu
farmer or agricultural educator must correct spoken register and meaning.

Sources of meaning: `intro-permaculture-l3` in `lib/course-modules.ts`, slides
15–22 of `docs/narration/intro-permaculture.en.md`, and the
[L3 comparison packet](intro-l3.zu.review.md). The older isiZulu narration has
categorical herb-harvest and regional wind/rain claims; do not restore them.
The English's north-west wind is an **observed example**, not a Highveld rule.

## Proposed learner lesson

### Title

Ama-Zone Nama-Sector: Ukuhlela Ipulazi Ngokuthi Uhamba Kangaki

### Body

Ama-zone nama-sector angakusiza unciphise ukuhamba nomsebenzi ongadingekile.
Ama-zone asuka ku-0 aye ku-5, ngokuthi uvakashela indawo kangaki. I-Zone 0
yindlu. Kulesi sibonelo, i-Zone 1 iseduze kwendlu futhi inezinto ozikha
kaningi, njengamakhambi nemifino yesaladi. I-Zone 2 yingadi enkulu
nendawo yezinkukhu, evakashelwa kanye noma kabili ngosuku. I-Zone 3 yinsimu
enkulu, evakashelwa masonto onke. I-Zone 4 i-semi-wild: izihlahla
zezithelo nokudla kwezilwane okudinga ukunakekelwa ngezikhathi ezithile.
I-Zone 5 ishiywa isesimweni semvelo.

Ama-sector akhombisa amandla afika evela ngaphandle kwendawo yakho:
ilanga, umoya, imvula, izikhukhula nomlilo. Bheka ukuthi umoya onamandla
uvame ukuvela ngakuphi **epulazini lakho**. Amarekhodi esiteshi sezulu
esiseduze angakusiza uhlole uhlangothi lomoya. Bheka lapho amanzi emvula
engena khona nalapho egeleza khona emhlabeni wakho. Dweba imicibisholo
yalokho okubone ngempela.

Dweba ama-zone nama-sector ephepheni. Lokho kungaba isisekelo sokuqala
sohlelo lwakho.

### Key points

1. Kulesi sibonelo, i-Zone 1 iseduze kwendlu futhi inamakhambi
   akhiwa kaningi.
2. Ama-zone ahlela umsebenzi ngokuthi kudingeka uvakashele indawo kangaki.
3. Ama-sector akhombisa amandla afika evela ngaphandle: ilanga, umoya,
   amanzi emvula, izikhukhula nomlilo.
4. Umdwebo olula wama-zone nama-sector uyisiqalo sokuhlela.

## Proposed quiz — translate and review every option

The option order and answer index must stay aligned with the current English
quiz. The proposed wording below is not approved learner text.

1. **Question:** Utshala amakhambi ku-Zone 3, insimu enkulu ekude
   nendlu. Iyiphi inkinga engavela?

   - A: Amakhambi azokhula abe makhulu kakhulu.
   - **B (correct):** Ukuhamba ibanga elide kungasho ukuthi uwakha noma
     uwahlola kancane.
   - C: Amakhambi azoxubana ngempova nezitshalo eziyinhloko.
   - D: I-Zone 3 inelanga eliningi kakhulu kumakhambi.

   **Rationale:** Beka isitshalo osikha kaningi eduze kwendlela oyihamba
   nsuku zonke. Umbhede okude wengeza ukuhamba futhi ungahlolwa kancane.

2. **Question:** Ubonile ukuthi umoya olimazayo uvela enyakatho-ntshonalanga
   epulazini lase-Highveld. I-windbreak kufanele ibe kuphi?

   - A: Emngceleni oseningizimu-mpumalanga.
   - **B (correct):** Emngceleni osenyakatho-ntshonalanga, phakathi komoya
     nezitshalo.
   - C: Maphakathi nendawo yakho.
   - D: Ama-windbreak awadingeki ngoba umoya uyashintsha ngesizini.

   **Rationale:** I-windbreak isebenza ngokuma phakathi komoya
   nendawo ongayifuni ilimale. Ibekwa ohlangothini umoya **obonwe ngempela**
   ukuthi uvela kulo. Lesi sibonelo asisho ukuthi wonke amapulazi ase-Highveld
   anomoya osuka enyakatho-ntshonalanga.

## Proposed spoken script — slides 15–22

### Ikhasi 15 — Ama-Zone: Ukuhlela Ngokuthi Uvakashela Kangaki

Ama-zone asuka ku-0 aye ku-5. Akhuluma ngokuhamba kwakho, hhayi ngezicingo.

I-Zone 0 yindlu. Kulesi sibonelo, i-Zone 1 iseduze kwendlu futhi inamakhambi
nemifino yesaladi oyikha kaningi.

I-Zone 2 yingadi enkulu nendawo yezinkukhu, evakashelwa kanye noma kabili
ngosuku. I-Zone 3 yinsimu enkulu, evakashelwa masonto onke.

[pause]

I-Zone 4 i-semi-wild: izihlahla zezithelo nokudla kwezilwane
okudinga ukunakekelwa ngezikhathi ezithile. I-Zone 5 ishiywa isesimweni
semvelo.

### Ikhasi 16 — Gcina Izinto Ozikha Kaningi Ziseduze

Amakhambi owakha kaningi kulula ukuwanakekela uma eseduze kwendlu.

Uma ukude nawo, ukuhamba ibanga elide kungakwenza uphuthelwe ukuwavakashela.
Angase anganakekelwa kahle.

Cabanga ngezindlela ozihamba nsuku zonke.

[pause]

Yini oyivakashela kaningi engase isondezwe?

### Ikhasi 17 — Ama-Zone Asiza Ukuhlela Umsebenzi

Yilona iphuzu lalo msebenzi.

Ama-zone asiza ukuhlela izindawo ngokomsebenzi wakho: ukuhamba, ukuthwala
nokuhlola okugcwalisa usuku.

Into ekudinga kaningi ingabekwa eduze. Lokho kunganciphisa ukuhamba
nomsebenzi phakathi nesizini.

[pause]

Yima emnyango wasekhishini. Yini ongayifinyelela kalula?

### Ikhasi 18 — Ama-Sector: Izinto Ezifika Zivela Ngaphandle

Ama-zone aqala ngaphakathi kwendawo yakho. Ama-sector akhombisa amandla
ezivela ngaphandle kwayo.

Ilanga, umoya, imvula, izikhukhula nomlilo kungafika kunqamule umngcele
wakho, noma ungakuhlelelanga.

Bheka ukuthi umoya onamandla uvela ngakuphi epulazini lakho. Amarekhodi
esiteshi sezulu esiseduze angakusiza uhlole uhlangothi lomoya. Bheka lapho
amanzi emvula engena khona nalapho egeleza khona emhlabeni wakho.

[pause]

Isimo sezulu esikulimazayo sivela ngakuphi?

### Ikhasi 19 — Buka: I-Windbreak Phakathi Komoya Nezitshalo

Bheka lesi sibonelo. Umoya uvela enyakatho-ntshonalanga.

Izihlahla nezihlahlana zimi phakathi kwalowo moya nezitshalo.

Omunye umoya udlula phakathi kwe-windbreak. Omunye udlula phezu kwayo noma
uzungeze iziphetho zayo.

I-windbreak inganciphisa ijubane lomoya ngemuva kwayo. Indlela eyakhiwe
futhi enakekelwa ngayo ibalulekile.

Endaweni yakho, qala ngokubheka umoya olimazayo ngaphambi kokukhetha lapho
uzotshala khona. Lesi sithombe asilona uhlelo lokutshala ipulazi lakho.

### Ikhasi 20 — Dweba Ukuze Uqale Uhlelo

Dweba umngcele wakho. Maka indlu. Dweba izindawo ezisuka eduze ziye kude
ngokuthi uvakashela kangaki.

Bese udweba imicibisholo engena ivela ngaphandle, ekhombisa ilanga,
umoya, umlilo namanzi.

Lelo phepha liyisisekelo sohlelo lwakho. Okunye okulesi sifundo kungakhiwa
phezu kwalo.

[pause]

Akudingeki libe lihle kakhulu. Kudingeka likhombise indawo yakho ngokunembile.

### Ikhasi 21 — Umsebenzi Wasensimini

Dweba ama-zone nama-sector akho ephepheni elilodwa.

Qala ngokumaka i-Zone 0 ne-Zone 1, bese uqhubeka ngaphandle kuze kufike
emngceleni wendawo yakho.

Faka umcibisholo wento ngayinye efika ivela ngaphandle, ubhale ukuthi
ivela ngakuphi.

Thatha isithombe somdwebo.

### Ikhasi 22 — Isenzo Sasensimini

Hamba uyohlola umdwebo wakho emhlabeni wangempela.

Yima emnyango wasekhishini ubheke lokho okuse-Zone 1 namuhla.

Thola into eyodwa etshalwe kude uma uqhathanisa nokuthi uyisebenzisa
kaningi kangakanani. Bhala ukuthi ingabekwa kuphi.

Bese ubuza umakhelwane osekhulile ukuthi umoya olimazayo uvela
ngakuphi. Qhathanisa impendulo yakhe nomcibisholo wakho.

## Review and release questions

- Does the spoken isiZulu distinguish **example** visit frequencies from rules
  for every farm? Are `amakhambi` and the borrowed terms `zone`, `sector`,
  `semi-wild` and `Highveld` natural and clear for the intended learners?
- Does the windbreak quiz make the north-west direction conditional on an
  **observed** wind, with no regional direction prescription? Is the borrowed
  term `i-windbreak` natural, or is there an established local alternative?
- Does slide 18 distinguish wind direction from **observed water flow**, so no
  learner infers a rainwater arrow from a prevailing-wind arrow?
- Does slide 19 preserve possible airflow through, over and around the break,
  and the conditional shelter effect? Does it clearly stop the example being
  used as a planting plan?
- Compare every quiz option with English and check that neither incorrect
  option sounds accidentally correct in isiZulu. Record reviewer name,
  relevant language/farming experience, date and exact corrections.

Until that review is documented, retain the English-only learner lesson and
audio. No media, species, spacing, direction rule or plant list is authorized
by this draft.
