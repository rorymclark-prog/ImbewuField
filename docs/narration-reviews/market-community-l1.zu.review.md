# Market Gardening & Community L1 — isiZulu review packet

**Status: unpublished review draft, checked 23 September 2026.** This packet
compares the current English lesson, quiz and narration with the existing
isiZulu draft. It is a copy and claim review, not an isiZulu translation or a
fluent-language review. Reconcile the draft before publication or recording,
then have a fluent isiZulu-speaking farmer or agricultural educator check the
meaning and learner clarity. No human, farmer, practitioner, learner or fluent
language approval is claimed.

L1 is learner lesson slides 4–8. Shared module outcomes and the farm-record
assignment are also noted where they affect this lesson's recordkeeping and
pricing. English lesson body and quiz: `lib/course-modules.ts`,
`market-community-l1`. English narration: `docs/narration/market-community.en.md`.
Matching English transcript: `lib/course-transcripts.ts`. IsiZulu draft:
`docs/narration/market-community.zu.md`.

## Quiz and media comparison

The L1 quiz in `lib/course-modules.ts` treats the price pair as the same
teaching example: the keyed action is to review the price, costs and whether a
different crop would return better, with customer demand considered. It does not
key “raise the price,” stop growing the crop, or assume a subsidy. The second
quiz uses a record showing a June/July food gap and asks the grower to work
backward from it using suitable local crops and harvest time. It does not
prescribe March/April planting. The isiZulu narration file has no quiz
translation; retain these boundaries if quiz translation is prepared.

The deployed L1 check is
[`market-l1-current-lesson-check.md`](../media/studies-animation-quality/market-l1-current-lesson-check.md).
The replacement slide 4 is a static four-destination record graphic; the
locally drawn film remains withdrawn and the player has no video. The English
audio manifest still titles slide 4 “Watch,” but this is not evidence of a
current isiZulu video. The current course audio and registered transcript are
English-only; no isiZulu audio, transcript or deck is registered.

## Source anchors and recordkeeping boundary

- The [Department of Basic Education Agricultural Studies notes](https://www.education.gov.za/Portals/0/Documents/Manuals/Occupational%20Subjects/Grade%208/Grade%208%20Agricultural%20Studies%20-%20Notes.pdf?ver=2023-11-22-133038-000)
  describe farm records as ongoing records for crops, animals, machinery and
  finances and name source documents as records of transactions. This is a
  teaching reference, not a new requirement in the current Market Gardening
  English lesson.
- The English source and the current [L1 lesson check](../media/studies-animation-quality/market-l1-current-lesson-check.md)
  confirm the price figures are expressly illustrative and support comparing
  costs, price, customer demand and harvest destinations; they do not support
  a real-market quote or guaranteed sale.
- No external current produce price or planting calendar is used here. Do not
  replace the illustrative figures or the grower's own food-gap record with
  invented local values.

## Review dependency

After reconciling the draft to the current English script, lesson and quiz, a
fluent isiZulu-speaking farmer or agricultural educator should check idiom,
recordkeeping terms and whether the distinctions among harvested amount,
destination, costs and cash are clear to learners. This packet makes no
accounting, tax, market-price or language approval claim.
