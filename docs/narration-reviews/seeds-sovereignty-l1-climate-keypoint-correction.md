# Seeds L1 climate keypoint correction

The original English keypoint said: “Genetic diversity across many saved varieties is real protection against climate unpredictability.” This made a categorical promise about protection at the farm level.

The English source now reads: “Crop and variety diversity can support adaptation to climate change when varieties are suited to local conditions.” The learner-visible isiZulu draft says: “Ukuhlukahluka kwezitshalo nezinhlobo zazo kungasiza ekuzivumelaniseni nokushintsha kwesimo sezulu, uma izinhlobo zifanele izimo zendawo.”

This is a narrow qualification of one keypoint. It does not change the lesson body, quiz wording, answer indexes, or rationales. The isiZulu wording remains an unreviewed draft and needs fluent-language and farming review.

FAO's International Treaty on Plant Genetic Resources for Food and Agriculture explains that adapting crop varieties to local ecological conditions can reduce risks from climate change, while noting the need to characterize, evaluate and make adapted germplasm available. That supports a conditional adaptation claim, not a guarantee of protection: [FAO, Sustainable Use: Overview](https://www.fao.org/plant-treaty/areas-of-work/sustainable-use/overview/en).
