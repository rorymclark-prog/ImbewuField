## Lesson body

### English meaning — current source

One kind of swale is a level trench on contour. It slows and spreads runoff so some water can soak into suitable soil. Other swales are designed with a slight, controlled grade to carry excess water slowly to a safe outlet. Which approach fits your land depends on the soil, slope, drainage and storm flow. Have a trained local adviser check the line, overflow and receiving point before digging.

The excavated soil forms a berm on the downhill side, where trees can be planted when the site design is suitable.

Trees planted there may draw on moisture stored in the soil after rain, depending on the site.

Heavy rain can fill a swale faster than water soaks into the soil. Plan a safe overflow before digging.

The route must not erode the slope or send damaging water to a neighbour. A downstream swale or dam must be able to receive it safely.

Ask a trained local adviser to assess the soil, slope and storm flow. A picture is not a construction design.

Slope alone does not tell you whether a swale is suitable. Soil, drainage, unstable ground and the water arriving from upslope all matter.

Keep good ground cover. Get a local assessment before digging on steep, wet or unstable land. Grass barriers and terraces also need a design suited to the site.

### isiZulu proposal

Amanye ama-swale aklanywa abe nomthambeka omncane olawulwayo, ohambisa amanzi amaningi kancane aye endaweni ephephile yokuphuma. Ukuthi iyiphi indlela efanele umhlaba wakho kuncike emhlabathini, emthambekeni, ekugelezeni kwamanzi nasekugelezeni kwamanzi ezikhukhula zemvula. Ngaphambi kokumba, cela umeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe ahlole umugqa, indlela yokuchichima nendawo ezokwamukela amanzi.

Umhlabathi okhishiwe wakha i-berm ohlangothini olungaphansi komthambeka. Izihlahla zingatshalwa kuyo uma ukwakheka kwendawo kuvuma.

Izihlahla ezitshalwe lapho zingasebenzisa umswakama ogcinwe emhlabathini ngemva kwemvula; lokhu kuncike endaweni.

Imvula enkulu ingagcwalisa i-swale ngokushesha kunokuba amanzi angene emhlabathini. Hlela indlela ephephile yokuchichima ngaphambi kokumba.

Indlela yamanzi akumele igugule umthambeka noma ithumele amanzi alimazayo komakhelwane. I-swale noma idamu elingezansi kufanele likwazi ukuwamukela amanzi ngokuphepha.

Cela umeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe ahlole umhlabathi, umthambeka nokugeleza kwamanzi emvula enamandla. Isithombe asiwona umklamo wokwakha.

Umthambeka wodwa awukutsheli ukuthi i-swale iyayifanelekela yini indawo. Umhlabathi, ukugeleza kwamanzi, umhlaba ongazinzile namanzi avela phezulu emthambekeni konke kubalulekile.

Gcina umhlabathi umbozekile. Thola ukuhlolwa kwendawo ngaphambi kokumba emhlabeni omqansa, omanzi noma ongazinzile. Imigoqo yotshani nama-terrace nakho kudinga ukwakhelwa indawo efanele.

### Question 1 — uneven water in a planned level contour swale

**English question:** A farmer planned a level contour swale. After heavy rain, one end holds most of the water. What should the farmer check before changing the earthwork?

**isiZulu proposal:** Umlimi uhlele i-swale elandela umugqa we-contour olinganayo. Ngemva kwemvula enkulu, amanzi amaningi aqoqana kolunye uhlangothi. Yini okufanele ayihlole ngaphambi kokushintsha umsebenzi womhlaba?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Whether the trench can be made deeper without a site check | Ukuthi umsele ungajuliswa yini ngaphandle kokuhlola indawo |
| 1 — correct | The intended design and measured levels with a trained local adviser; an unintended low point may be present | Uhlelo oluhlosiwe namazinga alinganisiwe kanye nomeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe; kungenzeka kube nendawo ephansi ebingahlosiwe |
| 2 | Whether a new dam at the lowest point will catch every overflow | Ukuthi idamu elisha endaweni ephansi lizobamba konke yini ukuchichima |
| 3 | Whether the soil should be compacted to stop all infiltration | Ukuthi umhlabathi kufanele ucindezelwe yini ukuze amanzi angangeni nhlobo |

**English rationale:** A level contour design should spread water along its length. Uneven filling may indicate an unintended low point, but some swales are intentionally graded to a safe outlet. Check the actual design, soil and overflow route before altering it.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** I-swale eklanywe ukuba ilingane ku-contour kufanele isabalalise amanzi ngobude bayo. Ukugcwala ngokungalingani kungakhombisa indawo ephansi ebingahlosiwe, kodwa amanye ama-swale aklanywa ngamabomu abe nomthambeka oholela endaweni ephephile yokuphuma. Hlola uhlelo lwangempela, umhlabathi nendlela yokuchichima ngaphambi kokulushintsha.

**English question:** A farmer wants to control erosion on steep land. What should she do before digging?

**isiZulu proposal:** Umlimi ufuna ukulawula ukuguguleka komhlabathi endaweni ewummango. Yini okufanele ayenze ngaphambi kokumba?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Standard swales dug as deep as possible | Ama-swale ajwayelekile ambiwe ajule ngangokunokwenzeka |

| 2 | A large dam at the bottom to catch all runoff | Idamu elikhulu ezansi ukuze libambe wonke amanzi agelezayo |

**English rationale:** Slope alone is not enough to choose an earthwork. Soil, drainage, stability and storm flow must also be assessed.

## Narration — exact L1 range: slides 3–9

### Slide 3 — Two Swale Designs

**English meaning:** A level contour swale is one design for slowing runoff. Its trench follows a level contour so water can spread and soak into suitable soil. Some swales have a slight deliberate grade and a designed safe outlet. Soil, slope, drainage and storm flow determine suitability; ask a trained local adviser to assess before digging.

**isiZulu proposal:** I-swale elandela umugqa olinganayo we-contour ingenye yezindlela zokubambezela amanzi agelezayo. Umsele wayo ulandela umugqa olinganayo ukuze amanzi asabalale futhi angene emhlabathini ofanele. Amanye ama-swale aba nomthambeka omncane oklanywe ngamabomu nendawo ephephile yokuphuma. Umhlabathi, umthambeka, ukugeleza kwamanzi nokugeleza kwamanzi emvula enamandla kunquma ukuthi iyiphi indlela efanele. Cela umeluleki wendawo oqeqeshiwe ahlole indawo ngaphambi kokumba.

### Slide 4 — A Swale May Let Water Soak In

**English meaning:** The picture shows a level contour swale as a concept. Water may spread along the trench and soak in where the soil allows it. The picture does not show how deep moisture reaches on your land. Check soil, slope and overflow before building.

**isiZulu proposal:** Isithombe sibonisa njengomqondo i-swale elandela umugqa olinganayo we-contour. Amanzi angasabalala emseleni futhi angene lapho umhlabathi ukuvumela khona. Isithombe asikhombisi ukuthi umswakama ufika ujule kangakanani emhlabeni wakho. Hlola umhlabathi, umthambeka nendlela yokuchichima ngaphambi kokwakha.

### Slide 5 — The Downhill Berm

**English meaning:** Excavated soil forms a berm on the downhill side. Trees may be planted there when the site design is suitable. Trees may draw on moisture stored in nearby soil after rain; results vary by site.

A downstream swale or dam must be able to receive it safely. A trained local adviser should assess soil, slope and storm flow. A picture is not a construction design.

### Slide 7 — Assess the Overflow Before Digging

**English meaning:** A swale needs a planned overflow route for heavy rain. Check it before digging. Water may go to another swale or a dam only if a site assessment shows the route, outlet and receiver can take the flow without damage.

**isiZulu proposal:** I-swale idinga indlela yokuchichima ehleliwe uma kunemvula enkulu. Yihlole ngaphambi kokumba. Amanzi angaya kwenye i-swale noma edamini kuphela uma ukuhlolwa kwendawo kukhombisa ukuthi indlela, indawo yokuphuma nendawo eyamukelayo kungawamukela amanzi ngaphandle komonakalo.
