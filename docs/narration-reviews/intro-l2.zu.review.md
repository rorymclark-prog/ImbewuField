# Introduction L2 isiZulu review packet — principles, swales, diversity and chicken access

**Status: REVIEW ONLY — the isiZulu draft is not approved for learners or recording.**

This packet compares the existing Introduction L2 isiZulu narration (slides 9–14) with the current English correction in `docs/narration/intro-permaculture.en.md` and `lib/course-modules.ts`. It identifies semantic gaps and the meanings a fluent South African isiZulu reviewer should confirm. It does not claim fluent isiZulu, farmer approval, or local agronomic approval. No learner-facing isiZulu or media was changed.

## Sources and boundaries

- Current English narration: `docs/narration/intro-permaculture.en.md`, slides 9–14.
- Current English lesson and quiz: `lib/course-modules.ts`, lesson `intro-permaculture-l2`.
- Existing isiZulu draft: `docs/narration/intro-permaculture.zu.md`, slides 9–14. Its appendix already says the whole script needs first-language farming review; the borrowing list still needs that review.
- Holmgren’s primary text, [*Essence of Permaculture*](https://www.permacultureprinciples.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/02/Essence_of_Pc_EN.pdf), presents his set of twelve design principles. The accompanying [official principles site](https://permacultureprinciples.com/about/) identifies the original concepts as developed by David Holmgren and identifies him as a co-originator with Bill Mollison.
- For the earthwork safeguard, FAO’s [water-harvesting guidance](https://www.fao.org/4/u3160e/u3160e07.htm) says design depends on water quantity, slope and soil, describes establishing the slope, and discusses erosion at overflow points. Its [soil and water conservation guidance](https://www.fao.org/4/t0321e/t0321e-11.htm) describes erosion protection at spillways and limits some structures to gentle slopes. This supports the corrected lesson’s need for assessment; it does not establish that every swale is suitable or provide a site-specific design.
- South Africa’s Department of Agriculture, Land Reform and Rural Development [fresh-fruit-and-vegetable hygiene code](https://www.nda.gov.za/images/Branches/AgricProducHealthFoodSafety/food-safety-and-quality-assurance/food-safety/other-related-legislation/codes-of-hygiene-practice-for-fresh-fruit-and-vegetables.pdf) says manure and natural fertilisers should be managed to limit contamination and that treatment should be selected to reduce or eliminate pathogens. Penn State Extension’s [home-garden food-safety guidance](https://extension.psu.edu/home-garden-food-safety-protecting-produce-from-contamination) specifically warns that poultry can carry pathogens and their droppings can contaminate soil, tools and produce; it says fresh poultry manure should not be applied directly to growing crops. These sources support preserving the new food-safety caution, not inventing a waiting period or a manure-treatment recipe.

## Quiz alignment and coverage

The current L2 lesson has two English quiz questions in `lib/course-modules.ts`; no corresponding isiZulu quiz text or rationale appears in the narration draft. The corrected English quiz remains in that file, so any future isiZulu quiz needs a separate fluent review against the **current** wording:

1. **Swales question, correct option index 1.** Preserve: observe where water flows and pools across at least one wet season. Its rationale now says observation is only a first step; check soil, slope, drainage and a safe overflow route with a trained local adviser before digging. The old rationale tied seasonal observation directly to what swale design must match, which overstates what observation proves.
2. **Integration question, correct option index 1.** The correct option now specifies an empty bed after harvest and checking safe management before edible crops return. The rationale retains pest/fertility benefits but says fresh manure can carry germs. The old option only said chickens rotate through beds after harvest and its rationale presented pest control and fertility without the food-safety condition.

The quiz currently exists only in English in the lesson source. Confirm whether isiZulu learners see this English quiz; if an isiZulu version is later authored, translate the question, all four options, and rationale together, retain each correct index, and do not weaken the qualifications above.
