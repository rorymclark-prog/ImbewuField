## What the reviewer needs to return

1. Mark every sentence or term that sounds unnatural or unclear when spoken
   to an isiZulu-first smallholder learner. Provide replacement wording.
2. Check each English meaning against the proposed isiZulu, especially every
   **if**, **may**, **can**, **before** and **only then** condition. Check all
   quiz distractors as well as the correct option and rationale.
3. Review the safety questions in each linked draft with local farming
   knowledge. If the **English** source needs to change, record that as a
   separate proposed source correction rather than silently changing the
   translation.
4. Record reviewer name, first-language/region and relevant farming or
   education experience, date, exact changes, and a per-lesson decision:
   accepted, revise, or hold. Name any part not reviewed.
