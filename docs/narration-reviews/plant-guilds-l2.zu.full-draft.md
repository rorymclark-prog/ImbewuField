## Lesson body

### English source — current

The clip shows a branch cut: the support tree remains standing. Leave enough healthy foliage for the plant to recover.

Match cutting to the species. Avoid frequent severe cuts on pigeon pea, especially when growing it for peas.

Mulch protects the surface, helps conserve moisture and returns organic material.

Leave access for watering and inspection. Cut material into manageable pieces and keep observing moisture and decomposition.

Obtain the correct cultivar. Bocking 14 does not spread by viable seed, but root pieces can regrow.

Place it where it has room and sufficient moisture. Cut leaves as it recovers; do not crowd the young fruit tree.

Many ladybirds eat aphids; some parasitoid wasps attack crop pests. Flowering members such as African basil can add resources.

Watch which insects visit and whether damage changes. A flowering plant does not guarantee pest control.

Tulbaghia violacea has narrow leaves and lilac flowers. Place a clump where it has light and room to grow.

Observe visiting insects. Do not promise that a ring of wild garlic will repel pests or cure an outbreak.

### IsiZulu proposal

Ividiyo ibonisa igatsha lisikwa, kodwa isihlahla esisekelayo sihlala simile. Shiya amaqabunga anempilo anele ukuze isitshalo silulame.

Thena ngokohlobo lwesitshalo. Gwema ukusika i-pigeon pea kakhulu futhi kaningi, ikakhulukazi uma uyitshalele ukuvuna uphizi.

I-mulch ivikela ubuso bomhlabathi, isize ukugcina umswakama futhi ibuyisele izinto eziphilayo emhlabathini.

Shiya indlela yokunisela nokuhlola. Sika izinsalela zibe izingcezu ezilawulekayo, uqhubeke ubheka umswakama nokubola kwazo.

Thola i-cultivar efanele. I-Bocking 14 ayisakazeki ngembewu ekwazi ukuhluma, kodwa izingcezu zezimpande zingaphinde zikhule.

Yibeke lapho inendawo nomswakama owanele khona. Sika amaqabunga njengoba isitshalo silulama; ungaminyanisi umuthi omncane wezithelo.

Ama-ladybird amaningi adla ama-aphid; amanye ama-parasitoid wasp ahlasela izinambuzane ezilimaza izitshalo. Izitshalo eziqhakazayo njenge-African basil zinganikeza lezi zinambuzane izinto ezizidingayo.

Bheka ukuthi yiziphi izinambuzane ezivakashayo nokuthi umonakalo uyashintsha yini. Isitshalo esiqhakazayo asiqinisekisi ukulawulwa kwezinambuzane ezilimazayo.

I-Tulbaghia violacea inamaqabunga amancane nezimbali ezinsomi ngokukhanyayo. Beka isixha lapho sithola ukukhanya nendawo yokukhula.

Bheka izinambuzane ezivakashayo. Ungathembisi ukuthi indilinga ye-wild garlic izoxosha izinambuzane ezilimazayo noma iqede ukuhlasela kwazo.

### Question 1 — what the branch-cutting clip shows

**English source:** What does the branch-cutting clip show?

**IsiZulu proposal:** Ividiyo yokusika igatsha ibonisani?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Removing the whole support tree | Ukususa sonke isihlahla esisekelayo |
| 1 — correct | Pruning a retained support tree for light and mulch | Ukuthena isihlahla esisekelayo esishiywayo ukuze kutholakale ukukhanya ne-mulch |
| 2 | Harvesting the fruit tree | Ukuvuna izithelo emthini wezithelo |
| 3 | Proof that root competition has stopped | Ubufakazi bokuthi ukuncintisana kwezimpande sekuphelile |

**English rationale:** A branch falls, while the support tree remains standing. That is pruning and chop-and-drop.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Igatsha liwa, kodwa isihlahla esisekelayo sihlala simile. Lokhu ukuthena bese kushiywa okusikiwe emhlabathini njenge-mulch.

### Question 2 — assessing flowering plants for helpful insects

**English source:** How should you assess flowering plants used to support helpful insects?

**IsiZulu proposal:** Kufanele uzihlole kanjani izitshalo eziqhakazayo ezitshalwe ukusiza izinambuzane eziwusizo?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Assume they will eliminate pests | Cabanga ukuthi zizoqeda izinambuzane ezilimazayo |
| 1 — correct | Observe insect visitors and changes in crop damage | Bheka izinambuzane ezivakashayo nokushintsha komonakalo ezitshalweni |
| 2 | Remove all flowers before they open | Susa zonke izimbali zingakavuleki |
| 3 | Count every flowering plant as a nitrogen fixer | Bala zonke izitshalo eziqhakazayo njengezibopha i-nitrogen |

**English rationale:** Flowers can supply resources, but their presence does not guarantee pest control.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Izimbali zinganikeza izinambuzane izinto ezizidingayo, kodwa ukuba khona kwazo akuqinisekisi ukulawulwa kwezinambuzane ezilimazayo.
