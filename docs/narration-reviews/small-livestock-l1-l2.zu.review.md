## Scope of what appears aligned

Across the remaining L1–L2 narration, the draft generally follows the current English lesson sequence and preserves its important limits: some insects/weed seeds (not all), conditional animal effects, suitable feed and care, no guaranteed fertility or yield, and asking experienced local advisers. This is a text comparison only. A first-language isiZulu farmer and poultry/beekeeping practitioner still need to check meaning, register, dialect, borrowed terms and whether the safety steps are clear when heard aloud.
