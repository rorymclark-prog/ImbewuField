The English source note calls for: (1) treating water leaving the property as an observation, not automatically a loss to prevent; excess water may need a safe escape route; (2) removing the unverified claim that two people can trace two hectares in a morning and the blanket “no surveyor” assurance; (3) keeping the A-frame as a preliminary contour-observation tool, distinct from designing or digging earthworks, which requires site assessment and suitable local technical advice; and (4) keeping the memorable water prompt while making construction and overflow site-specific. It supplies no new numeric threshold.

## Related English lesson and quiz alignment

The corrected English lesson body and key points distinguish observing water and contour height from designing an earthwork. The two-hectare morning rate, blanket “No surveyor needed” assurance, every-exit-is-a-loss claim, and universal high/mid/low placement rule were removed. Quiz 1 now asks what an A-frame can find, with equal-height points as the answer; its rationale says the tool cannot assess soil, drainage or suitability. Quiz 2 asks what to check before digging, with site assessment and safe overflow as the answer. The English learner media and audio must move together before release; this packet grants no approval for the isiZulu draft.

The existing isiZulu narration file contains no corresponding isiZulu quiz text or rationale. Please confirm whether learners can see or hear an isiZulu quiz elsewhere before any later translation is prepared. Any future quiz alignment needs to preserve the corrected English distinction between preliminary observation and site-specific earthwork design; do not carry over the former English answer key.

## Reviewer questions and limits

Please ask a first-language isiZulu reviewer familiar with South African smallholder farming to:

- Confirm that each working English meaning above accurately describes the isiZulu sentence, including the force of the verbs and borrowed terms.
- Check that any eventual correction sounds natural in ordinary spoken farming isiZulu and is understandable to a learner using a phone alone.
- Check locally used terms for contour lines, surveyor, swales, dams, and water escaping safely; do not coin technical words or assume English borrowings are familiar.
- Confirm that an A-frame is described only for preliminary contour observation and not as sufficient approval to site or dig an earthwork.
- Confirm that the water lesson does not imply retaining every drop or guarantee that slowing water high prevents erosion or damage.
- Record reviewer identity/role, date, edits, and the scope actually reviewed. Language review alone is not engineering, legal, or site-specific practitioner approval.

This packet changes no learner-facing isiZulu, quiz, audio, still, animation, or manifest. It does not authorize a translation or publication.
