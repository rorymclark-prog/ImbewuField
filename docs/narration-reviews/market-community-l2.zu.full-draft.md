# Market Gardening & Community L2 — complete isiZulu review draft

**REVIEW ONLY — not approved for learners, recording or publication.** This draft aligns the current English lesson body, key points, both quiz questions with all options/rationales, and exact L2 narration slides 9–13. It is a working translation for fluent isiZulu and local market/food-handling review; it is not legal advice or market approval.

## Sources and holds

- Current lesson: `market-community-l2` in `lib/course-modules.ts`.
- Current narration: slides 9–13 in `docs/narration/market-community.en.md`.
- Existing isiZulu reference: pages 9–13 in `docs/narration/market-community.zu.md`; it is unpublished and contains unverified market ranking, costs and income claims.
- Ask customers about product, quantity, quality, delivery and payment date. Compare fees, transport, packing and unsold produce as well as sale price.
- Check the market rules and local trading and food requirements. An informal stall does not automatically have no rules or costs. This draft does not decide which rules apply to a learner's exact route or product.
- Direct selling can retain more of the price but adds time and work. Regular box orders help planning only if growers and customers can keep their agreement; no route guarantees income.
- Garden area, customer count and prices do not by themselves predict income. No prices, customer targets, income estimates or universal market rankings are added.
- The quiz answer “informal market or neighbour sales with no fixed commitment” means flexibility about supply commitment; it does not exempt the seller from applicable trading and food requirements.

## Lesson body

### English meaning (current source wording)

Ask what the customer needs: product, quantity, quality, delivery and payment date.

Compare market fees, transport, packing and unsold produce as well as the selling price.

Check the market rules and local trading and food requirements. An informal stall does not automatically have no rules or costs.

Direct selling can retain more of the sale price, but it also takes time, packing, transport and customer care.

A box scheme supplies a regular selection to agreed customers.

Agree the contents, price, payment and what happens when crops are short. Regular orders help planning only when customers and growers can keep the agreement.

Start from what you can reliably supply and what customers want.

Check the costs and household food needs before promising regular boxes.

Garden area or customer count alone does not predict income. Try a manageable arrangement and record the results.

If production changes from week to week, avoid promising a fixed delivery you cannot supply.

Offer the surplus you have and agree clear terms with customers.

Describe your growing practices honestly. Check any certification or claim the buyer requires before using a label.

### Proposed isiZulu

Buza ukuthi umthengi udingani: umkhiqizo, inani, ikhwalithi, ukulethwa nosuku lokukhokha.

Qhathanisa izimali zemakethe, ezokuthutha, ukupakisha nomkhiqizo ongathengiswanga kanye nenani lokuthengisa.

Hlola imithetho yemakethe nezimfuneko zendawo zokuhweba nokudla. Ukuthi itafula lokuthengisa alihlelekile akusho ngokuzenzakalelayo ukuthi alinayo imithetho noma izindleko.

Ukuthengisa ngqo kungagcina ingxenye enkulu yenani lokuthengisa kumlimi, kodwa kudinga nesikhathi, ukupakisha, ukuthutha nokunakekela amakhasimende.

I-box scheme iletha izinhlobo ezivamile zemikhiqizo kumakhasimende okuvunyelwene nawo.

Vumelanani ngokuqukethwe kwebhokisi, inani, ukukhokha nokuthi kuzokwenzekani uma isivuno sishoda. Ama-oda avamile asiza ukuhlela kuphela uma amakhasimende nabalimi bekwazi ukugcina isivumelwano.

Qala ngalokho ongakuhlinzeka ngokwethembeka kanye nalokho okufunwa amakhasimende.

Hlola izindleko nezidingo zokudla komuzi ngaphambi kokuthembisa amabhokisi avamile.

Ubukhulu bengadi noma inani lamakhasimende kukodwa akubikezeli imali engenayo. Zama indlela ongakwazi ukuyiphatha, bese uqopha imiphumela.

Uma ukukhiqiza kushintsha isonto nesonto, gwema ukuthembisa ukulethwa okungaguquki ongeke ukwazi ukukufeza.

Nikeza ngomkhiqizo osele onawo bese nivumelana ngemigomo ecacile namakhasimende.

Chaza ngokwethembeka izindlela okhulisa ngazo izitshalo. Ngaphambi kokufaka ilebula, hlola isitifiketi noma isimangalo esidingwa umthengi.

## Key points

### English meaning (current source wording)

1. Agree product, quantity, quality, delivery and payment.
2. Compare costs and losses as well as selling price.
3. Promise regular boxes only when supply and customer terms support them.
4. Check market rules and describe growing practices honestly.

### Proposed isiZulu

1. Vumelanani ngomkhiqizo, inani, ikhwalithi, ukulethwa nokukhokha.
2. Qhathanisa izindleko nokulahleka komkhiqizo kanye nenani lokuthengisa.
3. Thembisa amabhokisi avamile kuphela uma umkhiqizo okwazi ukuwuhlinzeka nemigomo yamakhasimende kuvumela.
4. Hlola imithetho yemakethe futhi uchaze ngokwethembeka izindlela zokukhulisa izitshalo.

## Quiz

Correct answer positions match English: Question 1 option C (index 2); Question 2 option C (index 2).

### Question 1 — variable weekly supply

**English question:** A smallholder has inconsistent weekly production — surplus some weeks, little in others. Which channel suits her best?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Ukukhiqiza komlimi omncane akufani masonto onke — amanye amasonto uba nomkhiqizo osele omningi, kwamanye ube mncane. Iyiphi indlela yokuthengisa engamfanela kakhulu?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | A formal market stall needing consistent weekly supply | Itafula emakethe ehlelekile elidinga umkhiqizo ofanayo masonto onke |
| B | A box scheme needing the same produce weekly | I-box scheme edinga umkhiqizo ofanayo masonto onke |
| C — correct | An informal market or neighbour sales with no fixed commitment | Ukuthengisa emakethe engahlelekile noma komakhelwane ngaphandle kwesibopho senani elingaguquki |
| D | A daily-delivery school contract | Isivumelwano sesikole esidinga ukulethwa nsuku zonke |

**English rationale:** This is the one channel that doesn't require her to promise a fixed amount every week — she sells what she actually has.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Lena yindlela engamdingi ukuthi athembise inani elingaguquki masonto onke; uthengisa lokho anakho ngempela.

**Important qualifier:** This answer concerns flexible supply commitments only. It does not say informal sales have no market, trading or food requirements; those must still be checked locally.

### Question 2 — agreed regular orders

**English question:** How can agreed regular orders help a grower plan?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Ama-oda avamile okuvunyelwene ngawo angamsiza kanjani umlimi ahlele?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Box customers always pay more per kilogram | Amakhasimende e-box scheme ahlala ekhokha imali eningi ngekhilogremu |
| B | Box schemes let you charge extra for packaging | I-box scheme ikuvumela ukukhokhisa imali eyengeziwe yokupakisha |
| C — correct | Committed subscription income lets you plan production around real demand instead of growing speculatively | Ama-oda avunyelwene ngawo akunikeza ulwazi ngesidingo sangempela, ukuze uhlele ukukhiqiza esikhundleni sokutshala ungazi ukuthi kuzothengwa yini |
| D | Box schemes avoid tax obligations | Ama-box scheme akususa ezibophweni zentela |

**English rationale:** Confirmed orders give information about demand. Their value still depends on reliable supply, payment and the costs of fulfilling them.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ama-oda aqinisekisiwe akunikeza ulwazi ngokufunwa komkhiqizo. Ayasiza kuphela uma umkhiqizo uhlinzekeka ngokwethembeka, kukhokhwa, futhi izindleko zokufeza ama-oda zicatshangelwa.

## Reviewer questions and holds

1. Are `box scheme`, informal market, roadside stall and market fees clear in local spoken isiZulu? Avoid implying a nationwide market ranking.
2. Does the quiz's flexible channel remain separate from local rules? An informal/neighbour sale is not presented as exempt from trading or food requirements.
3. Does direct selling remain “can retain more,” with time, packing, transport and customer care costs preserved?
4. Do regular boxes remain conditional on agreed contents, price, payment, shortage terms, reliable supply and ability to fulfil? Is there any implied income guarantee?
5. Does the translation preserve the buyer's certification/claim requirement without asserting the learner needs a particular certification?
6. Confirm correct answer C for both quizzes and that option C's rationale does not imply tax or permit exemption.
7. Record reviewer name/role, language and market/farming experience, date, local context and exact corrections.

## Source and safety holds

- No prices, income, customer count, garden area, formal/informal ranking, permit exemption or market rule is invented.
- Local trading and food requirements depend on activity and place. This draft is not legal advice and does not say which rule applies to any learner.
- A flexible sales channel does not remove food-handling or market obligations.
- Regular orders are planning information, not guaranteed income. Compare actual fulfilment costs and household food needs.
