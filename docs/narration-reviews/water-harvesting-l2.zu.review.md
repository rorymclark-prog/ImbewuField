## Quiz alignment

The two English quiz questions test the risk of uncontrolled overtopping and maintaining designed bank cover while keeping the spillway clear. Neither quiz answer introduces a sizing number or species. Keep those answer meanings intact in any future ZU revision. In particular, do not translate the outdated 2 ha / 700 mm / storage estimate into the quiz or rationale.

## Source and review dependencies

- The L2 English check cites the [FAO manual for small earth dams](https://www.fao.org/4/i1531e/i1531e01.pdf) for catchment, storage and spillway design. The corrected English slide 12 line is the source for later fluent-language review; the still, narration, transcript and English audio were coordinated in this release candidate.
- No source in the reviewed lesson packet substantiates the ZU slide 11 yield estimate or slide 13 regional evaporation figure. Treat both as removed pending a primary, applicable source; do not try to preserve them as “examples.”
- A fluent isiZulu reviewer must check natural phrasing for runoff/catchment, site investigation, qualified dam design, spillway, overtopping/breach, bank cover and watercourse authorisation. The reviewer should also confirm the severity conveyed by “ngendlela eyinhlekelele” and whether the existing vocabulary is clear to the intended learners.
- Compare any translated still caption, narration, transcript and audio together after fluent review. Do not mark the language fluent-reviewed, farmer-approved or learner-tested without that review.
