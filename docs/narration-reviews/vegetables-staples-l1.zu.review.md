## Quiz coverage

Keep questions, options and correct indices from `lib/course-modules.ts` unchanged.

1. **Reachable bed width:** the answer is reach the centre from either side without stepping on growing soil, reducing compaction. Do not present 1–1.2 m as a bed width for every site.
2. **Direct-sown crop:** beans are the keyed answer. Current rationale's “avoids that setback entirely” is absolute; the English lesson check does not source-correct it. Preserve only if English remains the authority, and flag its certainty for the course owner. Do not add a guarantee in isiZulu.
