- Before changing a watercourse or building storage works, check required authorisation with the water authority. Do not turn this into a claim that every dam has one identical permit requirement.

## Lesson body

### English meaning — current source

A dam or pond can store runoff, but the amount available depends on local rain, the catchment, losses and how much water you use.

Rainfall seasons differ across South Africa. Use local records and plan for dry periods; a full dam is not guaranteed.

Before changing a watercourse or building storage works, check the required authorisation with the water authority.

A dam needs a site investigation and a design by a suitably qualified person. Catchment runoff, soil, foundations, downstream risk and a safe spillway all matter.

Do not assume that annual rainfall tells you the size of a flood or the storage you will have.

An uncontrolled overflow can erode and breach the wall. Plan a safe route for excess water before construction.

Water can be lost through evaporation and seepage. Check the water level and look for leaks or erosion.

Keep the spillway clear and maintain the bank cover specified in the design. Do not plant trees on an earth dam wall.

Animals can damage banks and add manure to the water. Their presence does not make the water clean or safe.

## Quiz

Correct answer index is 1 for both questions; preserve these positions.

### Question 1 — dam overflow

**English question:** A farmer builds a dam wall with no spillway. After an exceptional storm it overflows. What's the likely result?

**isiZulu proposal:** Umlimi wakha udonga lwedamu olungenayo i-spillway. Ngemva kwesiphepho esingavamile, amanzi ayachichima. Yimuphi umphumela ongase ulandele?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | The water irrigates lower fields beneficially | Amanzi anisela kahle amasimu angezansi |
| 1 — correct | It overtops and erodes the wall, risking a catastrophic breach | Amanzi agobhoza phezu kodonga alugugule, kube nengozi yokuthi ludilike kakhulu |
| 2 | The dam stays full and overflow drains harmlessly | Idamu lihlala ligcwele, amanzi aphumayo ahambe ngaphandle komonakalo |
| 3 | Storage capacity increases permanently | Umthamo wokugcina amanzi ukhula unomphela |

**English rationale:** Without a designed overflow route, excess water finds its own way over the wall — and that uncontrolled flow is what erodes and eventually breaches it.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** Uma ingekho indlela yokuchichima eklanywe kahle, amanzi amaningi azitholela eyawo indlela yokudlula phezu kodonga. Lokho kugeleza okungalawulwa kugugula udonga, kuze kulubhidlize ekugcineni.

### Question 2 — protecting an earth dam

**English question:** Which action helps protect an earth dam?

**isiZulu proposal:** Yisiphi isenzo esisiza ukuvikela idamu lomhlabathi?

| # | English option | isiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | A deep, exposed dam with no bank vegetation | Idamu elijulile elingenazitshalo emabhange alo futhi elivezwe emoyeni naselangeni |
| 1 — correct | Maintain the designed bank cover and keep the spillway clear | Nakekela izitshalo ezimboze ibhange ngokomklamo, ugcine ne-spillway ingenamfucumfucu |
| 2 | A full concrete lining and plastic cover | Faka ungqimba lukakhonkolo kuyo yonke indawo bese uyimboza ngepulasitiki |
| 3 | A larger surface area to spread evaporation evenly | Yandisa indawo engaphezulu ukuze ukuhwamuka kusabalale ngokulinganayo |

**English rationale:** A clear spillway and maintained banks help the dam work as designed. Trees should not be planted on an earth dam wall.

**isiZulu rationale proposal:** I-spillway engenamfucumfucu namabhange anakekelwayo kusiza idamu lisebenze ngokomklamo walo. Izihlahla akufanele zitshalwe odongeni lwedamu lomhlabathi.
