## English lesson body

> Dry seed properly before storing it: paper envelopes, not plastic, in a shaded, airy spot — never direct sun or sealed heat. The three enemies of seed viability are heat, light, and moisture; get all three low and seed can last for years.
>
> Label every envelope with crop, variety, and date saved. Store in a cool, dark, dry place — a sealed container with a little rice or dried milk powder as a moisture absorber works well.
>
> Before a new planting season, test a small batch for germination so you're not relying on seed that's quietly lost its viability.
>
> Organise a seed swap with neighbours this season. What one household saves well, several households can share — and the whole group's variety diversity grows with every swap.

### Proposed isiZulu body

Yomisa imbewu eyomile kahle ngaphambi kokuyigcina: yifake emvilophini yephepha, hhayi kwepulasitiki, uyibeke endaweni enomthunzi nenomoya ohambayo — ungayibeki elangeni eliqondile noma endaweni evaliwe eshisayo. Izinto ezintathu ezilimaza amandla embewu okuhluma ukushisa, ukukhanya nomswakama. Yehlisa konke lokhu, imbewu ingahlala iminyaka.

Bhala emvilophini ngayinye igama lesitshalo, uhlobo nosuku imbewu eyalondolozwa ngalo. Gcina endaweni epholile, emnyama neyomile — isitsha esivaliwe esinelayisi elincane noma impuphu yobisi olomisiwe singasiza ukumunca umswakama.

Ngaphambi kwesizini entsha yokutshala, hlola iqoqo elincane ukuthi liyahluma yini ukuze unganciki embewini esilahlekelwe amandla okuhluma ingaziwa.

Hlela ukushintshisana ngembewu nomakhelwane kule sizini. Lokho ikhaya elilodwa elikulondoloze kahle, amakhaya amaningana angabelana ngakho — futhi izinhlobo ezahlukahlukene zeqembu lonke ziyakhula kukho konke ukushintshisana.

## English key points

1. Dry seed in shade with good airflow; never in direct sun or sealed heat.
2. Store labelled seed cool, dark, and dry — heat, light, and moisture are the three enemies of viability.
3. Test a small batch for germination before relying on stored seed for planting.
4. Seed swaps grow everyone's variety diversity faster than saving alone.

### Proposed isiZulu key points

1. Yomisa imbewu emthunzini lapho kuhamba khona umoya; ungayibeki elangeni eliqondile noma endaweni evaliwe eshisayo.
2. Gcina imbewu enelebula endaweni epholile, emnyama neyomile — ukushisa, ukukhanya nomswakama yizinto ezintathu ezilimaza amandla okuhluma.

4. Ukushintshisana ngembewu kwandisa izinhlobo ezahlukene zeqembu ngokushesha kunokulondoloza uwedwa.

## Source-pair checks and reviewer questions

- Keep the source distinction between shaded, airy drying and storage after drying. Existing English narration says to seal only when the seed is completely dry, and slide 18 says paper packets go inside a sealed container after drying. The lesson body says “paper envelopes, not plastic” and later describes a sealed container. Ask the English content owner to make the sequence explicit; this draft preserves both instructions without adding a new rule.
- The lesson body says low heat, light and moisture mean seed “can last for years,” but gives no crop, conditions or duration. This packet translates the claim faithfully; do not turn it into a guaranteed lifespan. Ask the content owner whether the claim needs qualification.
- The English quiz rationale says a sealed container in a shaded cupboard works well but omits “completely dry” from that sentence. Review whether the rationale should carry the drying condition before this lesson is locked. Proposed translation preserves the current rationale and does not silently amend it.
- Rice and dried milk powder appear in the English lesson body as moisture absorbers. Existing English narration instead says “a food-safe desiccant” if available. Ask the content owner to reconcile these descriptions and confirm suitable local materials; do not add alternatives or imply a material is food-safe without a source.
- The English key points say test a “small batch”; existing narration specifies exactly ten seeds and shows six of ten germinating. The lesson body, key points and quiz do not set a count or germination threshold. This packet leaves those details out of the proposed lesson text and preserves the count in the existing narration only.
- The source says seed swaps grow variety diversity. Do not imply that every exchanged packet is viable, correctly identified, or safe to plant without its history; narration slide 22 separately teaches sharing information with seed.
- Quiz answer indices remain 1 and 1. Check that the legal-requirement distractor remains plainly incorrect in the local context and that neither rationale becomes a guarantee of storage performance.
- The learner draft is stored in `lib/course-translation-drafts.ts` and is served only through the visibly labelled draft presentation. The lesson fields and answer indexes were compared with the current English source when registered.
