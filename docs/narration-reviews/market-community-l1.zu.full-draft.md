## Lesson body

### English meaning (current source wording)

A harvest can feed the household, be sold, be shared, or be lost.

Recording these different uses helps you see what the farm produces and what reaches customers.

Use that information to protect household food and make better business decisions.

Write down every harvest as it happens.

Record kilograms of tomatoes, dozens of eggs, and bundles of morogo, then note where each went.

Use the same simple habit for food kept at home, produce sold, produce gifted, and produce composted.

Do not rely on memory at the end of the season.

One season of records answers practical questions.

Which crops give the best yield per bed? Which return the most for each hour of work?

Which crops use more seeds, water, and compost than they return?

The record also shows which months leave the household buying food.

Before setting a price, record production, packing and selling costs, including labour and transport.

Here is a teaching example, not a market price: tomatoes cost R18 per kilogram but sell for R15 per kilogram. That price does not cover the stated cost.

Review the price, costs and next planting. Check what customers will actually buy; a higher asking price is not a guaranteed sale.

Use your record to find when household food runs short.

Choose locally suitable crops and work backwards from the harvest you need. Check planting conditions and expected time to harvest.

A date that works on another farm may not work here. Include a backup plan when rain, water or crops fail.

## Key points

### English meaning (current source wording)

1. Record harvest amounts and destinations separately from cash.
2. Include production and selling costs when assessing a price.
3. Label worked examples; use your actual costs for decisions.
4. Plan for food gaps using local growing conditions and harvest timing.

### Proposed isiZulu

1. Qopha inani lesivuno nalapho siye khona ngokwehlukana nemali engenile.
2. Faka izindleko zokukhiqiza nokuthengisa lapho uhlola inani lokuthengisa.
3. Bhala ngokucacile ukuthi izibonelo ziyizibonelo; sebenzisa izindleko zakho zangempela lapho wenza izinqumo.
4. Hlela izikhathi zokushoda kokudla ngokwezimo zokutshala zendawo nesikhathi sokuvuna.

## Quiz

Correct answer indices match current English: Question 1 option C (index 2); Question 2 option B (index 1).

### Question 1 — teaching price example

**English question:** In this teaching example, tomatoes sell at R15/kg and cost R18/kg to produce. What should the farmer review?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Kulesi sibonelo sokufundisa, utamatisi uthengiswa ngo-R15/kg kodwa kubiza u-R18/kg ukuwukhiqiza. Yini okufanele umlimi ayibuyekeze?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Keep selling at R15 — short-term loss builds relationships | Qhubeka uthengisa ngo-R15 — ukulahlekelwa okwesikhashana kwakha ubudlelwano |
| B | Stop growing tomatoes entirely | Yeka ngokuphelele ukutshala utamatisi |
| C — correct | The selling price, costs and whether another crop would give a better return | Inani lokuthengisa, izindleko nokuthi esinye isitshalo singabuyisa inzuzo engcono yini |
| D | Apply for a subsidy to cover the gap | Faka isicelo soxhaso ukuze kuvalwe umehluko |

**English rationale:** The example price is below the stated cost. Review the gap and customer demand before making the next production decision.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Kulesi sibonelo inani lokuthengisa lingaphansi kwezindleko ezishiwo. Buyekeza lo mehluko nokufunwa ngabathengi ngaphambi kokwenza isinqumo esilandelayo sokukhiqiza.

### Question 2 — June/July food gap

**English question:** A farmer's records show she's short of vegetables every June and July. What's the useful action here?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Amarekhodi omlimi abonisa ukuthi uphelelwa imifino njalo ngoJuni nangoJulayi. Yisiphi isinyathelo esiwusizo?

| Option | English | Proposed isiZulu |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | Buy vegetables at market each June and July | Thenga imifino emakethe njalo ngoJuni nangoJulayi |
| B — correct | Work backwards from the food gap using suitable local crops and their harvest timing | Hlela uhlehle usuka kuleli gebe lokudla, usebenzise izitshalo ezifanele zendawo nesikhathi sazo sokuvuna |
| C | Accept her farm can't produce in winter | Yamukela ukuthi ipulazi lakhe alikwazi ukukhiqiza ebusika |
| D | The records show a soil fertility problem | Amarekhodi abonisa inkinga yokuvunda komhlabathi |

**English rationale:** Records identify the gap. Crop choice and sowing dates must then match the local climate, water and expected harvest time.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Amarekhodi akhomba isikhathi sokushoda. Ukukhetha izitshalo nezinsuku zokuzihlwanyela kufanele kuhambisane nesimo sezulu sendawo, amanzi nesikhathi esilindelekile sokuvuna.

### Slide 19 — Field Assignment: Make a Farm Record

**English source:**

Use headings for harvest, where it went, costs, and labour.

Record every kilogram, dozen, or bundle as it leaves the farm.

Include food kept by the household, produce sold, produce gifted, and produce composted.

At the end of one season, identify a crop that returns well and a month when the household buys food.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Sebenzisa izihloko ezithi isivuno, lapho siye khona, izindleko nomsebenzi.

Qopha wonke amakhilogremu, ama-dozen noma izinyanda njengoba ziphuma epulazini.

Faka ukudla okugcinelwe umuzi, umkhiqizo othengisiwe, ophisiwe nowafakwa ku-compost.

Ekupheleni kwenkathi eyodwa, thola isitshalo esibuyisa kahle kanye nenyanga umuzi othenga ngayo ukudla.

### Slide 20 — Field Action: Use the Record

**English source:**

Choose one real decision from your farm record.

Compare the cost and return of a crop or selling route, or plan for a household food gap using local growing conditions.

Then agree one practical seed swap, tool share, shared delivery or skills session with neighbours. Before a seed swap, check whether the variety is protected and whether permission is needed. Record responsibilities and review what happens.

**Proposed isiZulu:**

Khetha isinqumo esisodwa sangempela esivela kurekhodi lepulazi lakho.

Qhathanisa izindleko nembuyiselo yesitshalo noma yendlela yokuthengisa, noma uhlelele isikhathi sokushoda kokudla komuzi usebenzisa izimo zokutshala zendawo.

Bese nivumelana nomakhelwane ngesinyathelo esisodwa esisebenzayo: ukushintshisana ngembewu, ukwabelana ngethuluzi, ukuletha umkhiqizo ndawonye noma ukufunda ikhono. Ngaphambi kokushintshisana ngembewu, hlolani ukuthi uhlobo luvikelwe yini nokuthi imvume iyadingeka yini. Qophani izibopho zomuntu ngamunye bese nibuyekeza okwenzekile.
