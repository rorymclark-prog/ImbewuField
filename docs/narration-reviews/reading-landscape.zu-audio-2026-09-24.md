## Source-backed safeguards retained

- Heavy-rain observation happens from a safe place, and field walking only happens when it is safe. The lesson notes where excess water may need a safe route but does not prescribe a route or earthwork.
- A-frame marks are observations of equal-height points, not an earthworks design or approval. Site assessment by a trained local adviser is required before digging; soil, slope, drainage, storm flow and safe overflow all matter.
- Sun, slope, wind, frost and nursery placement remain conditional and site-specific. No regional fixed wind rule, frost-safe slope, or guaranteed nursery position is stated.
- For late blight, airflow and morning sun can help leaves dry; prolonged cool, damp weather can still favour disease, and moving a bed alone does not control it. Local crop-health advice remains explicit.
- The site sketch is marked “not to scale” until its distances are checked. Walking or pacing the boundary does not establish map scale. Slide 17 is narrated as a still picture guide, not a measured map or construction plan.
- Thick khakibos or blackjack growth is an observation to mark and a reason for a closer soil check. Their presence alone does not diagnose compaction or prescribe treatment.
