- [USDA NRCS soil-health assessment](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/conservation-basics/soil/soil-health/soil-health-assessment): assess multiple physical, biological and chemical indicators and compare patterns; colour or one biological clue does not diagnose a cause.
- [Montana State University Extension jar method](https://landresources.montana.edu/soilfertility/html/SoilTextureJarTest.html): settling occurs in stages; this supports the lesson's rough demonstration, not the old draft's exact recipe, timetable or treatment advice.

It reports the slide-5 still, a held Flow clod clip, and no source-backed correction to the current English L1 body or quiz. The hold matters: the clip shows one sample and does not replace the narrated comparison of several clues.

### Quiz coverage

The current L1 quiz has two questions and neither appears in the isiZulu script. Keep the questions, options and correct-answer indices as authored in `lib/course-modules.ts`.

1. **Cloudy jar water above sand:** fine particles may still be suspended; more observation is needed. Do not infer irrigation need, final proportions or treatment.
2. **Compacted soil and few worms:** check drainage, roots, moisture and management history. Do not conclude organisms died or add treatment before checking.

## Proposed isiZulu draft — slides 1–8

Draft only. Confirm natural spoken farming isiZulu, especially borrowed terms and how to express uncertainty. Text aims to preserve the English limits; it is not a fluent translation claim.

### Ikhasi 1 — Impilo Yomhlabathi Ne-Compost

Umhlabathi ungaphezu kokuba yindawo engaphansi kwezinyawo zakho.

Uyindawo ephilayo esiza izitshalo zikhule, ebamba amanzi, futhi esekela isivuno sakho.

Kule modyuli, funda ukubheka umhlabathi, ukunakekela i-compost, ukuvikela umhlabathi ongenalutho, nokusebenzisa izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi nama-worm farm.

### Ikhasi 2 — Kungani Lokhu Kubalulekile

Isimo somhlabathi sithinta izimpande, amanzi nesivuno.

Ukushiya umhlabathi ungenalutho njalo, ukuwucindezela nokulahlekelwa yizinto eziphilayo ezikuwo kungawulimaza. Umbala wodwa awuchazi konke.

Bheka umhlabathi wakho, bese ukhetha izindlela zokuwuvikela nokuwuthuthukisa.

### Ikhasi 3 — Imiphumela Yokufunda

Bheka izimpawu eziningana zesimo somhlabathi.

Sebenzisa ibhodlela eliyingilazi ukuze uhlole ukuthungwa komhlabathi, kodwa khumbula ukuthi lolu vivinyo lunemingcele.

Nakekela inqwaba ye-compost, vikela umhlabathi ongenalutho, futhi uhlukanise ama-worm castings noketshezi oluphuma emgqonyeni.

### Ikhasi 4 — Umhlabathi Uyaphila

Umhlabathi unezinhlobo eziningi zezinto eziphilayo. Amagciwane nesikhunta kusiza ukubolisa izinto eziphilayo nokujikeleza kwezakhamzimba.

Ezinye izinhlobo zesikhunta zisiza izimpande zimunce izakhamzimba. Imigudu yezibungu ingasiza amanzi nomoya kungene emhlabathini.

Bheka izimpande, ukwakheka komhlabathi nokuhamba kwamanzi, kanye nezinto eziphilayo ozibonayo.

### Ikhasi 5 — Buka: Funda Umhlabathi Wakho

Bhekisisa lezi zibonelo ezimbili zomhlabathi.

Okokuqala, bheka ukwakheka komhlabathi. Ensimini, thinta ucezu olumanzi kancane ubone ukuthi luphuka kanjani.

Bheka izimpande nezikhala ezizizungezile.

Bheka okumboze phezulu. Esinye isibonelo simbozwe nge-mulch exegayo.

Sebenzisa izimpawu eziningana ndawonye. Umbala wodwa awukutsheli impilo yomhlabathi. Hlola umswakama, ukukhula kwezitshalo nokungena kwamanzi emhlabathini.

### Ikhasi 6 — Hlola Ukuthungwa Komhlabathi Ngebhodlela

Faka umhlabathi namanzi ebhodleleni eliyingilazi elibonakalayo, kanye nensipho yokuhlakaza efanele kancane. Livale, ulinyakazise, bese ulishiya linganyakazi.

Isihlabathi sihlala phansi kuqala. I-silt ilandela; ubumba lungahlala luntanta isikhathi eside.

Lolu vivinyo oluyisisekelo lokufunda. Izigaxa nobumba olungakahlali phansi kungakudukisa. Uma kudingeka ukuthungwa okunembile, sebenzisa ilabhorethri yomhlabathi.

### Ikhasi 7 — Funda Ibhodlela Ngokuqapha

Ungqimba olujiyile lwesihlabathi ngaphansi kwamanzi afiphele alukakutsheli izilinganiso zokugcina. Ezinye izinhlayiya ezincane kungenzeka zisantanta.

Qhathanisa izingqimba esezihlale phansi, uthinte nomhlabathi osensimini.

Bhala lokho okubonayo nalokho okungakaqinisekiswa. Unganqumi indlela yokunisela noma yokwelapha umhlabathi ngebhodlela elilodwa kuphela.

### Ikhasi 8 — Hlola Ngaphambi Kokwelapha

Ukucinana komhlabathi, ukungaphumi kahle kwamanzi nokuncipha kwezinto eziphilayo kungavimbela izimpande nokuphila komhlabathi.

Umbala ophaphathekile noma izibungu ezimbalwa akufakazeli ukuthi amakhemikhali abulale umhlabathi. Umsebenzi wezibungu nawo uyashintsha nomswakama nesikhathi sonyaka.

Bheka amaphethini ezingxenyeni ezahlukene zensimu. Hlola umlando wokunakekela, ukuphuma kwamanzi nokukhula kwezitshalo ngaphambi kokukhetha ikhambi.

## Human review checklist

- Check every borrowed word and whether a farmer would say it aloud; especially `i-compost`, `i-mulch`, `ama-worm farm`, `i-silt`, `ubumba` and `insipho yokuhlakaza`.
- Confirm the meaning of “can”, “may”, “does not prove” and “rough exercise”; these qualifiers carry the safety boundary.
- Review the jar material and term for dispersing detergent locally; do not silently replace it with dish soap or add a fixed settling time.
- Confirm the narration matches the current two-panel slide-5 still and does not describe the held video as learner media.
