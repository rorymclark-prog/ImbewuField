## English lesson body

> Dry seed must mature before collection. Clean away chaff and damaged seed, then finish drying with shade and airflow.
>
> Seed in fleshy fruit needs a crop-specific method. Tomato seed can be briefly fermented to help remove its gel, then rinsed and dried thoroughly. Keep an active jar open or loosely covered. Fermentation is not required for every wet-seeded crop and does not guarantee disease-free seed.
>
> Pollination matters too. Maize is wind-pollinated and can cross with other varieties. Tomatoes mostly self-pollinate, but crossing is possible. Check the crop and variety before planning isolation or saving seed.

### Proposed isiZulu body

Imbewu eyomile kufanele ivuthwe ngaphambi kokuba iqoqwe. Susa amakhoba nembewu eyonakele, bese uqedela ukuyomisa emthunzini lapho kuhamba khona umoya.

Imbewu engaphakathi kwesithelo esinenyama idinga indlela eqondene naleso sitshalo. Imbewu katamatisi ingavutsheliswa isikhashana ukuze kususwe ijeli eyizungezile, bese iyahlanzwa futhi yomiswa ngokuphelele. Shiya imbiza esabilayo ivulekile noma imbozwe ngokuxega. Ukubilisa akudingeki kuzo zonke izitshalo ezinembewu emanzi futhi akuqinisekisi ukuthi imbewu ayinazifo.

Indlela impova ehamba ngayo nayo ibalulekile. Ummbila uthola impova ehamba nomoya futhi ungaxubana nezinye izinhlobo. Utamatisi uvame ukuzithuthela impova, kodwa ukuxubana kusengenzeka. Hlola isitshalo nohlobo lwaso ngaphambi kokuhlela ukusihlukanisa noma ukulondoloza imbewu yaso.

## English key points

1. Dry-method crops (beans, maize, sunflower) simply dry on the plant before collection.
2. Tomato fermentation removes gel; other wet seeds need their own processing method.
3. Tomatoes need little isolation; maize needs real distance between varieties to stay pure.
4. Rinse processed tomato seed and dry it thoroughly before storage.

### Proposed isiZulu key points

1. Izitshalo zendlela eyomile (ubhontshisi, ummbila, ubhekilanga) zomiswa esitshalweni ngaphambi kokuqoqwa.
2. Ukuvutshelisa imbewu katamatisi kusiza ukususa ijeli; enye imbewu emanzi idinga indlela yayo yokuyilungisa.
3. Utamatisi awudingi ukuhlukaniswa okukhulu; ummbila udinga ibanga elikhulu phakathi kwezinhlobo ukuze zigcine ubumsulwa bazo.
4. Hlanza imbewu katamatisi esilungisiwe, bese uyomisa ngokuphelele ngaphambi kokuyigcina.

## Quiz 1

**English question:** What does brief fermentation help remove when processing tomato seed?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Ukubilisa imbewu katamatisi isikhashana kusiza ukususa ini?

| Index | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Fermentation improves the seed's flavour | Ukubilisa kuthuthukisa ukunambitheka kwembewu. |
| 1 | It helps remove the gel around the seed before rinsing and drying | Kusiza ukususa ijeli ezungeze imbewu ngaphambi kokuyihlanza nokuyomisa. **Correct (index 1).** |
| 2 | It kills any pests inside the fruit | Kubulala zonke izinambuzane ezingaphakathi kwesithelo. |
| 3 | It's purely traditional with no practical function | Kuyisiko kuphela, akunamsebenzi osebenzayo. |

**English rationale:** Fermentation helps separate tomato seed from its gel. It is not a guarantee of germination or disease-free seed; dry, store and test the batch.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Ukuvutshelisa kusiza ukuhlukanisa imbewu katamatisi nejeli. Akuqinisekisi ukuhluma noma ukuthi imbewu ayinazifo; yomisa iqoqo, uligcine, bese ulihlola.

## Quiz 2

**English question:** Why does maize need much greater isolation distance than tomatoes to keep a variety pure?

**Proposed isiZulu question:** Kungani ummbila udinga ibanga elikhulu kakhulu lokuhlukaniswa kunotamatisi ukuze uhlobo lugcine izici zalo?

| Index | English option | Proposed isiZulu option |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Maize seed is more fragile | Imbewu yommbila intekenteke kakhulu. |
| 1 | Maize is wind-pollinated and crosses easily over distance; tomatoes mostly self-pollinate | Ummbila uthola impova ethwalwa ngumoya futhi ungaxubana kalula ngebanga; utamatisi uvame ukuzithuthela impova. **Correct (index 1).** |
| 2 | Tomatoes don't cross-pollinate at all under any conditions | Utamatisi awuxubani nempova ngaphansi kwanoma yiziphi izimo. |
| 3 | Maize flowers for a shorter period | Ummbila uqhakaza isikhathi esifushane. |

**English rationale:** Wind carries maize pollen far further than insect or self-pollination moves tomato pollen — that difference in pollination method drives the isolation requirement.

**Proposed isiZulu rationale:** Umoya uthwala impova yommbila ibanga elide. Lokhu kuhlukile endleleni impova katamatisi ehamba ngayo, njengoba utamatisi uvame ukuzithuthela impova. Yile ndlela ehlukile yokuthuthwa kwempova eyenza kudingeke izindlela ezahlukene zokuhlukanisa izitshalo.

## Source-pair checks and reviewer questions

- Keep dry seed maturity, cleaning and final shade/airflow drying. The key point says the listed crops “simply dry on the plant”; check that “simply” does not erase the body’s maturity and post-collection cleaning/drying steps.
- The source names beans, maize and sunflower as dry-method examples. Retain these exact crop names; add no species or crop-specific timing.
- Keep tomato fermentation brief and crop-specific. Existing isiZulu narration uses “ukubilisa kancane,” which can mean boiling; this draft uses “ukuvutshelisa,” which must be checked by a fluent reviewer. The source says it helps remove gel, then calls for rinsing and thorough drying; it does not promise disease-free seed.
- Current key point 3 says tomatoes need little isolation, while the body says crossing is possible and tells learners to check crop and variety. Existing narration likewise says tomato mostly self-pollinates, but crossing is possible. Ask the English content owner whether the key point needs qualification; this translation preserves its wording and does not create an isolation distance.
- Maize is described as wind-pollinated and able to cross with other varieties. No numeric distance is supplied. Do not add one.
- Quiz answer indices remain 1 and 1. Confirm that option 2 in quiz 2 remains clearly false without accidentally teaching that tomatoes never cross.
- Existing slide 14 narration gives a two-to-three-day fermentation window and signs. Those operational details are not in this English lesson body or its quiz; this packet does not move them into the lesson draft.
