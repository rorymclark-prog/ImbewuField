# Sesotho Study UI draft holds

This batch contains AI drafts for high visibility Study navigation and lesson controls. A fluent Sesotho speaker has not reviewed any of them. The Study page displays an unreviewed notice with its exact English source.

Three strings remain in English until a fluent reviewer can confirm natural wording. The candidate text below is retained for review only and is not shipped to learners.

| Key | English source | Unshipped candidate | Hold reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `studentCourseDescription` | Your permaculture course, one practical lesson at a time. | `Thupelo ya hao ya permaculture, thuto e le nngwe e sebetsang ka nako.` | Independent review found the construction for “one practical lesson at a time” unnatural. |
| `studentCourseComplete` | Course complete! | `Thuto e phethilwe!` | `Thuto` means a lesson; the source refers to the full course. |
| `studentMarkComplete` | Mark as complete | `Tshwaya e phethilweng` | Grammatical form and intended button meaning are uncertain. |

The UI falls back to the English source for these keys. No reviewer aside was used as replacement wording. The remaining Sesotho entries are machine drafts and still need fluent speaker review before they are treated as approved translations.
