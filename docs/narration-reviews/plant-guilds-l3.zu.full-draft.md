## Lesson body

### English source — current

Combine the support functions your site needs: nitrogen fixation, food, mulch, flowers and ground cover. Some plants serve several functions.

Keep the trunk area and path open. Reassess each member as the mango and its neighbours grow.

Keep its vines away from the young fruit tree and retain a route for care. Its roots also use water and nutrients.

Where resources are tight, compare living cover with an ordinary mulch basin.

Plant into a suitable season, mulch and maintain establishment water. Keep the access gap open.

Start with the number of support plants you can care for. Observe survival and growth before adding more.

Cut down selected competing supports to open space. Suitable cut material can stay as mulch: this is thinning through chop-and-drop.

Manage regrowth to keep the opening. Check light, soil moisture and growth; thinning does not instantly stop root competition.

Carry useful prunings back to established trees. Keep nearby plants only where they still perform well.

Mature fruit trees still need nutrients. Monitor growth, harvest and soil conditions; support plants do not remove that need.

Check fruit-tree growth, shade, soil moisture, useful harvests and pest damage. Note what was cut, returned or removed.

Use these observations to change the layout and care. A plant earns its place through what it does here.

### IsiZulu proposal

Hlanganisa imisebenzi edingwa yindawo yakho: ukubopha i-nitrogen, ukudla, i-mulch, izimbali nokumboza umhlabathi. Ezinye izitshalo zenza imisebenzi eminingi.

Gcina indawo yesiqu nendlela kuvulekile. Hlola ilungu ngalinye le-guild njengoba umango nezitshalo ezingomakhelwane zikhula.

Gcina iziqu ezinabayo zikabhatata kude nomuthi omncane wezithelo, ushiye indlela yokuwunakekela. Izimpande zawo nazo zisebenzisa amanzi nezakhamzimba.

Lapho izinsiza zinganele, qhathanisa izitshalo eziphilayo ezimboza umhlabathi nendawo ezungeze umuthi embozwe nge-mulch evamile.

Tshala ngesikhathi sonyaka esifanele, faka i-mulch futhi uqhubeke nokunikeza amanzi adingekayo ngesikhathi isitshalo simila. Shiya indlela yokufinyelela ivulekile.

Qala ngenani lezitshalo ezisekelayo okwazi ukuzinakekela. Bheka ukuthi ziyaphila yini nokuthi zikhula kanjani ngaphambi kokwengeza ezinye.

Sika phansi izitshalo ezisekelayo ezikhethiwe ezincintisana ngesikhala. Izinsalela ezifanele zingashiywa njenge-mulch; lokhu ukunciphisa inani lezitshalo ngokusika nokushiya okusikiwe emhlabathini.

Lawula ukukhula kabusha ukuze indawo evulekile ihlale ikhona. Bheka ukukhanya, umswakama womhlabathi nokukhula; ukunciphisa izitshalo akukuqedi ngokushesha ukuncintisana kwezimpande.

Buyisela amagatsha athenwe afanele ezihlahleni esezimile. Gcina izitshalo ezingomakhelwane kuphela uma zisaqhubeka nokwenza umsebenzi wazo kahle.

Izihlahla zezithelo esezikhulile zisadinga izakhamzimba. Bheka ukukhula, isivuno nesimo somhlabathi; izitshalo ezisekelayo azisusi leso sidingo.

Bheka ukukhula kwesihlahla sezithelo, umthunzi, umswakama womhlabathi, ukudla okuwusizo okuvunwayo nomonakalo wezinambuzane. Bhala ukuthi yini ethenwe, ebuyiselwe emhlabathini noma esusiwe.

Sebenzisa lokhu okubukile ukuze ushintshe ukuhlelwa nokunakekela. Isitshalo sihlala ku-guild uma sibonisa ukuthi senza umsebenzi owusizo kule ndawo.

### Question 1 — support plant still crowding the mango

**English source:** A support plant still crowds the mango after pruning. What can thinning involve?

**IsiZulu proposal:** Isitshalo esisekelayo sisaminyanisa umango ngemva kokuthena. Ukunciphisa izitshalo kungahlanganisani?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Only cutting another small twig | Ukusika elinye igatsha elincane kuphela |
| 1 — correct | Cutting down a selected competing support and managing regrowth | Ukusika phansi isitshalo esisekelayo esikhethiwe esincintisanayo bese ulawula ukukhula kabusha |
| 2 | Removing the mango instead | Ukususa umango esikhundleni salokho |
| 3 | Always carrying all cut biomass off the site | Ukususa zonke izinsalela ezisikiwe kule ndawo ngaso sonke isikhathi |

**English rationale:** Thinning reduces selected standing support plants. Suitable cut material may stay as mulch.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Ukunciphisa izitshalo kususa ezinye izitshalo ezisekelayo ezikhethiwe ezisamile. Izinsalela ezifanele zingashiywa njenge-mulch.

### Question 2 — sweet potato versus a mulch basin

**English source:** Is sweet potato always better than a mulch basin around a young fruit tree?

**IsiZulu proposal:** Ingabe ubhatata uhlale ungcono kunendawo ezungeze umuthi omncane wezithelo embozwe nge-mulch?

| Index | English option | IsiZulu proposal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | Yes, because it never uses water | Yebo, ngoba awusebenzisi amanzi |
| 1 | Yes, because it fixes nitrogen | Yebo, ngoba ubopha i-nitrogen |
| 2 — correct | No; compare its food and cover benefits with competition for resources | Cha; qhathanisa ukudla nokumboza komhlabathi okukulethayo nokuncintisana ngezinsiza |
| 3 | No, because no ground cover can ever be useful | Cha, ngoba izitshalo ezimboza umhlabathi azisoze zaba wusizo |

**English rationale:** Choose cover for the site. Keep access and the trunk area clear, and observe the young tree.

**IsiZulu rationale proposal:** Khetha okumboza umhlabathi ngokwendawo. Shiya indlela nendawo yesiqu kuvulekile, uqhubeke ubheka umuthi omncane.
