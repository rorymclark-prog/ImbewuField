These are the current English points against which the unchanged isiZulu draft needs review.

## English quiz and lesson alignment

The English quiz in `lib/course-modules.ts` was corrected in commit `7b26b635`. Quiz 1 (lines 232–240) asks where to first look for a frost-tender young pawpaw and makes a sunny spot outside a known frost hollow, after checking local frost patterns, the best answer; its rationale says this may reduce risk, while local observations guide the final position. Neither quiz claims wall protection or a fixed 2 m shade-cloth outcome. No corresponding isiZulu quiz questions or rationales appear in the narration file.
