# Site report audit · 5 September 2026

Scope: the supplied Zululand Lowveld PDF (34 pages), report generation, saved facts, reading view, PDF exporter, crop-plan inputs and bill of quantities. Design Studio geometry and render recipes are outside this change.

## Findings and implemented corrections

- **The report looked like a settings panel.** Beige behind muted text, tiny image captions, eight narrow statistic tiles and small map previews weakened readability. The reading view now has its own white-paper palette, forest header, readable statistic grid, larger saved-map and photo cards, and catalogue crop illustrations matched by crop identity. Illustrations are labelled as such; they are not evidence photographs.
- **Print and screen need different treatments.** Screen mode retains visual context. Print preview uses white backgrounds and dark text. Full PDF image appendices are opt-in. One- and five-page summaries are deterministic, have exactly the named number of pages, and state when extra rows need the full report. They can be read/exported without paying for another AI generation. Brief AI advice is labelled separately and no longer promises a one-page PDF.
- **The saved design must govern geometry.** The supplied report's legacy header could display a different area and water-volume estimate from its detailed design facts. The new header reads the saved canvas facts: boundary, growing area and stated tank capacity. These mean mapped/planned quantities, not verified hectares in production or water currently available. Old saved reports with no fact snapshot show unavailable values rather than borrowing a newer plan.
- **Soil placeholders were interpreted as facts.** The supplied PDF disclosed generic defaults in one section but drew a positive soil conclusion elsewhere. Generic pH/OC/texture values are now omitted from the generation instructions and headline cards. SoilGrids is identified as a model; laboratory results are distinct. Generic amendment targets and unsupported application rates have been removed from the instructions.
- **Two water calculations were unsupported.** Solar radiation was used as an evapotranspiration proxy, and a formula turned slope into swale spacing. Neither is sufficient to size a real water system. These formulas were removed. Whole-site rainfall volume is not presented as harvestable water; no arbitrary capture fraction is supplied. Roof runoff remains tied to the existing explicit roof model.
- **Crop inputs could cross gardens.** The global crop plan is now filtered against this canvas's physical bed IDs. Existing/one-time plantings are identified as once-only in the report. Summaries and the isiZulu full report include saved crop rows. Neither inferred yield nor availability is a seed purchase quantity; the seed list remains the source.
- **BOQ is a planned budget.** It retains measured quantities, existing-item exclusions, priced subtotal and unpriced lines. Missing rates never become zero, and no-input summaries do not invent an R0 budget. Transport, labour, tax and current local quotes still require review.
- **PDF page breaks changed the body font.** The footer now restores font, size and colour. Long text and oversized table rows carry across pages; table headers repeat and column widths cannot be starved by a long neighbouring cell. Unicode unsupported by the core PDF font is normalised before measuring. A second renderer confirmed that a separate Poppler font-substitution spacing problem in this workspace was not present in the generated PDF itself.
- **isiZulu stopped at AI prose.** Controls, section names, deterministic summaries and code-authored front/back matter now have isiZulu versions. Scientific names, recorded farmer labels and catalogue item names retain their identity. Previously saved prose is not silently translated: the UI explains when regeneration is needed. Other South African language front/back matter remains English and needs a separate translation pass; fluent local review of the new isiZulu wording is still needed.
- **MEL wording overclaimed.** Unrecorded harvest is not automatically zero. Unsold harvest is not necessarily food eaten at home. Proposed trees are not a planted survival cohort. The monitoring guidance now distinguishes those states and labels retrospective baselines.

## Remaining content limits

The model still supplies narrative advice and species recommendations, so source-safe prompts reduce invention but do not prove agronomic correctness. No paid generation was used during this change; new narrative output requires field review. The risk register is a rule-based screening aid, not calibrated probabilities or engineering approval. Archived report text is preserved as an archive, including earlier assumptions. Current photos and saved report prose may come from different dates; the image caption makes that distinction explicit.

The app contains saved site photos and generated catalogue illustrations, not a verified photographic species library for every plant. Substituting a plausible tree photo would create false identification evidence. This release uses existing assets honestly; a future species-photo library should carry scientific identity, source, licence and attribution for each image.

## Research basis

[W3C clear-content guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG2/supplemental/objectives/o3-clear-content/) supports short labels and clear instructions. [W3C image guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/tutorials/images/) distinguishes informative and decorative images; crop art here is decorative while saved photos retain descriptions. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) informed contrast, visible focus and reflow. [MDN printing guidance](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/CSS/Guides/Media_queries/Printing) supports separate screen and print styles.

[NMSU enterprise-budget guidance](https://pubs.nmsu.edu/_z/Z121/index.html) informed the separation between budgeted costs, recorded cash margin and complete profit. [FAO CropGrids](https://www.fao.org/agroinformatics/training-and-resources/data-sets/data-set-detail/cropgrids-data-on-harvested-and-crop-areas/en) distinguishes physical crop area from harvested area; repeated crops must not inflate hectares of land. Assessment design and funder publication controls are documented separately in the MEL audit.

## Verification

TypeScript and the repository test suite are required gates. Added regression checks cover unrelated-garden crop rows, unknown soil/costs and exact one/five-page PDF counts. PDF QA uses the repository's labelled sample design, not the user's real farm: both summaries were rendered, and a deliberately oversized paragraph/table retained their final lines after page breaks. Production checks and preview results are recorded in the pull request.
