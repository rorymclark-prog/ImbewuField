# Harvest-target weight entry and display

The buyer-target editor offers an explicit kilogram or pound choice, independent
of country, language and currency. It uses the shared mass facade and its verified
avoirdupois pound factor. Canonical weekly weight and optional grower-entered pack
weight remain kilograms. Produce-pack quantity, pack unit, saleable share and
inclusive harvest months are not converted. No box, bunch, head or tray weight is
supplied; harvested-produce trays are distinct from nursery equipment.

Dimension drafts retain exact authored canonical values through untouched toggles
and reopening. Valid edits convert once in their displayed unit; rounded display
text is never reparsed during a toggle. Already-saved floating-point values are
not migrated or rounded by unchanged text events; new entries use the shared
decimal arithmetic to avoid the reproduced extra-cycle boundary for equivalent
kilogram and pound input. Required pristine blank weight can choose
a unit before typing but cannot be saved. Incomplete, dirty-cleared, nonfinite or
nonpositive weight holds switching and save. Blank optional pack weight stays
unknown. Missing own saleable share is not promoted to 100 percent.

A separate closed purpose in the existing private preference authority remembers
only the weight-unit choice in the current account namespace. Field length and
nursery choices retain their original behavior. Sample choice remains in memory;
unavailable or quota-limited storage is described without a false remembered
claim. Server output begins with kilograms and withholds private buyer fields
until the actor/sample context is checked.

Edits and both catalogue and own-record deletions share one persist path. They
carry the original actor, site, source and actual parent request. Only a matching
opaque write/readback acknowledgment clears the edited form or certifies a
checked device/sample save. Boolean, void, thrown and uncertain outcomes retain
entries and require explicit saved-target reload before retry. A possibly
performed callback does not establish rollback or authorise automatic retry.

The actual producer may supply its context after mount. A pristine editor can
adopt that first context; earlier unbound legacy intent requires explicit reload.
Same-value clones and equivalent contexts on the same private base retain valid
dirty intent. Changed source, actor, sample, lifecycle or lease holds old intent.
A legitimate foreign-site rebase uses the actual confirmed source for subsequent
edits, preserving newer foreign targets. Delayed parent-context handoff is tied
to that original result/request and rechecks its actual token and lease; an old
successful source key cannot bless a later dirty draft under a replacement lease.

Catalogue assessment/review weights use the chosen display unit without mutating
target or proposal data. Own-record mode never invokes catalogue assessment,
suggestion or nursery producers. Existing sowing acceptance and parent guards
remain in place; no new crop capacity, observed stock, delivery curve or local
farming approval is supplied.

Mounted and pure checks establish wiring and arithmetic, not real-browser layout,
phone accessibility, human translation approval or farm suitability.
