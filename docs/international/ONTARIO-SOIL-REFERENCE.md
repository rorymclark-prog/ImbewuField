# Ontario Soil Survey Complex reference

The Canada dossier links the Ontario Data Catalogue’s Soil survey entry. The ministry describes a digital compilation of historic surveys for Southern Ontario and parts of Northern Ontario. The underlying surveys use different map scales. Check the individual map and local field conditions before using the information for a site decision.

The catalogue links the Open Government Licence – Ontario for this record. The licence calls for any provider-specified credit and a link where possible. Its default credit applies when the provider gives no specific credit or multiple credits are impractical. The app links the catalogue. Layer coverage, downloadable data, schema and service behaviour remain unverified. [Ontario licence terms](https://www.ontario.ca/page/open-government-licence-ontario)

The catalogue metadata shows 10 January 2022 as the record modification date. Listed resources show a 29 November 2019 update. These dates describe their respective records. The dossier preserves an unknown publication date and requires separate evidence for survey vintage at a specific site.
