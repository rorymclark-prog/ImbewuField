# Grower Operations Asia Translation Review Notes

## Semantic Notes & Drafting Distinctions

1. **Planned vs. Observed**: Drafted strings distinguish unverified grower estimates (*planned* / नियोजित / পরিকল্পিত / terencana) from recorded field events (*observed* / देखा गया / চাক্ষুষ / teramati). Work dates represent grower scheduling estimates, not certified agronomic advice.
2. **Exclusive Boundaries vs. Logged Work**: Phrases for *exclusive end boundary* (अपवर्जित समाप्ति सीमा (शामिल नहीं) / বহির্ভূত শেষ সীমানা (অন্তর্ভুক্ত নয়) / batas akhir eksklusif (tidak termasuk)) and *availability boundary* denote non-inclusive date limits where the boundary date is excluded from the occupied interval. They do not record completed work, clearing, new plantings, or free-bed status.
3. **Operational Terminology**:
   - *Field entry* / *next-entry*: Rendered neutrally as field entry without imposing transplanting, covering both direct establishment and transplanting.
   - *Measured bed*: Rendered as measured agricultural bed (क्यारी / বেড / bedengan); bed elevation or raised status is unspecified in source.
4. **Token & Literal Preservation**: Placeholders `{crop}` and `{date}` and literals `DEMO` and `UTC` are preserved exactly. Checklist ticks explicitly do not create harvest, sales, or stock records.
