## 2. Entry/Display Boundary and Acceptance Rules

- **Canonical Metres and Independent Unit**: Expose an explicit `'m' | 'ft'` length selector independent of country/language/currency. `FieldBlockConfig`, `BedBlockSpec`, `PlacedItem.wM/hM`, and `LineShape.widthM` stay canonical metres; signed `angleDeg`, `count`, and normalised/pixel coordinates (`x`, `y`, `points`, `imgW`, `imgH`) never convert.
- **Zero-Drift Toggling and Reopening**: Unit switching or rendering never writes geometry or rounds canonical math. Track per-input dirty state: unedited `selected.fieldBlock` dimensions retain exact saved metre floats across reopen and `'m' <-> 'ft'` toggles; only edited length drafts convert via `ft * 0.3048`.
- **Parser/Source Decision (Coordinator)**: Accept signed plain decimals, including `'.5'`, `',5'`, `'10.'`; geometry rejects prohibited dimensions. Blank/sign-only/decimal-only inputs remain incomplete. Explicitly state that dot or comma means decimal; grouping, mixed separators, suffixes and survey-foot strings are invalid. This supersedes the narrower unsigned-parser proposal. Angles remain separate. Bind drafts to stable site/field/configuration/geometry/frame/exclusion values, not object identity/timestamps. Identical-value clones retain dirty drafts; changed same-field sources require reload. Separate account/sample authority must hide prior-context drafts.

## 3. Proposed Negative Test Scenarios (Not Executed)

1. **Round-trip toggle/reopen drift**: Toggling `'m' -> 'ft' -> 'm'` or reopening an unedited `FieldBlockConfig` preserves identical canonical metre floats and bed placements.
2. **Mixed-unit and incomplete drafts**: Mixed-unit/separator, blank or sign-only drafts cannot apply beds or be silently reinterpreted by toggles.
3. **Fingerprint sync and allocation guard**: Switching `selected?.id` or saved value fingerprint revokes prior drafts (while cloned identical-value renders keep dirty edits), and `allocatedIds` blocks layout edits.
