# Grower Operations Translation Notes

These pilot-scope unreviewed AI drafts cover the 31 grower operations panel and task-board messages across French (`fr`), Spanish (`es`), Brazilian Portuguese (`pt-BR`), and Swahili (`sw`).

## Terminology Choices & Variants

- **Bed (`fr` planche, `es` bancal, `pt-BR` canteiro, `sw` tuta)**:
  - `fr`: *Planche* is standard market-gardening terminology for defined worked beds; *parcelle* was avoided as it denotes field-scale plots.
  - `es`: *Bancal* represents raised/cultivated beds; regional Latin American variants like *cama de cultivo* or *era* remain valid alternatives.
  - `pt-BR`: *Canteiro* is the established Brazilian agricultural term for vegetable beds.
  - `sw`: *Tuta* (pl. *matuta*) accurately describes agricultural raised beds; *kitanda* was rejected due to domestic furniture connotations.

- **Field Entry & Clearance (`field-clear`, `next-entry`)**:
  - *Field-clear* is rendered as *libération du champ* (`fr`), *liberación del campo* (`es`), *liberação do campo* (`pt-BR`), and *kusafisha shamba* (`sw`), emphasizing ground turnover rather than crop destruction.
  - *Next-entry* (*prochaine entrée* / *próxima entrada* / *kuingia tena*) reflects availability boundary scheduling without implying an automatic planting.

- **Estimates vs Observed Observations**:
  - The boundary between unverified grower planning estimates (*estimations* / *estimaciones* / *estimativas* / *makadirio*) and confirmed field observations (*travaux observés* / *trabajo observado* / *trabalho observado* / *kazi iliyoonekana*) is strictly preserved.

- **Checklist vs Production/Stock/Sales Records**:
  - Checklist interactions (*coches* / *marcas* / *marcações* / *kuweka tiki*) are explicitly separated from verified harvest, stock, and sales logs across all locales.
