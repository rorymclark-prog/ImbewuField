# Country foundation — release-note draft

Adds an international evidence review workspace at `/international` with
provisional Kenya, UK, US and Brazil dossiers. Reviewers can inspect separate
biodiversity, extension, weather/frost, soil and plant-law evidence, see explicit
source/access gaps and try one bounded public NASA POWER temperature reference
for a coarse demo place. Source links, model/data provenance and unknown values
remain visible.

This is a research and engineering foundation. No international production plan,
local sowing calendar, yield, species recommendation, plant-law permission or
South African fallback is enabled. The English interface does not imply translated
or locally reviewed farming guidance. Source dossiers have different research
depth; wider countries and Europe remain an unreviewed research queue.

Existing SA crop, climate, ecology, geometry, lessons and saved data are unchanged.
No dependencies, paid generation, deployment or PLAN_VERSION change.
