# Invoice currencies and protected records

This milestone lets a grower explicitly choose ZAR, KES, GBP, USD, EUR or BRL for a new local invoice. Country, interface language and browser locale do not choose the currency. Changing an unsaved choice clears prior prices and asks for agreed amounts; it never converts a Rand guide or remembered Rand price. Saved denomination is immutable. A historical invoice without a denomination stays historical Rand, while an unreadable present value remains held and retained.

## Record authority

The invoice model owns one protected local book containing saved invoices, pending sale links and numbering. Cutover retains exact evidence from all three previous storage keys, including absence. Diverging old writers, uncertain writes, unreadable rows and duplicate identifiers cannot be treated as a clean empty book. Unknown saved metadata is preserved. The shared storage factory retains the journal's existing physical keys, envelope, recovery, private proof and lease protocol; it does not migrate unrelated stores.

Every invoice mutation requires its original admitted source. A new lease cannot authorize fields opened earlier as a read-only snapshot: the grower explicitly reopens the current invoice or starts a new one. Quick forms use the same source hook. Cloud Rand-sale mutations check the exact saved or pending row, original actor/book and originating form before asynchronous reads and before queuing effects. A UID that leaves and returns does not revive an old handler. Timeouts retain pending evidence rather than inventing rollback or a second invoice.

## Documents and finance

The shared document model uses the existing RecordedMoney formatter for explicit currencies and preserves historical Rand formatting. Preview, print and PDF use that one captured document; stale success and failure completions are withheld. Download PDF is an explicit action alongside native Share PDF and Print. Practice documents carry DEMO in the shared document heading.

The current shared sales store remains Rand-only. Foreign invoices stay on the device, with a clear separation message; they are not sent as empty drafts that delete earlier sales. Affected Rand income, margins, charts and lender exports are withheld rather than converted or reported as zero. Readable produce quantities and costs remain separate. Missing invoice rows or an unavailable book also hold incomplete results; no fake invoice placeholders are introduced. Coherent read-only books remain readable. Currency-separated financial reporting, currency-aware agreed-price presets and cross-device invoice migration are future work.
