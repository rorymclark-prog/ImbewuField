# Speaker review sheets — money book and Invoice (Sesotho, Tshivenda, Xitsonga, isiZulu)

One CSV per language, for a fluent speaker (ideally one who farms or works with smallholders) to check the
**machine drafts** now live in the app. Nothing here has been approved by a speaker. English is the source authority.
Open in Google Sheets or Excel (UTF-8; Tshivenda and Xitsonga diacritics are preserved).

## Columns
- **priority**: `1 held` (the app shows English; a machine could not confirm a word — your replacement is the most useful
  thing here), `2 check` (shipped, but a money word or a repaired/one-review draft), `3 routine` (shipped, passed one blind machine review).
- **status**: `shipped (machine draft)` is what farmers see beside English; `held - shows English` is not shown.
- **machine draft**: for held rows this is the last rejected attempt, or empty if none.
- **why check / machine note**: the reason it was held, or what to look at.
- **speaker verdict**: `OK`, `CHANGE` (put the better wording in *speaker replacement*) or `BAD` (do not show it).
- Keep `{placeholders}` (for example `{price}`, `{kg}`) exactly as in the English.

## What to look at first
Words that touch money or a farmer's action: amount vs price, income vs cost, sale vs purchase, "this phone only" (a
photo that is saved on one device), Delete vs Remove vs Export, lender, cash / mobile payment, units. Wherever a word is
wrong, say so: showing English is always better than showing a wrong word.

## What happens with the returned sheet
Rows marked CHANGE or OK with a replacement can be loaded by id into `draft-<lang>.json` (the app reads
`lib/records-regional-drafts-data.ts`, generated by `node scripts/build-records-drafts.mjs`); rows marked BAD are held.
A speaker-approved row would then be recorded as such, separate from the machine status.
