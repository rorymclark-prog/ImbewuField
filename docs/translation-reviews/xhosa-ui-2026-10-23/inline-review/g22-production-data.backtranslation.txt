311. To prevent sheep scab, all small animals on the farm must be treated with medicines registered under Act 36 of 1947, and infected sheep treated twice, 8 to 10 days apart.
312. Every sheep owner must register an official identification mark and mark his sheep with it, under the Animal Identification Act.
313. Sheep may be slaughtered only at an abattoir, except for a farmer's own food or cultural or religious purposes.
314. There must be a treatment plan for sheep scab: this disease has a high economic and care cost.
315. Although FMD exists in South Africa, export of wool continues under the OIE (World Organisation for Animal Health) closing rule.
316. Wool from scab-infected sheep must be destroyed rather than sent to a buyer, where it would be marked as scab wool.
