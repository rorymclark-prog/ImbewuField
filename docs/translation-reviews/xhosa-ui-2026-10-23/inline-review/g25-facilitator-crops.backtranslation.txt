484. Each harvest number and date here comes from a published source or is marked as an estimate confirmed here locally — tap for method and sources.
485. Harvest points use the careful end of published KZN commercial references where the crop is listed. They compare plans; they don't estimate a household's harvest. Temperature/little-frost windows, distance and time use official tables where those tables cover the crop; safe methods from the catalogue and regional patterns still need local confirmation. Bed occupation uses the high end of published ripening/harvest so that two crops are not booked onto one ground, but the source says these times are estimates and assumes good conditions; real crops may ripen later. Every sowing or transplanting of the next season depends on considering that the previous crop is finished and bed clean. The app does not divide the number of a crop cycle into invented monthly kilograms. Recommend automatically places only crops with confirmed yield, time and field-distance support. The household plan starts from a different supported mix; the direct list of crops is a strict list. It needs irrigation because tight succession without a farm water plan is not safe. Its squeezing method is a visible way of estimating, not evidence of the overall highest yield or of real row adjustment. The commercial placement of crops is based on new weight in the crop cycle; it is not profit, nutrition or evidence of buyer demand.
