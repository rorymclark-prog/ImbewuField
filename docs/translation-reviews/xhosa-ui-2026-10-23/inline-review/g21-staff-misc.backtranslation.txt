163. What it does
164. Tools used by a supported farming programme
165. Farm design based on satellite
166. Draw a boundary on the satellite view of a real farm, and plan beds, water systems, land works and buildings directly on that land — not a general template.
167. Crop planning by season
168. A board of tasks for each season of the year — preparing, sowing, weeding, harvesting — built on the real planting time of each crop according to the rain patterns of South Africa.
169. Planting catalogue of 197 kinds
170. Each crop available in the farm design is a true kind with a source — traditional fruit trees, fruit trees and offspring from overseas, herbs, ground-covering and climbing crops — each with its own water need, size of ripening and use.
171. Records of produce, sales and costs
172. Farmers record kilograms produced, kilograms sold, and money coming in and going out — building a real picture of farm money, season after season.
173. AI site reports
174. A specific site report — climate, soil, water and garden design — produced for the exact place of the farm, saved so that it can be read again without making it again.
175. A 10-module training course
176. Ten modules, 33 lessons, covering the ethics of permaculture, reading the surrounding land, water harvesting, soil health, seeds, groups of crops, food forests, livestock and market gardening — each lesson has a short quiz.
177. Downloading the course to work offline
178. A whole module of the course — text, pictures and audio — is downloaded in town, so that the lesson works at home too with no signal at all.
179. English and ten languages of South Africa: isiZulu, isiXhosa, Afrikaans, Sesotho, Sepedi, Setswana, Xitsonga, Tshivenda, siSwati and isiNdebele.
