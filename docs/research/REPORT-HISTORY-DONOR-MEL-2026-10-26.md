# Historical comparisons and donor indicator agreements

Requested by Rory on 26 October 2026, including production publication.
The existing report composer, typography, colours, tables and PDF writer remain
the visual authority. This changes report pictures; PLAN_VERSION is unchanged.

## Published reference

The [IDC SEF page](https://www.idc.co.za/sef/) currently links the
[May 2025 strategic implementing partner call](https://www-idc-co-za.b-cdn.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Terms-of-Reference-Strategic-Implementing-Partners-Open-Call-2025.pdf).
Section 7 distinguishes routine reporting, employment, community benefit,
organisational capacity and participant capabilities. The optional app reference
points to these evidence areas; it does not certify a programme or replace its
signed agreement. Financial evidence belongs to programme accounts. Employment
evidence belongs to the designated system. Farm receipts, visits, session
attendance and self-reported skills are not substitutes.

ACT/SEF was selected as the first reference because the existing app already
names that programme. No donor obligation is inferred from a logo, project title
or generic category. Other donors can use their own agreement and indicator codes.

## Recorded donor commitments

Optional metadata extends the existing milestone record: donor, shareable
agreement reference, indicator code, measurement dates, population/denominator,
disaggregation/privacy rules, frequency and observation basis. Existing unit,
baseline, target, method, due date and dated evidence remain authoritative.
No parallel outcome store or fabricated result is added.

Only authorised managers can write these fields. They follow the existing
revision check, audit history, sample isolation and publication controls.
Publishing an indicator shares its donor metadata and evidence; the form states
this. Codes do not establish verification. Missing baselines/targets are visible.
Donor filters narrow milestones only; assessments retain their project scope.

## Historical comparisons

- Farmer reports select a date within a calendar month, season or year and compare
  with the preceding calendar period or the same period a year earlier. Calendar
  boundaries reuse the Records authority, including summer across New Year.
  Paid invoices replace linked sales once. Units stay separate. Missing/invalid
  entries withhold the affected comparison. Current periods are incomplete.
- Programme comparisons request 24 months of the existing authorised UTC monthly
  series, so both years are available for year-on-year comparisons. The network
  views retain their existing 12-month default. No new source collections or consent
  bypass is added; the same consent-filtered ledgers are bucketed for longer. Missing
  months and unreadable books are unavailable. No organisation-wide series is
  relabelled as a cohort, and current consent is not a historical participant panel.
- Mentor and facilitator comparisons use an equal preceding number of inclusive
  calendar days, with the same participant/project/record-owner filters. Current
  assignment and action status are not reconstructed as historical facts.
- Milestones compare the latest corrected readings at two cutoffs. The same
  carried-forward observation does not become zero activity. Reading differences
  are labelled according to snapshot, cumulative or separate-period basis.
  Donor result tables use observations inside the agreed measurement period.

All reports use records available now, including corrections entered later.
Unpaid invoices, current actions and assessment snapshots remain explicitly current.
No percent impact, household profit, payroll approval or causal claim is inferred.
