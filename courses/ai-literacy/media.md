# Media · Course 5 · AI Literacy

Art rules for every NEW item: flat, warm, instructional illustration with thick dark outlines;
palette Forest #1F4D2B, Mid-green #2E6B3A, Leaf #A8D88A, Ochre #C07A1E, Water #235E86 (water
only), Paper #F7F2E9, Ink #20190F. **No text inside the artwork.** Southern hemisphere: sun in
the north. People look like Black South African smallholders and youth of mixed ages and
genders. Generic plant shapes.

**Phones and screens in this course:** generic entry-level Android-style handsets, no brand
logos, no real app icons or trademarks. Screens show simple shapes only — chat bubbles as
rounded rectangles with grey lines, a keyboard as a grid, a microphone, a camera frame, a
padlock. Never readable words, never a real company's logo, never a real person's face on a
"fake video".

Animations: silent, 8–30 s, loopable, 1280×720, ≤ 3 MB, poster frame `.jpg` alongside.

Totals: **35 NEW** (14 poster-art, 11 images, 4 animations, 6 photo/screen sets) · **4 REUSE**.

## A-ANI-01 · The guessing machine
- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: A-D1-04, A-D1-S1
- Length: 16 s loop, silent

**Brief:** Picture-only sentence game. 0–4 s: many small picture tiles (cups of tea, coffee mugs,
water glasses, bowls of amahewu — tea cups most numerous) stream from the left into a simple
phone-shaped machine. 4–8 s: a half-sentence picture appears (a sunrise + a person + a cup
outline with a question mark). 8–11 s: the machine "thinks" (bubble pulses) and outputs the
most common tile — a teacup — with a confident bounce. 11–14 s: beside it, a learner figure holds
a different drink (amahewu); a small question mark appears between them. 14–16 s: fade back to
the start. No text.
