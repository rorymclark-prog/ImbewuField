# Resources · Course 5 · AI Literacy

Kit list, per-group materials, what learners bring, substitutes and budget. Counts assume
**30 learners, 6 groups of 5, 2 facilitators**. Prices are a **2026 guide only — check locally**;
phone, data and print prices change often and differ between towns and networks.

## 2. Connectivity and data

**Option A — a Wi-Fi hotspot (preferred).** A mobile Wi-Fi router ("MiFi") with a data SIM, or a
facilitator phone used as a hotspot. One password for the room, written on 6 small cards.
Test on Day 0 with 6 phones connected at once (day-0 §3).

**Option B — data bundles.** Buy a bundle for each **group phone** (not every learner) with a
voucher or "send data" from a facilitator account. Only if the hotspot fails.

**Rules:** no learner uses their own data or airtime for course tasks. No big downloads on course
Wi-Fi (updates, videos, games). App installs only if the programme asked for them.

### Data budget (work it out on Day 0)

What uses data in this course (rough guide — measure on Day 0):
- Text questions and answers to an AI chat: small (a few hundred KB for a session of questions).
- **Photos** sent to an AI or the app: the biggest use — each photo can be a few hundred KB to a
  few MB. Standard quality helps.
- Opening apps and web pages, loading the ImbewuField app screens: moderate.
- Videos: large — the course doesn't stream video on data; clips play from the tablet offline.

## 6. Learners bring

- Their smartphone, **fully charged**, and **their charger** (if they have one).
- One real question about their garden, farm, selling or family food.
- Nothing else. **No airtime or data needed.**
- Not required: an ImbewuField account. If the app track is on, the programme sets up accounts
  before the course (not during it).

## 7. Substitutes

| If you don't have… | Use… |
|---|---|
| Any phones at all / no signal | The full no-phone path: AI desk + B-cards, plant photo cards, paper phone, printed drafts |
| A hotspot | One data bundle per group phone |
| A kitchen scale | A 2 ℓ bottle of water as the known weight (≈ 2 kg); or borrow a scale from a spaza |
| Power at the venue | Power banks charged the night before; phones on low-power mode; screens dim |
| A tablet for clips | Skip clips — every clip is optional; the posters carry the lesson |
| Laminator | Clear packing tape over the cards, or card in plastic sleeves |
| Real till slips | D-05 printed slip |
| Coloured paper | White paper with a drawn circle / triangle / square and the colour word |

## 9. After the course

- Wipe any course accounts or chats from the spare phones; delete the fake voice note.
- Count laminated sets back; replace lost cards before the next course.
- Record data used per day in the facilitator report — it makes the next budget accurate.
- Recapture app screenshots (A-PHO-01, 03–06) if the app has changed.
