# Prompt cards and printed packs

Everything learners hold in their hands in this course. Print on A6 card (four to an A4 sheet)
unless it says otherwise. Laminate group sets — they are reused every course.

Every card has a line **Local language: ________** for the facilitator (or a first-language
speaker who farms) to write the local version **before** the course. A translation is read back
by a first-language speaker before printing (poster-standards.md, *Translation*).

| Set | What | Used in | Per group / learner |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P-01…P-20** | Farm prompt cards (weak question + the Question Hand version) | A-D1-S2, A-D1-S4 | 1 set per group |
| **B-00…B-21** | AI answer cards ("the AI said…") — some wrong on purpose | AI desk (Day 1), A-D2-S1 | 1 set for the AI desk; 4 per group on Day 2 |
| **T-01…T-12** | Traffic-light topic cards | A-D2-S1 | 1 set per group |
| **C-01…C-06** | Check-it cards (the ladder in your hand) | A-D2-S1, A-D1-S4 | 1 set per group |
| **H-01…H-16** | Share-or-not cards | A-D2-S2 | 1 set per group |
| **S-01…S-10** | Scam-spotting cards | A-D2-S2 | 1 set per group |
| **D-00…D-05** | AI draft sheets with mistakes (A4) | A-D2-S3 | 1 set per group |
| **K-01…K-09** | Take-home cards, sheets and picture sets | all | see each |

**The answer keys are for facilitators only.** Print them on a different colour, keep them in
the flip folder, and never put them in group envelopes.

## P · Farm prompt cards (20)

Front of each card: the **weak question** in big letters and a small drawing of the situation
(no text in the drawing). Back: the **Question Hand** version, finger by finger, and the
local-language line. Groups use the back only *after* they have tried to write their own.

Names and places are examples — swap them for local ones when you print.

**Weak:** *Spinach holes?*

it be? What should I look for under the leaves?"

## B · AI answer cards (22) — "the AI said…"

For the **AI desk** (Day 1: a facilitator plays the AI and reads these aloud, fast and sure)
and for **Find the mistakes** (Day 2 S1). Each card shows the question and the answer. The
**answer key** after the cards says what's wrong. B-01 to B-20 answer P-01 to P-20.

Print the front only (question + answer). **Do not mark the mistakes on the card.**

**B-00 · "Spinach?"** (the weak question)
*AI said:* "Spinach is a leafy green vegetable that originated in ancient Persia. It is rich in
iron, vitamin A, vitamin C and folate. It is grown all over the world, especially in China and
the United States. Spinach can be eaten raw in salads or cooked. Here is a recipe for creamed
spinach: melt butter, add cream and garlic…"

**B-01 · Holes in spinach**
*AI said:* "Small holes are often caused by insects like flea beetles or caterpillars, or by
snails at night. 1. Look under the leaves in the morning and evening and remove what you find
by hand. 2. Mulch and keep the bed healthy; pick badly damaged leaves. 3. If it gets worse, try a
home-made garlic and chilli spray on a few leaves first. Ask a local farmer what they see too."

**B-02 · When to plant tomatoes**
*AI said:* "Tomatoes grow best when it is cool and dry. Sow seed in March and plant out in May
or June. Tomatoes can handle light frost. Water every day."

**B-03 · Yellow maize**
*AI said:* "Yellowing from the tip of the lower leaves often means the plant is short of
nitrogen. Heavy rain can wash nitrogen out, and waterlogged soil makes it worse. 1. Put
well-rotted kraal manure or compost along the rows and water it in. 2. Next season, grow beans
before or with the maize. 3. Check that water drains away. Ask your extension officer if many
plants are affected."

**B-04 · Hens stopped laying**
*AI said:* "Hens stop laying because they are old. You should replace them with new layers from
the shop. You can also add antibiotic powder to the water to help them lay again."

**B-05 · Soil dries too fast**
*AI said:* "1. Cover the soil with black plastic sheeting from the hardware store (about R600).
2. Water in the middle of the day when it is hottest. 3. Add compost to help the soil hold
water. 4. Plant close together to shade the soil."

**B-06 · Compost smells bad**
*AI said:* "A rotten smell usually means the heap is too wet and has too much green material and
not enough air. 1. Mix in dry brown material — dry leaves, dry grass, torn cardboard. 2. Turn the
heap to let air in. 3. Keep it damp like a squeezed-out cloth, not soaking. It should smell like
soil in a week or two."

**B-07 · Seedlings falling over**
*AI said:* "This is damping off. Water the seedlings more often — twice a day — so they grow
strong. Keep the trays in a warm, closed place with no wind."

**B-08 · Aphids on cabbage**
*AI said:* "These sound like aphids. 1. Spray them off with water, or squash them by hand.
2. Home-made spray: soak a crushed garlic bulb and 2 chillies in 1 litre of water overnight,
strain, add 1 teaspoon of plain liquid soap. 3. Test on a few leaves first and wait a day.
Spray in the late afternoon, under the leaves. Keep it away from eyes; wash hands. Ladybirds eat
aphids — don't spray them."

**B-09 · Price for spinach**
*AI said:* "The average price of spinach in South Africa is R45 per bunch. Sell at R45 to make
the best profit."

**B-10 · Where to sell extra**
*AI said:* "1. Neighbours — a WhatsApp message each week. 2. The local spaza or tuck shop.
3. School or crèche kitchens. 4. Church days and pension pay days. 5. A stall at the taxi rank
(check if you need permission from the municipality)."

**B-11 · Sick goat**
*AI said:* "Your goat has pneumonia. Inject 5 ml of [antibiotic] into the muscle every day for
5 days. It will be better in a week. There is no need to call anyone."

**B-12 · How much spray**
*AI said:* "Mix 50 ml of the product in 20 litres of water. Spray every 3 days until the pests
are gone. You can use it on vegetables right up to harvest."

**B-13 · Save pumpkin seed**
*AI said:* "1. Cut open a ripe pumpkin and scoop out the seeds. 2. Wash off the flesh. 3. Dry
the seeds in the sun in a closed plastic bag. 4. Store them in the bag on a sunny window sill.
All pumpkin seed, including F1 hybrids, grows true to type."

**B-14 · Bean spacing**
*AI said:* "Plant bush beans about 10–15 cm apart in the row — about a hand's width — and about
3–4 cm deep. Leave about 40–50 cm between rows, or plant in a grid across a 1 m bed. Always check
the seed packet — varieties differ."

**B-15 · Water from my roof**
*AI said:* "Roof area = 5 × 4 = 20 m². 20 m² × 800 mm = 1 600 litres a year. So a 200 litre
drum is enough to catch all your roof water."

**B-16 · Feed my family from the garden**
*AI said:* "For a healthy plate, grow and eat kale, quinoa, avocados, blueberries and salmon.
Buy olive oil and nuts. Try a smoothie every morning."

**B-17 · Termites in the beds**
*AI said:* "Termites mostly eat dry, dead plant material and are part of the soil's recycling.
1. Keep the mulch and soil moist — termites like dry material. 2. Use well-rotted compost, not
dry stalks, around young plants. 3. Keep plants strong with water and compost. Ask local farmers
what works in your area."

**B-18 · Frost**
*AI said:* "In the Free State, frost usually comes from December to February. Cover your beds
at night in those months with grass or old sacks."

**B-19 · Loan for a borehole**
*AI said:* "This sounds like a good offer. No credit check makes it easy. Pay the R1 500 admin
fee quickly so you don't lose the loan."

**B-20 · Keep money records**
*AI said:* "Only write down the money you receive. Money you spend is not important for a small
farm. Add it up at the end of the year."

**B-21 · "How many kg?"** (Weigh station, Day 1 S3, no-phone path)
*AI said:* "That is about 5 kg of vegetables." *(Say it very sure, whatever is in the bag.)*

## S · Scam-spotting cards (10)

Print as a phone-screen drawing with the message typed in the bubble (typeset, not in the
artwork). Names and numbers are invented. **Never use real bank or company names** on these cards.

**S-01** · WhatsApp from an unknown number
"Hi Ma, this is my new number, my phone broke. Please send R300 on eWallet, I'll pay you back
tomorrow. Don't call, the line is bad."

**S-02** · SMS
"SASSA NOTICE: Your grant payment is ON HOLD. Confirm your ID and bank details at
sassa-pay-update.co within 24 hours or lose your grant."

**S-03** · WhatsApp from a friend's name
"Hey, I sent you a 6-digit code by mistake, please send it back to me quickly."

**S-04** · Facebook job ad
"FARM SUPERVISOR WANTED. R12 000 per month. No experience needed. Pay R350 for uniform and
registration to secure your place. Only 5 places left!"

**S-05** · SMS
"Congratulations! You qualify for a R20 000 loan TODAY. No credit check. Pay R1 500 insurance
first and the money is in your account in 1 hour."

**S-06** · WhatsApp from your mentor's number (saved in your phone for a year)
"Hi Thandi, the training on Tuesday moved to the church hall, still 10:00. Please bring your
record book. Call me if you can't come."

**S-07** · Voice note from an unknown number
*(A young man's voice)* "Ma, it's me, my phone fell in the water, this is my friend's phone,
please send R200 airtime quickly, I'll explain later."

**S-08** · Video on Facebook
A famous businessman (face and voice look real) says: "I'm giving back to South Africa. Put R500
into my AI trading app and get R5 000 in 7 days. Click below."

**S-09** · WhatsApp message with a government-looking logo
"Farmer Support Grant 2026. You qualify for R50 000 in seeds and inputs. Pay R499 processing fee
to our agent Mr Dlamini to release your grant. Offer ends Friday."

**S-10** · SMS that looks like your bank
"Did you just make a purchase of R2 499 at an online shop? If NOT, call us on the number on the
back of your card."

### S · Answer key (facilitators only)

| Card | Answer | Warning signs · what to do |
|---|---|---|
| S-01 | **Scam** | New number, money, hurry, "don't call". Call your child on the **old** number, or someone who is with them |
| S-02 | **Scam** | Link, hurry, asks for ID and bank details. SASSA does not ask for your details by SMS link. Go to the SASSA office or use the official toll-free number (0800 60 10 11 — check it on official SASSA material) |
| S-03 | **Scam** | Asks for a code — they want to steal your WhatsApp. Never send it. Your friend's account may already be stolen: phone them |
| S-04 | **Scam** | Pay to get a job, too good to be true, hurry. Real employers don't charge you to work |
| S-05 | **Scam** | Upfront fee, no credit check, hurry. Real lenders are **NCR-registered** and don't take money first |
| S-06 | **Real** | Known number, no money, no link, no hurry, you can phone to check. Not every message is a scam |
| S-07 | **Scam until checked** | Voice can be copied. Ask the family code question; call back on his real number |
| S-08 | **Scam (probably a deepfake)** | Famous face, too good to be true, click here. Check his real page or real news — but just the promise is enough to say no |
| S-09 | **Scam** | Pay a fee to get a grant, a named "agent", deadline. Government programmes don't charge processing fees. Ask the extension officer or local agriculture office |
| S-10 | **Not sure — check** | Banks do send alerts like this. But **don't** reply with details or click links. Phone the number **on the back of your own card**, not a number in the message |

### D-00 · Human or Machine? (energiser, A-D2-S3 — read aloud)

2. "Dear valued neighbours, I am delighted to announce the availability of fresh, organically
   grown spinach, harvested with care to nourish your family." *(machine)*
3. "Eggs finished today sorry, more on Sat, sorry!" *(person)*
4. "Discover the goodness of farm-fresh eggs! Packed with protein and flavour, our eggs are the
   perfect start to your day. Order now!" *(machine)*

Point: machine text is often smooth, fancy and general. But not always — sometimes people write
like that and machines write short. Don't rely on guessing; rely on checking.

### D-01 · "The AI wrote this" — WhatsApp selling message
> FRESH from Sipho's Farm!!! Premium organic spinach only R35 per bunch, and a dozen free-range
> eggs for R15! FREE delivery anywhere in Durban. Certified organic. Order now — limited stock!
> Payment to ID 9001015800087. — Sipho

**Mistakes (key):** price R35 is not the group's price (use their notes); eggs price too low /
wrong; "free delivery anywhere in Durban" — not true, no car; "certified organic" — not
certified (don't claim it); an **ID number** in a message (never); too fancy for neighbours;
no language choice. Corrected version: short, true, local language + English.

### D-02 · "The AI wrote this" — price list
> **PRICE LIST (prices include 15% VAT)**
> Spinach — R8 per kg
> Cabbage — R35 each
> Carrots — R12 per bunch
> Eggs — R40 per tray (30)
> Tomatoes — R5 per kg

**Mistakes (key):** VAT line — small growers usually aren't VAT-registered, so don't add or
mention VAT (ask Course 4 facilitator if unsure); spinach sold by the bunch, not kg; prices
don't match the group's notes (check each against the flipchart); eggs and tomatoes far too low;
items the group doesn't sell. Corrected version: only what they sell, their unit, their price.

### D-03 · "The AI wrote this" — CV
> **Lindiwe Mokoena** · ID 0203150678081 · 12 Main Road, [village] · Age 23
> **Education:** Diploma in Agriculture, University of the Free State (2022)
> **Experience:** Farm Manager, 50-hectare commercial vegetable farm (2019–2024). Managed 30 staff.
> **Skills:** tractor driving, irrigation design, drone mapping, fluent French.
> **References:** available on request.

**Mistakes (key):** ID number, home address and age — don't put these on a CV you send to
strangers or post online (give them later, to a checked employer); **invented** diploma, farm
manager job, 30 staff, drone mapping, French — **never lie on a CV**; real experience is enough:
"Community garden member, 2 years: seedlings, compost, water harvesting, record keeping, selling
at the pension pay point"; add the course certificates (attendance) and a real reference with
permission.

### D-04 · "The AI wrote this" — funding request (half page) with a "results so far" paragraph
> **Request for R2 000 000 — Sizanani Community Garden (NPO 123-456)**
> Our garden feeds 500 families every day. Since we started, our harvest has doubled every
> month and we have created 45 permanent jobs. **Results so far:** 12 tonnes of vegetables,
> R300 000 in sales, zero crop losses. We need a 10 000 litre tank to continue this success.

**Mistakes (key):** the amount doesn't match what a tank costs (get real quotes — check
locally); the NPO number is invented (use your real one or say you are registering);
500 families, doubled every month, 45 jobs, 12 tonnes, R300 000, zero losses — all invented or
unmeasured. **Use only what your records show** (harvest book, money book, register). A true,
modest request is more believable. If AI helped you write it, check every number against your
records — and be honest if asked.

> Compost 30 dm³ ………… R45.00
