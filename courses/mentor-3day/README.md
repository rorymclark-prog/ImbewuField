# Course 2 · Walking With Farmers — the mentor course

**For:** extension officers, community farmer-mentors, lead farmers and field supervisors on
ACT·SEF-type food-security programmes. They will support the farmers who did Course 1
(*Grow Food, Grow Strong*) through one growing season.
**Class size:** 16–24 is ideal (the farm visits need one host farmer per trio). Max 30.
Groups of 5 for practicals; **trios** (Mentor · Recorder · Observer) for farm visits.
**Two facilitators** per course, plus a driver/logistics person on Day 2.
**Two ways to run it (same outcomes):**

| Mode | Length | File |
|---|---|---|
| **In person** (default) | Day 0 prep + 3 days, 08:30–16:00 | this file, day-0 … day-3 |
| **Online / blended** | 6 weekly sessions of ~2 h + field tasks between | [online-programme.md](online-programme.md) |

**Why 08:30–16:00 and not 09:00?** Mentors are programme staff and lead farmers, not a
general public class. The extra 30 minutes pays for the Day 2 farm-visit travel without
cutting a session, and the day still ends at 16:00 so people who use taxis, and women who
care for children and elders, get home before dark. **If most learners rely on taxis that
arrive at 09:00**, start at 09:00 and take the cuts marked **✂ 09:00 start** in each day file
(they add up to 30 minutes per day). Do not run later than 16:00.

## What mentors leave with

By the end of Day 3 every mentor can:

1. **Explain** the difference between walking beside a farmer (mentor) and checking on a
   farmer (inspector), and **name** the mentor's promise (boundaries and ethics).
2. **Listen** actively and **ask** open questions that help a farmer find their own answer;
   **start** with what is working (a strength-based visit).
3. **Coach** a practical skill from Course 1 with *I do · We do · You do* and **record** the
   farmer's skill as *demonstrated* or *needs further support*.
4. **Make** an action agreement with a farmer: *what · who · by when · how we'll know* — and
   **close** it only with a date and evidence of what changed.
5. **Run** a 45–60 minute individual visit using **the Visit Hand** (5 steps) on a real farm,
   and **record** it on the paper visit form or in the app (Mentor workspace → Record visit).
6. **Sort** a caseload with the **traffic light** (red · amber · green), **plan** a week and a
   6-month season for ~40 farmers in 4 clusters, and **choose** the right contact for each
   farmer: individual visit, cluster visit, field day or phone check-in.
7. **Plan and run** a 2–3 hour cluster visit at a host garden for 8–12 farmers.
8. **Coach** a farmer's garden and money records — **on the app track** (Finances, till slip,
   Invoice builder, Reports) **or the paper track** (record book, money book, duplicate
   invoice book) — with a 10–15 minute on-visit script.
9. **Handle** hard situations (drought, theft, conflict, drop-out, dependency) and **know**
   when and to whom to refer (safeguarding, gender-based violence, health, SASSA).

They take home: the **visit playbook** (printed), 20 paper visit forms, an observer checklist,
their own traffic-light caseload sheet, a first-month week plan, a season plan, the A5 poster
cards, and a certificate of attendance.

## The visit model at the heart of the course: **the Visit Hand**

Five fingers, five steps. Every mentor has their hand with them on every visit.

| Finger | Step | App field it fills (Mentor → Record visit) |
|---|---|---|
| Thumb | **1. Look back** — "Last time we agreed … Show me." | Last agreed action → *Completed on* + *Completion evidence* |
| Pointing finger | **2. Walk & look** — walk the garden together; farmer leads | *Observations and issues*, photos, *Site location* (GPS optional) |
| Middle (tallest) | **3. Listen & ask** — the farmer's own priority | *Support requested*, support area (garden & water · soil & seedlings · harvest & food access · records · learning) |
| Ring finger | **4. Try it together** — coach one skill, hands on | *Skill observed* + *Observation result* |
| Little finger | **5. Agree & record** — one action, one owner, one date | *Agreed action*, *Responsible person*, *Follow-up date* |

Details: [visit-playbook.md](visit-playbook.md).

## Switching the app track on or off

Every farmer exercise and every mentor record exists twice: an **app** way and a **paper** way.
The outcome is the same. Decide on **Day 0** (in person) or before **Week 1** (online):

| Switch | Who decides | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Programme: APP ON** | Programme manager, after the phone check (app-track.md §2) | Mentors record in Mentor workspace → Record visit; farmers who can use a phone follow the app exercises; others go paper |
| **Programme: APP OFF** | Programme manager | Everything on paper: visit forms, record book, money book, duplicate invoice book. No phones needed |
| **Per farmer** (inside an APP ON programme) | Mentor and farmer together, at the first visit | Column "Track: A / P / A+P" on the caseload sheet. A+P = the farmer keeps paper and a helper (often a youth in the house) enters it in the app |

**In the day files**, every app session is written as a pair of equal length:
`[APP]` and `[PAPER]`. Teach the one that fits your programme; if the class is mixed, teach
the [PAPER] version to everyone first (it is the backbone) and add the [APP] version for the
app-track mentors in the same time, in a separate group with a co-facilitator.
**The paper track is never second best.** A programme with no phones is a complete programme.
Full detail: [app-track.md](app-track.md).

## Where the mentor course sits

- It follows **Course 1**: mentors use the farmer's 90-day plan (F-P21) and the farmer skills
  checklist (`shared/forms.md` §4) as the starting point of the season.
- The Course 1 **demonstration garden**, handed to a community group on F-Day 5, becomes the
  first **cluster-visit site**.
- The *"topics I want to learn more about"* sticker sheet from the Course 1 evaluation
  (forms.md §6) is the mentor's first list of what to coach.
- **Course 4 (Farmer Money)** goes deeper on the money coaching of Day 3 S3.
- **Course 5 (AI Literacy)** helps app-track mentors use Lima safely.

## Research the course draws on (in plain words)

| Idea | Where you meet it |
|---|---|
| Adults learn by doing, reflecting and trying again (experiential learning cycle) | Every session: Look · Try · Talk · Take home |
| Farmers learn best from farmers they trust (farmer-to-farmer extension, lead-farmer models, farmer field schools) | Cluster visits, host gardens, field days |
| Active listening and open questions help people own their decisions (coaching, motivational interviewing) | Day 1 S2 |
| Start from strengths (appreciative inquiry, positive deviance — "who is already doing well with the same resources?") | Visit step 1–2; cluster visits at the best garden |
| Show–do–watch (I do · We do · You do, gradual release) | Day 1 S3, visit step 4 |
| Specific, owned, dated action plans with a check (goal-setting research) | Action agreement |
| Evidence of change, not activity counts | "Show me what changed" rule |

## Delivery rhythm for facilitator teams

- Day 0 is never the same day as Day 1. Host farmers are confirmed **in person** the week before.
- Max three courses back to back, then a week at home. Day 2 is long for facilitators: the
  driver/logistics person handles transport, lunch and the cluster site.
- Lead and co-facilitator swap lead on alternate sessions. The co-facilitator keeps the
  session sheet (teacher-talk tally) every session.
- **Certificates** are for attending at least 2 of 3 days in person, or 5 of 6 weeks plus 3 of
  6 field tasks online. They are attendance certificates, **not** a qualification.
