# Resources — Walking With Farmers

Kit, printing, per-group materials, learner-brought items, substitutes and a budget guide.
All prices are **2026 guide prices in Rand — check locally** before you budget. Numbers below
assume **24 learners** (4 groups of 5 and 1 group of 4; 8 trios), 2 facilitators, 1 driver.
Scale up for 30 learners (10 trios).

## 2. Room kit (one per course)

- Flipchart stand or wall space; 1 flipchart pad (50 sheets); 20 markers (4 colours)
- Prestik, masking tape, cable ties (50) for posters on fences
- Sticky notes (5 pads), green stickers (no-photo), coloured dot stickers (green and ochre) for
  the Day 2 hand map
- Name tags (30), pens (30), pencils (30), sharpeners, rubbers
- Question Tree sheet, Parking bay sheet
- First aid kit, drinking water, sunscreen, 2 spare hats
- Hat + clipboard + hoe (props for the Day 1 inspector/mentor role-play)
- A bell or clap signal; a ball or rolled sock
- Power bank (2), phone charger, extension lead (if there is power)
- Optional: laptop/TV/projector for slides; 1 tablet per 2 groups for the clips

| All | Clipboard ×5, pencils ×5, coaching sheets ×5, observer strips ×5 | Card from a box as a clipboard |

**Records & money (Day 3 S3), per triad (8 triads):**
- 3 till slips (real, personal details blacked out) — photocopies are fine
- A5 exercise books ×2 (record book, money book), 1 duplicate invoice book, 1 A5 envelope
- A bunch of spinach or other leaves (buy at a local market — guide R10–R20 a bunch) and a
  kitchen or hanging scale (share 1 scale per 2 triads; guide R80–R200 each)
- **[APP]:** mentors' own phones, charged; the app installed; practice account or sample tour

## 4. Role-play garden (Day 0, venue)

Set up 5 problem stations (day-0 §3): a crowded bed, a bare dry bed, a leggy seedling tray, a
drum without a lid and a leaking bottle drip, a half-filled record book. Uses leftover seedlings,
2 drums, a few 2 ℓ bottles, one exercise book. Budget guide R150–R300 for seedlings.

## 6. Learners bring

- Their phone, charged (and the app installed if APP ON)
- A list of their farmers (names or numbers) and a rough map of where they live — Day 3
- Closed shoes, hat, water bottle — Day 2
- Their own experience: one success and one hard case from their work (for the case clinic)
