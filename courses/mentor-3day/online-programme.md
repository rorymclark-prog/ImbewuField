# Walking With Farmers — online / blended programme (6 weeks)

The same outcomes as the 3-day course, spread over **6 weekly live sessions of about 2 hours**,
with a **field task** between sessions. Built for low bandwidth: **WhatsApp is the classroom**;
Zoom or Google Meet is optional (audio only is fine).

**Use it when:** mentors are spread far apart, can't leave their farmers for 3 days, or the
programme can't pay for a venue and travel. It also works as **follow-up** after the in-person
course (use weeks 4–6 only).

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Group size | 8–20 mentors per facilitator pair (max 24) |
| Live session | ~2 h, same weekday and time each week (agree with the group; avoid pay days) |
| Between sessions | One field task (2–4 h, done on real visits) + 2 short WhatsApp check-ins |
| Tools | WhatsApp group (required) · Zoom/Meet (optional) · voice notes · photos · printed pack |
| Printed pack (posted or collected) | Visit playbook, 20 visit forms, observer checklist, A5 poster cards M-P01–M-P16, caseload/week/season sheets |
| Completion | 5 of 6 sessions + 3 of 6 field tasks → attendance certificate (not a qualification) |
| App track | Same switch as in person (app-track.md). Weeks 4 and 6 have [APP]/[PAPER] pairs |

## Before week 1 (the online Day 0)

- **Two weeks before:** collect names, phone numbers and consent (forms.md §2 read aloud in a
  voice note; mentors reply "I agree" or send a photo of the signed form).
- **Post or hand out the printed pack.** Mentors must have paper — nobody should need to read
  a PDF on a small phone while listening.
- **Data:** the programme gives each mentor a data bundle for the six weeks (budget in
  resources.md; check local prices). Voice-note sessions use far less data than video.
- **Test call (15 min each, week before):** can they hear? Can they send a voice note, a photo?
  Do they have a buddy nearby? (Pair mentors who live near each other: **buddies** do the field
  tasks together.)
- **WhatsApp group rules** posted as the group description:
  1. Session times only for session talk; other times for questions and field photos.
  2. Voice notes max 2 minutes. 3. No farmer's name or face without their consent — use first
  names or numbers. 4. No forwarding outside the group. 5. Respect: no mocking language or spelling.
- App track decision made (app-track.md §2).

## How a 2-hour live session runs

Two ways, same content. Choose per group — you can switch any week.

### Mode A — "call" (Zoom/Meet audio or a WhatsApp group call, ≤ 32 people)

| Min | Part | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Check-in & register | Register = each mentor types their name + one word on how they are |
| 10–25 | Look back at the field task | 3–4 mentors report (voice), others react in the chat |
| 25–45 | New idea from the poster card | Facilitator ≤ 10 min; everyone has the A5 card in hand |
| 45–90 | Practice in pairs/triads | Breakout rooms or phone calls between buddies; come back at the time |
| 90–110 | Debrief (three questions) | 1 answer per triad |
| 110–120 | Field task explained; close | Task posted as text + voice note in the group |

### Mode B — "WhatsApp drip" (no calls; 2 hours, messages in the group)

| Time | Facilitator posts | Mentors do |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Welcome voice note (1 min) + "register: reply with your name" | Reply |
| 0:10 | Photo of the poster card + voice note (2 min) | Listen; reply with one question |
| 0:25 | The task for pairs (text + voice) | Phone their buddy; do the practice (30 min) |
| 1:00 | "Voice note back: what happened?" | Each pair sends ≤ 2 min |
| 1:30 | Summary voice note: key messages; answers to questions | Read / listen |
| 1:50 | Field task for the week | Reply "OK" |

**Teacher talk:** in Mode A the facilitator talks ≤ 30 minutes of 120. In Mode B, ≤ 6 minutes of
voice notes per session.

## The six weeks

### Week 1 · Walk beside — and listen
**Outcomes:** explain mentor vs inspector; name the mentor's promise; show active listening.
**Cards:** M-P01, M-P02, M-P03. **Slides (optional):** M-ON-01 → M-ON-03.
- Look back: "The best helper I ever had" — each mentor sends a 1-minute voice note: what
  did they **do**?
- Idea: walk beside / in front / behind; the 5 promises; listen with ears, eyes and heart.
- Practice (triads by phone): speaker · listener · observer, 3 rounds × 8 min, using the
  observer strip from day-1.md S2 (printed in the pack).
- **Field task 1:** visit or phone one farmer and **only listen** for 20 minutes (say back, ask
  open questions, no advice). Voice note to the group: 3 things you heard + how the farmer
  reacted. *Evidence:* the voice note.

### Week 2 · Open questions and coaching a skill
**Outcomes:** climb the question ladder; coach a skill with I do · We do · You do.
**Cards:** M-P04, M-P05. **Slides:** M-ON-04 → M-ON-05.
- Look back: 4 field-task voice notes; "what did the farmer say when you didn't give advice?"
- Idea: the question ladder; strength-based start ("What are you proud of?").
- Practice: in the chat, the facilitator posts 10 closed questions; pairs climb 3 each and post
  the ladder. Then buddies phone each other: one "coaches" the other through a Course 1 skill
  **by phone** (hard! it shows why we coach in person).
- **Field task 2:** coach one Course 1 skill with a real farmer (spacing string, seedling tray,
  mulch). *Evidence:* a photo of the **farmer's hands** doing it (with consent), and the result:
  demonstrated / needs further support.

### Week 3 · The action agreement and the Visit Hand
**Outcomes:** write an action agreement; name the 5 steps of a visit.
**Cards:** M-P06, M-P07, M-P16. **Slides:** M-ON-06 → M-ON-07.
- Look back: photos of farmers' hands; "Who decided *demonstrated*? Why?"
- Idea: what · who · by when · how we'll know; the Visit Hand; "show me what changed".
- Practice: facilitator posts 3 weak agreements; pairs fix them in voice notes. Then a 10-minute
  Visit Hand speed rehearsal between buddies by phone (2 min per finger).
- **Field task 3 (the big one):** a full 60-minute Visit Hand visit with a real farmer; your
  **buddy observes** with the observer checklist (then swap on a second farmer if possible).
  Fill the visit form (paper) or Record visit (app). *Evidence:* photo of the filled observer
  checklist and of the visit form (farmer's name covered) or a screenshot of the saved visit.

### Week 4 · Visit debrief and recording a visit
**Outcomes:** name two strengths and one wish from their visit; record a visit correctly.
**Cards:** M-P07, M-P08, M-P14. **Slides:** M-ON-08.
- Look back: every pair reports: two stars and a wish (observer first after the mentor).
  Facilitator collects "the hardest finger" in the chat — tally.
- Idea: what to look at, stage by stage (M-P08); spacing check.
- **[APP] (45 min):** Mentor workspace → People & gardens → Record visit, field by field; save
  a draft offline; watch it sync; close an action with *Completed on* + *Completion evidence*.
  Facilitator shares screen (Mode A) or sends 6 screenshots in order (Mode B).
- **[PAPER] (45 min, same time):** the paper visit form, box by box; swap photos of forms with
  your buddy — "could a stranger understand it?"; copy the action to the farmer's card.
- **Field task 4:** go back to the **same farmer** from week 3. Thumb first: look back at the
  action — done? Close it with a date and evidence. *Evidence:* the closed action (form photo /
  screenshot) + before/after photo pair if the farmer agrees.

### Week 5 · Traffic light, caseload and group visits
**Outcomes:** sort a caseload; do the caseload sum; plan a week and a cluster visit; make a phone check-in.
**Cards:** M-P09, M-P10, M-P11, M-P12, M-P13. **Slides:** M-ON-09 → M-ON-10.
- Look back: closed actions — what changed? (This is evidence of change.)
- Idea: R/A/G; the sum (visit-playbook.md §6b); a mentor's week; cluster visit in 6 parts;
  field day; phone check-in in 5 questions.
- Practice: each mentor sorts **their real caseload** (numbers, not names, in the chat) and posts
  the sum: R×2 + A = visits needed vs visits available. Buddies do a back-to-back **phone
  check-in** role-play (they are on the phone anyway).
- **Field task 5:** send your caseload sheet (totals only), your week plan, and a **cluster visit
  plan** (planner §3c) for the coming 2 weeks. *Evidence:* photos of the three sheets.

### Week 6 · Coach records and money · hard situations · my season plan
**Outcomes:** coach a records/money exercise; choose a response to a hard situation; plan the season.
**Cards:** M-P14, M-P15, F-P20. **Slides:** M-ON-11 → M-ON-12.
- Look back: cluster visit plans — facilitator praises one detail per plan.
- **[APP] (40 min):** three exercises from app-track.md §4 — log a harvest, scan a till slip,
  Invoice builder. Each mentor does them on their own phone with sample data, then coaches
  their buddy by phone through one (they can see the result by screenshot).
- **[PAPER] (40 min, same time):** harvest page, money book + slip envelope, duplicate invoice
  book. Each mentor fills a sample page and sends a photo; buddies check each other's totals.
- Hard situations (20 min): one card per pair (drought, theft, conflict, drop-out, dependency);
  voice note with the four steps (M-P15).
- **Field task 6:** run your cluster visit; send the register count and one photo (consent);
  send your **season plan** (§9c). *Evidence:* the photo, the count, the plan.
- **Closing (last 15 min, in session):** one word each; evaluation by poll (6 questions from
  day-3.md closing, 1–5); certificates posted or handed over at the next programme meeting.

## Facilitating online — practical tips

- **Low bandwidth first.** Audio only; cameras off; no video clips in the session (send clip
  links before, optional). Posters are **printed cards**, not screen shares.
- **Say names.** In audio, call people by name to invite them in ("Thandi, what did you hear?").
- **Silence is fine.** Count to 10 after a question (people unmute slowly).
- **Language time works online too:** buddies retell the idea to each other in their language
  in a 3-minute phone call.
- **Late or missed session:** a 5-minute voice-note summary goes to the group within 24 hours;
  the field task is still due.
- **Registers:** screenshot the participant list (Mode A) or the replies (Mode B) — that is the
  register. Store it like a paper register.
- **Field tasks** are the heart: comment on every one within 3 days (two stars and a wish, in a
  voice note, privately if it is critical).
- **Safeguarding online:** no private messaging between facilitators and learners after hours
  except for course matters; farmer photos only with consent; any concern follows the same
  safeguarding route as in person (visit-playbook.md §11).
- **Fatigue:** two facilitators; alternate who leads; the other watches the chat and the time.

## Blended option (recommended when possible)

Weeks 1–2 online → **one in-person day** (the Day 2 farm-visit day, day-2.md) → weeks 4–6
online. The real, observed farm visit is the part that suffers most online; this puts it back.
