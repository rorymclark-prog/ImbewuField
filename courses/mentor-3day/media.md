# Media — Walking With Farmers

Every image, animation, photo and poster artwork used in the slides and posters.
**NEW** items are made by Codex (image model / animator); target path is set by the build script
(`public/course-media/mentor-3day/<ID>.<jpg|mp4>`). **REUSE** items point to existing files.

Art rules for every NEW item (from `shared/poster-standards.md`): flat, warm instructional
illustration with thick dark outlines; **no text in the artwork** (forms, books, phones and
calendars show blank lines or blank screens); southern hemisphere — sun in the north, shadows
fall south; people look like Black South African smallholders and mentors of mixed ages and
genders (gogos, youth, men and women), work clothes, gumboots, headscarves, caps; generic plant
shapes; local materials (20 ℓ drums, 2 ℓ bottles, droppers and wire fences, corrugated-iron
roofs, JoJo-style tanks). Palette: Forest #1F4D2B, Mid-green #2E6B3A, Leaf #A8D88A, Ochre
#C07A1E, Water #235E86 (water only), Paper #F7F2E9, Ink #20190F.
Recurring characters (same look everywhere): **the mentor** — woman in her 40s, green cap,
cross-body bag, notebook; **the farmer** — older man, hat, gumboots; **the gogo farmer** —
headscarf, apron; **the youth** — young man, T-shirt, phone in pocket.
Animations: silent, 8–30 s, loopable, ≤ 3 MB, 1280×720, with a still poster frame (.jpg).

Totals: **16 poster-art NEW · 10 images NEW · 2 animations NEW · 2 photos NEW (taken on
courses, with consent) · 5 images REUSE · 7 animations REUSE.**

## Animations (NEW)

## M-ANI-01 · Two ways to listen
- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: M-D1-07
- Length: 16 s loop, silent

**Brief:** 0–7 s: the farmer talks with open hands; the mentor stands, looks at her phone, glances
at her watch; the farmer's gestures slow and stop, shoulders drop. 7–8 s: soft wipe. 8–15 s:
same farmer; the mentor puts the phone in her bag, squats to his level, nods; the farmer
straightens, gestures more, points to a bed. 15–16 s: hold, loop.

## M-ANI-02 · I do · We do · You do
- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: M-D1-11, M-D1-12
- Length: 24 s loop, silent

**Brief:** Spacing string along a bed. 0–7 s: the mentor plants two seedlings at the knots while the
farmer watches. 7–15 s: the farmer plants with the mentor's hand guiding hers. 15–22 s: the
farmer plants three alone; the mentor steps back, hands in pockets. 22–24 s: they sit and talk
(no speech bubbles); loop.
