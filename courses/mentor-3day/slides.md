# Deck M-D1 · Day 1 — Listen, ask, coach

## M-D1-01 · Welcome
Type: section
Screen:
- Walking With Farmers
- Day 1 of 3: listen, ask, coach
Media: M-IMG-01
Notes:
M-D1-S0. Register at the door. Read the consent form aloud. Say: "You already help farmers. This course is about helping well." Agree the call and response.

## M-D1-02 · Our groups and our agreement
Type: activity
Screen:
- Groups of 5, named after plants
- Leader · Timekeeper · Materials · Recorder · Reporter
- Five rules we agree
Poster: F-P01
Notes:
M-D1-S0 steps 5–7. Mirror energiser first. Name round with number of farmers; add them up and keep the total for Day 3. Class agreement: add "What a farmer tells us stays in this room" if missing.

## M-D1-03 · Two visits
Screen:
- Visit A or Visit B?
- Which makes you try again?
Media: M-IMG-02
Poster: M-P01
Notes:
M-D1-S1 step 1–2. Act both visits live (2 min each) before showing this slide. Ask which visit makes a farmer want to try again, and which one we often do.

## M-D1-04 · Walk beside
Screen:
- In front: I lead
- Behind: I push
- Beside: we walk
Media: M-P01-ART
Poster: M-P01
Notes:
M-D1-S1 steps 3–5. Pairs tell "the best helper I ever had". Groups sort sticky notes under in front / beside / behind. Ask when "in front" is right (a new skill, danger).

## M-D1-05 · The mentor's promise
Screen:
- Listen first · real promises only
- Private · no gifts that bind
- Keep people safe
Media: M-P02-ART
Poster: M-P02
Notes:
M-D1-S1 step 6. Read the five promises with the room. Tea is hospitality; a chicken is a gift that binds. No promises of inputs, money, jobs or stipends.

## M-D1-06 · What would you do?
Type: activity
Screen:
- One card per group · 8 minutes
- Reporter answers in 1 minute
Poster: M-P02
Notes:
M-D1-S1 step 7. Cards: JoJo tank request; a chicken gift; a nosy neighbour; a child with bruises; a farmer asking you to over-report. Expected answers are in day-1.md. Safeguarding: supervisor same day; SAPS 10111, Childline 116.

## M-D1-07 · Listen with ears, eyes, heart
Screen:
- Ears: let them finish
- Eyes: same level, phone away
- Heart: notice feelings · say it back
Media: M-P03-ART, M-ANI-01
Poster: M-P03
Notes:
M-D1-S2 steps 1–2. Start with the 1-minute bad-listening game in pairs. Play M-ANI-01 on a tablet if you have one. "Say it back, then ask. Don't jump to advice."

## M-D1-08 · Listening triads
Type: activity
Screen:
- Speaker · Listener · Observer
- 5 min talk + 3 min feedback
- Rotate 3 times
Media: M-IMG-03
Poster: M-P03
Notes:
M-D1-S2 step 3–4. Observer ticks the strip: let them finish, said back, open question, no advice, body. Hardest part: no advice. Never cut the triads.

## M-D1-09 · Climb the question ladder
Screen:
- Closed → Open → Deeper
- → The farmer's own plan
- "What else?"
Media: M-P04-ART
Poster: M-P04
Notes:
M-D1-S2 steps 5–6. Climb "Are your seedlings healthy?" with the room. Open questions start with What, How, Where, When, Who, Tell me about. Soften "why". Groups climb 10 closed-question cards.

## M-D1-10 · Start with what's working
Type: activity
Screen:
- Fishbowl: two openings
- "What problems do you have?"
- "What are you proud of?"
Poster: M-P04
Notes:
M-D1-S2 step 7. Two volunteers, 3 minutes each opening. Ask what changed in the farmer. Name it: a strength-based start. Take home: ask three open questions at home tonight.

## M-D1-11 · I do · We do · You do
Screen:
- I do: show once, slowly
- We do: the farmer's hands
- You do: watch quietly · then talk
Media: M-P05-ART, M-ANI-02
Poster: M-P05
Notes:
M-D1-S3 steps 1–3. Energiser first. Hook: explain the A-frame by talking only, then ask a volunteer to build it. Adults learn by doing, reflecting and trying again.

## M-D1-12 · Coaching rounds
Type: activity
Screen:
- Coach · Farmer · 3 Observers
- 12 min coaching + 3 min feedback
- Two rounds
Media: M-ANI-03, M-ANI-02
Poster: F-P06
Notes:
M-D1-S3 steps 4–5. Stations: A-frame (F-P06), spacing string (F-P15), seedling tray (F-P14), record book. Only one kit? Carousel, never demo-only.

## M-D1-13 · Demonstrated or needs support?
Screen:
- Alone and correct = Demonstrated
- With help = Needs further support
- Not a failure: the next plan
Poster: M-P05
Notes:
M-D1-S3 step 6. Same idea as the Course 1 skills checklist (✔ ◐ ✘). In the app: Skill observed and Observation result.

## M-D1-14 · The action agreement
Screen:
- What? Who?
- By when?
- How will we know?
Media: M-P06-ART
Poster: M-P06
Notes:
M-D1-S4 steps 1–4. "I'll try to mulch" vs "I will mulch the 2 front beds by Friday". Groups fix two weak agreements; pairs write a real one from the triad problem. App: Agreed action, Responsible person, Follow-up date.

## M-D1-15 · Show me what changed
Screen:
- Before · after · the date
- Close only with evidence
- Not done? What got in the way?
Media: M-P16-ART, M-IMG-06
Poster: M-P16
Notes:
M-D1-S4 steps 5–6. Photo-pair game: which pair proves the action? App: Completed on + Completion evidence. Paper: date and what you saw.

## M-D1-16 · Closing circle
Type: section
Screen:
- One word about today
- Tomorrow: real farms, leave 10:05
- Closed shoes, hat, water
Notes:
M-D1-SC. Reporters read one action agreement each. Question Tree, parking bay. Tools back. Facilitators phone every Day 2 host tonight.
