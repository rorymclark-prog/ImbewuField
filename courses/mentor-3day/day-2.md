## M-D2-S1 · 08:50–10:05 · The Visit Hand, recording a visit, and the briefing (75 min)

**By the end, learners can:** name the five steps of a visit, record a visit on the form or
in the app, and play their trio role.

**Posters:** M-P07 (The Visit Hand), M-P08 (What to look at, stage by stage), M-P14 ·
**Slides:** M-D2-01 → M-D2-08
**Materials (per group of 5):** 5 A5 Visit Hand cards, 5 paper visit forms, 2 observer
checklists. Per trio: trio pack (Day 0 §3).
**Room:** circle, then trios.

| Time | Step | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|
| 08:50 | Hook — "hold up your hand": what are the 5 fingers of a visit? | 2 min |
| 08:55 | M-P07 — the Visit Hand, step by step, with timings | 8 min |
| 09:05 | Speed rehearsal: pairs run a 10-minute Visit Hand on each other | 1 min |
| 09:25 | M-P08 — what to look at, stage by stage (quick tour) | 4 min |
| 09:30 | **[APP] or [PAPER]** Record the rehearsal visit (25 min — see pair below) | 5 min |
| 09:55 | Trio briefing: roles, safety, the "no promise" rule, timings | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. ASK: "Hold up your hand. Yesterday we practised listening, asking, coaching and agreeing.
   If a visit has five steps, what are they?" Take answers. Then reveal M-P07.
2. DO: Walk through M-P07. For each finger say the step, the time, and the question to start it:

   | Finger | Step (time in a 60-min visit) | Opening words |
   |---|---|---|
   | Thumb | **1 Look back** (5–10 min) — greet, family, then last action | "Last time you planned to … Show me how it went." |
   | Pointing | **2 Walk & look** (15 min) — farmer leads the walk | "Show me your garden. What are you proud of?" |
   | Middle | **3 Listen & ask** (10 min) — farmer's priority | "What would help you most right now?" |
   | Ring | **4 Try it together** (15 min) — one skill | "Shall we try it together, right here?" |
   | Little | **5 Agree & record** (10 min) — action, who, when, evidence | "So what will you do, by when, and how will we know?" |

   SAY: "The thumb is first because it shows respect: we come back to what *they* planned."
3. PAIRS: 10-minute speed rehearsal — one is the farmer (use a real memory of a garden), one the
   mentor. 2 minutes per finger. Timekeeper calls each finger. Don't swap — the "farmer" leads
   on the real visit if they want.
4. DO: M-P08 quick tour. SAY: "What you look at depends on the stage of the season. Today
   most gardens are at [stage]. Look at the checks for that stage." Point to the **spacing
   check** (F-P15) and the **Course 1 skills** (forms.md §4). The full list is in
   visit-playbook.md §5.
5. Record the rehearsal visit — **[APP]** or **[PAPER]** block (below).
6. BRIEFING (5 min): see the trio briefing card.

### [APP] Record a visit in the Mentor workspace · 09:30–09:55 (25 min)

**For:** programmes with the app track ON. **Teacher talk:** 5 min.
**Materials:** each mentor's phone (charged, app installed, signed in as mentor); 1 facilitator
phone mirrored to a TV or passed round; the rehearsal notes.

| Time | Step | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30 | Open **Mentor workspace → People & gardens**; find a participant; tap **Record visit** | 2 min |
| 09:34 | Pairs fill the fields from the rehearsal (see mapping) — practice record only | 1 min |
| 09:46 | Save a **draft offline** (flight mode on), then turn flight mode off and watch it sync | 1 min |
| 09:51 | Check: can you find it again under **Visit records**? | 1 min |

Field mapping (the Visit Hand → the form):
- Thumb → open the last visit, fill **Completed on** + **Completion evidence** for the last action
  (only with a date and what you saw).
- Pointing → **Observations and issues** (with quantities: "3 beds, 1 mulched"), **photos**
  (only with consent), **Site location** (GPS optional).
- Middle → **Support requested** and the support area: garden & water · soil & seedlings ·
  harvest & food access · records · learning.
- Ring → **Skill observed** ("transplant to spacing") + **Observation result**
  (*Demonstrated during this visit* / *Needs further support* / *Not assessed*).
- Little → **Agreed action**, **Responsible person**, **Follow-up date**. **Save visit.**

SAY: "No signal at the farm? Save it. The app keeps visits, photos and signatures and sends
them when you have signal. Never wait until the evening to write it from memory."
Practice records: use your organisation's practice participant, or mark it clearly as a
training record, and follow your organisation's rule to remove it later.

### [PAPER] Record a visit on the paper form · 09:30–09:55 (25 min)

**For:** programmes with the app track OFF (or every mentor, as back-up). **Teacher talk:** 5 min.
**Materials:** visit form pads (visit-playbook.md §8), pencils, carbon sheet or a second copy.

| Time | Step | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|
| 09:30 | Tour of the form: 5 boxes = 5 fingers | 2 min |
| 09:34 | Pairs fill the form from the rehearsal | 1 min |
| 09:46 | Copy the bottom box onto the farmer's action-agreement card | 1 min |
| 09:51 | Check: swap forms — could a stranger understand it? | 1 min |

SAY: "Fill it **at the farm**, before you leave, with the farmer. Read the bottom box back to
them. The white copy stays in your pad; the card stays with the farmer. At the end of the
week, your supervisor collects the pad copies (or you photograph them) for the programme."

Both versions, **Key message:** write it at the farm, write what you saw, with numbers.

### The trio briefing card (read aloud at 09:55; each trio has a copy)
- **Roles.** **Mentor** leads all 5 steps. **Recorder** fills the form/app and the action card,
  and keeps time (whispers "10 minutes"). **Observer** stands back, says nothing, ticks the
  observer checklist, and watches the *farmer's* face and energy.
- **Timings.** Arrive 10:35–10:40. Visit 60 min. Leave by 11:40. Trio huddle **away from the
  house** for 5 min: observer gives two stars and a wish. Pick-up **11:45** at the drop-off point.
- **Promise.** No inputs, no money, no jobs promised. No gifts taken (water or tea is fine).
- **Photos.** Only what the host agreed on the consent sheet. Ask again before each photo.
- **Children.** No photos of children.
- **Safety.** Stay together. Dogs: wait at the gate and call. Anything that worries you
  (someone hurt, a threat, a child in danger): leave politely, phone the lead facilitator.
- **The host can stop the visit at any time.** Thank them, and leave.

**Observer checklist (A4, one per trio)**

| During the visit, did the mentor… | ✔ / ✘ | What I saw or heard (one example) |
|---|---|---|
| Greet properly, ask about family, sit/stand at the farmer's level | | |
| **Thumb:** ask about the last action / what the farmer planned (Course 1 90-day plan) | | |
| **Pointing:** let the farmer lead the walk; start with what is working | | |
| Ask more open than closed questions (tally: open ___ closed ___) | | |
| Say back what the farmer said | | |
| **Middle:** find the farmer's own priority before giving advice | | |
| **Ring:** coach one skill with I do · We do · You do; farmer's hands | | |
| **Little:** agree what · who · by when · how we'll know — farmer's words | | |
| Record at the farm; read it back to the farmer | | |
| Keep the promise (no promises of inputs; no gifts) | | |
| Mentor talk vs farmer talk: who talked more? | Mentor / Farmer / Equal | |
| Farmer's energy at the end compared with the start | Up / Same / Down | |

### Debrief (the three questions — short, in the vehicle)
1. What are the 5 fingers? 2. Why does the thumb go first? 3. What is your role today?

### Key messages
- **Look back · Walk & look · Listen & ask · Try it together · Agree & record.**
- The farmer leads the walk. The mentor leads the questions.
- Write it at the farm, with numbers. Read it back.

### If things go wrong
- App will not open / phone dead: use the paper form; enter it later.
- Late start: cut the M-P08 tour (they have the playbook) — never cut the briefing.

**✂ 09:00 start:** speed rehearsal 8 min, M-P08 tour 3 min.

## M-D2-S2 · 10:30–11:45 · Real farm visits in trios (75 min)

**By the end, learners can:** run a full Visit Hand visit with a real farmer and record it.

**Poster:** the A5 Visit Hand card in each trio pack · **Slides:** M-D2-09 (activity card)
**Materials:** trio pack each; facilitators carry the first aid kit and host sheet.

| Time | Step | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|
| 10:30–10:40 | Drop-offs; trio greets host at the gate | 0 |
| 10:40–11:40 | The visit (60 min), Visit Hand | 0 |
| 11:40–11:45 | Trio huddle away from the house: observer gives two stars and a wish | 0 |

### What facilitators do
- Visit 3–4 trios each, for 10–15 minutes each. **Stand back. Do not take over.** Tick your own
  copy of the observer checklist. Only step in for safety, or if the host is upset.
- Hand the programme's **thank-you pack** to each host as you leave (not the trainees).
- Note one strong moment per trio to praise in S4.

### Host cancellation plan (decided at 08:43)
1. Spare host (Day 0 list) → trio goes there.
2. No spare → the trio joins another trio: 2 mentors share steps (one leads fingers 1–3, the
   other 4–5), 2 observers.
3. No farm possible (weather, safety, transport broke) → **role-play garden at the venue**
   with the co-facilitator. Use the 5 problem stations and role cards below. Same 60-minute
   Visit Hand, same observer checklist.

### Role cards (for the role-play garden or any rehearsal)
Each "farmer" reads the card privately and plays it honestly. Never read it aloud.

| Station | You are… | What you say only if asked well |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Bed | MaDlamini, 58. Planted 30 cabbages in one bed. Proud of it. | You weren't sure of the spacing; your neighbour said "plant thick, some will die". You'd like bigger heads. |
| 2 Bare soil | Sipho, 24. Dug 3 beds, no mulch. Works part-time. | Grass is far to carry; you have no wheelbarrow. You have a bicycle. Your uncle cuts grass on Saturdays. |
| 3 Seedlings | Nomsa, 40. Tray under the house eave, seedlings long and pale. | The tray is in shade all day because goats eat it in the yard. You could move it onto the roof of the toilet. |
| 4 Water | Baba Mokoena, 67. A drum without a lid; a bottle drip that leaks. | Mosquitoes bite the grandchildren at night. You have an old sheet of corrugated iron. |
| 5 Records | Lerato, 31. Record book filled only in week 1. | You stopped because you sold on credit to neighbours and felt shy writing their names. You have a phone. |

### If things go wrong
- Host not home: wait 10 minutes, phone the host, then phone the lead → spare host.
- Host asks for inputs or money: the mentor answers with the promise (no promises), writes the
  request under *Support requested*, and tells the host the programme office will see it.
- Something upsetting (illness, violence, a child in danger): the trio leaves politely and phones
  the lead facilitator at once. The lead follows the programme safeguarding route. Do **not**
  discuss details in the S4 debrief.
- Late pick-up: trio waits together at the drop-off point, in shade. No walking alone.

### Language & inclusion notes
- The visit is in the host's language. If the mentor doesn't speak it well, the Recorder may
  interpret — note it for S4.
- If the host is elderly or has limited mobility, bring the "walk" to her: she sits, the mentor
  walks and describes, she directs.

## M-D2-S3 · 13:00–14:15 · Cluster visit, live (75 min)

**By the end, learners can:** name the parts of a cluster visit and take one role in running it.

**Posters:** M-P11 (hung on the host's fence), M-P16 · **Slides:** M-D2-10 → M-D2-12
**Materials:** 3 posters + cable ties; register (forms.md §1) for local farmers; POPIA consent
forms; a spacing string, 1 m tape, a hand-held scale (or bathroom scale), water and cups.
**Place:** the cluster site (Course 1 demo garden or host garden), 8–12 local farmers invited.

| Time | Step | Who leads | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13:00 | Welcome, register, consent (trainee team 1) | Trainees | 3 min |
| 13:10 | **Host's tour** — host farmer shows what works and why | Host | 0 |
| 13:25 | **Try it together** — stations: spacing check · seedlings · mulch/water (trainee teams 2–4, groups of 4) | Trainees | 5 min |
| 13:50 | **Harvest & share circle** — each farmer: one success, one question; farmers answer each other first | Trainee team 5 | 5 min |
| 14:05 | **Agree** — each farmer says one action; next cluster visit date and host | Trainees + lead | 5 min |
| 14:10 | Thank the host; tools back | Lead | 2 min |

(Facilitator talk = the lead's short framing and closing: 20 min in total, including coaching
trainees quietly at the side.)

### Say / ask / do
1. DO (12:45, over lunch): give each learner group a cluster-visit role: Team 1 welcome & register,
   Teams 2–4 one "try it" station each, Team 5 the circle. 5 minutes to plan with the M-P11 card.
2. SAY (to local farmers): "Thank you for coming. Today [host] opens her garden. We learn from
   each other; these mentors are practising with us." Consent read aloud; register signed.
3. Host tour: trainees ask the host open questions ("What helped this bed do so well?"). This is
   **positive deviance**: someone with the same soil and rain is doing well — find out how.
4. Stations: farmers rotate in groups of 3–4 (8 min per station, 3 stations). Each station
   uses I do · We do · You do on one skill.
5. Circle: "One thing that went well, one question." Trainees ask the **other farmers** first:
   "Who has tried something for that?" Mentor answers last.
6. Agree: each farmer says one action aloud (Recorder writes name + action). Agree the next host
   (rotate — someone else's garden next month).

### Debrief (at the site, 3 min, trainees only)
1. What did the farmers do? 2. Why do farmers learn well from each other? *(same conditions,
same language, trust, can see it working)* 3. What would you change for your own cluster?

### Key messages
- A cluster visit reaches 8–12 farmers in the time of 3 individual visits.
- The **host** teaches first; the mentor asks. Rotate the host each month.
- Every farmer leaves with one action, said aloud.

### If things go wrong
- Few local farmers come: run it with the hosts from the morning, or with trainees as farmers.
- No cluster site (weather, transport): at the venue, the role-play garden becomes the host
  garden; the co-facilitator plays the host.
- A farmer asks about inputs/stipends: park it — "We'll take your question to the programme."

### Language & inclusion notes
- Farmers may defer to the mentors: sit down, lower yourselves, let the host speak first.
- Women may speak less in a mixed circle: take one answer from a woman first, or run the
  circle in pairs first.

## M-D2-S4 · 14:45–15:40 · Visit debrief (55 min)

**By the end, learners can:** name two things they did well and one to improve on a visit, and
explain the farmer's view of the visit.

**Posters:** M-P07, M-P06 · **Slides:** M-D2-13 → M-D2-15
**Materials:** each trio's observer checklist, visit form, action card copy; flipchart with the
Visit Hand drawn large.

| Time | Step | Teacher talk? |
|---|---|---|
| 14:45 | Energiser *Rain Storm* (calming after travel) | 1 min |
| 14:50 | Trios: mentor speaks first, then observer (two stars and a wish), then recorder | 1 min |
| 15:05 | Hand map: each trio puts a green dot on the finger they did best, an ochre dot on the hardest | 2 min |
| 15:12 | Room discussion on the hardest finger | 4 min |
| 15:22 | Action agreements review: read 3 aloud; room checks what · who · when · how we'll know | 2 min |
| 15:32 | Facilitator praise: one strong moment per trio (from S2 notes); key messages | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. TRIOS: mentor first: "What went well? What would you change?" Then observer, using the
   checklist: two stars and a wish. Then recorder: "Is the record true and complete?"
2. DO: The big hand on the flipchart. Dots. Usually the ring finger (coaching) and the middle
   (not giving advice too soon) get ochre dots.
3. ASK on the hardest finger: "What got in the way?" *(the farmer wanted answers; time; I
   didn't know the answer)*. "What could you do?" *(ask the group; "I don't know — let's find
   out together"; ask Lima if app-ON; phone a colleague; bring it to the cluster visit)*.
4. Review 3 action agreements. ASK: "Could we check this in 2 weeks? What would we see?"
5. Mentor talk vs farmer talk: count the trios where the farmer talked more. SAY: "Target:
   the farmer talks more than half the time."

### Debrief (the three questions)
1. What happened on your visit? 2. What did the farmer's face tell you? 3. What will you do
on your first real visit next week?

### Key messages
- Mentors grow by being observed — ask a colleague to observe you once a month.
- The farmer should talk more than the mentor.
- "I don't know — let's find out together" is a strong answer.

### If things go wrong
- A trio's visit went badly (host upset, argument): talk privately, not in the room.
- Anyone saw something distressing: lead facilitator follows up privately and follows the
  safeguarding route; no details in public.

**✂ cut if short:** step 4 (do one agreement). **✂ 09:00 start:** not needed (S0 skipped).
