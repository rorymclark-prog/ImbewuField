# Posters — Walking With Farmers (M-P01 … M-P16)

16 posters: 13 × A1 portrait, 3 × A0 landscape (M-P08, M-P10, M-P13). Follow
`shared/poster-standards.md`: ≤ 25 words per poster (headline + labels + question), no text in
the art, numbered callouts, southern hemisphere, people who look like the learners.
Each poster has an A5 **Teach it** card for the back. Also used from Course 1: F-P01, F-P06,
F-P14, F-P15, F-P16, F-P20, F-P21.

Recurring characters (keep them the same on every poster so learners recognise them):
**the mentor** — a woman in her 40s, green programme cap, cross-body bag, notebook;
**the farmer** — an older man with a hat and gumboots (MaDlamini appears on some as a gogo farmer
in a headscarf); **the youth** — a young man in a T-shirt with a phone in his pocket.

---

## M-P01 · Walk beside
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S1, M-D2-S4
- Art: M-P01-ART

**Headline:** Walk beside, not in front
**Art brief (text-free):** Three small scenes stacked on a dirt path between vegetable beds.
Top: a person with a clipboard striding ahead, farmer trailing behind, head down (in front).
Middle: the same person behind the farmer, pointing and pushing (behind). Bottom, largest and
in full colour: mentor and farmer walking side by side, the farmer pointing at a healthy bed,
the mentor listening, both smiling. Top two scenes muted (Hairline/grey), bottom in full palette.
Sun in the north (upper left as drawn from the south-facing view; shadows fall south).
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. In front: I lead
2. Behind: I push
3. Beside: we walk
**Ask the room:** Which helper made you try again?
**Layout:** Headline top band; three scenes in the art area with ①②③; label strip below.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: Who was the best helper you ever had? What did they do?
2. POINT ①: the helper in front — when is that right? (new skill, danger)
3. POINT ②: behind — how does a farmer feel when pushed?
4. POINT ③: beside — the farmer chooses the path; the mentor walks with them.
5. GROUPS: sort your helper stories under ① ② ③.
KEY: Walk beside. The farmer chooses the path.

## M-P02 · The mentor's promise
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S1, M-D3-S4
- Art: M-P02-ART

**Headline:** The mentor's promise
**Art brief (text-free):** An open hand (palm facing viewer) drawn large, each finger with a small
round picture above it: an ear; a hand pushing away a box of goods; closed lips with a finger
(privacy); a hand politely refusing a live chicken while accepting a cup of tea; a shield over a
child and a woman. Warm Paper background, Ochre circles.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. I listen first
2. Only real promises
3. Your story stays private
4. No gifts that bind
5. I keep you safe
**Ask the room:** Which promise is hardest?
**Layout:** Hand centred; five callouts on the fingers; labels in a list below.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: What happens when a mentor promises a tank and none comes?
2. POINT ①–⑤ one by one; ask for one example of each.
3. GROUPS: one "What would you do?" card each (day-1.md S1).
4. SAY: Tea is hospitality. A chicken is a gift that binds.
5. SAY: Harm to a child or adult — tell your supervisor the same day.
KEY: Promise only what you can do.

## M-P03 · Listen with ears, eyes and heart
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S2
- Art: M-P03-ART

**Headline:** Listen with ears, eyes, heart
**Art brief (text-free):** The mentor squatting at the same level as the gogo farmer who sits on
an upturned 20 ℓ drum beside a bed. The mentor's phone is in her bag (visible, put away). The
gogo is talking with open hands; the mentor nods. Three soft Ochre circles highlight the
mentor's ear, her eyes (looking at the bed the gogo points to), and her hand on her chest.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. Ears: let them finish
2. Eyes: same level
3. Heart: notice feelings
4. Say it back
**Ask the room:** When did someone really listen to you?
**Layout:** Scene centre; four callouts; labels below.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. PAIRS: 1 minute of bad listening. How did it feel?
2. POINT ①: count to 3 before you speak.
3. POINT ②: sit or squat at their level; phone away.
4. POINT ③: "You sound tired of this."
5. POINT ④: "So the water runs out in week two…"
6. TRIADS: speaker · listener · observer, 3 rounds.
KEY: Say it back, then ask. No advice yet.

## M-P04 · The question ladder
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S2, M-D3-S2
- Art: M-P04-ART

**Headline:** Climb the question ladder
**Art brief (text-free):** A wooden ladder leaning against a fruit tree. On the bottom rung the
farmer stands with arms folded; on each higher rung the farmer climbs higher, looking more
engaged; at the top the farmer is reaching for a ripe fruit with a smile. The mentor steadies the
ladder at the bottom. Four rungs highlighted with Ochre numbers.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. Closed: yes or no
2. Open: how, what
3. Deeper: what happens
4. Their plan: what else?
**Ask the room:** Who picks the fruit?
**Layout:** Ladder runs bottom-left to top-right; callouts on rungs; labels beside each rung.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: "Did you water?" — what answer do you get? (yes)
2. POINT ②: "How do you water the beds?"
3. POINT ③: "What happens to the water after you pour it?"
4. POINT ④: "What could you try? What else?"
5. GROUPS: climb 10 closed-question cards.
6. SAY: Start with "What are you proud of?"
KEY: The best answer is the one the farmer finds.

## M-P05 · I do · We do · You do
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S3, M-D3-S3
- Art: M-P05-ART

**Headline:** I do · We do · You do
**Art brief (text-free):** Three panels left to right on one strip of garden, the same pair each
time, laying a knotted spacing string along a bed and planting seedlings. Panel 1: mentor plants,
farmer watches. Panel 2: both kneeling, the farmer's hands on the seedling, the mentor's hand
guiding. Panel 3: the farmer planting alone; the mentor standing back with hands in pockets,
watching. A small fourth panel: both sitting, talking (speech-shape without words).
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. I do: show once
2. We do: together
3. You do: watch quietly
4. Talk: what worked?
**Ask the room:** Whose hands do the work?
**Layout:** Four panels in a Z order; callouts ①–④; labels below each panel.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. DO: explain the A-frame by talking only. Ask a volunteer to build it. Why was it hard?
2. POINT ①: show once, slowly, say each step.
3. POINT ②: the farmer's hands, your help.
4. POINT ③: hands in pockets. Say nothing.
5. POINT ④: What went well? What was hard?
6. Result: demonstrated alone = Demonstrated. Anything else = Needs further support.
KEY: One skill per visit. The farmer's hands.

## M-P06 · The action agreement
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D1-S4, M-D2-S4, M-D3-S2
- Art: M-P06-ART

**Headline:** One action, one owner, one date
**Art brief (text-free):** The mentor and farmer shaking hands over a garden bed at the end of a
visit. Around them, four corner vignettes: a bundle of dry grass mulch on a bed (what); the
farmer's grandson carrying grass (who); a wall calendar with one square circled (by when — no
numbers); the same bed later, covered in mulch, with the mentor looking and the farmer pointing
(how we'll know).
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. What?
2. Who?
3. By when?
4. How will we know?
**Ask the room:** "I'll try" — or "I will, by Friday"?
**Layout:** Handshake centre; the four vignettes in the corners with ①–④.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: "I'll try to mulch" vs "I will mulch the 2 front beds by Friday" — which happens?
2. POINT ①: one small action, the farmer's words.
3. POINT ②: a named person.
4. POINT ③: before the next contact.
5. POINT ④: what we will see.
6. GROUPS: fix "Farmer must improve the soil".
KEY: What · Who · By when · How we'll know.

## M-P07 · The Visit Hand
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: M-D2-S1, M-D2-S2, M-D2-S4, M-D3-S2
- Art: M-P07-ART

**Headline:** Five fingers, five steps
**Art brief (text-free):** A large open hand (brown skin tone, palm towards viewer, fingers
spread). In each fingertip a small round scene: thumb — mentor and farmer looking at a mulched bed
together (look back); pointing finger — the pair walking between beds, farmer pointing; middle
finger (tallest) — the pair sitting in shade talking; ring finger — both kneeling, farmer's hands
planting; little finger — handshake with a notebook. Palm: a simple garden seen from above.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. Look back
2. Walk and look
3. Listen and ask
4. Try it together
5. Agree and record
**Ask the room:** Which finger do you skip?
**Layout:** Hand fills art area; labels curve beside each finger with ①–⑤.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: hold up your hand — what are the 5 steps of a visit?
2. POINT ①: "Last time you planned… show me." (5–10 min)
3. POINT ②: "What are you proud of?" Farmer leads. (15 min)
4. POINT ③: "What would help most right now?" (10 min)
5. POINT ④: one skill, I do · We do · You do. (15 min)
6. POINT ⑤: what · who · by when · how we'll know. Write it at the farm. (10 min)
KEY: Never cut the ring or little finger.

## M-P10 · A mentor's week
- Size: A0 landscape
- Used in: M-D3-S1
- Art: M-P10-ART

**Headline:** A mentor's week
**Art brief (text-free):** A simple map of four villages (A, B, C, D drawn as four clusters of
houses with gardens — letters typeset on the layout, not in the art) around a small town with a
taxi rank. Five columns below the map, one per weekday, each showing a small figure of the
mentor: Monday under a tree with a group of 10 farmers (cluster visit) then walking to 2
houses; Tuesday to Thursday walking a curved route to 3 houses in one cluster; Friday at a table
with a phone and a notebook. Dotted route lines on the map from the farthest house back to the
taxi rank.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words, each ≤ 4 words):**
1. One cluster a day
2. Cluster visit weekly
3. Farthest first
4. Friday: phone, records
**Ask the room:** Which day is your cluster day?
**Layout:** Map top 55%; weekday strip below; callouts ①–④.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. DO the sum: Red × 2 + Amber = visits needed.
2. POINT ①: each weekday belongs to one cluster — less travel.
3. POINT ②: the cluster visit rotates A → B → C → D.
4. POINT ③: start far, work back towards home.
5. POINT ④: phones, records, planning, buddy.
6. GROUPS: draw your week on the A3 grid.
KEY: Plan by cluster and route, and leave spare time.

**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
