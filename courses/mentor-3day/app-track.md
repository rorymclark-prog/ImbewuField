# The app track — switch it on, switch it off

Mentoring works **with or without** the ImbewuField app. This file explains:

1. What the app track is (and what stays the same)
2. How to switch it on or off — for the whole programme, or per farmer
3. The season sequence: 9 farmer exercises, app and paper side by side
4. The app exercises — a 10–15 minute "coach it on the visit" script and a check for each
5. The paper twins — the exact same outcomes with books and a pencil
6. What the mentor uses: Mentor workspace or paper forms
7. Phones, data and privacy

---

## 1. What the app track is

Every farmer in the programme learns to keep **garden records** and **money records** through
the season. The **app track** does it on a phone in ImbewuField. The **paper track** does it in
a record book, a money book and a duplicate invoice book.

**What stays the same on both tracks:**
- The same 9 exercises, in the same months.
- The same coaching method (I do · We do · You do) and the same **check** for each exercise.
- The same visit (the Visit Hand) and the same visit record fields.
- The same evidence in month 6: what the farmer can do, and what their records show.

**The paper track is never second best.** A farmer who keeps a clean money book is as good a
record keeper as one who uses an app. Many strong farmers will use both.

---

## 2. Switching the track on or off

### 2a. The programme switch (decide before the mentor course)

The programme manager decides, with the facilitators, **before Day 0** (or before Week 1 online):

| Question | If mostly YES → | If mostly NO → |
|---|---|---|
| Do most mentors have a smartphone that can run the app, and a way to charge it? | | APP OFF |
| Can the programme pay a small data allowance for mentors (and farmers on the app track)? | | APP OFF |
| Has the organisation set up ImbewuField accounts (mentor role; farmers linked under People & gardens)? | | APP OFF (or ON later) |
| Do at least some farmers have smartphones (or a youth in the household who does)? | APP ON | APP ON for mentors only |

**Phone check (10 minutes at the Course 1 closing or first visit):** for each farmer, ask:
"Do you have a smartphone? Does someone in your house? Do you have data or Wi-Fi sometimes?
Would you like to keep your records on the phone, on paper, or both?" Tick A / P / A+P.

The three programme settings:

| Setting | Mentors record visits in… | Farmers keep records in… |
|---|---|---|
| **APP ON** | Mentor workspace → Record visit (paper as back-up) | App if track A or A+P; paper if P |
| **APP ON for mentors only** | Mentor workspace | Paper for all farmers |
| **APP OFF** | Paper visit forms (visit-playbook.md §8) | Paper for all farmers |

There is **no switch inside the app** to press: "switching" is a programme decision written on
the caseload sheet and in the programme plan. With APP OFF, nobody needs a phone.

### 2b. The per-farmer switch (inside an APP ON programme)

Agreed at the **first visit** with the farmer, written on the caseload sheet (Track column):
- **A — App:** the farmer uses their own phone.
- **P — Paper:** record book, money book, duplicate invoice book.
- **A+P — Paper with an app helper:** the farmer keeps paper; a family helper (often a youth)
  enters it in the app weekly. The paper is the original.

**Changing track** is allowed at any visit ("my phone was stolen", "my grandson left", "I got a
phone"). Write the date of the change. Move **app → paper** at once if the phone is lost: the
farmer carries on in a book the same day. Move **paper → app** only when the farmer asks, and
keep the book for the months before (don't re-enter old months unless the farmer wants to).

### 2c. In the day files

Every session that uses the app is written as a pair **[APP] / [PAPER]** of equal length and the
same outcome. Teach the pair that fits; in a mixed class, split into two rooms/corners with one
facilitator each. Slides for app-only content carry `Track: app`.

## 3. The season sequence — 9 exercises

One exercise per individual visit (step 4 of the Visit Hand, "Try it together") or as a station
at the cluster visit. Green farmers can do the next one at the cluster visit and send proof by
WhatsApp or show it next time.

| Month | # | App exercise (track A) | Paper twin (track P) | The outcome (both) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | E1 | Set up profile & consent (Onboarding, language) | Consent form + record-book cover page | The farmer knows what is recorded and who sees it |
| 1 | E2 | **Map** your garden (boundary, water storage, pins) | Garden map on the first page of the record book | A map of the garden with beds and water |
| 2 | E3 | **Garden Survey** → beds + 6-week plan → **Crop Plan** | Bed plan + 6-week plan page | A written plan: what goes where, when |
| 3 | E4 | **Field Journal** — a weekly note and photo | Garden diary — a weekly line and drawing | A habit of noting what happens |
| 4 | E5 | **Log a harvest** (My Records → **Picked**, kg) | Harvest page (date, crop, kg, where it went) | Every harvest weighed and written |
| 5 | E6 | **Finances: Money in / Money out** (My Records → **Sold** / **Spent**) | Money book (in / out columns) | Every rand in and out written |
| 5 | E7 | **Scan a till slip** (Receipt photo; *Read this photo with Lima*; check it) | Slip envelope + money-out line | Every cost has its proof |
| 5 | E8 | **Invoice builder**: build, save, share an invoice | Duplicate invoice book | A buyer gets a clear written invoice; a copy stays |
| 6 | E9 | **Reports**: read (and share) a report | Month-end summary page | The farmer can say what the season gave and cost |

The app's **Studies** also carries an 8-unit Finance course (*Understand the farm's money* →
*Plan, compare and improve the business*). Green farmers on track A can take it between
visits; Course 4 (Farmer Money) covers the same ground in a classroom.

## 4. The app exercises — coach it on the visit

**Every script follows I do · We do · You do.** The farmer's own phone, the farmer's own hands.
During *You do* the mentor's hands stay in their pockets. Use sample details if the farmer is not
ready to enter real ones. If there is no signal, the app saves offline — show the farmer that.
Screen names below are the ones in the app; if a label has moved in a newer version, follow the
app's own guide in Studies and tell the programme office.

### E1 · Set up profile & consent (month 1, 12 min)
- **Before:** farmer has installed the app (or you help with the Play Store link and their own
  Google account — never yours).
- **I do (3 min):** show the language picker; read the consent screen aloud in the farmer's
  language: what is saved, who sees it.
- **We do (5 min):** farmer chooses language, reads/hears consent, types name; you point.
- **You do (3 min):** farmer opens the app again from Home and finds their profile.
- **Talk (1 min):** "Who can see your records?" *(the farmer, their mentor/organisation as
  agreed)*.
- **Check:** farmer opens the app alone, in their language, and can say who sees their records.

### E2 · Map your garden (month 1, 15 min)
- **I do (3 min):** on Map, show drawing a boundary with a finger, then one pin.
- **We do (6 min):** farmer draws the garden boundary; adds the water storage (drum/tank) and a
  pin for the compost heap. Walk to the corners together if GPS helps.
- **You do (4 min):** farmer adds one more pin (e.g. seedling nursery) alone and saves.
- **Talk (2 min):** "Where does the water come from and go?" (link to Course 1 site reading).
- **Check:** the map shows the boundary, water storage and at least 2 pins that match the real garden.

### E3 · Garden Survey → Crop Plan (month 2, 15 min)
- **I do (3 min):** open Garden Survey; show step 1 of the 5-step wizard.
- **We do (7 min):** farmer answers the steps (beds, sun, water, what they want to grow). The
  survey gives beds and a 6-week plan.
- **You do (3 min):** farmer opens **Crop Plan** and finds *this week's* tasks (Day/Week view).
- **Talk (2 min):** "Does the plan match your garden and your family's plate? What would you change?"
  Compare with the Course 1 90-day plan and the spacing check.
- **Check:** farmer can find this week's tasks and name the next thing to plant and where.

### E4 · Field Journal (month 3, 10 min)
- **I do (2 min):** add one journal entry: a photo of a bed and one line ("aphids on 3 cabbages").
- **We do (4 min):** farmer adds today's entry.
- **You do (3 min):** farmer finds last week's entry (or today's) again.
- **Talk (1 min):** "When in the week will you write?" Agree a day. Optional: ask **Lima** one
  question about what the photo shows — and check the answer against what you both see; if
  unsure, ask the mentor or extension officer.
- **Check:** at least one entry per week since the last visit (look back at the next visit).

### E5 · Log a harvest (month 4, 12 min)
- **Before:** have produce and a scale (kitchen or hanging scale).
- **I do (3 min):** weigh a bunch; open **My Records → Picked**; check *Recent harvests* first
  so it is not counted twice; choose the crop; type **Kg harvested**; save.
- **We do (4 min):** farmer weighs and records the next crop.
- **You do (3 min):** farmer records one more alone and finds it in *Recent harvests*.
- **Talk (2 min):** "Six bunches is not six kilograms — weigh first." The form uses today's
  date, so record on the day you pick (or keep the real date on paper). A harvest is not a sale.
  Optional: **Lima Vision** can help estimate or weigh from a photo — treat it as a helper, and
  use the scale for the record.
- **Check:** the saved kilograms match the scale; the farmer can find the entry again.

### E6 · Finances: Money in / Money out (month 5, 15 min)
- **I do (3 min):** show the one book with three tabs: **Picked · Sold · Spent**. Sold is money
  in; Spent is money out. Record one sale (sample: 5 bunches spinach, R10 each = R50).
- **We do (5 min):** farmer records a real cost under **Spent → Log a cost** (What for, Amount (R)
  with cents, Supplier optional). Check first that it isn't already there.
- **You do (5 min):** farmer records one sale and one cost alone.
- **Talk (2 min):** "What came in this week? What went out? What's left?" Use **Edit** to correct
  a wrong entry — don't add a second copy. Don't type 0 when you don't know the amount; find
  the slip first.
- **Check:** one sale and one cost saved, amounts match the evidence, farmer can find both.

### E7 · Scan a till slip (month 5, 10 min)
- **Before:** a real till slip (seed, manure, a hose fitting).
- **I do (2 min):** in **Log a cost**, add a **Receipt photo** (Take photo), then *Read this
  photo with Lima*.
- **We do (4 min):** farmer photographs their slip (flat, good light, all of it in the frame);
  compare what Lima suggests with the slip **line by line** — item, supplier, total.
- **You do (3 min):** farmer corrects anything wrong, saves, reopens it with **View slip**.
- **Talk (1 min):** "Lima reads; you check. The slip is the proof. Keep the paper slip too —
  the photo stays on this phone."
- **Check:** the saved cost equals the slip total to the cent; the slip opens from the entry.

### E8 · Invoice builder — build and send (month 5, 15 min)
- **Before:** a real or sample buyer (e.g. the crèche kitchen buys 10 bunches of spinach at R10,
  pays at month end). Buyer's name, what, how many, price, date, when they'll pay.
- **I do (3 min):** open **Invoice** (/invoice); show the parts: your name and contact, the
  buyer, the line (item · quantity · unit · price), the total, the dates.
- **We do (6 min):** farmer fills in the invoice; you read the total aloud together; save.
- **You do (4 min):** farmer reopens it from **Saved** and uses **Share PDF** (WhatsApp) or
  **Print**.
- **Talk (2 min):** "An invoice is **not** a payment. Mark it paid only when the money arrives.
  Another copy is **not** another sale — reopen the saved one." The VAT/tax-number field only
  prints a reference; it doesn't calculate VAT (most smallholders are not VAT vendors; check
  with SARS guidance or a bookkeeper).
- **Check:** a saved invoice with correct total and dates, shared to the buyer (or a sample
  contact); the farmer can say whether it is paid.

### E9 · Read a report (month 6, 15 min)
- **I do (3 min):** open **Reports**; choose the season; show the parts (harvest, money in, money
  out). Read one number aloud and ask "Is that right?"
- **We do (6 min):** read it together; check two numbers against what the farmer remembers or
  the paper slips. The report is written by AI from the records: it is only as good as the
  records, and it can be wrong — check it.
- **You do (4 min):** farmer tells you, in their own words, three things the season gave and cost.
  Print or share if they want (to the programme, a buyer, a savings group).
- **Talk (2 min):** "What will you grow more of next season? Less of?"
- **Check:** farmer can say total kg harvested, money in and money out for the season (or say
  which records are missing).

## 5. The paper twins — same outcome, books and a pencil

**The farmer's paper kit** (resources.md): one A5 exercise book = **record book** (map, plan,
diary, harvest pages); one A5 book = **money book**; one **duplicate invoice book** (carbon,
from a stationer or spaza); one **slip envelope** (a used A5 envelope stapled inside the money
book's back cover); a pencil and a pencil sharpener. Farmers who don't write: tally marks and
drawings, with a family helper for words and numbers.

Coach each twin exactly like the app exercise: I do · We do · You do · Talk, same minutes, same
check.

| # | Paper twin | What the page looks like | Check (same as app) |
|---|---|---|---|
| E1 | Consent form + cover | Consent form read aloud and signed/marked; cover: name, cluster, mentor's number | Farmer can say who sees their records |
| E2 | Garden map | Page 1: a bird's-eye drawing with N arrow (sun in the north), beds, water, compost, gate; paced measurements | Map shows boundary, water, 2+ features that match the garden |
| E3 | Bed plan + 6-week plan | Page 2: each bed a box with crop and spacing; page 3: weeks 1–6, what to sow/plant/do | Farmer names this week's task and the next planting |
| E4 | Garden diary | One line per week: date · what I saw · what I did (drawing fine) | One line per week since the last visit |
| E5 | Harvest page | Columns: Date · Crop · Kg · Eaten / Given / Sold / Stored | Kg matches the scale; entry findable |
| E6 | Money book | Columns: Date · What · Money in (R) · Money out (R); a line under each week with the week's totals | One sale and one cost written; amounts match proof |
| E7 | Slip envelope | Every slip in the envelope, numbered; the number written beside the money-out line | Money-out line equals the slip; slip found by its number |
| E8 | Duplicate invoice book | Invoice no. · date · farmer's name & phone · buyer · item, qty, unit, price · total · due date · "Paid on __" box. Top copy to buyer, carbon stays | Correct total and dates; copy in the book; paid box only filled when paid |
| E9 | Month-end summary | Last page each month: total kg harvested (by crop), total money in, total money out, in − out, one sentence "This month the garden…" | Farmer can say season totals from the summaries |

## 6. What the mentor uses

| Mentor task | APP ON | APP OFF |
|---|---|---|
| Farmer list & clusters | Mentor workspace → **People & gardens** | Caseload sheet (visit-playbook.md §9a) |
| Individual visit record | **Record visit** (support area, observations, skill observed + result, agreed action, responsible person, follow-up date, photos, optional GPS; close an action with *Completed on* + *Completion evidence*) | Paper visit form (§8a) |
| Phone check-in | Record visit, note "phone check-in" | Phone log (§8b) |
| Cluster visit, field day | **Training** (attendance register, signatures, photos, venue, feedback) | Register (forms.md §1) + cluster record (§8c) |
| Farmer study progress | **Learning** (module progress) | Skills checklist (forms.md §4) |
| No signal | **Offline & sync**: save visits, registers, signatures and photos offline; save unfinished drafts; they send later | Paper is always offline |
| Weekly handover | Records sync automatically; tell the supervisor if a draft is stuck | Photograph or hand in form copies every Friday |

**Rule for both:** record at the farm, with the farmer, and read it back.

## 7. Phones, data and privacy

- **The farmer's phone is the farmer's.** Ask before touching it; never read their messages or
  photos; hand it back for *You do*.
- **Shared phones:** a family phone may be used by several people. Show the farmer how to
  close the app; discuss who else can see the records.
- **Data costs:** most exercises work offline and sync later. Share over WhatsApp only small
  files (a PDF invoice is small; videos are not). Programmes should budget a data allowance
  for mentors (resources.md).
- **Lost or stolen phone:** switch to paper the same day (§2b). Tell the programme office so
  the account can be secured.
- **Consent:** photos of people only with consent (and never of children without guardian
  consent). Money records are private: in group practice always use sample numbers.
