before Day 1 (never the same day). Host-farmer visits start **one week before**.

## 1. One week before — host farmers (the most important job)

Day 2 sends learners in **trios** to real farmers who did Course 1 (or any programme farmer).

**How many hosts?** One per trio, plus **two spares**.
24 learners → 8 trios → **10 hosts**. 30 learners → 10 trios → **12 hosts**.
Plus **one cluster site** (best: the Course 1 demonstration garden or a strong farmer's garden)
where 8–12 local farmers are invited for the Day 2 afternoon cluster visit.

**Choosing hosts**
- Within **15 minutes' drive** of each other and of the cluster site. Hosts in 2–3 villages.
- A **mix**: some strong gardens, some struggling ones, some just starting. Not only the best.
- Hosts who *want* the visit and have something they would like help with.
- Never a household in crisis (illness, recent death, conflict) — that is not a training visit.

**Visit each host in person** (not only a phone call). Say, in their language:

> "On [date] a small group of three mentors-in-training would like to visit your garden for
> about one hour, from about 10:30. One will lead the visit, one will write, one will watch
> how the mentor does. They will ask about your garden and help with one thing you choose.
> They will not bring inputs or money. You can say no, and you can stop the visit at any time.
> May they come? May they take photos of the garden? Of you?"

Record on the **host sheet** (keep with the lead facilitator):

| Host name & phone | Village / GPS or directions | Consent: visit / photos of garden / photos of people | One thing they would like help with |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | ☐ ☐ ☐ | |

- Fill a POPIA consent form (shared/forms.md §2) with each host. A mark or thumbprint is fine.
- Tell hosts the programme gives a small **thank-you pack** (see resources.md) — from the
  programme, handed over by the facilitator, not by the trainees (the trainees practise the
  "no gifts, no promises" rule).
- Ask the cluster-site host to invite **8–12 neighbours** for 13:00–14:15. Offer water and a
  simple snack for them (budget line in resources.md).
- **Phone every host the day before Day 2** to confirm. Keep the two spare hosts warm.

**If a host cancels on the day:** the trio goes to a spare host. If no spare: the trio joins
another trio as a "second observer" team, **or** stays at the venue for the role-play garden
(section 3). Never cancel the whole visit day for one cancellation.

**If you cannot find enough hosts at all:** run Day 2 S2 as role-play visits at the venue
role-play garden, with local farmers or the co-facilitator as "farmers" reading role cards
(day-2.md has them). The Visit Hand is the same. Tell the programme this happened in your
daily report.

## 2. One week before — the app-track decision (see app-track.md §2)

- Programme manager decides **APP ON** or **APP OFF** for the whole programme.
- If ON: check each learner has a phone that can run the app, has data or Wi-Fi at the venue,
  and has an ImbewuField account with the **mentor** role linked to the organisation.
- Ask the organisation admin to assign each mentor's farmers under **People & gardens** so
  the Record visit button shows. For Day 2, the **host farmers** are the practice participants —
  only with their consent. If hosts are not in the system, trainees use the **paper form** on
  the visit and enter it in the app afterwards as a practice record (then delete or mark as
  training, following your organisation's rules).
- If OFF: nothing to install. Print extra paper visit forms.

## 3. Day 0 at the venue

### 08:30–09:30 · The room
- Circle of chairs (no tables in rows). 5 group stations with a table each, 3 m apart.
- Poster wall: hang **M-P01 to M-P06** (Day 1) in teaching order, left to right, at seated eye
  level, a blank flipchart sheet beside each. Days 2–3 posters rolled and labelled.
- Hang **F-P06, F-P14, F-P15** (Course 1 posters) near the garden door — used Day 1 S3.
- **Question Tree** on a wall or tree. **Parking bay** for "things for the programme office"
  (mentors raise policy questions: stipends, transport, inputs — park them, don't answer
  what you can't promise).
- Register table at the door with pens, name tags, green stickers (no-photo).

### 09:30–10:30 · The role-play garden (Day 1 S3 and the Day 2 back-up)
In a corner of the venue land (at least 6 m × 6 m), set up **5 "problem stations"** so
practice feels real:

| Station | What you set up | The hidden problem |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Bed | A bed with seedlings planted far too close together | Spacing not followed |
| 2 Bare soil | A dug bed with no mulch, dry and cracked | Soil cover / water |
| 3 Seedlings | A seedling tray with leggy, yellow seedlings (or draw it) | Too much shade, no feeding |
| 4 Water | A 20 ℓ drum with no lid, a leaking bottle drip | Mosquitoes, waste |
| 5 Records | A record book with only week 1 filled in | Records stopped |

Each station has a **role card** for the "farmer" (in day-2.md §Role cards).

### 10:30–11:30 · Kit counts
Count **per group of 5** (5 groups) — tick in resources.md:
- A-frame kit (3 poles 2 m, string, stone, nails/wire) · spacing string with knots · seedling
  tray + mix · clipboards ×5 · pencils ×5 · A4 observer checklists · visit forms.

**Trio packs** for Day 2 (one per trio, in a plastic A4 envelope):
- Host name, phone, directions / pin; consent summary (what they agreed to)
- 2 paper visit forms, 1 observer checklist, 1 action-agreement card (to leave with farmer)
- Pencil, 1 m tape or spacing string, a small notebook
- Emergency numbers: lead facilitator, driver, nearest clinic
- (APP ON) phone charged; the app open at Mentor workspace

### 11:30–12:30 · Rehearse
- Each facilitator runs the **listening triad** (Day 1 S2) and the **I do · We do · You do**
  demo (Day 1 S3) on the other. Time them. Cut teacher talk.
- Agree your **call and response** for the room.
- Agree who plays the **"inspector"** and who plays the **"mentor"** in the Day 1 S1 opening
  role-play (2 minutes each; practise both).

### 13:15–14:15 · App or paper packs
- **APP ON:** on the venue tablet/laptop, open the app, sign in with a mentor test account,
  check Mentor workspace → People & gardens → Record visit opens; check that saving works
  **offline** (switch on flight mode, save a draft, switch back, check it syncs). Load the
  sample farmer if your organisation uses one. Note the Wi-Fi password on the board.
- **APP OFF (or both):** 20 visit forms per learner, stapled into a pad with a card cover.
  Print the caseload sheet and week plan (visit-playbook.md §9).

### 14:15–15:00 · Day 2 logistics walk-through
- Transport: seats = learners + facilitators + 2. Fuel. Driver knows every host's location.
  One vehicle per 2–3 villages if hosts are spread. Taxi hire booked in writing.
- Drop-off order and pick-up order written down (route sheet). Pick-up from each host at
  11:45 sharp.
- Packed tea (10:05) and lunch (12:15) for learners + hosts at cluster site.
- Cluster site: shade, water, a place to hang 3 posters (M-P07, M-P11, M-P16), toilet access.
- Rain plan: if heavy rain or lightning, visits are postponed to Day 3 morning and Day 3 S1/S2
  swap to Day 2 — tell hosts the same day.

### 15:00–15:30 · Final check

- [ ] Registers (3 days), consent forms (learners), host consents, certificates (learners + 10%)
- [ ] Posters hung (Day 1), rolled (Days 2–3), A5 cards in flip folder
- [ ] Role-play garden ready, role cards printed
- [ ] Kit counted per group; trio packs made
- [ ] Transport confirmed in writing; hosts phoned
- [ ] First aid kit, drinking water, sunscreen, hats
- [ ] Phones and power banks charged; offline copies of day files on both phones
- [ ] Session sheets (forms.md §3) for the co-facilitator
- [ ] Parking bay sheet up; programme office contact for policy questions
