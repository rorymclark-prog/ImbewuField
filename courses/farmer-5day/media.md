# Course 1 media — Grow Food, Grow Strong

Every image, animation and card the slides, posters and day files use. `REUSE` items already exist
in the repo; `NEW` items are to be generated (see `../shared/PRODUCTION-GUIDE.md`). House rules for all art:
`../shared/poster-standards.md` §Art rules (southern hemisphere, no text in art, generic plants,
people who look like the learners, flat warm illustration, palette).

Poster artworks `F-P01-ART` … `F-P21-ART` are specified in `posters.md` (Art brief) and are
listed in the manifest automatically.

## F-ANI-03 · Two gardens through a dry season
- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: F-D1-08
- Length: 20 s loop, silent

**Brief:** Split screen, same gentle slope, same fixed camera. LEFT: bare pale soil, one crop in rows. RIGHT: mulched beds, mixed plants, a small tree, a swale. Sequence: (0–5 s) a summer storm — left: brown water runs off in channels carrying soil; right: water soaks in, swale fills and empties downward into the soil. (5–12 s) days pass (sun arcs across the NORTH side of the frame, shadows to the south) — left soil cracks, plants wilt; right stays green. (12–20 s) dry season — left bare and dusty; right still producing, mulch visible. Loop back softly. No text.

## F-ANI-17 · Liquid manure drum
- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: F-D3-14
- Length: 15 s loop, silent

**Brief:** Side cut-away of a 20 L plastic drum. (0–3 s) a hand lowers a hessian sack of dark manure on a string tied to a stick laid across the drum mouth. (3–6 s) water pours in to near the brim, a few green leaves added, lid placed. (6–10 s) a day-counter shown as sun/moon icons cycling 14 times while a stick stirs every few cycles; the water darkens to strong-tea brown. (10–15 s) one cup of the dark liquid poured into a watering can beside ten cups of clear water (cups appear one by one); the can waters the soil around a plant, not the leaves. No text, no numbers — the cups show the ratio.

- Type: animation
- Status: NEW
- Used in: F-D4-06
- Length: 15 s loop, silent
