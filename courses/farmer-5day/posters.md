# Course 1 posters — Grow Food, Grow Strong

21 posters, A1 portrait unless stated. Standards: `../shared/poster-standards.md` (layout grid,
≤ 25 words, text-free art + typeset labels, southern hemisphere, palette). Each poster's
artwork is a separate media item (`F-Pxx-ART`) for Codex; the labels are typeset on top so
they can be translated.

## F-P01 · Our class agreement
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: F-D1-S0, F-D1-S1, all days
- Art: F-P01-ART

**Headline:** How we learn together
**Art brief (text-free):** A circle of 10 seated people seen from slightly above — mixed ages,
a gogo in a headscarf, a young man in a cap, a woman with a baby on her back — each with an
open hand towards the middle, where a single seedling grows from a small mound. Keep the art in
the top 35% so the lower part is a **blank ruled area** (12 lines) for the class to write on.
**Labels on the poster (≤ 25 words):**
1. Our agreement (above the lines)
2. Sign here (small, beside a row of 30 empty thumb-print circles at the bottom)
**Layout:** Headline top; art band 35%; 12 ruled lines with a pale Leaf tint for writing;
30 thumb-print circles along the bottom edge.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. ASK: "What do we need from each other so everybody can learn?"
2. Write their words — not yours. Hand the marker to a learner.
3. Make sure these are there: phones silent · one voice at a time · right to pass · no laughing
   at anyone · everyone does the practicals · on time · what's personal stays here · respect
   the land.
4. Everyone signs or thumb-prints a circle.
KEY: If we forget, anyone can point to this poster.

## F-P08 · How much rain from your roof?
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: F-D2-S3
- Art: F-P08-ART

**Headline:** 1 mm on 1 m² = 1 litre
**Art brief (text-free):** Top: a single square metre of ground marked with four pegs and
string, a rain cloud over it, and one 1-litre bottle beside it. Middle: a simple house with a
corrugated roof, a measuring tape along the length and width at ground level, rain falling, and
a row of identical 20 ℓ buckets lined up beside a tank. Bottom: a tank close-up — gutter with a
gentle fall, a vertical first-flush pipe that fills before water reaches the tank, a lid, a tap
raised on a stand above a bucket, and an overflow pipe leading to a planted swale.
**Labels on the poster:**
1. Length × width = m²
2. × rain (mm) × 0.8
3. = litres caught
4. First rain goes out
5. Lid on
6. Overflow to the garden
**Layout:** Headline as a big equation; three bands (rule 25% · house 35% · tank 30%); a
blank white box (A4 size) where the facilitator writes the venue's own sum in marker.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. Show the 1 ℓ bottle: 1 mm on 1 m² = 1 litre.
2. A 20 mm storm on 1 m² = one 20 ℓ bucket.
3. GROUPS: measure the venue roof on the ground.
4. Worked example: 10 × 6 = 60 m² · × 20 mm × 0.8 = 960 ℓ ≈ 48 buckets.
5. Counters method for anyone who prefers: one bottle top = 1 m² = 1 bucket.
6. Tank parts: gutter, first-flush, lid, tap, overflow.
KEY: Roof area × rain × 0.8 = the water you can catch.

## F-P21 · My 90-day garden plan
- Size: A1 portrait
- Used in: F-D5-S4
- Art: F-P21-ART

**Headline:** My next 90 days
**Art brief (text-free):** A path drawn from bottom-left to top-right through three stepping
stones. Stone 1: a person mulching and starting a small compost pile beside a tap. Stone 2: the
same person digging one bed north of the house and planting seedlings with a spacing stick.
Stone 3: the same person harvesting leafy greens into a basket, a jar of seed on the windowsill
and a notebook in their pocket. A second smaller person (the buddy) waves from the side with a
phone. Leave three blank rounded boxes beside the stones for writing.
**Labels on the poster:**
1. First 2 weeks
2. By 6 weeks
3. By 90 days
4. My buddy
**Layout:** Headline; path art 60% with writing boxes; an A4 version is the learner's plan
sheet.
**Teach it — back-of-poster card (A5):**
1. Read the example plan (day-5.md S4).
2. Everyone fills 2–3 actions per stone (drawing fine).
3. 2 minutes each to the group; two stars and a wish.
4. Choose a buddy; write the name.
KEY: Small steps, written down, checked by a buddy.
