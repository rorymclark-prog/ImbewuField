# Day 0 · Preparation (facilitators only)

**Never run Day 0 on the same day as Day 1.** Best: 2–5 days before. At the latest: the
working day before. Both facilitators attend. Allow 6–7 hours.

## On Day 0 at the venue

### 1. Walk the land (60 min)
- Find **north** (compass or phone; or the sun at noon is in the north).
- Where does water run when it rains? Look for bare channels, silt, wet patches.
- Mark **5 practical stations** 3–4 m apart along a gentle slope with pegs + a coloured
  ribbon each (one per group: Bean, Pumpkin, Maize, Banana, Spinach, + Marula if 6 groups).
- Choose where the **swale** line will go (Day 2), the **compost** corner (Day 3), the **beds**
  (Day 3–4) and the **fruit-tree pit** (Day 4). Draw a quick sketch on the back of this page.
- Measure the venue **roof** (length × width of the part that drains to one side) — you will
  do it again with learners on Day 2, but check it's safe and reachable from the ground.
- Check for services underground (water pipes, cables) before any digging. Ask the caretaker.

### 2. Set up the room (60 min)
- Chairs in a **horseshoe or circle**, not rows. Chairs or crates for 30 + mats for the floor.
- Poster wall: hooks, string line or prestik. Put up **only Day 1 posters** (F-P01 → F-P06).
  Hang the others rolled in order, ready.
- **Question Tree** flipchart sheet on a wall or tree outside.
- A table for the register + consent forms at the door; name tags + markers.
- Kit table: tools laid out per group in 5 labelled piles.
- If using slides: test the laptop + TV/projector **in daylight**. If you can't read the
  screen at 3 pm with the curtains you have, plan for **poster mode + tablet for clips**.

### 3. Prepare materials (2–3 hours)
- **A-frame kits** × 5: 2 poles 2 m, 1 crossbar 1.2 m, wire/string, a stone or bolt for the
  plumb-bob, 1 marker, 10 pegs. (Build one yourself to check.)
- **Magazine pictures** (Day 1 S2): 40–60 pictures cut from magazines, newspapers and
  advertising flyers: gardens, bare fields, water, rubbish, markets, animals, families, forests,
  tins, plastic, cities. Put 10 in an envelope per group.
- **Cardboard house** (Day 1 S3): a shoebox with a roof and a door, and a torch or the real sun.
- **Jar test** (Day 3): one clear 1 ℓ jar per group with a lid; set up one the day before
  so learners can see a settled jar on Day 3.
- **Liquid manure drum** (Day 3): a 20 ℓ drum, a hessian/onion sack, string, a stick.
- **Seed-saving samples** (Day 5): dry bean pods, maize cob, a ripe tomato, a pumpkin — and
  one tomato ferment started **2 days before Day 5** so learners see the result.
- **Play money** (Day 5): R10/R20/R50/R100 notes and coins (from Course 4 printables).
- Fill in **group role cards** (Leader, Timekeeper, Materials, Recorder, Reporter) × 5 groups.

### 4. Rehearse (60 min)
- Walk through Day 1 minute by minute with the co-facilitator. Who leads which session?
  (Swap lead each session.) Who keeps the teacher-talk tally?
- Agree your **attention signal** and your local-language **call and response**.
- Agree the plan if it rains (see each day file: *If things go wrong*).
- Save the emergency numbers: nearest clinic, ambulance (10177), police (10111), programme
  office, venue caretaker.
