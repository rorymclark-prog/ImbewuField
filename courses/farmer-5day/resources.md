# Course 1 resources — kit, materials, budget

For one course of **25–30 learners in 5–6 groups**, 2 facilitators. Prices are 2026 South
African estimates for budgeting only — **check locally**. The **permanent kit** is bought once
and reused for every course; **consumables** are bought per course; **learner-brought** items
make the course belong to the community and cut costs.

## 7. Media for slide mode

- Download the course phone pack (`courses/build/farmer-5day/`) and all clips from
  `public/course-animations/` and `public/course-media/farmer-5day/` **before** travelling.
- A TV or projector must be tested in daylight on Day 0. If you can't read it at 15:00,
  use posters + the tablet for clips.
- Total clip size for this course ≈ 60–80 MB. Keep them on the tablet, not streamed.
