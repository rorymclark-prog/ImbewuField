# Course 1 slides — Grow Food, Grow Strong

Five decks, one per day. Every slide mirrors a poster, so the facilitator can switch between
the wall and the screen at any time. Speaker notes are short; the full script is the day file.
Built into `.pptx` by `scripts/courses/build.py`.

## F-D2-10 · Test it

## F-D2-11 · The magic rule
Screen:
- 1 mm of rain on 1 m² = 1 litre
Media: F-P08-ART
Poster: F-P08
Notes:
F-D2-S3. Hold up the 1 litre bottle. A 20 mm storm on 1 m² = one 20 litre bucket.

## F-D2-12 · Measure the roof
Type: activity
Screen:

- Never climb the roof
Media: F-IMG-11
Notes:
Knotted string (knot every metre) or a tape.

## F-D2-13 · How many buckets?
Screen:
- 10 m × 6 m = 60 m²
- 60 × 20 mm × 0.8 = 960 litres
- ≈ 48 buckets from one storm
Media: F-IMG-12
Poster: F-P08
Notes:
Worked example, then the venue roof. Counters method: one bottle top = 1 m² = 1 bucket. Yearly: × your area's yearly rain (check locally).

## F-D2-14 · Gutter, first-flush, tank

Media: F-ANI-10
Poster: F-P08
Notes:
Each learner estimates their own roof and writes the storm number on their map.
