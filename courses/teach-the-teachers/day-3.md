## T-D3-S1 · 09:00–10:20 · A practical, not a demo · safety · kit (80 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **name** why practicals collapse into demos, **plan** a practical
for groups of 5 (and a carousel when there is one kit), **find** hazards on a practical site,
and **count** kit in and out.

**Poster:** T-P07, T-P06, T-P16 · **Slides:** T-D3-02 → T-D3-06 · **Clip:** T-ANI-02 (optional)
**Materials:** sticky notes (2 colours); per group: 1 "rescue" scenario card, 1 flipchart, marker;
a **hazard scene** set up by the co-trainer before 09:00 outside the door (list in step 6);
first aid kit; one group crate with its kit list.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Look — the 2022 story; why do practicals collapse? | 4 min |
| 09:15 | Talk — T-P07 six moves | 5 min |
| 09:23 | Try — rescue the practical (scenario cards) | 2 min |
| 09:43 | Try — design a carousel for one A-frame | 2 min |
| 09:55 | Try — spot the hazard; safety rules | 5 min |
| 10:10 | Kit in, kit out; take home | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **SAY (09:00, lead — the story, 3 minutes max):** "In 2022 we visited 23 farmer courses in
   three provinces. In many of them, the big practical became one facilitator digging while
   25 people watched — even when the kit for four groups was in the car. Nobody planned it.
   It just happened. Today is about why, and how to stop it."
2. **GROUPS (09:03, 7 min):** "Why does it happen? One reason per sticky note." Then sort the
   notes onto two flipcharts: **We can fix it** / **We can't fix it**.
   *(Time pressure · fear of mistakes · easier to control · facilitator wants to show skill ·
   not enough tools · kit not unpacked · learners expect to watch · respect — learners wait for
   the teacher · heat · big class · facilitator tired.)*
   **ASK:** "How many are really on the 'can't fix' side?" *(Almost none.)*
3. **Talk (09:15):** Reveal **T-P07**. Point ①–⑥:
   ① **Count the kit per group** the day before. ② **Show for 3 minutes, max** — then hands.
   ③ **Groups of 5 with roles** (T-P06). ④ **Stations 3 m apart**, each with its own kit.
   ⑤ **Hands behind your back** — walk, ask, don't take the spade. ⑥ **One kit? Carousel** —
   never demo-only; nobody watches more than 10 minutes.
   **SAY:** "Put your hands behind your back when a group is working. If you take the spade,
   the practical is over."
4. **GROUPS (09:23, 12 min + 8 min report):** Each group gets one "rescue" card; plans the
   rescue on a flipchart; 2 minutes to present.

   | Rescue card | A strong rescue |
   |---|---|
   | Only one spade and 28 learners | Carousel: spade station + 4 stations that need no spade (collect browns, measure bed, mix soil by hand, draw the plan); swap every 8 min |
   | Heavy rain at 10:50 | Move the practical under the roof: tray/bucket versions (swale in a seedling tray, compost in a crate); dig tomorrow morning |
   | One learner takes over the group | Give him the Timekeeper or Reporter role; ask "who hasn't had a turn?"; talk privately at tea |
   | Elders won't get hands dirty | Honour it: give Leader or Recorder roles; ask them to judge the work and tell how it was done before; never force |

5. **GROUPS (09:43, 12 min):** "You have **one** A-frame kit and 25 learners. Design a carousel
   for Course 1's A-frame practical (F-D1-S4)." Show on a flipchart: 5 stations, 8 min each,
   what's at each one. Gallery-read in 2 minutes.
   *(Model answer: ① build/calibrate with the kit · ② mark the level with a water-hose level
   or a bottle-level · ③ draw my garden map · ④ collect and sort mulch · ⑤ roof-water sum at
   the building. Everyone does ① once.)*
6. **Spot the hazard (09:55, 10 min):** Walk outside. The co-trainer set up: a spade lying
   point-up · a fork leaning on a wall at head height · an open bottle labelled "chilli spray"
   next to the food · no drinking water · a hose across the path · the first aid kit locked in
   a car · a group working in full sun at 12:30 · a pick being swung near people (a trainee
   mimes it). **GROUPS:** "Find 8 hazards in 4 minutes." Reporters list them.
   **SAY** the safety rules (T-P07 back card + toolkit §10): tools carried points down; nobody
   behind a swinging tool; gloves or handwashing for manure; sprays away from eyes and food;
   hats, water, heavy work before 11:00; lightning = stop and go inside; first aid kit **open
   and visible**; know the clinic route; count people back.
7. **Kit in, kit out (10:10, 5 min):** Open a group crate. The Materials person reads the kit
   list; the group counts out loud; they sign it on the list. **SAY:** "Nobody leaves until the
   count matches. Every day."
8. **Take home (10:15):** Each person writes: "My rule so my practicals never become demos."

### Key messages
- Practicals collapse by accident. **Plan against it**: kit per group, 3-minute demo, roles,
  **hands behind your back**.
- **One kit = carousel.** Never demo-only.
- Safety is part of the lesson: first aid kit open, tools points-down, heat, lightning.

### If things go wrong
- **Trainees get defensive about the 2022 story ("we had no tools!"):** agree — then point to
  the carousel. "The fix is ours."
- **Hazard scene can't be set up:** use the illustration T-IMG-16 (hazard scene) or describe
  it; groups draw what's wrong.

### Language & inclusion
- The hazard walk is visual; non-readers do well here. Let them lead the report.
- ✂ **Cut if short:** the carousel design (step 5) becomes homework for the evening.

## T-D3-S2 · 10:40–12:10 · Model practical: living soil in groups of 5 — then unpack (90 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **do** four soil tests (squeeze, jar, worm count, infiltration),
**set up** a side-by-side trial, and **name** what the teacher did to keep every hand busy.

**Poster:** F-P10, F-P11, T-P21 · **Slides:** T-D3-07 → T-D3-10 · **Clips:** T-ANI-17, T-ANI-19 (slide mode)
**Materials (per group of 5):** 1 spade · 1 clear 1 ℓ jar with lid · 1 tsp salt or a few drops of
dishwashing liquid · 1 tin with both ends cut off (a 410 g tin) · 1 × 1 ℓ water + a jug ·
phone timer · 1 white sheet or feed bag (to sort soil on) · 2 × halved 2 ℓ bottles as pots
(with drainage holes) · 10 bean seeds · 1 cup compost · labels + pen · group crate ·
**practical-check card** for the observer. Class: the Day 0 settled jar.
**Room:** the dig patch, 4 stations 3 m apart; the co-trainer teaches.

**Observer:** each group's Recorder carries the **practical-check card**: for each of the 5
group members, one tick for every 5 minutes they had their hands in the task.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 10:40 | MODEL — Look: two handfuls of soil | 2 min |
| 10:45 | MODEL — Try: four tests, roles, stations | 3 min (≤ 3 min demo) |
| 11:15 | MODEL — Talk: debrief + F-P10; the settled jar | 4 min |
| 11:23 | MODEL — Try: side-by-side trial (T-P21) | 2 min |
| 11:32 | MODEL — Take home | 0 min |
| 11:35 | UNPACK — the practical-check cards | 3 min |
| 11:45 | UNPACK — what did the teacher do? | 3 min |
| 11:55 | CONTENT — soil & compost questions | 6 min |

### Say / ask / do — model (co-trainer; trainees are farmers)
1. **Look (10:40):** Pass round two handfuls: dark crumbly compost-rich soil; pale hard yard
   soil. **ASK:** "Smell. Squeeze. Which would you plant in? Why?" *(The dark one: smells of
   forest, crumbles, holds water.)*
2. **Try (10:45):** **SAY (≤ 3 min demo):** squeeze a moist ball, push it into a ribbon between
   thumb and finger — "a long ribbon is clay, no ribbon is sand" — and show the jar: fill
   one third soil, add water to near the top, a pinch of salt, shake. **Then hands behind back.**
   **GROUPS (27 min, roles on the card):**
   - **Squeeze & ribbon test** — each person does it with soil from their station.
   - **Jar test** — Materials fills, everyone shakes in turn (1 min each), set it aside.
   - **Worm count** — dig a hole a spade wide and a spade deep; sort the soil on the sheet;
     count worms and other life; put everything back.
   - **Infiltration** — push the tin 3 cm into the ground; fill with water; time how long it
     takes to soak in. Do it on bare ground and in a mulched/garden spot.
   Recorder draws results. Co-trainer walks, hands behind back, asks: "What surprised you?"
   "Why is the water slower here?" Calls "5 minutes left" at 11:07.
3. **Talk (11:15):** Reveal **F-P10**. Three-question debrief (one answer per group each).
   Show the Day 0 jar with settled layers: **ASK:** "Which layer is sand? silt? clay? What floats?"
   *(Sand at the bottom, then silt, clay on top; organic matter floats.)*
4. **Try — side-by-side (11:23, 9 min):** Reveal **T-P21**. **SAY:** "Farmers learn best when
   they test it themselves." **GROUPS:** fill one pot with yard soil, one with yard soil + a cup
   of compost; sow 5 beans in each; label; water the same. "We'll look on Day 5. What do you
   predict?" Recorder writes/draws the prediction.
5. **Take home (11:32):** "One test you'll do on your own soil this week."

### Say / ask / do — unpack (lead)
6. **DO (11:35):** Recorders read the practical-check cards. **ASK:** "Did anyone have fewer than
   4 ticks? Why?" *(Waiting for the spade; holding the jar; recording.)* "How could the teacher
   have fixed that?" *(Two spades; everyone does the ribbon test; Recorder swaps at half time.)*
7. **GROUPS (11:45, 10 min):** "What did the teacher do to keep every hand busy?" List on a flipchart.
   *(3-minute demo only; kit already at stations; four tasks at once; roles; hands behind
   her back; questions not answers; 5-minutes-left call; the jar made on Day 0 — no waiting.)*
   **ASK:** "What's the **side-by-side trial** for?" *(Farmers see the result themselves; the
   group owns the finding — this is how farmer field schools work.)*
8. **Content (11:55, 15 min):** Questions on soil and compost. Use the notes below.

### Content refresh notes
- **Texture:** sand (gritty, no ribbon, drains fast), silt (smooth, floury), clay (sticky, long
  ribbon, holds water and food but can waterlog). Loam = a mix. **Organic matter** improves
  every soil: it holds water in sand and opens up clay.
- **Soil life:** worms, fungi, bacteria, insects. More life = better structure and food for
  plants. Bare soil in the sun loses life fast → cover it (mulch).
- **Compost heap:** at least about 1 m × 1 m × 1 m to heat up; layers of brown (dry, carbon:
  dry grass, leaves, maize stalks) and green (fresh, nitrogen: kitchen scraps, fresh cuttings,
  manure) — roughly **2 parts brown to 1 part green** by volume; moist like a squeezed sponge;
  turn every 1–2 weeks; ready in about 2–3 months (longer if not turned).
- **Liquid manure:** a sack of manure in a drum of water, stirred, for about 2 weeks; dilute to
  weak-tea colour before use.
- **pH:** home vinegar/bicarbonate tests are rough; for real numbers, a lab test — check locally
  (Department of Agriculture or a co-op may help).
- **Farmer field school (FFS):** a group of farmers meets regularly through a season on a
  learning plot, **observes and compares** (e.g. with and without compost), and decides what
  works. Started by FAO in Asia (1989) for pest management; used across Africa.

### Debrief (the three questions — unpack)
1. What did the teacher do? 2. Why did every person get their hands in? 3. What will you copy
in Round 3 this afternoon?

### Key messages
- **Demo ≤ 3 minutes, then hands.** Four tasks at once keep 5 people busy.
- Prepare anything that takes time (the settled jar) **before** the day.
- Farmers **compare and decide** — side-by-side trials build confidence.

### If things go wrong
- **Ground too hard to dig:** water the dig patch the evening before; or bring soil in buckets.
- **No worms found:** that's a result! Ask why (dry, bare, compacted). Look under mulch or a stone.
- **A trainee digs for the whole group:** the observer's card will show it. Use it in the unpack.

### Language & inclusion
- Every test has a seated option (ribbon test, jar shaking, recording).
- Offer gloves; some cultures or health conditions make people avoid touching soil or manure.
- ✂ **Cut if short:** infiltration test (keep the other three).

## T-D3-S3 · 12:55–14:00 · Mode 2: slides — laptop · TV · projector · tablet · [PAPER] flip-book · handovers (65 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **set up** and **test** a slide show for a group (big screen or tablet),
**teach** two slides without reading them, **use** a paper flip-book when there is no power,
and **plan** a co-teaching handover.

**Poster:** T-P13, T-P14 · **Slides:** T-D3-11 → T-D3-15 · **Clips:** T-ANI-17 (tablet station)
**Materials:** laptop with `F-D2.pptx` and `F-D3.pptx`; TV (43"+) or projector + screen; HDMI cable,
extension lead; 4 tablets (animations saved in the gallery; decks as PDF); 4 printed **flip-books**
(A4, landscape, the F-D3 slides printed one per page, in a clear-file folder or ring-bound);
Station cards A, B, C.
**Room:** Room A, three stations. Draw curtains on the screen side if possible.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 12:55 | Energiser — trainee volunteer | 0 min |
| 13:00 | Look — can you read it from the back? | 2 min |
| 13:04 | Talk — T-P13 slide column: when slides help, how to set up | 6 min |
| 13:12 | Try — three stations × 8 min (big screen · tablet · flip-book) | 3 min |
| 13:38 | Talk — T-P14: hand over, don't take over | 4 min |
| 13:44 | Pair studio — plan Round 3 handovers, finish posters | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **Look (13:00):** Show slide F-D3-01 on the projector/TV with the room as it is (curtains
   open). Trainees stand at the back wall. **ASK:** "Who can read it?" *(Often: nobody, if it's a
   projector in daylight.)* **SAY:** "In 2022 a course lost a morning to an unreadable projector.
   That's why posters are the default." If there's no projector, show T-IMG-03.
2. **Talk (13:04):** Reveal **T-P13** (slides column). **ASK** first: "When would slides teach
   better than a poster?" *(Moving things: water under the ground, a heap heating over weeks,
   seeds germinating; photos of a pest out of season; many learners indoors.)*
   Then the set-up rules (read from the card, don't lecture):
   - Test **from the back, at the time of day you will teach**. Arrive 60 minutes early.
   - **TV** (43 inch or bigger) beats a projector in daylight; keep the group within about 4–5 m.
   - **Projector:** shade the screen side, lights off near the screen.
   - **Presenter view** — notes on the laptop, slide on the screen.
   - Press **B** (black screen) when you want eyes on you or on the practical.
   - **Tablet mode:** one tablet per group of 5, brightness up, clip saved offline; the
     Recorder holds it; watch → do → talk.
   - Carry the deck **three ways**: laptop, flash drive, PDF on your phone.
   - **Slides mirror the posters.** The poster stays on the wall; switch any time.
3. **Stations (13:12, 3 × 8 min):** Groups rotate.
   - **A · Big screen:** each pair teaches 2 slides from F-D3 in Presenter view, using **B** once,
     without reading the slide. Others: thumbs up if they could read it.
   - **B · Tablet:** watch T-ANI-17 (living soil, 12 s loop) on the tablet; then one trainee
     teaches the clip in 2 minutes while the tablet goes round; swap.
   - **C · [PAPER] flip-book:** the Reporter turns the pages; the teacher practises
     **show → ask → turn**: show the picture, ask a question, only then turn. Two trainees each.
   **No devices at all?** All three stations become flip-book stations with different decks.
4. **Talk (13:38):** Reveal **T-P14**. Point ①–⑤:
   ① **Plan who leads each step** (write names on the lesson plan).
   ② **Hand over out loud:** "Now Thandi will show you the jar."
   ③ **The one not leading works:** materials, tally, watching groups — no side-talk.
   ④ **Offer, don't take:** an agreed signal (touch your ear) = "may I add something?"; the
   leader says yes or no.
   ⑤ **Debrief together** after — star each other first.
5. **Pair studio (13:44, 16 min):** Pairs finish their Round 3 plan: who leads Look, Try,
   Talk, Take home; the handover lines; their signal; posters ready.

### Key messages
- Slides are a **bonus**, not the plan. Test from the back, in daylight.
- **Tablet per group of 5** beats a big screen for short clips.
- No power? The **flip-book** is the same deck on paper.
- Co-teaching: **hand over, don't take over.**

### If things go wrong
- **Laptop won't talk to the TV:** 2 minutes of trying, then flip-book. Don't lose the room.
- **Tablets flat:** one tablet shared by rotation; the others work on the flip-book.
- **A trainee is afraid of the laptop:** they go to the flip-book station first, then the
  tablet; pair them with a confident partner at the big screen.

### Language & inclusion
- Flip-books and posters are the same pictures — learners who can't read still follow.
- ✂ **Cut if short:** stations to 6 minutes each.

## T-D3-S4 · 14:00–15:55 · Micro-teaching Round 3: fifteen minutes, pairs, half-class, a practical (115 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **co-teach** a 15-minute session with a practical for small
groups, **hand over** smoothly, and **keep** every learner's hands busy.

**Poster:** T-P07, T-P14, T-P10 · **Slides:** T-D3-16 → T-D3-17
**Materials (per stream):** 10 full observer sheets + 5 practical-check cards; timer; time cards
(3 min, 1 min, STOP); each pair's kit, poster and objects (from the MT card); 2 group crates.
**Rooms:** **Stream A** (Bean + Pumpkin) in Room A with the lead; **Stream B** (Maize + Marula)
in the Stream B space with the co-trainer.

**Per teach (22 min):** 15 teach + 7 feedback. The 8 other trainees are farmers: they form
**two groups of 4** for the practical. Two of them are also **observers**: Observer 1 — talk
tally, questions, *Look · Try · Talk · Take home*; Observer 2 — practical-check card and
handovers.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 14:00 | Briefing in streams: order, observers, feedback | 5 min |
| 14:05 | Pair 1 (teach 15 + feedback 7) | 0 |
| 14:27 | Pair 2 | 0 |
| 14:49 | Pair 3 | 0 |
| 15:11 | Pair 4 | 0 |

### Say / ask / do
1. **SAY (14:00, each trainer in their stream):** "Fifteen minutes. A practical in two groups of
   four. You set up your kit **during the previous pair's feedback**." Name the observers for
   each pair (the next pair observes). Show the feedback order:
   - Pair speaks first — each partner stars the **other** partner (1 min).
   - Observer 1: ⭐⭐🌱 with talk % and questions (2 min).
   - Observer 2: practical-check result — did anyone watch more than 5 minutes? handovers? (2 min).
   - Trainer: one star only (1 min). Pair: "Thank you — next time we will…" (1 min).
2. **DO:** Trainer sits at the side, silent, completes their own observer sheet. **Never
   interrupt a teach** — unless there is a safety risk.
3. **After each teach:** the pair writes their talk % on the scoreboard during the next set-up.
4. **DO (15:55):** Streams return to Room A. Tick **Poster** (or the mode used) on the Mode tracker.

### Debrief
In the SC. (Feedback happens after every teach.)

### Key messages
- Two teachers, one lesson: **plan the handovers**.
- 15 minutes can hold a real practical if the kit is ready and the demo is short.

### If things go wrong
- **A pair overruns:** STOP at 15:00 — feedback is not cut. The schedule protects the last pair.
- **One partner does all the talking:** Observer 2 notes it; the wish: "Next time, split the steps."
- **A practical needs water/soil not in the room:** pairs fetch it at lunch; trainers check at 13:55.

### Language & inclusion
- Pairs may teach in two languages — one leads, one retells (that is also *language time*).
- ✂ **Never cut** Round 3. If the day is short, cut S3 stations to 6 minutes each.
