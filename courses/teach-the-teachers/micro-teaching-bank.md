# Micro-teaching bank

24 assignments cut from Course 1 (*Grow Food, Grow Strong*) sessions, plus the lesson-plan
template, the observer sheet and the small cards used in the model lessons.
Print each assignment as an **A6 card** (two sets). Session IDs follow the Course 1 grid in
`farmer-5day/README.md`.

## How the bank is used

| Pool | Cards | Rounds | Length | Who |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A · One idea** | MT-01 → MT-10 | 1 and 2 | 5 min, then 10 min | Solo, to own group of 4 |
| **B · A practical** | MT-11 → MT-18 | 3 and 4 | 15 min, then 20 min | Pairs, to half-class (8) |
| **C · A real session** | MT-19 → MT-24 | 5 | 30 min | Pairs, to half-class (8) |

**Choosing rules**
- Inside a group (Rounds 1–2) nobody teaches the same card; in Round 2 choose a card you did
  **not** teach in Round 1.
- Inside a stream (Rounds 3–5) no two pairs teach the same card in the same round.
- In Round 4 a pair picks a pool-B card **they** haven't taught (another pair may have — learn
  from their feedback and do it better).
- **Scaling:** every card says what to keep for 5 / 10 minutes (pool A) or 15 / 20 minutes
  (pool B). For 20 minutes add a device mode (slides or app) — see "Round 4 mode idea".

## Pool A · One idea (5 or 10 minutes, solo)

### MT-01 · Earth care, people care, fair share
- **From:** F-D1-S1 · **Poster:** F-P02 · **Clip:** T-ANI-11
- **Outcome (5 min):** Learners **name** the three ethics and **point** to one example of each.
- **Outcome (10 min):** …and **give** one example of each from their own yard.
- **Materials:** a handful of soil, a plate, a basket (or a bag).
- **The Try:** Hold up each object — "Which care is this?" Then pairs find one example of each
  in the room or yard.
- **Take home:** "One fair-share thing I'll do this week."

### MT-02 · The sun is in the north
- **From:** F-D1-S3 · **Poster:** F-P05 · **Clip:** T-ANI-13
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **show** which way a shadow points at noon.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **choose** the sunniest place for a vegetable bed at home.
- **Materials:** a stick, the real sun or a torch; cardboard house (10 min).
- **The Try:** Learners stand the stick and mark the shadow; (10) place the cardboard house and
  mark winter shade.
- **Take home:** "Where the sun is in my yard at noon."

### MT-03 · Mulch holds water
- **From:** F-D2-S4 · **Poster:** F-P07 · **Clip:** T-ANI-14
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **measure** runoff from bare and mulched soil.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **choose** a mulch they can get free at home.
- **Materials:** 2 halved 2 ℓ bottles with soil (one mulched), 1 ℓ water, 2 cups.
- **The Try:** Learners pour 500 ml on each and compare the cups.
- **Take home:** "Mulch one bed."

### MT-04 · How much rain from your roof?
- **From:** F-D2-S3 · **Poster:** F-P08 · **Clip:** T-ANI-15
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **calculate** litres from a roof for 10 mm of rain.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **measure** a real roof (or a drawn one) and do the sum.
- **Materials:** tape measure or knotted string (1 m knots), beans to count, a drawn roof.
- **The Try:** Pace or measure the roof; area × mm = litres (use beans for tens).
- **Take home:** "Measure my own roof."

### MT-05 · The squeeze test
- **From:** F-D3-S1 · **Poster:** F-P10 · **Clip:** T-ANI-19
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **find** if a soil is sandy or clayey by squeezing.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **compare** two soils and **say** which holds water better.
- **Materials:** 2 soils, water in a bottle.
- **The Try:** Everyone makes a ball and a ribbon.
- **Take home:** "Squeeze my garden soil."

### MT-06 · Greens and browns
- **From:** F-D3-S2 · **Poster:** F-P11 · **Clip:** T-ANI-18
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **sort** a pile into greens and browns.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **show** the 2 brown : 1 green mix in a bucket.
- **Materials:** a pile of mixed garden waste, 2 feed bags, a bucket.
- **The Try:** Sort by hand; (10) layer a bucket.
- **Take home:** "Start a brown pile."

### MT-07 · Liquid manure
- **From:** F-D3-S2 · **Poster:** F-P12
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **name** the three things in liquid manure (manure, water, time).
- **Outcome (10):** …and **mix** a jar-sized batch and **show** the weak-tea colour to use.
- **Materials:** a small bag of dry manure, a sock or cloth, a jar, water, gloves.
- **The Try:** Make a "tea bag" of manure; dunk it; compare colours.
- **Take home:** "Set up a drum with a sack."

### MT-08 · Harden off seedlings
- **From:** F-D4-S1 · **Poster:** F-P14 · **Clip:** T-ANI-26
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **explain** why seedlings need a week of "toughening".
- **Outcome (10):** …and **plan** a 7-day hardening schedule on a drawn calendar.
- **Materials:** 2 seedlings (one soft, one hardened) or pictures; 7 day cards.
- **The Try:** Compare the two; (10) put day cards in order: shade → morning sun → full sun.
- **Take home:** "Harden my next tray."

### MT-09 · Seed you can save
- **From:** F-D5-S1 · **Poster:** F-P18 · **Clip:** T-ANI-22
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **sort** seed packets into open-pollinated and hybrid (F1).
- **Outcome (10):** …and **choose** three crops to save seed from this season.
- **Materials:** 8 empty seed packets (some F1), a jar of dry beans.
- **The Try:** Find "F1" on the packets; sort.
- **Take home:** "Mark one plant to save seed from."

### MT-10 · Money in, money out
- **From:** F-D5-S3 · **Poster:** F-P20 · **Clip:** T-ANI-23
- **Outcome (5):** Learners **record** two sales and one cost with beans in two tins.
- **Outcome (10):** …and **write or draw** them in a money book and **find** the balance.
- **Materials:** 20 beans, 2 tins, 3 event cards, a ruled sheet.
- **The Try:** Move beans for each event.
- **Take home:** "Start a money book."

## Pool B · A practical (15 or 20 minutes, pairs, two groups of 4)

### MT-11 · Calibrate an A-frame
- **From:** F-D1-S4 · **Poster:** F-P06 · **Clip:** T-ANI-10
- **Outcome:** Learners **calibrate** an A-frame and **mark** three level points on a slope.
- **Materials (per group of 4):** 1 A-frame (pre-built for 15 min; build for 20 min), string +
  stone, marker, 5 pegs. **One A-frame only?** Carousel: group 2 marks water signs, then swap.
- **Keep for 15:** calibration + 3 pegs. **Add for 20:** a clip on a tablet before the Try.
- **Take home:** "Find the level line above my garden."

### MT-12 · A swale on the level line (tray model)
- **From:** F-D2-S2 · **Poster:** F-P09 · **Clip:** T-ANI-16
- **Outcome:** Learners **build** a swale in a tray of soil and **show** where water soaks in.
- **Materials (per group):** a seedling tray or box of damp soil, a stick, a 500 ml bottle with
  holes in the lid ("rain").
- **Keep for 15:** model + rain. **Add for 20:** compare with a "down-slope" channel.
- **Take home:** "Where a swale would go on my land."

### MT-13 · The jar test
- **From:** F-D3-S1 · **Poster:** F-P10
- **Outcome:** Learners **make** a jar test and **name** the layers in a settled jar.
- **Materials (per group):** 1 clear jar with lid, soil, water, salt; **one settled jar made the
  day before** (for the class).
- **Keep for 15:** make + read the settled jar. **Add for 20:** squeeze test to compare.
- **Take home:** "Jar-test my garden soil."

### MT-14 · A trench bed in layers
- **From:** F-D3-S3 · **Poster:** F-P13
- **Outcome:** Learners **build** the layers of a trench bed in a bucket or clear 5 ℓ bottle.
- **Materials (per group):** a cut-open 5 ℓ bottle or bucket, tins / stones, dry sticks, greens,
  browns, manure, soil.
- **Keep for 15:** the layers. **Add for 20:** where a keyhole bed puts the compost basket.
- **Take home:** "Mark out one trench bed."

### MT-15 · Sow a seedling tray
- **From:** F-D4-S1 · **Poster:** F-P14 · **Clip:** T-ANI-26
- **Outcome:** Learners **mix** seedling soil and **sow** a row of cells at the right depth.
- **Materials (per group):** 1 tray (or egg boxes), sand, compost, soil, seeds, labels, water.
- **Keep for 15:** mix + sow 6 cells. **Add for 20:** labels and a watering plan.
- **Take home:** "Sow one tray this week."

### MT-16 · Spacing with string and stick
- **From:** F-D4-S2 · **Poster:** F-P15
- **Outcome:** Learners **mark** planting spots at the right spacing with a knotted string.
- **Materials (per group):** a knotted string (knots every 30 cm), a 50 cm stick, 10 bottle tops.
- **Keep for 15:** mark cabbages. **Add for 20:** spinach and onions in the same bed.
- **Take home:** "Make my own spacing string."

### MT-17 · The ladder and a natural spray
- **From:** F-D4-S3 · **Poster:** F-P16 · **Clip:** T-ANI-20
- **Outcome:** Learners **order** the five ladder steps and **make** a small garlic-chilli-soap
  spray safely.
- **Materials (per group):** ladder cards, 1 garlic bulb, 2 chillies, a little bar soap, a jar,
  water, a strainer, gloves, a spray bottle.
- **Keep for 15:** ladder + spray. **Add for 20:** pest/friend picture sort.
- **Take home:** "Walk my garden every morning for pests."

### MT-18 · Germination test
- **From:** F-D5-S1 · **Poster:** F-P18 · **Clip:** T-ANI-21
- **Outcome:** Learners **set up** a 10-seed germination test and **calculate** the % from a
  finished one.
- **Materials (per group):** a damp cloth or paper towel, 10 seeds, a plastic bag, a label; one
  finished test (made a week before) with sprouted seeds.
- **Keep for 15:** set up + count the finished one. **Add for 20:** drying and storing seed.
- **Take home:** "Test my saved seed before planting."

**Round 4 mode idea (any pool-B card):** *slides* — show 2–3 slides from the matching F-deck
on a TV or a tablet per group before the Try; *app* — play the lesson animation from **My
Studies** (offline) on one tablet per group, and record attendance in **Mentor → Training**.
*Paper twin:* the flip-book pages or printed stills.

## Pool C · A real Course 1 session (30 minutes, pairs)

Each card is a **30-minute cut** of a Course 1 session. Pairs hand-make the A1 poster (T-P09
rules) and write the *Teach it* card.

### MT-19 · Two gardens and six principles
- **From:** F-D1-S2 · **Posters:** F-P03, F-P04 (+ T-P19 for trainers)
- **Outcome:** Learners **explain** what makes the "good" garden stronger and **match** four
  garden pictures to principles.
- **Try (12 min):** magazine-picture hunt — groups cut or choose pictures for each principle.
- **Materials (per group):** 6 old magazines or 20 printed photos, scissors, glue, a flipchart.
- **Take home:** "One principle I'll use at home."

### MT-20 · Read the land
- **From:** F-D1-S3 · **Poster:** F-P05 · **Clips:** T-ANI-13, T-ANI-14
- **Outcome:** Learners **find** north, sun, shade, wind, slope and water flow on a short walk
  and **draw** them on a site map.
- **Try (15 min):** a 4-stop site walk (stop-and-talk), then groups draw the map.
- **Materials (per group):** clipboard, A3 paper, pencil, compass (phone), a ribbon for wind.
- **Take home:** "Draw my yard with north on it."

### MT-21 · The water journey
- **From:** F-D2-S1 · **Posters:** F-P07, F-P08 · **Clip:** T-ANI-14, T-ANI-15
- **Outcome:** Learners **show** slow–spread–sink–store on a drawn homestead and **calculate**
  roof water.
- **Try (14 min):** groups place cut-out water features (tank, swale, mulch, pit) on a big
  homestead drawing; then the roof sum.
- **Materials (per group):** A2 homestead drawing, cut-out cards, beans, a tape measure.
- **Take home:** "One place water leaves my yard — and my fix."

### MT-22 · Food-forest layers and a fruit-tree pit
- **From:** F-D4-S4 · **Poster:** F-P17 · **Clip:** T-ANI-24
- **Outcome:** Learners **name** the seven layers and **mark** a fruit-tree pit in the right place
  (not shading the beds).
- **Try (14 min):** layer-card stack; then peg out a pit and say what goes in it.
- **Materials (per group):** 7 layer cards, pegs, string, tape measure.
- **Take home:** "Where my first fruit tree goes."

### MT-23 · Eat the rainbow — the family plate
- **From:** F-D5-S2 · **Poster:** F-P19
- **Outcome:** Learners **fill** a family plate for a day and **plan** two garden crops to fill
  the gap.
- **Try (14 min):** groups build plates from real vegetables or picture cards; then a
  "garden to plate" match.
- **Materials (per group):** a plate, 12 food pictures or real vegetables, a flipchart.
- **Take home:** "One colour my family is missing."

### MT-24 · Selling surplus: price and record
- **From:** F-D5-S3 · **Poster:** F-P20 · **Clip:** T-ANI-23
- **Outcome:** Learners **set** a price for a bunch of spinach from costs and the market, and
  **record** a day of sales.
- **Try (14 min):** cost cards + "market price" cards → set a price; then a sales role play
  with beans as money and the money book.
- **Materials (per group):** 6 cost cards, 3 market-price cards, beans, tins, money book sheet.
- **Take home:** "Write my prices before market day."

## Small cards used in the model lessons

**Talk-tally card (T-D1-S2):** "One mark for every minute the teacher talks to the whole room.
Don't count when groups talk." Space for 45 marks in rows of 5.

**Ladder tally card (T-D2-S2):** three rows — *Closed* · *Open* · *Deeper* — mark each question.

**Practical-check card (T-D3-S2, Rounds 3–5):** 5 names down; columns every 5 minutes; a tick
when that person has hands in the task. Bottom line: "Anyone watching more than 5 minutes? Who?"

**Mode tracker (wall):** names down; columns *Poster* · *Slides / flip-book* · *App / paper twin*.

**Talk scoreboard (wall):** names down; columns *R2 · R3 · R4 · R5* — trainees write their own %.
