## 6 · Paperwork (flip folder)

See the Day 0 table for counts. In short: registers (7 pages), POPIA forms (24), lesson-plan
templates (110), observer sheets (400), practical-check cards (60), session sheets (30), skills
checklists and picture quizzes (20 each), course evaluation (20), personal teaching plans (22),
certificates (22), A5 learning journals (20), name tags and green dots.

## 7 · What trainees bring

Ask in the joining letter:
- A phone (charged), if they have one — with the ImbewuField app installed if the programme uses it.
- A hat, water bottle, work shoes or gumboots for Days 2–3.
- One **object from home** they could teach with (for the Day 1 name circle).
- For Rounds 3–5: small local materials on their MT card (tins, bottles, a bag of soil or manure,
  seed from home).
