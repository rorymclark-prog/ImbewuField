**Brief:** Four scenes appear in turn around a circle, joined by a Mid-green arrow that grows

- Status: NEW

**Brief:** Top view of a practical site. 0–4 s: one facilitator digging, 25 small figures

walk to five stations 3 m apart, each with its own small pile of tools. 8–12 s: every figure is
