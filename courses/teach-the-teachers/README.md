# Course 3 · Teach the Teachers — permaculture & agroecology facilitation

**For:** people who will deliver Course 1 (*Grow Food, Grow Strong*) and other permaculture
or agroecology courses: co-facilitators, young graduates (22–35), extension staff and lead
farmers. Some trainees have a permaculture certificate. Some have only practical farming.
Both are welcome: the course builds **teaching skill** and **content confidence** together.

**Class size:** max **20 trainees**, in 4 groups of 5. **Two trainers** (lead and co-trainer).
Why 20, not 30: every trainee must teach five times and be seen teaching. With 20 trainees
the micro-teaching arithmetic works (see "Micro-teaching ladder" below). With 25 it does not.

**Hours:** 08:30–16:30, five consecutive days, plus **Day 0** for the trainers.
**Venue:** a room for 20 with wall space for 25 posters, **plus** outdoor space (a tree, a
fence, a patch of ground to dig), **plus** a second teaching space (a second room, a
veranda or a big tree) for the two parallel micro-teaching streams on Days 3–5.

### Why 08:30–16:30 (and not 09:00–16:00 like Course 1)

- Trainees are staff, graduates and lead farmers, usually transported or staying nearby.
  An 8-hour day is normal working time for them.
- Each day holds five things: a teaching skill, a content refresh, a poster studio or teaching
  mode, a micro-teaching round and a feedback circle. Micro-teaching alone needs 110–200
  minutes a day. At 09:00–16:00 something would be cut, and it would be the practice.
- 65 minutes of breaks a day, a 45-minute lunch, and **no new content after 15:50**.
- If transport makes 08:30 impossible, see "Short-day version" at the end of this file.

## What trainees leave with

By the end of Day 5 every trainee can:

1. **Plan** a session in the shape *Look · Try · Talk · Take home*, with an outcome that starts
   with a doing word.
2. **Teach** a Course 1 session for 30 minutes with a partner, keeping teacher talk **at or
   under 30%**, measured by an observer.
3. **Run** a hands-on practical for groups of 5 with named roles, **without** it turning into
   a demonstration.
4. **Ask** questions that climb from closed to open to deeper, and wait for answers.
5. **Make** a hand-drawn teaching poster that follows the poster rules (≤ 25 words, one idea,
   numbered callouts) and **teach** from it with no device.
6. **Teach** the same lesson three ways: **poster only**, **slides** (laptop, TV, projector or
   tablet for a small group) and **with the ImbewuField app** — and switch to paper when a
   device fails.
7. **Explain** the permaculture ethics and principles, the FAO **10 elements of agroecology**,
   and the core practices of Course 1 (land, water, soil, plants, seed, food and money)
   well enough to answer farmers' questions or say "I will find out".
8. **Use** stories, songs, role play, a gallery walk, real objects and improvised teaching aids.
9. **Include** everyone: non-readers, elders, youth, people with a disability, and speakers of
   several languages.
10. **Handle** difficult moments calmly (a dominant talker, a silent group, conflict, a
    learner in distress).
11. **Run** the paperwork: daily register, POPIA consent, session sheet, skills checklist,
    picture quiz, evaluation and attendance certificates.
12. **Give and receive** feedback with *two stars and a wish*.
13. **Plan** their own delivery: kit, Day 0, co-teaching handovers, and a rhythm that does not
    burn them out.

And they take home: every poster they made (5+), their five lesson plans with observer
sheets, a personal teaching plan, the course phone pack, and a certificate of attendance.

## How each day works

Every day blends five strands:

| Strand | What it is | Where in the day |
|---|---|---|
| **A · Teaching skill** | One facilitation skill, taught the way we want them to teach | S1 |
| **B · Content refresh** | A trainer **models** a real Course 1 lesson; then trainees "unpack" how it was taught | S2 |
| **C · Poster studio / teaching mode** | Make posters; practise poster, slide and app modes | S3 |
| **D · Micro-teaching** | Trainees teach each other. It gets longer and harder every day | S4 |
| **E · Feedback & course craft** | *Two stars and a wish*, one piece of course admin, tomorrow's task | SC |

The trainers **teach this course the way trainees must teach Course 1**: posters up before
the session, groups of 5 with rotating roles, ≤ 30% trainer talk, three-question debriefs,
the Question Tree. Trainees are told this on Day 1 and asked to watch for it all week.

### Standard day (Days 2 and 3)

| Time | Block | Min |
|---|---|---|

## The micro-teaching ladder

| Round | Day | Length | Who teaches | To whom | Feedback | Minutes in the room |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | **5 min** | Solo | Own group (4 peers) | 5 min | 5 teachers × 10 = 50, 4 groups in parallel |
| 2 | 2 | **10 min** | Solo | Own group (4 peers) | 6 min | 5 × 16 = 80, 4 groups in parallel |
| 3 | 3 | **15 min** | Pairs | Half-class (8 peers) | 7 min | 5 pairs × 22 = 110, 2 streams in parallel |
| 4 | 4 | **20 min** | Pairs, chosen mode | Half-class (8 peers) | 7 min | 5 pairs × 27 = 135, 2 streams |
| 5 | 5 | **30 min** | Pairs, a real Course 1 session | Half-class (8 peers as farmers) | 10 min | 5 pairs × 40 = 200, 2 streams |

**Why not the whole class on Day 5?** 10 pairs × 40 minutes = 6 h 40 min — the whole day,
with no evaluation or certificates. Two parallel streams of 10 (one trainer each) give every
pair a real audience of 8 and a trainer's eye. Streams swap membership between Day 4 and
Day 5 so every trainee sees 15+ different colleagues teach.

**Three-mode rule:** by the end of Day 5 every trainee has taught at least once from a
**poster only**, once with **slides** (or the paper flip-book twin) and once with the
**app** (or its paper twin). A *Mode tracker* flipchart on the wall is ticked after each round.

Topics come from the [micro-teaching bank](micro-teaching-bank.md): 24 assignments cut from
Course 1 sessions, each with an outcome, a poster, materials and time.

## Three ways to teach (practised in this course)

| Mode | Kit | Practised | Paper twin |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Poster only** (default) | A1 posters, real objects, phone notes or A5 *Teach it* card | Every day; Mode 1 on Day 2 | — it *is* the paper mode |
| **Slides** | Laptop + TV or projector, *or* a tablet passed round a group of 5 | Mode 2 on Day 3; Round 4 | A4 picture **flip-book** made from the same slides |
| **App (ImbewuField)** | Phones / tablets with lessons saved in **My Studies → Study offline**; **Mentor → Training** for the register | Mode 3 on Day 4; Round 4–5 | Printed stills, paper register (forms.md §1), paper feedback |

Switch the app track off for a programme with no devices: every `[APP]` block has a
`[PAPER]` twin of the same length and outcome. Slides carrying `Track: app` are skipped.

## Delivery rhythm (for trainers of this course, and for trainees afterwards)

- **Day 0 is never the same day as Day 1.** Trainers need the full day.
- A trainer pair runs **at most two Teach-the-Teachers courses back to back** (it is more
  tiring than Course 1: four streams to watch, 100+ observer sheets to read). Then a week off.
- Trainees, once qualified to co-facilitate Course 1: **max three courses back to back, then a
  full week at home** (as in the Course 1 README).
- A new facilitator's first Course 1 is as **co-facilitator** beside an experienced lead,
  teaching about half the sessions. Lead on the second or third course.
- Mentoring after the course: each trainee is observed teaching **one real Course 1 session**
  within 8 weeks, with the same observer sheet (micro-teaching-bank.md). Plan it on Day 5.

## Certificates

Attendance certificate for **at least 4 of 5 days** and **all five micro-teaching rounds**
(a missed round can be made up on the spot the next day). It is **not** an accredited
qualification. Accredited trainer or facilitator qualifications go through the QCTO / a SETA
process — check locally if your programme needs one. Wording is in `day-5.md`, T-D5-SC.

## How it connects to the other courses

- **Course 1** is the content trainees will teach. Micro-teaching uses real Course 1 sessions.
- **Course 2 (mentors)** uses the same *two stars and a wish*, the same skills checklist and
  the same **Mentor → Record visit** screen. Good facilitators often become mentors.
- **Courses 4 and 5** use the same lesson shape, poster rules and groups of 5, so a trainee
  who finishes this course can co-facilitate them after reading the day files.

## Short-day version (09:00–15:30)

If transport forces a short day: drop *Course craft 15* into Day 0 reading, cut Round 1 to
groups of 5 without the stretch, and run S2 model lessons at 75 minutes (each day file marks
**✂ cut if short**). Do **not** cut micro-teaching minutes or the feedback after each teach.

## A note on sources

This course draws on well-known adult-learning practice (the experiential learning cycle;
adults learn best from problems they recognise and their own experience), farmer field
schools, farmer-to-farmer (campesino-a-campesino) extension, and FAO's *10 elements of
agroecology* (2018). The session design, posters and wording are ImbewuField's own.
