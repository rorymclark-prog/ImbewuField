## T-D5-S0 · 08:30–09:00 · Opening circle · the side-by-side pots (30 min)

Same shape as T-D2-S0 (register by a trainee, energiser, "you said, we did", sleep on).
**Add (5 min):** groups look at their Day 3 **side-by-side pots** (compost vs no compost).
Beans often take 5–10 days to come up, so there may be little to see yet: **ASK** "What do you
see? If nothing, how could we look without harming it?" *(Gently dig one seed from each pot and
compare the roots.)* **SAY:** "A trial takes a season. Your group takes the pots home and reports
on the WhatsApp group in two weeks." That is how a field school keeps learning after the course.
**Poster:** T-P21 · **Slides:** T-D5-01 · **Trainer talk:** 5 min.

## T-D5-S1 · 09:00–09:40 · Model lesson: food & money with beans and tins — then unpack (40 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **record** money in and money out with beans and a drawn record
book, **explain** the family plate in one sentence, and **name** two ways to teach numbers to
people who don't read.

**Poster:** F-P19, F-P20 · **Slides:** T-D5-02 → T-D5-04 · **Clip:** T-ANI-23 (farm record, slide mode)
**Materials (per group of 5):** 30 dry beans (1 bean = R5); 2 tins marked with a drawn arrow
**in** and **out** (plus the words IN / OUT); 6 event picture cards; 1 A4 sheet ruled as a money
book (date · in · out · what) with pictures in the headings; a plate; 6 vegetables or pictures
of different colours.
**Room:** Room A, groups at tables. The lead teaches.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | MODEL — Look: the plate | 2 min |
| 09:04 | MODEL — Try: beans and tins | 2 min |
| 09:19 | MODEL — Talk & take home | 3 min |
| 09:25 | UNPACK — how do you teach numbers without reading? | 2 min |
| 09:33 | CONTENT — pricing and records questions | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **Look (09:00):** Put a plate on the floor and 6 vegetables. **ASK:** "Fill this plate for a
   child for one day. What's missing?" *(Usually: something orange/yellow, beans, a dark leaf.)*
   **SAY:** "Eat the rainbow — many colours, and beans or eggs, most days." Point to F-P19.
2. **Try (09:04, 14 min):** Each group starts with 10 beans in the **IN** tin (R50 savings).
   Read (or show) 6 event cards, one every 2 minutes:
   ① sold spinach to a neighbour, R40 · ② bought seed, R25 · ③ sold eggs, R30 ·
   ④ bought a hosepipe connector, R15 · ⑤ taxi to the market, R20 · ⑥ sold 3 cabbages, R45.
   Groups move beans (R5 each) and the Recorder draws/writes each line in the money book.
   **ASK** at the end: "How much is in the tin? Did the garden make or lose money this week?"
   *(Start 50 + 40 − 25 + 30 − 15 − 20 + 45 = R105 → 21 beans. Money in R115, out R60,
   R55 more than at the start.)*
3. **Talk & take home (09:19):** F-P20. **ASK:** "Why write it down if you can remember?"
   *(You forget; you see where money leaks; you can price better; family trusts it.)*
   Take home: "Start a money book tonight — even for the house."
4. **Unpack (09:25, 8 min):** **ASK:** "How did I teach sums to people who may not read?"
   *(Beans as money; pictures on cards; tins; the book with picture headings; one event at a
   time; groups check each other.)* **ASK:** "What else could stand for money?" *(Bottle tops,
   stones, pieces of paper.)*
5. **Content (09:33, 7 min):** Questions. Notes below.

### Content refresh notes
- **Pricing:** know your costs (seed, water, transport, your time); check what others charge at
  the same market; price so you cover costs — and sell before it wilts. Don't promise income:
  speak of **saving money on food** and **selling surplus**.
- **Records:** money in / money out; harvest weights; what sold, where, for how much. The
  ImbewuField **Finances** screen (Money in / Money out, "scan a till slip") and **Records**
  hold the same; the paper book is always enough.
- **Stokvels & savings groups:** members save a fixed amount regularly; payouts in turn or at
  year end; rules agreed and written. Course 4 (*Farmer Money*) goes deep.
- **Borrowing:** only from NCR-registered lenders; never give your SASSA card or PIN to anyone.
- **Family plate:** half the plate vegetables and fruit of many colours; some starch (pap,
  bread, potatoes); beans, eggs, fish, chicken or meat for protein.

### Key messages
- Numbers can be taught **with objects**: beans, tins, a picture money book.
- Write it down — the book shows where money leaks.
- Say "save money on food" and "sell surplus" — never promise income.

### If things go wrong
- **Groups argue about the total:** great — they're checking. Count beans together.
- ✂ **Cut if short:** the plate (step 1) to 2 minutes.

### Language & inclusion
- Event cards use pictures plus the Rand amount in big numbers. Anyone can move beans.

## T-D5-S2 · 09:40–14:05 · Final teaching practice: thirty minutes, pairs, a real Course 1 session (200 min in two streams)

**By the end, trainees can:** **teach** a 30-minute Course 1 session as a pair, from a
hand-made A1 poster (plus any mode), with a hands-on practical for groups, teacher talk ≤ 30%,
clean handovers, and a take-home action from every group.

**Poster:** each pair's hand-made poster + the matching F-poster · T-P03, T-P04, T-P07, T-P10, T-P14
**Slides:** T-D5-05 → T-D5-07
**Materials (per stream):** 10 full observer sheets, 5 practical-check cards, 5 trainer
readiness notes (below); timer and time cards (5 min, 2 min, STOP); pairs' kits; devices as booked.
**Streams:** A = Bean + Maize (lead, Room A) · B = Pumpkin + Marula (co-trainer, Stream B).

**Per slot (40 min):** 30 teach + 10 feedback. The 8 other trainees are **Course 1 farmers** —
two groups of 4 for the practical. Two are observers (the next pair).

| Time | Slot | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 09:40 | Slot 1 (30 + 10) | 1 min |
| 10:20 | Slot 2 | 1 min |
| 11:00 | Tea | |
| 11:20 | Slot 3 | 1 min |
| 12:00 | Slot 4 | 1 min |
| 12:40 | Lunch | |
| 13:25 | Slot 5 | 1 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **SAY (09:40, 1 min):** "Thirty minutes. You are running a real Course 1 session. We are your
   farmers. We'll behave like farmers — including questions you don't expect."
2. **Farmers:** trainers brief two "farmers" per slot, secretly, to ask one real question each
   (e.g. "My grandfather said never plant when the moon is ___ — is that true?") so the pair
   practises bounce-back and "I'll find out".
3. **Feedback (10 min):**
   - Pair: each partner stars the other; then one wish for themselves (2 min).
   - Observer 1: ⭐⭐🌱 + talk %, questions by rung, *Look · Try · Talk · Take home* (3 min).
   - Observer 2: practical check, handovers, the poster (6-metre check) (2 min).
   - Trainer: **two stars and a wish** (2 min). Pair: "Thank you — in my first course I will…" (1 min).
4. **Trainer readiness note (private, not shared in the room):** for each trainee, circle one:
   **Ready to co-facilitate** · **Ready with a mentor beside them** · **Needs another practice
   first** — plus one sentence of evidence from the observer sheets across the week. Used in
   T-D5-S4 conversations and passed to the programme office (with the trainee's knowledge).
5. **After slot 5 (14:05):** everyone ticks the Mode tracker; pairs write their final talk % on
   the scoreboard.

### Key messages
- A real session: **register-style start, poster, practical in groups, debrief, take home**.
- 30 minutes can hold *Look · Try · Talk · Take home* — if you trust the Try.

### If things go wrong
- **A trainee is absent for their slot:** partner teaches solo with a trainer as co-facilitator;
  the absent partner teaches first thing on a follow-up day (or during S3, shortened) to meet
  the certificate rule.
- **A pair runs over:** STOP at 30:00. The next pair's slot is protected.
- **Rain spoils an outdoor practical:** tray / table version indoors; carry on.

### Language & inclusion
- Pairs teach in the language they'll use with their farmers; the observer who doesn't share
  it watches hands, faces and the tally.
- ✂ **Never cut** the final practice.

## T-D5-S3 · 14:05–14:55 · Did they learn it? Checking learning & the paperwork (50 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **run** the Course 1 picture quiz without reading, **record** skills
on the ✔ ◐ ✘ checklist during a practical, **run** the learner evaluation, and **name** every
form in the flip folder and when it's used.

**Poster:** T-P15 · **Slides:** T-D5-08 → T-D5-11
**Materials:** 8 picture-quiz cards (F-IMG-30 → F-IMG-37, or hand-drawn), corner signs A, B, C;
20 copies each of forms.md §3, §4, §5, §6; 1 flip folder (sample, fully organised); stickers.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 14:05 | Look — the picture quiz, run on trainees | 3 min |
| 14:13 | Talk — T-P15: three ways to check learning | 4 min |
| 14:20 | Try — skills checklist from Round 5 | 1 min |
| 14:30 | Try — groups practise the evaluation | 1 min |
| 14:40 | Try — build the flip folder | 3 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **Look (14:05):** Lead runs 4 of the 8 picture-quiz questions (forms.md §5). Hold up the card,
   say the question, point to corners A, B, C; trainees **walk** to their answer. Count heads.
   **ASK:** "Did anyone need to read?" *(No.)* **SAY:** "Class totals only, Day 1 and Day 5."
2. **Talk (14:13):** Reveal **T-P15**. Point ①–③:
   ① **Watch them do it** — the skills checklist (✔ did it alone · ◐ with help · ✘ not yet).
   ② **Picture quiz** — start and end of the course; class totals.
   ③ **Ask them** — evaluation with fingers 1–5 and sticker lists.
   **SAY:** "The best proof is ①: a farmer doing the skill. And it's a record of what they can
   **do** — not a qualification."
3. **Try (14:20, 10 min):** In the Round 5 streams' groups: each trainee fills the skills-checklist
   row for the two "farmers" they watched most in Round 5 (the relevant skill from the pair's
   session), then compares with a neighbour. **ASK:** "When during a practical do you tick it?"
   *(While walking — hands behind your back — not afterwards from memory; the co-facilitator
   ticks while the lead teaches.)*
4. **Try (14:30, 10 min):** Groups practise the learner evaluation (forms.md §6) on each other —
   fingers 1–5 for two questions; the two sticker lists. **ASK:** "What is the gap between
   'understand best' and 'want to learn more' for?" *(It's the plan for mentor visits.)*
5. **Try (14:40, 15 min):** Build the **flip folder**. Groups put a pile of forms into the right
   order and say when each is used:

   | Form | When |
   |---|---|
   | Register (§1) | Every day, AM and PM |
   | POPIA consent (§2) | Day 1, before any photo or data |
   | Session sheet (§3) | Every session — the co-facilitator |
   | Skills checklist (§4) | During practicals |
   | Picture quiz (§5) | Day 1 start and Day 5 end |
   | Learner evaluation (§6) | Day 5, last session |
   | Facilitator daily report (§7) | End of each day, with the register photo |
   | Certificates (§8) | Day 5 — registered + 10% |

   **[APP]:** the same records sit in **Mentor → Training** (attendance, signatures, photos,
   feedback) and **Mentor → Learning** (module progress). **[PAPER]:** the flip folder is enough.

### Key messages
- Check learning by **watching them do it**, a **picture quiz**, and **asking them**.
- Class totals, not individual scores. It's a record, not a qualification.
- The flip folder, in order, every course.

### If things go wrong
- **Short of time after Round 5:** skip step 4; the SC evaluation is itself the practice.

### Language & inclusion
- Walking to corners replaces reading; elders may point from their seat.

## T-D5-S4 · 14:55–15:35 · My teaching plan · co-teaching · keep the teacher strong (40 min)

**By the end, trainees can:** **plan** their first Course 1 (with whom, which sessions they lead,
their Day 0), **name** their two strengths and one growth area with evidence, and **choose**
three habits that keep them strong through a season of courses.

**Poster:** T-P18, T-P14, T-P16 · **Slides:** T-D5-12 → T-D5-14
**Materials:** personal teaching plan (A4, template below) ×20; each trainee's observer sheets
from the week; the Talk scoreboard.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 14:55 | Look — the Talk scoreboard, Round 2 → Round 5 | 2 min |
| 14:59 | Talk — T-P18: keep the teacher strong | 5 min |
| 15:06 | Try — personal teaching plan | 1 min |
| 15:26 | Pairs share; trainers' readiness conversations | 1 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **Look (14:55):** **ASK:** "Look at the scoreboard. What happened to our talk % from Round 2
   to Round 5?" *(It dropped for most.)* Applause.
2. **Talk (14:59):** Reveal **T-P18**. Point ①–⑥:
   ① **Rhythm:** at most **three courses back to back**, then a full week at home.
   ② **Day 0 is sacred** — never teach Day 1 without it.
   ③ **Voice:** use the signal, not shouting; water all day; face the group, not the wind.
   ④ **Body:** hat, water, sit when learners work, heavy work before 11:00.
   ⑤ **A debrief buddy:** 10 minutes with your co-facilitator every evening — two stars and a
   wish for each other.
   ⑥ **Boundaries:** you're not a counsellor, a bank or a transport service. Listen, refer,
   follow up with the programme office.
   **ASK:** "What drains you most in a course week?" Take 3. "What will you do about it?"
3. **Try (15:06, 20 min):** Everyone fills the **personal teaching plan** (below), using their
   observer sheets. Trainers call each trainee over for a **2-minute private readiness talk**
   (one star, one wish, and the readiness note — agreed together).
4. **Pairs (15:26, 9 min):** Share one strength, one growth area and one self-care habit with
   your co-teaching partner.

**Personal teaching plan (A4):**

### Key messages
- Plan your first course **beside** an experienced lead.
- Be observed **once more within 8 weeks**, with the same observer sheet.
- Keep the teacher strong: **rhythm, Day 0, voice, body, buddy, boundaries.**

### If things go wrong
- **A trainee is "needs another practice":** say it kindly and specifically; agree a date to
  teach one session with a mentor observing. It's a next step, not a failure.

### Language & inclusion

## T-D5-SC · 15:35–16:30 · Evaluation, final stars & certificate ceremony (55 min)

**By the end:** trainees have evaluated the course, starred their partner, and received their
certificates.

**Poster:** T-P01, T-P10 · **Slides:** T-D5-15 → T-D5-16
**Materials:** evaluation sheet (below) on a flipchart + stickers; 22 certificates (filled);
camera/phone (check green dots); a song chosen by trainees.

| Time | Step | Trainer talk |
|---|---|---|
| 15:35 | Course evaluation | 3 min |
| 15:50 | Final stars — the partner circle | 1 min |
| 16:00 | Certificate ceremony | 6 min |
| 16:20 | Closing circle: one word, song, register PM | 2 min |

### Say / ask / do
1. **Evaluation (15:35, 15 min):** Same method as forms.md §6, with this course's questions.
   Fingers 1–5, counted by a trainee:
   1. The language used was easy to understand.
   2. I taught five times and got useful feedback each time.
   3. There was too much to learn. *(a high score is a warning)*
   4. I feel ready to teach Course 1 beside an experienced lead.
   5. I can teach with posters only.
   6. I can teach with slides or the app — and switch to paper.
   Sticker lists: **"I'm most confident teaching…"** / **"I want more practice with…"** (2 each).
   Groups answer on a flipchart: most useful · least useful · how to make it better.
2. **Final stars (15:50, 10 min):** Standing circle. Each trainee gives their co-teaching partner
   **one star** from the week — specific. Right to pass. No wishes today.
3. **Ceremony (16:00, 20 min):** Lead reads each name clearly; co-trainer hands the certificate;
   handshake (or the greeting the person prefers); photo only for those without a green dot.
   **SAY** (before the first name): "This certificate says you attended and taught five times.
   It is not an accredited qualification. Your real certificate is the first farmer you teach."
4. **Closing (16:20, 10 min):** One word each. The trainees' song. Register **PM**. Crates counted.
   Posters come down **together** — trainees take home the ones they made.

**Certificate wording:**

> **Certificate of Attendance**
> This certifies that **[name]** attended the 5-day course
> **Teach the Teachers — permaculture & agroecology facilitation**
> at **[venue]**, **[dates]**, and completed five micro-teaching practices, teaching with
> posters, slides and the ImbewuField app.
> Trainers: ________ ________ Organisation: ________ [logo] [funder logo]
> *Attendance of at least 4 of 5 days and all five micro-teaching rounds. This is not an
> accredited qualification.*

### Key messages
- Thank each other by name.
- The certificate records attendance and practice — the real test is the first course.
