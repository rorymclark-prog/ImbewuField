# Day 0 · Preparation day (trainers only)

**Who:** the lead trainer and the co-trainer. Nobody else.
**When:** the working day before Day 1 — **never** the morning of Day 1.
**Goal:** at 16:30 today the venue could start Day 1 at 08:30 with no power, no signal and
no printer.

## 08:30 · Walk the venue (60 min)

Find and mark (masking tape + a marker on a card):

| Space | What you need | Used |
|---|---|---|
| **Room A** | 20 chairs in a circle, no tables in the middle; 25 m of wall for posters | All days |
| **Stream B space** | A second room, veranda or big tree 20 m+ from Room A, seats for 10 | Days 3, 4, 5 |
| **Tree-talk spot** | Shade for 20, space for a circle, a trunk or fence to hang an A1 | Day 2 model lesson |
| **Sun spot** | Open ground, full sun, where you can see north | Day 2 |
| **Dig patch** | 3 m × 5 m of ground you are allowed to dig (ask the owner!) | Days 2, 3 |
| **Gallery walls** | 6 stations, 3 m apart (walls, trees or fences) | Day 4 |
| **Poster studio** | A clean floor or 4 tables, near light, away from the door | Every day |
| **Water point** | Tap or 2 × 20 ℓ drums filled | Days 2, 3 |

- Find **north** with a phone compass or the sun (at noon the sun is in the north). Put a
  big card "N" on the north wall of Room A. Trainees will use it all week.
- Check toilets, handwashing water, soap. Find the nearest clinic and write the route and
  number on the inside cover of the flip folder. Who has a car in an emergency?
- Check power: where are the plugs? Does it go off (load-shedding / outages)? Note the times.
- Check light: can you darken one wall for a projector? (Most halls: no. Plan TV or tablets.)

## 09:30 · Kit count (co-trainer, 60 min)

Use the checklist in `resources.md`. Tick every line. Anything missing: buy it today, or
write the substitute on the checklist now. **Count per group of 5** (4 groups + 1 spare set).

Pack four **group crates** (a 20 ℓ bucket or a crate each), labelled with the group's plant
name. Each holds: role cards, 1 flipchart marker set, Prestik, masking tape, scissors, glue
stick, 1 clipboard, 5 pencils, 5 observer sheets per day.

## 09:30 · Poster wall (lead, 60 min)

1. Hang **only Day 1 posters** now: T-P01 (blank for the agreement — only the headline and art),
   T-P02, T-P03, T-P04, T-P06, T-P09, T-P10, T-P11, T-P19, T-P20. Also F-P02, F-P03, F-P04.
2. Hang them at the **eye level of a seated person**, left to right in teaching order.
3. Hang a blank flipchart sheet beside each poster for trainees' own words.
4. Make the **Question Tree**: draw a big tree on a flipchart, hang it near the door, with a
   pad of sticky notes and 3 markers on a string.
5. Make the **Mode tracker**: a flipchart grid, 20 names down the side, three columns
   (Poster · Slides/flip-book · App/paper twin). Ticks go in after each round.
6. Make the **Teacher-talk scoreboard**: 5 rounds across, names down. Trainees write their own
   measured % after each round. (Only trainees fill it in. It is for growth, not ranking.)
7. Keep Days 2–5 posters rolled in order in the poster tube, labelled with the day.

## 14:30 · Groups of 5 (both, 60 min)

Use the trainee list (names, roles, languages, experience). Make **4 groups of 5**. Mix:

- permaculture-trained with farming-experienced;
- young graduates with lead farmers and elders;
- men and women;
- at least one strong speaker of each main language in every group, where you can;
- colleagues who work together **apart** (they will co-teach later, but learn apart first).

Name the groups **Bean, Pumpkin, Maize, Marula** (Course 1 names, so trainees get used to them).
Write the groups on a flipchart, but **do not hang it** — you hand out group cards on Day 1.

**Streams for Days 3–5:** Stream A = Bean + Pumpkin, Stream B = Maize + Marula for Days 3–4.
Day 5: swap half — Stream A = Bean + Maize, Stream B = Pumpkin + Marula. Pairs are made
**inside** groups on Day 2 at the close (trainees choose, trainers check the mix).

### If things go wrong on Day 0

| Problem | Do this |
|---|---|
| Kit missing | Substitute from `resources.md`; never plan a demo-only practical because of it |
| No second teaching space | Use the tree; or split Room A with a line of chairs and posters back to back |
| No power at all | Poster mode all week. Day 3 slide mode uses the **paper flip-book** twin; Day 4 app mode runs `[PAPER]` |
| Fewer than 16 trainees | 3 groups (some of 4). Keep 2 streams on Days 3–5, with 3–4 pairs each; spare time goes to longer feedback and a re-teach |
| More than 20 arrive on Day 1 | Accept max 20. Take names for the next course. Explain why: "you each need to teach 5 times" |
