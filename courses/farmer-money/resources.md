## 3. Play money — print spec

**Why play money:** learners *move* money instead of listening to sums. It is also the calculator
for learners who do not read numbers well.

| Piece | Size | Colour code (band) | Art in the oval | Per group set | Value |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R100 note | 120 × 60 mm | Forest | maize cob | 20 | R2 000 |
| R50 note | 120 × 60 mm | Ink, dotted | cabbage | 30 | R1 500 |
| R20 note | 120 × 60 mm | Ochre | spinach leaf | 40 | R800 |
| R10 note | 120 × 60 mm | Leaf | bean pod | 40 | R400 |
| R5 coin | 30 mm circle | Ochre | seed pattern | 40 | R200 |
| R2 coin | 30 mm circle | Mid-green | seed pattern | 40 | R80 |
| R1 coin | 30 mm circle | Ink outline | seed pattern | 20 | R20 |
| **Total** | | | | **230 pieces** | **R5 000** |

- Artwork: R-IMG-03. Every note and coin carries **"PLAY MONEY — NOT LEGAL TENDER"** (typeset),
  is **smaller than a real note**, and does **not** copy any real South African banknote or coin
  design (the law protects currency designs — never photocopy real money).
- Print notes 8 per A4 sheet on 160 gsm card; coins 48 per A4 sheet. One set = 17 note sheets +
  3 coin sheets = **20 A4 sheets**. Seven sets (6 groups + bank) = **140 sheets**.
- Cheapest: print in black on coloured card (green, yellow, blue, white) — the colour code still
  works. Cut with a guillotine at the print shop (ask; usually R20–R50 a job).
- Coins can be replaced by **bottle tops marked with a paint pen** (R1 plain, R2 one dot, R5 two
  dots).
- Store each set in a zip bag with a count card. Count back in at every closing circle.

## 5. What learners bring

Ask on the invitation (read it aloud when inviting):
- **Three empty tins with lids** (coffee, milk powder, jam) — or we give envelopes.
- A pen.
- A phone if they have one (app ON), charged.
- Their Course 1 record book if they have it.
- **Nothing about their own money.** Say this on the invitation: *"You will not be asked about
  your own money."*

## 10. Background reading for facilitators

- The app's **Studies → Finance** course (8 units) — same words, deeper. Units 1–6 match this
  course; read unit 3 (costs and prices) and unit 6 (borrowing and reserves) before Day 2 and 3.
- **SASSA** (www.sassa.gov.za) — current grant amounts and pay dates; card safety.
- **National Credit Regulator** (www.ncr.org.za) — how to check a lender; what lenders may not do;
  debt counselling.
- **Corporation for Deposit Insurance (CODI)** — bank deposits protected up to R100 000 per
  person per bank.
- **NASASA** (National Stokvel Association of South Africa) — how stokvels organise and keep records.
- **Savings groups (VSLA) method** — CARE and other NGOs publish free field guides on village
  savings and loan associations: three-lock box, share purchase, social fund, share-out.
- Course 1 day files (F-P20, Day 5 S3) — the starter lesson this course builds on.
