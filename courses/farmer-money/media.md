# Media · Farmer Money

Every image, animation and photo the slides and posters use. `REUSE` items already exist in the
repo; Codex makes only the `NEW` ones. Target path for NEW items (set by the build script):
`public/course-media/farmer-money/<ID>.<jpg|mp4>`.

**Rules for all NEW art** (from `shared/poster-standards.md` and the common brief):
flat, warm, instructional illustration with thick dark outlines · palette Forest #1F4D2B,
Mid-green #2E6B3A, Leaf #A8D88A, Ochre #C07A1E, Water #235E86 (water only), Paper #F7F2E9
background, Ink #20190F · **no text, letters or numbers inside the artwork** · southern
hemisphere (sun in the north) · generic plant shapes (leafy greens may be drawn as generic
Swiss-chard-like bunches) · people look like Black South African smallholders of mixed ages
and genders, work clothes, headscarves, caps, gumboots.

**Money-specific rules:** money is drawn as **generic** notes (plain rectangles with an oval and
a colour band) and plain coins — never real South African banknote or coin designs, no faces,
no animals, no SARB marks. No bank, lender, network or shop brands or logos. Lenders are never
drawn by nationality or ethnicity (hands only, or neutral silhouettes). Families are drawn with
dignity.

Animations: silent, 8–30 s, loopable, ≤ 3 MB, 1280 × 720, with a still poster frame `.jpg`.
Stills for slides: 1920 × 1080 unless stated. Card sheets: A4 portrait at 300 dpi, panels on a
cutting grid.

Count: **NEW 37** (17 poster art, 11 images and card sheets, 6 animations, 3 photos) ·
**REUSE 16** (5 lesson images, 8 app screenshots, 3 animations). See the summary at the end.

## R-IMG-01 · The three families
- Type: image
- Status: NEW
- Used in: R-D1-01, R-D1-02, R-D1-04, R-D1-16, R-D3-20
- Length: still

**Brief:** Three side-by-side scenes, equal width, one per family, same style, a thin Hairline
divider between them. **Left — the Dlamini household (rural KZN):** a rondavel and a small
block house on a green hillside; a 2 500 ℓ plastic water tank beside the house; trench beds with
leafy greens, cabbages and a row of maize. A grandmother (70s, headscarf, cardigan) seated on a
chair; her daughter (40s) standing with a hoe; a young man (early 20s) with a phone in his
pocket; two children (8 and 12) carrying a watering can. **Middle — the Mokoena household
(peri-urban Free State):** a small RDP-style house with a fence of droppers and wire, flat dry
grassland beyond; three raised beds with greens and onions, a small plot of green maize. A woman
(mid-30s, braids) tying a bunch of greens; a boy (11) and a girl (6) at her side. **Right —
Sizanani Youth Garden (Mpumalanga):** a church building with a corrugated-iron roof; beside it a
plot with seedling trays on a bench under shade-cloth and greens in rows; three young people
(two men, one woman, 21–27) carrying buckets of water. Sun high in the north (shadows fall to the
south). No text.
