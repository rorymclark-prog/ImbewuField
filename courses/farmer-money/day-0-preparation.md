cutting and bagging take most of the day. Do not leave it for the morning of Day 1.

## 1. Read-through (08:00–08:30)

- Both facilitators read the **three case-family cards** aloud to each other. Learn the names.
  You will use them all course. Decide the local language names for "money in", "money out",
  "money owed", "balance" and "break-even" and write them on a card.
- Read the **Protecting learners** table in the README. Agree what you will each do if a learner
  becomes upset, discloses debt trouble, or discloses abuse over money (see *If things go wrong*
  in each day file).
- Share out the sessions. The facilitator with the strongest numeracy leads R-D2-S3 and
  R-D3-S3. The facilitator with the strongest local language leads R-D1-S3 (who decides).

## 3. Pack the group bags (10:30–11:30)

One strong bag or 20 ℓ bucket per group, labelled with the group's plant name
(*Bean, Pumpkin, Maize, Banana, Marula, Spinach*). Inside:

- Play money set (in a zip bag, with a count card: "R5 000 — 20 × R100, 30 × R50, 40 × R20,
  40 × R10, 40 × R5 coins, 40 × R2, 20 × R1").
- **Counters:** 200 dry beans (or bottle tops) in a tin with a lid.
- 3 empty tins (or 3 envelopes) with blank labels — the **three tins**.
- All card sets for the group (see above).
- 1 flipchart sheet, 4 thick markers (black, green, orange, blue), prestik.
- 1 calculator (cheap solar is fine) — used **only to check**, never to start a sum.
- 1 kitchen scale (5 kg) *or* share 2 between 6 groups for Day 2.
- 10 rubber bands for bunches (Day 2).
- Group role card (Leader · Timekeeper · Materials · Recorder · Reporter).

The **facilitator box**: bank play-money set, spare counters, A0/A1 posters rolled, prestik,
cable ties, sticky notes, stickers (green dots for "no photos", coloured dots for evaluation),
scissors, stapler, the flip folder with all forms, first aid kit, a bell or whistle.

**Count everything and write the count on the bag.** The count is checked at every closing
circle ("money back").

## 4. Rehearse the sums (10:30–11:30, lead facilitator)

Work every worked example in the day files **with beans on a table**, not on a calculator.
If you cannot show it with beans, you cannot teach it. Check especially:

- Lerato's week (R-D2-S1): opening R150 → closing R315; sales R590, cash R390, owed R200.
- Spinach bed (R-D2-S2/S3): cash costs R510; 150 bunches; cost of one bunch R3.40;
  sales R1 080; cash profit R570; food eaten worth R480.
- Break-even (R-D2-S3): R600 ÷ R10 = 60 bunches.
- Loan offers (R-D3-S3): total cost R1 500 / R840 / R450 / R0.

## 5. Set up the venue (11:30–12:30)

- **Tables in 6 islands**, 5 chairs each, space to walk between. Money work needs flat
  surfaces. If there are no tables: 6 plastic sheets or reed mats on the floor, with chairs
  around for elders who cannot sit on the floor.
- **Poster wall:** Day 1 posters (R-P01 → R-P07) up at seated eye level, left to right, each
  with a blank flipchart sheet beside it. Day 2 and 3 posters stay rolled until their day.
- **R-P13 (A0 season calendar)** needs a 1.5 m wall space or a fence panel — find it now.
- **Question Tree** on its own wall or tree.
- **Market-day corner** (Day 3): 3 tables that can become "shops" (spaza, creche, hawker).
- **Garden bed** for Day 2 S2: agree with the host which bed (spinach or any leafy crop) groups
  may harvest a little from, and that the harvest goes to the host or the lunch pot.
  No bed? Buy 6 bunches of spinach and 2 kg of any vegetable on the morning of Day 2.
- Safe place to lock the play money and scales overnight.

## 7. App check (13:00–14:00, app ON only)

Skip this if the programme runs app OFF.

- One smartphone per group with ImbewuField installed, signed in, POPIA consent done in
  onboarding, language set. Use facilitator or programme phones if learners have none.
- On each phone, open **My Records**: check the tabs **Picked · Sold · Spent · Charts** show.
- On one phone, tap *Load sample data* (*Try the Ubhejane money book*) so you can show a full
  book. **Do not load sample data on a learner's own account.**
- Test **Spent → Receipt photo → Take photo → Read a receipt with Lima** with a real till slip.
  Note: it needs data/Wi-Fi to read the slip; the photo itself saves offline.
- Test the **Invoice builder**: make one invoice, *Share PDF*, then delete it.
- Test offline: switch on flight mode, add a cost, switch off, check it syncs.
- Charge every phone and a power bank. Buy data bundles if the venue has no Wi-Fi.

If anything fails, **switch that session to [PAPER]**. Never spend teaching time fixing phones.

## 8. Rehearse three hard sessions (14:00–15:00)

Each facilitator teaches the other for 15 minutes, with beans and play money:

1. **R-D1-S2 the money map** — practise saying "this is the Mokoena family, not you".
2. **R-D2-S3 break-even with beans** — the co-facilitator plays a confused learner.
3. **R-D3-S3 borrowing** — practise the "spot the illegal lender" signs without naming any
   company or person.

Two stars and a wish after each (facilitation-toolkit §8).

## 9. Safety and referral (15:00–15:30)

Write these on the inside cover of the flip folder, with **local** names and numbers filled in:

| Need | Where to send the learner |
|---|---|
| Grant problem, lost/stolen SASSA card, strange deductions from a grant | SASSA toll-free 0800 60 10 11 · the local SASSA office: ______ |
| Lender keeping ID or card, reckless lending, complaints | National Credit Regulator 0860 627 627 · www.ncr.org.za |
| Too much debt | An NCR-registered debt counsellor (find on the NCR website) · local advice office: ______ |
| Bank card fraud, money taken from account | Phone the bank's fraud line on the back of the card **at once**, then report at SAPS |
| Violence or threats over money in the home | GBV Command Centre 0800 428 428 · local social worker: ______ · SAPS 10111 |
| Advice office / paralegal nearby | ______ |

Check each number is still correct before the course (numbers change).
