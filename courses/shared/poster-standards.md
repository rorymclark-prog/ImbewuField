# Poster standards (all three courses)

Posters are the main teaching tool. A facilitator with a set of posters, a phone and the
practical kit can run every course with no power at all.

## Sizes and printing

| Use | Size | Paper | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teaching wall (default) | **A1 portrait** 594 × 841 mm | 200 gsm matt, or PVC banner for field kits | Readable from 6 m |
| Big diagrams (maps, cycles, calendars) | **A0 landscape** 1189 × 841 mm | Banner / flex | Max 3 per course |
| Back-of-poster card | **A5** | 300 gsm, laminated | Clipped to the poster's lower corner or kept in the flip folder |
| Learner handout version | A4 | 80 gsm, black & white must still work | Optional |

- Field kit posters: print on **PVC flex banner with eyelets** in the four corners (survives
  rain, rolls into a tube, hangs on a fence with cable ties). Paper posters for indoor venues.
- Budget guide (2026, SA print shops): A1 paper full colour R90–R150; A1 PVC banner with eyelets
  R180–R280; A0 banner R350–R500. Laminated A5 cards about R12 each.
- Every poster is also exported as a phone image (1080 × 1528 px, ≤ 400 KB) for the phone pack.

## Layout grid (A1 portrait)

```
┌──────────────────────────────┐
│ ID · course · day   (small)  │  6%  top strip — Forest #1F4D2B, poster ID in Leaf
│ HEADLINE (≤ 6 words)         │ 12%  Newsreader Bold, 120–160 pt, Ink on Paper
├──────────────────────────────┤
│                              │
│   ART (text-free)            │ 60%  one idea, big, thick outlines
│   with numbered callouts ①②③ │
│                              │
├──────────────────────────────┤
│ ① label  ② label  ③ label    │ 18%  Public Sans Bold 48–64 pt, ≤ 4 words each
├──────────────────────────────┤
│ one question to ask the room │  4%  italic, Ochre
└──────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Max 25 words** on the whole poster (headline + labels + question).
- Numbered callouts (①②③) link art to labels — they work when the labels are translated.
- Minimum text size: 40 pt (readable at 6 m). Headline ≥ 120 pt.
- Leave a 30 mm margin; eyelets sit 15 mm from each corner.

## Colour (ImbewuField palette)

| Name | Hex | Use on posters |
|---|---|---|
| Forest | `#1F4D2B` | Top strip, headline on light, outlines of plants |
| Mid-green | `#2E6B3A` | Plants, secondary shapes |
| Leaf | `#A8D88A` | Highlights, living things |
| Ochre | `#C07A1E` | Callout circles, the "ask the room" line, soil |
| Water | `#235E86` | **Water only** |
| Paper | `#F7F2E9` | Background |
| Ink | `#20190F` | Body text |
| Hairline | `#E2D8C4` | Dividers |

Posters must still read when photocopied in black and white: never rely on colour alone to
separate two things (use pattern or a label as well).

## Art rules (same as the app's course images)

1. **Southern hemisphere.** Sun in the **north**, shadows fall **south**. Any compass shows N up.
2. **No text inside the artwork.** Words are typeset on the layout so they can be translated.
3. **Generic plant shapes** unless the poster is *about* telling crops apart (then the art brief
   names the crop and it is drawn accurately). Never draw a recognisable invasive species.
4. **People look like the learners:** Black South African smallholders, mixed ages and genders,
   gogos and youth, work clothes, gumboots, headscarves, caps. Nobody in a lab coat.
5. **Flat, warm, instructional illustration.** Thick dark outlines. Not photoreal, not 3D.
6. **One idea per poster.** If you need two ideas, it is two posters.
7. Show **local materials**: 20 ℓ drums, 2 ℓ bottles, tins, wire, droppers, kraal manure, grass
   mulch, a corrugated-iron roof, a JoJo-style tank, fencing with droppers.

## Translation

Every poster ships as an editable layout (Figma/Canva/SVG or PowerPoint master) with all words
in text boxes. Order of translation for ACT·SEF sites: **isiZulu, then Sesotho, siSwati,
Xitsonga, Sepedi, isiXhosa** as programmes need. A translation must be read back by a first-
language speaker who farms before it is printed.

## The back-of-poster card (A5)

Each poster has a "Teach it" card: 5–8 numbered prompts that let a facilitator teach the poster
without a phone. It is written in `posters.md` under **Teach it**. Format:

```
F-P06  Build an A-frame                     (≈ 15 min + practical)
1. ASK: How do we know water will stay in a ditch and not run away?
2. POINT ①②: two legs, one crossbar, one string with a stone
3. …
KEY: A level line is where water stops and soaks.
```

## Poster wall habits (teach these in Course 3)

- Posters go up **before** the session and stay up for the whole course: by Day 5 the room is
  a map of everything learned.
- Hang them at eye level of a seated learner, in the order they were taught, left to right.
- Leave one blank sheet of flipchart beside each poster for the group's own words and drawings.
- The **Question Tree** (a flipchart sheet on a tree or wall): any question that doesn't fit
  the session is written there and answered before the day ends.
