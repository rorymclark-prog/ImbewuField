# Forms (print these; the app can hold the same records)

Print on A4. Keep in the flip folder. Each form lists the app screen that holds the same
record, for programmes using ImbewuField — but **the paper form is always enough.**

---

| Course: | Venue: | Date: | Day _ of _ | Facilitators: |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| # | Full name | ID / participant no. | M/F/Other | Age group (18–35 / 36–59 / 60+) | Phone | Signature AM | Signature PM |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | | |

- 30 rows per page. Signature AM at the opening circle, PM at the closing circle.
- Facilitator signs and dates the bottom of every page: "I confirm this register is correct."
- Photograph each page at the end of the day (phone) as a backup.

> follow-up support. We will only share them with **[organisation]** and the funder
> **[funder]** for reporting. We will not sell them.
> We may take photographs of the course. Tick one:
> ☐ Yes, you may use photos of me in reports and training materials
> ☐ Yes, but only in reports (not public)
> ☐ No photos of me, please
> You may change your mind at any time — tell the facilitator.
>
> Name: ____________ Signature / mark: ________ Date: ______

Read it aloud in the local language. A thumbprint or mark is a valid signature. Learners who
tick "No photos" get a small green sticker on their name tag so photographers can see.

## 3. Session sheet (co-facilitator fills in, every session) · *paper only*

| Session ID | Planned time | Actual start–end | Teacher talk minutes (tally) | Everyone did the practical? (Y / some / demo only) | What to change next time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Tally teacher talk with a mark every minute the facilitator is talking to the whole room.
Target ≤ 30% of the session. "Demo only" must be explained in the last column.

| Learner | Build & calibrate A-frame | Mark a contour & dig swale | Calculate roof water | Build compost heap | Make liquid manure | Sow a seedling tray | Transplant to spacing | Make natural pest spray | Save & label seed | Fill money in/out book |

This is the record of what learners can **do** — it is not a certificate of competence.

## 5. Picture quiz — start (Day 1) and end (Day 5) · Course 1

Eight picture cards (see `farmer-5day/media.md`, F-IMG-70 to F-IMG-77). The facilitator holds up
each card; learners point to or stand beside the answer (A / B / C corners of the room) — no
reading needed. Count hands per answer. Record the class totals, not individual scores.

| # | Question (said aloud) | Answers (pictures) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where does the sun sit at midday in winter? | North · South · Straight up |
| 2 | Which garden keeps water longer? | Bare soil · Mulched soil · Sand |
| 3 | Which heap will get hot? | Only grass · Grass + manure + water · Only manure, dry |
| 4 | Where do you dig a swale? | Down the slope · Along the level line · Anywhere |
| 5 | Which seed can you save and replant true? | Open-pollinated · Hybrid (F1) · Treated seed |
| 6 | What is the first step against pests? | Spray poison · Healthy soil & mixed plants · Pull up the crop |
| 7 | How far apart for cabbage? | A hand · Half a metre · Two metres |
| 8 | Money from selling spinach goes… | Straight to spending · Written in the book first · Nowhere |

## 6. Learner evaluation (Day 5, last session)

Read aloud; learners answer by showing fingers (1 = not at all … 5 = very much) or with
stickers on a large printed sheet. Collect per question as class totals.

1. The language used was easy to understand.
2. The course was well organised.
3. There was too much to learn. *(a high score here is a warning, not praise)*
4. What we learned is useful for my garden.
5. The facilitators knew their subject and were prepared.
6. I did the practicals with my own hands (not just watched).

Then two lists on a flipchart, each learner places 2 stickers on each:
- **Which topics do you now understand best?**
- **Which topics do you want to learn more about?**

The gap between the two lists is the plan for mentor visits.

Three open questions (answered in groups, Recorder draws or writes):
- What was most useful?
- What was least useful?
- How can we make this course better?

## 7. Facilitator daily report (5 minutes, end of day) · *App: Mentor → Training feedback*

- Date, venue, day, number present (from register)
- Sessions run as planned? Which were changed and why?
- Practicals: all hands-on? (Y/N per practical)
- Materials used up / broken / missing
- Photos taken (with consent) — how many, stored where
- One learner quote (with permission)
- What I need from the programme office

Send the photo of the register + this report on the programme WhatsApp group each day.

## 8. Certificate (Day 5) · print registered learners + 10%

> **Certificate of Attendance**
> This certifies that **[name]** attended the 5-day course
> **Grow Food, Grow Strong — agroecological food gardening**
> at **[venue]**, **[dates]**, and took part in practical work on water harvesting, living
> soil, seedlings, natural pest management, seed saving and garden records.
> Facilitators: ________ ________ Organisation: ________ [logo] [funder logo]

Attendance certificates are given for attending **at least 4 of 5 days**. They are **not** a
qualification and not a statement of accredited competence. The ceremony matters: hand each
certificate personally, say the name clearly, shake hands, take a photo (with consent).
