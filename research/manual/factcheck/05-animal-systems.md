## Could not verify / softened / left out

- **Beekeeper registration renewal period** — sources conflict (every 24 months vs annually). Text says registration is compulsory and free and must be renewed when it expires. The official control-measures PDF could not be opened in this session.
- **Exact rules on moving bees** — text says do not move bees between the Cape bee region and the rest of SA without written permission; the precise regional boundaries were not checked.
- **AFB distribution** — confirmed present since 2009 and found in samples from SA beyond the first Western Cape outbreak; current provincial spread not confirmed. The widely quoted "40% of Western Cape bees wiped out" was not used.
- **Newcastle revaccination interval** — "about every three months" is from village-chicken vaccination studies and FAO guidance; the text defers to vaccine label and local animal health staff.
- **GM share of SA maize and soya** — stated as "much"; not re-checked against ISAAA/DALRRD figures this session.
- **Quarantine of new birds (2–3 weeks)** — standard biosecurity advice, not tied to a specific SA source this session.
- **Hen water intake** — given as "about twice the feed weight"; exact ml figures vary (140–500 ml/day) with heat and size, so no single figure was given.
- **Commercial hybrid egg numbers** — not checked; no figure given.
- Removed as unsupported: "maximum group number about 20"; "98% of eggs by 9 or 10 am" (softened); "tea bags"; "apiary a dying art"; "most commonly pesticides" as the main cause of colony loss; sunn hemp as grazing; *Dalbergia obovata* uses; carob "nitrogen fixing"; torchwood "fast"; sweet thorn "coffee, glue"; sweet chestnut.
- Italian ryegrass, chicory, lucerne, carob and English oak are alien plants; none was found on the NEMBA 2020 lists in the summaries consulted (oak confirmed not listed), but the full DFFE list PDF could not be opened to confirm every species.
- No income or price figures appear in this chapter of the source, so none needed dating.
