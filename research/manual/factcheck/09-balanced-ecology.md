## Could not verify / open questions

- ***Sesbania sesban*** NEMBA status could not be confirmed (the 2020 list PDF was blocked). *S. punicea* is listed. I did not recommend *S. sesban*.
- **Pine species listing.** I could not open the 2020 NEMBA list to confirm which *Pinus* species and categories; the text says only "several kinds of pine are declared invaders", which is well established.
- **Pigeon pea** (*Cajanus cajan*) NEMBA status: not listed to my knowledge; confirm.
- **"Grasslands ... catch and store the water that feeds our main rivers"**: widely stated by SANBI and WWF-SA (strategic water source areas lie largely in grassland), but I could not fetch a page to cite it in this session.
- **Forest extent**: sources give about 0.1% (forest biome, 1 062 km²) to 0.56% (Afromontane forest). The text says "less than 1%", which covers both.
- **Frog insect consumption**: no reliable figure found; figure removed.
- **Firebreak obligation** refers to the National Veld and Forest Fire Act 101 of 1998; not re-checked online in this session.
