# Fact-check log — 06-tree-systems

Source: `research/manual/rvcc-handbook-source.txt`, lines 2683–3779 (printed pages 36–48; file markers 37–49).
Output: `public/manual/en/06-tree-systems.md`.

**Research limits this session.** WebFetch and direct downloads were blocked by the network proxy for every site tried (dffe.gov.za, invasives.org.za, laws.ewt.org, hlem.co.za, conservancieskzn.org.za, wikipedia, iNaturalist API), so the NEMBA 2020 Government Gazette (GN 1003, *Government Gazette* 43726, 18 September 2020) could **not** be opened and searched line by line. NEMBA statuses below come from web-search snippets of the gazette and of SA sources that quote it. The session's web-search budget then ran out, so some plant sizes and horticultural details were checked against established reference knowledge (mainly SANBI PlantZAfrica profiles as remembered) rather than re-read online; these are marked "not re-checked online". **A person with gazette access should confirm the "not found" rows in the species table before publication.**
