# Tshivenḓa glossary — permaculture manual (ve)

> **Machine-drafted glossary for fluent-speaker review.** This list was drafted by machine to keep
> the Tshivenḓa manual chapters (`public/manual/ve/`) consistent with each other and with the
> ImbewuField app. It has **not** been checked by a fluent Tshivenḓa speaker. A reviewer should
> confirm or correct every row — especially those marked **(check)** — before the translations are
> treated as final.

How to use it:

- Use the Tshivenḓa term in the second column every time the English term appears, so all
  chapters agree. If a chapter needs a new term, add a row here.
- **Explain on first use** means: the first time the term appears in a chapter, give the
  Tshivenḓa and put the English in brackets after it, e.g. "mukubo (swale)". After that the
  Tshivenḓa alone is enough.
- **Loan word** means the English/Afrikaans word, spelt the Tshivenḓa way, is what farmers and
  extension officers actually say.
- "App:" shows where the same term is already used in the ImbewuField app. `ve.ts` =
  `lib/locales/ve.ts`; `drafts-ve` = `lib/course-translation-drafts-ve.ts`; `drafts-ve-<name>` =
  `lib/course-translation-drafts-ve-<name>.ts`.
- Letters: always ḓ ḽ ṅ ṋ ṱ (never plain d l n t where the dotted/circumflex letter belongs).
  A few app strings are spelt without these letters; this glossary gives the corrected spelling
  and notes the difference.
- Latin plant names stay in italics, unchanged. Numbers and units (mm, m, kg, °C) stay as in English.
