# Endnote points removed — 09-balanced-ecology

Legal / authority points:
- Black wattle (*Acacia mearnsii*), *Leucaena leucocephala* and red sesbania (*Sesbania punicea*) are listed invasive species under NEMBA (Alien and Invasive Species Lists, 2020); the chapter now just says they "spread out of control in South Africa".
- Several *Pinus* species are listed invaders under NEMBA.
- Mosquitofish (*Gambusia affinis*) is a NEMBA-listed invasive fish; do not stock it in garden ponds.
- Manage grassland with fire and grazing according to local rules, and keep firebreaks as the National Veld and Forest Fire Act (101 of 1998) requires.

Safety warnings removed from the chapter:
- Snakes and scorpions may shelter in rock and log piles: wear gloves and boots when moving rocks or logs (the chapter keeps "build them away from the house and where children play").
- Rat poison also kills cats and dogs that eat poisoned rodents (the chapter keeps the owl point).
