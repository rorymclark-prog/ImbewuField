# Story slots — 06-tree-systems

- Section "Food forests": ask Rory for a real food forest he planted or visited in KZN or Lesotho (for example the Durban food forest in the photo) and what it looked like before and after five years.
- Section "Food forests", Coppicing: ask Rory for a real case of a coppiced woodlot or wild-olive stand giving a household firewood.
- Section "Planting a food forest": ask Rory for a time pigeon pea or thorn-tree pioneers, chopped and dropped, visibly lifted a young fruit tree.
- Section "Windbreaks": ask Rory for a windbreak on a Lesotho or Highveld garden that saved crops in a windy spring (or a garden lost for lack of one).
- Section "Planting a tree": ask Rory for a planting day where trees in wide holes with deep weekly watering outlived trees planted the quick way.
