# 08-soil — legal and "check with" points removed from the chapter

- Mexican sunflower (*Tithonia diversifolia*) is a NEMBA Category 1b invader in South Africa: it is against the law to plant or sell it (the chapter now just says it is an invader and names sunn hemp or lablab instead).
- Check any unfamiliar "green manure" or "soil builder" plant against the NEMBA Alien and Invasive Species Lists (2020), or with a local extension office, before planting it.
- Ask a local agricultural extension office or co-operative which soil laboratory to send a sample to.
- Human waste is only safe after long, careful treatment in a properly managed composting or urine-diversion toilet system; follow official (e.g. WHO / Department of Water and Sanitation) guidance for these.
