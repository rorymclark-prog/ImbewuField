# Story slots — 05-animal-systems

- Section "Place animals where they work for you": ask Rory for a real case from Lesotho or KZN where an animal's habit was given a job (e.g. hens put onto a harvested bed, and what the bed looked like after).
- Section "Animal tractors": ask Rory for a chicken or pig tractor he built or saw on a project: what it was made of, how often it was moved, what went wrong at first.
- Section "Chickens": ask Rory for a Newcastle disease outbreak he saw in a village flock, and what changed once the households vaccinated.
- Section "Pigs": ask Rory for a time pigs cleared a patch of rough or weedy ground, and what was planted after.
- Section "Ducks": ask Rory for a time ducks (or Muscovies) cleared snails or slugs from a garden.
- Section "Bees": ask Rory for a hive-siting lesson from the field (e.g. a hive placed too near a path or a tethered animal, or one raised on tyres).
