# 07-earthworks-and-water — story slots for Rory

- Section "What earthworks can do": ask Rory for a time a swale or earthworks plan held water through a dry spell (the Fig 35 property plan with three swales and a 12 m³ pond could be the story).
- Section "Ponds and dams": ask Rory for the story of the runoff pond in the photos: where it was, the catchment, and whether it filled after 40 mm or 45 mm of rain (the source has both captions; the figure caption says 45 mm).
- Section "Sand dams": ask Rory what happened with the Bethel Business and Community Development Centre sand dam in Lesotho (did it fill with sand, is the water used?).
- Section "Swales": ask Rory for a time he dug swales with a TLB versus by hand, and what the planted berm (amadumbe, lemongrass, sweet potato) gave in its first season.
- Section "Preparing the ground": ask Rory for the volunteer-dug garden, or a time animals (chickens or pigs) prepared a site.
- Section "Using greywater for irrigation": ask Rory for a real greywater system he set up (e.g. the drum and grease trap feeding a swale) and what grew on it.
