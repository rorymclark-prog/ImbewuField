# 02-planning-your-farm — story slots (ask Rory; do not invent)

- Section "Step 1: Site assessment": ask Rory for a time a family or group drew their base map together (the "planning together" photo), and what they saw on paper that they had missed on the ground.
- Section "Step 2: Zone planning": ask Rory for a homestead where moving the garden, tap or chickens closer to the house changed how well it was looked after.
- Section "Step 3: Slope planning": ask Rory for a Lesotho or KZN site where a tank or dam placed uphill fed a garden by gravity, or where greywater was sent down to trees.
