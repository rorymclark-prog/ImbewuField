# 02-planning-your-farm — legal and "check with" points removed from the chapter

- Under the Conservation of Agricultural Resources Act (43 of 1983) and its regulations, land steeper than 20% may not be cultivated without written permission from the Department of Agriculture; in some erosion-prone areas listed in the regulations the limit is 12%. (The slope table row "20%: South African law needs written permission to cultivate" and the matching Key point were removed.)
- Ask an extension officer which slope limit applies to your land.
- Black wattle, Port Jackson, leucaena and many gum (*Eucalyptus*) species are listed invaders under NEMBA (Alien and Invasive Species Lists, 2020); planting them is banned or needs a permit. (The chapter now just recommends sweet thorn instead of wattle or gum.)
- The local municipality may hold a map (cadastral / property plan) of your land that you can use as a base map.
