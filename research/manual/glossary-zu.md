# isiZulu glossary — Permaculture Manual

> **Machine-drafted glossary for fluent-speaker review.** Every isiZulu term here was drafted by
> a machine and has not yet been checked by a fluent isiZulu speaker with farming knowledge. Do
> not treat it as final. Terms marked **(check)** are the least certain and should be reviewed
> first.

## How to use this glossary

- Use the isiZulu term in this table every time the English term appears in a `zu` chapter, so
  all chapters agree with each other and with the app.
- Register: plain, rural, spoken isiZulu — how an extension officer would say it in the village.
- **Loan words** keep the English stem with a hyphenated isiZulu prefix: *i-mulch*, *ama-swale*,
  *i-food forest*. This is how the app already writes them.
- **Explain on first use**: where the notes say so, the first time the term appears in a chapter
  give the isiZulu phrase and the English in brackets, e.g. *umsele wokubamba amanzi (swale)*.
  After that the short form is enough.
- Keep Latin plant names in italics, unchanged. Numbers and units stay as in English.
- If a chapter needs a term that is not listed, add it here.

## Inconsistencies found in the app (for the reviewer)

The manual follows the choices above. These app strings disagree with each other and should be
reviewed together with this glossary:

- **compost**: *i-compost* (course, modules), *umquba* (calendar), *Umvundo* (zu.ts `soilAmendmentCompost`, `infraCompostBay`). *Umquba* also means manure in the course.
- **soil**: *umhlabathi* (course, calendar) and *inhlabathi* (zu.ts). Both are correct.
- **frost**: *isithwathwa* (course, zu.ts sector labels) and *iqhwa* (other zu.ts strings).
- **sector**: *ama-sector* (course) and *Umkhakha* (zu.ts `designStepSector`).
- **contours**: zu.ts map layer toggle *Imikhawulo* ("limits") and *imigqa yokuphakama* in the design canvas.
- **greywater**: *amanzi asetshenzisiwe* (zu.ts, course) and *amanzi ampunga* (modules).
- **mulch**: *i-mulch* (course, calendar) and *Isembozo* (zu.ts `soilAmendmentMulch`).
- **no-dig**: zu.ts `landPrepNoneDesc` has *indlela yokungamboli* (probably a typo for *yokungambi*).
- **maize / spinach spelling**: zu.ts has *Umbila* and *Ispinashi*; the manual uses *ummbila* and *isipinashi*.
- **worms**: *izikelemu* (worm farm), *izibungu* (soil worms), *izikelemu* (animal parasites).
