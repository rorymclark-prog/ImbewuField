# Handbook figures — inventory and verdicts

Source: *The RVCC Permaculture Gardening Handbook* (compiled by Rory Clark). Images were extracted from the PDF into the Google Drive folder `1_Kl_jO2-uV02v33KT4oZzF9T0q7LVp4n`. Files are named `pNNN_i.png`: file page NNN, image i. **Printed page = file page − 1** (the `===== page N =====` markers in `rvcc-handbook-source.txt`).

- Drive folder: **151 files**. 49 are under 20 KB (bullets, icons, section-number badges) and were **not downloaded** (listed at the end).
- **101 of 102 large images downloaded** to `research/manual/figures-source/` (git-ignored working area). `p051_12.png` (6.4 MB) could not be fetched: the Drive connector session expired each time.
- Captions come from `rvcc-handbook-source.txt` and the `research/manual/factcheck/<slug>.md` logs. Where a page has several captions and several images, the caption was matched by what the photo shows; "order-based match" means the content was not decisive.

**How the verdict rules were applied**

- **REPLACE, invasive plant:** only when the listed plant is the subject or is presented as the thing to plant. Incidental background trees (for example plantation gums on a far hillside) are noted in the reason but do not change a USE. Sesbania is treated as REPLACE because chapter 06 now says "never plant … sesbania" and the *S. sesban* NEMBA status is unconfirmed (factcheck 06 and 09).
- **REPLACE, faces:** a private person's face is the main subject or fills a large part of the frame, usually looking at the camera. People at mid-distance or with their faces turned away are USE, sometimes with "confirm consent".
- **REPLACE, third party:** the "Reproduced with permission" graphic (eesi.org) and two images whose origin makes reuse doubtful: a supplier photo with a watermark, and an owl-box painting credited only "Source Wikipedia". The other "Reproduced with permission" credits in the source (Brad Lancaster, p. 49; beesfordevelopment.org, p. 34) belong to **text** lists, not to any image in the folder.
- No image shows tobacco spray or another unsafe practice.
