# Permaculture Manual — cover and chapter-page prompts for ChatGPT

Paste one prompt at a time into ChatGPT (image generation). Generate **artwork only, with no
words in the image**. ChatGPT often misspells text, especially in isiZulu, Sesotho, Tshivenḓa
and Xitsonga, so the title text goes on afterwards in Canva (or I can place it for you). The
exact title text for each language is at the bottom of this file.

**Size:** ask for portrait **1024 × 1536** (A4-shaped). For print, upscale or rebuild at
2480 × 3508 px (A4 at 300 dpi).

---

## 1. Style block — paste this at the start of EVERY prompt

> Illustration style: warm, hand-painted gouache with a slight risograph texture, flat but rich
> colours, soft natural light, calm and hopeful mood. Colour palette only: forest green #1F4D2B,
> mid green #2E6B3A, leaf green #A8D88A, ochre #C07A1E, deep water blue #235E86, warm paper
> cream #E4DCC6, dark earth brown #20190F. Setting: rural South Africa (summer-rainfall
> grassland and bushveld, rondavels and small block houses, red-brown soil). People: Black
> South African smallholder farmers of different ages, mostly women, dressed for real farm work,
> shown with dignity and skill, never as poor or passive. Plants must be ones that really grow
> there and are NOT invasive: no wattle, eucalyptus/gum trees, jacaranda, prickly pear,
> lantana or pine. Good choices: marula, sweet thorn (Vachellia karroo), wild olive, aloes,
> indigenous grasses, maize, sorghum, beans, pumpkins, morogo, sweet potato, bananas,
> citrus, peach. No text, letters, numbers, logos or watermarks anywhere in the image.

---

## 2. Main cover (use the same artwork for all five languages)

> [STYLE BLOCK] A portrait book cover illustration. A thriving permaculture homestead seen from
> a gentle hillside at golden hour. In the foreground, a woman farmer kneels by a curved
> mulched vegetable bed full of maize, beans and pumpkins (the "three sisters") and leafy
> greens, holding a handful of dark crumbly soil. Behind her: a keyhole garden, a small food
> forest of fruit trees with a marula tree, a round rainwater tank beside a rondavel with a
> corrugated-iron roof, a few free-range chickens, and a curved swale following the contour of
> the slope, holding a thin line of water. Soft hills and an indigenous grassland horizon in
> the distance. Leave the TOP THIRD of the image as calm, open sky in warm cream and pale
> ochre, with no detail, so a title can be placed there. Leave a plain strip along the bottom
> for a subtitle.

**If you want a second option**, a close-up, more symbolic version:

> [STYLE BLOCK] A portrait book cover illustration. Two hands, one older and one younger, cup
> a seedling growing from dark rich soil. Around the hands, in a loose circle, small painted
> vignettes of a permaculture farm: a rain tank, a mulched bed, a fruit tree, a chicken, a
> beehive, a worm farm, a pumpkin vine and a sun in the north. Plain warm cream background.
> Leave the top third empty for a title.

## 3. Chapter pages (one per chapter, 12 in all)

Use the same style block and the same format: portrait, a picture in the lower two-thirds,
and the **top third left plain** for the chapter number and title.

| # | Chapter | Prompt (after the style block) |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Introduction | A farmer opening a well-used notebook at a wooden table on a stoep, a garden and hills behind her, seedlings in tins on the table. |
| 1 | What is permaculture? | Three linked circles made of natural things: a circle of soil and plants (earth care), a circle of people working together (people care), and a circle of shared harvest baskets (fair share). |
| 2 | Planning your farm or homestead | A hand-drawn farm plan on paper, weighed down by stones, with zones as rings around a house; behind it, the real homestead matches the plan. |
| 3 | Sector planning | A homestead seen from above with gentle arrows showing the sun's path across the NORTH of the sky, a winter wind, a summer storm and a fire risk from the dry grass slope. |
| 4 | Vegetable and staple crops | Rich raised beds of spinach, cabbage, tomatoes and onions beside rows of maize, sorghum and beans; a woman harvesting into a woven basket. |
| 5 | Animal systems | Chickens in a movable wire "chicken tractor" on a garden bed, ducks near a small pond, and a wooden beehive under a tree at a safe distance from the house. |
| 6 | Tree systems | A layered food forest: tall marula, fruit trees, bushes, herbs, a pumpkin groundcover and climbing beans, with a windbreak of indigenous trees behind. |
| 7 | Earthworks and water | A curved swale on contour holding water after rain, a rainwater tank fed by a roof gutter, and a small pond, with people digging with spades. |
| 8 | Soil | A cut-away view of healthy soil: dark topsoil full of roots, earthworms and fungal threads, with a compost heap and a worm farm on the surface. |
| 9 | A balanced, productive ecology | Birds, bees, ladybirds, a lizard and a frog living among flowers, herbs and vegetables in a lively garden edge. |
| 10 | Natural pest control | A close view of a vegetable bed with marigolds, a ladybird eating aphids, a praying mantis, and a farmer checking the underside of a leaf. |
| 11 | The sustainable home | A rondavel kitchen area with a rocket stove, a wonder bag cooking pot, a solar dryer with sliced fruit, a small solar panel and a biogas dome nearby. |
