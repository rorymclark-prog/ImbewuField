# Amanothi nemithombo

Le ncwadi ikhuluma ngendlela yokulima. Lapha, ngemuva, kunezinto ezimbili ezingezangemuva: inothi ngokuphepha nomthetho, nemithombo esekela amaqiniso akuso sonke isahluko.

## Ukuphepha nomthetho

Le ncwadi ikukhombisa ukuthi wenze kanjani umsebenzi; ayingeni esikhundleni somthetho noma solwazi lwabantu abayazi indawo yakini, futhi imithetho iyahluka ngokwezindawo futhi iyashintsha ngokuhamba kwesikhathi. ENingizimu Afrika, qala ubuze abantu abafanele ngaphambi kokuthi wakhe idamu noma ichibi, noma wenze noma yimuphi umsebenzi emfuleni, emfudlaneni, esiphethwini noma exhaphozini (uMnyango Wezamanzi Nokuthuthwa Kwendle, i-National Water Act, umthetho kazwelonke wamanzi); ngaphambi kokuthi ulime umhlaba ongumqansa (uMnyango Wezolimo, i-Conservation of Agricultural Resources Act, umthetho wokongiwa komhlaba wezolimo); ngaphambi kokuthi ushise i-firebreak noma wokhele umlilo endle (i-Fire Protection Association yakini, inhlangano yokuvikela umlilo, ne-National Veld and Forest Fire Act, umthetho wemililo yasendle nasemahlathini); ngaphambi kokuthi utshale, uthengise noma uhambise isitshalo noma inhlanzi ongayazi, ngoba eziningi zimenyezelwe njengezihlaselayo, nangaphambi kokuthi usike isihlahla somdabu, ngoba eziningi zivikelwe (i-NEMBA Alien and Invasive Species Lists, 2020, uhlu lwezinhlobo ezihlaselayo, ne-National Forests Act, umthetho wamahlathi); ngaphambi kokuthi ufuye izinyosi, okufanele zibhaliswe; nangaphambi kokuthi ufake ugesi welanga, odinga umkhandi kagesi oqeqeshiwe (electrician). Bika ukufa okungazelelwe kwezinkukhu noma kwezingulube kudokotela wezilwane kahulumeni. Phatha umquba, i-greywater, izifutho, umlilo, igesi, amabhethri, amanzi ajulile nemishini ngokucophelela, ugcine izingane kude nakho, futhi uma umuntu elimele kakhulu, etinyelwe noma edle ushevu, yiya emtholampilo noma ushayele u-10177 (u-112 ocingweni oluphathwayo). ELesotho, e-Eswatini naseZimbabwe, buza iziphathimandla zezwe lakho, umeluleki wakho wezolimo noma umkhandlu wendabuko.

## Imithombo

Isahluko ngasinye sahlolwa kule mithombo ngoMandulo 2026. Le ncwadi isekelwe encwadini ethi *The Permaculture Gardening Handbook*, eyahlanganiswa nguRory Clark yephrojekthi ye-RVCC (i-UNDP noHulumeni waseLesotho, 2020–21), eyathatha ingxenye yayo encwadini ye-African Conservation Trust ethi *Introduction to Permaculture and Homestead Gardening* (2014). Sonke isahluko sabhalwa kabusha ngamazwi ethu; izingcaphuno ezimfushane zibongwa lapho zivela khona.
