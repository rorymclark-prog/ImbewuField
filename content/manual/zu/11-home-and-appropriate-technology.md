# Ikhaya elisimeme: i-Zone 0 ne-appropriate technology

Wonke amapulazi anesikhungo, futhi yikhishi. Yilapho izibaseli, amanzi nemali eningi kuya khona. Umndeni ungatshala ukudla okuningi kodwa usabe mpofu ngamandla: uthenga upharafini, uthwala izinkuni amahora, uhlala ebumnyameni lapho ugesi uvaleka.

Ikhaya yilapho i-permaculture ibuyisela ngokushesha kakhulu. Lonke ilitha lemvula elibanjiwe, lonke ukhuni olonyiwe nalo lonke ihora lelanga lamahhala elisetshenzisiwe kuyimali esala emzini.

**Ekupheleni kwalesi sahluko uzokwazi:**

- Ukuchaza ukuthi i-Zone 0 ne-appropriate technology kusho ukuthini
- Ukuhlela ukuthi ungayenza kanjani indlu ifudumale ebusika futhi ipholile ehlobo ngelanga
- Ukukhetha ithuluzi elifanele lelanga lokukhanyisa, amanzi ashisayo, ukupheka nokomisa ukudla
- Ukupheka ngezibaseli ezincane usebenzisa i-wonder bag ne-rocket stove
- Ukuchaza ukuthi i-biogas digester iguqula kanjani umquba ube yigesi yokupheka

## Ikhaya liyi-Zone 0

Ku-permaculture sihlela umhlaba ngama-**zone**, ngokuthi sivakashela ingxenye ngayinye kangaki. Indlu **iyi-Zone 0**, isikhungo somklamo.

Cabanga ngekhaya:

- elakhiwe ngezinto zendawo, njengobumba nezingodo ezivela eduze, ezingashintshwa uma seziguga
- elihlala lifudumele ebusika futhi lipholile ehlobo ngokushisisa noma ukupholisa okuncane
- elizenzela ugesi walo ngelanga, ukuze izibani zihlale zikhanya lapho ugesi kazwelonke uvaleka
- elipheka ngegesi eyenziwe ngomquba wezilwane
- elibamba amanzi emvula emathangini nasemachibini, anele ukulidlulisa enkathini eyomile uma esetshenziswa ngokucophelela

Amathuluzi enza lokhu kwenzeke abizwa ngokuthi **i-appropriate technology**: amathuluzi nezindlela ezilula, ezingabizi kakhulu, abantu bendawo abangazakha, bazisebenzise futhi bazilungise ngokwabo. Asebenza ngamakhono nezinto zendawo futhi afeze izidingo eziyisisekelo zamandla, amanzi, ukudla nemfucuza. Amanye ungawenza kule mpelasonto; amanye adinga ukutshala imali encane. Onke ayazikhokhela ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.

Akudingeki ukwenze konke ngesikhathi esisodwa. Isinyathelo ngasinye songa imali futhi senza umuzi uvikeleke kakhulu.

## Klamela indlu ilanga

ENingizimu ne-Afrika ilanga lisenyakatho. Lihamba liphezulu ehlobo futhi liphansi ebusika. Indlu esebenzisana nalokhu iyazishisisa futhi iyazipholisa mahhala.

1. Beka amakamelo amakhulu okuhlala namafasitela amaningi ohlangothini olusenyakatho, ukuze ungenise ilanga lasebusika.
2. Yakha umphetho wophahla ophumele ngaphandle (eaves) noma i-pergola phezu kwamafasitela asenyakatho. Uvimba ilanga eliphezulu lasehlobo futhi ungenise ilanga eliphansi lasebusika.
3. Gcina amafasitela asentshonalanga emancane. Ilanga lasehlobo lantambama lishisisa indlu ngokweqile.
4. Sebenzisa izinto ezisindayo, njengamatshe, izitini, ubumba noma phansi kukakhonkolo, lapho ilanga lasebusika liwela khona. Le **thermal mass** imunca ukushisa emini bese ikubuyisela ebusuku.
5. Faka isilingi esine-insulation. Igcina ukushisa ngaphakathi ebusika nangaphandle ehlobo.
6. Tshala izihlahla noma imivini ehlubula amaqabunga (elahla amaqabunga ayo ebusika) enyakatho nasentshonalanga. Yenza umthunzi ehlobo futhi ivumele ilanga lidlule ebusika.

Uma wakha, nquma lezi zinto ngaphambi kokubekwa kwesitini sokuqala. Endlini esivele ikhona, qala ngesilingi.

## Amandla elanga

Amandla avuselelekayo ayazivuselela: ilanga, umoya, amanzi ageleza **ne-biomass** (izinto zezitshalo nezilwane, njengezinkuni nomquba). Amalahle akunjalo: uma eseshile, aphelile. Amalahle asenza cishe u-83% kagesi waseNingizimu Afrika ngo-2024, futhi ukuwashisa kungcolisa umoya futhi kubange i-climate change.

Ilanga yilona mandla avuselelekayo sonke esabelana ngawo. Ngehora elilodwa, ukukhanya kwelanga okufika eMhlabeni kuthwala amandla acishe alingane nalawo umhlaba wonke owasebenzisa ngonyaka. INingizimu ne-Afrika ithola elinye lamalanga anamandla kakhulu emhlabeni, futhi amaphaneli elanga manje abiza ngaphansi kuka-1% walokho ayekubiza ngawo-1970.

### Amaphaneli elanga

Amaphaneli elanga, abizwa nangokuthi ama-PV panel, aguqula ukukhanya kwelanga kube ugesi. Amabhethri awugcinela ubusuku.

1. Wabeke lapho kungekho lutho oluwenzela umthunzi phakathi kuka-9 ekuseni no-3 ntambama.
2. Wabhekise enyakatho.
3. Wathambekise cishe nge-engeli ye-latitude yakho: cishe ama-degree angu-26 eGoli, angu-34 eKapa.
4. Sula uthuli nendle yezinyoni.

I-kit encane ikhanyisa izibani ze-LED, ishaje amafoni futhi idlalise umsakazo. Uhlelo olukhulu lusebenzisa ifriji, i-TV noma ikhompyutha. Ludinga **i-inverter**, eguqula ugesi oqondile (DC) wamaphaneli ube ugesi oshintshanayo (AC) osetshenziswa yizinto eziningi zikagesi.

Gcina ugesi welanga wezibani, amafoni, amafriji nama-TV. Ama-geyser, amaketela, izifudumezi nezitofu kudla ugesi omningi, futhi ukukusebenzisa ngamaphaneli kudinga uhlelo olukhulu, olubizayo. Shisisa amanzi futhi upheke ngamathuluzi angezansi.

### I-geyser yelanga

Emakhaya amaningi aseNingizimu Afrika i-geyser yiyona esebenzisa ugesi omningi kakhulu: ucwaningo lwe-Eskom lwathola ukuthi ingaba ngu-39% wogesi osetshenziswa ngumuzi ojwayelekile. I-geyser yelanga ishisisa amanzi esiqoqelweni samaphaneli ayisicaba noma amashubhu engilazi esophahleni futhi iwagcine ethangini elinezivikeli zokushisa. Ohlotsheni olulula kakhulu, amanzi ashisayo akhuphukela ethangini ngokwawo, ngakho ithangi kufanele libe ngaphezu kwesiqoqeli.

- Bhekisa isiqoqeli enyakatho bese usithambekisa njengephaneli yelanga.
- Lapho kunesithwathwa, okuyingxenye enkulu yethafa langaphakathi nezwe, sebenzisa i-geyser **engaqondile** (indirect). Ishisisa uketshezi olungaqandi esiqoqelweni esikhundleni samanzi. I-geyser eqondile ingaqanda iqhume.
- Faka isikhathi (timer) esakhini sikagesi esisizayo, ukuze sisebenze kuphela lapho ilanga lingakwenzanga umsebenzi.

> **Icebiso:** Isigubhu noma ibhodlela lamanzi elipendwe ngokumnyama elibekwe elangeni ekuseni linika amanzi afudumele okugeza ntambama, mahhala.

### Isitofu selanga

Isitofu selanga (solar cooker) sipheka ngokukhanya kwelanga kuphela, futhi asibizi lutho ukusisebenzisa.

- **Isitofu esiyibhokisi:** ibhokisi elinezivikeli zokushisa, elimnyama ngaphakathi, elinesivalo sengilazi noma sepulasitiki ecwebile. Libamba ukushisa njengemoto evaliwe elangeni futhi lifinyelela cishe ku-100 kuya ku-150 °C, okwanele ukupheka ngisho nokubhaka, kancane.
- **Isitofu samaphaneli:** amaphaneli acwebezelayo ashaya ukukhanya kwelanga ebhodweni elimnyama elingaphakathi kwesikhwama esicwebile. Siyashibhile futhi kulula ukusenza.
- **Isitofu esiyisitsha (parabolic):** isitsha esigobile sezibuko siqoqa ukukhanya kwelanga ebhodweni. Sidlula ku-250 °C futhi sipheka ngokushesha, kodwa kufanele sijikiswe kaningi silandele ilanga.

1. Pheka phakathi kuka-10 ekuseni no-3 ntambama.
2. Sebenzisa amabhodwe amancane, amnyama anezivalo eziqinile.
3. Jikisa isitofu sibheke ilanga cishe njalo ngehora.
4. Vumela isikhathi esengeziwe kunasesitofini. Uma ilanga lihamba ngaphambi kokuba inyama noma ubhontshisi kuvuthwe kahle, kuqedele esitofini.

## Ukomisa ukudla ngelanga

Ihlobo linika okuningi kunalokho umndeni ongakudla; ubusika bunika okuncane. Ukomisa kudlulisela okusele ngaphesheya. Kuyasebenza ngoba isikhunta namagciwane onakalisa ukudla akwazi ukukhula ngaphandle kwamanzi.

### Ukomisa elangeni

Ukomisa elangeni elivulekile yindlela eshibhe kakhulu, kodwa ihamba kancane: ukudla kungabola ngesikhunta ngaphambi kokoma, futhi izimpukane, uthuli namazolo kufinyelela kukho. Kwenze kahle:

1. Sebenzisa imikhiqizo emisha, ehlanzekile. Yigeze, uphinde ugeze izandla zakho.
2. Yisike ibe izingcezu ezincane, ezilinganayo.
3. Yomise phezu kwezitendi eziphakanyisiwe noma kumathileyi, ungalokothi uyomise phansi.
4. Yimboze ngenetha elihlanzekile ukuvimba izimpukane.
5. Yingenise ebusuku, noma uyimboze ukuvimba amazolo.
6. Phendula izingcezu kanye noma kabili ngosuku.

### I-solar dryer

**I-solar dryer** (ibhokisi elomisa ukudla ngokushisa kwelanga) iyashesha futhi ihlanzekile. Ingaba lula njengebhokisi lokhuni elinesivalo sepulasitiki ecwebile namathileyi enetha noma endwangu yomthunzi, noma iqine njengohlaka lwensimbi olunengilazi. Ama-solar dryer avumela umoya ofudumele ugeleze phakathi kwamathileyi asebenza ngokushesha, ngoba umoya ohambayo uthwala umswakama.

Ukudla kome ngokwanele lapho:

- **izithelo** zifana nesikhumba futhi ziyagoba ngaphandle kwamabala amanzi uma zikhanywa
- **imifino** iyaphuka kalula futhi iyaqhekeka noma iyaklabalasa
- **ummbila, ubhontshisi namantongomane** kuqinile futhi kuyaklabalasa, komiswe kwaba ngaphansi komswakama ongaba ngu-13% phakathi kwezinsuku ezimbili noma ezintathu ngemva kokuvuna

Omisa okusanhlamvu namantongomane ngokushesha, bese ulahla noma yikuphi okunesikhunta, okushintshe umbala noma okushwabene. Isikhunta sokusanhlamvu okumanzi senza **i-aflatoxin**, ubuthi besibindi ukupheka okungabubhubhisi. Gcina ukudla okomisiwe ezitsheni ezihlanzekile ezingangeni moya endaweni epholile, emnyama, bese ulahla noma yini enuka ukubola.

Ukucwilisa izingcezu zezithelo kujusi kalamula namanzi kugcina umbala wazo. Ukuhlambulula imifino (ukuyicwilisa ngokushesha emanzini abilayo) kuyisiza igcineke.

## Ukupheka ngokugcina ukushisa: i-wonder bag

Ibhodwe lesitambu nobhontshisi lingabila kancane amahora, futhi lelo nalelo hora lishisa izinkuni, igesi noma upharafini. Isitofu sokugcina ukushisa sivumela ibhodwe liqede ukupheka ngokushisa kwalo.

**I-wonder bag** yisikhwama esinezivikeli zokushisa esigcina ibhodwe elibilayo lishisa amahora. I-Wonderbag yasungulwa eNingizimu Afrika ngo-2008; ibhokisi lotshani elenziwe ekhaya, eligcwaliswe ngotshani obomile noma ngezingubo ezindala, lisebenza ngendlela efanayo. Izibaseli ezincane ezishiswayo zisho imali encane esetshenziswayo nentuthu encane endlini.

1. Bilisa ukudla kuze kubile ngempela bese ukuyeka kubile kancane imizuzu embalwa. Isitambu, ubhontshisi nenyama eqinile kudinga ukubila isikhathi eside. Cwilisa ubhontshisi owomile, bese ububilisa ngamandla okungenani imizuzu eyi-10 ngaphambi kokuba ungene esikhwameni.
2. Isivalo sisephezulu, hambisa ibhodwe ngqo esikhwameni bese usivala.
3. Sebenzisa ibhodwe elingena kahle neligcwele kahle. Ibhodwe eligcwele ligcina ukushisa kwalo isikhathi eside.
4. Ungasivuli isikhwama ukuze uhlole. Konke ukubheka kukhipha ukushisa.
5. Dla ukudla kusashisa kuphuma umhwamuko. Uma sekufudumele nje, kuqale ukubilise futhi. Ungalokothi ushiye ukudla esikhwameni ubusuku bonke.

## Ama-rocket stove

Izinkuni zisindayo ukuzithwala futhi zihamba kancane ukuphinda zikhule. Futhi intuthu iyabulala. I-World Health Organization ilinganisela ukuthi abantu abacishe babe yizigidigidi ezi-2.1 (2.1 billion) bapheka emililweni evulekile noma ezitofini ezilula ezishisa izinkuni, ubulongwe, izinsalela zezitshalo, amalahle noma upharafini. Intuthu emakhaya abo yadala ukufa okulinganiselwa ezigidini ezi-2.9 ngo-2021. Abesifazane nezingane ezincane, abachitha isikhathi esiningi eduze komlilo, bahlupheka kakhulu.

**I-rocket stove** ishisa izinti ezincane kushimula omfushane, onezivikeli zokushisa, omise okuka-L. Umlilo uvutha ushisa futhi uhlanzekile, bese ukushisa kukhuphuka ngqo kuye ebhodweni. Izivivinyo zasenkundleni zathola ukuthi ama-rocket stove asebenzisa izinkuni ezincane ngo-30 kuya ku-50% kunomlilo ovulekile wamatshe amathathu (iziko), anentuthu encane kakhulu ne-carbon monoxide. Amanye ayathwalwa; amanye akhelwe endaweni, anoshimula.

1. Shisa izinkuni ezomile. Izinkuni ezimanzi zinentuthu futhi zinika ukushisa okuncane.
2. Faka izinti ezincane ezimbalwa ngesikhathi bese uzishovela ngaphakathi njengoba zisha.
3. Gcina isikhala somoya ngaphansi kwezinti sivulekile.
4. Hlalisa ibhodwe kahle, nesikhala esincane sokuthi amagesi ashisayo ageleze alizungeze.
5. Pheka ngaphandle noma ekhishini elinoshimula.

I-rocket stove isenza i-carbon monoxide, igesi ongeke uyibone noma uyinuke. Ungalokothi ungenise isitofu esivuthayo, imbawula noma umlilo wamalahle egumbini elivaliwe ukuze ufudumale.

## Ama-biogas digester

Umquba uyisibaseli nomanyolo ngesikhathi esisodwa, uma siwuphatha kahle. **I-biogas** yigesi yokubasa eyenzeka lapho amagciwane ehlakaza umquba, izinsalela zokudla noma indle yangasese ethangini elivalekile elingenawo umoya. Ithangi **yi-biogas digester**. I-biogas icishe ibe ngu-50 kuya ku-70% we-methane, ingxenye evuthayo. Iningi lokusele yi-carbon dioxide, ne-hydrogen sulphide encane, enuka njengamaqanda abolile. Emzini, ukusetshenziswa okungcono kakhulu ngukupheka. Uketshezi oluphumayo, olubizwa ngokuthi **i-slurry**, lungumanyolo omuhle.

Ungathenga i-digester noma uyakhe ngamathangi, amapayipi nezinto zokuxhuma zasesitolo sezinto zokwakha. Kufanele ingangenwa umoya nhlobo.

1. Yondle ngomquba omusha wezinkomo, wezingulube noma wezinkukhu oxutshwe namanzi acishe alingane nawo, kanye nezinsalela zemifino eziqotshiwe.
2. Ungafaki izinto zokhuni, amahlanga, amathambo, ipulasitiki, insipho, i-bleach namanye amakhemikhali. Amakhemikhali abulala amagciwane.
3. Yondle kancane nsuku zonke kunokuyondla okuningi ngesikhathi esisodwa. Umndeni ngokuvamile udinga ubulongwe obusha bezinkomo noma izingulube eziningana nsuku zonke.
4. Yigcine ifudumele. Amagciwane asebenza kangcono kakhulu cishe ku-30 kuya ku-38 °C futhi enza igesi encane kakhulu ngaphansi kuka-15 °C. E-Highveld naseLesotho, yigqibe, uyifake izivikeli zokushisa noma ubeke ithanele elincane likapulasitiki phezu kwayo.
5. Hlola izihlanganiso ukuthi aziputshuzi yini ngamanzi anensipho, lapho amabhamuza ekhombisa ukuputshuza. Gcina amalangabi kude ne-digester namapayipi, pheka ekhishini elingena umoya kahle, futhi uvale umpompi wegesi ngemva kokupheka.
6. Mboza imigodi yokungena neyokuphuma. Ungalokothi ungene ku-digester noma emgodini, ngisho noma kungenalutho: amagesi angaphakathi angabulala.
7. Vumela i-slurry ime noma uyenze i-compost amasonto athile. Bese uyifaka ezihlahleni zezithelo nasezitshalweni ozipheka, ungalokothi uyifake emifinweni oyidla iluhlaza.

## Zama lokhu

Yenza ibhokisi lotshani bese upheka ngalo kuleli sonto.

1. Thola ibhokisi, ikhreyithi, ubhasikidi noma ibhakede elidala elikhulu ngokwanele ukuphatha ibhodwe lakho nesikhala esingangobubanzi besandla nxazonke.
2. Bekela phansi kakhulu ngotshani obomile, izingubo ezindala, imicamelo noma izingcezu ze-foam.
3. Misa ibhodwe kulo bese uminyanisa okunye kwalezo zinto ezinhlangothini. Phakamisa ibhodwe ulikhiphe, ushiye isidleke.
4. Yenza umcamelo ojiyile ngezinto ezifanayo wokumboza phezulu.
5. Pheka okuthile okuvame ukubila kancane isikhathi eside, njengesitambu nobhontshisi. Kubilise, kuyeke kubile kancane imizuzu embalwa, faka ibhodwe elinesivalo esidlekeni bese ulimboza ngomcamelo.
6. Kuyeke amahora ambalwa ungakuvuli. Bhala ukuthi kuthathe isikhathi esingakanani nokuthi usebenzise izibaseli ezingakanani uma kuqhathaniswa nendlela ejwayelekile.

## Amaphuzu amqoka

- Ikhaya liyi-Zone 0. I-appropriate technology ngamathuluzi alula ongawakha futhi uwalungise ngokwakho.
- Bhekisa indlu namaphaneli elanga enyakatho, futhi usebenzise i-thermal mass nomphetho wophahla ukubamba ilanga lasebusika nokuvimba ilanga lasehlobo.
- Gcina ugesi welanga wezibani, amafoni namafriji. Shisisa amanzi futhi upheke nge-geyser yelanga, isitofu selanga, i-wonder bag, i-rocket stove noma i-biogas.
- Omisa ukudla ngokushesha, kuhlanzekile futhi kungekho phansi.

Qala ngethuluzi elilodwa kule nyanga. Lapho inqwaba yezinkuni ihlala isikhathi eside, uzokwazi ukuthi yiliphi elilandelayo ozolakha.
