# Ikhaya elisimeme: i-Zone 0 ne-appropriate technology

## Ikhaya liyi-Zone 0

Kuze kube manje sibhekile ukutshala ukudla nokuhlangabezana nezinye izidingo ngendlela esimeme. Manje sesifika enkabeni yomklamo: ikhaya uqobo lwalo. Ku-permaculture indlu ibizwa ngokuthi i-Zone 0.

Cabanga ngekhaya:

- elakhiwe ngezinto zendawo, njengobumba olutholakala eduze noma izingodo ezivela ehlathini elincane lendawo, ezingashintshwa uma seziguga
- elaklanyelwe isimo sezulu sakho, ukuze lihlale lifudumele ebusika futhi lipholile ehlobo, lingadingi kakhulu ukufudunyezwa noma ukupholiswa
- elenza ugesi walo ohlanzekile ngamaphaneli elanga, ukuze izibani zihlale zikhanya nalapho ugesi ovamile unqamuka
- elenza igesi yokupheka ngomquba wezilwane **ku-biogas digester** (ithangi elivalekile elenza igesi yokupheka ngobulongwe)
- elibamba amanzi emvula emathangini nasemachibini, agcinwe anele ukudlula inkathi yokoma uma esetshenziswa ngokucophelela
- elisebenzisa obunye ubuchwepheshe obuningi obulula ukwehlisa izindleko, ukubamba amandla amahhala nokwakha ukuqina ezikhathini ezinzima **naku-climate change** (ukushintsha kwesimo sezulu)

Awudingi ukwenza konke lokhu ngasikhathi sinye. Isinyathelo ngasinye esincane songa imali futhi senza umndeni uphephe kakhudlwana.

## I-appropriate technology

**I-appropriate technology** (ubuchwepheshe obufanele) isho amathuluzi nemishini elula, emincane, ethi:

- ifanele abantu abayisebenzisayo namasiko abo
- ingakhiwa, isetshenziswe futhi ilungiswe ngamakhono nangezinto zendawo
- ihlangabezane nezidingo eziyisisekelo ezifana namandla, amanzi, ukudla nokulahlwa kwemfucuza

Ubuchwepheshe obukulesi sahluko busetshenziswe ngempumelelo yimizi kulo lonke iNingizimu ne-Afrika. Obunye ungabenza wena ngokwakho. Obunye budinga imali encane ozoyitshala. Bonke bukusiza ukwakha umuzi osimeme, futhi buyazikhokhela ngokuhamba kwesikhathi.

## Klama indlu ngokulandela ilanga

ENingizimu ne-Afrika ilanga liba **ngasenyakatho**. Liphezulu esibhakabhakeni ehlobo futhi liphansi ebusika. Indlu eklanywe kahle iyakusebenzisa lokhu:

- Beka amakamelo amakhulu okuhlala namafasitela amaningi ohlangothini olusenyakatho, ukuze angenise ilanga elifudumele lasebusika.
- Yakha uphahla oluphumela ngaphandle (eaves) noma i-pergola (uhlaka lwezinsika olumbozwe yizitshalo ezikhuphukayo) ngaphezu kwamafasitela asenyakatho. Kuvimba ilanga eliphezulu lasehlobo kodwa kuvumele ilanga eliphansi lasebusika lingene.
- Amafasitela asentshonalanga makabe mancane, ngoba ilanga lantambama ehlobo lishisisa indlu kakhulu.
- Izinto ezisindayo njengamatshe, izitini, ubumba naphansi kukakhonkolo zigcina ukushisa kwasemini bese zikukhipha ebusuku. Lokhu kubizwa ngokuthi **i-thermal mass** (izinto ezigcina ukushisa).
- Isilingi esine-insulation (into evimba ukushisa ukuthi kungadluli) sigcina ukushisa ngaphakathi ebusika futhi sikuvimbe ngaphandle ehlobo.
- Tshala izihlahla ezihlubula amaqabunga ebusika noma izitshalo ezikhuphukayo ngasenyakatho nangasentshonalanga, ukuze zenze umthunzi ehlobo futhi zivumele ilanga lingene ebusika.

## Amandla avuselelekayo

Amandla avuselelekayo (ahlanzekile) avela emithonjeni yemvelo ehlala ibuyiselwa njalo, njengokukhanya kwelanga, umoya namanzi agelezayo.

Amalahle etshe (coal) ahlukile. Ayinsiza engavuselelekiyo: uma eseshisiwe, aphelile, futhi ngelinye ilanga ayophela nya. Iningi likagesi waseNingizimu Afrika lisavela eziteshini zikagesi ezishisa amalahle etshe. Ukushisa amalahle etshe kungcolisa umoya, kunomthelela emvuleni ene-asidi futhi kukhipha i-carbon dioxide eningi kakhulu, eqhuba i-climate change.

Izinhlobo eziyinhloko zamandla avuselelekayo yilezi:

- amandla elanga (avela elangeni)
- amandla amanzi (hydropower, avela emanzini agelezayo)
- amandla omoya
- **i-biomass** (amandla avela ezitshalweni nasemfucuzeni yezilwane, njengezinkuni ne-biogas)

## Amandla elanga

Abantu basebenzise amandla elanga ukupheka, ukufudumeza nokutshala izitshalo iminyaka eyizinkulungwane. Ukukhanya kwelanga okufika eMhlabeni kukhulu kakhulu: ilanga lehora elilodwa cishe liphethe amandla alingana nalawo umhlaba wonke uwasebenzisa ngonyaka. INingizimu ne-Afrika inelinye lamalanga amahle kakhulu emhlabeni.

Intengo yamaphaneli elanga nayo yehle kakhulu. Manje amaphaneli abiza ngaphansi kuka-1% walokho ayekubiza ngeminyaka yawo-1970. Ezikhungweni ezintsha zikagesi, ilanga manje lingenye yezindlela ezishibhe kakhulu zokwenza ugesi.

### Amaphaneli kagesi welanga (PV)

Amaphaneli elanga, abizwa nangokuthi ama-photovoltaic noma ama-PV panel, enziwe ngezinto ezifana ne-silicon eziguqula ukukhanya kwelanga kube ugesi.

Ungawasebenzisa kanjani ukuze akunike okuningi:

1. Wabeke ophahleni noma ohlakeni lapho kungekho lutho oluwenzela umthunzi phakathi kuka-9 ekuseni no-3 ntambama, cishe.
2. ENingizimu ne-Afrika, wabheke **enyakatho**.
3. Wathambekise nge-angle elingana cishe ne-latitude yendawo yakho (ibanga ukusuka enkabeni yomhlaba, i-equator). Eningini leNingizimu Afrika lokhu kucishe kube ama-degree angu-25 kuya ku-35.
4. Wagcine ehlanzekile. Uthuli nendle yezinyoni kunciphisa ugesi awenzayo.

Ugesi ungagcinwa kumabhethri ukuze usetshenziswe ebusuku.

**Izinhlelo ezincane** zikhanyisa izibani ze-LED, zishaja omakhalekhukhwini futhi zidlalisa imisakazo. Aziduri futhi kulula ukuzifaka.

**Izinhlelo ezinkulu** zingasebenzisa ifriji, i-TV noma ikhompyutha. Amaphaneli elanga enza ugesi we-direct current (DC), kodwa iningi lezinto zikagesi zasendlini lisebenzisa i-alternating current (AC), ngakho uhlelo olukhulu ludinga i-inverter (umshini oguqula i-DC ibe yi-AC).

Sebenzisa ugesi welanga ngokuhlakanipha. Ama-geyser kagesi, amaketela, izifudumezi nezitofu zikagesi kusebenzisa ugesi omningi, ngakho ukukusebenzisa ngelanga kudinga uhlelo olukhulu nolubizayo. Kushibhile ukugcinela ugesi welanga izibani, amafriji, omakhalekhukhwini, amakhompyutha nama-TV, bese ufudumeza amanzi futhi upheke ngobunye ubuchwepheshe obuhlanzekile: ama-geyser elanga, ama-heat pump, i-biogas, izitofu zelanga, izikhwama ezigcina ukushisa **nama-rocket stove** (izitofu zezinkuni ezisebenzisa izinkuni ezimbalwa).

> **Ukuphepha:** Noma yini enkulu kunesethi yezibani exhunywa ngepulaki kumele ifakwe ngumuntu oqeqeshelwe ugesi (qualified electrician), okufanele akunike i-Certificate of Compliance (isitifiketi esikhombisa ukuthi umsebenzi uhambisana nemithetho). Uhlelo oluxhunywe kugesi kamasipala noma we-Eskom kumele luvunywe futhi lubhaliswe kumasipala wakho noma e-Eskom ngaphambi kokuba luvulwe. Amabhethri agcina amandla amaningi: wagcine endaweni eyomile, engena umoya, kude nezingane. Amabhethri e-lead-acid akhipha igesi evuthayo lapho eshajwa, ngakho ungalokothi ubheme noma wenze izinhlansi eduze kwawo. Thenga amabhethri e-lithium kuphela uma enohlelo olufanele lokulawula ibhethri olwakhelwe ngaphakathi (battery management system).

### I-geyser yelanga (isifudumezi samanzi selanga)

I-geyser yelanga (solar geyser) isebenzisa ilanga ukufudumeza amanzi. Amanzi noma olunye uketshezi lufudunyezwa ku-collector (amaphaneli ayisicaba noma amashubhu engilazi) ophahleni, bese luya ethangini lokugcina elivikelwe ekubandeni (insulated). Ezinhlelweni ezilula amanzi ashisayo azikhuphukela ngokwawo ethangini, ngakho ithangi kumele libe phezulu kune-collector. Ezinye izinhlelo zisebenzisa iphampu encane.

- Njengamaphaneli elanga, i-collector kufanele ibheke enyakatho futhi ithambekiswe ukuze ibambe ilanga elingaba ningi ngangokunokwenzeka.
- Ezindaweni ezinesithwathwa, okuyiningi lethafa eliphakeme elingaphakathi nezwe, sebenzisa uhlelo olungaqondile (indirect, closed-loop). Lufudumeza uketshezi oluvimba ukuqanda (antifreeze) ku-collector esikhundleni samanzi. Uhlelo oluqondile (direct), lapho amanzi asendlini egeleza khona ku-collector, lungaqanda luqhume ezindaweni ezinesithwathwa.
- Iningi lama-geyser elanga line-element kagesi njengesipele sezinsuku ezinamafu. Yifake ku-timer ukuze ivuleke kuphela uma kudingeka.

Emizini eminingi yaseNingizimu Afrika, ukufudumeza amanzi yingxenye eyodwa enkulu kunazo zonke yesikweletu sikagesi: ucwaningo lukhombisa ukuthi i-geyser isebenzisa cishe u-35 kuya ku-40% kagesi womuzi ojwayelekile. Ngakho i-geyser yelanga ingonga imali eningi noma izinto zokubasa, futhi iyimali etshalwe kahle.

> **Ukuphepha:** Amanzi avela ku-geyser yelanga angashisa kakhulu aze akushise isikhumba, ikakhulukazi ezinsukwini ezinelanga. Cela ofakayo ukuthi afake i-valve yokuxuba amanzi (mixing noma tempering valve). Sebenzisa umkhandi wamapayipi (plumber) oqeqeshiwe olandela izinga laseNingizimu Afrika lokufaka izifudumezi zamanzi zelanga (SANS 10106).

> **Icebiso:** Isitsha samanzi esipendwe ngopende omnyama, esishiywe elangeni ekuseni, sizokunika amanzi afudumele okugeza ntambama, mahhala.

### Isitofu selanga

Isitofu selanga (solar cooker) sisebenzisa amandla elanga ukufudumeza nokupheka ukudla. Kunezinhlobo ezintathu eziyinhloko:

- **Isitofu sebhokisi (box cooker):** ibhokisi elivikelwe ekuphumeni kokushisa, elimnyama ngaphakathi, elinesivalo sengilazi noma sepulasitiki ebonisayo. Libamba ukushisa njengemoto evaliwe emi elangeni. Ngokuvamile lifika cishe ku-100 kuya ku-150 °C, okwanele ukupheka ngisho nokubhaka, kancane.
- **Isitofu samaphaneli (panel cooker):** amaphaneli acwebezelayo abuyisela ukukhanya kwelanga ebhodweni elimnyama elingaphakathi kwesikhwama esibonisayo. Asibizi futhi kulula ukusenza.
- **Isitofu sesitsha esigobile (parabolic cooker):** isitsha esigobile sezibuko siqoqela ukukhanya kwelanga ebhodweni, njengengilazi ekhulisayo. Sishisa kakhulu (ngaphezu kuka-250 °C) futhi sipheka ngokushesha, kodwa kumele siphendulwe njalo ukuze silandele ilanga.

Izitofu zelanga eziningi azibizi futhi zilula, futhi zingathengwa noma zenziwe ekhaya. Uma usunaso, asisebenzisi zinto zokubasa futhi asikubizi lutho ukusisebenzisa.

Amacebiso okupheka ngelanga:

1. Pheka phakathi nemini, cishe phakathi kuka-10 ekuseni no-3 ntambama.
2. Sebenzisa amabhodwe amnyama, anodonga oluncane, anezivalo ezivala kahle.
3. Phendula isitofu sibheke elangeni cishe njalo ngehora.
4. Nika isikhathi esengeziwe kunasesitofini esivamile.

> **Ukuphepha:** Izitofu zesitsha esigobile ziqoqela ukukhanya kwelanga endaweni eyodwa eshisa kakhulu. Ungalokothi ubheke ngaphakathi kwesitsha sezibuko, futhi ugqoke izibuko zelanga uma usisebenzisa. Gcina izingane kude, futhi ugcine utshani obomile namaphepha kude nendawo lapho kuqoqelwa khona ukukhanya. Sebenzisa amagilavu kahhavini: amabhodwe nezivalo kushisa kakhulu.

> **Ukuphepha:** Ukudla okuphekwe esitofini selanga kumele kushise ngempela (ngaphezu kuka-60 °C, futhi inyama nobhontshisi kumele kuvuthwe kuze kube ngaphakathi). Ungayishiyi inyama eluhlaza esitofini ngosuku olunamafu noma sekuntambama kakhulu, lapho kufudumala kancane nje. Uma ilanga lingasekho, qedela ukupheka esitofini.

## Ukomisa ukudla ngelanga

Ukomisa kulondoloza izithelo, imifino nokusanhlamvu kuze kube yizinyanga lapho ukudla okusha kuyivelakancane. Kuyasebenza ngoba isikhunta namagciwane onakalisa ukudla akwazi ukukhula ngaphandle kwamanzi.

### Ukomisa elangeni

Ukomisa elangeni kuyindlela eshibhe futhi elula kunazo zonke. Beka izithelo noma imifino esikwe yaba izingcezu endaweni ehlanzekile elangeni izinsuku eziningana ezishisayo nezomile.

Izinkinga zokomisa elangeni ngaphandle yilezi:

- kuhamba kancane, futhi ukudla kungaba nesikhunta ngaphambi kokuba kome
- izimpukane, ezinye izinambuzane, izinyoni, izilwane, uthuli namagciwane kungafinyelela ekudleni
- imvula noma amazolo kungakumanzisa futhi

Ngakho izinga lokudla livame ukuba libi, futhi kungenzeka kungahlanzeki. Ungenza ukomisa elangeni kuphephe kakhudlwana:

1. Sebenzisa kuphela umkhiqizo omusha, ohlanzekile, osezingeni elihle. Wugeze kuqala, ugeze nezandla zakho.
2. Sika ukudla kube izingcezu ezincanyana, ezilinganayo.
3. Kumise esilulwini esiphakanyisiwe noma kumathileyi, hhayi phansi emhlabathini.
4. Kumboze ngenetha ehlanzekile noma ngendwangu ukuze kuvikeleke ezinambuzaneni.
5. Kungenise endlini ebusuku noma ukumboze kahle, ukuze amazolo angakumanzisi.
6. Phendula izingcezu kanye noma kabili ngosuku.

### I-solar dryer

**I-solar dryer** (isomisi selanga) yibhokisi elomisa ukudla ngokushisa kwelanga. Ishesha futhi ihlanzekile kunokomisa elangeni ngaphandle. Ingenziwa ekhaya ngezinto ezilula noma ithengwe ngentengo efanele. Ingaba ilula, njengohlaka lwebhokisi lokhuni olumbozwe ngepulasitiki ebonisayo, noma iqine kakhudlwana, njengohlaka lwensimbi olunepulasitiki ebonisayo noma ingilazi, namathileyi endwangu yomthunzi noma enetha.

Amanye ama-solar dryer adlulisa umoya ofudumele emathileyini. Amanye ayibhokisi elishisayo elingenawo umoya omningi ohambayo. Ukuhamba komoya kuyasiza: kususa umswakama.

Ukudla kome ngokwanele nini?

- **Izithelo** kufanele zizwakale njengesikhumba futhi zigoqeke ngaphandle kwamabala amanzi uma uzicindezela.
- **Imifino** kufanele iqhephuke kalula noma iqine, futhi iphuke ngomsindo noma inqekeze uma uyinyakazisa.
- **Ummbila, ubhontshisi namantongomane** kumele komiswe ngokushesha ngemva kokuvuna, kuze kube ngaphansi kuka-13% womswakama, cishe. Okusanhlamvu kufanele kuqine futhi kunqekeze, futhi akumele kuhlale kumanzi isikhathi esingaphezu kwezinsuku ezimbili noma ezintathu.

Gcina ukudla okomisiwe ezitsheni ezihlanzekile ezingangenisi moya, endaweni epholile nemnyama. Kuhlole njalo. Lahla noma yini enuka isikhunta noma ebonakala inesikhunta.

> **Ukuphepha:** Isikhunta esommbileni omanzi nasemantongomaneni singenza i-aflatoxin, ushevu olimaza isibindi futhi ongabangela umdlavuza. Ukupheka akuwubulali. Omisa okusanhlamvu namantongomane ngokushesha, ukugcine kome, futhi ulahle okusanhlamvu namantongomane anesikhunta, aguqule umbala noma ashwabene. Ungaziphi nezilwane okusanhlamvu okunesikhunta.

> **Icebiso:** Ukucwilisa izithelo ezisikiwe kujusi kalamula oxutshwe namanzi ngaphambi kokuzomisa kusiza ukugcina umbala wazo. Ukucwilisa imifino emanzini abilayo isikhashana (blanching) ngaphambi kokuyomisa kuyisiza ukuthi igcineke kangcono. Inyama nenhlanzi kudinga usawoti nezindlela ezikhethekile; landela iresiphi ehloliwe.

## Ukupheka ngokugcina ukushisa: i-wonder bag

Isitofu sokugcina ukushisa (heat-retention cooker) yisikhwama noma ibhokisi elivikelwe ekuphumeni kokushisa, eligcina ibhodwe elishisayo lishisa amahora amaningi. Esaziwa kakhulu yi-Wonderbag, eyasungulwa eNingizimu Afrika: **i-wonder bag** (isikhwama esigcina ukushisa, ukudla kuqhubeke kuzivuthela ngaphandle komlilo). Ungazenzela nebhokisi lotshani ("hay box") ngebhokisi noma ngobhasikidi ogcwaliswe ngotshani obomile, ngezingubo ezindala noma ngemicamelo.

Isebenza kanje: ubilisa ukudla esitofini, bese ufaka ibhodwe, livaliwe ngesivalo salo, ngqo esikhwameni bese usivala. Ukushisa okuvalelwe ngaphakathi kuqhubeka nokupheka ukudla kancane amahora amaningana, ngaphandle kokubasa okwengeziwe. Kungonga izinkuni eziningi, igesi, upharafini noma ugesi, futhi uma kushiswa izinto zokubasa ezimbalwa kusho intuthu encane endlini.

Indlela yokuyisebenzisa:

1. Bilisa ukudla kuze kubile ngempela bese ukuyeka kubile kancane imizuzu embalwa. Ubhontshisi, isitambu nenyama eqinile kudinga ukubila kancane isikhathi eside kuqala.
2. Gcina isivalo sivaliwe bese uhambisa ibhodwe esikhwameni ngokushesha.
3. Sebenzisa ibhodwe elingena kahle esikhwameni, neligcwele kahle. Ibhodwe eligcwele ligcina ukushisa isikhathi eside kunelingcwele uhhafu.
4. Ungasivuli isikhwama ukuhlola ukudla ngesikhathi kupheka. Njalo uma usivula, ukushisa kuyaphuma.
5. Uma ukhipha ukudla, kusafanele kushise kukhiphe umusi. Uma sekupholile kwaba kufudumele nje, kubilise futhi ngaphambi kokudla.

> **Ukuphepha:** Ukudla kumele kuhlale kungaphezu kuka-60 °C sonke isikhathi kusesikhwameni. Amagciwane abangela ukugula ngenxa yokudla (food poisoning) akhula ngokushesha phakathi kuka-5 °C no-60 °C, cishe. Ungalokothi ufake ukudla okufudumele nje esikhwameni, futhi ungashiyi ukudla esikhwameni ubusuku bonke ukuze ukudle ngakusasa.

> **Ukuphepha:** Ubhontshisi obomvu (red kidney beans) nobunye ubhontshisi obomile bunoshevu wemvelo obhujiswa kuphela ngokubilisa kahle. Cwilisa ubhontshisi obomile, bese ububilisa ngamandla okungenani imizuzu eyi-10 ngaphambi kokubufaka esikhwameni. Ukushisa okuncane ngaphandle kwalokho kubila ngamandla kuqala kungenza ushevu ube namandla kakhulu.

## Ama-rocket stove

I-rocket stove yisitofu sezinkuni esisebenzisa izinkuni ezimbalwa: sishisa izinti ezincane zezinkuni kahle kakhulu. Umlilo uvutha ngaphakathi koshimula (chimney) omise okuka-L, ovikelwe ekuphumeni kokushisa. Lokhu kwenza umlilo ushise kakhulu futhi uhlanzekile, futhi uthumele ukushisa ngqo phezulu ebhodweni.

Ukuhlolwa okwenziwe emakhaya kwathola ukuthi ama-rocket stove asebenzisa izinkuni ezincane ngo-30 kuya ku-50% cishe kunokupheka emlilweni ovulekile wamatshe amathathu (iziko), futhi akhipha intuthu ne-carbon monoxide encane kakhulu.

Lokhu kubalulekile empilweni. INhlangano Yezempilo Yomhlaba (World Health Organization) ilinganisela ukuthi abantu abacishe babe yi-2.1 billion, cishe ingxenye eyodwa kwezine yabantu bomhlaba, bapheka emililweni evulekile noma ezitofini ezilula ezishisa izinkuni, ubulongwe, izinsalela zezitshalo, amalahle etshe noma upharafini. Intuthu emakhaya abo yabangela ukufa kwabantu abalinganiselwa ezigidini ezingu-2.9 ngo-2021. Abesifazane nezingane ezincane, abachitha isikhathi esiningi eduze komlilo, yibo abahlupheka kakhulu.

Ama-rocket stove anobukhulu obuhlukahlukene. Amaningi ayaphatheka, uhambe nawo. Amanye akhiwa ngaphakathi ekhishini, njengesitofu esivamile, anoshimula okhipha intuthu iye ngaphandle.

Indlela yokusebenzisa i-rocket stove kahle:

1. Sebenzisa izinkuni ezomile. Izinkuni ezimanzi zenza intuthu futhi zinika ukushisa okuncane.
2. Faka izinti ezimbalwa ezincane ngesikhathi, uziqhubezele phakathi njengoba zisha.
3. Gcina imbobo yomoya engaphansi kwezinti ivulekile.
4. Beka ibhodwe lihlale kahle esitofini, kube nesikhala esincane sokuthi amagesi ashisayo ageleze alizungeze.

> **Ukuphepha:** I-rocket stove ihlanzekile kunomlilo ovulekile, kodwa isenza intuthu ne-carbon monoxide, igesi ongeke uyibone noma uyinuke futhi ebulalayo. Pheka ngaphandle noma ekhishini elingena umoya kahle elinoshimula. Ungalokothi ungenise isitofu esivuthayo, imbawula (brazier) noma umlilo wamalahle etshe ekamelweni elivaliwe ukulifudumeza, ikakhulukazi ebusuku. Njalo ebusika eNingizimu Afrika kukhona abantu abafela ebuthongweni ngale ndlela.

> **Ukuphepha:** Misa isitofu endaweni eqinile, eyisicaba, kude nezingane, utshani nanoma yini engasha. Ungalokothi usebenzise uphethiloli ukubasa umlilo. Gcina upharafini esitsheni esibhalwe kahle ukuthi yini, esinesivalo izingane ezingakwazi ukusivula; ungalokothi uwugcine ebhodleleni likakoldrinki noma lobisi. Ukugwinya upharafini kuyimbangela evame kakhulu yokudliswa ushevu ngengozi kwezingane ezincane eNingizimu Afrika.

## Ama-biogas digester

I-biogas iyigesi yokubasa eyenziwa lapho amagciwane ebolisa imfucuza ephilayo, njengomquba wezilwane, indle yabantu noma izinsalela zokudla, ethangini elivaliwe elingenawo umoya. Inqubo eyenzeka ngaphandle kwe-oxygen ibizwa ngokuthi i-anaerobic. Ithangi libizwa ngokuthi i-digester.

I-biogas ngokuvamile iyi-methane engu-50 kuya ku-70% cishe, okuyigesi evuthayo. Iningi elisele yi-carbon dioxide, kanye nezinye izigesi ezincane, kuhlanganise ne-hydrogen sulphide, enuka njengamaqanda abolile.

I-digester iguqula imfucuza ibe amandla. Igesi ingasetshenziselwa ukupheka, ukukhanyisa, noma, ezinhlelweni ezinkulu, ukwenza ugesi. Emzini, ukupheka kuyindlela ewusizo kakhulu yokuyisebenzisa. Uketshezi oluphuma ku-digester, olubizwa ngokuthi **i-slurry** noma i-digestate, luwumanyolo omuhle.

Ama-digester angathengwa esenziwe ngomumo, noma akhiwe ngezinto ezitholakala esitolo sezinto zokwakha (hardware), njengamathangi epulasitiki, amapayipi nezixhumi. Kumele angangenisi moya nhlobo.

### Yini ongayifaka ku-digester

- **Kuhle:** umquba omusha wezinkomo, wezingulube noma wezinkukhu, oxutshwe namanzi alinganayo nawo, cishe; imifino nezinsalela zokudla ezisikwe zaba zincane; indle yasendlini yangasese ku-digester eyakhelwe yona.
- **Gwema:** izinto zokhuni, utshani obomile, amathambo, ipulasitiki, insipho, i-bleach, izibulali-magciwane namanye amakhemikhali. Amakhemikhali abulala amagciwane enza igesi.

Faka kancane nsuku zonke, kunokufaka okuningi ngasikhathi sinye. Njengesiqondiso esilinganiselwe, ubulongwe benkomo eyodwa bosuku lonke bunikeza igesi eyanele ukupheka cishe ihora elilodwa elangabini elilodwa lesitofu (burner). Umndeni uvame ukudinga ubulongwe bezinkomo noma bezingulube eziningana, obuqoqwa nsuku zonke.

Amagciwane asebenza kangcono uma i-digester ifudumele, cishe ku-30 kuya ku-38 °C. Ngaphansi kuka-15 °C cishe akhiqiza igesi encane kakhulu. Ezindaweni ezinobusika obubandayo, njenge-Highveld neLesotho, gqiba i-digester emhlabathini noma uyivikele ekubandeni, noma uyimboze ngendlwana yezitshalo encane yepulasitiki, ukuze ihlale ifudumele.

> **Ukuphepha:** I-biogas iyasha futhi ingaqhuma. I-methane exutshwe nomoya iqhuma uma igesi ingu-5 kuya ku-15% emoyeni. Ungalokothi ubheme noma ubase ilangabi eduze kwe-digester, kwesikhwama segesi noma kwamapayipi. Hlola ukuvuza ngamanzi anensipho (amagwebu akhombisa lapho ivuza khona), ungalokothi uhlole ngelangabi. I-methane iyakhuphuka iqoqeke ngaphansi kophahla, ngakho sebenzisa izinto zokupheka nge-biogas ekhishini elingena umoya kahle kuphela, futhi uvale igesi kumpompi ngemva kokupheka.

> **Ukuphepha:** Ungalokothi ungene ku-digester, emgodini noma ethangini, ngisho noma lingenalutho. Kungaba ne-hydrogen sulphide, igesi enoshevu, ne-carbon dioxide, engashiyi umoya wokuphefumula. Abantu bake bafa bezama ukuhlenga abanye. Mboza imigodi yokungenisa neyokukhipha, futhi ugcine izingane kude nayo.

> **Ukuphepha:** I-slurry isenamagciwane, ikakhulukazi uma ivela endleni yabantu noma yezingulube. Yenze **i-compost** (yixube nezinsalela zezitshalo uyiyeke ibole kahle enqwabeni) noma uyiyeke ime amasonto ambalwa ngaphambi kokuyisebenzisa. Yisebenzise ezihlahleni zezithelo, emmbileni nasezitshalweni eziphekwayo. Ungayifaki emifinweni enamaqabunga noma kwezinye izitshalo ezidliwa ziluhlaza. Gqoka amagilavu futhi ugeze izandla zakho ngemva kokuyiphatha.

## Amaphuzu amqoka

- Ikhaya liyi-Zone 0, inkaba yomklamo we-permaculture.
- I-appropriate technology ilula, ayibizi, futhi ingakhiwa ilungiswe ngamakhono endawo.
- Klama indlu ngokulandela ilanga: eNingizimu ne-Afrika, amafasitela amakhulu namaphaneli elanga abheka enyakatho.
- Sebenzisa ugesi welanga ezibanini, komakhalekhukhwini nasemafrijini. Fudumeza amanzi futhi upheke ngama-geyser elanga, i-biogas, izitofu zelanga, izikhwama ezigcina ukushisa nama-rocket stove.
- Amaphaneli elanga nezinhlelo ezixhunywe kugesi ovamile kumele zifakwe ngumuntu oqeqeshelwe ugesi futhi zibhaliswe lapho kudingeka khona. Ezindaweni ezinesithwathwa, sebenzisa i-geyser yelanga engaqondile (indirect).
- Omisa ukudla ngokushesha, ngokuhlanzeka futhi kungabi phansi emhlabathini. Omisa ummbila namantongomane ngokushesha futhi ulahle okusanhlamvu okunesikhunta.
- Esikhwameni sokugcina ukushisa, qala ubilise ukudla futhi ukugcine kungaphezu kuka-60 °C. Bilisa ubhontshisi obomile ngamandla imizuzu eyi-10 kuqala.
- Ama-rocket stove onga izinkuni futhi anciphisa intuthu, kodwa ungalokothi ubase noma yimuphi umlilo ekamelweni elivaliwe.
- I-biogas iyaqhuma futhi imigodi yayo ingabulala: akukho malangabi, hlola ukuvuza ngensipho, ungalokothi ungene ku-digester, futhi ungasebenzisi i-slurry emifinweni edliwa iluhlaza.
