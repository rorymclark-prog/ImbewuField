# Ukuhlela ipulazi lakho noma umuzi wakho

Ungatshala ukudla cishe noma kuphi. Kodwa ingadi esendaweni engafanele ikulahlekisela nsuku zonke: izinyathelo ezengeziwe uya empompini, amabhakede engeziwe ekhuphuka igquma, umhlabathi okhukhuleka ngaso sonke isiphepho. Umklamo omuhle wonga isikhathi, imali namandla, futhi ipulazi livunda kakhulu unyaka nonyaka.

I-permaculture iyindlela yokuklama. Iveza konke umhlaba ongakwenza futhi ihlangabezane nezidingo zezitshalo, izilwane, amanzi, izakhiwo nabantu ndawonye. Abalimi abancane abacebe kakhulu akuvamile ukuba yilabo abanomhlaba omkhulu.

**Ekupheleni kwalesi sahluko uzokwazi:**

- Ukudweba imephu eyisisekelo yomhlaba wakho nokumaka lokho osekukhona
- Ukufunda indawo yakho ngokubheka amanzi, umthambeka, uhlangothi obheke kulo nezindlela zokufinyelela
- Ukuhlukanisa umhlaba wakho ube **ama-zone** (izindawo ezihlelwe ngokuthi uzivakashela kangaki), ukuze izinto ozivakashela nsuku zonke zibe seduze nekhaya
- Ukusebenzisa umthambeka ukuze amandla okudonsela phansi akuthuthele amanzi, i-compost nomquba

Ukuhlela ama-sector, isinyathelo sesine, kulandela esahlukweni esilandelayo.

## Isinyathelo 1: Ukuhlola indawo

Iphutha elisephepheni alibizi lutho ukulilungisa. Iphutha elisemhlabathini libiza umjuluko. Ngakho funda umhlaba ngaphambi kokuwushintsha.

### Qoqa ulwazi futhi ubheke

Thola ukuthi umhlaba wawusetshenziswa kanjani ngaphambilini; imvula yawo, isithwathwa, ukushisa nomoya; yini ekuwo noma eseduze kwawo (amanzi, umquba, i-mulch, izinto zokwakha, abasizi); nalapho ungathengisa khona lokho okutshalayo.

Bese uhamba umhlaba kaningi, ngengqondo evulekile, futhi ungasheshi ukunquma. Bheka:

- lapho amanzi emvula egeleza khona ngemva kwesiphepho, nalapho ebuthana khona
- ukuthi izihlahla zitsheke kanjani noma zilolongwe kanjani ngumoya
- yiziphi izilwane ezinqamula umhlaba
- yiziphi izindawo ezizwakala zifudumele, zibanda, zimanzi noma zomile.

Yonke inkathi yonyaka nesiphepho ngasinye kukufundisa okuthile okusha.

### Dweba imephu eyisisekelo

**Imephu eyisisekelo** umdwebo womhlaba wakho ubonwa ngaphezulu. Khombisa:

- imingcele, uthango, imigwaqo, izindlela namasango
- indlu, amashede, izibaya nezinye izakhiwo
- ukuthi ingxenye ngayinye isetshenziswa kanjani manje: insimu, idlelo noma ingadi
- amanzi: iziphethu, imifudlana, imithombo ejulile, amathangi, amapompi nezindawo ezimanzi
- izihlahla ezinkulu nezindawo ezinamatshe
- imigqa **ye-contour** uma unayo. Umugqa we-contour uxhumanisa amaphuzu asekuphakameni okufanayo.

Landela ngepensela isithombe samahhala sesathelayithi (isibonelo esivela ku-Google Earth), noma ngesiqeshana esincane ulinganise ngetheyiphu bese uzidwebela eyakho. Amamephu kahulumeni e-topographic angu-1:50 000 ahlanganisa yonke iNingizimu Afrika, anemigqa ye-contour ehlukene ngo-20 m: akhombisa ukuma okukhulu komhlaba, hhayi imigodi emincane. Dweba ngesilinganiso, isibonelo u-1 cm ephepheni ku-1 m emhlabathini.

## Okufanele ukubheke endaweni yakho

### Amanzi

Amanzi yinto yokuqala okufanele uyihlele (isahluko 7 sikhuluma ngawo ngokugcwele).

1. Thola imvula yendawo yakho. Iningi leNingizimu Afrika lithola imvula ehlobo; iNtshonalanga Kapa iyithola ebusika.
2. Bheka **i-catchment**: umhlaba ongaphezu kowakho othululela amanzi emhlabeni wakho.
3. Bheka **i-runoff**, amanzi emvula ageleza phezu komhlaba esikhundleni sokungena, nezimpawu zayo ezindala: iziwa, izindawo ezingenalutho nomhlabathi okhukhuliwe. I-runoff ithwala umhlabathi nezakhamzimba ozifakile.
4. Maka indawo yokugcina amanzi onayo, izindawo lapho kungaba khona amathangi, amachibi noma amadamu, nemithombo yakho yamanzi eyelekelelayo: umthombo ojulile, umfula, isiphethu noma umpompi.
5. Maka izindawo ezomile kakhulu nezimanzi kakhulu, bese uhlela ukuthi uzonisela kanjani izithombo ngomzamo omncane kakhulu.

### Ukuma komhlaba nomthambeka

Indawo engcono kakhulu umthambeka omnene, ophuma amanzi kahle, obheke ilanga. Umhlaba ongumqansa unzima ukuwusebenza futhi uhlaselwa **i-erosion**: umhlabathi okhukhulwa ngamanzi.

Umthambeka uvame ukunikezwa njengephesenti: ukwehla ngaphezu kwama-100 m. Ukwehla ngo-1 m ngaphezu kwe-10 m kungumthambeka ongu-10%.

| Umthambeka | Ukwehla | I-engeli | Okushiwo yikho |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5% | 1 m ku-20 m | cishe ku-3° | Kulula ezingadini nasemasimini |
| 12% | 1 m cishe ku-8 m | cishe ku-7° | Vikela umhlabathi ngemibhede ye-contour, imigqa yotshani noma ama-terrace |
| 32% | 1 m cishe ku-3 m | cishe ku-18° | Gcina ngaphansi kwezihlahla noma utshani obuhlala njalo |

UBill Mollison, omunye wabasunguli be-permaculture, weluleka ukuthi imithambeka ewumqansa ngaphezu kwama-degree angaba ngu-18 igcinwe ngaphansi kwezihlahla ezihlala njalo. Khumbula lokhu uma ukhetha umhlaba ozowuthenga noma owuqasha.

### Uhlangothi umhlaba obheke kulo

**Uhlangothi obheke kulo** (aspect) yindlela umthambeka noma isakhiwo esibheke ngayo. ENingizimu Afrika ilanga lisenyakatho, ngakho:

- **Imithambeka ebheke enyakatho nasempumalanga** ithola ilanga kakhulu futhi ifudumala ekuseni. Ingcono kakhulu ezitshalweni eziningi.
- **Imithambeka ebheke entshonalanga** ithola ilanga elishisayo lantambama, elingashisa izitshalo ehlobo.
- **Imithambeka ebheke eningizimu** ipholile futhi inomthunzi ebusika. Ifanele izihlahla, futhi ezindaweni ezomile ihlala imanzi isikhathi eside.

Awukho umsebenzi ongashintsha lapho ilanga liphuma khona. Hlola lapho izihlahla nezakhiwo ziphonsa khona imithunzi yazo; imithunzi yasebusika mide kakhulu (isahluko 3).

### Izindlela zokufinyelela

Imigwaqo nezindlela cishe zihlala unomphela. Yenza ezintsha zilandele i-contour. Indlela esemgqeni we-contour ingaba udonga lwe-**swale**, umsele owembiwa ngokulandela i-contour ukuze ubambe amanzi emvula (isahluko 7). Nika indlela ngayinye ukwehla okuncane kakhulu, ukuze imvula igobhozeke iye ku-swale noma esitsheni sesihlahla esikhundleni sokugijima yehla ngendlela.

## Isinyathelo 2: Ukuhlela ama-zone

Ibanga ohamba ngalo linquma ukuthi yini oyinakekelayo. Ingadi esekugcineni iyakhohlwa; ingadi eseduze komnyango wekhishi iyaniselwa. Ukuhlela ama-zone kuhlukanisa ipulazi libe ama-zone ngokuthi uwavakashela kangaki: izinto zansuku zonke eduze kwendlu, izinto ezingavakashelwa kaningi kude.

| I-zone | Iyini | Uyivakashela kangaki | Izibonelo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Indlu | Ngaso sonke isikhathi | Ikhishi, isitolo sembewu, amathangi asendlini |
| 1 | Eduze kakhulu nendlu | Nsuku zonke, kaningi | Ingadi yasekhaya, amakhambi, indawo yezithombo, i-compost, izilwane ezincane |
| 2 | Eduze nendlu | Izinsuku eziningi | I-food forest, izihlahla zezithelo, izinkukhu, izitshalo eziyisisekelo |
| 3 | Indawo enkulu yokulima | Masonto onke, noma uma kudingeka | Izitshalo zasensimini nezemakethe, ingadi yezithelo, amadlelo, amadamu |
| 4 | Indawo esendle kancane, enakekelwa kancane | Ngezikhathi ezithile | Ihlathi lezinkuni, amadlelo |
| 5 | Endle | Kuyaqabukela, ukubuka nokufunda | Idlelo lemvelo noma ihlathi |

**I-Zone 1** inika isivuno esikhulu kakhulu endaweni encane kakhulu. Beka lapha imifino eyi-**annual** (ephila isizini eyodwa) nemifino oyikha kaningi, amakhambi asekhishini, izihlahla zezithelo ezimfushane, izilwane ezincane njengezigwaca nonogwaja, nezindawo ezincane zokugcina amanzi. Mboza wonke amathangi nemithombo, futhi ubiyele noma umboze amachibi, ukuze kungabikho ngane engawela kuwo.

**I-Zone 2** inkulu futhi ayidingi ukunakekelwa okungaka. **I-food forest** yayo, ingadi etshalwe ngezingqimba njengehlathi lemvelo, ingafaka izihlahla ze-**coppicing**: ukusika isihlahla phansi sibe yisiqu esifushane ukuze sihlume izigxobo ezintsha. Ukuze uthole izinkuni nezigxobo, tshala umunga (*Vachellia karroo*), isihlahla somdabu (esikhula ngokwemvelo lapha), esikhundleni sowatela noma ugamthilini, asabalala endle nasemifudlaneni.

**I-Zone 3** iphethe imfuyo, izitshalo zasensimini namadamu asepulazini. **I-Zone 4** inika amadlelo, izinkuni nezingodo. **I-Zone 5** ngumhlaba wemvelo oshiywe unjalo, isiphephelo sezinyoni nezinambuzane ezisiza ukulawula izinambuzane ezilimazayo. Yivakashele ukuze ubuke, ufunde futhi uqoqe ukudla kwasendle ngokucophelela.

Akuyona yonke indawo enendawo yawo wonke ama-zone. Isiza sasedolobheni esingaba ngamamitha-skwele ayi-1 000 (ingxenye yesine ye-acre) sinendawo ye-Zone 1 nezingxenye zamanye; ikhona elishiyelwe izinyoni nezinambuzane liyi-Zone 5 encane. Emhlabeni womphakathi, ama-Zone 4 no-5 angaba ngamadlelo noma indle yomphakathi. Futhi ama-zone awayona imibuthano emihle: umthambeka, amanzi nezindlela kuyawagoba abe nezinye izimo.

## Isinyathelo 3: Ukuhlela umthambeka

Amanzi ageleza ehla mahhala. Ukuhlela umthambeka, ngokwesibonelo sikaBill Mollison, kusho ukubheka umhlaba wakho ngasohlangothini bese uzibuza: ngimi kuphi egqumeni? Sebenzisa umthambeka kahle, amandla okudonsela phansi azokuthwalela.

- **Amanzi phezulu.** Beka amadamu namathangi ngaphezu kwendlu nengadi, bese amandla okudonsela phansi ethutha amanzi ngaphandle kwepompo. Wonke amamitha ayi-10 okuphakama anika cishe i-bar engu-1 yamandla okucindezela.
- **Izihlahla emithambekeni ewumqansa engaphezulu** zinciphisa ijubane le-runoff, zisize amanzi angene futhi zibambe umhlabathi.
- **Imibhede, izindlela nama-swale ku-contour** kugcina amanzi emhlabeni.
- **Izinqwaba ze-compost nezindawo zezilwane zivuleka zibheke phansi,** ukuze i-compost nomquba kwehlele engadini.
- **Amanzi okuphuza ahlala ehlanzekile.** Gcina umquba, i-compost nezibaya zezilwane ngezansi kwemithombo ejulile, imithombo neziphethu, futhi kude nazo.
- **I-greywater yehlela ezihlahleni.** **I-greywater**, amanzi asetshenzisiwe okugeza, ombhavu nawezitsha zokugeza izandla, igeleza ngamandla okudonsela phansi iye ezitsheni zezihlahla ezine-mulch noma kuma-swale angezansi kwendlu, ingalokothi iye emifinweni noma kweminye imifino oyidla iluhlaza (isahluko 7).

## Zama lokhu

Qala imephu yakho eyisisekelo kuleli sonto.

1. Thola isithombe sesathelayithi somhlaba wakho esiphrintiwe, noma ishidi elikhulu lephepha. Maka inyakatho.
2. Hamba umngcele. Dweba imiphetho, izakhiwo, izindlela, amapompi, amathangi nezihlahla ezinkulu.
3. Ngemva kwemvula enhle elandelayo, phinde uhambe umhlaba. Maka ngemicibisholo lapho amanzi egeleza khona nalapho ebuthana khona.
4. Maka izindawo ezifudumele, ezibandayo, ezimanzi nezomile.
5. Usuku olulodwa, bala lonke uhambo olwenzayo uya endaweni ngayinye: empompini, ezinkukhwini, engadini, enqwabeni yezinkuni.
6. Ngepensela, dweba ama-zone alinganiselwe phezu kwemephu. Ingabe indawo oyivakashela kakhulu iseduze kakhulu nendlu?

## Amaphuzu amqoka

- Buka umhlaba wakho kuzo zonke izinkathi zonyaka, bese ubeka lokho okubonayo emephini eyisisekelo.
- Funda indawo yakho ngokubheka amanzi, umthambeka, uhlangothi obheke kulo nezindlela zokufinyelela. Imithambeka ebheke enyakatho nasempumalanga yiyona efudumele kakhulu.
- Ama-Zone 0 kuya ku-5 abeka izinto ngokuthi uzivakashela kangaki.
- Beka amanzi phezulu bese uvumela amandla okudonsela phansi awathuthe. Gcina umquba kude namanzi okuphuza, ne-greywater kude nemifino edliwa iluhlaza.

Gcina imephu yakho odongeni bese wengeza kuyo isizini ngayinye. Eminyakeni embalwa izoba yincwadi ewusizo kakhulu ngomhlaba wakho.
