# Imifino nezitshalo eziyisisekelo

Umndeni udla nsuku zonke, ngakho ingadi kufanele iwondle nsuku zonke. Okunye ukudla sikukha isandla ngesandla: imifino, iparsley, isitsha sikabhontshisi. Okunye sikuvuna kanye ngesizini bese siyakugcina: ummbila owomile, ubhontshisi, ubhatata.

Uhlobo ngalunye lokudla ludinga ingadi yalo, endaweni yalo. Beka ngayinye lapho ifanele khona, futhi lonke ipulazi lizosebenza ngomzamo omncane.

**Ekupheleni kwalesi sahluko uzokwazi:**

- Ukubeka izingadi zakho ukuze zihambisane nesimo sezulu sakho futhi izitshalo zingangeni esithwathweni
- Ukulinganisa ubukhulu bengadi yakho nesikhathi nemali onakho ngempela
- Ukuhlela ingadi yasekhaya, ingadi yemifino, ingadi yezimakethe nengadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo
- Ukutshala ummbila, amabele, ubhontshisi owomile, ubhatata nomdumbula ngesikhathi esifanele nangebanga elifanele

## Ukutshala ukudla ezimweni zezulu zaseNingizimu ne-Afrika

Alikho ikhalenda elilodwa lokutshala lapha. Yazi isimo sezulu sakho:

- **Phakathi nezwe, lapho imvula ina ehlobo** (i-Highveld, iFreistata, ingxenye enkulu yeMpumalanga Kapa, amaphakathi aKwaZulu-Natal, iLesotho): imvula ina cishe kusukela kuMfumfu kuya kuNdasa; ubusika bomile futhi buyabanda, buvame ukuba nesithwathwa.
- **INtshonalanga Kapa, lapho imvula ina ebusika**: imvula ina cishe kusukela kuNhlaba kuya kuMandulo. Izitshalo zasehlobo zidinga ukuniselwa.
- **I-lowveld nogu olungenasithwathwa** (ugu lwaKwaZulu-Natal, i-lowveld yaseLimpopo neyaseMpumalanga): uma unamanzi, ungatshala cishe unyaka wonke.

Ezindaweni ezibandayo, isithwathwa siyingozi enkulu. Umoya obandayo uyasinda. Ngobusuku obuzolile nobucwebile ugeleza wehle egqumeni njengamanzi bese uqoqeka ezindaweni eziphansi ezibizwa ngokuthi **ama-frost pocket** (izindawo eziphansi lapho kuqoqeka khona umoya obandayo nesithwathwa). Sebenzisana nawo:

1. Shiya igebe ngezansi ezindongeni nasothangweni ohlangothini olungaphezulu, ukuze umoya obandayo uqhubeke ugeleza udlule uhambe.
2. Tshala izitshalo ezizwela isithwathwa phezulu emthambekeni, ungalokothi uzitshale phansi esigodini.
3. Sebenzisa imithambeka ebheke enyakatho ezitshalweni zokuqala nezokugcina. Ithola ilanga elikhulu kakhulu lasebusika.
4. Yelula isizini ngethanele likapulasitiki, elishibhile kunengilazi. Uma lakhiwe odongeni lwendlu olusenyakatho, lisiza nokufudumeza indlu. Abanye abalimi bagcina izinkukhu ezimbalwa ekhoneni elibiyelwe lalo ebusika ukuze kufudumale futhi bathole umquba; zinike umoya omusha omningi, ngoba umquba ukhipha i-ammonia.

## Hlela ngokuya ngempilo onayo

Ingadi enkulu ongakwazi ukuyinakekela ngokushesha iba yingadi egcwele ukhula, echithekile. Ngaphambi kokuba wembe, tshela iqiniso ngesikhathi **sakho**, **imali** nentshisekelo **yakho**.

1. Yenza **ukuhlola indlela ophila ngayo** (lifestyle audit) okulula. Bhala ukuthi mangaki amahora ngesonto ongawanika ingadi, yini oyifunayo kuyo (ukudla, imali noma kokubili) nokuthi ungasebenzisa malini.
2. Uma isikhathi sincane, kwenze kube lula. Tshala ama-**perennial** amaningi, izitshalo eziphila iminyaka, njengezihlahla zezithelo, amakhambi ne-asparagus. Yonga umsebenzi nge-**drip irrigation** (amapayipi aconsisa amanzi kancane esitshalweni ngasinye), nesibali-sikhathi esifakwa empompini (tap timer).
3. Uma ufuna imali futhi unesikhathi namandla, yenza kube kukhulu futhi kujule.

Izingadi ezine ezingezansi zilandela ama-**zone** e-permaculture: izingxenye zepulazi ozivakashela kakhulu ziseduze kakhulu nendlu.

## 1. Ingadi yasekhaya (i-Zone 1)

Ingadi yasekhaya iku-**Zone 1**, izinyathelo ezimbalwa ukusuka emnyango wekhishi. Uyidlula kaningi ngosuku, ngakho kha kuyo kaningi ngosuku.

Tshala lokho okusebenzisa nsuku zonke:

- imifino namaqabunga esaladi ongawasika **aphinde ahlume** (kha amaqabunga ambalwa bese uyeka isitshalo siphinde sihlume): isipinashi, i-Swiss chard, ulethisi
- ubhontshisi nophizi
- utamatisi omncane (cherry)
- amakhambi okupheka: iparsley, i-coriander, u-anyanisi oluhlaza, i-thyme

Iningi lawo ngama-**annual**, izitshalo eziphila isizini eyodwa, kanye namakhambi ambalwa aphila iminyaka.

Tshala uminyanise. Sebenzisa yonke indawo, futhi utshale nangokuphakama, ubhontshisi othangweni. Ngemitha-skwele le yingadi yakho ekhiqiza kakhulu, ngakho idinga ukunakekelwa kakhulu: ukuhlwanyela, ukuhlakula, ukufaka **i-mulch** (ukumboza umhlabathi ngezinto zezitshalo ezomile), ukondla umhlabathi nokucosha izinambuzane.

Yenze ibe nhle. Incane futhi iseduze kwendlu, ngakho zinike isikhathi sokubiyela imibhede ngezingodo, izitini ezindala noma amatshe, bese umboza izindlela ngamatshana noma ngamazenge ezihlahla.

## 2. Ingadi yemifino (i-Zone 2)

Ingadi yemifino inkulu. Iphethe izitshalo ezidinga indawo enkulu noma ezikhula isikhathi eside: iklabishi, ubhitrudi, i-leek, u-anyanisi, upelepele, utamatisi ne-okra. Uyivakashela kanye ngosuku noma njalo ezinsukwini ezimbalwa, ngakho iba kude kancane, ku-**Zone 2**. Amathanele asekhaya nezindlu ezinenetha lomthunzi nazo zingezalapha.

1. Hlela imibhede ecacile enezindlela phakathi kwayo. Emthambekeni, yenza imibhede nezindlela zilandele **i-contour**, umugqa onqamula umthambeka ekuphakameni okukodwa, ukuze amanzi angene esikhundleni sokugeleza ahambe.
2. Tshala izitshalo eziningi ndawonye. Engeza ichibi elincane lamaxoxo nezimbali ezondla izinambuzane eziwusizo (Isahluko 10).
3. Ungayibiyeli. Lolonga imibhede kabusha ngesandla izikhathi ezimbalwa ngonyaka.

Umsebenzi uqhubeka njalo: ukuhlola amapayipi aconsisayo, ukufaka i-mulch, ukwengeza **i-compost** (izinsalela zezitshalo nezilwane ezibolile), ukuhlakula nokuhlwanyela.

## 3. Ukulima imifino okuncane kwezohwebo (i-Zone 3)

Ingadi yemifino etshalwa ikakhulu ukuze kuthengiswe iyingadi yezimakethe. Kusukela cishe ku-0.4 hectare (i-acre eyodwa) kuya phezulu, ingeye-**Zone 3**, indawo enkulu yokulima.

1. Tshala isitshalo esisodwa embhedeni ngamunye. Ukuhlwanyela, ukuhlakula nokuvuna kuhamba ngokushesha. Shintsha isitshalo embhedeni ngamunye isizini ngayinye (Isahluko 10).
2. Tshala uminyanise emibhedeni ejulile efakwe i-compost kahle. Ukutshala uminyanise kunika ukudla okuningi emitheni-skwele futhi kuvimbe ukhula ngomthunzi.
3. Sebenzisa amathuluzi onga isikhathi: umshini wokuhlwanyela ophushwayo ne-**broadfork**, imfologo ebanzi enezibambo ezimbili exegisa umhlabathi ngaphandle kokuwuphendula. Ipulazi elikhulu lingengeza ugandaganda wamasondo amabili ohanjwa ngemuva kwawo.

Izingadi zezimakethe ziyasebenza ngoba abathengi bafuna ukudla okusha okutshalwe eduze. Thengisela ngqo amakhasimende, esangweni lepulazi, emakethe, ezikoleni, ezitolo ze-spaza noma ezindaweni zokudlela, bese uthola intengo yokuthengisa ngqo (retail), hhayi intengo ephansi yokuthengisa ngobuningi (wholesale) ekhokhwa ngumhwebi. Izindleko zokuqala ziphansi, futhi imikhiqizo ehlukahlukene (imifino, izithelo, amaqanda, uju, ujamu) igcina imali ingena.

> **Icebiso:** Khuluma nabathengi bakho ngaphambi kokuthi utshale. Thola ukuthi bafunani, kangakanani, kangaki nangentengo engakanani. Tshalela imakethe oyaziyo, hhayi leyo oyithembayo.

## 4. Ingadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo (i-Zone 3)

**Izitshalo eziyisisekelo** ngukudla okugcwalisa ipuleti lomndeni izinsuku eziningi. Okusanhlamvu, amazambane nezinye izimpande kunika amandla. Ubhontshisi namanye **ama-legume**, umndeni kabhontshisi, anika i-protein.

Izitshalo eziyisisekelo zidinga indawo enkulu ukuze zinike isivuno esimaphakathi, zithatha izinyanga ukuvuthwa futhi zivunwa zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa, ngakho ziya ku-Zone 3. Ingadi yazo idinga umsebenzi omncane futhi ikhula ikakhulu ngemvula, ngakho khetha izitshalo ezifanele isimo sezulu sakho bese uzitshala ukuze zibambe imvula.

| Iqembu | Izitshalo |
|---|---|
| Okusanhlamvu | ummbila, amabele, unyawothi; ukolweni, i-oats nebhali (ikakhulu izitshalo zasebusika) |
| Ubhontshisi namanye ama-legume | ubhontshisi we-sugar nobunye ubhontshisi owomile, ama-cowpea, izindlubu, amantongomane, i-soya, i-pigeon pea, ubhontshisi we-mung, ama-chickpea |
| Izimpande nama-tuber | amazambane, ubhatata, amadumbe, umdumbula (ezindaweni ezingenasithwathwa kuphela) |
| Imifino edinga indawo enkulu | amathanga, i-butternut, amakhabe, ama-melon namaselwa |

### Ummbila

1. Ezindaweni ezithola imvula ehlobo, tshala ngemva kokuba imvula enhle isimanzisile umhlabathi futhi isithwathwa sesiphelile, ngokuvamile kusukela kuMfumfu kuya kuZibandlela.
2. Tshala ngemigqa ehlukene ngo-90 cm. Ezindaweni ezomile shiya cishe u-50 cm phakathi kwezitshalo emgqeni, ukuze ngasinye sibe namanzi anele. Uma kunemvula enhle, ukunisela noma umhlabathi ovundile, zitshale zihlukene ngo-30 cm.
3. Khetha imbewu **evulekele impova** (open-pollinated) uma ufuna ukulondoloza eyakho. Imbewu ye-hybrid ikhiqiza kahle okokuqala, kodwa imbewu elondolozwe esivunweni se-hybrid ikhiqiza cishe ingxenye yesithathu kancane.

### Amabele

Amabele amelana nokushisa nesomiso kangcono kunommbila. Lapho amanzi eyindlala, awatshale ngemigqa ebanzi, ehlukene ngo-0.9 m kuya ku-2.3 m kuye ngemvula nomhlabathi, ukuze isitshalo ngasinye sibe nomhlabathi omningi sokuphuza kuwo.

### Ubhontshisi owomile, ama-cowpea nezindlubu

1. Tshala ubhontshisi owomile ngemva kwemvula yokuqala enhle, lapho umhlabathi usushisa ngaphezu kuka-15 °C: kusukela maphakathi noMfumfu kuya kuZibandlela ezindaweni eziningi ezithola imvula ehlobo, kuze kube cishe maphakathi noMasingana lapho kunesithwathwa khona.
2. Butshale eceleni noma phakathi kommbila. Bondla umhlabathi nge-nitrogen.
3. Ezindaweni ezishisayo nezomile, tshala ama-cowpea nezindlubu, ezimelana kahle nesomiso.

### Ubhatata

1. Sika izingcezu zemicu ezingu-20 kuya ku-30 cm ubude ezitshalweni eziphilile.
2. Zitshale emigqeni ephakanyisiwe ehlukene ngo-1 m, zihlukene ngo-30 cm emgqeni.
3. Lapho kunesithwathwa, tshala kusukela maphakathi noLwezi kuya ekuqaleni kukaZibandlela. Ezindaweni ezingenasithwathwa, tshala kusukela kuNcwaba kuya kuNdasa.

Tshala uhlobo olunenyama ewolintshi. Lunothe nge-vitamin A futhi lulungele izingane.

### Umdumbula

Umdumbula ukhula kuphela ezindaweni ezifudumele ezingenasithwathwa, njengogu lwaKwaZulu-Natal ne-lowveld, futhi umelana kahle nesomiso uma usuqinile. Izimpande zawo zikhipha i-cyanide, ubuthi, ngakho asilokothi siwudle uluhlaza: hlubula izimpande bese uzipheka kahle, futhi izinhlobo ezibabayo uziqale ngokuzicwilisa emanzini izinsuku eziningana, ulahle lawo manzi.

### Izimiso zengadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo

1. **Xuba izitshalo.** Tshala ummbila nobhontshisi namathanga, hhayi isitshalo esisodwa sodwa.
2. **Phazamisa umhlabathi kancane ngangokunokwenzeka.**
3. **Shiya izinhlanga namaqabunga phezu komhlabathi njenge-mulch.** Kuvikela umhlabathi futhi kuwondle njengoba kubola.
4. **Tshala izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi** phakathi kwezitshalo ezinkulu.
5. **Tshala ku-contour** ukuze imvula ingene futhi umhlabathi uhlale endaweni yawo.
6. **Shintshanisa izitshalo** (i-crop rotation) ukuze singabuyi isitshalo endaweni efanayo unyaka nonyaka.
7. **Phumuza umhlaba** ngaphansi kwesitshalo sokumboza umhlabathi noma idlelo ngemva kwezinkathi ezimbalwa.

## Zama lokhu

Dweba izingadi zakho ezine kuleli sonto.

1. Dweba indlu yakho nomhlaba oyizungezile ephepheni.
2. Yima emnyango wekhishi bese uhamba izinyathelo eziyi-10. Maka indawo engcono kakhulu yengadi yasekhaya ngaphakathi kwaleli banga.
3. Maka indawo yengadi yemifino, eseduze ukuhamba futhi iseduze namanzi.
4. Maka indawo yezitshalo eziyisisekelo, bese ubhala ukuthi umhlaba uthambeke ngakuphi.
5. Kusihlwa okuzolile nokubandayo, zwa lapho umoya obandayo uhlala khona. Maka leyo ndawo, i-frost pocket yakho.
6. Eceleni kwengadi ngayinye, bhala amahora ngesonto ongayinika wona.

## Amaphuzu amqoka

- Yazi isimo sezulu sakho, futhi uvumele umoya obandayo ugeleze usuke ezitshalweni ezithambile.
- Linganisa izingadi zakho nesikhathi, imali nentshisekelo onakho ngempela.
- Ingadi yasekhaya iseduze futhi iminyene; ingadi yemifino iku-contour; ingadi yezimakethe inemibhede yesitshalo esisodwa, ithengisela ngqo.
- Tshala izitshalo eziyisisekelo ukuze zibambe imvula, futhi ungalokothi udle umdumbula uluhlaza.
- Xuba izitshalo, phazamisa umhlabathi kancane, faka i-mulch, shintshanisa izitshalo futhi uphumuze umhlaba.

Qala ngengadi eseduze komnyango wekhishi. Uma isikondla nsuku zonke, uzokwazi ukuthi usulungele elandelayo nini.
