# Imifino nezitshalo eziyisisekelo

## Ukulima ukudla ezimweni zezulu zaseNingizimu ne-Afrika

INingizimu Afrika inezimo zezulu eziningi ezehlukene, ngakho ayikho ikhalenda yokutshala eyodwa esebenza yonke indawo.

- **Ingaphakathi lezwe elinemvula yasehlobo** (iHighveld, iFree State, ingxenye enkulu yeMpumalanga Kapa, iNdawo Ephakeme yaKwaZulu-Natal (Midlands), iLesotho): iningi lemvula lina kusukela cishe ngo-Okthoba kuya kuMashi. Ubusika bomile futhi bubanda, futhi ezindaweni eziningi kuba **nesithwathwa**. Inkathi enkulu yokulima yintwasahlobo nehlobo.
- **INtshonalanga Kapa enemvula yasebusika**: iningi lemvula lina kusukela cishe ngoMeyi kuya kuSepthemba. Ihlobo liyashisa futhi lomile, ngakho izitshalo zasehlobo zidinga ukuniselwa.
- **Izindawo eziphansi nogu olungenaso isithwathwa** (isibonelo ugu lwaKwaZulu-Natal nezindawo eziphansi (lowveld) zaseLimpopo naseMpumalanga): ungatshala izitshalo eziningi cishe unyaka wonke uma unamanzi.

Ezindaweni ezibanda kakhulu inkathi yokulima imfushane, futhi isithwathwa sasebusika siyingozi enkulu. Uma uklama izingadi zakho zokudla, cabanga ngalokhu okulandelayo.

### Vikela izitshalo esithwathwa

Umoya obandayo usinda. Ebusuku obuthule nobucwebile ugeleza wehle emthambekeni njengamanzi, uqoqane ezindaweni eziphansi. Lezi zindawo zibizwa ngokuthi **ama-frost pocket** (izindawo eziphansi lapho kuqoqeka khona umoya obandayo nesithwathwa).

- Ungawuvaleli umoya obandayo ngezindonga, izakhiwo noma uthango oluphilayo oluminyene ohlangothini oluphezulu lwengadi yakho. Shiya isikhala phansi ukuze umoya obandayo uqhubeke ugeleza wehle udlule.
- Tshala izitshalo ezingasibekezeleli isithwathwa phezulu emthambekeni, hhayi phansi esigodini.
- Imithambeka ebheke enyakatho ithola ilanga elikhulu kakhulu ebusika, futhi iyizindawo ezifudumele kakhulu zezitshalo zakuqala nezakamuva.

### Yelula inkathi yokulima

Ithanele lepulasitiki noma indlu yezitshalo (greenhouse) igcina izitshalo zifudumele futhi ikuvumela ukuthi utshale isikhathi eside ekwindla nasebusika. Ingilazi ibiza kakhulu; ipulasitiki ishibhile.

- Ithanele noma indlu yezitshalo eyakhelwe odongeni olubheke enyakatho lwendlu igcina ukushisa kwelanga, futhi ingasiza ukugcina indlu ifudumele nayo ebusika.
- Abanye abalimi bagcina izinkukhu ezimbalwa engxenyeni ehlukile, ebiyelwe, yethanele ebusika. Ukufudumala kwemizimba yazo kwengeza ukufudumala okuncane, futhi umquba wazo wondla umhlabathi. Gcina indawo ingena umoya omningi, ngoba umquba wezinkukhu ukhipha i-ammonia (igesi enuka kabi), elimaza kokubili izinyoni nezitshalo.

## Hlela ngokuya ngempilo onayo

Ngaphambi kokuthi uqale, qiniseka ukuthi lokho okwakhayo yikho ngempela okufunayo. Yiba neqiniso ngokuthi **isikhathi**, **imali** **nentshisekelo** onakho ngokulima kungakanani.

1. Yenza **ukuhlola indlela ophila ngayo** okulula (lifestyle audit). Bhala phansi ukuthi ungayinika ingadi amahora amangaki ngesonto, ukuthi ufunani kuyo (ukudla komndeni, imali, noma kokubili) nokuthi ungayisebenzisa malini.
2. Uma unesikhathi esincane, gcina uhlelo lulula. Tshala **ama-perennial** amaningi (izitshalo eziphila iminyaka eminingi, njengezihlahla zezithelo, amakhambi ne-asparagus) futhi wonge umsebenzi ngamathuluzi afana **ne-drip irrigation** (ukunisela ngamaconsi) nesikhathi sompompi (tap timer).
3. Uma ufuna ukulimela imali futhi unesikhathi, amandla nezinsiza, ingadi enkulu nesebenza kakhulu ingakufanela.

Klama izingadi zakho ukuze zihambisane nezimpendulo zakho. Ingadi enkulu ongakwazi ukuyinakekela izoshesha ibe ingadi egcwele ukhula, echithekile.

Izinhlobo ezine zezingadi zokudla ezingezansi zilandela **ama-zone** e-permaculture (izindawo ezihlelwe ngokuthi uzivakashela kangaki): izingxenye zepulazi ozivakashela kakhulu zibekwa eduze kakhulu nendlu.

## 1. Ingadi yasekhaya eseduze nekhishi (i-Zone 1)

Ingadi yasekhaya eseduze nekhishi (kitchen garden) iyingxenye **ye-Zone 1**, indawo ezungeze indlu, ngokuvamile engamamitha ambalwa ukusuka emnyango wekhishi. Iyingadi yokudla eseduze kakhulu nekhishi futhi yiyona oyivakashela kakhulu, ngakho kumele kube lula futhi kushesha ukufika kuyo.

Tshala izitshalo ozikha nsuku zonke:

- imifino yesaladi **oyikhayo iphinde ikhule** (ukha amaqabunga ambalwa bese uyeka isitshalo siphinde sikhule), njengolethisi, isipinashi ne-Swiss chard
- ubhontshisi nophizi
- otamatisi abancane (cherry tomatoes)
- amakhambi owasebenzisa ekuphekeni, njenge-parsley, i-coriander, u-anyanisi omncane (spring onions) ne-thyme

Iningi lezitshalo lapha **ngama-annual** (izitshalo eziphila isizini eyodwa kuphela), kanye namakhambi ambalwa angama-perennial.

### Tshala uminyanise, unakekele eduze

Ingadi yasekhaya itshalwa iminyene kakhulu, kusetshenziswa yonke indawo, kokubili phansi naphezulu (isibonelo ubhontshisi osothangweni noma ezisekelweni). Ngemitha eyisikwele ngayinye iyona ekhiqiza kakhulu kunazo zonke izingadi zakho, ngakho idinga nokunakekelwa okukhulu kakhulu. Ukuhlakula, ukuhlwanyela, ukutshala, ukufaka **i-mulch** (isembozo somhlabathi), ukuvuna, ukulawula izinambuzane ezilimaza izitshalo nokondla umhlabathi konke kuyingxenye yomsebenzi wansuku zonke.

### Yenze ibe wusizo futhi ibe yinhle

Izingadi zasekhaya ziza ngezimo namaphethini amaningi. Okubaluleke kakhulu wukuthi ingadi isebenze kahle. Ngoba iseduze kakhulu nendlu, yenze ibukeke kahle futhi. Indawo incane, ngakho ungathatha isikhathi esengeziwe ukuze uzungeze imibhede ngezingodo, izitini ezindala noma amatshe, futhi umboze izindlela ngamatshana noma ngamazenge ezihlahla.

## 2. Ingadi yemifino (i-Zone 2)

Ingadi yemifino inkulu kunengadi yasekhaya. Lapha utshala imifino edinga indawo enkulu noma ethatha isikhathi eside ukuvunwa, isibonelo iklabishi, ubhitrudi, i-leek, u-anyanisi, upelepele, utamatisi ne-okra.

Uyivakashela kancane kunengadi yasekhaya, mhlawumbe kanye ngosuku noma njalo ezinsukwini ezimbalwa, ngakho ibekwa kude kancane nendlu, **ku-Zone 2**.

### Indlela ehlelwe ngayo

- Tshala izinhlobo eziningi zezitshalo ndawonye. Engeza izinto ezisiza ukulawula izinambuzane, njengechibi elincane lamaxoxo namanye **ama-predator** (izilwane ezidla izinambuzane), nezitshalo ezinezimbali ezihehe izinambuzane eziwusizo.
- Hlela imibhede yokutshala ecacile enezindlela phakathi kwayo. Emthambekeni, yenza imibhede nezindlela zilandele **umugqa we-contour** (umugqa ohamba ezingeni elifanayo emthambekeni), ukuze amanzi angene emhlabathini kunokuba ageleze ahambe.
- Ngoba indawo inkulu, akudingeki uzungeze imibhede. Vele uyilungise kabusha ngesandla izikhathi ezimbalwa ngonyaka.

Umsebenzi usemningi: ukususa izifafazi noma ukuhlola amapayipi e-drip irrigation, ukufaka i-mulch, ukufaka **i-compost** (izinsalela zezitshalo ezibolile ezondla umhlabathi), ukulawula izinambuzane, ukuhlakula, nokuhlwanyela imbewu njalo noma ukutshala izithombo.

Amathanele amakhulu epulasitiki, izindlu zezitshalo namathanele endwangu yomthunzi (shade-net) omndeni nawo angaphakathi ku-Zone 2, eduze nengadi yemifino.

Ingadi yemifino yomndeni akudingeki ibe nkulu kakhulu. Ungayandisa kamuva uma ufuna ukuthengisa okusele ukuze wengeze emalini oyitholayo.

## 3. Ukulima imifino yokuthengisa okuncane (i-Zone 3)

Ingadi yemifino enkulu etshalwa ikakhulukazi ukuze kuthengiswe ivame ukubizwa **ngengadi yezimakethe** (market garden). Kusukela cishe ku-0.4 hectares (i-acre eyodwa) kuya phezulu, kungcono ibekwe **ku-Zone 3**, indawo enkulu yokulima.

### Hlela ukuze ukhiqize

Ingadi yezimakethe ihlelwa ukuze ikhiqize okuningi ngangokunokwenzeka ngomsebenzi omncane ochithekayo.

- Tshala uhlobo lwemifino olulodwa embhedeni ngamunye. Lokhu kwenza ukuhlwanyela, ukuvuna neminye imisebenzi kushesha. Shintsha isitshalo embhedeni ngamunye isizini ngesizini (**i-crop rotation**).
- Tshala uminyanise emibhedeni elungiswe kahle, ene-compost eyanele. Ukutshala uminyanise kunikeza ukudla okuningi ngemitha eyisikwele futhi kwenza umthunzi ovimbela ukhula.
- Sebenzisa amathuluzi alula onga isikhathi, njengomshini wokuhlwanyela oqhutshwa ngesandla (push-seeder) ne-**broadfork** (imfologo ebanzi enezibambo ezimbili ethambisa umhlabathi ngaphandle kokuwuphendula). Ipulazi elikhulu lingasebenzisa ugandaganda omncane onamasondo amabili ohanjwa ngemuva kwawo (walk-behind tractor) ukulima kancane nokugunda izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi.

### Kungani izingadi zezimakethe zingaphumelela

Izingadi zezimakethe sezidumile emhlabeni wonke, kuhlanganise naseNingizimu ne-Afrika, ngoba abathengi abaningi manje bafuna ukudla okusha okutshalwe eduze nasekhaya.

- **Thengisa ngqo.** Uma uthengisela amakhasimende ngqo (esangweni lepulazi, emakethe yendawo, komakhelwane, ezikoleni, ezitolo ze-spaza noma ezindaweni zokudlela) uthola intengo **yokuthengisa ngqo** (retail) esikhundleni sentengo ephansi **yokuthengisa ngobuningi** (wholesale) ekhokhwa ngabathengisi.
- **Izindleko zokuqala eziphansi.** Indawo encane enamathuluzi esandla ibiza kancane kakhulu ukuqala kunepulazi elikhulu elisebenzisa imishini.
- **Nikeza izinhlobo eziningi.** Abalimi bezingadi zezimakethe abaphumelelayo bavame ukuthengisa imikhiqizo eminingi: imifino, izithelo, amajikijolo (berries), amaqanda, uju, inyama noma ubisi. Abanye futhi benyusa inani ngokwenza ujamu, ama-pickle, izithelo ezomisiwe neminye imikhiqizo.

> **Icebiso:** Ngaphambi kokuthi utshalele ukuthengisa, khuluma nabathengi bakho. Thola ukuthi bafunani, kangakanani, kangaki nangaliphi intengo. Tshalela imakethe oyaziyo, hhayi leyo oyithembayo nje.

## 4. Ingadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo (i-Zone 3)

**Izitshalo eziyisisekelo** (staple crops) ziwukudla okwakha ingxenye enkulu yokudla komndeni, njengommbila, amabele, ubhontshisi nobhatata.

- **Okusanhlamvu, amazambane nezinye izitshalo zezimpande** kunikeza **ama-carbohydrate**: ukudla kwamandla.
- **Ubhontshisi namanye ama-legume** (izitshalo zohlobo lukabhontshisi) kunikeza **amaprotheni**: ukudla kokwakha umzimba.

Izitshalo eziyisisekelo zidinga indawo enkulu ukuze kuvunwe isivuno esimaphakathi. Eziningi zithatha izinyanga eziningana ukuvuthwa, futhi zivame ukuvunwa zonke ngesikhathi esisodwa, njengommbila owomile noma ubhontshisi owomile. Ngakho sibeka ingadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo endaweni yokulima, **ku-Zone 3**.

Ingadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo idinga umsebenzi omncane kunengadi yasekhaya noma yemifino, futhi uyivakashela kancane. Ezinye izitshalo ziniselwa ngezikhathi ezithile, kodwa iningi litshalwa ngemvula kuphela. Khetha izitshalo ezifanele isimo sezulu sakho, futhi uzitshale ngesikhathi esifanele ukuze zisebenzise kahle imvula.

### Izitshalo eziyisisekelo zaseNingizimu ne-Afrika

| Iqembu | Izitshalo |
|---|---|
| Okusanhlamvu | ummbila, amabele, unyawothi (pearl millet); ukolweni, i-oats nebhali (ikakhulukazi izitshalo zasebusika) |
| Ubhontshisi namanye ama-legume | ubhontshisi owomile njenge-sugar beans, i-cowpea, izindlubu (jugo bean, Bambara groundnut), amantongomane, i-soya, i-pigeon pea, i-mung bean, i-chickpea |
| Izitshalo zezimpande nezigaxa | amazambane, ubhatata, amadumbe (taro), umdumbula (cassava) (ezindaweni ezingenaso isithwathwa kuphela) |
| Imifino edinga indawo enkulu | ithanga, i-butternut, ikhabe (watermelon), amanye amakhabe namaselwa |

### Ummbila

Ummbila uwukudla okuyisisekelo okukhulu engxenyeni enkulu yaseNingizimu Afrika.

- Ezindaweni ezinemvula yasehlobo, tshala ngemva kokuba imvula enhle isimanzise umhlabathi futhi ingozi yesithwathwa isidlulile, ngokuvamile kusukela ngo-Okthoba kuya kuDisemba.
- Tshala ngemigqa eqhelelene cishe ngo-90 cm. Ezindaweni ezomile, qhelelanisa izitshalo kakhulu (cishe isitshalo esisodwa njalo ku-50 cm emgqeni) ukuze isitshalo ngasinye sithole amanzi anele. Uma imvula ingcono, kukhona ukunisela noma umhlabathi unomvundo, izitshalo zingaba seduze (cishe ku-30 cm).
- Izinhlobo **zembewu evulekele impova** (open-pollinated) zingaphinde zitshalwe ngembewu yakho oyilondolozile. Ummbila we-hybrid unikeza isivuno esikhulu ngembewu ethengiwe, kodwa imbewu elondolozwe ku-hybrid ayikhuli ifane nomzali wayo futhi ivuna kancane.

### Amabele

Amabele abekezelela ukushisa nesomiso kangcono kunommbila, futhi ayisinqumo esihle ezindaweni ezomile. Lapho amanzi eyindlala khona, wawatshale **ngemigqa ebanzi** (eqhelelene cishe ngo-0.9 m kuya ku-2.3 m, kuya ngemvula yakho nomhlabathi wakho) ukuze isitshalo ngasinye sibe nendawo enkulu yomhlabathi esidonsa kuyo amanzi. Ezindaweni ezinemvula eningi imigqa emincane ilungile.

### Ubhontshisi owomile ne-cowpea

- Tshala ubhontshisi owomile lapho umhlabathi usufudumele (ngaphezu kuka-15 °C) ngemva kwemvula yokuqala enhle: ezindaweni eziningi ezinemvula yasehlobo lokhu kusukela maphakathi no-Okthoba kuya kuDisemba, kanti ezindaweni ezinesithwathwa kuze kube cishe maphakathi noJanuwari.
- Ubhontshisi ukhula kahle ngemigqa phakathi noma eceleni kommbila. Njengamanye ama-legume, usiza ukondla umhlabathi nge-nitrogen.
- I-cowpea nezindlubu kubekezelela ukushisa nesomiso kangcono kunobhontshisi owomile.

### Ubhatata

Ubhatata utshalwa **ngezingcezu zemicu yawo** (vine cuttings), hhayi ngembewu.

1. Sika izingcezu zomcu ezingu-20–30 cm ubude ezitshalweni eziphilile.
2. Zitshale emigqeni ephakanyisiwe (ridges) noma ezinqwabeni zomhlabathi eziqhelelene cishe ngo-1 m, izingcezu ziqhelelene cishe ngo-30 cm emgqeni.
3. Ezindaweni ezinesithwathwa, tshala kusukela cishe maphakathi noNovemba kuya ekuqaleni kukaDisemba. Ezindaweni ezingenaso isithwathwa ungatshala kusukela cishe ngo-Agasti kuya kuMashi.

Ubhatata onenyama ewolintshi ucebile nge-vitamin A futhi muhle kakhulu ezinganeni.

### Umdumbula (cassava)

Umdumbula ukhula kahle kuphela ezindaweni ezifudumele, **ezingenaso isithwathwa**, njengezingxenye zaKwaZulu-Natal, nezindawo eziphansi zaseMpumalanga naseLimpopo. Ubekezelela isomiso kakhulu uma usumile kahle.

> **Ukuphepha:** Izimpande namaqabunga omdumbula aqukethe amakhemikhali emvelo akhipha i-**cyanide**, okuwubuthi. Izinhlobo "ezibabayo" ziqukethe okuningi kakhulu kunezinhlobo "ezimnandi", futhi izitshalo ezihlushwe yisomiso ziqukethe okuningi. Ungalokothi udle umdumbula ungaphekiwe. Hlubula izimpande bese uzipheka zivuthwe kahle. Umdumbula obabayo kumele futhi ucwiliswe emanzini izinsuku eziningana (amanzi alahlwe), noma ugaywe ucutshungulwe, ngaphambi kokuwupheka. Buza umeluleki wezolimo wendawo ukuthi unaluphi uhlobo.

### Izimiso zengadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo

Kunezindlela eziningi zokutshala izitshalo eziyisisekelo. Engcono kakhulu kuwe incike esimweni sezulu sakho, emasikweni endawo okulima, emathuluzini onawo nasezitshalweni ozikhethayo. Lezi zimiso zisiza kuzo zonke izimo:

1. **Tshala izitshalo eziningi ndawonye, hhayi uhlobo olulodwa kuphela** (polycultures, not monocultures). Tshala izitshalo eziningana ndawonye, isibonelo ummbila nobhontshisi nethanga.
2. **Phazamisa umhlabathi kancane ngangokunokwenzeka.** Gwema ukulima ujule ngegeja lapho ungakwazi khona.
3. **Shiya izinsalela zezitshalo emhlabathini njenge-mulch.** Iziqu namaqabunga kuvikela umhlabathi futhi kuwondle lapho kubola.
4. **Tshala izitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi** (cover crops) ukuze uvikele futhi wondle umhlabathi phakathi kwezitshalo ezinkulu.
5. **Tshala ulandela i-contour** ukuze imvula ingene futhi umhlabathi uhlale endaweni yawo.
6. **Shintshanisa izitshalo.** Ungatshali isitshalo esifanayo endaweni efanayo unyaka nonyaka.
7. **Phumulisa umhlaba.** Ngemva kwamasizini ambalwa, yeka insimu iphumule ngaphansi kwezitshalo zokumboza umhlabathi noma amadlelo.

> **Qaphela:** Ubhontshisi ungukudla kwansuku zonke ezingxenyeni eziningi ze-Afrika. ERwanda naseBurundi, abantu badla ngokwesilinganiso cishe u-27–31 kg kabhontshisi umuntu ngamunye ngonyaka, okuyinani eliphezulu kunawo wonke emhlabeni.

## Amaphuzu amqoka

- Yazi isimo sezulu sakho: imvula yasehlobo engaphakathi kwezwe, imvula yasebusika eNtshonalanga Kapa, izindawo eziphansi nogu olungenaso isithwathwa.
- Vumela umoya obandayo ugeleze wehle; gcina izitshalo ezingasibekezeleli isithwathwa ziphume kuma-frost pocket.
- Hlela izingadi ukuze zihambisane nesikhathi, imali nentshisekelo onakho ngempela.
- Ingadi yasekhaya (i-Zone 1): incane, iminyene, imifino namakhambi ansuku zonke, eduze kakhulu nekhishi.
- Ingadi yemifino (i-Zone 2): izitshalo ezinkulu, imibhede nezindlela ezilandela i-contour.
- Ingadi yezimakethe (i-Zone 3): isitshalo esisodwa embhedeni ngamunye, ukutshala uminyanise, thengisa ngqo ukuze uthole intengo engcono.
- Ingadi yezitshalo eziyisisekelo (i-Zone 3): ummbila, amabele, ubhontshisi, ubhatata nezinye izitshalo zamandla nezamaprotheni, ezitshalwa ikakhulukazi ngemvula.
- Ungalokothi udle umdumbula ungaphekiwe; wuhlubule, uwucwilise futhi uwuphekele kahle.
- Xuba izitshalo, uphazamise umhlabathi kancane, faka i-mulch, shintshanisa izitshalo, tshala ulandela i-contour futhi uphumulise umhlaba.
