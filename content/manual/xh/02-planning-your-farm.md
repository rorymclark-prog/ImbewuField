# Ukucwangcisa ifama okanye ikhaya lakho

Ungatyala ukutya phantse kuyo yonke indawo. Kodwa igadi ekwindawo engafanelekanga ikubiza yonke imihla: iinyathelo ezongezelelekileyo ukuya kwitephu, iibhakethi ezongezelelekileyo ezinyuka induli, umhlaba ohamba nesaqhwithi ngasinye. Uyilo oluhle lugcina ixesha, imali namandla, kwaye ifama iya ichuma ngakumbi ngonyaka ngamnye.

I-permaculture yindlela yokuyila. Iveza onke amandla omhlaba, ifeze iimfuno zezityalo, izilwanyana, amanzi, izakhiwo nabantu kunye. Abalimi abancinci abatyebe kakhulu kunqabile ukuba babe ngabo banomhlaba omkhulu kunabanye.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuzoba imephu esisiseko yomhlaba wakho uphawule oko sele kukho kuwo
- Ukufunda indawo yakho ngokwamanzi, ithambeka, icala elijonge kulo ithambeka (aspect) nendlela yokungena
- Ukwahlula umhlaba wakho ube ngee-zone, ukuze izinto ozityelela yonke imihla zibe kufutshane nekhaya
- Ukusebenzisa ithambeka ukuze umxhuzulane uhambise amanzi, umgquba nobulongwe ngenxa yakho

Ucwangciso lwee-sector (amandla endalo angena endaweni yakho, njengelanga, umoya, umlilo namanzi), elona nyathelo lesine, lulandela kwisahluko esilandelayo.

## Inyathelo 1: Uvavanyo lwendawo

Impazamo ephepheni ayibizi nto ukuyilungisa. Impazamo emhlabeni ibiza ukubila. Ngoko funda umhlaba phambi kokuba uwutshintshe.

### Qokelela ulwazi uze ubukele

Fumanisa ukuba umhlaba wawusetyenziswa njani ngaphambili; imvula yawo, iqabaka, ubushushu nomoya; yintoni ekuwo okanye ekufutshane nawo (amanzi, ubulongwe, i-mulch, izinto zokwakha, abancedisi); nokuba unokuthengisa phi oko ukulimayo.

Emva koko hamba kumhlaba rhoqo, ngengqondo evulekileyo, kwaye ungaqukumbeli izigqibo ngokukhawuleza. Jonga:

- apho amanzi emvula ahamba khona emva kwesaqhwithi, nalapho aqokelelana khona
- indlela umoya ogoba okanye owumba ngayo imithi
- ezi zilwanyana ezihamba kumhlaba
- iindawo ezivakala zifudumele, zibanda, zimanzi okanye zomile.

Isizini nganye nesaqhwithi ngasinye sikufundisa into entsha.

### Zoba imephu esisiseko

**Imephu esisiseko** umzobo wepropati yakho obonwa ukusuka phezulu. Bonisa:

- imida, uthango, iindlela ezinkulu, iindlela ezincinci neegeyithi
- indlu, iindlu zokugcina izixhobo, izibaya nezinye izakhiwo
- indlela icandelo ngalinye elisetyenziswa ngayo ngoku: intsimi, idlelo lendalo (veld) okanye igadi
- amanzi: imithombo, imilanjana, imingxuma yamanzi (boreholes), iitanki, iitephu neendawo ezimanzi
- imithi emikhulu neendawo ezinamatye
- imigca ye-**contour** ukuba unayo. Umgca we-contour udibanisa amanqaku asezingeni elifanayo lokuphakama.

Landela ifoto yesatellite ekhululekileyo (umzekelo evela kwi-Google Earth), okanye ukuba iplothi incinci, linganisa ngomtya wokulinganisa uzobe eyakho. Iimephu zikarhulumente ze-topographic ze-1:50 000 zigubungela lonke uMzantsi Afrika, ngemigca ye-contour eyahlukene nge-20 m: zibonisa ubume obukhulu bomhlaba, kungekhona imingxuma emincinci engenanzulu. Zoba ngesikali, umzekelo i-1 cm ephepheni ibe yi-1 m emhlabeni.

## Oko kufuneka ukujonge kwindawo yakho

### Amanzi

Amanzi yeyona nto yokuqala ekufuneka uyicwangcisele (isahluko 7 siyaxoxa ngayo ngokubanzi).

1. Fumana imvula yommandla wakho. Uninzi loMzantsi Afrika lufumana imvula ehlotyeni; iNtshona Koloni iyifumana ebusika.
2. Jonga **i-catchment** (indawo yokuqokelela amanzi): umhlaba ongaphezu komhlaba wakho ohambisela amanzi emhlabeni wakho.
3. Jonga **i-runoff** (amanzi aqukuqela esihla), amanzi emvula aqukuqela phezu komhlaba endaweni yokungena, kunye neempawu zayo zakudala: iimisantsa yokukhukuliseka, iindawo ezingenanto nomhlaba ohambe namanzi. I-runoff ihamba nomhlaba nezondlo ozongezileyo.
4. Phawula indawo yokugcina amanzi onayo, iindawo apho kunokubekwa khona iitanki, amachibi okanye amadama, nemithombo yakho yokuxhasa: umngxuma wamanzi (borehole), umlambo, umthombo okanye itephu.
5. Phawula iindawo ezomileyo kakhulu nezimanzi kakhulu, uze ucwangcise indlela yokunkcenkcesha izithombo ngomzamo omncinci.

### Ukuma komhlaba nethambeka

Eyona ndawo ilungileyo yindawo ethambekayo kancinci, enamanzi aphumayo kakuhle, ejonge elangeni. Umhlaba otsiba kakhulu unzima ukusebenza kwaye ubandezeleka yi-**erosion** (ukukhukuliseka komhlaba): umhlaba ohamba namanzi.

Ithambeka lihlala linikwa ngepesenti: ukuwa kwi-100 m. Ukuwa kwe-1 m kwi-10 m yithambeka le-10%.

| Ithambeka | Ukuwa | Ikona | Ithetha ntoni |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5% | I-1 m kwi-20 m | malunga nama-3° | Lilula kumagadi nasezintsimini |
| 12% | I-1 m kwi-8 m ngokuqikelela | malunga nama-7° | Khusela umhlaba ngeebhedi ze-contour, imicu yengca okanye ii-terrace |
| 32% | I-1 m kwi-3 m ngokuqikelela | malunga nama-18° | Gcina phantsi kwemithi ehlala ihleli okanye ingca |

UBill Mollison, omnye wabasunguli be-permaculture, wacebisa ukugcina amathambeka atsiba ngaphezu kwama-18 degrees phantsi kwemithi ehlala ihleli. Kukhumbule oku xa ukhetha umhlaba wokuthenga okanye wokuqeshisa.

### Icala elijonge kulo ithambeka (aspect)

**Icala elijonge kulo ithambeka (aspect)** yicala elijongwe lithambeka okanye isakhiwo. KuMzantsi Afrika ilanga lisemntla, ngoko:

- **Amathambeka ajonge emntla nalawo ajonge empuma** afumana ilanga elininzi kwaye ashushu kwangoko. Alungele uninzi lwezityalo.
- **Amathambeka ajonge entshona** afumana ilanga elishushu lamasiko, elinokutshisa izityalo ehlotyeni.
- **Amathambeka ajonge emzantsi** abanda kwaye anomthunzi ebusika. Alungele imithi, kwaye kwiindawo ezomileyo agcina ukufuma ixesha elide.

Akukho msebenzi onokutshintsha apho ilanga liphuma khona. Jonga apho imithi nezakhiwo zenza umthunzi wazo; imithunzi yasebusika inde kakhulu (isahluko 3).

### Indlela yokungena

Iindlela ezinkulu neendlela ezincinci phantse zihlala zihleli. Yenza ezintsha zihambe ngokulandela i-contour. Indlela ekwi-contour inokuba lidonga le-**swale**, umsele ombiweyo olandela i-contour ukubamba amanzi emvula (isahluko 7). Nika indlela nganye ukuwa okuncinci kakhulu, ukuze imvula iphume iye kwi-swale okanye kwisitya somthi endaweni yokuqukuqela ezantsi endleleni.

## Inyathelo 2: Ucwangciso lwee-zone

Umgama owuhambayo unquma ukuba uyikhathalele ntoni. Igadi ekude iyalityalwa; igadi ekumnyango wekitshi iyankcenkcesheka. Ucwangciso lwee-zone luyahlula ifama ibe ngee-**zone** ngokokuba uyityelela kangakanani: izinto zemihla ngemihla zisondele endlini, izinto ezisetyelelwa kuqhelekile zikude ngakumbi.

| I-zone | Yintoni | Uyityelela kangakanani | Imizekelo |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Indlu | Ixesha lonke | Ikitshi, indawo yokugcina imbewu, iitanki ezikwindlu |
| 1 | Ecaleni kwendlu kanye | Yonke imihla, rhoqo izihlandlo ezininzi | Igadi yekitshi, imifuno enevumba (herbs), inkulisa, i-compost, izilwanyana ezincinci |
| 2 | Kufutshane nendlu | Uninzi lweentsuku | I-food forest, imithi yeziqhamo, iinkukhu, izityalo eziphambili zokutya |
| 3 | Indawo ephambili yokulima | Veki nganye, okanye xa kufuneka | Izityalo zentsimi nezemarike, igadi yemithi yeziqhamo, amadlelo, amadama |
| 4 | Esendle ngokuyinxenye, ephathwa kancinci | Ngamaxesha athile | Ihlathi lokuvuna iinkuni, amadlelo |
| 5 | Yasendle | Kunqabile, ukubukela nokufunda | Idlelo lendalo okanye ihlathi lendalo |

**I-zone 1** inika isivuno esiphezulu kwindawo encinci. Beka apha imifuno ye-**annual** (ephila isizini enye) nemifino oyikha rhoqo, imifuno enevumba yasekhitshini, imithi yeziqhamo emifutshane, izilwanyana ezincinci ezinjenge-quail neemvundla, neendawo ezincinci zokugcina amanzi. Gquma yonke itanki nequla lamanzi, uze ubophe uthango okanye ugquma amachibi, ukuze kungabikho mntwana ungawela kuzo.

**I-zone 2** inkulu kwaye ayinzima kangako. **I-food forest** yayo (igadi ityalwe ngamaleko njengehlathi lendalo) inokuquka imithi ye-**coppicing** (ukunqunqa isihlahla emhlabeni ukuze sihlume kwakhona): ukunqumla umthi ube sisiphunzi esiphantsi ukuze uhlume iintonga ezintsha. Ukufumana iinkuni neentonga, tyala umnga (*Vachellia karroo*), umthi womdabu (okhula ngokwendalo apha), kunemithi ye-wattle okanye ye-gum, eyasazeka emadlelweni endalo nasemilanjeni.

**I-zone 3** inemfuyo, izityalo zentsimi namadama efama. **I-zone 4** inika amadlelo, iinkuni nomthi wokwakha. **I-zone 5** ngumhlaba wendalo ushiyiwe ube sendle, indawo yokubalekela iintaka nezinambuzane ezinceda ukulawula izinambuzane ezonakalisayo. Yityelele ukubukela, ukufunda nokuqokelela ukutya kwasendle ngononophelo.

Akukho ndawo inendawo yazo zonke ii-zone. Isiza sasedolophini esimalunga nama-1 000 eemitha ezikwere (ikota yeekeri) sinendawo ye-zone 1 neziqwenga zezinye; ikona eshiywe iintaka nezinambuzane yi-zone 5 encinci kakhulu. Kumhlaba oyimpahla yoluntu (communal land), ii-zone 4 no-5 zinokuba lidlelo okanye idlelo lendalo elabelanwayo. Kwaye ii-zone azisoze zibe ziisangqa ezicocekileyo: ithambeka, amanzi neendlela ziziguqula zibe ziimilo ezahlukeneyo.

## Inyathelo 3: Ucwangciso lwethambeka

Amanzi ahamba esihla simahla. Ucwangciso lwethambeka, ngokwemodeli kaBill Mollison, lithetha ukujonga umhlaba wakho usecaleni uze ubuze: ndikweyiphi indawo yethambeka? Sebenzisa ithambeka kakuhle kwaye umxhuzulane uya kwenza umsebenzi wokuthwala.

- **Amanzi phezulu.** Beka amadama neetanki ngaphezu kwendlu negadi, kwaye umxhuzulane uhambisa amanzi ngaphandle kwempompo. Yonke i-10 m yokuphakama inika malunga ne-1 bar yoxinzelelo.
- **Imithi kumathambeka aphezulu atsiba kakhulu** icothisa i-runoff, inceda amanzi angene kwaye ibamba umhlaba.
- **Iibhedi, iindlela nee-swale ezikwi-contour** zigcina amanzi emhlabeni.
- **Iinqwaba ze-compost neendawo zokukhusela izilwanyana zivulekele ezantsi ethambekeni,** ukuze i-compost nobulongwe ziye ezantsi egadini.
- **Amanzi okusela ahlala ecocekile.** Gcina ubulongwe, i-compost nezibaya zezilwanyana ezantsi ethambekeni kwimingxuma yamanzi (boreholes), amaqula amanzi nemithombo, kwaye kude kakhulu nayo.
- **I-greywater iye emithini.** **I-greywater**, amanzi asetyenzisiweyo ekuhlanjeni, kwiibhafu nasezitsheni zokuhlamba, ihamba ngomxhuzulane iye kwizitya zemithi egqunywe nge-mulch okanye kwii-swale ezantsi kwendlu, ingaze ifike kwimifino okanye kwenye imifuno oyitya ingaphekwanga (isahluko 7).

## Zama oku

Qalisa imephu yakho esisiseko kule veki.

1. Fumana ifoto eshicilelweyo yesatellite yomhlaba wakho, okanye iphepha elikhulu. Phawula emntla.
2. Hamba ngemida. Zoba imida, izakhiwo, iindlela, iitephu, iitanki nemithi emikhulu.
3. Emva kwemvula elandelayo elungileyo, hamba kumhlaba kwakhona. Phawula ngemicibisholo apho amanzi ehamba khona nalapho aqokelelana khona.
4. Phawula iindawo ezifudumeleyo, ezibandayo, ezimanzi nezomileyo.
5. Usuku olunye, bala yonke imizamo oyenzayo ukuya kwindawo nganye: itephu, iinkukhu, igadi, indawo yeenkuni.
6. Ngepensile, zoba ii-zone ezingaqinisekanga phezu kwemephu. Ngaba indawo oyityelela kakhulu isondele kakhulu endlini?

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Bukela umhlaba wakho kuzo zonke iisizini, uze ubeke oko ukubonayo kwimephu esisiseko.
- Funda indawo yakho ngokwamanzi, ithambeka, icala elijonge kulo ithambeka (aspect) nendlela yokungena. Amathambeka ajonge emntla nalawo ajonge empuma yiwona afudumeleyo.
- Ii-zone 0 ukuya ku-5 zibeka izinto ngokokuba uzityelela kangakanani.
- Beka amanzi phezulu uvumele umxhuzulane uwahambise. Gcina ubulongwe kude namanzi okusela, ne-greywater kude nemifuno oyitya ingaphekwanga.

Gcina imephu yakho eludongeni uyongeze kuyo kwisizini nganye. Emva kweminyaka embalwa iya kuba yeyona ncwadi iluncedo ngomhlaba wakho.
