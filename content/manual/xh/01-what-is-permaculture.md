# Yintoni i-permaculture?

Uninzi lwethu lwafunda ukulima ngokulwa: ukulwa nokhula, nezinambuzane, nemozulu. Siyalima, siyatshiza, sithenga isichumiso, kwaye ngonyaka ngamnye umhlaba usinika kancinci.

I-permaculture iqala kwenye indawo. Ibuza ukuba umhlaba sele wenza ntoni, nokuba singasebenza kunye nawo njani. Yindlela yokucinga, esekelwe kwimigaqo emithathu yokuziphatha neyemigaqo yoyilo elishumi elinesibini, onokuyithatha uyise kuyo nayiphi na indawo yomhlaba, ukusuka kwigadi yasemva kwendlu yasezilokishini ukuya kwintsimi yasentabeni.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuchaza ngamagama alula ukuba yintoni i-permaculture nokuba igama livela phi
- Ukukhankanya imigaqo emithathu yokuziphatha uze uyisebenzise ukuvavanya isigqibo kwifama yakho
- Ukukhankanya imigaqo yoyilo elishumi elinesibini kaDavid Holmgren nokunika umzekelo wefama womgaqo ngamnye
- Ukuqalisa ukubukela umhlaba wakho njengoko umyili enza

## Oko igama lithetha

Igama elithi **i-permaculture** lidibanisa amagama amabini esiNgesi: **perma**nent (okuhlala kuhleli) ne-agri**culture** (ezolimo). Lithetha ukulima okunokondla usapho izizukulwana nezizukulwana. Lithetha nokuba **yinkcubeko** (culture), indlela yokuphila, ehlala ixesha elide.

Abantu baseOstreliya ababini baliqamba eli gama phakathi kweminyaka yee-1970: **uBill Mollison** (1928–2016) **noDavid Holmgren**, owayengumfundi wakhe ngelo xesha eTasmania. Incwadi yabo yokuqala, ethi *Permaculture One*, yaphuma ngo-1978. Ukususela ngoko i-permaculture isasazeke emhlabeni wonke, kubandakanywa noMzantsi weAfrika.

UMollison wachaza i-permaculture ngokuthi "uyilo nokugcinwa ngabom kwee-ecosystem ezivelisa ukutya, ezinokwahlukahlukana, ukuzinza namandla okuphinda aphakame ee-ecosystem zendalo".

Ngamagama alula: siyicwangcisa size siyiqhube ifama ukuze isebenze njengendalo. **I-ecosystem** liqela lezityalo, izilwanyana nobomi bomhlaba abahlala kunye nomhlaba, amanzi nomoya ojikelezileyo. Idlelo lendalo elinempilo linemihlobo emininzi yobomi (**ukwahlukahlukana**). Liyaqhubeka unyaka nonyaka (**ukuzinza**). Liphinda liphakame emva kwembalela, komlilo okanye emonakalweni (**amandla okuphinda aphakame**). Ifama ye-permaculture ijolise kwezi zinto zintathu, kwaye ikondla abantu nabo.

## Kutheni i-permaculture ibalulekile eMzantsi weAfrika

Iintsapho eziphila ngomhlaba ziyiva kuqala imozulu. Kukho uxinzelelo olubini olukhulayo.

- **I-climate change** (utshintsho lwemozulu) kukutshintsha kwemozulu ixesha elide, okubangelwa ikakhulu ngabantu. Abalimi sele bebona imini eshushu ngakumbi, imvula efika emva kwexesha okanye ize yonke kunye, iimbalela ezinde nezaqhwithi ezinamandla. Uninzi lwabalimi abancinci lulima ngemvula, lungasebenzisi unkcenkceshelo, ngoko lulithwele kakhulu ingozi.
- **I-erosion** (ukukhukuliseka komhlaba) kukukhukuliswa komhlaba ngamanzi okanye ukuphephethwa ngumoya. Umhlaba ongenanto, odliwe kakhulu yimfuyo okanye olinyiwe ethambekeni elitsiba kakhulu ulahlekelwa yi-**topsoil** yawo (umhlaba ongaphezulu ofumileyo) kwisaqhwithi ngasinye. I-erosion yomhlaba ichaphazele ngaphezu kwe-70% yomhlaba woMzantsi Afrika. ILesotho, apho esi sikhokelo saqala khona, nayo inokukhukuliseka okunzima neemisantsa enzulu.

UMzantsi Afrika naso lilizwe elomileyo. Lufumana malunga ne-450 mm yemvula ngonyaka ngokomndilili, phantse isiqingatha somndilili wehlabathi we-860 mm. Yonke ithonsi ibalulekile.

Umhlaba olahlekelwa ngumhlaba wawo namanzi uyayeka ukondla abantu bawo, kwaye bayemka. Asikwazi ukulima ngendlela endala silindele isiphumo esahlukileyo.

I-permaculture isinika imephu nezixhobo: khusela umhlaba, bamba imvula, tyala izityalo ezininzi, sebenza nendalo. Ibuyisela kwimihla ngemihla yobomi.

- **Ukutya unyaka wonke.** Imifuno, iziqhamo, iinkozo kunye **nee-legume** (usapho lweembotyi neephizi), zikhuliswe ngaphandle kwetyhefu. Isitya esiphuma ezininzi sisempilweni ngakumbi.
- **Imali egciniweyo nemali enzuzwayo.** Izinto ezithengiweyo ezimbalwa, neemveliso ezininzi, ukuze isityalo esinye esingaphumelelanga singawudilizi umzi. Intsalela inokuthengiswa, yomiswe okanye ifakwe kwiibhotile.

## Imigaqo emithathu yokuziphatha

Zonke izigqibo kwifama ye-permaculture ziphicothwa ngemigaqo emithathu, **imigaqo yokuziphatha**, amaxabiso asikhokelayo. UBill Mollison noDavid Holmgren bayibeka entliziyweni ye-permaculture.

### 1. Ukukhathalela uMhlaba

Khathalela zonke izinto eziphilayo (izityalo, izilwanyana, ubomi obukumhlaba) kunye nomhlaba, amanzi nomoya ezixhasa ubomi. Sixhomekeke kuzo zonke.

### 2. Ukukhathalela abantu

Phumeza iimfuno zabantu zokutya, amanzi, indawo yokuhlala, impilo, ukufunda nobuhlobo ngaphandle kokonakalisa uMhlaba. Yakha ukuzimela kusapho lwakho nakuluntu lwakho.

### 3. Ukwabelana ngokufanelekileyo

UMhlaba unemida. Ukwabelana ngokufanelekileyo, okuthiwa rhoqo **fair share**, kuthetha ukuthatha oko sikudingayo size sibuyisele intsalela (ukutya, imbewu, ixesha, izakhono nemali) ekukhathaleleni uMhlaba nabantu.

Sebenzisa le migaqo njengovavanyo. Phambi kokuba ulime ithambeka, uthenge ityhefu yokutshiza okanye uthinge umthombo, buza imibuzo emithathu. Ilungele umhlaba? Ilungele abantu? Ifanelekile? Ukuba nayiphi na impendulo ithi hayi, funa enye indlela.

## Imigaqo yoyilo ye-permaculture

Imigaqo yokuziphatha isixelela into ebalulekileyo. Imigaqo yoyilo zizixhobo zokucinga zokuyiyila: zokucwangcisa ifama, ukubona ukuba amacandelo ayo adibana njani nokusombulula iingxaki ngokusisigxina.

Abafundisi basebenzisa uluhlu olwahlukileyo kancinci. Ezi shumi elinesibini zivela kwincwadi kaDavid Holmgren ethi *Permaculture: Principles and Pathways Beyond Sustainability* (2002). UHolmgren uqhagamshele umgaqo ngamnye nesaci esidala, kwaye sibonisa ezinye zazo apha.

### 1. Bukela uze usebenzisane

Awukwazi ukuyila umhlaba ongawaziyo. Amanzi ahamba phi? Iqabaka lihlala phi? Yintoni ekhula kakuhle ngaphandle kosizo? Iincwadi nabafundisi bayanceda, kodwa akukho nto ithatha indawo yexesha lakho kumhlaba wakho.

1. Hamba kumhlaba wakho, nakwidlelo elijikelezileyo, kuzo zonke iisizini.
2. Hlala ngokuthula ubukele izityalo, iintaka, izinambuzane nezilwanyana.
3. Gcina incwadi yamanqaku yemvula, yeqabaka lokuqala nelokugqibela, nokuba izinto ziyintyatyamba nini. Abalimi abaninzi baphawula nezigaba zenyanga.
4. Hamba ngexesha nasemva kwemvula enkulu. Jonga apho amanzi ahamba khona nalapho aqokelelana khona.

Emva konyaka wokubukela, uya kuwazi umhlaba wakho ngcono kunaso nasiphi na isivakashi. Uyilo lwakho luya kuwuhambela endaweni yokulwa nawo.

### 2. Bamba uze ugcine amandla

*Yomisa ingca ilanga likhona.*

Indalo ihamba ngeesangqa: ubuninzi, emva koko ukunqongophala. Kwindawo eninzi yangaphakathi yoMzantsi Afrika, ihlobo elifudumeleyo, elimanzi lixesha lokukhula, ubusika bube bubanda, bomile, bucotha. KwiNtshona Koloni imvula iyana ebusika kwaye ihlobo limile. Sibamba oko kuninzi size sikugcine ixesha lokunqongophala.

- Bamba ilanga lasehlotyeni njengezityalo: imifuno, iziqhamo, amandongomane, ukutya kwemfuyo, iinkuni nenyama.
- Bamba imvula kwiitanki, kumachibi nakumhlaba ngokwawo, **nge-mulch** (ingca eyomileyo okanye amagqabi agubungela umhlaba) **nee-swale** (imisele ebekwe ethe tye, ihamba macala ethambekeni ukufunxa imvula).
- Yomisa, fakela kwiibhotile uze ugcine intsalela yasehlotyeni ebusika.

Ifama egcina kakuhle ayidimazeki xa imvula iyeka.

### 3. Fumana isivuno

*Awukwazi ukusebenza ngesisu esingenanto.*

**Isivuno** yinto nayiphi na eluncedo oyinikwa ngumhlaba: ukutya, ukutya kwemfuyo, iinkuni, umthunzi, amayeza, imbewu okanye ingeniso. Umthi weziqhamo unokufuna iisizini ezininzi phambi kokuba uthele, ngoko tyala izivuno ezikhawulezayo nezicothayo kunye, ukuze kuhlale kukho into yokubonisa umsebenzi wale sizini. Siyila ukuze kubekho intsalela: eyaneleyo kusapho, kumhlaba, kwizilwanyana, kubamelwane nakwimarike.

- Amahlumela e-mung beans okanye eelentile alungele ukutyiwa kwiintsuku ezi-2 ukuya kwezi-5. Sebenzisa amanzi acocekileyo, uwahlambulule kabini ngosuku, uze ulahle nawaphi na anuka kakubi okanye ahlala etyibilika.
- Imithi yeziqhamo icotha ukuqala, kodwa inika isivuno iminyaka emininzi xa sele iqala ukuthela.
- Tyala iintyatyambo zeenyosi. Amakhala amaninzi omdabu aqhakaza ebusika, xa kungekho zinto zininzi ziqhakazayo. Iinyosi zichumisa izityalo zakho kwaye zinokukunika ubusi.

### 4. Zilawule uze wamkele impendulo

Iinkqubo zendalo zizigcina zilingene. Kwidlelo lendalo elinempilo asikho isityalo okanye isilwanyana esinye esilawula ixesha elide. Abantu bahlukile: sihlala sithatha ngaphezu kwalo oko sikudingayo, kwaye oko kusetyenziswa gqitha kubangela utshintsho lwemozulu nokulahleka kwezityalo nezilwanyana zasendle.

Ngoko **sizibekela imida**: siphila ngokulula size sithathe oko sikudingayo, ukuze abantwana bethu bafumane umhlaba ophilayo. Kwaye **samkela impendulo (feedback)**, imiqondiso esixelela ukuba oko sikwenzayo kuyasebenza. Iimpazamo ziyinxalenye yokufunda. Mamela izityalo, umhlaba, izilwanyana nabanye abantu, uze utshintshe oko kungasebenziyo.

- Dibanisa iindlela zokuthuma zibe luhambo olunye, hamba ngeenyawo, okanye uhambe ngebhayisikili.
- Funda izityalo zakho. Amagqabi aphuzi, ukukhula okubuthathaka okanye ukuqubuka kwezinambuzane ngumhlaba okuxelela ukuba into ethile emhlabeni, emanzini okanye ekukhetheni kwakho izityalo kufuneka itshintshe.

### 5. Sebenzisa uze uxabise izibonelelo neenkonzo ezihlaziyekayo

Ilanga, umoya, imvula, izityalo nezilwanyana ziyazihlaziya. Ipetroli, idizili nesichumiso sekhemikhali zisetyenziswa kanye zize ziphele. Zibiza imali, kwaye ukutshisa amafutha ezinto zakudala kongeza kutshintsho lwemozulu.

Izityalo nezilwanyana zinokwenza uninzi lomsebenzi wetrektara okanye womatshini wokusika ingca. Zinika amafutha nesichumiso, zilawula ukhula nezinambuzane, ziyayeka i-erosion, zijikelezisa izondlo zikhulule umhlaba. Ziyathatha ixesha ukwakha, kodwa ekuhambeni kwexesha ziyazala zizigcine.

- **Ii-animal tractor.** **I-animal tractor** yindlu ehambayo yeenkukhu okanye yeehagu, ezikrwela zize zimbe iintshulube, iingcambu nezinambuzane. Zivale iintsuku ezimbalwa kwindawo ekhule ngokugqithisileyo kwaye ziyicoce zize ziyityebise, ilungele ukutyalwa.
- **Ubulongwe.** Izilwanyana zitya izityalo zize zibuyisele izondlo njengobulongwe neti yobulongwe. Yenza i-compost yobulongwe phambi kokuba busondele kwizityalo ozityayo ziluhlaza.
- **Uthango oluphilayo.** Uthango lwezityalo eziluncedo, ezingahlaseliyo, lunika indawo yokukhuseleka, ukutya kwemfuyo neenkuni ngaphandle kocingo okanye amafutha.

### 6. Ungavelisi nkunkuma

*Ungalahli nto, ungasweli nto.*

Endalweni akukho nkunkuma: isiphumo seqhekeza elinye siba yinto efakwa kwelilandelayo. Efameni sijolise ekwenzeni **izangqa ezivaliweyo** (closed loops), apho izondlo namandla zijikeleza zize ziphume njengesivuno. Oko kushiya ifama ekugqibeleni kufuneka kucoceke. Umgaqo olungileyo: **phinda ucinge, nciphisa, lungisa, phinda usebenzise, vusa kwakhona (recycle).**

- **Intsalela yasekhitshini** yondla iintshulube, kwaye yinxalenye exabisekileyo "eluhlaza" (enenayitrojeni eninzi) kwinqwaba **ye-compost**. I-compost yinkunkuma yezityalo nezilwanyana ebolile ibe ngukutya okumnyama, okuthambileyo komhlaba.
- **Ingca esikiweyo** yenza i-mulch ejikeleze izithombo. Igcina umhlaba upholile ufumile ize ibambe ukhula. Yisasaze incinci okanye uyiyeke yome kuqala, kuba umaleko oshinyeneyo wengca entsha uyabambana uze utshise.
- **I-greywater** ngamanzi asetyenzisiweyo abhafini, kwizihlambi nakwiibhesini. Ayiya phantsi kwe-mulch kwimithi yeziqhamo, ungaze uyisebenzise kwimifuno yamagqabi oyityayo iluhlaza, kwaye amanzi asekhitshini okanye ayeedayapha awafakwa kuyo. Sebenzisa iisepha ezibuthathaka, ezinetyuwa encinci.

### 7. Yila usuka kwiipatheni uye kwiinkcukacha

Indalo izele ziipatheni: ukuthi nga kwesebe lomlambo okanye lomthi, isangqa seqokobhe, amanzi ehla ethambekeni. Siqala sifumane ipatheni enkulu yendawo, emva koko sizalise iinkcukacha. UMollison wayecinga ukuba ipatheni ibalulekile kangangokuba wayinika isahluko esipheleleyo kwincwadi yakhe ethi *Permaculture: A Designers' Manual* (1988).

1. Qala ngobume bomhlaba: amathambeka awo **ne-contour**, imigca ewela ithambeka ehleli ekuphakameni okufanayo.
2. Fumana i-**catchment**, indawo apho amanzi akho evela khona, uze ulandele apho aya khona.
3. Qaphela imozulu nendlela yelanga. KuMzantsi weAfrika ilanga lisemntla, kwaye liphantsi ebusika.
4. Kuphela emva koko beka iinkcukacha: yeyiphi ibhedi, yimuphi umthi, sesiphi isityalo.

I-contour yipatheni; i-swale egrunjwe ecaleni kwayo yinkcukacha. Boleka nezimbo zendalo: iindlela ezijikajikayo njengomlambo, iibhedi ezimile okwesiphotho (spiral) okanye okwemngxuma wesitshixo (keyhole).

### 8. Hlanganisa endaweni yokwahlula

*Izandla ziyahlambana.*

Xa inxalenye nganye yefama inceda ezinye, iyonke iba namandla ngaphezu kwamacandelo ayo. Ukubeka into nganye (indlu, igadi, iinkukhu, itanki, imithi) apho inokukhonza ezinye kuyintliziyo yoyilo lwe-permaculture. Nabantu bayafana: abantwana nabantu abadala banezinto ezininzi zokunika nokuzifunda, kwaye bangabokuhlala ebomini befama.

- Xuba imithi, izityalo nezilwanyana (**i-agroforestry**). Imithi inika umthunzi nokutya kwemfuyo, izilwanyana zinika ubulongwe, kwaye izityalo zinika ukutya neentsalela zezilwanyana.
- Yakha **ii-guild**: amaqela ezityalo ahlelwe ujikeleze isityalo esikhulu, ngokuqhelekileyo umthi weziqhamo, awunceda ngokulawula izinambuzane, ngokuchuma komhlaba okanye ngomthunzi.
- Yenza iindawo ezikhuselekileyo apho abantwana nootatomkhulu nomakhulu bangasebenza bafunde egadini.

### 9. Sebenzisa izisombululo ezincinci nezicothayo

*Kancinci kancinci kuyafikelelwa.*

Abalimi abaninzi baqala ngokuphakamisa, benenkalipho enkulu, baze balahlekelwe lithemba xa isiqwenga sikhulu kakhulu ukuba silawulwe. Indawo encinci ekhathalelwe kakuhle inika ngaphezu kwenkulu eshiywe iyendle.

1. Qala ngasemnyango wakho wangasemva ngegadi encinci yasekhitshini.
2. Yenze isebenze phambi kokuba uqale inyathelo elilandelayo.
3. Ngonyaka ngamnye, zisa umhlaba omncinci ngaphezulu ekuveliseni.
4. Yakha umhlaba isizini ngesizini nge-compost, i-mulch nezityalo zokugquma.

Itrektara enkulu kwintsimi encinci iyabiza kwaye iqinisa umhlaba; iinkukhu kwindlu ehambayo zisebenza kancinci kodwa zicoca zize zityebise umhlaba ngento encinci kakhulu. Utshintsho olucothayo lukunika ixesha lokubukela indlela umhlaba ophendula ngayo, kwaye utshintsho olwenziwa ngale ndlela luhlala. Yiba nesicwangciso, uze ulindele ukuba sitshintshe.

### 10. Sebenzisa uze uxabise ukwahlukahlukana

*Ungazifaki zonke iqanda zakho kwibhasikithi enye.*

Ukwahlukahlukana kuyinto enamandla. Cinga ngentambo: umtya omnye uyaqhawuka, kodwa imitya emininzi ijijiweyo iyabamba. **I-monoculture**, isityalo esinye kwindawo enkulu esoloko isuka kwimbewu efanayo, ihlwempuzekile ngobomi kwaye ivulekele zonke izinambuzane, izifo nezizini ezimbi. Ifama eyahlukahlukeneyo iyagoba iphinde ime.

Ukwahlukahlukana kusebenza kuphela xa amacandelo edibana. Isityalo ngasinye nesilwanyana sidinga indawo nenjongo.

- Tyala imifuno, iinkozo, ii-legume, nemithi yeziqhamo neyamandongomane enika ukutya ngamaxesha ahlukeneyo onyaka. Ukuba isityalo esinye siyasilela, esinye sisakondla.
- Gcina imbewu kwizityalo ezininzi ezinempilo kakhulu isizini nganye. Imbewu iziqhelanisa nomhlaba wakho, kwaye ukugcina kwizityalo ezininzi kuyigcina ihlukahlukene.
- Gcina izilwanyana ezincedisanayo. Iinkukhu ezilandela iinkomo zikrwela ubulongwe zize zitye izibungu zeempukane ezikuwo.

### 11. Sebenzisa imiphetho uze uxabise okusemaphethelweni

**Umphetho** yindawo apho iindawo ezimbini ezahlukileyo zidibana: ichibi nomhlaba owomileyo, ihlathi nedlelo. Imiphetho ihlala iyeyona ixineneyo nevelisa kakhulu, ityelelwa zizidalwa zazo zombini iinxalenye. NjengoHolmgren, "Indawo yokudibana phakathi kwezinto yeyona apho kwenzeka izinto ezinomdla kakhulu."

Iindawo ezisemaphethelweni (iikona, uthango, iindonga, iinxili ezithe nkqo neendawo ezimanzi) zihlala zinganakwa. Zisebenzise.

- Tyala uthango lwezityalo zomdabu ecaleni kweentsimi. Umqokolo (*Dovyalis caffra*) wenza uthango olunameva oluthela iziqhamo. I-Cape honeysuckle (*Tecoma capensis*) ne-plumbago (*Plumbago auriculata*) zitsala iintaka nezinambuzane eziluncedo.
- Yenza indlela phakathi kweendawo ezimbini zokukhula, ezinjenge-**food forest** (igadi etyalwe ngamaleko njengehlathi lendalo) kunye neebhedi zemifuno, ukuze uzikhathalele zombini ngaxeshanye.
- Beka indawo yeenkukhu ecaleni kwegadi. Ukhula luyangena; ubulongwe buyaphuma.
- Tyala izityalo ezikhwela eludongeni nakuthango. Iimbotyi ezikhwelayo, amakhukhamba neeselwa guqula umphetho ongenanto ube kukutya.

### 12. Sebenzisa utshintsho ngokuyila uze uphendule kulo

*Umbono akukokubona izinto njengoko zinjalo kodwa njengoko ziya kuba njalo.*

Akukho nto endalweni ime ndawonye. Asikwazi ukuyeka utshintsho, kodwa sinokulindela size senze ngexesha elifanelekileyo. Umbungu udinga ukutya okwahlukileyo kwibhabhathane aba lilo; ifama nayo idinga ukhathalelo olwahlukileyo kwinqanaba ngalinye.

- I-**food forest** ikhula ukusuka kwimithi emincinci nezityalo ezikhula ngokukhawuleza ukuya kwihlathi elinomthunzi, elikhulile. Oko utyalayo nendlela oyikhathalela ngayo kufuneka kutshintshe nayo.
- Imvula nobushushu ziyatshintsha ngonyaka. Vumela ukhetho lwakho lwezityalo lutshintshe nazo.

## Zama oku

1. Khetha indawo enye apho ubona uninzi lomhlaba wakho. Hlala apho imizuzu engama-20 ekuthomeni kokusa okanye ngokuhlwa, kathathu kule veki.
2. Bhala phantsi yonke into oyiqaphelayo: iintaka, izinambuzane, apho ilanga liwela khona kuqala, apho umoya uvela khona, ezi zityalo zibonakala zomeleleyo nezi ziyasokola.
3. Ngexesha elilandelayo imvula iyana kakhulu, hamba kumhlaba wakho. Landela amanzi. Phawula apho engena khona, apho ehamba ngokukhawuleza nalapho aqokelelana khona.
4. Zoba imephu ethe ngqo yomhlaba wakho njengoko intaka iwubona: indlu, iindlela, ukuhamba kwamanzi, iindawo ezomeleleyo nezibuthathaka.
5. Ecaleni kwemephu, bhala into enye leyo umgaqo ngamnye wokuziphatha ewucelayo kule ndawo, kunye nomgaqo omnye ongaqala ngawo.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- I-permaculture, evela kwi-permanent agriculture nakwi-permanent culture, yaqanjwa nguBill Mollison noDavid Holmgren phakathi kweminyaka yee-1970.
- Iyila iifama ezisebenza njenge-ecosystem zendalo: ezahlukahlukeneyo, ezizinzileyo nezinamandla okuphinda aphakame.
- Imigaqo emithathu yokuziphatha iphicotha zonke izigqibo: ukukhathalela uMhlaba, ukukhathalela abantu, ukwabelana ngokufanelekileyo.
