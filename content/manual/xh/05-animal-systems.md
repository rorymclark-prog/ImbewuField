# Iinkqubo zezilwanyana

Izilwanyana zivala isangqa. Inkukhu iguqula amaxolo asekhitshini abe ngamaqanda nobulongwe. Ihagu iguqula iziqhamo eziwileyo zibe yinyama nebhedi ecocekileyo. Inyosi iguqula iintyatyambo ezisecaleni koluthango lwakho zibe bubusi kunye nesivuno esinzima samathanga.

Xa zigcinwe kakubi, izilwanyana ziba ngumsebenzi nexhala. Xa zigcinwe kakuhle, zenza uninzi lomsebenzi ngaphandle kwakho. Umahluko ukuyilo: apho isilwanyana sihlala khona, oko sikutyayo, nokuba yeyiphi kwizinto esizenza ngokwendalo oyinike umsebenzi.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukufunda iimfuno, iimveliso neendlela zokuziphatha zesilwanyana, nokusibeka apho senza khona umsebenzi owusizo
- Ukwakha nokuhambisa i-animal tractor yeenkukhu okanye yeehagu
- Ukondla, ukuhlalisa nokukhusela iqela elincinci leenkukhu, nokugcina isifo i-Newcastle singangeni
- Ukugcina iihagu namadada apho enceda khona ifama kwaye engenako ukuyonakalisa
- Ukubeka umzi wenyosi ngokukhuselekileyo nokwenza umhlaba wakho ube likhaya elilungileyo leenyosi

## Beka izilwanyana apho zisebenzela khona

Ibhokhwe idla amagqabi emithi nezihlahla. Ihagu iyagrumba. Inkukhu iyakrwela kwaye itya zonke izibungu eziyifumanayo. Xa zishiywe zenza zodwa, ezi ndlela zenza umonakalo. Xa ziyilwe zisebenze, zikuhlawulela iintsuku ezininzi zomsebenzi.

Izilwanyana ezibekwe kakuhle zinika ubulongwe, umhlaba ogrunjiweyo, ingca emfutshane, izinambuzane ezimbalwa, kunye nenyama, amaqanda, ubisi, uboya nobusi. Iinkabi neembongolo zirhuqa ipulawu, kwaye izinja, kwanee-goose, zikulumkisa xa kufika abantu ongabaziyo.

Ukubeka isilwanyana kwindawo efanelekileyo, jonga izinto ezintathu:

1. **Iimfuno**: oko kufuneka sibe nako. Ukutya, amanzi, indawo yokukhuselwa, isithuba, inkampani.
2. **Iimveliso**: oko sikunikayo. Amaqanda, inyama, ubulongwe, iintsiba, ubushushu.
3. **Iindlela zokuziphatha**: oko sikwenza ngokwendalo. Ukukrwela, ukugrumba, ukudla ingca, ukuzingela izinambuzane.

Emva koko dibanisa nganye kwezi nenye inxalenye yefama. Inkukhu idinga ukutya kwaye iyakuthanda ukukrwela. Ibhedi emva kokuvunwa ifuna ukucocwa ukhula nezinambuzane. Beka zombini kunye, kwaye zozibini iimfuno ziyaneliseka.

Njengoko imozulu iya ngokuthi ingaqikelelekeki, khetha iindidi ezomeleleyo ezikwaziyo ukumelana nobushushu, ubanda nembalela.

## Ii-animal tractor

**I-animal tractor** sisibaya esihambayo: izilwanyana zisebenza kwiqhekeza elinye lomhlaba, emva koko uhambisa isibaya siye kwelandelayo. Iinkukhu, amadada, ii-goose, iihagu neebhokhwe zonke zisebenza ngale ndlela. Ziyabeka ubulongwe, ziyakrwela, ziyadla ingca kwaye ziyagrumba njengoko zisenza rhoqo, kodwa ngoku kwenzeka apho ufuna khona: kwibhedi yegadi phakathi kwezityalo, ngaphantsi kwemithi yeziqhamo, kwidlelo elidiniweyo, kumhlaba ogqunywe yingca nokhula, okanye emazantsi echibi elitsha, apho iihagu zigrumba zize ziliqinise.

I-animal tractor evaliweyo yimeko yeplanga okanye yeentsika egqunywe ngomnatha wocingo, ngelinye icala inophahla lokunika umthunzi nokukhusela emvuleni, kwaye ilula ngokwaneleyo ukuba bantu ababini bayirhuqe. I-animal tractor evulekileyo sisangqa somnatha wombane ohambayo, osebenza ngesixhobo esincinci sokunika uthango umbane, esisebenza ngelanga (solar energiser).

1. Beka i-animal tractor kwiqhekeza ofuna ukulisebenza.
2. Shiya izilwanyana de umhlaba ube ukrwelekile kwaye unobulongwe, kodwa ungabi ungenanto kwaye uqine ngokunyathelwa. Kwiinkukhu ezimbalwa kwibhedi yegadi, oko kuhlala kuyimini embalwa.
3. Hambisa i-animal tractor iye kwiqhekeza elilandelayo.
4. Tyala iqhekeza elisetyenzisiweyo, okanye uyeke ingca yalo ibuye.

Landela iihagu, iibhokhwe okanye iinkomo ngeenkukhu: zisasaza ubulongwe, zibukrwele zibufake emhlabeni kwaye zitya izibungu zeempukane.

## Iinkukhu

Iinkukhu zihlala zizizilwanyana zokuqala zosapho. Zincinci, ziyabiza kancinci kwaye azinzima emhlabeni, kwaye zinika amaqanda, inyama, iintsiba, ulawulo lwezinambuzane, umhlaba ocociweyo nobunye bobona bulongwe butyebileyo kwifama. Zimele zibe kwii-**Zone** 1 nese-2, iindawo ezikufutshane nendlu oyityelela yonke imihla.

### Ukondla iinkukhu

Iinkukhu **zidla yonke into** (omnivores): zitya izityalo nezilwanyana. Inkukhu ebekayo ifuna:

- **Iprotheni**, evela kwizinambuzane, iintshulube, iimbotyi eziphekiweyo nembewu ye-pigeon pea
- **Amandla**, avela kwiinkozo ezinjengombona, amazimba nengqolowa
- **Imifuno eluhlaza**, evela kwingca, ukhula nenkunkuma yegadi
- **I-calcium** yamaqokobhe aqinileyo, evela kumaqokobhe e-oyster atyunyuziweyo, amatye amancinci ekalika okanye amaqokobhe amaqanda omisiweyo atyunyuziweyo
- **Amatye amancinci (grit)**, amatye amancinci aqinileyo okanye isanti engqindilili ayigcina esiswini esigayayo (gizzard) ukugaya ukutya
- **Iivithamini**, ngakumbi i-A evela kumagqabi aluhlaza nakumbona otyheli, kunye ne-D evela elangeni
- **Amanzi acocekileyo** ngamaxesha onke

Inkukhu ebekayo itya malunga ne-110 ukuya kwi-140 g yokutya ngosuku kwaye isela amanzi anobunzima obumalunga nobuphindwe kabini bokutya, ngakumbi xa kushushu.

Ukutya kweenkukhu zamaqanda okuthengiweyo kulinganisiwe kodwa kuyabiza. Uninzi lombona waseMzantsi Afrika, kunye nephantse yonke isoya yalapha, ityalwe ngembewu etshintshwe ngokwemfuza (genetically modified, GM), kwaye ukutya okuthengiweyo kuhlala kuqulathe zozibini, ngoko abanye abalimi be-permaculture bakhetha ukutyala ukutya kwabo. Ukutya okutyalwe ekhaya kusafuneka kulinganiswe: iinkukhu ezondliwa iinkozo kuphela ziyabeka amaqanda ambalwa. Ukuba ukubeka kuyehla, yongeza iprotheni okanye ukutya kweenkukhu zamaqanda okuthengiweyo.

Ukutya okungabizi nto kuyafumaneka kuyo yonke ifama: intsalela yasekhitshini nesonka esindala; ii-slug, ii-snail nezibungu; amagqabi aneeaphid; amagqabi acokisiweyo azele zizinambuzane nembewu; ngaphandle kwenqwaba **ye-compost** (izinto zezityalo ezibolileyo ezondla umhlaba), apho intsalela ibola khona; ubulongwe beenkomo obuzele zizibungu; neentloko zentyatyambo yelanga, ezixhonywe zivuthiwe ukuze iintaka zizitye.

> **Ingcebiso:** Omisa uze utyumze amaqokobhe amaqanda ngaphambi kokuwabuyisela ekutyeni. Iinkukhu ezibona iqokobhe liphelele zinokuqala ukutya amaqanda azo.

Zinike indawo ekhuselekileyo yomhlaba owomileyo nothambileyo, isanti okanye uthuthu lweenkuni zokuhlamba ngothuli (dust bath). Ukuqengqeleka kuyo kugcina iintsiba zazo zicocekile.

### Indlu, amanzi nezilwanyana ezizingelayo

Iinyoka, ii-mongoose, ii-genet, iikati zasendle, izinja, amagundane neentaka ezizingelayo zonke ziyazithatha iinkukhu. Ezilalini, indlu elungileyo yeyona nxalenye ibalulekileyo yokuzikhusela.

1. **Isithuba.** Vumela iinkukhu ezi-3 ukuya kwezi-4 kumitha enye eyisikwere yomgangatho. Iinkukhu ezixinene ziyalwa, ziyagula kwaye zibeka kancinci.
2. **Iintonga zokulala.** Iinkukhu zilala phezulu, njengasemthini. Nika inkukhu nganye i-15 ukuya kwi-20 cm yentonga yokulala, zonke zikwinqanaba elinye, ngaphandle koko ziya kulwa ngeyona iphezulu. Beka iintonga zokulala ngaphezu kweebhokisi zezidleke.
3. **Iibhokisi zezidleke.** Malunga ne-30 cm ububanzi, ubunzulu nokuphakama, enye kwiinkukhu ezine okanye ezintlanu ezibekayo. Iibhokisi zakudala zeziqhamo ziyasebenza. Zifake ingca eyomileyo okanye iziqwenga zeplanga ezigudiweyo.
4. **Umoya.** Vumela umoya udlule endlini, kungabikho moya obandayo ohlaba iinkukhu ebusuku.
5. **Umnatha.** Sebenzisa umnatha wocingo oqinileyo onemingxuma emincinci. Wungcwabe, okanye uwugobe uye ngaphandle emhlabeni, ukuze kungabikho nto igrumba ingene ngaphantsi.
6. **Ebusuku.** Vala iinkukhu ngobusuku ngabunye. Gcina amantshontsho amancinci kwindawo egqunyiweyo enomnatha.
7. **Amanzi.** Ibhotile ye-cold drink ejijekileyo emi kwisitya esingenzulu yenza isisela esilula. Sigcine emthunzini size sicoce rhoqo.

Iinkukhu ziyakrwela nezithombo kunye ne-**mulch**, ingca eyomileyo namagqabi agubungela aze akhusele umhlaba, ngoko zijikelezise: ibhedi emva kokuvunwa, emva koko zibuyele egcekeni lazo de ibhedi elandelayo ilunge.

### Zeziphi iinkukhu?

**Iindidi zasekhaya** ziye zagcinwa eMzantsi weAfrika izizukulwana. Zomelele, ziyazifunela ukutya kwaye zingooma abalungileyo (zikhulisa kakuhle amantshontsho), kodwa zikhula kancinci kwaye zibeka kancinci kuneenkukhu zorhwebo. Iindidi ezaziwayo zaseMzantsi Afrika yi-**Potchefstroom Koekoek**, i-**Venda**, i-**Ovambo**, i-**Naked Neck** ne-**Boschveld**, uhlobo olutsha olwenziwe eLimpopo ukusuka kwiinkukhu ze-Venda, i-Matabele ne-Ovambo.

Kwiimvavanyo ze-ARC ezinokutya nendlu elungileyo, ezi ndidi zaqala ukubeka xa zineeveki ezimalunga nama-24 ukuya kwama-26. Iinkukhu ze-Koekoek zabeka malunga ne-196 yamaqanda ngonyaka, i-Venda malunga ne-154, i-Naked Neck malunga ne-139 kunye ne-Ovambo malunga ne-126. Inkukhu yasezilalini efumana lonke ukutya kwayo ibeka amaqanda amancinci kakhulu, rhoqo amaqela amathathu amalunga ne-10 elowo ngonyaka, kuba iyayeka ukubeka ukuze ihlalele amaqanda ikhulise amantshontsho ayo.

**Ii-hybrid zorhwebo**, **iinkukhu zamaqanda** (layers) zamaqanda kunye **neenkukhu zenyama** (broilers) zenyama, zivelisa ngakumbi kodwa zidinga ukutya nokukhathalelwa kakuhle, kwaye azomelelanga kangako xa zihamba zodwa.

### Ukugcina iinkukhu zisempilweni

**Isifo i-Newcastle** sesona sibulala iinkukhu zasezilalini eMzantsi weAfrika. Singatshabalalisa uninzi lweqela leenkukhu elingagonywanga ngeentsuku.

1. **Gonya.** Amayeza okugonya i-Newcastle eqela elincinci athengiswa kwii-co-op nakwiivenkile zefama. Amathonsi emehlweni akhusela kakhulu; ugonyo emanzini okusela lusebenza nalo. Landela imiyalelo ephakeni.
2. **Phinda.** Ukukhuseleka kuyaphela, ngoko amaqela asezilalini ahlala egonywa kwakhona malunga neenyanga ezintathu ngalinye.

**I-biosecurity** (ukukhusela izifo) yimikhwa yemihla ngemihla egcina izifo kude nefama yakho:

- Gcina iintaka ezintsha zahlukile kwiqela lakho malunga nenyanga, kwaye uzibukele ukuba aziguli.
- Gcina iintaka zasendle kude nokutya namanzi eenkukhu zakho.
- Gcina izindlu zicocekile zomile, kwaye utshintshe izinto zezidleke rhoqo.
- Hlamba izandla emva kokubamba iintaka okanye ubulongwe.
- Ngcwaba okanye utshise iintaka ezifileyo, apho izinja zingenakuzikha.

Xa kufika iintaka ezintsha, linda imini okanye iintsuku ezimbini zokulwa ngoxa **i-pecking order** (ucwangciso lwabaphezulu nabaphantsi) isombulula. Zongeze ngokuhlwa, emva kokuba iqela liye lalala, ezimbini okanye ngaphezulu kunye ukuze kungabikho nanye ehlaselwayo, kwaye kubekho indawo yokuba intaka ehlaselweyo ibaleke.

## Iihagu

Iihagu zezona zicoca kakhulu. Zibeke kumhlaba onzima, okhule ingca nokhula kwaye kwiveki ziya kuba ziwulungisile kunepiki okanye ifolokhwe. Zilungele ii-Zone 2, 3 no-4, kwaye zisebenza kakuhle kwigadi yemithi yeziqhamo nakwi-**food forest**, igadi etyalwe ngamaleko njengehlathi lendalo.

### Ukondla iihagu

Iihagu zitya ingca, izityalo ezikhwelayo, iziqhamo eziwileyo, amandongomane nayiphi na iingcambu ezinokuzigrumba. Zinike amanzi acocekileyo nomthunzi ngamaxesha onke. Zondle imifuno nenkunkuma yegadi evela kwifama yakho, kunye neenkozo okanye ukutya okuthengiweyo. Ungaze uzinike iintsalela zenyama okanye inkunkuma yasekhitshini evela kwezinye izindlu, kwiirestyu okanye kwiihotele. Ukutya okunjalo kunokuthwala **i-African swine fever** (isifo sehagu saseAfrika), esibulala iihagu kwaye akukho unyango okanye ugonyo lwaso.

### Ii-pig tractor

1. Thinga iqhekeza ngothango lombane oluhambayo: ingcingo enye okanye ezimbini ekuphakameni kweempumlo zeehagu.
2. Yeka iihagu zitye uninzi lwezityalo ezingaphezu komhlaba kuqala. Aziyi kugrumba ngoxa kukho ukutya okuninzi ngaphezulu.
3. Qinisekisa ukuba umhlaba ufumile. Iihagu zigrumba kumhlaba othambileyo, hayi kumhlaba oqinileyo nomileyo.
4. Emva kweveki enye iqhekeza liye lagrunjwa. Hambisa uthango.
5. Landela iihagu nge-chicken tractor (indlu yeenkukhu ehambayo) ukuze isasaze ubulongwe bazo.

Kwigadi yemithi yeziqhamo, iihagu zitya iziqhamo eziwileyo nezibungu zeempukane zeziqhamo ezikuzo, nto leyo ncedayo ukophula umjikelo weempukane zeziqhamo.

> **Ingcebiso:** Fundisa iihagu ezincinci uthango lombane zisemancinci, kwisibaya esiqinileyo apho ucingo lombane lugqithe ngaphakathi. Ihagu engazange iluve ucingo iya kutyhala idlule nje.

### Ukugcina iihagu zingaphumi

Ihagu ekhululekileyo inokonakalisa imithi emincinci negadi yemifuno yonke ngobusuku obunye. Zithinge kakuhle, kwaye ukhethe ngononophelo apho zivunyelwe ukuya khona.

Iihagu ziyathanda inkampani, ngoko gcina ubuncinane ezimbini. Ukuba awufuni kuzalisa, gcina iihagu ezisisikazi kuphela okanye iinkunzi ezisikiweyo (castrated). Ziphe indawo yokukhusela eyomileyo engenamoya obandayo ebusika nomthunzi ehlotyeni.

## Amadada

"Awunayo ingxaki ye-snail, unentsilelo yamadada." Eli gama liye linikwe uBill Mollison ngokubanzi.

Amadada alungele i-Zone 2. Akakrwela njengeenkukhu, ngoko anesisa kwi-mulch. Awavumele azulazule kwigadi yemithi yeziqhamo, nakwigadi yemifuno emva kokuvunwa, kodwa awagcine kude nezithombo ezincinci, awazinyathelayo okanye awazityayo.

Amadada atya ii-slug, ii-snail nezinambuzane. Ii-Muscovy ziyatya ingca ziyenze emfutshane. Amaqanda amadada makhulu kunamaqanda amaninzi eenkukhu, kwaye amadada abeka ekuseni kakhulu: awagcine endlini de kube phakathi kwekuseni uze ufumane amaqanda endlini, ingekuko efihlwe egadini. Inyama ye-Muscovy ayinamafutha amaninzi kwaye imnyama.

Oko amadada akudingayo:

1. **Indlu ekhuselekileyo ebusuku**, ngokufanayo neenkukhu.
2. **Amanzi anzulu ngokwaneleyo ukuze intloko yonke ingene.** Amadada ahlamba ukutya emehlweni nasempumlweni. Ibhesini enzulu okanye ibhakethi iyaneleka; akadingi ichibi.
3. **Amanzi amatsha rhoqo.** Amadada angcolisa amanzi awo ngokukhawuleza. Awatshintshe yonke imihla kwaye uwagalele amanzi amadala kwimithi yeziqhamo nakwizityalo ozipheka. Aphethe ubulongwe, ngoko ungawagaleli kwi-morogo okanye nakweyiphi enye into etyiwa iluhlaza.
4. **Uthango.** Uninzi lwamadada efama, anjenge-Pekin, i-runner ne-Khaki Campbell, luvela kwi-mallard yasendle. Ii-mallard ezibaleka zizalana namadada asendle omdabu anjenge-yellow-billed duck zize ziwagxothe. Gcina amadada akho ethinjiwe, kude nemilambo, amadama nezindawo ezimanzi. I-Muscovy luhlobo olwahlukileyo.

Uninzi lwamadada linengxolo. Ii-Muscovy ziyodwa ezithe cwaka: ikakhulu iinkunzi ziyahwaqa (hiss).

## Iinyosi

Iinyosi **ziyi-pollinator** (izinambuzane ezichumisayo): zithwala umgubo ukusuka kwintyatyambo ukuya kwenye ukuze iziqhamo nembewu zakheke. Emhlabeni wonke, malunga neenxalenye ezintathu kwezine zezona zityalo ziphambili zokutya zizuza ekuchumisweni zizilwanyana. Umbona, ingqolowa namazimba ziyachumiswa ngumoya, kodwa uninzi lweziqhamo nemifuno yethu lufuna ii-pollinator.

### Ondla iinyosi

Zama ukuba kubekho into eyintyatyambo kuyo yonke isizini.

- Tyala uthango lweentyatyambo lwemithi **yomdabu**, ekhula apha ngokwendalo, enjenge-karee (*Searsia lancea*) nomnga (sweet thorn, *Vachellia karroo*).
- Tyala amakhala. Aqhakaza ebusika, xa kunqabile ezinye.
- Lima i-lavender, i-rosemary, i-borage, i-rocket, imithi yeziqhamo nezihlahla zeeberry.
- Shiya iqhekeza lengca yasendle nokhula luqhakaze.
- Beka isitya esingenzulu samanzi esinamatye, ukuze iinyosi zisele zingaminzi.
- Yeka ukusebenzisa ityhefu yokutshiza izinambuzane. Ukuba kufuneka utshize, sebenzisa eyona ithambileyo, ngokuhlwa kakhulu xa iinyosi zingabhabhi, kwaye ungaze utshize kwiintyatyambo ezivulekileyo.

Emhlabeni wonke, iinyosi zobusi ziyafa ngenxa yezinambuzane, izifo, indlala netyhefu yezinambuzane. Ibali loMzantsi Afrika linethemba ngakumbi. **I-varroa mite**, isidalwa esiphila kwesinye, yafika apha ngo-1997, kwaye iinyosi zethu zobusi zaye zakwazi ukumelana nayo kwiminyaka embalwa, ngoko abagcini beenyosi apha ngokuqhelekileyo abayinyangi. Esona sisongelo sikhulu yi-**American foulbrood**, isifo esibangelwa yibhaktheriya esibulala iinyosi ezincinci. Sakuqinisekiswa okokuqala eNtshona Koloni (Western Cape) ngo-2009, kwaye ii-spore zaso ziphila amashumi eminyaka kubusi, kwi-wax nakwizixhobo zakudala zomzi wenyosi.

### Iinyosi zobusi zoMzantsi Afrika

Sinazo iinyosi ezibini zobusi: inyosi yaseAfrika okanye yesavanna (*Apis mellifera scutellata*) kuninzi lwelizwe, kunye nenyosi yaseKapa (Cape honeybee, *Apis mellifera capensis*) eNtshona Koloni nasezindaweni ezikufutshane. Zozibini ziyazikhusela ngamandla. Ukuba uphazamisa umzi wenyosi, ziyaluma ngobuninzi, kwaye zingalandela umntu okanye isilwanyana amakhulu eemitha. Funda kwikhosi nakumgcini weenyosi onamava wasekuhlaleni phambi kokuba uvule umzi wakho wokuqala wenyosi.

### Ukubeka imizi yeenyosi

Abagcini beenyosi ngendlela yendalo bavula umzi wenyosi izihlandlo ezimbalwa ngonyaka, ngoko unokuma emaphethelweni e-Zone 2, okanye kwi-Zone 3 okanye 4.

1. **Kude nabantu nezilwanyana.** Gcina imizi yeenyosi kude kakhulu neendlu, iindlela, iindlela ezinkulu, izikolo, abantwana, nezilwanyana ezibotshiweyo okanye ezisesibayeni. Uthango lwezityalo okanye ucingo phambi kwemizi yeenyosi lwenza iinyosi zibhabhele phezulu zidlule ngaphezulu kweentloko zabantu.
2. **Ulinganisiwe kwaye uphakanyisiwe.** Beka umzi ngamnye wenyosi ulingane kwindawo emileyo eqinileyo, ukuze ungathambeki kwaye amakhekhe ahlale engaqhekeki. Uphakamise emhlabeni ukulwa namagundane nokufuma. Imfumba yeetaya zakudala iyasebenza.
3. **Iimbovane.** Misa imilenze yesitendi somzi wenyosi kwiitoti zamanzi okanye zeoyile, okanye uyihlambe ngamafutha anamathelayo.
4. **Ilanga nomoya.** Khusela umzi wenyosi emoyeni onamandla nelangeni elinzima lasemva kwemini, ukuze iinyosi zisebenzise amandla amancinci ukuwupholisa nokuwufudumeza. Jongisa indlela yokungena empuma, kwilanga lasekuseni.
5. **Ukukhuselwa kwemizi.** Ubusi butsala amasela nezilwanyana. Thinga iqela leemizi, okanye ubophe ngetyathanga ukhiye umzi omnye.

### Ukugcina iinyosi ngendlela yendalo

Ukugcina iinyosi ngendlela yendalo kuphazamisa ikholoni kancinci kangangoko. Yithathe ikholoni njengomzimba omnye ophilayo. Yivule ngokungafane, ukuze isidleke sigcine ivumba nobushushu baso. Vumela iinyosi zakhe ikhekhe lazo, kwaye ziphindaphinde ngokuhamba ngamaqela azo. Gcina iinyosi zasekuhlaleni, kwaye imizi emininzi kuphela kangangoko iintyatyambo zasekuhlaleni zinokuyondla. Ungafaki nto emzini wenyosi engaveli kwiinyosi, thatha ubusi kuphela iinyosi ezinokubunikela, kwaye vumela oko zikwenzayo kukukhokele.

Zishiyele ubusi nomgubo wentyatyambo owaneleyo ebusika nasemaxesheni omileyo, endaweni yokuziphuzisa amanzi eswekile. Ungaze uzondle iinyosi ngobusi, kwaye ungaze usebenzise imizi yeenyosi, ii-frame okanye i-wax esetyenzisiweyo ngaphandle kokuba uyazi apho ivela khona: zombini ezi zinto zinokuthwala ii-spore ze-foulbrood.

### Ukuxhelwa yinyosi

Ukuxhelwa ziinyosi ezininzi ngaxeshanye, okanye ukuxhelwa kanye ngumntu one-allergy, kunokubulala.

1. Anxiba i-veil, isuti yeenyosi okanye iimpahla ezinemikhono emide neebhulukhwe ezinde, namagilavu.
2. Sebenza ngokuzola, usebenzisa i-smoker (isixhobo sokutshiza umsi). Ungavuli imizi yeenyosi emini ebandayo, emanzi okanye enomoya, okanye ebusuku.
3. Ukuba umntu uxhelwe, hamba ukusuka kumzi wenyosi. Khupha isihlungu ngokusikrweba ngecala ngenzipho okanye ngommese; ungasicinezeli. Hlamba indawo uze uyipholise.
4. Ukuba ubuso, imilebe, ulwimi okanye umqala ziyavuvuka, ukuba kunzima ukuphefumla okanye ukugwinya, okanye ukuba umntu uyajikeleziswa yintloko, uyaquleka okanye ugqunywe ngamabala olusu asasazekayo, biza i-ambulensi kwangoko: 10177, okanye 112 ukusuka kwiselula. Yenza ngokunjalo nakuye nabani na oxhelwe kaninzi.

Nabani na owakha waba nempendulo embi ekuxhelweni yinyosi akufuneki asebenze neenyosi.

## Izityalo zokutya kwemfuyo

**I-fodder** kukutya kwezityalo okutyalelwa okanye okuqokelelwa izilwanyana. Izona zityalo zilungileyo zokutya kwemfuyo zenza ngaphezu komsebenzi omnye. Ezininzi zii-**legume** (usapho lweembotyi) kwaye zii-**nitrogen fixer**, ezongeza inayitrojeni evela emoyeni emhlabeni. **I-annual** iphila isizini enye; **i-perennial** iphila iminyaka emininzi.

## Zama oku

Nika isilwanyana esinye umsebenzi kule veki.

1. Khetha isilwanyana osigcinayo, okanye osicinga ukusigcina.
2. Ephepheni, zoba iikholam ezintathu: **Iimfuno**, **Iimveliso**, **Iindlela zokuziphatha**. Zalisa nganye.
3. Hamba emhlabeni wakho ephethe uluhlu. Kwimveliso nganye, fumana indawo eyifunayo: ubulongwe kwibhedi, ukukrwela kwiqhekeza elinokhula.
4. Kwimfuno nganye, fumana into esele ikhona efameni eyinikezelayo: intsalela, ukhula, umthunzi, amanzi.
5. Zama olona dibaniso lulungileyo iintsuku ezimbalwa. Kwiinkukhu, zibeke zimbalwa kwibhedi evuniweyo.
6. Bhala oko ukubonayo: ukhula lulahlekile, umhlaba ukrwelekile, amaqanda abekiwe, nawuphi na umonakalo.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Jonga iimfuno, iimveliso neendlela zokuziphatha zesilwanyana ngasinye, uzidibanise nezinye iinxalenye zefama.
- Ii-animal tractor zihambisa izilwanyana emhlabeni ukuze ubulongwe bazo, ukukrwela nokugrumba kwenze umsebenzi wosizo.
- Iinkukhu zidinga ukutya okulinganisiweyo, i-grit, i-calcium, amanzi acocekileyo nendlu ekhuselekileyo kwizilwanyana ezizingelayo, kunye negonyo le-Newcastle rhoqo emva kweenyanga ezimbalwa.
- Iihagu zicoca umhlaba, amadada atya ii-snail, kodwa zozibini kufuneka zithingwe kakuhle.
- Beka imizi yeenyosi kude nabantu, ondla iinyosi ngeentyatyambo unyaka wonke, kwaye ungaze uzondle ngobusi.

Qala ngesilwanyana esinye nomsebenzi omnye. Owesibini uya kuziveza, emva koko wesithathu.
