# Ikhaya elizinzileyo: i-Zone 0 ne-appropriate technology

Embindini wayo yonke ifama kukho ikhitshi. Apho kuya khona amafutha, amanzi nenxalenye enkulu yemali. Usapho lunokukhulisa ukutya okuninzi kodwa lusaswele amandla: lithenga iparafini, lithwala iinkuni iiyure, lihlala ebumnyameni xa umbane ucime.

Ikhaya yindawo apho i-permaculture iphindisela ngokukhawuleza kakhulu. Ilitha nganye yemvula ebanjiweyo, isiqwenga ngasinye senkuni esigciniweyo neyure nganye yelanga lasimahla elisetyenzisiweyo yimali ehlala emzini.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuchaza ukuba zithetha ntoni i-Zone 0 ne-appropriate technology
- Ukucwangcisa indlela yokwenza indlu ifudumale ebusika ipholile ehlotyeni ngelanga
- Ukukhetha isixhobo selanga esifanelekileyo sokukhanyisa, amanzi ashushu, ukupheka nokomisa ukutya
- Ukupheka ngamafutha ambalwa usebenzisa i-wonder bag ne-rocket stove
- Ukuchaza ukuba i-biogas digester iguqula ubulongwe ibe ngerhasi yokupheka njani

## Ikhaya yi-Zone 0

Kwi-permaculture sicwangcisa umhlaba ngee-**zone** (imimandla), ngokokuba siyityelela kangakanani inxalenye nganye. Indlu yi-**Zone 0**, indawo ephakathi yoyilo.

Cinga ngekhaya:

- elakhiwe ngezinto zasekhaya, ezinjengodongwe neenkuni zasekufutshane, ezinokutshintshwa xa zidala
- elihlala lifudumele ebusika lipholile ehlotyeni ngokufudumeza okanye ukupholisa okuncinci
- elenza umbane walo ngelanga, ukuze izibane zihlale zikhanya xa igridi yombane icima
- elipheka ngerhasi eyenziwe ngobulongwe bezilwanyana
- eliqokelela amanzi emvula kwiitanki nakumachibi, anele ukuliwelisa ixesha elomileyo xa esetyenziswa ngononophelo

Izixhobo ezenza oku kube nokwenzeka ziyabizwa ngokuba yi-**appropriate technology** (ubuchwephesha obulula obufanelekileyo): izixhobo neendlela ezilula, ezingabizi, abantu basekuhlaleni abanokuzakha, bazisebenzise bazilungise ngokwabo. Zisebenza ngezakhono nezinto zasekhaya kwaye zihlangabezana neemfuno ezisisiseko zamandla, amanzi, ukutya nenkunkuma. Ezinye ungazenza kule mpelaveki; ezinye zifuna utyalomali oluncinci. Zonke ziyazihlawulela ngokuhamba kwexesha.

Akufuneki ukwenze konke ngaxeshanye. Inyathelo ngalinye longa imali kwaye lenza usapho lukhuseleke ngakumbi.

## Yila indlu ngokwelanga

KuMzantsi weAfrika ilanga lisemntla. Likhwela phezulu ehlotyeni liphantsi ebusika. Indlu esebenza naloo nto ifudumala kwaye ipholile ngokwayo simahla.

1. Beka amagumbi okuhlala aphambili neefestile ezininzi ngaseMntla, ukuze kungene ilanga lasebusika.
2. Yakha umphetho ophumayo wophahla (eaves) okanye i-pergola phezu kweefestile zasemntla. Ithintela ilanga eliphakamileyo lasehlotyeni kwaye ivumela ilanga eliphantsi lasebusika ukuba lingene.
3. Gcina iifestile zasentshona zincinci. Ilanga lasemva kwemini lasehlotyeni lenza indlu ishushu kakhulu.
4. Sebenzisa izinto ezinzima, ezinjengelitye, isitena, udongwe okanye umgangatho wekhonkrithi, apho ilanga lasebusika liwela khona. Le **thermal mass** (izinto ezigcina ubushushu) ifunxa ubushushu emini ibubuyisele ebusuku.
5. Faka isilingi esine-**insulation** (into ethintela ubushushu ukuba bungene okanye buphume). Igcina ubushushu ngaphakathi ebusika kwaye ibugcine bungangeni ehlotyeni.
6. Tyala imithi okanye izityalo ezikhwelayo ezophulukana namagqabi ebusika emntla nasentshona. Zinika umthunzi ehlotyeni zize zivumele ilanga ukuba lingene ebusika.

Ukuba uyakha, gqiba ngezi zinto phambi kokuba kubekwe isitena sokuqala. Kwindlu esele ikhona, qala ngesilingi.

## Amandla elanga

**Amandla ahlaziyekayo** azihlaziya: ilanga, umoya, amanzi ahambayo ne-**biomass** (izinto zezityalo nezilwanyana, ezinjengeenkuni nobulongwe). Amalahle awenzi njalo: xa etshisiwe, aphelile. Amalahle asaveliswa malunga ne-83% yombane woMzantsi Afrika ngo-2024, kwaye ukuwatshisa kungcolisa umoya kwaye kubangela i-**climate change** (utshintsho lwemozulu).

Ilanga ngamandla ahlaziyekayo esiwabelana ngawo sonke. Kwiyure enye, ukukhanya kwelanga okufika kuMhlaba kuthwele phantse amandla alinganayo nawo wonke umhlaba awasebenzisayo ngonyaka. UMzantsi weAfrika ufumana elinye lamalanga anamandla kakhulu naphi na, kwaye iipaneli zelanga ngoku zibiza ngaphantsi kwe-1% yoko zazibiza ngeminyaka yee-1970.

### Iipaneli zelanga

Iipaneli zelanga, ezibizwa nangokuba ziipaneli ze-PV, ziguqula ukukhanya kwelanga zibe ngumbane. Iibhetri zigcina umbane ebusuku.

1. Zibeke apho kungekho nto izithwesa umthunzi phakathi kwentsimbi yesi-9 ekuseni neyesi-3 emva kwemini.
2. Zijonge emntla.
3. Zithambekise ngeengile ezikufutshane nelatitude yakho: malunga needigri ezingama-26 eJohannesburg, ezingama-34 eKapa.
4. Sula uthuli nobulongwe beentaka.

Isethi encinci iqhuba izibane ze-LED, itshaja iifowuni idlale irediyo. Inkqubo enkulu iqhuba ifriji, ithelevishini okanye ikhompyutha. Idinga i-**inverter**, eguqula umbane othe ngqo (DC) wamaphaneli ube ngumbane otshintshanayo (AC) osetyenziswa zizixhobo ezininzi.

Gcina umbane welanga kwizibane, iifowuni, iifriji nee-TV. Ii-geyser, iiketile, izifudumezi neesitovu zitya amandla amaninzi, kwaye ukuziqhuba ngamaphaneli kufuna inkqubo enkulu, ebizayo. Fudumeza amanzi upheke ngezixhobo ezingezantsi.

### I-geyser yelanga

Kumakhaya amaninzi aseMzantsi Afrika i-geyser yeyona isebenzisa umbane omkhulu: uphando lwe-Eskom lwayibeka kuma-39% okusetyenziswa kwekhaya eliqhelekileyo. I-geyser yelanga ifudumeza amanzi kwi-collector (iphaneli eqokelela ubushushu elangeni) yamaphaneli athe tyaba okanye yemibhobho yeglasi ophahleni ize iwagcine kwitanki egqunyiweyo (insulated). Kuhlobo olulula, amanzi ashushu enyuka aye kwitanki ngokwawo, ngoko itanki kufuneka ibe ngaphezu kwe-collector.

- Jonga i-collector emntla uyithambekise njengephaneli yelanga.
- Apho kuba neqabaka, nto leyo ekwiindawo ezininzi zethafa langaphakathi, sebenzisa i-geyser **engathanga ngqo (indirect)**. Ifudumeza ulwelo olungabandi (antifreeze) ngaphakathi kwi-collector endaweni yamanzi. I-geyser ethe ngqo inokukhenkceka iqhume.
- Beka i-element yombane yokugcina (back-up) kwi-timer, ukuze isebenze kuphela xa ilanga lingawugqibanga lo msebenzi.

> **Ingcebiso:** Idrum okanye ibhotile yamanzi epeyintwe ngomnyama efakwe elangeni ekuseni inika amanzi afudumeleyo okuhlamba emva kwemini, simahla.

### Isitovu selanga

Isitovu selanga sipheka ngelanga kuphela, kwaye asibizi nto ukusiqhuba.

- **Isitovu sebhokisi:** ibhokisi egqunyiweyo (insulated), emnyama ngaphakathi, enesigqubuthelo seglasi okanye seplastiki esicacileyo. Sibamba ubushushu njengemoto evaliweyo elangeni kwaye sifikelela malunga ne-100 ukuya ku-150 °C, ezaneleyo ukupheka nokubhaka, kancinci kancinci.
- **Isitovu sephaneli:** iipaneli ezikhazimlayo zibuyisa ukukhanya kwelanga kwipoti emnyama ngaphakathi kwebhegi ecacileyo. Asibizi kwaye silula ukusenza.
- **Isitovu se-parabolic:** isitya esijikayo seesipili sijolisa ukukhanya kwelanga kwipoti. Sifikelela ngaphezu kwe-250 °C kwaye sipheka ngokukhawuleza, kodwa kufuneka sijikwe rhoqo ukulandela ilanga.

1. Pheka phakathi kwentsimbi ye-10 ekuseni neyesi-3 emva kwemini.
2. Sebenzisa iipoti ezibhityileyo, ezimnyama ezinezigqubuthelo ezivalayo ngokuqinileyo.
3. Jika isitovu sijonge ilanga rhoqo emva kweyure okanye malunga nalo.
4. Yongeza ixesha kunaleyo ungayifuna esitovini esiqhelekileyo. Ukuba ilanga liyemka phambi kokuba inyama okanye iimbotyi ziphekwe ngokupheleleyo, ziqhubele phambili ekuphekeni esitovini.

## Ukomisa ukutya ngelanga

Ihlobo linika ngaphezu kwesinokudliwa ngusapho; ubusika bunika kancinci. Ukomisa kuthwala intsalela. Kusebenza kuba ukungunda neentsholongwane ezonakalisa ukutya azikwazi ukukhula ngaphandle kwamanzi.

### Ukomisa elangeni

Ukomisa elangeni ngaphandle yeyona ndlela ibiza kancinci, kodwa icotha: ukutya kunokungunda phambi kokuba komelele, kwaye iimpukane, uthuli nombethe (dew) kuyafikelela. Yenze kakuhle:

1. Sebenzisa iimveliso ezintsha, ezicocekileyo. Zihlambe, uhlambe nezandla zakho.
2. Zinqumle zibe ziziqwenga ezibhityileyo, ezilinganayo.
3. Zomise kwiitreyi okanye kwiirakhi eziphakanyisiweyo, ungaze uzibeke emhlabeni.
4. Zigubungele ngomnatha ococekileyo ukuze zingafikeleli iimpukane.
5. Zingenise ebusuku, okanye uzigubungele ukuze zingafumani mbethe.
6. Jika iziqwenga kanye okanye kabini ngosuku.

### I-solar dryer

**I-solar dryer** (ibhokisi eyomisa ukutya ngobushushu belanga) ikhawulezile kwaye icocekile. Inokuba lula njengebhokisi yeplanga enesigqubuthelo seplastiki esicacileyo neetreyi zomnatha okanye zomnatha womthunzi, okanye iqine njengesakhelo sensimbi esineglasi. Ii-dryer ezivumela umoya oshushu ukuba uhambe kwiitreyi zisebenza ngokukhawuleza, kuba umoya ohambayo ususa ukufuma.

Ukutya kuye komiswa ngokwaneleyo xa:

- **iziqhamo** zinjengesikhumba kwaye ziyagoba ngaphandle kwamabala amanzi xa zicinezelwa
- **imifuno** ibuthathaka iyaqhawuka okanye ikhale xa ishukunyiswa
- **umbona, iimbotyi namandongomane** ziqinile kwaye ziyakhala, zomiswe zaya ngaphantsi kwe-13% yokufuma ngokuqikelelwa, kwiintsuku ezimbini okanye ezintathu emva kokuvuna

Yomisa iinkozo namandongomane ngokukhawuleza, ulahle nawaphi na angunde, atshintshe umbala okanye adukumeke. Ukungunda kwiinkozo ezimanzi kwenza **i-aflatoxin**, ityhefu yesibindi evela ekungundeni engatshabalaliswa kukupheka. Gcina ukutya okomisiweyo kwizikhongozeli ezicocekileyo, ezivalwa ngokuqinileyo ukuze umoya ungangeni, kwindawo epholileyo, emnyama, ulahle nantoni na enuka ukungunda.

Ukufaka iziqwenga zeziqhamo kumxube wejusi yelamuni namanzi kugcina umbala wazo. Ukuntywilisa imifuno ngokukhawuleza emanzini abilayo (blanching) kuyinceda ukuba ihlale ixesha elide.

## Ukupheka ngokugcina ubushushu: i-wonder bag

Ipoti ye-samp neembotyi inokubila kancinci iiyure, kwaye yonke iyure itshisa iinkuni, irhasi okanye iparafini. Isixhobo sokupheka esigcina ubushushu sivumela ipoti igqibe ukupheka ngobushushu bayo.

**I-wonder bag** (ingxowa egcina ubushushu) yingxowa egqunyiweyo (insulated) egcina ipoti ebilayo ishushu iiyure. I-Wonderbag yaphuhliswa eMzantsi Afrika ngo-2008; ibhokisi yengca eyenziwe ekhaya, ezaliswe ngengca eyomileyo okanye iingubo ezindala, isebenza ngendlela efanayo. Ukutshisa amafutha ambalwa kuthetha imali encinci echithwayo kunye nomsi omncinci endlini.

1. Bilisa ukutya kude kubile kakhulu, uze ukuyeke kubile kancinci imizuzu embalwa. I-samp, iimbotyi nenyama enzima zifuna ukubila kancinci ixesha elide. Cwilisa iimbotyi ezomileyo emanzini, uze uzibilise kakhulu ubuncinane imizuzu eyi-10 phambi kokuba zingene engxoweni.
2. Ipoti isagqunyiwe, yifake ngqo engxoweni uze uyivale.
3. Sebenzisa ipoti ehlala kakuhle kwaye izeleyo. Ipoti ezeleyo igcina ubushushu ixesha elide.
4. Musa ukuvula ingxowa ukuze ujonge. Ukujonga ngokunye kukhupha ubushushu.
5. Tya ukutya kusabhobhoza umphunga. Ukuba sele kufudumele nje, kubilise kwakhona kuqala. Ungaze ushiye ukutya engxoweni ubusuku bonke.

## I-rocket stove

Iinkuni zinzima ukuzithwala kwaye zicotha ukukhula kwakhona. Kwaye umsi uyabulala. I-World Health Organization ibala ukuba malunga nebhiliyoni ezi-2.1 zabantu ziyapheka kwimililo evulekileyo okanye kwizitovu ezilula ezitshisa iinkuni, ubulongwe, intsalela yezityalo, amalahle okanye iparafini. Umsi ezindlini zabo wabangela ukufa okuqikelelwa kwizigidi ezi-2.9 ngo-2021. Abasetyhini nabantwana abancinci, abachitha ixesha elininzi ecaleni komlilo, bahlupheka kakhulu.

**I-rocket stove** (isitovu seenkuni esisebenzisa iinkuni ezimbalwa) itshisa iintonga ezincinci kwishimini elifutshane, eligqunyiweyo, elimila okwe-L. Umlilo utshisa ushushu kwaye ucocekile, kwaye ubushushu bunyukela ngqo kwipoti. Uvavanyo lwasentsimini lufumene ukuba ii-rocket stove zisebenzisa iinkuni ezimbalwa ngokuqikelelwa kuma-30 ukuya kuma-50% kunomlilo wamatye amathathu ovulekileyo, zinomsi omncinci kakhulu ne-carbon monoxide encinci. Ezinye ziyathwalwa; ezinye zakhiwe ngaphakathi, zinishimini.

1. Tshisa iinkuni ezomileyo. Iinkuni ezimanzi zinika umsi kwaye zinika ubushushu obuncinci.
2. Faka iintonga ezincinci ezimbalwa ngaxeshanye uzityhale njengoko zitsha.
3. Gcina isithuba somoya ezantsi kweentonga sicocekile.
4. Beka ipoti ngokuqinileyo, ushiye isithuba esincinci ukuze iirhasi ezishushu zijikeleze ipoti.
5. Pheka ngaphandle okanye ekhitshini elinishimini.

I-rocket stove isenza i-carbon monoxide, irhasi ongayibonisiyo okanye ongayinuki. Ungaze ungenise isitovu esitshayo, imbawula okanye umlilo wamalahle egumbini elivaliweyo ukuze ufudumale.

## Ii-biogas digester

Ubulongwe bungamafutha nesichumiso ngaxeshanye, xa sibusebenzisa kakuhle. **I-biogas** yirhasi yamafutha eyenziwa xa ibhaktheriya ibola ubulongwe, intsalela yokutya okanye inkunkuma yasendlini yangasese kwitanki evaliweyo engenamoya. Itanki yi-**biogas digester** (itanki evaliweyo eyenza irhasi yokupheka). I-biogas ngama-50 ukuya kuma-70% yi-methane, inxalenye etshayo. Uninzi lwesinye yi-carbon dioxide, kunye ne-hydrogen sulphide encinci, enuka ngamaqanda abolileyo. Kwikhaya, owona msebenzi ulungileyo kukupheka. Ulwelo oluphumayo, olubizwa ngokuba yi-**slurry**, sisichumiso esihle.

Ungathenga i-digester okanye uyakhe ngeetanki, imibhobho nezixhobo zokudibanisa ezithengwa evenkileni yehardware. Kufuneka ivale ngokupheleleyo ukuze umoya ungangeni.

1. Yondle ngobulongwe obutsha beenkomo, iihagu okanye iinkukhu exutywe nomlinganiselo ofanayo wamanzi, kunye neentsalela zemifuno ezinqunyiweyo.
2. Musa ukufaka izinto ezinjengeenkuni, ingca eyomileyo, amathambo, iplastiki, isepha, i-bleach nezinye iikhemikhali. Iikhemikhali zibulala ibhaktheriya.
3. Yondle kancinci mihla le kunokuba uyondle kakhulu ngexesha elinye. Usapho luqhele ukufuna ubulongwe obutsha beenkomo okanye iihagu ezininzi ngosuku.
4. Yigcine ifudumele. Ibhaktheriya isebenza kakuhle malunga nakuma-30 ukuya kuma-38 °C kwaye yenza irhasi encinci kakhulu ngaphantsi kwe-15 °C ngokuqikelelwa. KwiHighveld naseLesotho, yingcwabe emhlabeni, yigqume nge-insulation okanye ubeke itonela encinci yeplastiki phezu kwayo.
5. Vavanya amalungu ukubona apho kuvuza khona ngamanzi anesepha, apho amaqamza abonisa ukuvuza. Gcina amalangatye kude ne-digester nemibhobho, upheke kwikhitshi elinomoya ofikelelayo kakuhle, uvale itephu yerhasi emva kokupheka.
6. Gubungela imingxuma yokungenisa neyokuphumisa. Ungaze ungene kwi-digester okanye emngxunyeni, kwanokuba ayinanto: iirhasi ezingaphakathi zinokubulala.
7. Yeka i-slurry ime okanye uyiguqule ibe yi-compost iiveki ezimbalwa. Emva koko yifake kwimithi yeziqhamo nakwizityalo ozipheka, ungaze uyifake kwimifuno yamagqabi (morogo) oyityayo iluhlaza.

## Zama oku

Yenza ibhokisi yengca upheke ngayo kule veki.

1. Fumana ibhokisi, ikreyithi, ibhaskithi okanye ibhakethi endala enkulu ngokwaneleyo ukuthwala ipoti yakho kunye nesithuba sobubanzi besandla macala onke.
2. Gquma ezantsi ngokuqinileyo ngengca eyomileyo, iingubo ezindala, iikhusheni okanye iziqwenga zefoam.
3. Beka ipoti kuyo uze ugcwalise macala onke ngezinto ezifanayo ngokuqinileyo. Phakamisa ipoti uyikhuphe, ushiye isidleke.
4. Yenza umqamelo oqinileyo ngezinto ezifanayo wokubeka phezulu.
5. Pheka into ebiza ukubila kancinci ixesha elide, njenge-samp neembotyi. Yibilise, uyeke ibile kancinci imizuzu embalwa, faka ipoti enesigqubuthelo esidleleni uyigubungele ngomqamelo.
6. Yiyeke iiyure ezimbalwa ngaphandle kokuyivula. Bhala ixesha elithathe ngalo kunye namafutha owasebenzisileyo xa ithelekiswa nendlela eqhelekileyo.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Ikhaya yi-Zone 0. I-appropriate technology yizixhobo ezilula onokuzakha uzilungise ngokwakho.
- Jonga indlu neepaneli zelanga emntla, uze usebenzise i-thermal mass nemiphetho yophahla ukubamba ilanga lasebusika nokuthintela ilanga lasehlotyeni.
- Gcina umbane welanga kwizibane, iifowuni neefriji. Fudumeza amanzi upheke nge-solar geyser, isitovu selanga, i-wonder bag, i-rocket stove okanye i-biogas.
- Yomisa ukutya ngokukhawuleza, ngococeko, kwaye kungekho emhlabeni.

Qala ngesixhobo esinye kule nyanga. Xa inqwaba yeenkuni ihlala ixesha elide, uya kwazi ukuba yeyiphi onokuyakha okulandelayo.
