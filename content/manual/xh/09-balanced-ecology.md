# Ukwakha indalo elungeleleneyo nevelisayo

Indalo yayilima lo mhlaba kudala phambi kwethu, ngaphandle kwengxowa yesichumiso. Ingca ibamba ilanga nemvula. Iinkomo neenyamakazi zidla ingca. Ubulongwe bondla umhlaba, umhlaba uze wondle ingca. Akukho nto ilahlwayo, kwaye akukho mntu utshizayo.

Asidingi kuba ziingcali zesayensi ukuze silime ngale ndlela. Sidinga ukuyiqonda injini yendalo ngokwaneleyo ukuze sidibanise ifama yethu kumandla ayo asimahla, singalwi nayo. Esona sixhobo silungileyo sisekokona sidala: ixesha elichithwa ukubukela.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuchaza ukuba yintoni i-ecosystem nokuba ipiramidi yobomi isebenza njani
- Ukusebenzisa i-succession ukuphilisa umhlaba ongenanto okanye odleke kakhulu
- Ukwakha amakhaya kwifama yakho abancedisi basendle abanjengamaxoxo, ii-lizard nezikhova
- Ukongeza ukwahlukahlukana ukuze ifama yakho yabele umngcipheko kwaye igcine izinambuzane zilawulekile

## Yintoni i-ecosystem?

**I-ecology** kukufundisisa indlela izinto eziphilayo ezinxibelelana ngayo kunye nendawo ezijikelezwe yiyo. **I-ecosystem** liqela lezityalo, izilwanyana nobomi bomhlaba abahlala kunye nomhlaba, amanzi, umoya nokukhanya kwelanga abajikelezileyo. Lingaba lincinci njengechibi okanye unqwaba we-**compost** (izinto zezityalo ezibolileyo ezondla umhlaba), okanye likhulu njengamadlelo e-Highveld.

Yonke i-ecosystem inamaqela amathathu asebenzayo:

- **Abavelisi:** izityalo eziluhlaza. Zona zibamba ukukhanya kwelanga zize zondle yonke enye into.
- **Abatyi:** izilwanyana, ukusuka kwii-aphid ukuya kwiinkomo nabantu. Zona zitya izityalo okanye zityana.
- **Ababolisi:** ukungunda, ibhaktheriya, iintshulube nobunye ubomi bomhlaba. Bona babola izityalo ezifileyo, ubulongwe nemizimba, babuyisele izondlo emhlabeni.

Iinkqubo ezine ziyigcina isebenza:

- **Umjikelo wamanzi.** Imvula iyangena emhlabeni okanye iqukuqele esihla, izityalo ziyayisebenzisa, ize ibuyele emoyeni.
- **Umjikelo wezondlo.** Izondlo zisuka emhlabeni ziye kwisityalo, emva koko ziye kwisilwanyana, zize zibuyele emhlabeni ngobulongwe nokubola.
- **Ukuhamba kwamandla.** Izityalo zibamba amandla elanga zize ziwadlulisele phezulu kwikhonkco lokutya. Amandla awajikeleziswa kwakhona: amagqabi aluhlaza kufuneka awabambe amatsha yonke imihla.
- **Utshintsho ngokuhamba kwexesha.** Ukuxubana kwezityalo nezilwanyana kutshintsha unyaka ngonyaka (jonga i-Succession apha ngezantsi).

Xa ezi zisebenza kakuhle, umhlaba usinika simahla amanzi acocekileyo, umhlaba ochumileyo, ukuchumiswa kwezityalo, ulawulo lwezinambuzane namadlelo.

## Amakhonkco okutya nepiramidi yendalo

Cinga ngobomi befama njengepiramidi.

- Esiseleni zizityalo, ezibamba amandla elanga. Zimi phezu komhlaba nababolisi bawo.
- Ngaphezulu kwazo kukho izilwanyana ezitya izityalo, ezinjengeembungu, ii-aphid, amagundane neenkomo.
- Ngaphezulu ngakumbi kukho **ii-predator** (izilwanyana ezidla ezinye) ezizityayo, ezinjengee-ladybird, ii-spider, iintaka nee-lizard.
- Phezulu kuphela kukho ii-predator ezimbalwa eziphezulu (top predators), ezinjengezikhova, oonkozi nempungutye.

Inqanaba ngalinye eliya phezulu linezinto eziphilayo ezimbalwa, kuba amandla alahleka kwinyathelo ngalinye. Kudingeka ingca eninzi ukondla inkomo enye, namagundane amaninzi ukondla isikhova esinye.

Oku kuthetha ntoni efameni:

1. Musa ukulinga ukubulala zonke izinambuzane ezonakalisayo. Ukuba utshiza ii-aphid zonke ngetyhefu, ii-ladybird neentaka ezizityayo ziyalamba zize zihambe. Xa ii-aphid zibuya, akukho nto izimisayo.
2. Ondla esiseleni. Yonke ipiramidi imi phezu kwezityalo nomhlaba, ngoko impilo yomhlaba ibona ukuba ubomi obungakanani, nesivuno esingakanani, umhlaba wakho onokubuthwala.
3. Banike ii-predator ikhaya (jonga i-Habitat apha ngezantsi).

## I-succession

**I-succession** kukutshintsha kwezityalo nezilwanyana kwindawo, inyathelo ngenyathelo, ngokuhamba kwexesha. Yindlela umhlaba oziphilisa ngayo. Emva kokulima, ukudliwa gqitha yimfuyo, umlilo okanye isikhukula:

1. **Izityalo zokuqala ukumila (pioneer plants)** ziza kuqala, ngokuqhelekileyo ukhula lwee-**annual** (izityalo ezikhula zife emva konyaka omnye) abalimi abangaluthandiyo. Zikhula ngokukhawuleza, zigubungela umhlaba ongenanto zize zibambe. Xa zifa, ziwondla.
2. **Izityalo ezomeleleyo ngakumbi ziyalandela:** **ii-perennial** (izityalo ezihlala iminyaka emininzi), iingca, imifuno enevumba nezihlahla ezincinci, ezininzi zazo zii-**legume** (usapho lweembotyi neephizi) ezongeza inayitrojeni emhlabeni. Zikhusela izityalo ezincinci zize zondle izinambuzane neentaka.
3. **Uluntu lwezinto eziphilayo ezihlala ixesha elide** lweza ekugqibeleni. Umhlaba uya enzulu nasekuchumeni, kwaye umhlaba ube nobomi obuninzi, neentlobo ezininzi zobomi.

### Akuzo zonke iindawo ezihlala zibe lihlathi

Iincwadi ezibhalwe kumazwe anemvula eninzi zithi umhlaba wonke uphela uyihlathi. KuMzantsi weAfrika akunjalo. Ihlathi lendalo ligubungela ngaphantsi kwe-1% yoMzantsi Afrika, kwiziqwenga ezincinci apho imvula ininzi kwaye iqabaka lisenzeka kancinci, njengakwi-Garden Route nakwi-escarpment emanzi empuma. Uninzi lomhlaba wethu uphela uyinto eyahlukileyo, endala kwaye ityebile ngeentlobo ngokufanayo:

- **Amadlelo engca yendalo (grassland)** agubungela inxalenye enkulu ye-Highveld, iDrakensberg neLesotho. Iqabaka, umlilo nokudla kwemfuyo kugcina imithi ingekho. Aya madlelo asemandulo anezityalo ezininzi ezinqabileyo kwaye abamba amanzi atyebisa imilambo yethu emikhulu.
- **I-savanna** (bushveld), ingca enemithi ethe saa, igubungela inxalenye enkulu yasemantla nasempuma. Umlilo, ukudla ingca kwezilwanyana nokudla amagqabi emithi kugcina ukulingana phakathi kwengca nemithi.
- **I-fynbos**, eNtshona Koloni enemvula yasebusika, ifuna umlilo malunga rhoqo emva kweminyaka eyi-10 ukuya kweyi-15. Izityalo ezininzi ze-fynbos, ezifana nee-protea, zikhulula zize zihlume imbewu yazo emva komlilo kuphela.
- **IKaroo** eyomileyo ngokwendalo ngamatyholo (shrubland).

Ukuba ugcina umlilo ungabikho kwaye imfuyo idla kakhulu, amadlelo engca yendalo ne-savanna ayaxinana ngamatyholo anameva. Oku, **ukuxinana kwamatyholo (bush encroachment)**, sele kugubungele ii-hektare ezingaphezu kwezigidi ezi-7 eMzantsi Afrika, kwaye kulahlekisa amadlelo namanzi.

> **Qaphela:** Ukutyala imithi kwidlelo lengca yendalo okanye kwi-fynbos akukuphuculi. Kuyonakalisa i-ecosystem enqabileyo enika amanzi. Tyala imithi ejikeleze ikhaya nakwi-**food forest** (ihlathi lokutya), uze ushiye idlelo lakho lendalo (veld) lihleli liyidlelo lengca yendalo, i-savanna okanye i-fynbos.

### Ukusebenzisa i-succession kwifama yakho

1. **Izityalo zokuqala ukumila kuqala.** Kumhlaba ongenanto, ohlwempuzekileyo, hlwayela izityalo zokugquma ezomeleleyo nee-**green manure** (izityalo ezityalelwa ukutyebisa umhlaba), ezinjenge-cowpea. Tyala izityalo ezifuna kakhulu emva kwazo.
2. **Yenza i-food forest yakho ngamanqanaba.** Zalisa phakathi kwemithi yeziqhamo emincinci ngezityalo ezixhasayo ezikhula ngokukhawuleza, uzinqumle zibe yi-**mulch** (ingca okanye amagqabi agubungela umhlaba) njengoko imithi ithatha indawo.
3. **Khetha izityalo ezixhasayo ezihlala apho zityalwe khona.** Imithi emininzi "ebopha inayitrojeni" kwiincwadi zangaphandle, enjenge-black wattle, i-*Leucaena* ne-red sesbania, isasazeka ingalawuleki eMzantsi Afrika. Tyala i-pigeon pea endaweni yazo, kunye nomnga womdabu (sweet thorn, *Vachellia karroo*) apho ulungele indawo. Umnga usasazeka kwidlelo elidliwe gqitha, ngoko uwugcine ngaphakathi kwiindawo zakho ezityaliweyo.
4. **Hlonipha isiphelo sendawo yakho.** Kwiindawo zamadlelo engca yendalo, i-**Zone 5** yakho, ummandla wakho wasendle, kufuneka ibe ngamadlelo engca yendalo asempilweni, kungabi lihlathi elitshaliweyo.

### Ukulima ngokulwa nendalo, okanye ngokuhamba nayo

Ukulima kwale mihla okuninzi kugcina umhlaba kwinqanaba lokuqala lokungabi nanto: lima, tshiza, chumisa, tyala isityalo esinye, uphinde. Indalo igubungela umhlaba ongenanto ngokhula ukuze iqale ukuphilisa, umfama azitshize. Oku kuthatha ixesha, amafutha neekhemikhali, kushiya umhlaba ongazinzanga, ohlwempuzekileyo ngobomi kwaye ukhawuleza ukonzakala yimbalela, zizinambuzane nge-**erosion** (ukukhukuliseka komhlaba).

I-permaculture ihamba kwelinye icala. Sakha umhlaba nokwahlukahlukana, ukuze ifama ichume ize izinze ngakumbi unyaka ngamnye, ibe nobomi obaneleyo bokulawula izinambuzane nezifo.

## I-habitat

**I-habitat** (indawo yokuhlala yezilwanyana) yikhaya lesityalo okanye lesilwanyana: indawo enika ukutya, amanzi, indawo yokukhuseleka, isithuba nendawo ekhuselekileyo yokuzalela. Ixoxo lidinga amanzi okubeka amaqanda alo, ingca ende namatye ejikeleze ichibi ukuze lizifihle kwiintaka neenyoka, kunye nezinambuzane zokutya. Elo chibi yi-habitat yalo. Ukulahleka kwe-habitat lelinye lezizathu eziphambili ezenza izityalo nezilwanyana ziphele.

Cinga nge-habitat njengesibhengezo somsebenzi. Ifama inemisebenzi emininzi: ukutya izinambuzane ezonakalisayo, ukuchumisa iintyatyambo, ukusebenzisa kwakhona inkunkuma. Endaweni yokubhatala ukuze yenziwe, sibhengeza ukufuna abancedisi basendle, kwaye isibhengezo kufuneka sinike ukutya, amanzi nendawo ekhuselekileyo yokulala.

1. **Nika amanzi.** Isitya samanzi seentaka okanye ichibi elincinci liyatsala iintaka, amaxoxo nee-dragonfly, ezitya izinambuzane ezininzi. Beka amatye okanye iintonga emanzini ukuze iinyosi zisele zingantywili. Thinga amachibi ngothango okanye uwagubungele ngomnatha oqinileyo ukuze abantwana abancinci bangawi kuwo. Amaxoxo, ii-tadpole nezibungu zee-dragonfly zitya izibungu zee-mosquito; musa ukongeza i-mosquitofish, kuba nazo zitya ii-tadpole.
2. **Shiya iindawo zasendle.** Gcina nayiphi na idlelo lendalo (veld) okanye indawo emanzi emhlabeni wakho. Le yi-Zone 5 yakho. Ukuba unganayo, buyisela ikona enye kwizityalo zomdabu. Mhlawumbi esona sinto sibalulekileyo ongasenza kwi-habitat, kuba izityalo zasekhaya zondla izinambuzane neentaka zasekhaya ezininzi kunezityalo ezingezizo ezasekhaya.
3. **Tyala uthango oluphilayo.** Tyala imigca exubileyo ecaleni koocingo nasemaphethelweni eentsimi: i-Cape honeysuckle (*Tecoma capensis*), i-plumbago (*Plumbago auriculata*), i-sand olive (*Dodonaea viscosa*), umqokolo (Kei apple, *Dovyalis caffra*), kunye ne-karee nezinye iintlobo ze-*Searsia*. Zinika indawo yokukhuseleka, ukutya neendawo zokwakha izidleke, kwaye zithintela umoya.
4. **Yakha imfumba yamatye neyeenkuni ezinkulu** kude nendlu nalapho abantwana badlala khona. Ii-lizard zotha ematyeni afudumeleyo zize zitye izinambuzane ezininzi. Iinkuni ezinkulu zikhusela amabhungane, amaxoxo nokungunda.
5. **Beka ibhokisi yesikhova** kwipali, emthini okanye kwisakhiwo ubuncinane ku-3 m ukusuka emhlabeni, ingafikeleleki kwiikati. Intsapho yezikhova (barn owls) inokutya amagundane neempuku angama-1 000 okanye ngaphezulu ngonyaka. Izikhova zifa ngokutya amagundane aneetyhefu, ngoko ukuba ufuna izikhova, yeka ukusebenzisa ityhefu yamagundane. Apho izikhova ziyoyikwa njengomqondiso ombi, thetha nabamelwane bakho ngomsebenzi owenziwa zizikhova.

## I-biodiversity

**I-biodiversity** (ukwahlukahlukana kwezinto eziphilayo) yintlobo-ntlobo yobomi kwindawo: iintlobo ezininzi zezityalo, izilwanyana, izinambuzane, ukungunda nobomi bomhlaba, nokwahluka phakathi kweyona ntlobo nganye. Hamba kwidlelo lendalo (veld) elinempilo okanye kwi-fynbos uyibone. Indawo etyalwe ngeepayini yona ibonisa okuchaseneyo: ihektare emva kwehektare yomthi omnye, kwaye kukhula izinto ezimbalwa kakhulu ngaphantsi kwawo. Iintlobo ezininzi zepayini zisasazekela kumadlelo engca yendalo nakwi-fynbos, kwaye zisebenzisa amanzi amaninzi.

Ukwahlukahlukana kuyanceda:

- **Kwabela umngcipheko.** Ukuba isityalo esinye siyasilela ngenxa yembalela, izinambuzane okanye izifo, ezinye zisanika isivuno.
- **Kunceda ekulawuleni izinambuzane.** Ukuxuba izityalo kondla kukhusele ii-predator ezitya izinambuzane ezonakalisayo, kwaye izinambuzane zinzima ukufumana isityalo zaso azithandayo phakathi kwezininzi. Iigadi ezahlukahlukeneyo azikho kwingozi enkulu yokuqubuka okunzima kwezinambuzane, kodwa azisoze zingabi nazo izinambuzane kwaphela.
- **Kwakha umhlaba onempilo.** Iingcambu ezahlukileyo neentsalela zezityalo zondla uluhlu olubanzi lobomi bomhlaba.

Ukukwakha:

1. Sebenzisa **ukutyala iintlobo ezininzi kunye (polyculture)**: izityalo ezininzi kunye kwibhedi yegadi enye okanye kwintsimi enye, endaweni yesityalo esinye kwimigca emide.
2. Tyala ii-perennial (izityalo ezihlala iminyaka emininzi, ezinjengemithi yeziqhamo, imifuno enevumba nemifuno ehlala iminyaka emininzi) ecaleni kwezityalo zakho ze-annual.
3. Tyala i-food forest, igadi enamaleko ukusuka kwimithi emide ukuya kwii-**groundcover** (izityalo ezigubungela umhlaba) nezityalo zeengcambu.
4. Gcina izilwanyana ezixubeneyo, nganye inomsebenzi: iinkukhu zitya izinambuzane ezonakalisayo, kwaye ubulongwe beenkomo bondla i-compost.
5. Lima iintyatyambo, kuquka ezomdabu, ukondla iinyosi nezinye izinambuzane eziluncedo unyaka wonke.

Xa oko kuveliswa linxalenye elinye kondla enye (ubulongwe kumhlaba, intsalela yezityalo kwizilwanyana, izinambuzane ezonakalisayo kwiintaka), ifama iqala ukuzikhathalela.

## Zama oku

Yenza ikona ye-habitat kule veki.

1. Khetha ikona engasetyenziswayo ecaleni kweebhedi zakho zemifuno, ubuncinane i-2 m ngo-2 m, kude nalapho abantwana badlala khona.
2. Faka emhlabeni ibhesini endala, idrum enqunyulweyo okanye ithayara efakwe iplastiki ngaphakathi. Yizalise ngamanzi uyijikeleze ngamatye. Beka amatye ambalwa okanye iintonga ngaphakathi ukuze izinambuzane zikwazi ukunyuka zisuke. Yigubungele ngomnatha oqinileyo ukuba kukho abantwana abancinci ngakubo.
3. Yakha imfumba encinci yamatye ngakwicala elinelanga (emntla) nemfumba yeenkuni ezinkulu ngakwicala elinomthunzi.
4. Tyala, okanye ushiye, izityalo zomdabu nengca ende ejikeleze indawo.
5. Yityelele ekuthomeni kokusa nasekuhlwa kanye ngeveki. Bhala phantsi sonke isidalwa osibonayo.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Ipiramidi yobomi imi phezu kwezityalo nomhlaba. Ukuba ubulala zonke izinambuzane ezonakalisayo, uyalambisa ii-predator ezikunceda.
- I-succession yindlela umhlaba oziphilisa ngayo. KuMzantsi weAfrika idla ngokuphela ngamadlelo engca yendalo, i-savanna okanye i-fynbos, kungabi lihlathi.
- Amanzi, iikona zasendle, uthango oluphilayo, imfumba yamatye neebhokisi zezikhova zizisa abancedisi basendle ukuba basebenze efameni yakho.
- Ukwahlukahlukana kwabela umngcipheko kwaye kugcina izinambuzane zilawulekile.

Emva kwenyanga, hlala ecaleni kwechibi uze ubale oko kungene kulo. Undwendwe ngalunye olutsha ngumsebenzi ongafanga umqeshile.
