# Ukulawula izinambuzane ngendlela yendalo

Umbungu okwikhaphetshu akayena utshaba. Kukutya. Iintaka, amaxoxo, ii-lizard, ii-spider, ii-ladybird nee-wasp ezincinci zonke ziphila ngezidalwa esizibiza ngokuthi zizinambuzane ezonakalisayo. Ukuba sibulala zonke ezonakalisayo, sibaphelisela nabancedisi ukutya, kwaye igadi ayizinzi ngakumbi, iyazinza ngaphantsi.

Ngoko xa izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ziqhambuka ngequbuliso ngobuninzi, siyithatha njengomyalezo. Kukho into engalingananga: umhlaba odiniweyo, izityalo ezinxaniweyo yimbalela, okanye isityalo esinye esininzi kakhulu endaweni enye. Isisombululo esikhawulezileyo sendalo singasindisa isivuno salesi sizini. Umsebenzi osisigxina kukunyanga unobangela.

Oku akukuhlala ungenzi nto. "Fumana isivuno" ngomnye wemigaqo yethu, kwaye usapho kufuneka lutye. Sikhusela izityalo ezisoloko zisengozini, ezinjengeekhaphetshu nezithombo. Kodwa asilindeli ukuba zonke izityalo ziza kusinda. Isahlulo esincinci sibuyela kwinkqubo, kwaye inkqubo yenza uninzi lokulawula izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ngenxa yethu.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuqaphela izinambuzane ezonakalisayo eziphambili zegadi eMzantsi weAfrika nokujongana nenye nenye ngaphandle kwetyhefu
- Ukutsala ii-predator egadini yakho ngeentyatyambo, ngamanzi nangendawo yokukhusela
- Ukusebenzisa ii-trap crop nabamelwane abalungileyo, nokwazi ukuba zeziphi iinkcazo ze-companion planting ezingqinelweyo
- Ukwenza isitshizi segalikhi, itswele nepilipili nokusisebenzisa ngaphandle kokonzakalisa iinyosi
- Ukucwangcisa i-crop rotation ukuze kungabikho sapho lwezityalo lubuyela kwibhedi enye kangangeminyaka emithathu ukuya kwemine

## Kutheni singasebenzisi ityhefu yeekhemikhali

Ityhefu yezinambuzane yeekhemikhali ibiza imali kwisizini nganye kwaye yenzakalisa abantu nezilwanyana. Ukulawula izinambuzane ngendlela yendalo kubiza kancinci xa sele kusekwe, kwaye kuba lula nyaka ngamnye.

Ityhefu ikwabamba umfama kumjikelo:

1. Isitshizi sibulala izinambuzane ezonakalisayo, sibulale kwakunye **nee-predator**, izilwanyana nezinambuzane ezizingela zize zidle izinambuzane ezonakalisayo.
2. Izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ziyazala ngokukhawuleza kakhulu kunee-predator zazo.
3. Izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ziyabuya kuqala, kwaye ngoku akukho nto izitya.
4. Umfama utshiza kwakhona. Ingxaki iyakhula, kwaye izinambuzane ezininzi ezonakalisayo zimelana netyhefu.

Ngaphambi kokulima ngeekhemikhali kwakungekho gama elithi "organic" (ngendlela yendalo), kuba konke ukulima kwakusenziwa ngendlela yendalo. Iindlela ezindala zisasebenza, kwaye igadi eselula izidinga kakhulu.

## Indlela yokulawula izinambuzane ezonakalisayo: inyathelo ngenyathelo

Uninzi lokulawula izinambuzane ezonakalisayo lwenzeka phambi kokuba kufike inambuzane. Izityalo ezomeleleyo emhlabeni ochumileyo (Isahluko 8) ziyamelana nokuhlaselwa. **I-biodiversity**, uluhlu olubanzi lwezityalo, izinambuzane, iintaka nobomi bomhlaba, inika yonke inambuzane ezonakalisayo utshaba (Isahluko 9). Ibhloko enkulu yesityalo esinye yenza okuchasene noko: inambuzane iwufumana lula umthombo wokutya kwayo kwaye iyanda.

Xa izinambuzane ezonakalisayo zifika, yenza la manyathelo ngokulandelelana:

1. **Bukela.** Hamba egadini kabini ngeveki ubuncinane, ujonge ngaphantsi kwamagqabi. Izinambuzane ezonakalisayo zilawuleka lula xa uzifumana kwangoko.
2. **Tsala ii-predator.** Zinike ukutya, amanzi nendawo yokukhusela.
3. **Zikhe ngesandla.** Khupha imibungu, ii-snail namabhungane. Nyonyoba amaqela e-aphid.
4. **Sebenzisa izithintelo.** Umnatha, umnatha womthunzi, iikhola noothango zigcina izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ngaphandle.
5. **Zisa iintaka.** Phakathi kwezityalo, iinkukhu okanye amadada akwindlu ehambayo, **i-animal tractor** (Isahluko 5), ahlambulula ii-slug, ii-snail nemibungu yasemhlabeni. Zigcine kude nezithombo ezincinci, kuba ziyaluxovula.
6. **Tshiza kuphela njengendlela yokugqibela**, kwaye tshiza kuphela izityalo ezihlaselweyo.

Ukulingana kunokuthatha izizini ezimbalwa ukuba kumile. Ukuba ityhefu incinci kwaye ukwahlukahlukana kuninzi, kufika ngokukhawuleza.

## Izinambuzane ezonakalisayo ezixhaphakileyo eMzantsi weAfrika

Mazi utshaba lwakho phambi kokuba wenze. Uninzi lwezinambuzane ezonakalisayo ziinambuzane, kwaye zitya ngeendlela ezimbini. **Izinambuzane ezifunxa ijusi** zibhoboza isityalo zisele ijusi yaso: ii-aphid, ii-scale insect, ii-mealybug, ii-whitefly, ii-thrips, ii-red spider mite (izalamane ezincinci zee-spider) kunye nee-root-knot nematode (iintshulube ezincinci kakhulu ezingabonwayo ngamehlo, ezenza amaqhuma kwiingcambu). Xa zininzi ziyawuthambisa umzimba wesityalo, kwaye ezininzi zithwala iintsholongwane (viruses) ukusuka kwesinye isityalo ziye kwesinye. **Izinambuzane ezitya izityalo** zihlafuna amagqabi, iziqu, iziqhamo okanye iingcambu: imibungu, amabhungane, ii-slug nee-snail. Zinkulu, ngoko ngokufuthi unokuzikha ngesandla. Iintsele, iinungu neentaka ezithile nazo zizinambuzane ezonakalisayo.

### Ii-aphid

Ii-aphid zizinambuzane ezincinci, ezithambileyo, ezimile okwepere, eziluhlaza, ezimnyama okanye ezirhwada, ezixineneyo kumahlumela amatsha nangaphantsi kwamagqabi. Ikhaphetshu, iimbotyi neephizi zilimala kakhulu. Ziluthintela ukukhula kwezityalo, zijike amagqabi, kwaye zithwala iintsholongwane. Emozulwini efudumeleyo, ezisetyhini ziza nabantwana abaphilayo ngaphandle kokudibana nenkunzi, ngoko iqela lingaqhuma ngeeveki ezimbalwa. Xa sele kuxinene, ii-aphid ezinamaphiko ziyabhabha ziye kwizityalo ezintsha.

1. Nyonyoba amaqela amancinci ngeminwe yakho, okanye uwahlambe ngomjelo onamandla wamanzi.
2. Khusela ii-ladybird, ii-hoverfly, ii-lacewing nee-parasitic wasp. Ziyazitya ii-aphid okanye ziyazala ngaphakathi kuzo.
3. Lawula iimbovane ezizikhuselayo (jonga Iimbovane, ngezantsi).
4. Tyala i-nasturtium emphethweni webhedi yegadi njenge-**trap crop**, isityalo esithandwa yinambuzane ngaphezu kokutya kwakho (jonga I-companion planting).
5. Ungongezi ubulongwe obutsha okanye inayitrojeni ngokugqithisileyo. Ukukhula okuthambileyo, okukhawulezayo, okunejusi kutsala ii-aphid.

> **Ingcebiso:** Iqela le-aphid eselijike laba mdaka-nsundu libe lithambile njengephepha lixelwe zii-parasitic wasp. Lishiye njalo. Ii-wasp ezilandelayo ziyaphuma kulo.

### Ii-cutworm

Ii-cutworm ziimbungu ezityebileyo, ezimdaka-rhwada ezijija zibe yi-C xa ziphazanyiswa. Zizimela emhlabeni emini, ziphume ebusuku zinqumle izithombo emgangathweni womhlaba: iitumato, iikhaphetshu, umbona neembotyi.

1. Susa ukhula kwibhedi iiveki ezimbalwa phambi kokutyala, ukuze imibungu emincinci ingabi nanto yokutya.
2. Xa ufumana isithombo esinqunyiweyo, mba ngobunono ujikeleze. I-cutworm ihlala iphakathi kweesentimitha ezimbalwa. Yisuse.
3. Beka ikhola ejikeleze isithombo ngasinye: ityhubhu yekhadibhodi, okanye ibhotile yeplastiki enqunyulwe phezulu naphantsi, ityiwe emhlabeni nge-3 ukuya kwi-5 cm.
4. Phendula umhlaba phambi kokutyala uvumele iintaka neenkukhu zihlole kuwo.

### I-African bollworm

I-African bollworm (*Helicoverpa armigera*) ngumbungu onemigca, oluhlaza, omdaka okanye obomvu obuthambileyo, ogqobhoza kwiitumato, iinkobe zombona, iimbotyi, iiphizi, ii-pepper namazimba. Umngxuma omncinci ojikelezileyo kwitumato luphawu oluqhelekileyo.

1. Jonga izityalo rhoqo ngoxa ziqhakaza ziqhamisa. Khupha amaqanda nemibungu.
2. Khupha uze utshabalalise iziqhamo ezonakeleyo, ukuze imibungu engaphakathi ingakwazi ukugqiba umjikelo wobomi bayo.
3. Khusela ii-parasitic wasp, izinambuzane ezizingelayo neentaka.
4. Ukuba kufuneka utshize, sebenzisa i-Bt (*Bacillus thuringiensis*), ibhaktheriya yendalo ebulala imibungu kuphela. Isebenza kwimibungu emincinci, phambi kokuba ingene kwisiqhamo.

### I-diamond-back moth

I-diamond-back moth (*Plutella xylostella*) yeyona nambuzane ibi kakhulu kwikhaphetshu, i-broccoli, i-cauliflower, i-rape nezalamane zazo. Umbungu mde malunga ne-1 cm, uluhlaza okukhanyayo kwaye ubukhali kumacala omabini. Xa uwuchukumisa uyagxuma umva uze uwe ngomtya wesilika. Imibungu emincinci shiya "iifestile" ezibonakalayo kwigqabi; emikhulu itya imingxuma ngqo ngaphakathi. Le bhabhathane lasebusuku liyoyisile ityhefu yezinambuzane yeekhemikhali ezininzi, ngoko ukulawula ngendlela yendalo kubaluleke kakhulu apha.

1. Jonga ngaphantsi kwamagqabi uze ususe imibungu ngesandla.
2. Khusela ii-wasp ezincinci ezizala ngaphakathi kwemibungu. Ityhefu izibulala kuqala.
3. Gubungela izityalo ezincinci ngomnatha ocolekileyo okanye ngomnatha womthunzi.
4. Yiyeke ibhedi ize ingatyalwa izityalo zosapho lwekhaphetshu, ukuze umjikelo wobomi bebhabhathane lasebusuku wophuke.
5. Ukunkcenkcesha ngokutshiza ngaphezulu kuwisa imibungu emincinci emininzi. Ukuba kuyadingeka, tshiza i-Bt kwimibungu emincinci.

### I-fall armyworm

I-fall armyworm (*Spodoptera frugiperda*) yeza ivela kwiiMelika. Yaqinisekiswa okokuqala eMzantsi Afrika, eLimpopo, ngenyanga yeyoMdumba ngo-2017, yaza yasasazeka ngokukhawuleza. Ihlasela kakhulu umbona, kwakunye namazimba nezinye iindidi zengca. Jonga i-"Y" ephantsi-phezulu ephaphathekileyo entloko, neendawo ezine ezimnyama ezimile okwesikwere kwinxalenye yomzimba yesibini ukusuka ekugqibeleni. Imibungu emikhulu itya imingxuma engalinganiyo yaye izalisa ifanele (indawo ephakathi apho amagqabi amatsha avela khona) yombona oselula ngobulongwe obufana nomgubo wamaplanga.

1. Ukususela ngomhla umbona uvela emhlabeni, hlola kabini ngeveki. Jonga ngaphakathi kwefanele ufumane ubulongwe obutsha.
2. Nyonyoba amaqela amaqanda (amaqela anoboya emagqabini) nemibungu emincinci.
3. Tyala kwangoko, ngexesha elinye nabamelwane bakho, ukuze umbona wakho ungabi mncinci xa inani lee-armyworm liphezulu.
4. Tyala umbona uxutywe neembotyi, ii-cowpea okanye amathanga. Iintsimi ezixutyiweyo zinazo ngakumbi iimbovane, ii-earwig, izinambuzane ezizingelayo nee-parasitic wasp ezitya ii-armyworm.
5. Ukuba kufuneka utshize, faka i-Bt kwifanele ngoxa imibungu isemincinci.

### Iimpukane zeziqhamo

Iimpukane zeziqhamo ziqanda phantsi kwesikhumba seziqhamo ezivuthayo. Imibungu itya ngaphakathi, kwaye isiqhamo siyabola siwe. UMzantsi Afrika unee-Mediterranean fruit fly ne-Natal fruit fly, ne-Oriental fruit fly (*Bactrocera dorsalis*), eyafunyanwa okokuqala eLimpopo ngo-2010 kwaye ngoku isele ihleli kwiiphondo ezininzi ezisemntla. Impukane yamathanga yenza okufanayo kumathanga, kwii-melon nakumakhukhamba.

1. Emva kweentsuku ezimbalwa nganye, qokelela zonke iziqhamo eziwileyo nezonakeleyo. Zivale ngaphakathi kwingxowa yeplastiki uze uyishiye elangeni elishushu de imibungu ife, okanye uzigqibele emhlabeni ubuncinane nge-50 cm ngobunzulu.
2. Vuna iziqhamo kwangoko xa sele zivuthiwe.
3. Biyela iingxowa zephepha okanye zelaphu phezu kwee-guava, iimango neepesika eziselula.
4. Xhoma izicupho ezinesithako esitsala iimpukane emithini.
5. Sebenzisana nabamelwane bakho. Iimpukane zeziqhamo ziyatshintsha zisuka kwigadi ukuya kwenye, kwaye ingadi enye yemithi yeziqhamo engakhathalelwanga ibangela ukuba zonke ezinye zigcwale kwakhona.

### Ii-slug nee-snail

Ii-slug nee-snail zihlala ezindaweni ezimanzi kwaye zitya amagqabi, zibangela umonakalo ngenene xa zininzi okanye xa zifikelela kwizithombo. **I-mulch**, ingubo yezinto zezityalo ezomileyo esizisasaza emhlabeni, izigcina zimanzi zikhuselekile, kodwa i-mulch erhabaxa, eyomileyo, ekrwexayo ayizifanelanga kakhulu kunezinto ezimanzi, ezithambileyo.

1. Zikhe ngetoshi ngokuhlwa, ingakumbi emva kwemvula.
2. Beka ibhodi okanye ingxowa endala emhlabeni omanzi. Ekuseni qokelela ii-snail ezifihlwe phantsi kwayo.
3. Yiyeke amadada okanye iinkukhu zihlambulule iibhedi phakathi kwezityalo.
4. Khuthaza amaxoxo (frogs, toads), iintaka namabhungane omhlaba.
5. Tsalela i-mulch kude nezithombo ezincinci de ziomelele.

Ii-snail nee-slug zingathwala i-rat lungworm, i-parasite efumaneka kumagundane aseMzantsi Afrika enokugulisa abantu kakhulu. Nxiba iiglavu okanye uhlambe izandla emva kokuzikha, uze uhlambe kakuhle imifino (morogo) phambi kokuba uyitye. Asisebenzisi ii-slug pellet: zinetyhefu kwiintja nakwiikati kanye nakwii-snail.

### Iintaka

Uninzi lweentaka ngabahlobo. I-red-billed quelea yeyona ingaphandle: ibhabha ngamaxhoba amakhulu kwaye inokuhlutha amazimba, i-millet nengqolowa, ingakumbi xa imbewu yengca endle inqabile.

1. Gubungela iindawo ezincinci zeenkozo ngomnatha.
2. Oyikisa iintaka ekuseni kakhulu nasemva kwemini, xa zondla, ngengxolo, iiflegi okanye ngetheyiphu ekhazimlayo.
3. Vuna kwangoko xa iinkozo sele zivuthiwe.
4. Ungabeki ityhefu. Ukhozi nezikhova ezitya iintaka ezifileyo nazo ziyafa.

### Iimbovane

Iimbovane azitye kakhulu izityalo. Zondliwa yi-**honeydew**, ulwelo olumnandi oluveliswa ngee-aphid, ii-scale insect nee-mealybug, kwaye ngenxa yoko ziyazikhusela ezi zinambuzane zifunxa ijusi kwii-ladybird nakwii-wasp. I-honeydew ikwabangela ukungunda okumnyama kwamagqabi, okuvala ukukhanya. Kwezinye iindawo iimbovane zizingela ii-termite nemibungu, ngoko senza kuphela apho zifuya khona izinambuzane ezifunxa ijusi.

1. Emithini yeziqhamo, bophelela umgca wamafutha okanye into abambelelayo ejikeleze isiqu, kwisiqwenga selaphu okanye setheyiphu endaweni yokuba kube sekhobeni lomthi.
2. Nqumla amasebe achukumisa umhlaba okanye udonga. Iimbovane ziwasebenzisa njengeebhulorho.
3. Lawula ii-aphid nee-scale, iimbovane ziya kuyeka umdla.

### Iintsele neenungu

Ii-"mole" ezitya iibhalbhu, iitapile nebhatata eMzantsi Afrika zihlala ziziintsele: izilwanyana ezifana negundane ezisunduza iindulo zomhlaba ezisuka kwimigodi enzulu. Ii-golden mole sisilwanyana esahlukileyo. Zimba imigodi ngaphantsi nje komhlaba zize zitye izinambuzane neentshulube, hayi izityalo. Ezinye iintlobo zinqabile, ngoko zishiye zodwa.

1. Gquma umphantsi weebhedi ezuphakanyisiweyo, nemingxuma yokutyala yezityalo ezixabisekileyo, ngomnatha wocingo oqinileyo.
2. Tyala iibhalbhu nemithi emincinci kwiibhasikithi zocingo.
3. Wamkela ii-mole snake nezikhova. Ungasebenzisi ityhefu: intsele ebulawe yityhefu ibulala isikhova, inyoka, inja okanye ikati eyityayo.
4. Iinungu zimba iingcambu zize ziqebe ikhoba lomthi. Uthango oluqinileyo, olunomnatha ongcwatyelwe nzulu emhlabeni, luzithintela ekumbeni ngaphantsi.

## Khuthaza ii-predator

Banike abazingeli **i-habitat**, indawo enokutya, amanzi nendawo yokukhusela abayidingayo, baze bangene: amatye neengodo kwii-lizard, ichibi elincinci lamaxoxo, i-mulch yamabhungane omhlaba, iintyatyambo zee-wasp nee-hoverfly.

- **Oonwabu nee-lizard** zitya izinambuzane kwaye zikhuseleka phakathi kwamatye afudumeleyo.
- **Amaxoxo** (frogs and toads) atya ii-slug nezinambuzane. Adinga indawo emanzi okanye ichibi elincinci.
- **Ii-ladybird** nemibungu yazo zitya ii-aphid ezininzi.
- **Ii-hoverfly** zibukeka njengeenyosi ezincinci. Imibungu yazo itya ii-aphid.
- **Ii-lacewing** zizinambuzane ezithambileyo, eziluhlaza. Imibungu yazo itya ii-aphid, ii-mite nemibungu emincinci.
- **Ii-parasitic wasp** zincinci kwaye azinakubaxela abantu. Zizala amaqanda azo ngaphakathi kwemibungu nee-aphid.
- **Amabhungane omhlaba, ii-praying mantis nee-spider** zizingela phantse yonke inambuzane encinci kunazo.
- **Iintaka ezidla izinambuzane** zitya imibungu, amabhabhathane asebusuku, ii-termite, ii-scale insect kwanee-snail.

> **Ingcebiso:** Aziyonto yonke ii-ladybird ngabahlobo. Ezikhazimlayo zitya ii-aphid. Ezi-orenji ezingakhazimliyo ezinamachaphaza amnyama amaninzi nomqolo onoboya obuncinci, kunye nemibungu yazo etyheli enameva, zitya amagqabi amathanga, iitapile nezalamane zazo. Funda ukuzahlula.

### Iintyatyambo ezondla ii-predator

Ii-wasp, ii-hoverfly nee-lacewing ezikhulileyo ziphila ngokutya i-nectar nomgubo wokuchumisa, kwaye iintyatyambo ezincinci ezivulekileyo ezinjengezo zosapho lwekherothi zizifanele ngakumbi. Tyala umxube ontyatyamba unyaka wonke:

- Imifuno enevumba entyatyambileyo: i-dill, i-coriander, i-parsley ne-caraway, namakherothi ambalwa ashiywe ukuba antyatyambe
- I-yarrow, i-calendula, i-chamomile, i-zinnia, intyatyambo yelanga, i-borage ne-lemon verbena
- Ii-marigold zaseFrance nezaseAfrika, ne-cosmos (kufuphi nedlelo lendalo, nqumla iintloko phambi kokuba imbewu iwe, kuba i-cosmos isasazeka ingene kwingca yendalo)
- Iintyatyambo **zomdabu**, ezo zivela apha ngokwendalo: i-gazania, ii-African daisy (*Arctotis*, *Osteospermum*, *Dimorphotheca*), i-pincushion (*Scabiosa*) nee-everlasting (*Helichrysum*)

Shiya imifuno enevumba nemifuno embalwa ukuba intyatyambe kwibhedi nganye. Abancedisi bahlala apho kukhona ukutya.

## I-companion planting

**I-companion planting** kuthetha ukutyala izityalo, imifuno enevumba neentyatyambo kunye ukuze zincedisane. Yindala kakhulu. Abalimi baseTshayina babetyala i-fern encinci ebhadula emanzini kunye nerayisi iinkulungwane, kuba yongeza inayitrojeni emanzini. KwiiMelika, umbona, iimbotyi ne-squash zazikhula kunye njengo-"odade abathathu": umbona uxhasa iimbotyi, iimbotyi zongeza inayitrojeni, namagqabi amakhulu e-squash anika umhlaba umthunzi aze acinezele ukhula.

Ii-trap crop, iintyatyambo zee-predator nokutyala izityalo ezixutyiweyo kuvavanywe kakuhle. Ezinye iingxelo ezininzi ziingxelo zabalimi kuphela. Zizame, ujonge ngononophelo, ugcine oko kusebenza kumhlaba wakho.

**Ukutyala okuxutyiweyo.** Umxube wezityalo wondla ubomi bomhlaba obuninzi nee-predator ezininzi. Izinambuzane ezininzi zifumana isityalo sazo sokutya ngemilo, ngombala nangevumba lasyo, kwaye ibhedi enemilo nemibala emininzi iyazidida, ngoko ezinye ziyahamba.

**Ii-trap crop.** I-trap crop isityalo esityalwa kufutshane nesityalo sokutya ukutsalela inambuzane kude. I-nasturtium etyalwe kufutshane nesityalo ihlala itsalela ii-aphid, ingakumbi ii-aphid ezimnyama, kude naso. Ukuyisebenzisa:

1. Tyala i-nasturtium emphethweni webhedi, hayi embindini.
2. Yijonge kabini ngeveki.
3. Xa amagqabi egcwele ii-aphid, wakhe uwanikele iinkukhu.

I-trap crop ongaze uyijonge iba yindawo yokuzalela.

**Izinto ezidida ivumba.** Ivumba elinamandla lingafihla isityalo esizingelwa yinambuzane. Abalimi begadi batyala amatswele ejikeleze amakherothi ngokuchasene ne-carrot fly. Uphando lwaseNgilani lufumene ukuba isebenza kuphela xa amatswele emaninzi kakhulu kunamakherothi, kwaye kuphela ngoxa amatswele esaselula enamagqabi, phambi kokuba enze iibhalbhu.

**Izityalo ezomeleza.** I-basil etyalwe kunye neetumato yehlise ii-thrips nee-whitefly kancinci kwezinye iimvavanyo. Ibango lokuba iphucula incasa yazo alikangqineki.

### Izityalo ezinokugxotha izinambuzane

- **Ii-marigold zaseFrance nezaseAfrika** zehlise ii-whitefly kwiitumato zendlu yezityalo kwiimvavanyo. Zibambezela ii-root-knot nematode kuphela xa zityalwa zishinyene njenge-cover crop (izityalo zokugquma) iinyanga ezi-2 ukuya kwezi-4 phambi kwemifuno. Izityalo ezimbalwa ezisasazekileyo zenza kancinci.
- **I-mint, i-rosemary, i-sage, i-lavender ne-wild wormwood** (*Artemisia afra*, esetyenziswa ngokwesiko ukugcina izinambuzane kude) zithiwa ngabalimi ziyehlisa ii-aphid namabhabhathane asebusuku ekhaphetshu. Kufanelekile ukuzama. Tyala i-mint kwitina, kuba isasazeka ngokukhawuleza.

## Abamelwane abalungileyo nabangafanelekanga

Umgaqo ongqinelwe kakuhle ulula: gcina izityalo **zosapho olunye** zahlukene. Iitapile, iitumato, ii-brinjal nee-pepper zonke ziyabandezeleka ngee-blight, ii-wilt nee-nematode ezifanayo, ngoko umqolo wesinye ecaleni kwesinye uzidlulisela ingxaki. Eseleyo yetafile ivela kumava abalimi begadi. Qala ngayo, uze ekuhambeni kwexesha ukholose ngamanqaku akho.

| Isityalo | Abamelwane abalungileyo | Zigcine zahlukene ne- |
|---|---|---|
| Iimbotyi | Umbona, iitapile, amakherothi, ikhaphetshu, amakhukhamba | Amatswele, igalikhi, ii-leek |
| Usapho lwekhaphetshu | Amatswele, i-celery, ibhitrudi, iitapile, iletisi, imifuno enevumba | Iitumato, ii-strawberry |
| Amakherothi | Amatswele, ii-leek, ii-chive, iiphizi, iletisi | — |
| Ipilipili ne-pepper | Amakherothi, amatswele, i-basil, i-parsley | Iitapile, usapho lwekhaphetshu |
| Umbona | Amathanga, iimbotyi, iiphizi, amakhukhamba | — |
| Usapho lwamatswele | Amakherothi, iletisi, ibhitrudi, usapho lwekhaphetshu, iitumato | Iiphizi, iimbotyi |
| Iiphizi | Amakherothi, ii-radish, amatheniphu, umbona, amakhukhamba | Usapho lwamatswele |
| Iitapile | Iimbotyi, umbona, ikhaphetshu | Iitumato, ii-brinjal, ii-pepper, amathanga, amakhukhamba |
| Ithanga, i-squash, i-butternut | Umbona, iimbotyi, ii-radish | Iitapile |

> **Ingcebiso:** Abalimi bafumanisa ukuba iintyatyambo zelanga zicothisa ukukhula kwezityalo ezikufutshane nazo. Zityale emaphethelweni egadi, apho zisondla iinyosi khona.

## Izitshizi zokulawula izinambuzane ezenziwe ekhaya

Isitshizi yindlela yokugqibela. Kwanesitshizi "sendalo" sibulala ii-ladybird, ii-hoverfly neenyosi osebenze nzima ukuzitsala. Igalikhi, itswele nepilipili kakhulu zigxotha izinambuzane endaweni yokuzibulala, kwaye ivumba liyaphela, ngoko isitshizi kufuneka siphindwe.

### Isitshizi segalikhi, itswele nepilipili

Esi sitshizi sigxotha izinambuzane ezithambileyo ezinjengee-aphid nemibungu emincinci. Isepha incedisa ukuba sinamathele emagqabini.

Udinga:

- Itswele eli-1 eliphakathi
- Iziqwenga ezi-4 zegalikhi
- Iikomityi ezi-2 zamagqabi amatsha e-mint, okanye amathonsi angama-20 amafutha e-peppermint
- Izipuni ezinkulu ezi-2 zomgubo wepilipili okanye i-cayenne pepper, okanye isandla sepilipili ezishushu ezintsha
- Izipuni ezinkulu ezi-2 (30 ml) zesepha ethambileyo engamanzi, okanye isepha eqinileyo ehlanjululwe emanzini, engenabhleshi, engenasisusi-mafutha okanye ivumba elinamandla
- Iilitha ezi-3 zamanzi

Indlela:

1. Nxiba iiglavu zerabha kwaye gcina izandla zakho kude namehlo. Ipilipili negalikhi ziyatshisa.
2. Nquma itswele, igalikhi, i-mint nepilipili. Zityumze kwi-mortar and pestle (isixhobo sokutyumza), okanye uzixube ngomatshini wokuxuba kunye nelitha eli-1 lamanzi malunga.
3. Gubungela uzishiye zintywile emanzini iiyure ezimbalwa.
4. Hluza ngelaphu elicolekileyo okanye isihluzi.
5. Yongeza amanzi aseleyo ayiilitha ezi-2 uze udibanise isepha ngobunono.
6. Yitshele kwibhotile yokutshiza uze uyibhalele ilebhile. Ungaze usigcine isitshizi kwibhotile ye-cooldrink okanye yobisi.
7. Sivavanye kumagqabi ambalwa uze ulinde usuku olunye okanye ezimbini. Ukuba amagqabi abonisa amabala atshisiweyo okanye aphuzi, yongeza amanzi amaninzi.
8. Tshiza ngaphezulu nangaphantsi kwamagqabi ezityalo ezihlaselweyo kuphela, emva kwemini kakhulu okanye ngokuhlwa xa iinyosi sele ziyekile ukubhabha. Musa ukutshiza iintyatyambo ezivulekileyo, okanye izityalo ezibunile ebushushwini bemini.

Sisebenzise kanye okanye kabini ngeveki, nasemva kwemvula enkulu. Yenza ibhetshi entsha ngalo lonke ixesha, uze uhlambe imifuno etshizelweyo phambi kokuba uyitye.

I-nicotine iyayetyhefa abantu nezilwanyana kunye nezinambuzane, ngoko asenzi sitshizi sogwayi okanye secuba (snuff). Ingena ngesikhumba somntu otshizayo, kwaye umntwana ongayisela angafa. Asenzi nezitshizi ngeziqhamo ze-syringa (*Melia azedarach*) okanye i-thorn apple (moonflower, *Datura*), kuba nazo zinetyhefu.

## I-crop rotation

**I-crop rotation** kuthetha ukuhambisa usapho ngalunye lwezityalo kwibhedi eyahlukileyo kwisizini nganye, ukuze izinambuzane nezifo zingaqokeleli. Ukuba utyala isityalo esinye kwindawo enye unyaka nonyaka, umhlaba ulahlekelwa zizondlo ezifanayo ngalo lonke ixesha, ngoxa izinambuzane zomhlaba zilinda ukutya kwazo ukuba kubuye. I-rotation iphula umjikelo wobomi bazo kwaye ivumela umhlaba ukuba uphinde uphile.

### Ukujikelezisa ngosapho lwezityalo

Izityalo zosapho olunye zidinga ukutya okufanayo kwaye ziyabandezeleka ngezinambuzane ezifanayo. Linda ubuncinane iminyaka emithathu, kungcono emine, phambi kokuba usapho lubuyele kwibhedi. I-rotation elula yeebhedi ezine:

1. **Unyaka 1:** usapho lwekhaphetshu (umzekelo ikhaphetshu)
2. **Unyaka 2:** usapho lwekherothi okanye izityalo zeengcambu (umzekelo amakherothi)
3. **Unyaka 3:** usapho lweembotyi (umzekelo iimbotyi)
4. **Unyaka 4:** usapho lwamatswele (umzekelo amatswele)

Oku kulungele abalimi abatyala isityalo esinye kwibhedi nganye.

| Usapho | Izityalo |
|---|---|
| Usapho lwekhaphetshu | Ikhaphetshu, i-cauliflower, i-broccoli, i-kale, i-rape, i-mustard greens, itheniphu, i-radish |
| Usapho lwetumato | Iitumato, iitapile, ii-pepper, ipilipili, ii-brinjal |
| Usapho lwekherothi | Amakherothi, i-celery, i-parsley, i-coriander, i-dill |
| Usapho lwebhitrudi | I-Swiss chard, ibhitrudi, isipinatshi, imbuya (morogo) |
| Usapho lwethanga | Amakhukhamba, amathanga, i-butternut, i-squash, ii-melon, amakhabe |
| Usapho lweembotyi (ii-legume) | Iiphizi, iimbotyi, ii-cowpea, amandongomane, iindlubu (jugo beans), iimbotyi ze-soya |
| Usapho lwamatswele | Amatswele, ii-leek, igalikhi, ii-spring onion, ii-chive |
| Usapho lwengca | Umbona, i-sweetcorn, amazimba, i-millet |

Iitapile zizosapho lwetumato. Ungalandelisi iitumato, ii-pepper okanye ii-brinjal ngeetapile, okanye iitapile zilandelwe zizo. Apho i-bacterial wilt okanye ii-nematode zibe yingxaki, shiya ikhefu elide ngakumbi.

### Ukujikelezisa ngokokuba zilamba kangakanani

Sinokuhlela izityalo ngokokuba zilamba kangakanani:

- **Izityalo ezidla kakhulu** zidinga umhlaba otyebileyo: ikhaphetshu, i-broccoli, i-cauliflower, umbona, iitumato, iitapile, amathanga ne-butternut.
- **Izityalo ezidla kancinci nezidla ngokuphakathi** zidinga okuncinci: iletisi, i-Swiss chard, amakherothi, ibhitrudi, amatswele, igalikhi nee-radish.
- **Abakhi bomhlaba** ngee-**legume**, izityalo zosapho lweembotyi: iimbotyi, iiphizi, ii-cowpea, amandongomane neembotyi ezibanzi. Iibhaktheriya ezikumaqhuma amancinci (nodules) kwiingcambu zazo zithatha inayitrojeni emoyeni ziyenze ifumaneke ezityalweni.

Jikelezisa ngamanyathelo amathathu:

1. Lima izityalo ezidla kakhulu emva kwee-legume, okanye emva kwe-**compost** (inkunkuma yezityalo nezilwanyana ebolileyo) eninzi okanye ubulongwe.
2. Zilandelise ngezityalo ezidla kancinci.
3. Emva koko lima ii-legume ukwakha kwakhona umhlaba, uze uqalise kwakhona.

Oku kulungele iibhedi ezixutyiweyo: hambisa iqela lezityalo ezincedisanayo kumanyathelo ngokudibeneyo.

Uninzi lwenayitrojeni ebotshwa yi-legume ingena embewini yayo kwaye iphuma nesivuno. Shiya iingcambu emhlabeni uze ubuyisele iziqu namagqabi kwibhedi okanye kunqwaba we-compost.

## Zama oku

Hlola ibhedi enye kule veki, uhlangane nezidalwa ozabelana nazo.

1. Khetha ibhedi enye yemifuno. Thatha incwadi yamanqaku.
2. Hamba ngokucotha ecaleni kwayo ekuseni kakhulu okanye ngokuhlwa. Phendula amagqabi alishumi esityalo ngasinye.
3. Bhala phantsi yonke into ephilayo oyibonayo, enonakalisayo okanye encedayo. Zoba ezo ungakwaziyo ukuzibiza ngamagama.
4. Phawula nawuphi na umonakalo: imingxuma, "iifestile", amagqabi ajijekileyo, izithombo ezinqunyiweyo, ubulongwe kwifanele yombona.
5. Kwinambuzane nganye enonakalisayo, khetha inyathelo lokuqala kweli sahluko uze ulenze namhlanje.
6. Hlwayela umqolo omfutshane we-coriander, i-dill okanye ii-marigold esiphelweni webhedi.
7. Hlola ibhedi enye emva kweveki uthelekise amanqaku akho.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Ukuqhambuka kwezinambuzane luphawu lokungalingani. Nyanga unobangela, hayi uphawu kuphela.
- Ityhefu ibulala ii-predator kunye nezinambuzane ezonakalisayo, kwaye ihlala yenza ingxaki ibe mbi ngakumbi.
- Hlola kabini ngeveki, ufumane izinambuzane ezonakalisayo kwangoko uze uzisuse ngesandla, ngezithintelo okanye nge-trap crop.
- Ondla abancedisi ngeentyatyambo, ngamanzi, nge-mulch nangamatye, kwaye ugcine usapho olufanayo lwezityalo lwahlukene luhlala luhamba.
- Tshiza kuphela njengendlela yokugqibela, ngokuhlwa nakude neentyatyambo. Asiyenzi isitshizi sogwayi.

Isizini ngesizini, uluhlu lwabancedisi encwadini yakho yamanqaku luyanda. Lujonge lukhula, uze uyeke isitshizi siqokelele uthuli.
