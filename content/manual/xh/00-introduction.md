# Intshayelelo

Kunqabile ukuba abalimi abancinci abatyebe kakhulu babe ngabo banomhlaba omkhulu kakhulu. Ngabo abo umhlaba wabo ubasebenzelayo: umhlaba obamba amanzi, imithi eyondlayo ikhusele, nezilwanyana ezityebisa umhlaba.

Yiloo nto i-permaculture ayinikayo kumfama woMzantsi weAfrika. Imvula yethu ayiqinisekanga, iimbalela ziya zinde, kwaye imbewu, isichumiso neetyhefu ezithengiweyo ziyabiza. I-permaculture iphendula ngoyilo: sibukela indlela indalo esebenza ngayo, size sicwangcise ifama ukuze amanzi, umhlaba, izityalo nezilwanyana zixhasane, kusetyenziswa ikakhulu oko sele kukho emhlabeni.

UBill Mollison, omnye wabasunguli be-permaculture, wathi ngokulula: "Utshintsho olukhulu esifuna ukulwenza luphuma ekusebenziseni lusiya ekuveliseni, nokuba kungowona mlinganiselo mncinci, kwiigadi zethu."

**Ekupheleni kwale ntshayelelo uya kukwazi:**

- Ukuchaza ukuba kutheni i-permaculture ilungele ifama encinci eMzantsi weAfrika
- Ukufumana isahluko somsebenzi okuphambi kwakho
- Ukwazi apho ungajonga khona igama elitsha

## Ngubani esi sikhokelo silungiselwe yena

Abalimi abancinci nabalimi basekhaya eMzantsi weAfrika, kunye nabacebisi, abafundi nabasebenzi basentsimini abasebenza nabo. Udinga isiqwenga somhlaba, nokuba sincinci kangakanani, nenkalipho yokuqala.

## Apho esi sikhokelo sivela khona

URory Clark wahlanganisa esi sikhokelo. Saqala njengencwadi ethi *The Permaculture Gardening Handbook* yekhosi yoqeqesho yeprojekthi ethi Reducing Vulnerability from Climate Change (RVCC), eyayiqhutywa yi-UNDP norhulumente waseLesotho ngo-2020 nango-2021. Ukususela ngoko sihloliwe saza sahlengahlengiswa kuMzantsi Afrika.

Iinxalenye ezithile zithathwe, ngemvume, kwincwadi ethi *Introduction to Permaculture and Homestead Gardening* (2014) ye-African Conservation Trust.

## Indlela yokusebenzisa esi sikhokelo

1. **Qalisa ngeengcamango.** Isahluko 1 sichaza i-permaculture nemigaqo yayo elishumi elinesibini. Izahluko 2 no-3 zibonisa indlela yokucwangcisa ifama yakho ngokujikeleza ilanga, umoya, amanzi nomlilo.
2. **Emva koko yiya emsebenzini.** Izahluko 4 ukuya kwi-11 zijongana nezityalo, izilwanyana, imithi, amanzi, umhlaba, indalo, izinambuzane nekhaya. Esinye nesinye sime sodwa kwaye siphela ngomsebenzi othi "Zama oku".
3. **Zama into enye ngexesha.** Yibukele isebenza phambi kokuba wongeze elandelayo.
4. **Sebenzisa Uluhlu lwamagama (Glossary).** Amagama obuchwephesha anjenge-swale ne-mulch achazwa xa avela okokuqala, kwaye onke achazwa kunye kuluhlu lwamagama ekupheleni.
5. **Fumana amanqaku asemva.** Amanqaku ngomthetho nangendlela yokugcina abantu nezilwanyana bekhuselekile aqokelelwe kwindawo enye ngasemva.

Esi sikhokelo sikwakhona ngesiNgesi, isiZulu, iSesotho, iTshivenḓa neXitsonga, njengeedrafti ezisahlolwa ngabantu abazithetha kakuhle. Nolu lwimi lwesiXhosa lulidrafti elenziwe ngomatshini nalo. Ukuba kukho into engacacanga, thelekisa nesiNgesi.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- I-permaculture yakha ifama ekondla ngamaxesha anzima, ngoyilo kungekhona ngezinto ezithengiweyo.
- Qalisa ngezahluko 1 ukuya kwezi-3, uze uye kwisahluko osidingayo.
- Amagama amatsha akuluhlu lwamagama; amanqaku ngomthetho nokhuseleko asekupheleni.

Qalisa apho umi khona. Isahluko 1 siqala ngeengcamango ezibambe yonke into ndawonye.
