# Umhlaba: yondle umhlaba, nawo uya kukondla

Umhlaba kukuqala kwayo yonke into. Awububumdaka. Yi-ecosystem ephilayo, kwaye sonke isidlo esisityayo saqala kuyo.

Umhlaba wakheka kancinci kancinci. Ilanga, imvula, iqabaka neengcambu ziqhekeza ilitye libe ziziqwenga ezincinci. Izityalo, izilwanyana, ukungunda nezidalwa ezincinci ezingenakubalwa (**ii-micro-organism**, izidalwa ezincinci kakhulu ezingabonwayo ngamehlo) zijika amagqabi afileyo, iingcambu nobulongwe zibe yi-**organic matter**, okuthetha nantoni na emhlabeni eyayiphila ngaphambili. Kunokuthatha ukuya kutsho kwiminyaka eyi-1 000 ukwakha i-1 cm yomhlaba. Isaqhwithi esinye singayihlanza loo sentimitha.

Ngoko siwujika umbuzo oqhelekileyo. Asibuzi ukuba isityalo sidinga ntoni. Sibuza ukuba umhlaba udinga ntoni. Yondla umhlaba, ungasiyondli isityalo.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukufunda amaleko omhlaba wakho nokwahlula isanti, i-silt, udongwe ne-loam ngezandla nangengqayi yeglasi
- Ukufunda isiphumo se-pH uze wazi ukuba umhlaba wakho udinga ikalika na
- Ukutyala i-green manure nokusebenzisa ubulongwe bezilwanyana ungagulisi mntu
- Ukugquma ibhedi nge-mulch, nokujika ingca ibe yibhedi entsha ungambanga
- Ukwakha unqwaba we-compost otshisayo kunye ne-worm farm esebenzayo

## Amaleko omhlaba

Umngxuma emhlabeni ufana nencwadi yembali. Wumbe, okanye jonga kwindlela ecuthiweyo okanye emsantsini, uya kubona amaleko: **ubume bomhlaba** (soil profile).

| Ileko | Oko ukubonayo |
|---|---|
| Amagqabi awileyo | Amagqabi afileyo ephezulu, aqala ukubola. |
| **I-topsoil** | Ileko elimnyama kakhulu, izele ziingcambu, ubomi bomhlaba ne-organic matter. Rhoqo inzulu nje kuphela i-10 ukuya kwi-30 cm. |
| Umhlaba ongaphantsi (subsoil) | Ukhanya ngakumbi, une-organic matter encinci. Udongwe, i-iron neeminerali ezihlanjwe zisuka ngaphezulu ziqokelelana apha. |
| Ilitye elibolileyo | Ilitye lesiseko eliqhekekileyo elixutywe nomhlaba omncinci. |
| Ilitye elingaphantsi (bedrock) | Ilitye eliqinileyo. |

I-topsoil yindawo apho ukuchuma kuhlala khona. I-topsoil enzulu, emnyama ibamba amanzi kakuhle, kuba i-organic matter yayo nolwakhiwo lwayo olubhuqekayo kushiya izithuba ezincinci zomoya namanzi. Imithi nezityalo ezine-**taproot** (ingcambu enye enkulu ehla ngqo) zifikelela kumhlaba ongaphantsi zifune amanzi neeminerali, kwaye xa amagqabi azo ewa ebola, ezinye zeminerali zibuyela phezulu.

I-topsoil yeyona nto iqala ukulahleka ngenxa ye-**erosion**, ukuhlanjwa okanye ukuphephethwa komhlaba ngamanzi nomoya. Yigcine igqunyiwe kwaye iondliwe, ileko elimnyama liya kunzulu.

## Uhlobo lwemihlaba: isanti, i-silt, udongwe ne-loam

Uhlobo lomhlaba lugqiba ngokuba amanzi ahla ngokukhawuleza kangakanani emhlabeni wakho nokuba ungabamba ukutya kwezityalo kangakanani. Umhlaba wenziwe ngamasuntswana eeminerali ezintathu ngobukhulu: **isanti** egrabileyo yeyona inkulu, **i-silt** efana nomgubo iphakathi, kunye **nodongwe** olunamathelayo lolona luncinci. Umxube wezi zintathu ngowo ubizwa ngokuba **luhlobo lwemihlaba** (soil texture).

| Umhlaba | Esandleni sakho | Xa kukho amanzi |
|---|---|---|
| Umhlaba onesanti | Ugrabileyo kwaye ukhululekile. Uyadilika. | Amanzi ahla ngokukhawuleza kwaye uyoma. Izondlo ziyahlanjwa. |
| Umhlaba odongwe | Wenamathela xa umanzi, ube lukhuni xa womile. Uqengqeleka ube sisosisi ende, emxinwa. | Ubamba amanzi nezondlo kodwa amanzi ahla kancinci. Unokuzaliswa ngamanzi, emva koko uqhekeke wenze ixolo eliqinileyo. |
| Umhlaba oxubeneyo (loam) | Mnyama, uthambile kwaye uyabhuqeka. | Amanzi ahla kakuhle kodwa ubamba amanzi nokutya okwaneleyo. |

Udongwe lunokuba lubomvu, lunsundu, lumthubi, lungwevu okanye lumnyama. Iindawo ezingwevu okanye ezingwevu-luhlaza okwesibhakabhaka zihlala zithetha ukuba umhlaba uhlala umanzi ixesha elide.

I-loam ngowona mhlaba mhle kwimifuno emininzi. Awukwazi ukutshintsha uhlobo lwemihlaba, kodwa ungatshintsha **ulwakhiwo** lomhlaba wakho (indlela amasuntswana adibana ngayo abe ziingqumba ezincinci) nokuba ubamba i-organic matter engakanani. I-organic matter yimpendulo kwiintlobo zombini zemihlaba enengxaki: inceda isanti ibambe amanzi njengesipontshi, kwaye ivula udongwe ukuze umoya namanzi bahambe. Xa iondliwe ngolu hlobo unyaka nonyaka, umhlaba onesanti nodongwe zombini ziya kusondela kwi-loam.

## Ukuvavanya umhlaba wakho

Uninzi lwabalimi abancinci lunengcinga ngomhlaba walo. Le ngcinga rhoqo iyaphosakala.

### Uvavanyo lokuchukumisa (uvavanyo lweribhoni)

1. Thabatha isandla esincinci somhlaba esikwi-10 cm ezantsi ngokuqikelela. Susa amatye neengcambu.
2. Yongeza amanzi kancinci kancinci uze uxove de umhlaba ufane nentlama kwaye uyeke ukunamathela esandleni sakho.
3. Wuvakalise. Okugrabileyo kuthetha isanti. Okugudileyo nokukhaphukhaphu kuthetha i-silt. Okunamathelayo kuthetha udongwe.
4. Cinezela ibhola phakathi kwesithupha nomnwe wokuqala wenze iribhoni etyaba, uyiyeke ixhome de iqhawuke.
5. Linganisa iribhoni.

| Ubude beribhoni | Umhlaba wakho |
|---|---|
| Akukho ribhoni, uyadilika | Isanti okanye isanti ene-loam |
| Ingaphantsi kwe-2.5 cm | I-loam enesanti okanye i-loam |
| I-2.5 ukuya kwi-5 cm | I-loam enodongwe |
| Ngaphezu kwe-5 cm | Udongwe |

### Uvavanyo lwengqayi

1. Thabatha umhlaba kwiindawo ezininzi ebhedini, kwi-15 cm ephezulu. Uxube uze ususe amatye, iingcambu namagqabi.
2. Zalisa ingqayi ecacileyo, enendawo ethe tye, ngesithathu somhlaba, ude ufike phantse emlonyeni ngamanzi, uze wongeze ithisipuni enye yelikhwidi yokuhlamba izitya ukunceda amasuntswana ahlukane.
3. Yihlube kakhulu imizuzu emi-3 ukuya kwemi-5 uze ubeke ingqayi apho akukho mntu uya kuyishukumisa.
4. Emva **komzuzu o-1**, phawula ngaphezulu kweleko elingezantsi: le yi-**santi**.
5. Emva **kweeyure ezi-2**, phawula ngaphezulu kweleko elilandelayo: yi-**silt**.
6. Emva **kosuku olu-1 ukuya kweentsuku ezi-2**, xa amanzi ecace ngokubanzi, phawula ileko eliphezulu: **udongwe**. Udongwe olunzima lunokuthatha iveki okanye ngaphezulu. Nantoni na ezintantayo yi-organic matter.
7. Thelekisa ileko nganye nobude bonke bomhlaba kwingqayi.

I-loam inemalunga ne-40% yesanti, ne-40% ye-silt kunye ne-20% yodongwe. Ukuba isanti ingaphezu kwesiqingatha, umhlaba unesanti; ileko eshinyeneyo yodongwe namanzi ahlala engacacanga iintsuku ezininzi kuthetha ukuba umhlaba wudongwe.

### Funda umhlaba wakho

Ngoxa umba, bhala phantsi oko ukubonayo. Umhlaba omnyama uhlala ubamba i-organic matter eninzi. I-sedge namahlanga zibonisa umhlaba omanzi. Amatye afana ne-sandstone, i-shale okanye i-basalt (ilitye elimnyama lentaba-mlilo laseLesotho naseDrakensberg) akuxelela ukuba umhlaba wenziwe ngantoni. Umhlaba onempilo unevumba elimnandi lomhlaba; ivumba elimuncu linokuthetha ukuba amanzi aqokelelene. Bala iintshulube kwispeyidi esinye somhlaba. Jonga ukuba imvula iyangena okanye iyaqukuqela esihla. Kwaye buza ukuba umhlaba wawusetyenziselwa ntoni ngaphambili: izibaya ezindala, iintsimi neendawo zenkunkuma zonke zitshintsha umhlaba.

### I-pH yomhlaba: obawayo okanye omnandi?

**I-pH** ilinganisa ukuba umhlaba unamandla okubawa (acid) okanye umnandi (alkaline) kangakanani, kwisikali esisuka kwi-0 ukuya kwi-14. I-pH ye-7 ayithathi cala (neutral), engaphantsi kwe-7 ibonisa umhlaba obawayo, nengaphezu kwe-7 ibonisa umhlaba omnandi.

- Imifuno emininzi ikhula kakuhle kwi-pH ephakathi kwe-6 ne-7 malunga, xa ilinganiswe emanzini.
- Emhlabeni obawa kakhulu, i-phosphorus iyavalelwa kwaye i-aluminium iyazityhefisa iingcambu.
- Imihlaba emininzi empuma yoMzantsi Afrika enemvula ngakumbi iyabawa. Imihlaba esentshona eyomileyo nakwiindawo eziphantsi ingaba mnandi okanye inetyuwa.

I-kit ye-pH engabizi inika umlinganiselo ongachanekanga; ilebhu yomhlaba inika umlinganiselo ochanekileyo kunye nekalika oyidingayo. Iilebhu zoMzantsi Afrika zihlala zixela **i-pH (KCl)**, efunda ngaphantsi malunga ne-0.5 ukuya kwi-1 yeyunithi kune-pH emanzini. I-pH (KCl) engaphantsi malunga ne-4.5 ibawa kakhulu kwizityalo ezininzi. Sasaza ikalika yezolimo okanye eyedolomayithi (dolomitic) ngomlinganiselo osencwadini yeelebhu. I-organic matter inceda nokuthintela i-pH ukuba itshintshe ngokukhawuleza.

## Ukunyamekela umhlaba wakho

Indalo ayilishiyi mhlaba ungenanto. Umhlaba wehlathi okanye wedlelo lendalo elinempilo uhlala ugqunyiwe, uhlala uondliwe, awuze umbiwe. Sikulinganisa oko.

- **Gcina ugqunyiwe** nge-**mulch** (ingubo yengca eyomileyo, utyani lwenkozo okanye amagqabi) okanye izityalo eziphilayo. Umhlaba ongenanto uyabhakwa, wenze ixolo eliqinileyo kwaye uyaphephethwa ngumoya.
- **Yondle.** Intsalela yezityalo, i-**compost** (inkunkuma yezityalo nezilwanyana ebolileyo) nobulongwe kubuyela emhlabeni, ingaze itshiswe emlilweni.
- **Tyala izityalo ezininzi ezahlukeneyo.** Iingcambu ezahlukeneyo zifikelela ekunzulwini okwahlukileyo kwaye zondla ubomi bomhlaba obahlukeneyo.
- **Lima kancinci.** Ukumba okanye ukulima ngepulawu ngalunye kuphula ulwakhiwo lomhlaba kwaye kutshisa i-organic matter. Kunoko, wuvule ngefolokhwe.
- **Musa ukunyathela ebhedini**, ingakumbi xa imanzi. Hamba ezindleleni.
- **Yivumele iphumle.** Nika ibhedi ediniwe ixesha lonyaka ligqunywe nge-green manure okanye i-mulch.

Umhlaba ogqunyiweyo, ongaphazanyiswayo uya ngokunzulu nokuchuma unyaka ngamnye. Zintlanu iindlela eziphambili zokuwondla: i-green manure, ubulongwe bezilwanyana, i-mulch, i-compost neentshulube.

## Indlela 1: I-green manure

Izityalo zinokwenza ukuchuma kanye apho umi khona, ngelanga, umoya nemvula. **I-green manure** (isityalo esityalelwa ukutyebisa umhlaba) sisityalo osityalela ukondla umhlaba, kungekhona ukuvuna.

I-green manure igubungela umhlaba ongenanto, ibulala ukhula ngokulugubungela, ondla iinyosi xa iqhakaza, yongeza i-organic matter xa ibola. Ezininzi ii-green manure zenza iingcambu ezinzulu zaphule umhlaba oqinileyo. Ukuba yi-**legume** (isityalo sosapho lweembotyi neephizi), yongeza nenayitrojeni: ibhaktheriya ezikumaqhuma amancinci asezingcambini zayo, abizwa ngokuba ngama-**nodule** (amaqhuma eengcambu), zithatha inayitrojeni emoyeni. Oko kwenza i-legume ibe yi-**nitrogen fixer** (isityalo esibopha inayitrojeni).

### Indlela yokusebenzisa i-green manure

1. Yihlwayele ngokuxineneyo ebhedini ephumlileyo, okanye phakathi kwezityalo.
2. Yiyeke ikhule de iqale ukuqhakaza, xa inamagqabi nenayitrojeni ezininzi kakhulu.
3. Yinqumle ingekabi nembewu, uyigaye ibe ziziqwenga ezifutshane ngespeyidi okanye nge-slasher.
4. Yishiye ngaphezulu njenge-mulch (**i-chop and drop**, nqumla uyeke phantsi), okanye uyimbele kancinci kwi-10 ukuya kwi-15 cm ephezulu.
5. Linda iiveki ezi-3 ukuya kwezi-4 phambi kokuba utyale ebhedini, ixesha elide kancinci kwimozulu ebandayo.

> **Ingcebiso:** Ngexesha lokuqala utyala i-legume entsimini, thenga i-**rhizobium inoculant** efanayo (umgubo webhaktheriya eluncedo yeengcambu) kumthengisi wembewu uyixube nembewu. Izityalo ziya kubopha inayitrojeni eninzi.

### Ii-green manure zoMzantsi weAfrika

| Ixesha | Isityalo | Kutheni ulityala |
|---|---|---|
| Ihlobo | I-sunn hemp (*Crotalaria juncea*) | I-legume. Ikhula ngokukhawuleza yaye iphakame, ineengcambu ezinzulu. |
| Ihlobo | I-cowpea (*Vigna unguiculata*) | I-legume. Inyamezela ubushushu nembalela, kwaye ungatya amagqabi neembotyi zayo. |
| Ihlobo | I-lablab (*Lablab purpureus*) | I-legume. Isityalo esikhwelayo esinyamezela imbalela kwaye silungele ukutya kwemfuyo. |
| Ihlobo | I-buckwheat (*Fagopyrum esculentum*) | Iqhakaza kwiiveki ezimalunga ne-6. Iinyosi ziyayithanda kakhulu. |
| Ubusika | I-oats (*Avena sativa*) kunye ne-vetch (iintlobo ze-*Vicia*) | I-oats inika ubuninzi; i-vetch, i-legume, yongeza inayitrojeni. |
| Ubusika | I-fodder radish (*Raphanus sativus*) | Ine-taproot ende eyaphula umhlaba oqinileyo. |

Kwiindawo ezinemvula yasehlotyeni, hlwayela ezehlobo imvula sele iqalile kwaye umhlaba sele ufudumele. KuNtshona Koloni onemvula yasebusika, okanye naphi na apho ungakwazi ukunkcenkcesha, hlwayela ezobusika ekwindleni. I-Mexican sunflower (*Tithonia diversifolia*), edunyiswa kwiincwadi ezininzi ze-permaculture, sisityalo esingengowasekhaya esihlaselayo eMzantsi Afrika; kunoko tyala i-sunn hemp okanye i-lablab ukuze ufumane ubuninzi.

## Indlela 2: Ubulongwe bezilwanyana

Sonke isilwanyana ekufameni yifektri encinci yesichumiso. Ubulongwe bongeza i-organic matter nezondlo, ingakumbi inayitrojeni, kwaye bondla ubomi bomhlaba. Ubulongwe beenkomo, bezimvu (iigusha), beebhokhwe, bamahashe, bemivundla nabeenkukhu bonke buyasebenza; ubulongwe beenkukhu bunamandla kakhulu, kwaye butshisa iingcambu xa busesemtsha. **I-animal tractor**, isibaya seenkukhu esihambayo esimi phezu kwebhedi ephumlileyo, sisasaza ubulongwe ngqo emhlabeni (jonga isahluko ngeenkqubo zezilwanyana).

### Indlela yokusebenzisa ubulongwe

1. Yenza i-compost ngobulongwe phambi kokuba busondele kwizityalo ozityayo ziluhlaza. I-compost ibulala uninzi lweentsholongwane nembewu yokhula, ithintela ubulongwe ukuba butshise iingcambu, kwaye ibujika bube yi-**humus**: inxalenye emnyama, ezinzileyo ye-organic matter ebamba amanzi nezondlo.
2. Ukuba kufuneka usebenzise ubulongwe obungekabolisi, bumbele emhlabeni ubuncinane iinyanga ezi-4 (iintsuku ezi-120) phambi kokuba uvune izityalo ezichukumisa umhlaba okanye ezityiwa ziluhlaza, ezifana neletisi, isipinatshi neekherothi. Kwizityalo ezikhula phezu komhlaba, ezifana nombona nemithi yeziqhamo, shiya ubuncinane iinyanga ezi-3 (iintsuku ezi-90). Ungaze ubeke ubulongwe obutsha ecaleni kwemifuno esele ikhula.
3. Ungaze usebenzise ubulongwe babantu, okanye ubulongwe bezinja okanye bekati egadini yokutya. Bukhwelisa iinqolowa nezinye ii-parasite ezosulela abantu, ingakumbi abantwana.
4. Sebenzisa ubulongwe behagu kuphela emva kokwenza i-compost etshisayo ngokupheleleyo nokugcinwa ixesha elide, kwaye ungaze uyisebenzise kwizityalo ezityiwa ziluhlaza. Amaqanda ayo e-roundworm asinda iminyaka.
5. Hlamba izandla emva kokuphatha ubulongwe, uhlambe imifuno phambi kokuba uyitye.

Yazi apho ubulongwe bwakho buvela khona. Ungasebenzisi ubulongwe bezilwanyana ezigulayo, nobulongwe bezilwanyana ezinikwe amayeza ngokuchasene neenqolowa kwiiveki ezimbalwa ezidlulileyo (nge-ivermectin, umzekelo), kuba oko kulimaza amabhungane obulongwe neentshulube. Ezinye iityhefu zokubulala ukhula ezihlala ixesha elide ezitshizelwe kwidlelo zidlula ngezilwanyana zize zisinde ekwenzeni i-compost, kwaye iinxalenye ezincinci kakhulu zonakalisa itumato, iimbotyi neetapile, ngoko buza ukuba izilwanyana ziye zadlela kwidlelo elitshizelweyo na.

## Indlela 3: I-mulch

Ngena kwidlelo lendalo elinempilo ubeke ingca ecaleni. Umhlaba ongaphantsi umnyama, umanzi kwaye upholile. I-mulch yenza umsebenzi ofanayo egadini: ingubo yengca eyomileyo, utyani lwenkozo, amagqabi, iziqwenga zemithi okanye intsalela yezityalo esasazwe phezu komhlaba. Kuzo zonke iindlela zokunyamekela umhlaba, qala apha.

I-mulch ikhusela umhlaba elangeni, emoyeni nemvuleni enamandla, inceda imvula ingene, inciphisa kakhulu ukuphela kwamanzi ngumphunga ukuze unkcenkceshele kancinci, igcina umhlaba upholile ehlotyeni nofudumele ebusika, kwaye icothisa ukhula. Njengoko ibola, ondla ubomi bomhlaba kwaye ibe yi-humus.

### Indlela yokugquma nge-mulch

1. Nkcenkceshela ibhedi kakuhle kuqala, okanye gquma ngokukhawuleza emva kwemvula elungileyo.
2. Sasaza i-mulch ibe **nzulu i-5 ukuya kwi-10 cm**. Sebenzisa ileko encinci yezinto ezicolekileyo ezifana nengca enqunyiweyo, ezidibana zibe yingubo emanzi eshinyeneyo.
3. Gcina i-mulch ikude malunga ne-5 cm kwiziqu zemifuno nakwi-10 cm kwiziqu zemithi, kungenjalo iziqu ziya kuhlala zimanzi zibole.
4. Yongeza kwakhona njengoko ibola.

I-mulch eyenziwe ngomthi enjengeziqwenga zemithi okanye umgubo wamaplanga ithatha inayitrojeni kumphezulu womhlaba xa ibola. Yibeke ezindleleni nasemithini, okanye ubeke ileko encinci yobulongwe okanye ye-compost ngaphantsi kwayo.

> **Ingcebiso:** I-slug ne-snail ziyithanda indawo emnyama emanzi engaphantsi kwe-mulch kwaye zityile izithombo ebusuku. Zikhe ngetotshi ngokuhlwa, gcina i-mulch ikude ngobubanzi besandla kwizithombo ezincinci, uze uvumele amadada okanye iinkukhu zizingele ebhedini xa izityalo sele zikhulile kangangokuba azisenakukhuhlwa.

### Ukugquma ngamanqanaba (sheet mulching)

Ukugquma ngamanqanaba kujika ingca okanye ukhula libe yibhedi entsha yegadi ungambanga.

1. Nqumla ingca okanye ukhula uyishiye iphantsi. Nkcenkceshela kakuhle, usasaze ileko encinci yobulongwe okanye ye-compost phezu kwengca enqunyiweyo.
2. Yigubungele ngekhadibhodi etyaba okanye iphepha-ndaba eliqinileyo, uzigubungele ziphindelelane nge-15 cm ukuze kungangeni kukhanya. Susa itheyiphu neestepile, ungafaki iphepha elikhazimlayo okanye elishicilelwe ngemibala.
3. Manzisa ikhadibhodi, uze uyigubungele nge-10 cm okanye ngaphezulu kwe-straw, amagqabi okanye ingca eyomileyo.
4. Ukutyala, tyhala ispeyidi kwi-mulch uze usike umngxuma kwikhadibhodi. Wuzalise ngezandla ezimbalwa ze-compost, utyale isithombo, isihlahla esincinci okanye i-tuber, unkcenkceshele kakuhle.

Ikhadibhodi ibulala ingca ngokuyigubungela kwaye ibola kancinci kancinci. Iintshulube ziyangena ngaphantsi ziwuvule umhlaba ngenxa yakho.

### Indawo yokutyala i-mulch (mulch bank)

Apho i-mulch inqabile, yityale nje. **Indawo yokutyala i-mulch (mulch bank)** yindawo yezityalo ezikhula ngokukhawuleza ozinqumlayo rhoqo ukufumana i-**biomass**, izinto zezityalo ezininzi ezibe yi-mulch ne-compost. Ikwaphula umoya kwaye ibamba umhlaba ethambekeni.

Khetha izityalo **zomdabu** (zizalelwe apha) okanye ezaziwayo ukuba azisasazeki: i-thatch grass (*Hyparrhenia hirta*), i-red grass (*Themeda triandra*) kunye ne-weeping love grass (*Eragrostis curvula*); ingca ye-vetiver engenambewu (*Chrysopogon zizanioides*) kwimigca ye-contour; i-pigeon pea (*Cajanus cajan*); kunye nezihlahla ezihluma kwakhona emva kokunqunyulwa, ezifana ne-Cape honeysuckle (*Tecoma capensis*) kunye ne-sand olive (*Dodonaea viscosa*).

## Indlela 4: I-compost

Umhlaba wehlathi wenza i-compost kancinci kancinci, rhoqo. Unqwaba olungileyo wenza oko ngokukhawuleza kakhulu. I-compost imnyama, iyabhuqeka kwaye inevumba elimnandi. Iphucula imihlaba enesanti nodongwe, ibamba amanzi, ondla izityalo kancinci kancinci, kwaye ijika inkunkuma ibe kukuchuma kunokuba ibe ngumsi.

### Oko i-compost ikudingayo

I-compost idinga **izinto ezomileyo** (browns), **izinto eziluhlaza** (greens), **umoya** namanzi. Emva koko idinga **ixesha**.

Izinto ezomileyo zomile kwaye zinekhabhoni eninzi. Izinto eziluhlaza zimatsha kwaye zinenayitrojeni eninzi. Ulinganiselo phakathi kwazo lubizwa ngokuba lulinganiselo lwekhabhoni kwinayitrojeni. Kunqwaba otshisayo, jolisa kwiinxalenye ezi-25 ukuya kwezi-30 zekhabhoni kwinxalenye e-1 yenayitrojeni, nto leyo ekusebenzeni kuthetha malunga **neebhakethi ezi-2 ukuya kwezi-3 zezinto ezomileyo kwibhakethi nganye yezinto eziluhlaza**.

| Izinto ezomileyo (ikhabhoni) | Izinto eziluhlaza (inayitrojeni) |
|---|---|
| Utyani lwenkozo nengca eyomileyo | Ingca emitsha enqunyiweyo |
| Amagqabi omileyo | Ukhula oluluhlaza olungenambewu |
| Iziqu zombona ezinqunyiweyo | Amaxolo emifuno neziqhamo |
| Ikhadibhodi neephepha-ndaba ezinqunqiweyo ziziqwenga ezincinci | Ubulongwe bezilwanyana |

Ukuba ityibilika kwaye inevumba le-ammonia: yongeza izinto ezomileyo. Ukuba ihleli ibandile: yongeza izinto eziluhlaza okanye ubulongwe. Ukuba inevumba elibolileyo: imanzi kakhulu, ngoko yiphendule uze wongeze izinto ezomileyo.

**Umoya.** Ubomi be-compost budinga i-oksijini. Ukuphendula kungenisa umoya, kwaye nesiseko sezinti senza oko.

**Amanzi.** Gcina unqwaba umanzi njengesipontshi esicinezelweyo. Cinezela isandla esizeleyo: kufuneka kuphume ithonsi okanye mbini kuphela.

Musa ukufaka ubulongwe bezinja nobekati nobabantu; inyama, intlanzi, amathambo nobisi, ezitsala amagundane; izityalo ezigulayo nokhula olunembewu, ngaphandle kokuba unqwaba utshisa ngokufanelekileyo; iingcambu zengca ye-couch (zibeke emanzini ebhakethini iiveki ezimbalwa kuqala, ukuze zife); kunye nomthi otshizwe ngamayeza okuwugcina, uthuthu lwamalahle neplastiki. Uthuthu oluncinci lweenkuni oluqhelekileyo luyamkeleka.

### Indlela yokwakha unqwaba we-compost otshisayo

**Unqwaba we-compost otshisayo** ufikelela kwi-55 ukuya kwi-65 °C, utshisa ngokwaneleyo ukubulala iintsholongwane ezininzi nembewu yokhula. Ukuze utshise kangaka kufuneka ube ububanzi, ubude nokuphakama obungengaphantsi kwe-1 m, kwaye ungabi mde ngaphezu kwe-1.5 m ukuze ukwazi ukuwuphendula.

1. Khetha indawo enomthunzi, etyaba ekufutshane negadi namanzi. Yakha ngqo emhlabeni ukuze iintshulube zingene.
2. Beka malunga ne-20 cm yezinti, amasebe amancinci okanye iziqu zombona, ukuze umoya ungene namanzi ahle.
3. Yongeza i-10 ukuya kwi-15 cm yezinto ezomileyo.
4. Yongeza i-5 ukuya kwi-10 cm yezinto eziluhlaza. Ngcwaba inkunkuma yasekhitshini phakathi, kude nemiphetho, ukuthintela iimpukane namagundane.
5. Yongeza ileko encinci yobulongwe, malunga ne-5 cm.
6. Fafaza isandla somhlaba wegadi okanye i-compost endala ukuzisa ii-micro-organism.
7. Nkcenkceshela ileko nganye ide ibe manzi kodwa ingantywilisiwe.
8. Phinda amanyathelo 3 ukuya kwa-7 de unqwaba ube ngu-1 ukuya kwi-1.5 m ukuphakama.
9. Wugubungele ngengca eyomileyo kwaye emva koko ngeengxowa ezindala okanye itarpaulin ukuthintela imvula enamandla, shiya amacala avuleke kancinci ukuze umoya ungene.

### Ukunyamekela unqwaba

1. Kwiintsuku ezi-2 ukuya kwezi-4 unqwaba kufuneka utshise. Hlaba intonga yensimbi phakathi imizuzu emi-5; xa uyikhupha kushushu kakhulu ukuba ungayibamba ngokukhululekileyo, unqwaba uyasebenza.
2. Emva kweentsuku ezi-5 ukuya kwezi-7 uqala ukupholile. **Wuphendule ngoku.** Uhlukanise wonke unqwaba ngefolokhwe uwufake kwinqwaba entsha ecaleni kwawo, into yangaphandle iye phakathi nento yaphakathi iye ngaphandle, wongeze amanzi ukuba womile.
3. Uphinda utshise. Wuphendule rhoqo xa upholile, malunga nje ngeveki kwinyanga yokuqala. Ukuphendula okutshisayo kane okanye kahlanu kubulala iintsholongwane ezininzi nembewu yokhula.
4. Ukuba udlula malunga ne-70 °C, wuphendule okanye wongeze amanzi. Ubushushu obugqithisileyo bubulala ii-micro-organism eziluncedo.
5. Yeka ukongeza izinto ezintsha. Qala unqwaba wesibini wenkunkuma entsha.

Unqwaba otshisayo ophenduliwe kakuhle ulungile kwiinyanga ezimbalwa ngexesha elifudumeleyo; unqwaba ongaze uphenduliwe ungathatha iinyanga ezi-6 ukuya kunyaka okanye ngaphezulu. Uncipha ngesithathu ukuya kwisiqingatha. I-compost ekulungeleyo imnyama, iyabhuqeka kwaye inuka njengomhlaba wehlathi. Ukuba isashushu xa uyiphendula, linda: i-compost ebolile isiqingatha ithatha inayitrojeni emhlabeni, kwaye izityalo zakho ziya kuba mthubi. Xa unqwaba sele upholile, ii-red wiggler (jonga Indlela 5) ziya kuyigqibezela. Gcina i-compost ekulungeleyo igqunyiwe kwaye uyisebenzise kwiinyanga ezimbalwa.

## Indlela 5: Iintshulube ne-worm farm

UCharles Darwin wachitha iminyaka ebukele iintshulube. Ngo-1881 wabhala: "Kunokuthandabuzwa ukuba zikho ezinye izilwanyana ezininzi ezidlale indima ebalulekileyo kangaka kwimbali yehlabathi, njengezi zidalwa ezincinci ezakhiwe ngokulula."

### Iintshulube emhlabeni wakho

Iintshulube ngabasebenzi abathe cwaka begadi. Zitsala amagqabi afileyo ziwabeke emhlabeni, imigodi yazo ivumela umoya namanzi, kwaye **ubulongwe beentshulube** (castings) bubopha umhlaba ube ziingqumba ezincinci ezibonisa umhlaba onempilo. Ukuzitsala ngakumbi, gquma nge-mulch, yongeza i-compost, lima kancinci kwaye ungasebenzisi tyhefu.

### Iintshulube ze-compost (composting worms)

**I-worm farm** yikhonteyina apho iintshulube ezikhethekileyo ze-compost zijika inkunkuma yasekhitshini ibe ngubulongwe beentshulube obutyebileyo. Ezi ntshulube zihlala emagqabini abolayo nasebulongweni, hayi nzulu emhlabeni, kwaye zizala ngokukhawuleza kwibhokisi ezaliswe zizo. **I-red wiggler** (*Eisenia fetida*) yeyona ixhaphakileyo eMzantsi Afrika kwaye yeyona inyamezela ngcono ubanda nobushushu. **I-African nightcrawler** (*Eudrilus eugeniae*) **ne-Indian blue worm** (*Perionyx excavatus*) zisebenza kakuhle kwiindawo ezifudumeleyo kodwa azisebenzi kakuhle ebusika obubandayo.

Zithenge kwi-worm farm okanye kwinkulisa (nursery). Iintshulube zegadi aziyi kusinda kwi-worm farm.

I-worm farm ikunika izinto ezibini:

- **Ubulongwe beentshulube** (worm castings): i-compost egudileyo, emnyama enezondlo ezifumaneka ngokulula kwizityalo ngaphezu kwenkunkuma okanye komhlaba ezivela kuwo. Busebenzise njengesasazo ngaphezulu (top-dressing), kumxube wezithombo, okanye isandla emngxunyeni ngamnye wokutyala.
- **I-worm tea**: ulwelo oluphuma kwi-farm. Ludibanise neenxalenye zamanzi ezili-10 malunga, ude ufane neti ebuthathaka, uze uluthulule emhlabeni ojikeleze izityalo. Ukuba lunuka kakubi, i-farm imanzi kakhulu; nika olo lwelo kunqwaba we-compost okanye kwimithi, hayi kwimifuno.

### Ukwakha nokuqhuba i-worm farm

I-worm farm yikhaya nje elinomthunzi, elimanzi, elimnyama, elikhuphela kakuhle amanzi, elikhuselekileyo kwiinkukhu, iintaka neembovane. Ibhafu endala emi phezu kweebhritsi iyasebenza, kunye nomngxuma wokuphuma kwamanzi ekupheleni okuphantsi ngaphezu kwebhakethi, amatye nomnatha womthunzi emazantsi, kunye nengxowa ye-hessian neshiti yophahla ngaphezulu. Eyona ilula ukuvuna sisitaki seekhreyithi okanye iibhakethi ezintathu:

1. Fumana iikhonteyina ezintathu ezimnyama ezilinganayo, ezinzulu malunga ne-20 ukuya kwi-30 cm.
2. Shiya isiseko **sekhonteyina esezantsi** siqinile: iqokelela ulwelo. Yifakele itephu encinci, okanye uyimise ngaphezu kwebheseni.
3. Hlaba imingxuma emininzi emalunga ne-5 mm kwiziseko **zekhonteyina ezimbini ezingaphezulu**, kwaye imingxuma embalwa yomoya kufutshane nephezulu emacaleni azo.
4. Misa ikhonteyina esezantsi phezu kweebhritsi emthunzini, kunye nekhonteyina enye esebenzayo ngaphezulu.
5. Zalisa ikhonteyina esebenzayo ngamacandelo amabini kwamathathu nge**ndawo yokulala yeentshulube** (bedding): ikhadibhodi eqhekekileyo, iphepha-ndaba, amagqabi omileyo ne-straw, kuntywilisiwe kwacinezelwa, kunye nobulongwe obubolileyo kakuhle nesandla somhlaba.
6. Yongeza malunga ne-500 g yeentshulube, ezimalunga newaka. Isandla esinye sithatha iinyanga ezininzi ukuze siqale ukusebenza kakuhle.
7. Gquma ngengxowa emanzi nesiciko. Qala ukondla emva kweentsuku ezimbalwa.
8. Xa ikhonteyina esebenzayo izele bubulongwe obumnyama beentshulube, rhoqo emva kweenyanga ezi-2 ukuya kwezi-4, beka ikhonteyina yesithathu ngaphezulu kunye nendawo yokulala entsha nokutya, ichukumisa ubulongwe obungezantsi.
9. Kwiiveki ezimbalwa iintshulube ziyakhwela. Khupha ikhonteyina esezantsi, ubuyisele nayiphi na intshulube, uze usebenzise ubulongwe beentshulube. Ikhonteyina engenanto ibe yeyona iphezulu elandelayo.
10. Khupha ulwelo kwikhonteyina esezantsi rhoqo emva kweentsuku ezimbalwa ukuze ingaze ikhukulise iintshulube.

Ii-red wiggler zenza kakuhle phakathi kwe-15 ne-25 °C malunga, kwaye ngaphezu kwe-30 °C malunga ziqala ukufa. Gcina i-farm emthunzini onzulu kunye nengxowa emanzi ngaphezulu ehlotyeni, ikhuselekile ebusika obubandayo, kwaye indawo yokulala imanzi njengesipontshi esicinezelweyo.

Xa i-farm izele ziintshulube kunye **neqanda leentshulube** (cocoons) elincinci elimile okwelamuni, khupha izandla ezimbalwa ukuqala enye i-farm okanye ukongeza kunqwaba we-compost opholileyo.

### Ukondla iintshulube zakho

1. Qala kancinci. Yongeza ngakumbi kuphela xa uninzi lokutya kokugqibela sele kuphelile.
2. Nqumla okanye utyumze ukutya ukuze kubole ngokukhawuleza.
3. Kungcwabe ngaphantsi kwendawo yokulala, kwikona eyahlukileyo ngalo lonke ixesha, ukugcina iimpukane zeziqhamo kude.
4. Yongeza isandla sendawo yokulala eyomileyo ekondleni ngalunye.

I-farm esele iqinile idla ukuya kutsho kwisiqingatha sobunzima bayo bokutya ngosuku: i-1 kg yeentshulube, ukuya kutsho kwi-500 g yenkunkuma.

| Iintshulube ziyakuthanda | Iintshulube aziyithandi |
|---|---|
| Amaxolo emifuno neziqhamo | Itswele, igalikhi, ipilipili kunye ne-citrus eninzi |
| Amagqabi eti, inkunkuma yekofu kunye namaqokobhe amaqanda aqhekekileyo | Inyama, intlanzi, amathambo nobisi |
| Ikhadibhodi neephepha ezintywilisiweyo | Ukutya okunamafutha, okunetyuwa okanye okuphekiweyo |
| Ubulongwe beenkomo, bamahashe okanye bemivundla obubolileyo kakuhle | Ubulongwe bezinja nobekati |

Abanye abalimi benza **i-compost tea**, bedlulisa umoya ngeebhubhu kubulongwe beentshulube emanzini kunye ne-molasses encinci. Uphando malunga nokuba iyanceda na luxubene, kwaye i-molasses inokondla iintsholongwane ezifana ne-*E. coli* ne-*Salmonella*. I-worm tea ecacileyo ilula ngakumbi. Nayiphi na kuzo, yithululele emhlabeni, ungaze uyithululele phezu kwemifuno etyiwa ziluhlaza.

## Zama oku

Funda umhlaba wakho kule veki.

1. Khetha iindawo ezintathu: ibhedi yakho engcono, ibhedi yakho embi kunazo zonke kunye nendawo yedlelo lendalo elingaphazanyiswanga.
2. Kwenye nenye, mba umngxuma onzulu ngespeyidi enye. Linganisa i-topsoil emnyama kwaye ubale iintshulube kwispeyidi sonke somhlaba.
3. Yenza uvavanyo lweribhoni ngesandla somhlaba esikwi-10 cm ezantsi.
4. Thulula ibhakethi yamanzi ecaleni komngxuma ngamnye. Ingena okanye iyaqukuqela?
5. Qala uvavanyo lwengqayi ngomhlaba osebhedini yakho ephambili, uze uyifunde kwiintsuku ezimbini ezilandelayo.
6. Bhala yonke into phantsi kunye nomhla. Gquma ibhedi yakho embi kunazo zonke nge-mulch, uze uyivavanye kwakhona ngexesha elifanayo ngonyaka olandelayo.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Umhlaba uyaphila kwaye wakheka kancinci kakhulu. Gcina ugqunyiwe, wondle, ulime kancinci.
- I-organic matter iphucula isanti nodongwe. Imifuno emininzi ithanda i-pH engama-6 ukuya kwi-7 malunga.
- Nqumla ii-green manure zingekabi nembewu. Yenza i-compost ngobulongwe phambi kokuba busondele kwizityalo ezityiwa ziluhlaza.
- Gquma nge-mulch ubunzulu be-5 ukuya kwi-10 cm, kude neziqu.
- Unqwaba otshisayo udinga izinto ezomileyo, izinto eziluhlaza, umoya namanzi. Iintshulube zidinga umthunzi, indawo yokulala emanzi nokutya okuncinci.

Mba ispeyidi enye yomhlaba kwindawo efanayo unyaka ngamnye. Umhlaba ugcina ingxelo yawo, cm nge-cm, malunga nokuba ukunyamekela kwakho kusebenza na.
