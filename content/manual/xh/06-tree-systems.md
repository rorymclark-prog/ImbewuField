# Iinkqubo zemithi

Imithi ngumdlalo wexesha elide. Ibhedi yemifuno ikondla ixesha elinye lonyaka. Umthi otyalwe kakuhle ukondla abantwana bakho nabantwana babo, ngeziqhamo, iinkuni, **i-fodder** (ukutya kwemfuyo), umthunzi namayeza, kwaye awuphindi udinge ukumbiwa okanye ukuhlwayelwa.

Imithi isenza nomsebenzi othulileyo. Iingcambu zayo zibamba umhlaba kwaye zifikelela kumanzi angakwaziyo ukufikelelwa yimifuno. Amagqabi ayo ayawa axhase umhlaba. Amasebe ayo aphula umoya kwaye akhusela iintaka ezitya izinambuzane ezonakalisa izityalo zakho. Ihlathi lakhiwa phezu kwehlathi: isityalo ngasinye senza umhlaba ube ngcono kwesilandelayo.

Iinkqubo ezintathu zemithi zithwala uninzi lwalo msebenzi kwifama encinci: **i-food forest** (igadi etyalwe ngamaleko njengehlathi lendalo); **i-windbreak** (ibhanti lemithi nezihlahla elicothisa umoya); nomthi omnye otyalwe kakuhle. Zonke izityalo ezicetyiswayo apha **zezomdabu** (zikhula ngokwendalo eMzantsi weAfrika) okanye azikho kuluhlu lwezityalo ezihlaselayo ezibhengeziweyo eMzantsi Afrika. Imithi emininzi ebongwayo kwiincwadi ze-permaculture zaphesheya ngee-**invasive species** apha: izityalo ezisuka kwezinye iindawo zize zisasazeke zingalawulwa. Thenga ngegama lesiLatini.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukukhetha indawo ye-food forest nokukhetha imithi yeziqhamo ehambelana neqabaka nobanda lwasebusika bendawo yakho
- Ukutyala i-food forest ngocwangco olufanelekileyo, uqale ngezityalo zokuqala ukumila ezibopha inayitrojeni
- Ukucwangcisa i-windbreak ngaphesheya komoya onobungozi, usebenzisa imithi nezihlahla zomdabu
- Ukutyala umthi omnye ukuze ume ngeengcambu ezinzulu kwaye usinde kwixesha lawo lokuqala elomileyo

## Ii-food forest

Imifuno nezityalo eziphambili zokutya zifuna kakhulu kuthi: ukumba, ukuhlwayela nokunkcenkcesha, ixesha ngexesha. Ngenxa yokuba umhlaba uguqulwa uphinde utyalwe rhoqo ngonyaka, awusoze udlule kwinqanaba lokuqala le-**succession** (utshintsho lwendalo, inyathelo ngenyathelo, ukusuka kumhlaba ongenanto ukuya kwingca, izihlahla nemithi). Izityalo ze-annual nazo zibandezeleka kakhulu zizinambuzane namaxesha omileyo.

I-food forest yigadi etyalwe njengehlathi lendalo, ngamaleko, ukusuka kwimithi emide ukuya kwizityalo eziphantsi nakwizityalo zeengcambu. Asiyonto ngqokelela yemithi emi emhlabeni ongenanto. Uninzi lwezityalo zayo zii-**perennial** (izityalo ezihlala ngaphezu kweminyaka emibini). Kanye imisiwe, i-food forest:

- ayidingi ukutyalwa kwakhona ngexesha ngalinye lonyaka
- ikhulisa iingcambu ezinzulu ezifikelela kumanzi nakwizondlo ezingenakufikelelwa yimifuno engenazingcambu zinzulu
- igcina umhlaba ugqunyiwe unyaka wonke
- iyondla ngamagqabi ayo awileyo nangamasebe ayo anqunyiweyo
- inika amakhaya eentaka nezinambuzane ezigcina izinambuzane ezonakalisa izityalo zilawulekile

Kwi-food forest enye unokuvuna iziqhamo, amandongomane, iibheri, imifuno enevumba, izinongo, iinkuni, iintsika, i-fodder, izityalo zeengcambu ne-**mulch** (ingca okanye amagqabi agubungela umhlaba ukuze uhlale ufumile kwaye ukhuseleke). Umsebenzi osele kukunqunqa ngokwexesha lonyaka, i-**chop and drop** (ukunqumla izityalo uzishiye phantsi zibe yi-mulch), ukuzalisa izityalo ezitsha, nokuhambisa i-**animal tractor** (indlu yeenkukhu ehambayo) ngeli xesha nelo. Imithi emincinci isadinga amanzi kwixesha layo lokuqala elomileyo elinye okanye amabini, kwaye kwiindawo ezomileyo uninzi lwemithi yeziqhamo ludinga amanzi athile unyaka ngamnye.

Abantu sele belima ngale ndlela ixesha elide kakhulu. Empuma yeAmazon, abantu bakhathalela ihlathi ukuze bafumane izityalo zokutya ubuncinane iminyaka eyi-4,500, kwaye ihlathi lilapho lisazele yimithi etyiwayo abayikhethayo.

### Apho utyala khona i-food forest

Kwiindawo ezininzi zoMzantsi Afrika, ihlathi alilokuphela kwendalo kwi-succession. Amadlelo engca yendalo, i-fynbos, amatyholo aseKaroo ne-savanna ziinkqubo zakudala, ezigqibeleleyo ngokwazo, hayi ihlathi elilindileyo ukuvela. Imithi etyalwe kwingca yendalo esempilweni okanye kwi-fynbos ixinezela izityalo ezinqabileyo kwaye isebenzisa amanzi amaninzi.

1. Tyala kumhlaba **osele uphazamisekile** (wonakaliswe ngaphambili): iintsimi ezindala, amagceke angenanto, umhlaba okhukulisiweyo, umhlaba ojikeleze ikhaya, okanye umhlaba osusiwe kuwo izityalo ezihlaselayo.
2. Shiya idlelo lendalo, ingca yendalo, i-fynbos nendawo emanzi (wetland) ezisempilweni zinjengoko zinjalo.
3. Gcina imithi kude nemilanjana nakwiindawo ezimanzi, ngaphandle kokuba yimithi yomdabu yasemaphethelweni emilambo.

### Iintlobo ezimbini ze-food forest

I-permaculture icwangcisa ifama ngee-**zone** (imimandla ehlelwe ngokokuba uyityelela kangakanani), ngokokuba uyityelela kangakanani inxalenye nganye.

**I-food forest yekhaya (i-Zone 2).** Ikufutshane nendlu, apho udlula khona kanye okanye kabini ngemini. Ikondla usapho: iziqhamo, amandongomane, iibheri, imifuno enevumba yokupheka neyamayeza, nee-annual ezizihlwayelayo. Ibambe iintlobo ezininzi. Apho indawo incinci, tyala imithi emikhulu embalwa kuphela.

**I-food forest enkulu okanye igadi yeziqhamo (i-Zone 3).** Ikude ngakumbi, inemithi emikhulu, yokuthengisa. **Igadi yeziqhamo ye-permaculture** (permaculture orchard) yi-food forest elula ngakumbi enemihlobo embalwa, etyalwe kwindawo enkulu kwaye rhoqo inkcenkceshelwa. Isaphethe **ii-nitrogen fixer** (izityalo ezongeza emhlabeni inayitrojeni evela emoyeni) neentyatyambo zezinambuzane. Yityale ngemicu enamadlelo phakathi kwayo, nto leyo ethi iwenze lula nokuvuna.

### I-coppicing

**I-coppicing** kuthetha ukunqunqa umthi usondele kumhlaba ukuze uthumele amahlumela amatsha amaninzi. Uwanqunqa amahlumela kwakhona rhoqo emva kweminyaka embalwa ukuze ufumane iinkuni, iintsika zothango, iinkonkxa nezibambo zezixhobo, kwaye umthi uyaqhubeka uphila. Amahlumela akhula ngokukhawuleza kakhulu kunomthi omtsha ovela embewini, kuba iingcambu sele zinkulu. Hayi yonke imithi ihluma kwakhona. Wild olive (*Olea europaea* subsp. *cuspidata*) ngumthi womdabu ohluma kakuhle emva kokunqunqwa, kwaye isiqwenga se-coppice sawo kwi-Zone 2 sinika iinkuni unyaka nonyaka.

## Ukuyila i-food forest

Yila kuqala ephepheni. Umthi otyalwe kwindawo engafanelekanga ulahlekisa iminyaka.

1. **Bhala iimfuno zakho.** Usapho lwakho, izilwanyana zakho nezilwanyana zasendle zendawo zidinga ntoni? Ukutya, iinkuni, i-fodder, amayeza, umthunzi?
2. **Fanisa izityalo nemozulu yakho.** Khetha izityalo zomdabu nezivela phesheya ezifanelana nemvula yakho, iqabaka nobushushu. Yongeza ezimbalwa ezisanyamezela nje kwindawo yakho. Njengoko imozulu itshintsha, ezi zikunika ukhuseleko.
3. **Xuba iintlobo:** iziqhamo, amandongomane, iibheri, ii-nitrogen fixer, izityalo zamayeza, iintyatyambo zeenyosi neentaka, nezityalo ze-mulch.
4. **Sasaza isivuno unyaka wonke.** KuMzantsi Afrika iiavokhado zivela ikakhulu ukusuka malunga neyoKwindla (March) ukuya kweyoMsintsi (September), kwaye i-citrus ivela ukwindla nasebusika, ngoxa amagilebhisi (grapes) neepesika zivuthwa ehlotyeni kwaye i-almond ivuthwa ekupheleni kwehlobo. Uninzi lweziqhamo luza ngeentlobo zokuqala, ezophakathi nezokugqibela. Tyala ezinye zazo zonke.
5. **Cinga ngesondlo.** Cwangcisa ukuze usapho lutye iziqhamo nemifuno eyahlukeneyo unyaka wonke.
6. **Fanisa i-rootstock nomhlaba wakho.** Imithi yeziqhamo yenziwa nge-**grafting** (ukudibanisa uhlobo oluhle lwesiqhamo kwiingcambu zesinye isityalo). I-**rootstock** yinxalenye yeengcambu leyo, kwaye ii-rootstock ezahlukeneyo zifanela imihlaba eyahlukeneyo. Buza kwinkulisa (nursery) ukuba yeyiphi i-rootstock efanelana nomhlaba wakho.
7. **Khetha kuqala izityalo ezixhasayo zomdabu.** Kwii-nitrogen fixer, kwii-**groundcover** (izityalo eziphantsi ezisasazeka phezu komhlaba) nakwizityalo zezilwanyana zasendle, khetha kuqala iintlobo zomdabu.

> **Ingcebiso:** Phambi kokuba ubhale uluhlu lwezityalo zakho, hamba kancinci kancinci kwidlelo lendalo okanye ihlathi eliseduze kakhulu. Qaphela ukuba zeziphi ii-groundcover ezikhula emthunzini nezeziphi ezikhula elangeni, nokuba yimiphi imithi yokuqala ukumila evela kuqala emhlabeni ophazamisekileyo. Idlelo lendalo lelona titshala lilungileyo lokuba yintoni eya kukhula kakuhle emhlabeni wakho.

### Iipatheni zokutyala nomgama

Cwangcisa ihlathi ngokweqela-qela, uthango, imigca ethe ngqo, okanye imicu yezityalo phakathi kwemigca yemithi. Khetha oko kufanelana nendawo yakho. Nokuba iphatheni injani, umthi ngamnye udinga ilanga. KuMzantsi weAfrika ilanga lisemntla, ngoko beka imithi emide ehlala iluhlaza **emzantsi**, apho umthunzi wayo uwela ngaphandle kwegadi, okanye phakathi kwemithi ephulukana namagqabi ebusika. Sebenzisa i-**edge** (indawo edibana ngayo iindawo ezimbini) ekhanyiswa lilanga kwimifuno enevumba ethanda ilanga, njengoko ihlathi lendalo likwenza.

1. Linganisa indawo yakho.
2. Fumana ububanzi obupheleleyo be-**canopy** yomthi ngamnye: ububanzi besithsaba sawo esinamagqabi xa ukhulile.
3. Zoba isicwangciso ephepheni, okanye uphawule indawo ngeenkonkxa.
4. Shiya indawo. Umthi one-canopy ye-4 m udinga malunga ne-6 ukuya kwi-7 m ukuya komnye umthi, ngaphandle kokuba utyala uthango. Indawo ephakathi ibamba iindlela, ii-nitrogen fixer nezityalo ezincinci.
5. Yenza uluhlu lwakho lokuthenga ngesicwangciso.

## Ukutyala i-food forest

Ihlathi lendalo lithatha amashumi eminyaka, nokuba ngamakhulu eminyaka, ukukhula. Siyakhawulezisa ngokutyala izityalo zokuqala ukumila kuqala.

Cinga ngokubasa umlilo. Ingca eyomileyo ibasa amasuntswana, amasuntswana abasa amasebe, ekugqibeleni kuvutha iinkuni ezinkulu. Kwi-food forest, izityalo zokuqala ezikhula ngokukhawuleza zakha ukuchuma komhlaba kuqala, kwaye imithi yeziqhamo iyathatha njengoko ikhula.

Ezona zityalo zokuqala ziluncedo ziyi-**legume** (izityalo zosapho lweembotyi neephizi). Uninzi lwazo luyabopha inayitrojeni: iingcambu zazo zisebenza nebhaktheriya ukuthatha inayitrojeni emoyeni zize ziyongeze emhlabeni. Zikwenza nobuninzi be-**biomass** (amagqabi namasebe) ye-mulch.

| Isityalo | Ukusetyenziswa | Amanqaku |
|---|---|---|
| Umnga (sweet thorn) *Vachellia karroo* | I-nitrogen fixer, i-fodder, iinkuni, igamu | Yomdabu kwaye iyomelela kakhulu. Ixinana kwidlelo lendalo elidliwe ngokugqithisileyo, ngoko yityale kwi-food forest yakho, ungayityali kwingca yendalo esempilweni. |
| Common hook thorn *Senegalia caffra* | I-nitrogen fixer, i-fodder, iinkuni, uthango | Yomdabu; kwiindawo zemvula yasehlotyeni. |
| Keurboom *Virgilia oroboides* | I-nitrogen fixer ekhula ngokukhawuleza, iintyatyambo zeenyosi | Yomdabu kwindawo esemazantsi nakwiNtshona Koloni; ayihlali ixesha elide. |
| Pigeon pea *Cajanus cajan* | I-nitrogen fixer, iiphizi ezityiwayo, i-chop and drop | Sisihlahla esincinci esihlala iminyaka embalwa; sibulawa liqabaka eliqatha. |
| Cowpea *Vigna unguiculata* | I-groundcover ebopha inayitrojeni, ukutya | I-annual yasehlotyeni. |
| Lablab bean *Lablab purpureus* | I-nitrogen fixer ekhwelayo, i-fodder | Yasehlotyeni; ibuthathaka eqabakeni. |
| Lupin *Lupinus angustifolius* | I-groundcover ebopha inayitrojeni yasebusika | Ifanelana nobusika baseNtshona Koloni. Imbewu ye-lupin ekrakra ityhefu, ngoko tya okanye ondle imfuyo ngeentlobo ezimnandi kuphela. |

Ii-wattle, i-leucaena ne-sesbania zisetyenziselwa lo msebenzi kwiincwadi ezininzi zaphesheya, kodwa ziyizihlaselayo apha. Imithi enameva yomdabu engentla yenza umsebenzi ofanayo.

### Amanyathelo okutyala

1. **Beka iinkonkxa ezibonisa izityalo.** Linganisa imigama ngobungakanani be-canopy uphawule indawo nganye ngenkonkxa okanye ngekalika encinci. Shiya imigama emikhulu phakathi kwemithi emikhulu.
2. **Phawula yonke into.** Beka uphawu lwegama ecaleni kwesityalo ngasinye esibalulekileyo. Uya kulibala ukuba yintoni phi.
3. **Mba yonke imingxuma nezitya zokutyala.**
4. **Lungisa i-compost ne-mulch.** **I-compost** yinkunkuma yezityalo nezilwanyana ebolile ibe ngukutya okumnyama, okuthambileyo komhlaba.
5. **Qala ngokutyala imithi emikhulu, ehlala ixesha elide,** emva koko imithi ebopha inayitrojeni.
6. **Tyala imithi ephakathi** phakathi kwemikhulu.
7. **Tyala izihlahla ezincinci nemifuno enevumba** malunga ne-1 m ukusuka kwimithi eyintloko yeziqhamo.
8. **Tyala izityalo zeengcambu ezi-perennial,** emva koko **ii-groundcover ze-perennial** phakathi kwezitya.
9. **Hlwayela ii-groundcover ze-legume ze-annual ekugqibeleni:** i-cowpea ehlotyeni, i-lupin ebusika. Kanye phambi kokuhlwayela, mamanzisa imbewu kancinci uyixube ne-**inoculant** efanelekileyo (umgubo webhaktheriya ebopha inayitrojeni ye-legume leyo, othengiswa kunye nembewu okanye kwiivenkile zezixhobo zezolimo). Gcina imbewu enayo i-inoculant ingekho elangeni. Yisasaze ngobuninzi uyigubungele kancinci nge-mulch.
10. **Nkcenkceshela** yonke into kakuhle.

## Indlela ekhula ngayo i-food forest

I-food forest idlula kwiinqanaba ezininzi phambi kokuba izinze. Singacinga ngezigaba ezibini.

**Isigaba 1: ukumiswa (iminyaka 0 ukuya kwi-5).** Ii-legume zokuqala ezikhula ngokukhawuleza ziyakhokela. Zizalisa iindawo ezivulekileyo, zithintela ukhula ngomthunzi kwaye zikhusela imithi yeziqhamo emincinci. Kwixesha lemvula, xa sele zikhulile ngokwaneleyo, yenza i-chop and drop: zinqumle amasebe azo kakhulu uze ushiye into enqunyiweyo phantsi njenge-mulch. Iyabola yondle umhlaba nokungunda okudingwa yimithi yehlathi. Ezinye iingcambu ezicolekileyo namaqhuma eengcambu e-legume nazo ziyafa emva kokunqunqwa zikhulule inayitrojeni encinci. Awusayi kuvuna iziqhamo ezininzi okwangoku, kodwa emva konyaka wokuqala kukho iibheri, imifuno yamagqabi ezihlwayelayo, izityalo zeengcambu, imifuno enevumba neenkuni ezivela koko kunqunyiweyo.

**Isigaba 2: sele imisiwe (unyaka wesi-5 uqhubeke).** Imithi eye yenziwa nge-grafting inokuthela kwiminyaka yayo emithathu yokuqala; eminye kuphela ngonyaka wesi-5 ukuya kwisi-7. Uninzi lwee-food forest luqala ukuvuthwa malunga nonyaka wesi-5.

1. Njengoko imithi yeziqhamo izaliswa, nqumla izityalo zokuqala kakhulu ngakumbi ngonyaka ngamnye, de kuseleyo imithi embalwa kuphela ebopha inayitrojeni ehlala ixesha elide.
2. Yeka iinkukhu, iihagu okanye ezinye izilwanyana zidle phakathi ixesha elifutshane. Khusela iziqu zemithi emincinci kwiibhokhwe nakwiihagu, ezixobula ixolo.
3. Nqumla ukuvumela ukukhanya apho kufuneka khona, kwaye usebenzise ii-**microclimate** ezikhuselekileyo (iingingqi ezincinci ezinemozulu yazo) ezisele zenzekile.
4. Qalisa ukunqunqa imithi ye-coppice ukuze ufumane iinkuni.

Izilwanyana zasendle ziyafika zodwa. Iintaka, ii-lizard nezinambuzane ziyangena xa amaleko evaleka, kwaye iziqhamo, amandongomane neebheri ziyanda unyaka nonyaka.

## Amaleko e-food forest

I-food forest ichazwa rhoqo ngamaleko asixhenxe. Khetha izityalo leko ngalinye ezifanelana nemozulu yakho. I-**understorey** yileko lemithi emincinci nezihlahla ezingaphantsi kwemithi emide kakhulu.

|---|---|---|

| 2. Imithi emincinci (i-understorey) | Uninzi lwemithi yeziqhamo, igcinwa incinci ngokunqunqa | Iapile, ipere, quince, ipesika, iplamu, apricot, fig, persimmon, citrus, iavokhado, carob; yomdabu umqokolo (Kei apple) *Dovyalis caffra*, sour plum *Ximenia caffra*, buffalo thorn *Ziziphus mucronata*, monkey plum *Diospyros lycioides*, red ivory *Berchemia zeyheri* |
| 3. Izihlahla ezincinci | Izityalo ezinomthi ezisusela malunga ne-0.5 ukuya kwi-3 m | Forest num-num *Carissa bispinosa*, pigeon pea, wilde als *Artemisia afra*, wild rosemary *Eriocephalus africanus*, spekboom *Portulacaria afra*, krantz aloe *Aloe arborescens*, rosemary, lavender, Cape gooseberry |

Ngeebheri, tyala umqokolo (Kei apple), cross-berry okanye num-num kunokuba utyale elderberry okanye white mulberry, eziyizihlaselayo apha. Iintaka zithwala imbewu ye-fig ziyisa kwidlelo lendalo, ngoko khupha izithombo ze-fig ozifumana kude negadi. Ngamayeza, i-pepperbark *Warburgia salutaris* ifanelana neegadi ezingenaqabaka; thenga izityalo ezikhuliswe kwinkulisa, kuba umthi lowo uxobulwe endle ngenxa yexolo lawo.

## Ii-windbreak

Umoya womisa izityalo, wophula imithi emincinci kwaye uthwala umhlaba ongagqunywanga. KwiKaroo, kwiNtshona Koloni nakwithafa eliphakamileyo eliphakathi, ngomnye wezona zinto zinzima egadi ijamelana nazo.

I-windbreak ngumgca okanye ibhanti lemithi, izihlahla okanye ingca ende ecothisa umoya yaye ikhusela izityalo, izilwanyana neendlu. Akukho luyilo lunye; indawo nganye idinga eyayo. I-windbreak elungileyo:

- icothisa imimoya enamandla, ekhawulezayo kwaye imisa i-**erosion** yomoya (umhlaba ophephetwa ngumoya okanye ukhukulisiwe ngamanzi)
- ikhusela imithi yeziqhamo emincinci, i-food forest nemifuno
- inika i-fodder emaphethelweni ayo (gcina izilwanyana zingekho ngoxa isemncinci) neenkuni ezivela ekunqunqeni
- ikhupha izondlo ngeengcambu ezinzulu, ezibuyela ngaphezulu emagqabini awileyo
- yondla kwaye ikhusele iinyosi, izinambuzane neentaka
- inika iziqhamo namandongomane athile, nangona umoya unciphisa isivuno, ngoko ubone iziqhamo njengebhonasi

### Indlela yokucwangcisa i-windbreak

1. **Fumana umoya onobungozi.** Jonga ukuba imimoya enamandla neyomileyo ivela phi kwixesha ngalinye lonyaka. KwiNtshona Koloni, umzekelo, umoya wasemzantsi-mpuma (south-easter) uvuthuza ehlotyeni kwaye umoya wasemntla-ntshona (north-wester) uzisa imvula yasebusika.
2. **Tyala ngaphesheya kwawo,** ngobume obungqamene nomoya onobungozi, ecaleni kwegadi apho umoya uvela khona.
3. **Yenze ibe ende, ingenamingxunya.** Umoya uyakhawuleza ngemingxunya nangeziphelo.
4. **Vumela umoya udlule kancinci.** I-windbreak eshinyeneyo ngangesiqingatha sodonga ikhusela umgama omde kunodonga oluqinileyo, olenza umoya uwele uphinde ujikeleze ngasemva kwalo.
5. **Tyala imigca eliqela:** imithi emide ngasemva (kwicala lapho umoya uvela khona), imithi ephakathi embindini, izihlahla ezishinyeneyo ngaphambili.
6. **Shiya indawo yokukhanya.** Indawo ekhuselekileyo ifikelela emva komoya (kwicala apho umoya uya khona) ngamaxesha amaninzi okuphakama kwe-windbreak, rhoqo ngamaxesha ayi-10 ukuya kwayi-20, ngoko ayidingi ukuma ngqo ecaleni kwegadi. Iingcambu zemithi ziphikisana nezityalo ngamanzi. Ngenxa yokuba ilanga lisemntla, i-windbreak ekwicala **lasemntla** igubungela igadi ngomthunzi; ekwicala lasemzantsi ikhupha umthunzi omncinci.

I-beefwood (*Casuarina*), i-gum ne-pine ziqhelekile njenge-windbreak zasefama, kodwa ziyasasazeka ziye kwidlelo lendalo nasemilanjeni. Imithi nezihlahla zomdabu zenza lo msebenzi kwaye zondla nezilwanyana zasendle.

### Ii-windbreak nomlilo

I-windbreak eluhlaza, enkcenkceshelwayo inokuwucothisa umlilo, kodwa ayiyo **i-firebreak** (umcu ocociweyo ongenakuwelwa ngumlilo wedlelo lendalo), kwaye inokuthwala umlilo iwisa endlini yakho. Izityalo ezinevumba ezinjenge-wilde als, i-wild rosemary, i-rosemary, i-lavender ne-camphor bush zizele ngeoli ezivutha kakhulu. Izityalo ezigcina amanzi emagqabini (succulents) ezinjenge-spekboom, amakhala ne-sour fig azivuthi lula.

1. Apho imililo yedlelo lendalo ihamba khona, gcina i-firebreak ecociweyo phakathi kwedlelo lendalo ne-windbreak.
2. Cima izinto ezomileyo, ezifileyo phantsi kwe-windbreak phambi kwexesha lemililo.
3. Gcina izihlahla ezinevumba kude necala apho imililo ivela khona.

### Izityalo ze-windbreak

**I-wild dagga** (*Leonotis leonurus*) sisihlahla esikhula ngokukhawuleza, siphakama kwi-2 ukuya kwi-5 m, sinentyatyambo ezi-orenji ezitsala ii-sunbird. Siyanyamezela imbalela nomoya, senza ityholo elishinyeneyo, kwaye sinokutyalwa kufutshane negadi. Xa sinqunyiwe okanye sitshisiwe, siyahluma kwakhona ukusuka ezantsi.

**I-false olive** (*Buddleja saligna*) ngumthi omncinci oshinyeneyo, rhoqo ophakama kwi-4 ukuya kwi-5 m kwi-Highveld, onentyatyambo ezinombala okhrimu ezinevumba lobusi ukusuka ekupheleni kobusika ukuya ehlotyeni ezithandwa ziinyosi. Unyamezela umoya, iqabaka nembalela, kwaye unokunqunqwa abe luthango oluxineneyo.

**I-karee** (*Searsia lancea*) yenye yemithi enyamezelayo kakhulu eMzantsi weAfrika. Inyamezela iqabaka nembalela, ikhula ukuya malunga nama-8 m, kwaye iingcambu zayo azinabungozi. Yibeke kumgca wangasemva ukuze ufumane umthunzi, i-fodder neenkuni.

Zonke izityalo etafileni zezomdabu, ngaphandle kwe-rosemary ne-lavender, ezingasasazeki.

## Ukutyala umthi

Ixesha elinzima kakhulu lomthi omncinci lixesha lawo lokuqala elomileyo. Umngxuma omncinci kakhulu, okanye amanzi anetha ubuso kuphela, anele ukuwubulala. Utyale kakuhle kanye, kwaye umthi uzikhathalela amashumi eminyaka.

Tyala ekuqaleni kwemvula: ukusuka eyeDwarha (October) ukuya eyoMnga (December) emva kwemvula yokuqala elungileyo kwiindawo zemvula yasehlotyeni, kwaye malunga nokusuka uTshazimpuzi (April) ukuya eyeKhala (July) kwiNtshona Koloni. Tyala imithi ebuthathaka eqabakeni emva kweqabaka lokugqibela. Imithi yeziqhamo ephulukana namagqabi inokutyalwa phakathi ebusika, ngoxa ingenamagqabi.

1. **Khetha umthi omhle.** Thenga umthi oqinileyo onesiqu esityebileyo namagqabi asempilweni. Phepha imithi enezingcambu ezijikelezayo ngaphakathi esingxoweni. Kwi-citrus, thenga imithi eqinisekisiweyo engenazifo.
2. **Wutyale kungekudala** emva kokuwuthenga.
3. **Coca isangqa** esiyi-1 m ububanzi sikhulwe ukhula nengca, ingakumbi i-kikuyu, ukuze zingawathathi amanzi.
4. **Mba obanzi, ungambi nzulu.** Yenza umngxuma ube ubanzi kabini ukuya kathathu kunebhola leengcambu kwaye ube nzulu ngokufanayo. Kumhlaba oqinileyo, mba malunga ne-60 cm ububanzi nobunzulu uze ukhulule ezantsi namacala. Beka i-**topsoil** (umaleko ongaphezulu, omnyama kakhulu) ecaleni linye nomhlaba ongaphantsi opaleyo ecaleni lesinye.
5. **Vavanya ukuphuma kwamanzi.** Zalisa umngxuma ngamanzi. Ukuba amanzi asemi ngomso olandelayo, tyala umthi kwinduli ephakanyisiweyo endaweni yoko.
6. **Wuxhase kwiindawo ezinomoya.** Betha inkonkxa malunga ne-50 cm emhlabeni ecaleni lapho umoya uvela khona phambi kokutyala, ukuze ungazigxobi iingcambu.
7. **Xuba umhlaba wokugcwalisa:** malunga nesahlulo esinye se-compost kwezintathu ze-topsoil, kunye nobulongwe obubolileyo kakuhle obuncinci. I-compost eninzi kakhulu igcina iingcambu ngaphakathi emngxunyeni, ngokungathi sembiza.
8. **Beka ubude.** Khulula naziphi na iingcambu ezijikelezayo. Misa umthi ukuze phezulu kwebhola leengcambu kulingane nomhlaba ojikelezileyo, kwaye i-graft union (indawo yokudibanisa, apho umthi weziqhamo wadityaniswa khona) ihlale ikakuhle phezu komhlaba.
9. **Zalisa uze uqinise** ngobunono ngezandla okanye ngeenyawo. Musa ukunyathela ngamandla.
10. **Wuqhose** ngokukhululekileyo enkonkxeni ngomtya othambileyo ngohlobo lwenani u-8, ukuze isiqu sinokunyikima kancinci. Susa umtya emva kwemalunga nonyaka.
11. **Yenza isitya** esiyi-1 m ububanzi esinomqolo ophantsi womhlaba wokubamba amanzi. Sandise njengoko umthi ukhula.
12. **Gquma** isitya nge-mulch enobunzima obuyi-5 ukuya kwi-10 cm, uyigcine malunga ne-10 cm ukusuka esiqwini ukuze singabolisi.
13. **Nkcenkceshela nzulu:** ibhakethi epheleleyo ye-20 L okanye ngaphezulu kanye emva kokutyala. Ukuba imvula ayinayo, nkcenkceshela rhoqo emva kweentsuku ezimbini ukuya kwezintathu iiveki ezimbini, uze emva koko unike intywilisela enzulu malunga kanye ngeveki kwixesha lokuqala elomileyo, rhoqo ngakumbi kwisanti okanye ebushushwini obukhulu. Faka umnwe emhlabeni: nkcenkceshela xa womile ezantsi kwi-5 ukuya kwi-10 cm.

Intywilisela enzulu ngamaxesha athile ithumela iingcambu ezantsi zilandela amanzi. Amanzi amancinci yonke imihla agcina iingcambu kufutshane nobuso bomhlaba, apho zomiswa ngexesha lokuqala lobushushu obukhulu, kwaye kwidongwe anokuzibola.

## Zama oku

Phawula isiqwenga sakho sokuqala se-food forest kule veki.

1. Khetha isiqwenga somhlaba ophazamisekileyo kufutshane nendlu, ubuncinane i-10 m ngokuphindwe yi-10 m, kunelanga elivela emntla.
2. Yima kuso emva kwemini enomoya uphawule apho umoya onobungozi uvela khona.
3. Khetha imithi yeziqhamo emibini okanye emithathu ehambelana neqabaka lakho, ubhale ububanzi be-canopy ekhulileyo yomthi ngamnye.
4. Phawula izikhundla zayo, zinomgama we-6 ukuya kwi-7 m kwimithi ene-canopy ye-4 m. Beka inkonkxa ye-nitrogen fixer phakathi kwesibini ngasinye.
5. Mba umngxuma omnye wokutyala, ububanzi bawo bube kabini ukuya kathathu kunengxowa yenkulisa, uze wenze uvavanyo lokuphuma kwamanzi.
6. Zoba isiqwenga kwincwadi yamanqaku: iinkonkxa, icala lomoya, intla, nento eboniswe luvavanyo lokuphuma kwamanzi.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Tyala ii-food forest kumhlaba ophazamisekileyo kufutshane nekhaya. Amadlelo engca yendalo asempilweni ne-fynbos ziinkqubo ezigqibeleleyo, hayi ihlathi elingagqitywanga.
- Fanisa imithi yeziqhamo neqabaka lakho nobanda lwasebusika, usasaze isivuno unyaka wonke.
- Qala ngezityalo zokuqala ukumila ezibopha inayitrojeni, uzenze i-chop and drop njengoko imithi yeziqhamo ikhula, uze ukhethe izityalo ezixhasayo zomdabu kunezihlaseli.
- Tyala ii-windbreak ngaphesheya komoya onobungozi, kwimigca eliqela, zingenamingxunya kodwa zivumele umoya udlule kancinci, uzigcine zingekho kwicala lasemntla legadi.
- Mba ububanzi, tyala ngomgama onzulu ofanayo nalapho umthi wawukhule khona, gquma nge-mulch, kwaye nkcenkceshela nzulu kanye ngeveki kunokuba kancinci yonke imihla.

Tyala izityalo zokuqala ukumila kwesi sizini, uze emva kwazo utyale imithi yeziqhamo. Kwiminyaka emihlanu unokuhamba emthunzini apho kwakukho umhlaba ongenanto, iintaka zizisa imbewu yehlathi elilandelayo.
