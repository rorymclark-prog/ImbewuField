# Ucwangciso lwee-sector

Umhlaba wakho asisiqithi. Umoya, umlilo, ilanga, amanzi nomoya obandayo zonke zivela ngaphaya kocingo lwakho, kwaye ziya kuqhubeka zifika kade emva kokuba utyale umthi wakho wokuqala. Awukwazi ukuzimisa. Unokukhetha apho zidibana khona nefama yakho, nokuba yintoni emi apho xa zifika.

Umfama owaziyo ukuba umoya oshushu uvela phi utyala i-windbreak kwindawo echanekileyo ekuqaleni. Umfama ongawaziyo, uyityala kabini.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukufumana emntla oyinyani ngekhampasi, ngelanga liphuma okanye ngomthunzi wasemini
- Ukuzoba imephu ye-sector yamandla anqumla umhlaba wakho
- Ukubeka ii-windbreak, ii-firebreak nezithintelo eziluhlaza apho umoya nomlilo uqhele ukuvela khona
- Ukusebenzisa ikona yelanga nokuhamba komoya obandayo ukubeka iibhedi, imithi nendlu
- Ukufunda imozulu yakho, uze ukhethe izityalo nezikhuseli ezihambelana nayo

## Ukuzoba imephu ye-sector

**I-sector** yinto enamandla okanye amandla angena kumhlaba wakho evela ngaphandle: ilanga, umoya, umlilo, amanzi nezikhukula, iqabaka nomoya obandayo, imibono emihle nembi, iindlela zezilwanyana zasendle, ingxolo nothuli.

Imephu ye-sector izoba nganye kuzo njengesiqwenga, njengesiqwenga sekeyiki, esijonge ekhayeni lakho ukusuka kwicala elivela kulo.

1. Thatha imephu yakho esisiseko (isahluko 2) uphawule embindini wekhaya lakho okanye wegadi.
2. Phawula emntla oyinyani.
3. Kwinto nganye enamandla, zoba isiqwenga esisuka kwicala elivela kulo. Umoya oshushu, owomileyo wobusika ungaba sisiqwenga esisuka emntla-ntshona.
4. Bhala ixesha lonyaka ecaleni kwesiqwenga ngasinye. Uninzi lwala mandla luyatshintsha ngamaxesha onyaka.

Emva koko linganisa, kwinto nganye, ukuba uyayamkela, uyayithintela okanye uyayikhokela. Umoya ophola wasehlotyeni uyawuvumela ungene endlini; umoya obandayo wasebusika uyawuthintela. Imephu ikubonisa ukuba **i-windbreak** (umgca wemithi okanye wezihlahla ocothisa umoya) kunye **ne-firebreak** (umcu womhlaba ongenanto onokutsha) zifanele ukuba ziphi, yimiphi imibono ekufuneka ihlale ivuliwe, nalapho ilanga lasebusika liwela khona.

## Ukufumana emntla

Yonke i-sector ixhomekeke emntla. Nazi iindlela ezintathu ezilula zokuwufumana.

### Ngekhampasi

Ikhampasi ibonisa emntla wemagnethi, hayi emntla oyinyani. KuMzantsi Afrika, emntla wemagnethi usentshona yemntla oyinyani malunga ne-20 ukuya kwi-28 yeedigri: ngo-2025 malunga ne-21 eJohannesburg, ne-27 eKapa (Cape Town) kunye ne-28 eDurban. Ngoko emntla oyinyani ukwisandla sokunene (empuma) kwenaliti, kancinci. Ii-app ezininzi zekhampasi zefowuni zikubonisa emntla oyinyani.

### Ngelanga liphuma

Jonga ilanga liphuma: emntla kusekhohlo kwakho, emzantsi kusekunene. Kodwa ukuphuma kwelanga kuyatshintsha. Liphuma empuma ngqo kuphela malunga nowama-21 kweyoKwindla (March) nowama-23 kweyoMsintsi (September). KweyoMnga (December) liphuma malunga ne-26 ukuya kwi-29 yeedigri emzantsi wempuma, kwaye kweyeSilimela (June) malunga ne-26 ukuya kwi-29 yeedigri emntla wempuma.

### Ngomthunzi wakho wasemini

Emini ilanga lisemntla phantse kuyo yonke indawo yoMzantsi weAfrika, ngoko umthunzi wakho ujonge emzantsi. Imini ngokwelanga iwela phakathi kwentsimbi ye-11:45 ne-13:00 ngokwewotshi, kamva entshona. Kuphela emntla kakhulu weLimpopo phakathi kwehlobo apho ilanga linokuba semzantsi kancinci.

> **Ingcebiso:** Ukuba sele ufumene emntla, uphawule kwigadi ngelitye elipeyintiweyo okanye ngepali, kwaye awusayi kuphinda ufune ukuwufumana.

## Umoya

Ilanga lifudumeza umhlaba, ulwandle namatye ngokungalinganiyo. Umoya ofudumeleyo uyenyuka, umoya opholileyo uyehla, kwaye umoya uhamba usuka kwindawo enoxinzelelo oluphezulu uye kwenoxinzelelo oluphantsi. Lowo ngumoya.

Umoya womisa umhlaba nezityalo, wophula izityalo nemithi, uphephetha i-**topsoil** (umaleko ongaphezulu womhlaba, omnyama nophilayo) kwaye uqhubela phambili imililo. Uqhuba iipompo zamanzi, upholisa indlu ehlotyeni kwaye uthwala umgubo wokuchumisa wombona.

### Imimoya eMzantsi weAfrika

| Umoya | Phi | Nini | Wenza ntoni |
|---|---|---|---|
| I-berg wind | Unxweme ne-escarpment yeNtshona Koloni, yeSouthern Cape, yeMpuma Koloni naseKwaZulu-Natal | Ikakhulu ebusika, rhoqo ngemini enye okanye ezimbini ngaphambi kwe-cold front | Umoya oshushu, owomileyo kakhulu ovela ngaphakathi uye ngasolwandle. Ingozi enkulu kakhulu yomlilo. |
| Imimoya ye-cold front | KwiNtshona Koloni ngamandla amakhulu, emva koko kwelinye ilizwe | Ebusika | Imimoya yasemntla-ntshona izisa imvula kwiNtshona Koloni. Emva kwe-front, imimoya ebandayo yasemzantsi izisa iqabaka ngaphakathi nekhephu emhlabeni ophakamileyo. |
| I-south-easter ("Cape Doctor") | ECape Town nasemzantsi-ntshona weNtshona Koloni | Ehlotyeni | Umoya onamandla, owomileyo, ovuthuza ngamaxesha, omisa umhlaba, wonakalisa izityalo kwaye usasaza umlilo. |
| Imimoya yeThupha (August) | I-Highveld nangaphakathi | Ekupheleni kobusika nasekuqaleni kwentwasahlobo | Imimoya eyomileyo, evuthuza ngamaxesha phezu kwedlelo lendalo (veld) elomileyo. Uthuli kunye nengozi enkulu kakhulu yomlilo. |

### Fumana izikhokelo zemimoya yakho ephambili

1. Bukela unyaka wonke: imithi egobileyo, umsi, uthuli, impahla yokuhlamba ebekwe emgceni.
2. Misa i-wind vane okanye i-windsock, ityhubhu yelaphu ebekwe kwipali.
3. Buza abantu abadala ekufuphi ukuba yeyiphi imimoya emibi kakhulu, nokuba iza nini.
4. Qamba umoya ngamnye ngokwalapho uvela khona: umoya wasemntla-ntshona uvela emntla-ntshona.
5. Zoba omnye nomnye kwimephu yakho ye-sector kunye nexesha lonyaka, uze utyale ii-windbreak ngaphesheya kwemimoya eyonakalisayo (isahluko 6).

## Umlilo

Amadlelo engca yendalo, i-savanna ne-fynbos zidinga umlilo ngamaxesha afanelekileyo. Kodwa umlilo wedlelo ongalawulekiyo ubulala abantu nemfuyo, kwaye utshisa amadlelo, izityalo, izindlu noothango.

Kwiindawo ezifumana imvula yasehlotyeni, ixesha lomlilo liqala malunga noCanzibe (May) liye kweyeDwarha (October), libi kakhulu ekupheleni kobusika nasekuqaleni kwentwasahlobo ngaphambi kwemvula. KwiNtshona Koloni efumana imvula yasebusika, liqala malunga neyoMnga (December) liye kweyoKwindla (March). KwiSouthern Cape naseKwaZulu-Natal, iintsuku ze-berg wind zasebusika zizisa ingozi yomlilo enkulu kakhulu ngephanyazo.

### Indlela umlilo oziphatha ngayo

Umlilo unokuvela kuyo nayiphi na indawo, kodwa kwimihlaba emininzi uvela ikakhulu kwicala elinye: i-sector yakho yomlilo. Ngokuqhelekileyo lelo licala lomoya oshushu, owomileyo, kwaye abamelwane bakho baya kulazi.

Umlilo unyuka ithambeka ngokukhawuleza kakhulu kunokuhla. Bamba imatshisi etshayo ngentloko ezantsi ubuke ilangatye lenyuka. Umgaqo oqhelekileyo kukuba isantya somlilo siphindwe kabini kwiyo nganye ye-10° yethambeka elinyukayo, ngoko kwithambeka le-20° lisasazeka ngokukhawuleza ngokuphindwe kane malunga kunasemhlabeni ogudileyo. Indlu ephezulu kwithambeka elitsiba, elinengca, isemngciphekweni omkhulu kakhulu. Beka izikhuseli zakho ezantsi kwethambeka, nakwi-sector yomlilo.

Khusela abantu nendlu kuqala, emva koko imfuyo, izindlu zokugcina izixhobo, ukutya kwemfuyo okugciniweyo nemithi yeziqhamo.

### Iindlela zokunciphisa ingozi yomlilo

1. **Nciphisa into etshisayo.** Kwi-sector yomlilo nangaphandle kwezakhiwo, gcina ingca imfutshane uze ususe amagqabi omileyo nemithi efileyo. Gcina iinkuni, utyani nophahla lwengca kude nendlu.
2. **Yenza ii-firebreak.** Susa umcu wazo zonke izinto ezinokutsha. Iindlela, umhlaba olinyiweyo namadama nazo zisebenza njengee-firebreak. Tshisa i-firebreak kuphela ngemini epholileyo, engenamoya, xa ingozi yomlilo iphantsi, ungaze wenze oko wedwa, kwaye ube nabantu abaneleyo, amanzi nezixhobo zokubetha umlilo (fire beaters) ukuyibamba.
3. **Tyala izityalo eziluhlaza ezitsha kancinci.** Akukho sityalo esingatshiyo, kodwa amagqabi anenyama, anamanzi kwaye anamafutha okanye i-resin encinci atsha kancinci: amakhala, i-spekboom (*Portulacaria afra*, engathandi iqabaka elinzima), *Cotyledon*, ii-vygie (*Delosperma*), i-sour fig (*Carpobrotus edulis*), i-bietou (*Osteospermum moniliferum*) kunye ne-num-num (*Carissa*).
4. **Gcina imithi enamafutha kude nezakhiwo.** Iipayini, ii-gum nee-wattle zitsha ngamandla.
5. **Gcina amanzi ekulungele**: itanki nepayipi lamanzi ngakwindlu, kunye nezixhobo zokubetha umlilo okanye i-knapsack sprayer.
6. **Yenza izinto ezininzi ngenye.** Igadi okanye igadi yemithi yeziqhamo enkcenkcesheliweyo ejikeleze indlu sisithintelo esiluhlaza, kwaye umcu wezityalo ezitsha kancinci ucothisa umoya kunye nomlilo.

## Ukukhanya kwelanga

Uninzi lwemifuno neziqhamo ludinga ubuncinane iiyure ezintandathu zelanga elithe ngqo ngemini, kwaye ilanga lasekuseni linexabiso elikhulu. Kodwa umhlaba ongenanto ophantsi kwelanga elinamandla uyabhaka womile, kwaye ubomi bawo buyahlupheka: iintshulube ziya nzulu okanye ziyafa. Gcina umhlaba ugqunyiwe nge-**mulch** (umaleko wengca eyomileyo, amagqabi okanye utyani) okanye ngezityalo, kwaye unike iitumato nepilipili umthunzi wasemva kwemini kwiindawo ezishushu kakhulu.

### Iikona zelanga

UMzantsi Afrika ukwi-22 ukuya kwi-35 yeedigri emzantsi we-Equator. Emini ilanga lisemntla: liphezulu ehlotyeni, liphantsi ebusika, ngoko imithunzi yasebusika inde kakhulu.

| Indawo | Ukuphakama kwelanga emini, ubusika obukhulu (21 kweyeSilimela, June) | Ukuphakama kwelanga emini, ihlobo elikhulu (21 kweyoMnga, December) | Umthunzi wodonga lwe-2 m emini, ubusika obukhulu |
|---|---|---|---|
| Polokwane (24° S) | 43° | phantse ngqo ngaphezulu | malunga ne-2.2 m |
| Johannesburg (26° S) | 40° | 87° | malunga ne-2.4 m |
| Durban (30° S) | 37° | 84° | malunga ne-2.7 m |
| Cape Town (34° S) | 33° | 80° | malunga ne-3.1 m |

Ebusika ibhedi yegadi ekwicala elisemzantsi lesakhiwo okanye lomthi omkhulu ifumana ilanga elincinci. Ngoko beka imithi emide nezakhiwo kwicala elisemzantsi legadi, nezityalo ezimfutshane emntla.

### Ukusebenzisa ilanga

- **Imithi nemidiliya ephulukana namagqabi ebusika** iwawisa amagqabi ayo ebusika. Zityale emntla wendlu ukuze ufumane umthunzi ehlotyeni nelanga ebusika: imidiliya, ikhiwane, i-white stinkwood (*Celtis africana*) ne-wild pear (*Dombeya rotundifolia*).
- **I-thermal mass** yimathiriyeli enzima, efana nezitena, amatye, umhlaba okanye amanzi, efunxa ubushushu emini iwubuyisele ebusuku. Yibeke apho ilanga lasebusika lifika khona, njengasemva kweefestile ezijonge emntla.
- **Imiphetho yophahla.** Yenza umphetho wophahla osemntla ube banzi ngokwaneleyo ukuthunzela iifestile kwilanga eliphezulu lasehlotyeni, kodwa ube mfutshane ngokwaneleyo ukuvumela ilanga eliphantsi lasebusika lingene.

## Imozulu

Imozulu kukuhamba kwemozulu yendawo ngomndilili kwiminyaka emininzi. Ngaphezu kwayo nayiphi na enye into, ibonisa oko ungakulimayo.

UMzantsi Afrika ulizwe elomileyo. Ufumana malunga ne-464 mm yemvula ngonyaka ngomndilili, xa kuthelekiswa nomndilili wehlabathi we-860 mm, kwaye luya lomile ukusuka empuma ukuya entshona. Uninzi lwelizwe, kunye neLesotho, iEswatini neZimbabwe, lufumana **imvula yasehlotyeni**, ikakhulu ukusuka malunga neyeNkanga (November) ukuya kweyoKwindla (March), kwaye ubusika bomile. Ingingqi yasemzantsi-ntshona yeNtshona Koloni ifumana **imvula yasebusika**, ikakhulu ukusuka malunga noCanzibe (May) ukuya kweyeThupha (August), kwaye amahlobo ashushu, omileyo. Unxweme lweSouthern Cape lufumana imvula unyaka wonke. Ukuphakama kubalulekile njengendawo ngokwayo: i-Highveld inamaqabaka rhoqo ebusika, ngoxa unxweme lwaseKwaZulu-Natal ne-Lowveld phantse zingenaqabaka.

Funda eyakho: imvula yokuqala iza nini, kwaye iqabaka lokuqala nelokugqibela liwa nini.

> **Ingcebiso:** Gcina isixhobo sokulinganisa imvula (rain gauge) nethemometha, ubhale imvula nobushushu obubandayo kakhulu yonke imihla. Emva kweminyaka embalwa uya kuyazi imozulu yakho ngcono kuneyiphi na imephu.

### Ii-microclimate

**I-microclimate** yindawo encinci imozulu yayo yahlukile kumhlaba oyijikelezileyo. Nganye ikuvumela ukuba ulinge into obungenakuyilima ngaphambili. Zifumane ngokubukela apho iqabaka lihlala ixesha elide nalapho izityalo ziqhakaza kwangoko. Iindawo eziphantsi zeentlambo namacala omlambo ayabanda. Iikona ezingasemva kweendonga zifudumele kwaye azikho emoyeni: tyala iitumato kudonga olujonge emntla.

Ukwenza ii-microclimate ezifudumeleyo kwiindawo ezibandayo:

- **Akha kwibhanti elifudumeleyo (thermal belt).** Ebusuku obucacileyo, obungenamoya, umoya obandayo uhla ethambekeni njengamanzi uze uqokelelane kwiindawo eziphantsi ezibizwa ngokuba zii-**frost pocket** (imingxuma yeqabaka). Ibhanti eliphakathi kwethambeka lihlala lifudumele. Beka indlu nezityalo ezinyamezela kakubi iqabaka apho.
- **Tyala imithi ngaphezulu ethambekeni.** Umgca wemithi ongaphezulu kwendlu ucothisa uze uphambukise umoya obandayo ohla ebusuku.
- **Sebenzisa amanzi.** Nalo ichibi elincinci okanye itanki ekufutshane nendlu ilinganisa ubushushu.
- **Jongisa indlu emntla**, uze ubeke unqwaba we-**compost** (izinto zezityalo ezibolayo) ngaphakathi kwitonela yeplastiki ukufumana ubushushu ebusika.

### Imozulu efudumeleyo, ephakathi (temperate) nomileyo

**Iindawo ezifudumeleyo**, ezinje nge-Lowveld, unxweme lwaseKwaZulu-Natal neentlambo ezishushu zeLimpopo, azinaqabaka okanye ziqabaka kancinci. Lima iavokhado, ibhanana, imango, i-litchi, ipapaya (paw-paw), i-macadamia neziqhamo zesitrasi (citrus).

**Iindawo ezinemozulu ephakathi (temperate)** zineehlobo ezifudumeleyo nobusika obubandayo, rhoqo obunamaqabaka. Imithi emininzi yeziqhamo ephulukana namagqabi idinga ubanda lwasebusika, olubizwa ngokuba ngu-"chill", ukuze iqhakaze ize ithele kakuhle; kwiindawo ezifudumeleyo khetha iintlobo ezidinga i-chill encinci. Lima i-apile, ipere, iplamu, ipesika, i-apricot, i-almond, ikhiwane, imidiliya, i-pomegranate, i-persimmon, i-black mulberry ne-pecan.

**Iindawo ezomileyo**, ezinje ngeKaroo nenxalenye enkulu yeMntla Koloni, zifumana imvula engaphantsi kakhulu kwe-500 mm ngonyaka, kwaye ezinye iindawo ngaphantsi kwe-200 mm. Amazimba, i-pearl millet nee-cowpea zenza kakuhle kunombona apha.

## Ukuhamba kwamanzi (hydrology)

Ukuhamba kwamanzi (hydrology) yibali lamanzi emhlabeni wakho. Phambi kokuba ucwangcise, fumana ukuba ngawaphi amanzi onokuwasebenzisa (imilanjana, imithombo, imingxuma yamanzi, iitephu), ixesha lakho lemvula nobuninzi bemvula ngonyaka, ukuba umhlaba wakho uyawakhupha kakuhle na amanzi uze uwabambe, ingakanani imvula onokuyibamba ngophahla lwakho, nokuba i-climate change inokuyenza ntoni imvula yakho.

Qala ngophahla. Imvula ye-1 mm kwimitha yesikwere ye-1 inika ilitha eli-1 lamanzi. Uphahla lwe-50 yeemitha zesikwere kwindawo efumana imvula ye-500 mm ngonyaka lunokubamba ukuya kwiilitha ezingama-25,000 ngonyaka, ngaphandle kwezinye ezilahlekileyo.

Ezi nombolo zibeka imida yoko umhlaba wakho onokukulima.

## Iqabaka

Iqabaka lifika rhoqo ebusika kuninzi lwangaphakathi: i-Highveld, iFreyistata (Free State), iKaroo, iMpuma Koloni nezindawo eziphakathi zeKwaZulu-Natal, neLesotho. Lihlala nakwiintlambo ezibandayo kwezinye iindawo. Uninzi lonxweme kunye ne-Lowveld lufumana kancinci okanye alufumani nalutho.

Ebusuku obucacileyo, obungenamoya, umhlaba nezityalo zilahlekelwa bubushushu bazo ziye esibhakabhakeni esivulekileyo zibe ngamandla ukubanda kunomoya. Umphunga wamanzi uqhwaba phezu kwazo njengomkhenkce omhlophe: iqabaka elimhlophe. Umoya obandayo uyatyibilika ehla uze uqokelelane, ngoko iqabaka libi kakhulu kwiindawo eziphantsi. Xa umoya womile kakhulu, izityalo zinokuqhwaba ngaphandle komkhenkce kwaphela. Oku kubizwa ngokuba **yiqabaka elimnyama (black frost)**, kwaye akunalumkiso: ngomso amagqabi neentsika zijika zibe mnyama zife.

Iqabaka linokunqanda ukukhula komthi omncinci weziqhamo iminyaka emininzi. Lungiselela phambi kobusika.

1. **Gquma izityalo ebusuku obunamaqabaka** ngelaphu lokukhusela iqabaka, amashiti amadala okanye utyani, ufikelela emhlabeni. Susa ukugquma kusasa.
2. **Khusela igadi** nge-windbreak okanye ngothango oluphilayo, kodwa shiya isithuba kwicala elingezantsi ukuze umoya obandayo ukwazi ukuphuma kunokuba uqokelelane.
3. **Phambukisa umoya obandayo ujikeleze igadi** ngothango olunjengesihlangu sehashe ecaleni elingaphezulu ethambekeni, isiphelo esivulekileyo sijonge ezantsi.
4. **Yenza iindawo ezibamba ilanga (sun traps).** Udonga olujonge emntla okanye uthango olugobileyo luphatha umoya ofudumeleyo, kwaye amatye okanye iigubu zamanzi ejikeleze izityalo zigcina ubushushu bemini.
5. **Tyala imithi ekhathalelayo (nurse trees).** **I-canopy** (uphahla lwamagqabi) elula yemithi yokuqala ukumila ehlala ixesha elifutshane ikhusela imithi emincinci yeziqhamo.
6. **Gquma ngenkathalo.** I-mulch ikhusela iingcambu, kodwa ithintela umhlaba ukuba ukhuphe ubushushu bawo ebusuku, ngoko izityalo ezimfutshane kakhulu zinokufumana iqabaka elininzi ngaphezu kwe-mulch eshinyeneyo. Kwiibhedi ezisemngciphekweni weqabaka, nkcenkcesha umhlaba ngemini ngaphambi kobusuku obunamaqabaka: umhlaba onamanzi ugcina ubushushu obungakumbi.
7. **Sebenzisa ii-guild.** **I-guild** liqela lezityalo ezityalwe kunye ukuncedisana, kuquka nokulwa neqabaka (isahluko 6).

## I-climate change

**I-climate change** kukutshintsha kwemo yemozulu ixesha elide, okubangelwa ikakhulu yimisebenzi yabantu: ubushushu obungakumbi, imvula etshintshayo, imozulu enamandla ngakumbi nokuncipha kwamanzi.

UMzantsi weAfrika ufudumala ngokukhawuleza kunomndilili wehlabathi, ezinye iindawo zangaphakathi malunga nokuphindwe kabini. Amagagasi obushushu aqhelekile ngakumbi, kwaye kulindeleke iimbalela ezingakumbi nezinsuku ezininzi zengozi yomlilo. I-climate change yenze imbalela yeKapa (Cape Town) ka-2015 ukuya ku-2017 ibe nokwenzeka ngokuphindwe kathathu.

### Ukuyila ngenxa yemozulu etshintshayo

Asisakwazi ukukholosa ngonyaka oqhelekileyo. Ngoko siyila unyaka ombi, size sakhe **amandla okuphinda aphakame** (resilience): amandla okumela ukothuka aze aphinde aphakame.

1. Tyala izityalo ezininzi ezahlukeneyo, ukuze ukusilela kwesinye kungakubhubhisi.
2. Khetha izityalo ezomeleleyo, kuquka ezo **zomdabu** ezikhula apha ngokwendalo, kunye **nee-perennial**, ezihlala amaxesha amaninzi.
3. Gcina iintlobo zemfuyo ezomeleleyo zasekhaya: iinkukhu zomdabu, iibhokhwe neenkomo zeNguni.
4. Tyala ii-windbreak uze ugcine amanzi ukunciphisa ubukhulu bemozulu eyoyikekayo.
5. Gcina umhlaba ugqunyiwe ngezityalo zokugquma nge-mulch, uze unceda imvula ingene ngee-**swale**, imisele ethe tye egrunjwe ngaphesheya kwethambeka.
6. Gcina intsalela yakho. Yomise, yifake kwiviniga eninzi, okanye wenze ijam ne-chutney. Ukufaka ekhaya kwiibhotile ukutya okune-asidi encinci njengeembotyi, ithanga okanye inyama ngaphandle kwendlela ehloliweyo kunokubangela i-botulism, ityhefu yokutya ebulalayo.
7. Gcina imbewu yakho, ukuze ihambelane nomhlaba wakho, uze uyabelane ngayo ngebhanki yembewu yoluntu.
8. Hambisa izilwanyana ezidlayo rhoqo, uze unike idlelo lendalo (veld) ixesha lokuphumla.
9. Akha ngaphandle kwendawo ezaleka ngezikhukula. Gcina ii-firebreak namanzi ekulungele, uze ugcine ukutya, imbewu nokutya kwemfuyo ngamaxesha anzima.

## Zama oku

Zoba imephu yakho yokuqala ye-sector kule veki.

1. Ngemini enelanga, misa intonga ethe tye ngqo emhlabeni ovulekileyo. Malunga nemini, xa umthunzi wayo ufutshane kakhulu, umthunzi ujonge emzantsi.
2. Zoba kancinci ikhaya lakho, igadi nomda ephepheni, kunye notolo olujonge emntla.
3. Hamba emhlabeni. Phawula apho imimoya enamandla ivela khona, apho imililo yangaphambili yavela khona, apho amanzi angena khona emva kwemvula, nalapho iqabaka lihlala ixesha elide khona.
4. Zoba amandla ngamnye njengesiqwenga esijonge ekhayeni lakho, kunye nexesha lonyaka.
5. Buza ummelwane omdala ngomoya nomlilo ombi kakhulu awukhumbulayo, uze ubongeze.
6. Ngqinga amandla akukhathazayo kakhulu. Yiyo leyo apho kuya kuya khona i-windbreak, i-firebreak okanye uthango lwakho lokuqala.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Imephu ye-sector ibonisa apho ilanga, umoya, umlilo, amanzi, iqabaka, imibono nengxolo kuvela khona, ukuze uwamkele, uwathintele okanye uwakhokele ngamnye.
- Ikhampasi eMzantsi Afrika ikhomba malunga ne-20 ukuya kwi-28 yeedigri entshona yemntla oyinyani; ilanga lasemini lisemntla.
- Umlilo unyuka ithambeka ngokukhawuleza: beka izikhuseli zomlilo ezantsi kwethambeka nakwi-sector yomlilo.
- Ilanga eliphantsi lasebusika livela emntla, kwaye umoya obandayo uqokelelana kwiindawo eziphantsi. Cwangcisa iibhedi, imithi nendlu ujonge zombini.
- UMzantsi weAfrika ufudumala ngokukhawuleza. Phendula ngokwahlukahlukana, ngee-perennial, ngomhlaba ogqunyiweyo, ngamanzi agciniweyo nangembewu egciniweyo.

Hamba umhlaba wakho kuzo zonke iisizini uphethe imephu. Isizini nganye iya kongeza isiqwenga obungasibonanga.
