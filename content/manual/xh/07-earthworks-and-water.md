# Imisebenzi yomhlaba namanzi

Imvula sisivuno esiwela simahla kwifama nganye. Emhlabeni wethu omninzi iqukuqela ngqo isihla, ithabathe i-**topsoil** (umhlaba ongaphezulu omnyama nophilayo) kunye nayo. Isaqhwithi esinye esizalisa indlela ngezikhukula siyishiya intsimi isomile emva kweveki.

**Imisebenzi yomhlaba** (earthworks), oko kukuguqula umhlaba, ukumba, ukuhambisa nokuwuqwebela, itshintsha oko. Ngokuwuqwebela umhlaba ngepiki, ngesipeyidi okanye ngomatshini, siwacothisa amanzi, siwasasaze, size siwavumele angene emhlabeni. Imisebenzi yomhlaba ngamathambo efama. Amagadi, imithi nezilwanyana zonke zixhomekeke kuwo.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukulinganisa uphahla lwakho ubone ukuba ingakanani imvula onokuyiqokelela, nokuba udinga itanki enkulu kangakanani
- Ukwakha i-A-frame, ukuphawula i-contour ngezikhonkwane nokumba i-swale kumgama ofanelekileyo ukusuka kwenye
- Ukukhetha indlela yokulungisa umhlaba ephazamisa umhlaba wakho kancinci
- Ukucwangcisa iibhedi nokukhetha indlela yonkcenkceshelo ehambelana namanzi akho
- Ukusebenzisa amanzi ebhafu neshawa kwimithi ngokukhuselekileyo

## Okwenziwa yimisebenzi yomhlaba

Amanzi aqhubela ngaphandle komhlaba ongenanto athwala umhlaba nawo. Amanzi ahlala emhlabeni angena aze atyise iingcambu iiveki. Imisebenzi yomhlaba igqiba ukuba kwenzeka eyiphi kwezi zimbini.

Imisebenzi yomhlaba elungileyo:

- ivumela imvula ingene emhlabeni endaweni yokuqukuqela isihla
- izalisa kwakhona amanzi angaphantsi komhlaba, amanzi agcinwe nzulu emhlabeni nasematyeni
- imisa i-**erosion**, oko kukukhukuliswa okanye ukuphephethwa komhlaba
- inkcenkceshela imithi ngaphandle kokuba umntu athwale ibhakethi
- ikhokelela amanzi kwizityalo
- yenza amakhaya amaxoxo, eentaka nezinambuzane

Imisebenzi yomhlaba inokwenza umonakalo omkhulu nayo. Amanzi aqokelelwe kwindawo enye aze akhululwe ngequbuliso anokumba umsantsa omkhulu wokukhukuliseka (donga) ngesaqhwithi esinye. Ngoko **siyila sicinga isaqhwithi esikhulu kakhulu**. Umsebenzi womhlaba ngamnye unendlela ekhuselekileyo yokuphuphuma, ukuze amanzi agqithisileyo emke kancinci ngaphandle kokophula udonga lomhlaba.

Imisebenzi yomhlaba esiyisebenzisa kakhulu:

- **Ii-swale:** imisele ebiwe ilingana ewela ithambeka, nomhlaba okhutshiweyo ubekwe ngecala elingezantsi njengebhanki.
- **Izitya zokungenisa amanzi (infiltration basins):** imingxuma engenzulu, enomgangatho olinganayo, ezaliswa yi-**runoff** (imvula equkuqela emhlabeni endaweni yokungena) iyivumele ingene.
- **Amachibi namadama:** ugcino lwamanzi avulekileyo, luzaliswa yi-runoff okanye kuphuphuma itanki.
- **Imisele neenduli zokuphambukisa amanzi:** imisele emincinci okanye iinduli eziphantsi ezijika amanzi ezisuse endleleni zize kwi-swale, kwisitya okanye kwichibi.
- **Iinkqubo ze-net-and-pan:** imisele emincinci (i-net) ekhokelela i-runoff kwizitya zokutyala (ii-pan).
- **I-hügelkultur:** iinduli ezakhiwe phezu kweenkuni ezinkulu namasebe angcwatyiweyo, afunxa amanzi njengesiponji njengoko ebola.

Imvula nethambeka zigqiba ukuba eyiphi kuzo efanele umhlaba wakho.

## Ukuqokelela nokugcina amanzi emvula

Amanzi athunyelwa ngemibhobho esuka kude anokuma nangaliphi na ixesha. Amanzi owaqokeleleyo wena akhona xa uwadinga. Ngoko umgaqo ulula: **qokelela imvula kufutshane kangangoko nalapho iwela khona**, uze uyigcine.

Siyigcina kwiidrum, kwiitanki, kumachibi nakumadama. Kodwa eyona ndawo inkulu yokuyigcina ngumhlaba ngokwawo. Umhlaba otyebe nge-**organic matter** (izinto eziphilayo ezibolayo), ogutyungelwe zizityalo nge-**mulch** (ingubo yengca eyomileyo, yamagqabi okanye yotyani), ufunxa imvula uyibambe ngcono kakhulu kunomhlaba ongenanto, oqinileyo. I-permaculture ibiza lo mhlaba ngokuthi **ispontshi esiphilayo** (living sponge).

### Imigaqo esibhozo yokuqokelela imvula

Incwadi kaBrad Lancaster ethi *Rainwater Harvesting for Drylands and Beyond* ibeka imigaqo esibhozo. Ngamazwi ethu:

1. **Bukela ixesha elide.** Hamba kumhlaba wakho imvula iyana. Bona apho amanzi aqukuqela khona nalapho ema khona. Yakha phezu kwento esele isebenza.
2. **Qala ngaphezulu uze usebenze uhle.** Ngaphezulu kwethambeka amanzi mancinci kwaye ahamba ngokucotha, ngoko kulula ukuwabamba. Ukusuka apho anokuhla aye apho uwadinga khona.
3. **Qala kancinci nangokulula.** Imisebenzi emininzi emincinci onokuyakha uze uyilungise ngokwakho ingcono kunomsebenzi omnye omkhulu.
4. **Wacothise, wasasaze, wangenise.** Ungavumeli amanzi aqhubele ngaphandle komhlaba.
5. **Cwangcisa indlela yokuphuphuma uze uyisebenzise.** Khokelela amanzi agqithisileyo apho anokwenza okuncedo ngakumbi.
6. **Gcina umhlaba ugutyungelwe** zizityalo nge-mulch, ukuze ispontshi sikhule unyaka ngamnye.
7. **Yenza umsebenzi ngamnye wenze imisebenzi emininzi.** Ibhanki ye-swale inokuba yindlela eyomileyo; imithi enkcenkceshelwa sisitya inokuthwala umthunzi endlini.
8. **Qhubeka ubukela ulungise.** Ixesha ngalinye lemvula likufundisa into entsha, nto leyo ikubuyisela kumgaqo wokuqala.

### Ukuqokelela amanzi ophahleni

Uphahla olukhuni nolugudileyo lunika amanzi amaninzi kakhulu. Isinki, iishiti ze-IBR neethayile zinika amanzi amaninzi kakhulu kunophahla lwengca.

1. Fakela ii-gutter emaphethelweni aphantsi ophahla, zithambekele kancinci ukuya kwitanki ukuze amanzi angemi kuzo.
2. Beka **ihluzo lamagqabi** phezu kwe-gutter okanye phezu komngxuma wokungena wetanki.
3. Faka **i-first-flush diverter** phambi kwetanki. Le yipayipi okanye isitya esibamba amanzi okuqala nawona angcole kakhulu emvuleni nganye, anothuli, ubulongwe beentaka namagqabi. Yikhuphe amanzi emva kwemvula nganye.
4. Faka indlela yokuphuphuma kufutshane nencopho yetanki uze uyikhokelele kwenye itanki, kwichibi, kwi-swale okanye kwisitya somthi, kungabi kudonga.
5. Dibanisa iitanki ezimbini okanye ngaphezulu ngepayipi ekufutshane nesiseko, ukuze zizale kwaye zikhuphe amanzi kunye.

Amagqabi afika kwitanki ayabola aze azise iintsholongwane, ngoko gcina ii-gutter nezihluzi zicocekile. Gcina yonke itanki ivaliwe ngesigqubuthelo esiqinileyo esinomnatha, ukuze abantwana, izilwanyana neemosquito zingabi nakungena. Amanzi avela ophahleni awacocekanga ngokwaneleyo ukuba asele enjalo: kuqala wabilise.

### Unokuqokelela amanzi amaninzi kangakanani?

I-1 mm yemvula kwi-1 m² inika **ilitha eli-1** lamanzi. Ngoko:

**Amanzi aqokelelweyo (iilitha) = indawo (m²) × imvula (mm) × umlinganiselo we-runoff**

**Umlinganiselo we-runoff** (runoff coefficient) yinxalenye yemvula equkuqelayo, ebhalwa njengenani phakathi kwe-0 ne-1. Umlinganiselo we-0.8 uthetha ukuba i-80% iqukuqela; intsalela ifumeza uphahla, ichitheka ngaphaya kwe-gutter okanye iya kwi-first-flush diverter.

Linganisa uphahla **usuka phezulu**, ubandakanya nemiphetho ephumela ngaphandle. Imvula iwa ngqo ezantsi, ngoko uphahla olutsiba kakhulu alubambi mvula ingakumbi kunolo lulinganayo ngaphezu komgangatho ofanayo.

**Umzekelo osebenzayo: uphahla kwindawo enemvula engama-750 mm**

1. Uphahla lulinganisa i-12 m × 10 m xa ulukhangela usuka phezulu = **120 m²**.
2. Imvula eweleyo kuyo kunyaka ophakathi = 120 × 750 = 90 000 iilitha.
3. Uphahla lwesinki olunee-gutter ezilungileyo lunomlinganiselo we-0.8 ukuya kwi-0.9. Cwangcisa nge-**0.8**.
4. Amanzi onokuwaqokelela = 120 × 750 × 0.8 = **72 000 iilitha ngonyaka**.

Uphahla olutsha olunee-gutter ezigqibeleleyo lunokufikelela kwi-0.95 (85 500 iilitha), kodwa cwangcisa nge-0.8. Unyaka owomileyo unika amanzi amancinci kakhulu.

Ifomula efanayo isebenza nakweyiphi na enye indawo. Kutshintsha kuphela umlinganiselo. I-runoff iphezulu emathambekeni atsiba kakhulu, kumhlaba odongwe nakwizaqhwithi ezinkulu.

| Indawo | Umlinganiselo we-runoff |
|---|---|
| Uphahla lwesinki | 0.8 ukuya kwi-0.9 |
| Uphahla lweethayile | 0.6 ukuya kwi-0.9 |
| Uphahla lwengca | malunga ne-0.2 |
| Ikhonkrithi okanye itar | 0.7 ukuya kwi-0.95 |
| Indlela yegraveli | 0.5 ukuya kwi-0.7 |
| Umhlaba ongenanto, oqinileyo | 0.3 ukuya kwi-0.6 |
| Ingca okanye idlelo lendalo (veld) | 0.05 ukuya kwi-0.35 |

Umhlaba ogutyungelweyo uvumela uninzi lwemvula ukuba lungene, kwaye oko kuyinto esiyifunayo emhlabeni wokutyala. Iindawo eziqinileyo ziyo apho siqokelela khona.

### Itanki mayibe nkulu kangakanani?

Usebenzisa amanzi unyaka wonke, ngoko itanki ayidingi ukubamba imvula yonke yonyaka. Idinga nje ukukudlulisa kwiinyanga ezomileyo.

**Umzekelo osebenzayo: itanki yekhaya**

1. Abantu abahlanu bacwangcisa iilitha ezingama-25 umntu ngamnye ngemini zokusela, zokupheka nokuhlamba: **iilitha ezili-125 ngemini**.
2. Ngonyaka oko kuyi-125 × 365 = 45 625 iilitha. Uphahla lwe-120 m² oluchaziweyo lunika malunga ne-72 000 iilitha, ngoko uphahla lukhulu ngokwaneleyo.
3. Kwingingqi yangaphakathi enemvula yasehlotyeni, ixesha elomileyo liqhuba malunga nokusuka ngoCanzibe ukuya ngeyoMsintsi: malunga neentsuku ezili-**150**. (KwiNtshona Koloni ixesha elomileyo lihlobo.)
4. Amanzi adingekayo ukulidlula = 125 × 150 = **18 750 iilitha**.
5. Ngoko ikhaya lidinga malunga ne-**20 000 iilitha** zogcino, umzekelo iitanki ezibini ezingama-10 000 iilitha, ezizele xa imvula iyeka.

Ukufumana impendulo echanekileyo ngakumbi, bhala phantsi iinyanga ezili-12. Kwinyanga nganye, bala oko uphahla lukunika kona (indawo × imvula yaloo nyanga × umlinganiselo) nento oyisebenzisayo, uze ugcine ubalo oluqhubekayo lwento esele kwitanki. Ukunqongophala okuzulu kakhulu ngexesha elomileyo kubonisa ugcino oludingayo.

Amanzi egadi ayongezelelo, kwaye igadi yemifuno yexesha elomileyo iselela amanzi amaninzi kakhulu kunekhaya. Ngoko sigcina amanzi nasemhlabeni.

## Izitya zokungenisa amanzi

**Isitya sokungenisa amanzi** (infiltration basin) yingxuma engenzulu enomgangatho olinganayo obamba i-runoff uyivumele ingene (**i-infiltration** kukungena kwamanzi emhlabeni). Izitya zilungele amathambeka athambileyo. Zibamba amanzi asezindleleni ezinkulu nezincinci, aphuphuma kwiitanki nakwi-greywater (amanzi asetyenzisiweyo ebhafu neshawa).

1. Mba ingxuma engenzulu, enomgangatho olinganayo, ngezantsi kwalapho amanzi evela khona.
2. Nika isitya ngasinye indlela yokuphuphuma ingene kwesilandelayo, ukuze ikhonkco lezitya lidlulise amanzi lisihla ethambekeni, ekugqibeleni lize kwi-swale okanye kwindawo evulekileyo ekhuselekileyo.
3. Tyala imiphetho ngemithi yeziqhamo, yamandongomane okanye yemithi **yomdabu** (imithi ekhula ngokwendalo kwingingqi yethu).
4. Gquma isitya kakuhle nge-mulch. I-mulch igcina umhlaba uvulekile ukuze amanzi angene ngokukhawuleza.

Gcina izitya zingenzulu ngaphezu kwe-50 cm malunga, uqinisekise ukuba amanzi angena emhlabeni kungadlulanga iiyure ezimalunga ne-12. Iingcambu zidinga umoya, kwaye iimosquito zidinga iintsuku ezininzi zamanzi amileyo ukuze zizale. Ukuba isitya sihlala simanzi ixesha elide, senze singenzulu ngakumbi, yongeza i-mulch okanye wabelanise amanzi kwizitya ezininzi.

## Amachibi namadama

Ichibi liguqula isaqhwithi esinye sibe ngamanzi eeveki. Linkcenkceshela izilwanyana nezityalo, licothisa amanzi esaqhwithi, lingakhulisa iintlanzi, laze libize amaxoxo, iintaka nee-dragonfly ezidla izinambuzane ezonakalisayo.

Cinga ngezi zinto ngaphambi kokuba umbe:

- **Umhlaba.** Amanzi aphuma ngesanti. Ichibi libamba amanzi kuphela emhlabeni onodongwe olwaneleyo, okanye elinomgubungelo wodongwe olucinezelweyo okanye i-liner yechibi.
- **Ithambeka.** Kokukhona umhlaba utsiba, kokukhona kunzima ukuyila nokwakha ichibi okanye idama. Idama lingumsebenzi womyili onamava.
- **Indlela yokuphuphuma (spillway).** Ichibi ngalinye nedama ngalinye lidinga indlela ebanzi yokuphuphuma, ekhuselwe ngamatye okanye ngengca eshinyeneyo, ukuze amanzi angaze ahambe phezu kodonga.
- **Ukuphela ngumphunga.** Kuninzi loMzantsi Afrika umphezulu wamanzi avulekileyo uphulukana nangaphezulu kwe-1 400 mm ngonyaka, kwaye kuninzi lwangaphakathi olomileyo i-2 000 mm okanye ngaphezulu. Oko kudla ngokuba ngaphezulu kwemvula. Ngama-5 mm ngemini, ichibi elinomphezulu we-20 m² liphulukana neelitha ezili-100 ngemini. Ichibi elinzulu elinomphezulu omncinci, elisithelwe yimithi, liphulukana nokuncinane.
- **Abantu.** Thambekisa macala njengoko umba ukuze angawi, kwaye gcina bonke abantu kude kakhulu nomatshini osebenzayo. Thinga ichibi eligqityiweyo ngesango umntwana angenakukwazi ukulivula, uze wenze icala elinye lithambekele kancinci ukuze umntu okanye isilwanyana esiwelayo sikwazi ukunyuka siphume. Apho i-bilharzia ikhoyo, ungahlambi, ungantshi, ungemi ngeenyawo ezingenanto emanzini echibi.

### Ukubala umthamo wechibi

**Umthamo (m³) ≈ ubude × ububanzi × ubunzulu obuphakathi.** Kwichibi elimile okwesitya, ubunzulu obuphakathi bumalunga nesiqingatha seyona ndawo inzulu. I-m³ enye iphatha iilitha ezili-1 000.

**Umzekelo osebenzayo: ichibi elincinci le-runoff**

1. Ichibi lide nge-5 m, libanzi nge-4 m, lizulu nge-1.2 m embindini. Ubunzulu obuphakathi ≈ 0.6 m.
2. Umthamo ≈ 5 × 4 × 0.6 = **12 m³**, okanye iilitha ezili-12 000.
3. Ukulizalisa ngemvula enye ye-40 mm ukusuka kwindawo eqinileyo (umlinganiselo malunga ne-1), udinga 12 000 ÷ 40 = **300 m²** zophahla okanye zepevimenti.
4. Ukusuka kwidlelo lendalo elidliwe (veld, umlinganiselo malunga ne-0.2), i-m² nganye inika 40 × 0.2 = iilitha ezi-8, ngoko udinga 12 000 ÷ 8 = **1 500 m²**.

Ichibi elitsha lidinga ngaphezu koku, kuba umhlaba owomileyo ojikelezileyo usela kuqala amanzi.

## Amadama esanti

Kwilizwe elomileyo, imilambo iba yisanti ixesha elininzi lonyaka. Phantsi kwaloo santi, amanzi adla ngokusahamba. Idama lesanti (sand dam) liwabamba apho, kude nelanga.

Idama lesanti ludonga olomeleleyo lwamatye akhiweyo anentsimbi okanye lwekhonkrithi, ludla ngokuphakama nge-1 ukuya kwi-5 m, lwakhiwe phesheya **komlambo onesanti wexesha elithile** (seasonal sandy river).

1. Xa umlambo uhamba, isanti enzima ihlala ngasemva kodonga, kwaye i-silt elula ihlanjwa iye ezantsi.
2. Phakathi kwexesha elinye ukuya kwamane emvula, isanti izalisa indawo engasemva kodonga.
3. Amanzi abanjwa kwizithuba eziphakathi kweenkozo zesanti. Isanti inokubamba kude kube malunga nama-40% emthamo wayo njengamanzi.
4. Uninzi lokuhamba kwamanzi lusadlula phezu kodonga. I-Excellent Development, umbutho wokunceda owakhe amadama esanti amaninzi eAfrika, uchaza ukuba yi-97% ukuya kwi-99%.
5. Abantu bakha amanzi emthonjeni obiwe ecaleni kwedama, okanye ngepayipi esodongeni eya kwitephu okanye kwiphampu.

Lo mbutho ukwaxela ukuba idama lesanti elikhulu linokubamba kude kube yiilitha ezizizigidi ezingama-40, ezanele abantu abangaphezu kwe-1 000 unyaka wonke. Phantsi kwesanti, amanzi amancinci kakhulu aphelela ngumphunga, kwaye isanti iyawahluza ithintele izilwanyana neemosquito. Inqanaba lamanzi angaphantsi komhlaba liyanyuka ejikeleze idama, kwaye imithi, izityalo namadlelo zikhula ngcono.

Amadama esanti asebenza kuphela kwindawo efanelekileyo. Udonga kufuneka lume phezu kwelitye elingaphantsi eliqinileyo (bedrock) lide lingene ubuncinane nge-1.5 m ngaphaya komphetho wesikhukula kwibhanki nganye, ukuze izikhukula zingakwazi ukusika ejikeleze. Umlambo kufuneka uthwale isanti enkulu eyirhabaxa, hayi ikakhulu i-silt, kwaye kufuneka uqhubeke uhamba njengakuqala. Lo ngumsebenzi womyili onamava. ELesotho, i-Bethel Business and Community Development Centre yakha elinye njengomzekelo.

## Ii-swale

**I-swale** (umsele ogcina amanzi emvula) ngumsele ombiwe ngqo ecaleni kwe-**contour**, umgca ocingelwayo owela ithambeka udibanisa amanqaku asekuphakameni okufanayo. Kuba umsele ulinganayo, amanzi awabaleki ecaleni kwawo. Asasazeka angene emhlabeni, kwaye imithi esezantsi ikhulisa iingcambu ezinzulu ziye kula manzi agciniweyo.

I-swale inamacandelo amabini: umsele, kunye ne-**berm** (udonga oluphantsi lomhlaba), ibhanki yomhlaba obuthuliweyo ubekwe ngakwicala elihlayo ethambekeni.

### Ukudibanisa ii-swale namachibi

1. I-runoff izalisa i-swale isasazeke ecaleni kwayo iye kwichibi esinye isiphelo.
2. Xa ichibi lizele, amanzi abuyela umva azalise i-swale.
3. Amanzi agqithisileyo aphuma ngaphezu kwe-**spillway** (indlela yokuphuphuma): icandelo elifutshane, elilinganayo le-berm eliphantsi kunalo lonke, elikhuselwe ngamatye okanye ngengca eshinyeneyo. Gcina incopho ye-berm iphezulu ubuncinane ngobubanzi besandla kune-spillway, ukuze amanzi angaze agalele phezu kwe-berm.
4. I-spillway inika i-swale elandelayo ezantsi, neyalandelayo, de amanzi afike kwichibi eliphantsi okanye aphume emhlabeni.

Amanzi aphuma kwinkqubo enje acotha kwaye acaca, kwaye athwala umhlaba omncinci kakhulu. **Induli yokuphambukisa amanzi** (diversion mound) esezantsi eyakhiwe iyithe tye phesheya kwendlela yemoto iya kuphambukisa i-runoff yayo iye kwi-swale nayo.

### Zingakanani, zibekwa kude kangakanani?

I-swale encinci yegadi inokuba nzulu ngeeblade ezimbini zesipeyidi kwaye ibanzi ngenye. I-swale yentsimi inokuba nkulu kakhulu. I-swale nganye kufuneka ibambe i-runoff yomhlaba ongaphezulu kwayo kwisaqhwithi esikhulu, kwaye ifomula yophahla ikuxelela ukuba kungakanani oko.

**Umzekelo osebenzayo: umgama phakathi kwee-swale**

1. I-swale ibanzi malunga ne-0.5 m kwaye ibamba amanzi anzulu nge-0.3 m ngezantsi kwe-spillway. Imitha nganye ibamba 0.5 × 0.3 = 0.15 m³ = **iilitha ezili-150**.
2. Cwangcisa isaqhwithi esikhulu se-**50 mm**.
3. Umhlaba ongaphezulu yidlelo lendalo elidliwe, umlinganiselo malunga ne-0.3. I-m² nganye inika 50 × 0.3 = **iilitha ezili-15**.
4. Imitha nganye ye-swale inokwamkela i-runoff evela kwi-150 ÷ 15 = 10 m² zomhlaba: umtya obanzi nge-1 m kwaye ude nge-10 m unyuka ethambekeni.
5. Ngoko ii-swale zibekwa zahlukene ngokwe-**10 m** malunga, kulinganiswe kusihla ethambekeni. Kwisaqhwithi esikhulu ngakumbi, ii-spillway zithwala intsalela.

Apho imvula ilula okanye umhlaba ugutyungelwe kakuhle, ii-swale zinokubekwa kude ngakumbi. Emhlabeni ongenanto, emathambekeni atsiba ngakumbi okanye kwiindawo ezimanzi ngakumbi, zisondeze.

### Apho ungafanele umbe ii-swale

- **Amathambeka atsiba kakhulu.** Gcina ii-swale kumathambeka angaphantsi kwe-15% malunga (ukuhla kwe-15 m kwi-100 m). Umhlaba omanzi kwindawo etsiba kakhulu unokutyibilika. Sebenzisa ii-terrace (amanyathelo omhlaba) nemithi endaweni yoko.
- **Udongwe olunzima okanye umhlaba owele amanzi.** Ukuba amanzi awangeni, i-swale iba ngumsele onamanzi amileyo, abolileyo. Sebenzisa ichibi okanye umsele wokuphambukisa amanzi.
- **Kufutshane nezakhiwo.** Gcina ii-swale nezitya ubuncinane ngo-3 m kwiindonga nasezisekweni, kwaye ungaze uzibeke ethambekeni ngaphezulu kwendlu yangasese engumngxuma, itanki ye-septic okanye i-soak-away.
- **Iindawo ezimanzi kakhulu.** Apho umhlaba umanzi ixesha elininzi lonyaka, udinga imisele ekhokelela amanzi aphume.

### Ukumba i-swale

Qala uphawule i-contour ngezikhonkwane usebenzisa i-A-frame (jonga "Zama oku" ekupheleni kwesi sahluko). Emva koko:

1. Mba umsele ecaleni kwezikhonkwane, ugcine umgangatho wawo ulinganayo.
2. Beka umhlaba namatye ngakwicala elisezantsi uze ucinezele i-berm ngamanqanaba ngeenyawo.
3. Khusela iziphelo ne-spillway ngamatye okanye ngengca eqinileyo, ukuze amanzi angakwazi ukusika ejikeleze.
4. Tyala uze ugquma i-berm ngosuku olunye, imvula ingekayihlambi.

### Ukutyala i-swale ne-berm

Ngoku kuqala ukonwaba! Izityalo ezithanda amanzi zingena emseleni, imithi eluncedo ingena kwi-berm nangaphantsi kwayo, nezityalo ezithanda ukoma ngakumbi zingena phezulu.

- **Kwi-berm:** imithi nezihlahla zokuqala ukumila ezikhula ngokukhawuleza, ezinjengemithi yomdabu ye-**legume** (izityalo zosapho lweembotyi, uninzi lwazo lufaka inayitrojeni emhlabeni) efana neentlobo ze-*Vachellia*, ukubamba ibhanki nokwakha ukuchuma komhlaba.
- **Izityalo ze-mulch kwi-berm:** i-comfrey, i-lemongrass, i-yarrow, i-nettle nengca ye-vetiver engavelisi mbewu ehlumayo, eyanqunyulwa rhoqo ukuze kwenziwe i-mulch ne-**compost** (inkunkuma yezityalo ebolile ibe kukutya komhlaba okumnyama, okuthambileyo). I-berm iba yibhanki ye-mulch.
- **Abakhi bomhlaba:** i-clover, i-lucerne ne-sunn hemp. Vumela i-dandelion ne-plantain zikhule uze uzinqumle ukuze zibe yi-mulch.
- **Emseleni:** i-cowpea okanye i-lupin njengesigquma, nezityalo ezithanda amanzi ezifana namadumbe (taro, *Colocasia esculenta*). Gcina amadumbe kwii-swale nasezibhedini zegadi, kude nemilanjana neendawo ezimanzi (wetlands), apho anokusasazeka khona.
- **Ngezantsi kwe-swale:** amalamuni nezinye ii-citrus, iibhanana apho kungekho qabaka, neetumato. Cinga ngomthunzi eziya kuwenza.
- **Phantsi kwemithi:** i-**groundcover** (izityalo eziphantsi ezigubungela umhlaba) ezinyamezela umthunzi ezifana nebhatata ne-New Zealand spinach.

## Ukulungisa umhlaba

Ngalo lonke ixesha siphendula umhlaba, siphula iitonela, ukungunda neziqwengana zomhlaba ezithe zathatha iminyaka ukwakhiwa. Ngoko khetha indlela ephazamisa umhlaba kancinci. Njengoko ifama ikhula yenza i-mulch ne-compost yayo, uya kudinga ukuyiphazamisa kancinci nangakumbi.

### Qala ngendlela enye rhoqo

Nokuba sisiphi isixhobo esilandelayo, qala ngolu hlobo:

1. Khupha ukhula olubi olusasazekayo, olufana ne-kikuyu (iingcambu zayo ezihamba phezu komhlaba) ne-couch grass, ekuya kuthi ukumba kuyandise.
2. Nqumla izityalo ukuya kufutshane nomhlaba kangangoko unako.
3. Nkcenkceshela kakuhle, okanye linda imvula, uze uvumele umhlaba uphume amanzi de ufume kodwa ungamanzi.

### Ngesandla

Kwindawo encinci okanye ephakathi, sebenzisa ipiki, ispeyidi nefolokhwe yegadi.

1. Susa izinto ozinqunyileyo uzisebenzisele i-compost okanye i-mulch.
2. Thambisa i-topsoil ngefolokhwe.
3. Rekha inkunkuma yonke uze ulinganise ubuso.

### Ngetrektara

Ukulima ngokuphindaphindiweyo yenye yezona zizathu eziphambili ze-erosion yomhlaba nokulahleka kwekhabhoni yomhlaba kwihlabathi lonke. Oomatshini abanzima bacinezela umhlaba, kwaye ukulima rhoqo ngonyaka kwenza umaleko oqinileyo ngaphantsi kwebleyidi, obizwa ngokuthi **i-plough pan** (umhlaba oqinileyo ngaphantsi kokulima). Ukulima kunika ukuqhuma okufutshane kokuchuma njengoko umoya ungena kakhulu emhlabeni, emva koko ukuchuma kuyehla ixesha ngalinye.

Ngoko ukuba sisebenzisa itrektara kwaphela, siyisebenzisela ikakhulu ukuqala ifama. **Ii-ripper nee-chisel plough** zivula umhlaba oqinileyo ngaphandle kokuwuphendula, kwaye zilungile ekulungiseni umhlaba owonakeleyo. Khetha izixhobo eziphendula umhlaba kancinci, uze uzidlulise ngomhlaba kancinci kangangoko unako.

**Itrektara enamavili amabini** (two-wheel tractor) ngumatshini oqhutywa ngokuhamba ngasemva kwawo, oqhuba i-rotavator (ii-blade ezijikelezayo ezityumza umhlaba), ipulawu encinci okanye umatshini wokusika ingca. Ibiza kancinci kakhulu ukuyithenga nokuyisebenzisa kunetrektara enkulu. Sebenzisa i-rotavator kancinci; ukuyisebenzisa rhoqo nako kuyawonakalisa ulwakhiwo lomhlaba.

### Ii-animal tractor

**I-animal tractor** (indlu yeenkukhu okanye yeehagu ehambayo) isebenzisa izilwanyana ukucoca nokulungisa umhlaba (jonga iSahluko 5). Iinkukhu, amadada, iigusha, iibhokhwe neenkomo ziyawudla phantsi; iihagu nazo ziyamba.

1. Nqumla izityalo ezinkulu zide zilingane nomhlaba. Ukuba ufuna iihagu zimbe, nkcenkceshela indawo kuqala.
2. Beka uthango oluhambayo ejikeleze indawo. Uthango lombane olusebenza ngesixhobo samandla elanga lusebenza kakuhle.
3. Nika izilwanyana indawo yokukhuselwa ngaphakathi ukuba ziyayidinga, njengendlu yeenkukhu ehambayo.
4. Landelisa iinkomo okanye iihagu ngeenkukhu. Zisasaza ubulongwe zize zidle imibungu yeempukane ekuyo.
5. Thambisa umhlaba ngefolokhwe okanye nge-chisel plough, ungawuphendulanga, uze urekhe uwulinganise.

### Ukulima ngokungamba nokugquma ngamanqanaba

Ukulima ngokungamba (no-dig) kulinganisa umhlaba wehlathi. Amagqabi ayawa, abole aze atyise umhlaba, kwaye uba lula njengesiponji ngaphandle kokumba kwaphela. Iintshulube nokungunda zenza umsebenzi. Imbewu yokhula engcwatyiweyo ihlala ebumnyameni ilele, nto leyo ikonga iiyure zokukhupha ukhula. Ixabiso lelokuba kudingeka izinto ezininzi eziphilayo.

**Ukugquma ngamanqanaba** (sheet mulching, iibhedi zelasagne) kwakha amanqanaba okungamba phezu komhlaba. Kuvalela ingca ize ifele kwaye kutyise ubomi bomhlaba usuka ngaphezulu, yaye yeyona ndlela ikhawulezayo yokuqala igadi encinci emhlabeni oqinileyo, ongenanto.

1. Nqumla ingca nezihlahla uzishiye apho ziwela khona. Nkcenkceshela kakuhle.
2. Sasaza ubulongwe bezilwanyana obuyi-6 ukuya kwi-10 cm ubukhulu. Ubulongwe obutsha bufakwa apha kuphela, ezantsi, ukuvusa ubomi bomhlaba.
3. Bugubungele ngekhadibhodi okanye ngephephandaba, uzigqithisise phezu kwazo ukuze kungangeni kukhanya. Yinkcenkceshele.
4. Yongeza i-10 ukuya kwi-15 cm yengca eyomileyo okanye i-mulch eyomileyo, emva koko umaleko we-compost.
5. Yongeza enye i-10 ukuya kwi-15 cm yengca eyomileyo engenambewu.
6. Gqiba nge-compost okanye nge-topsoil elungileyo. Into oyityalela kuyo kufuneka ibe yi-compost eyenziwe kakuhle okanye ubulongwe obubolileyo kakuhle, ungaze ube bubulongwe obutsha.
7. Nkcenkceshela, vula imingxuma emincinci ngamanqanaba angaphezulu, utyale izithombo kwi-compost, ugquma ejikeleze.

### Ukushushisa ngelanga nokugquma nge-tarp

**Ukushushisa umhlaba ngelanga** (solarisation) kuphekela ukhula ngelanga. Nkcenkceshela umhlaba, uwugubungele ngokuqinileyo ngeplastiki **ecacileyo**, ungcwabe imiphetho uyishiye ubuncinane iiveki ezi-4 ukuya kwezi-6 ngexesha elishushu kakhulu. Ibulala nobunye ubomi bomhlaba obuluncedo, ngoko yisebenzise kuphela apho kufuneka.

**Ukugquma nge-tarp** (tarping) kusebenzisa iplastiki **emnyama**. Ishushisa umhlaba kancinci kwaye isebenza ngokuvala ukukhanya. Gubungela indawo enqunyuliweyo nenkcenkceshelweyo, ubambe ishiti ngamatye okanye ngeengxowa zesanti uze uyishiye iiveki ezi-6 ukuya kwezi-10. Emva koko thambisa i-topsoil ngokushukumisa ifolokhwe uyiya nokubuya, ungayiphendulanga.

Abalimi bemifuno yentengiso basebenzisa i-tarp ukwenza **ibhedi yembewu elinde** (stale seedbed): lungisa uze unkcenkceshele ibhedi, uyigubungele, uvumele imbewu yokhula ihlume ife ebumnyameni, emva koko susa i-tarp emva kweeveki ezimbalwa uhlwayele kwibhedi ecocekileyo.

## Ukwenza iibhedi zokutyala imifuno

Ibhedi yindawo apho amanzi, umhlaba nomqolo wakho badibana khona. Yenze kakuhle kwaye umhlaba uya kuphucuka unyaka ngamnye. Ezi zizikhokelo, azizo mithetho.

Iibhedi zegadi eziphakanyisiweyo zilungele imvula ephakathi nephezulu. Kwiindawo ezomileyo kakhulu yenza **iibhedi zegadi ezingaphantsi komhlaba** (sunken beds), ezisezantsi kancinci kunendlela, ukuze zibambe imvula njengezitya.

Ukuba ithambeka litsiba ngaphezu kwe-3% malunga (ukuhla kwe-3 m kwi-100 m), beka iibhedi neendlela **ecaleni kwe-contour**, ukuze imvula enkulu ingakwazi ukuhla kuzo. Emhlabeni othe tyaba ukhululekile ukwenza izangqa, ii-spiral nee-mandala.

### Ii-double-reach bed eziphakanyisiweyo

**I-double-reach bed** (ibhedi enokufikelelwa kumacala omabini) imxinwa ngokwaneleyo ukuba ufikelele embindini usuka kwiindlela ezisemacaleni omabini, ukuze ungamiyi kuyo ucinezele umhlaba.

- Iibhedi ezibanzi nge-80 ukuya kwi-120 cm. Amagadi entengiso adla ngokusebenzisa i-75 ukuya kwi-90 cm, elungele izixhobo zesandla neetrektara ezinamavili amabini.
- Iindlela ezibanzi nge-40 ukuya kwi-60 cm.
- Iibhedi ezimalunga ne-8 m ubude kwigadi encinci, ukuya kuthi ga kwi-30 m kwigadi yorhwebo.

Iibhedi eziphakanyisiweyo ziphakamisa iingcambu zisuke emhlabeni owele amanzi, zenza i-topsoil ibe nzulu ngomhlaba osuka kwiindlela, kwaye i-compost iya kuphela apho izityalo zikhula khona. Umhlaba ongaze unyatheliswe nongambiweyo kangako uphucuka unyaka ngamnye, de ifolokhwe yegadi ingene kude kufike kwisibambo.

1. Phawula i-contour ephezulu nge-A-frame. Ukuba kukho indawo, beka i-swale encinci ngaphezulu kwebhedi ephezulu.
2. Linganisa ezinye iibhedi neendlela ukuhla ethambekeni, uphawule imiphetho ngekalika okanye ngothuthu. Beka ipayipi yamanzi ecaleni kwemiphetho egobileyo.
3. Uqala ngaphezulu, mba umhlaba kwindlela engaphezulu kwebhedi nganye uwulahlele kwibhedi engezantsi. Phakamisa ibhedi eqhelekileyo nge-10 ukuya kwi-15 cm.
4. Linganisa ibhedi nganye ngerekhi wenze imiphetho ecocekileyo.
5. Yongeza ikalika okanye i-gypsum ukuba uvavanyo lomhlaba lubonisa ukuba uyidinga, kunye ne-compost engange-3 cm malunga.
6. Vulela ibhedi ngefolokhwe ngaphandle kokuyiphendula, uyigquma ngomaleko oshinyeneyo uze unkcenkceshele.
7. Yiyeke ibhedi iphumle iiveki ezimbalwa phambi kokutyala.

Kumagadi amancinci, bophelela iibhedi ngamatye, ngamasebe aqinileyo okanye ngamaplanga e-pallet. Beka ikhadibhodi phezu kwengca engaphakathi, uzalise nge-topsoil elungileyo uze ugqibe nge-compost. Oku kusebenza nasekhonkritini.

### Iibhedi zemisele

**Ibhedi emseleni** (trench bed) yibhedi enzulu ezaliswe zizinto eziphilayo. Iguqula umhlaba onamatye okanye odongwe ube ngumhlaba wokutyala yaye ibamba amanzi kwiindawo ezomileyo. Kunzima ukuyimba, kodwa ihlala iminyaka.

1. Phawula ibhedi ebanzi malunga ne-**1 m** kwaye ide nge-2 ukuya kwi-5 m, uyimbe nzulu malunga ne-**60 cm**.
2. Beka i-topsoil emnyama kwelinye icala, nomhlaba ongaphantsi okhanyayo kwelinye.
3. Beka iintonga okanye izinto ezirhabaxa ezantsi.
4. Zalisa ngamanqanaba: malunga ne-20 cm yezinto eziphilayo (amagqabi omileyo, ingca, intsalela yasekhitshini, ubulongwe), emva koko malunga ne-10 cm yomhlaba ongaphantsi. Nkcenkceshela inqanaba ngalinye.
5. Gqiba nge-topsoil, emva koko ugquma.

Ibhedi iyehla njengoko ibola. Yongeze nge-compost ngexesha ngalinye.

### Iibhedi zamanyathelo

Kumhlaba otsiba kakhulu, i-terrace linyathelo elilinganayo elinqunyulwe ethambekeni. Ibhanki yalo engaphambili isebenza njenge-berm ye-swale, ibamba amanzi nomhlaba. Akha iindonga ngamatye, ngeenkuni ezinkulu okanye ngeetayara ezindala. Apho kuhlasele i-wattle, iziqu ezinqunyuliweyo ezilukiweyo zenza izikhuseli ezibamba umhlaba kakuhle; sebenzisa kuphela iziqu ezomileyo ezingenazo iingobozi zembewu, ukuze kungasasazeki mbewu.

### Iibhedi zemingxuma neesangqa zebhanana

**Ibhedi emngxunyeni** (pit bed) ngumngxuma ojikelezileyo ongange-50 cm ubunzulu (ukuya kwi-1 m) kwaye ububanzi be-1 ukuya kwi-2 m, ozaliswe zizinto eziphilayo. Izityalo zikhula ejikeleze umphetho zize zityiswe kukubola namanzi asembindini. **Isangqa sebhanana** (banana circle) luhlobo olwaziwa kakhulu.

1. Khetha indawo apho kufika khona amanzi: ngaphantsi kwetephu, kufutshane nendawo amanzi aphuma kancinci emhlabeni, okanye apho kuhamba khona i-greywater okanye i-runoff.
2. Phakamisa iziqwenga zengca nomhlaba kunye ne-topsoil uzibeke ecaleni.
3. Mba umsele omncinci wokukhokelela amanzi aye embindini, emva koko umbe umngxuma uwuzalise ngezinto eziphilayo.
4. Buyisela umhlaba njengesangqa esiphantsi ejikeleze umphetho, utyale uze ugquma kakuhle.
5. Qhubeka usondla embindini ngezinto eziphilayo.

Iibhanana, amadumbe, ibhatata, i-ginger nepapaya zikhula kakuhle kwiindawo ezingenaqabaka. Unokubeka nomgangatho wamaplanga onemingxuma phezu komngxuma uze uhlambe ngeshawa apho.

### Ii-mandala nee-keyhole bed

Iibhedi ezijikelezileyo, **ii-keyhole bed** (ibhedi ejikelezileyo enendlela ekhethiweyo ukuya embindini) nee-mandala zilungele umhlaba othe tyaba. Usuka kwindawo enye ungafikelela kuninzi lwebhedi, ngoko beka izityalo ozikha rhoqo kufutshane nendlela. Beka izityalo ezinde ngakwicala elingasemzantsi, kuba ilanga lisemntla.

## Unkcenkceshelo

Ithonsi ngalinye olithwalayo okanye olipompayo likuxabisa into. Unkcenkceshelo olungcono lubeka amanzi ezingcambini kuphela, kungekho ngokukhawuleza kunendlela umhlaba onokuwasela ngayo. Nkcenkceshela kusasa kakhulu okanye ngexesha lokuhlwa, uze ugquma yonke into oyinkcenkceshelayo.

### Ukunkcenkcesha ngesandla

Ipayipi okanye ikeni ilahla amanzi kwiindlela kwaye inkcenkceshela ngokungalinganiyo. Endaweni yoko, yenza isitya esincinci okanye umsele ecaleni kwesityalo ngasinye okanye umqolo, uwuzalise, uwuyeke ungene, uze uqhubeke. Kwizithombo sebenzisa ikeni enentloko ecolekileyo (rose); amanzi ahleli elangeni abalungeleyo kunamanzi abandayo omngxuma wamanzi (borehole).

### Unkcenkceshelo ngebhotile

1. Sika isiseko sebhotile endala yeplastiki.
2. Yenza imingxuma emincinci embalwa kwisigqubuthelo.
3. Ngcwaba ibhotile intamo ithe ntsi ecaleni kwesityalo, nomlomo ovulekileyo ungaphezulu kancinci komhlaba.
4. Yizalise. Amanzi ayangena kancinci ukuya ezingcambini.

### Unkcenkceshelo ngamathonsi

**Unkcenkceshelo ngamathonsi** (drip irrigation) luvumela amanzi athontsize kancinci emhlabeni ezingcambini zesityalo ngasinye. Iindlela ziyahlala zomile, ukhula lukhula kancinci kwaye kulahleka amanzi amancinci: malunga ne-90% ifikelela ezingcambini, xa kuthelekiswa nama-75% ukuya kuma-85% kwizitshizi.

- Sebenzisa umbhobho wamathonsi we-16 mm okanye we-20 mm onee-dripper (izitshizi zamathonsi) eziwakhelwe ngaphakathi ezahlukene ngo-20 ukuya kwi-30 cm, obekwe phantsi kwe-mulch.
- Beka imibhobho ukuze ibe ngo-30 ukuya kwi-50 cm ukwahlukana; isondele ngakumbi kwisanti, apho amanzi esasazeka kancinci ecaleni. Ibhedi ye-1 m idinga imibhobho emi-2, ibhedi ye-1.5 m ezi-3.
- Rhoqo faka isihluzi, kuba ukungcola kuvala ii-dripper, uze ukhuphe amanzi ngeziphelo zemibhobho ngamaxesha athile.

**Uxinzelelo olusuka kwitanki:** wonke ama-10 m okuphakama phakathi kwamanzi akwitanki netephu anika malunga ne-1 bar. Itanki ekwisitendi esingu-1 m inika i-0.1 bar kuphela, eyaneleyo kwi-kit yonkcenkceshelo ngamathonsi esebenza ngoxinzelelo oluphantsi lwamanzi ahla ngobunzima bawo (gravity) kodwa ayanelanga kwii-dripper eziqhelekileyo okanye izitshizi. Funda uxinzelelo olufunwa yi-kit yakho kwiphakheji.

### Izitshizi

**Izitshizi ezincinci** (micro-sprinklers) zilungele **ii-zone** 1 no-2, iibhedi ezisondele kakhulu endlini oziyela yonke imihla. Intloko nganye encinci ime phezu kwesikhonkwane, ondliwa ngombhobho obhityileyo osuka kwipayipi enoxinzelelo oluphantsi, kwaye ifumeza isangqa esimalunga neemitha ezimbalwa ububanzi. Zilahlekelwa ngamanzi amaninzi ngumoya kunamathonsi.

**Izitshizi ezishayayo** (impact sprinklers), ezo zithi klik njengoko zijika, ziphosa amanzi esangqeni esibanzi. Zidinga uxinzelelo olungakumbi, zilahlekelwa ngamanzi amaninzi kakhulu ngumoya nelanga, kwaye zifumeza amagqabi, nto leyo isasaza izifo zamagqabi. Zigcine amadlelo neentsimi ezinkulu.

## Ukunkcenkcesha nge-greywater

Ibhafu nganye neshawa nganye zithumela amanzi amahle ayokuphuma ngombhobho. Kwifama eyomileyo, la manzi anokugcina umgca wemithi yeziqhamo iphila ngexesha elomileyo.

**I-greywater** (amanzi asetyenzisiweyo ekhaya) ngamanzi asetyenzisiweyo asuka ebhafini, eshaweni, kwizihlambi zezandla nakumatshini wokuhlamba iimpahla. **I-blackwater** ngamanzi asuka kwiindlu zangasese. I-blackwater namanzi eedayapha athwala iintsholongwane kwaye ungaze uwafake egadini. Amanzi esinki sasekhitshini yeyona greywater ingcolileyo, izele ngamafutha nenkunkuma yokutya; yisebenzise kuphela ngokudlula kwisithiyo samafutha (grease trap), kwaye kuphela kwimithi nakwiintyatyambo.

| Umthombo wamanzi | Egadini? |
|---|---|
| Ishawa, ibhafu, isihlambi sezandla | Ewe, ngononophelo |
| Umatshini wokuhlamba iimpahla | Ewe, ngesepha ebuthathaka enetyuwa encinci |
| Isinki sasekhitshini | Kuphela ngokudlula kwisithiyo samafutha, kwimithi nakwiintyatyambo |
| Ukuhlamba iidayapha okanye indlu yangasese | Hayi |

### Imigaqo

1. **Emhlabeni, phantsi kwe-mulch.** Khokelela i-greywater kwisitya somthi esigqunyiweyo, kwi-swale okanye kwibhedi emngxunyeni, ngepayipi ephelela phantsi kwe-mulch. Ungaze uyitshizele.
2. **Ungaze uyifake kokutya okuyiwa kuluhlaza.** Yigcine kude nemifino yamagqabi (morogo), iletisi neengcambu ezifana neekherothi. Yisebenzise kwimithi yeziqhamo, iibhanana, izihlahla nakwiintyatyambo.
3. **Ungabi namachibi.** Mayingene emhlabeni ngokukhawuleza, apho abantu nezilwanyana bengayi kuyichukumisa.
4. **Yisebenzise ngosuku olunye.** I-greywater egciniweyo iyonakala kwaye izala iintsholongwane neemosquito.
5. **Kude namanzi okusela.** Ungaze ibe kufutshane okanye ethambekeni ngaphezulu komthombo obiweyo (well), komngxuma wamanzi (borehole) okanye komthombo wendalo (spring).
6. **Iisepha ezibuthathaka.** Iityuwa, i-bleach, i-borax neekhemikhali ezinzima ziyaqokelela kwaye zonakalisa umhlaba nezityalo. Sebenzisa iisepha ezibuthathaka, ezibolayo, ezinetyuwa encinci; isepha yolwelo yokuhlamba iimpahla idla ngokuba netyuwa encinci kunomgubo.
7. **Yihambise.** Jikelezisa phakathi kwezitya uvumele imvula ihlambulule umhlaba. Imiphetho yamagqabi emiphuzi okanye umaleko omhlophe oqinileyo zithetha ukuba ityuwa iyaqokelela.

### Inkqubo elula ye-greywater

1. Fumana apho umbhobho wamanzi aphuma ebhafini, eshaweni nasesihlambini ushiya indlu, ngokwesiqhelo ecaleni kodonga lwegumbi lokuhlamba.
2. Dibanisa ipayipi ye-PVC kwindawo ephumayo uyisise egadini, ithambekele kancinci ezantsi yonke indlela.
3. Faka ipayipi ethambileyo ekupheleni, ukuze ukwazi ukuhambisa indawo ephumayo phakathi kwezitya ezigqunyiweyo.
4. Gcina indawo ephumayo ingcwatyiwe kwi-mulch.

Xa i-greywater sele ingene emhlabeni onempilo, ogqunyiweyo, ubomi bomhlaba neengcambu zibolisa uninzi lwento ethwele yona.

## Zama oku

Yakha i-A-frame uphawule i-contour yakho yokuqala. Ayibizi nto ngaphandle kweentonga ezintathu nemva kwemini enye.

1. Bophelela phezulu iintonga ezimbini ezithe ngqo, ezimalunga nama-2 m ubude zide zilingane ngqo, zibe yi-V eguqulweyo.
2. Bophela intonga emfutshane phakathi kwemilenze yomibini, ukuphakama okufanayo kwicala ngalinye, wenze u-A.
3. Xhoma ilitye emtyeni ophezulu ukuze lilenge ngokukhululekileyo lidlule intonga ephakathi.
4. Misa ifreyimu ethambekeni uphawule apho umtya uwela khona kwintonga. Jika ifreyimu ukuze imilenze itshintshane iindawo kwezo ndawo mbini zifanayo ngqo, uphawule kwakhona. **Uphawu lokulingana** (level mark) lusesiqingatheni phakathi kwezi zimbini.
5. Betha isikhonkwane apho ufuna umgca wakho uqale khona, uze umise umlenze omnye ecaleni kwaso.
6. Hambisa omnye umlenze uwuphakamisa okanye uwuhlisa ethambekeni de umtya ulenge kuphawu lokulingana. Betha isikhonkwane kuloo ndawo.
7. Gcina loo mlenze uzolile, jikisa owokuqala uwuse kwelinye icala, fumana ukulingana kwakhona uze ubethe isikhonkwane. Qhubeka uwela ethambekeni.
8. Hamba ecaleni komgca uthambise iindawo ezijikajikayo kakhulu. Beka amatye okanye amasebe ecaleni kwawo, uze ubukele ukuba imvula elandelayo yenza ntoni apho.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Yila uthathele ingqalelo isaqhwithi esikhulu kakhulu: qala ngaphezulu, uze unike umsebenzi womhlaba ngamnye indlela ekhuselekileyo yokuphuphuma.
- Imvula eqokelelweyo (iilitha) = indawo (m²) × imvula (mm) × umlinganiselo we-runoff. Uphahla lwesinki lwe-120 m² kwindawo enemvula engama-750 mm lunika malunga ne-72 000 iilitha ngonyaka.
- Linganisa itanki ngexesha elomileyo, hayi ngemvula yonke yonyaka.
- Ii-swale zilandela i-contour ngqo; zibeke kumgama ofanelekileyo ukuze nganye ibambe isaqhwithi esikhulu.
- Phazamisa umhlaba kancinci kangangoko, nkcenkceshela ezingcambini, uze uthumele i-greywater phantsi kwe-mulch iye kwimithi.

Hamba umhlaba wakho kwimvula enkulu elandelayo ubukele apho amanzi aya khona. Sele ekubonisa apho i-swale yokuqala ifanele ibe khona.
