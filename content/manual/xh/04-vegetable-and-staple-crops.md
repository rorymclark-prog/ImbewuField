# Imifuno nezityalo eziphambili zokutya

Usapho lutya yonke imihla, ngoko igadi kufuneka iluphe yonke imihla. Ezinye izinto zokutya siziqwenga ngesandla sinye ngexesha: imifino, iparsley, isitya seembotyi. Ezinye sizivuna kanye ngesizini size sizigcine: umbona owomileyo, iimbotyi, iibhatata.

Uhlobo ngalunye lokutya lufuna igadi yalo, kwindawo yalo. Beka ngalunye apho lufanele khona kwaye yonke ifama iya kusebenza ngomzamo omncinci.

**Ekupheleni kwesi sahluko uya kukwazi:**

- Ukubeka amagadi akho ngokweemozulu zakho nokugcina izityalo zingafikelelwa liqabaka
- Ukuvumelanisa ubungakanani begadi yakho nexesha nemali onayo ngokwenene
- Ukucwangcisa igadi yekitshi, igadi yemifuno, igadi yentengiso (market garden) negadi yezityalo eziphambili zokutya
- Ukutyala umbona, amazimba, iimbotyi ezomileyo, ibhatata ne-cassava ngexesha elifanelekileyo nangomgama ofanelekileyo

## Ukulima ukutya kwiimozulu zoMzantsi weAfrika

Akukho kalenda enye yokutyala apha. Yazi imozulu yakho:

- **Ingaphakathi elinemvula yasehlotyeni** (i-Highveld, iFreyistata, uninzi lweMpuma Koloni, ii-Midlands zaKwaZulu-Natal, iLesotho): imvula iyana ukusuka malunga neyeDwarha ukuya kweyoKwindla; ubusika bumile kwaye bubanda, rhoqo kukho iqabaka.
- **INtshona Koloni enemvula yasebusika**: imvula iyana ukusuka malunga noCanzibe ukuya kweyoMsintsi. Izityalo zasehlotyeni zidinga unkcenkceshelo.
- **I-lowveld nonxweme olungenaqabaka** (unxweme lwaKwaZulu-Natal, i-lowveld yeLimpopo neyeMpumalanga): xa unamanzi, unokulima ixesha elininzi lonyaka.

Kwiindawo ezibandayo, iqabaka lelona ngozi iphambili. Umoya obandayo unzima. Ebusuku obuthe cwaka, obucacileyo, uhamba esihla njengamanzi uqokelelane kwiindawo eziphantsi ezibizwa ngokuba zii-**frost pocket** (imingxuma yeqabaka). Sebenza nawo:

1. Shiya isithuba esiphantsi kwiindonga nakuthango oluphilayo ngaseluphezulu ethambekeni, ukuze umoya obandayo uqhubeke uhamba udlule uye kude.
2. Tyala izityalo ezibuthathaka kwiqabaka phezulu ethambekeni, ungaze utyale emazantsi entlambo.
3. Sebenzisa amathambeka ajonge emntla kwizityalo zakuqala nezokugqibela. Afumana ilanga elininzi ebusika.
4. Yandisa isizini ngetonela yeplastiki, engabizi kangako kuneglasi. Ukuba yakhiwe ecaleni kodonga lwasemntla lwendlu, ikwanceda ukufudumeza indlu. Abanye abalimi bahlalisa iinkukhu ezimbalwa ebusika kwikona eboshwe ngocingo kuyo ukufumana ubushushu nobulongwe; zinike umoya ococekileyo omninzi, kuba ubulongwe bukhupha i-ammonia.

## Cwangcisa ngobomi onabo

Igadi enkulu ongakwaziyo ukuyikhathalela ngokukhawuleza iba yigadi efunge ukhula, elahlekileyo. Phambi kokuba umbe, yiba nenyaniso ngokuphathelele **ixesha**, **imali** nomdla onabo.

1. Yenza **uvavanyo lwendlela yokuphila (lifestyle audit)** olulula. Bhala ukuba zingaphi iiyure ngeveki onokuzinika igadi, into oyifunayo kuyo (ukutya, ingeniso okanye zozibini) nento onokuyichitha.
2. Ukuba ixesha lincinci, yenza kube lula. Tyala ii-**perennial** ezininzi, izityalo ezihlala iminyaka emininzi, ezinje ngemithi yeziqhamo, imifuno enevumba nee-asparagus. Gcina umsebenzi nge-**drip irrigation** (unkcenkceshelo ngamathonsi), amapayipi ancancisa amanzi kancinci kwisityalo ngasinye, kunye nesixhobo sokulinganisa ixesha esifakwa kwitephu (tap timer).
3. Ukuba ufuna ingeniso kwaye unexesha namandla, yenza igadi enkulu ngakumbi, esebenza ngokuxineneyo ngakumbi.

Amagadi amane akweli candelo alandela ii-**zone** ze-permaculture: iindawo zefama oziyityelela kakhulu zisondele kakhulu endlini.

## 1. Igadi yekitshi (i-zone 1)

Igadi yekitshi ikwi-**zone 1**, kwiinyathelo ezimbalwa ukusuka kumnyango wekitshi. Uhamba udlula kuyo izihlandlo ezininzi ngemini, ngoko kha kuyo izihlandlo ezininzi ngemini.

Lima oko usebenzisa yonke imihla:

- imifino namagqabi esaladi onokuwanqumla ahluma kwakhona (**cut and come again**: kha amagqabi ambalwa uyeke isityalo sihlume kwakhona): isipinatshi, isipinatshi se-Swiss chard, iletisi
- iimbotyi neephizi
- amatumato e-cherry
- imifuno enevumba yokupheka: iparsley, i-coriander, amatswele aluhlaza (spring onions), i-thyme

Uninzi lwazo zii-annual, izityalo ezihlala isizini enye, kunye nemifuno enevumba embalwa ye-perennial.

Tyala usondele. Sebenzisa yonke indawo yomhlaba, kwaye ulime nangaphezulu, ngeembotyi ezikhwela ocingweni. Kwimitha nganye ekwere le yeyona gadi ivelisa kakhulu, ngoko ifuna ukhathalelo oluninzi: ukuhlwayela, ukukhupha ukhula, ukugquma nge-**mulch** (ukugquma umhlaba ngezinto zezityalo ezomileyo), ukondla umhlaba nokukha izinambuzane ezonakalisayo.

Yenze intle. Incinci kwaye ingasecaleni kwendlu, ngoko thatha ixesha lokuhombisa imiphetho yeebhedi ngamabhloko emithi, iibrikhi ezindala okanye amatye, uze ugquma iindlela ngamatye amancinci okanye ngeziqwenga zemithi.

## 2. Igadi yemifuno (i-zone 2)

Igadi yemifuno inkulu ngakumbi. Inezityalo ezidinga indawo engakumbi okanye ezikhula ixesha elide: ikhaphetshu, ibhitrudi, ii-leek, amatswele, iipilipili, amatumato ne-okra. Uyityelela kanye ngemini okanye emva kweentsuku ezimbalwa, ngoko ikude kancinci, kwi-**zone 2**. Iitonela zasekhaya neendlu zomnatha womthunzi nazo zikule ndawo.

1. Beka iibhedi ezicacileyo ngeendlela phakathi kwazo. Ethambekeni, beka iibhedi neendlela ngokulandela i-**contour** (umgca owele ethambekeni usezingeni elifanayo), ukuze amanzi angene endaweni yokuqukuqela.
2. Tyala izityalo ezininzi kunye. Yongeza ichibi elincinci lamaxoxo neentyatyambo ezondla izinambuzane eziluncedo (isahluko 10).
3. Yeka ukwenza imiphetho. Phinda uyilise iibhedi ngesandla izihlandlo ezimbalwa ngonyaka.

Umsebenzi uhlala usoloko: ukujonga imigca yamathonsi, ukugquma nge-mulch, ukongeza i-**compost** (inkunkuma yezityalo nezilwanyana ebolileyo), ukukhupha ukhula nokuhlwayela.

## 3. Ukulima imifuno ukuze uthengise ngobungakanani obuncinci (i-zone 3)

Igadi yemifuno elinyelwe ikakhulu ukuthengisa yi-**market garden** (igadi yentengiso). Ukusuka kuma-0.4 eehektare (ikeri enye) ukuya phezulu, ikwi-**zone 3**, indawo ephambili yokulima.

1. Tyala isityalo esinye kwibhedi nganye. Ukuhlwayela, ukukhupha ukhula nokuvuna kuhamba ngokukhawuleza. Tshintsha isityalo kwibhedi nganye kwisizini nganye (isahluko 10).
2. Tyala usondele kwiibhedi ezinzulu ezifakwe i-compost eninzi. Ukutyala usondele kunika ukutya okuninzi kwimitha nganye ekwere kwaye kuthintela ukhula ngomthunzi.
3. Sebenzisa izixhobo ezigcina ixesha: umatshini wokuhlwayela ophushwayo (push-seeder) kunye ne-**broadfork** (ifolokhwe ebanzi enezibambo ezimbini ekhulula umhlaba ngaphandle kokuwuphendula). Ifama enkulu ngakumbi inokongeza itrektara enamavili amabini ekhokelwa ngumntu ohamba emva kwayo.

Amagadi entengiso asebenza kuba abathengi bafuna ukutya okutsha okulinywe kufuphi. Thengisa ngqo kubathengi, emnyango wefama, kwimarike, ezikolweni, kwiisitolo zasekhaya (spaza shops) okanye kwiirestyuranti, kwaye uya kufumana ixabiso **lokuthengisa ngesiqwenga (retail)**, kungekhona ixabiso eliphantsi **lokuthengisa ngobuninzi (wholesale)** elihlawulwa ngumthengisi. Iindleko zokuqalisa ziphantsi, kwaye uluhlu lweemveliso (imifuno, iziqhamo, amaqanda, ubusi, iijam) lugcina imali ingena.

> **Ingcebiso:** Thetha nabathengi bakho phambi kokutyala. Fumanisa ukuba bafuna ntoni, bangakanani, rhoqo kangakanani nangexabiso elingakanani. Lima imarike oyaziyo, kungekhona leyo ulindele.

## 4. Igadi yezityalo eziphambili zokutya (i-zone 3)

**Izityalo eziphambili zokutya (staple crops)** ngukutya okuzalisa isitya sosapho uninzi lweentsuku. Iinkozo, iitapile neminye izityalo zeengcambu zinika amandla. Iimbotyi nezinye ii-**legume** (izityalo zosapho lweembotyi) zinika iprotheyini.

Izityalo eziphambili zidinga indawo enkulu ukuze zinike isivuno esiphakathi, zithatha iinyanga ukuvuthwa kwaye zifika zonke kunye, ngoko zibekwa kwi-zone 3. Igadi yazo ifuna umsebenzi omncinci kwaye ikhula kakhulu ngemvula, ngoko khetha izityalo ezifanele imozulu yakho uzityale ukuze zibambe imvula.

| Iqela | Izityalo |
|---|---|
| Iinkozo | umbona (mielies), amazimba (sorghum), i-pearl millet; ingqolowa, i-oats nebhali (ikakhulu izityalo zasebusika) |
| Iimbotyi nezinye ii-legume | iimbotyi ze-sugar bean neminye iimbotyi ezomileyo, ii-cowpea, iindlubu (jugo beans), amandongomane (groundnuts), isoya, ii-pigeon pea, iimbotyi ze-mung, ii-chickpea |
| Izityalo zeengcambu | iitapile, iibhatata, amadumbe, i-cassava (kuphela kwiindawo ezingenaqabaka) |
| Imifuno edinga indawo enkulu | amathanga, i-butternut, ikhabe, iimeloni neselwa |

### Umbona (mielies)

1. Kwiindawo ezinemvula yasehlotyeni, tyala emva kokuba imvula elungileyo ibele umhlaba kwaye iqabaka liphelile, ngokuqhelekileyo ukusuka kweyeDwarha ukuya kweyoMnga.
2. Tyala kwimigca ekude malunga nama-90 cm. Kwiindawo ezomileyo shiya malunga nama-50 cm phakathi kwezityalo emgceni, ukuze esinye nesinye sibe namanzi anele. Xa kukho imvula elungileyo, unkcenkceshelo okanye umhlaba otyebileyo, zityale zikude malunga nama-30 cm.
3. Khetha imbewu ye-**open-pollinated** (echumiswa ngokukhululekileyo) ukuba ufuna ukugcina eyakho. Imbewu ye-hybrid inika isivuno esihle okokuqala, kodwa imbewu egcinwe kwisityalo se-hybrid inika isivuno esincinci ngomnye kwisithathu ngokuqikelela.

### Amazimba (sorghum)

Amazimba anyamezela ubushushu nembalela ngaphezu kombona. Apho amanzi emancinci, awatyale kwimigca ebanzi, ekude malunga nama-0.9 m ukuya kuma-2.3 m ngokwemvula nomhlaba, ukuze isityalo ngasinye sibe nomhlaba omkhulu ngakumbi wokusela kuwo.

### Iimbotyi ezomileyo, ii-cowpea neindlubu (jugo beans)

1. Tyala iimbotyi ezomileyo emva kwemvula yokuqala elungileyo, xa umhlaba ufudumele ngaphezu kwe-15 °C ngokuqikelela: ukusuka phakathi kweyeDwarha ukuya kweyoMnga kwiindawo ezininzi ezinemvula yasehlotyeni, nokuya kuma phakathi kweyoMqungu apho kukho iqabaka.
2. Zilime ecaleni okanye phakathi kombona. Ziondla umhlaba ngenayitrojeni.
3. Kwiindawo ezishushu, ezomileyo, lima ii-cowpea neindlubu (jugo beans), ezinyamezela imbalela kakuhle.

### Ibhatata

1. Nqumla iziqwenga zemisonto yebhatata (vine cuttings) ezinde ngama-20 ukuya kuma-30 cm kwizityalo ezinempilo.
2. Zityale kwimiqolo ephakanyisiweyo (ridges) ekude malunga nawo-1 m, ngama-30 cm phakathi kwazo emqolweni.
3. Apho kukho iqabaka, tyala ukusuka phakathi kweyeNkanga ukuya ekuqaleni kweyoMnga. Kwiindawo ezingenaqabaka, tyala ukusuka kweyeThupha ukuya kweyoKwindla.

Lima uhlobo olunenyama eorenji. Lutyebile nge-vitamin A kwaye lulungele abantwana.

### I-cassava

I-cassava ikhula kuphela kwiindawo ezifudumeleyo, ezingenaqabaka, ezinje ngonxweme lwaKwaZulu-Natal ne-lowveld, kwaye inyamezela imbalela kakuhle xa sele isekile. Iingcambu zayo zikhupha i-cyanide, ityhefu, ngoko asiyityi i-cassava ingaphekwanga: xobula iingcambu uze uzipheke kakuhle, kwaye ubeke emanzini iintlobo ezibabayo iintsuku ezininzi kuqala, ulahle amanzi.

### Imigaqo yegadi yezityalo eziphambili zokutya

1. **Xuba izityalo.** Lima umbona neembotyi namathanga, kungekhona isityalo esinye sodwa.
2. **Phazamisa umhlaba kancinci kangangoko kunokwenzeka.**
3. **Shiya iziqu namagqabi emhlabeni njenge-mulch.** Kukhusela umhlaba kwaye kuwondla njengoko kubola.
4. **Tyala izityalo zokugquma** phakathi kwezityalo eziphambili.
5. **Tyala ngokulandela i-contour** ukuze imvula ingene kwaye umhlaba uhlale uhleli.
6. **Jikelezisa izityalo** ukuze kungabikho sityalo sibuyela kwindawo enye unyaka nonyaka.
7. **Phumza umhlaba** ngokuwutyala izityalo zokugquma okanye idlelo emva kwezisizini ezimbalwa.

## Zama oku

Zoba amagadi akho amane kule veki.

1. Zoba indlu yakho nomhlaba okujikelezileyo kwiphepha.
2. Yima kumnyango wekitshi uhambe ngeenyathelo ezili-10. Phawula indawo engcono yegadi yekitshi ngaphakathi kwalo mgama.
3. Phawula indawo yegadi yemifuno, ekufutshane ongahamba kuyo lula nakufutshane namanzi.
4. Phawula indawo yezityalo eziphambili zokutya, uze uqaphele ukuba umhlaba utsibela ngaphi.
5. Ngokuhlwa okuthe cwaka, okubandayo, vakalisa ukuba umoya obandayo uhlala phi. Phawula loo frost pocket.
6. Ecaleni kwegadi nganye, bhala iiyure ngeveki onokuzinika yona.

## Amanqaku aphambili

- Yazi imozulu yakho, uvumele umoya obandayo uphume kwizityalo ezibuthathaka.
- Linganisa amagadi akho ngexesha, imali nomdla onabo ngokwenene.
- Igadi yekitshi ikufutshane kwaye ityalwe ngokuxineneyo; igadi yemifuno ilandela i-contour; igadi yentengiso ineebhedi zesityalo esinye, kwaye ithengiswa ngqo.
- Tyala izityalo eziphambili ukuze zibambe imvula, uze ungaze utye i-cassava ingaphekwanga.
- Xuba izityalo, phazamisa umhlaba kancinci, gquma nge-mulch, jikelezisa uze uphumze umhlaba.

Qalisa ngegadi ekumnyango wekitshi. Xa ikondla yonke imihla, uya kwazi ukuba sekuyilixesha lokuya kwelandelayo.
