What changed after studying professional practice

  • One place, one story. The door and garden retain their positions while the camera approaches. The explanation holds that same view.
  • Movement carries meaning. The camera establishes proximity; the short route draws from the door to the garden. Labels appear beside what they identify.
  • Space to understand. Graphics appear in sequence and clear away. A final question gives the learner something to do.
  • Restraint. No simultaneous weather arrows, decorative music, stacked paragraphs or toy diorama treatment.

This is our applied art direction, informed by CUMI’s documentary-map workflow, Esri’s map-choreography guidance, and Mayer’s multimedia-learning principles.

The export was checked for actual picture motion, stable registration during the deliberate hold, audible narration, duration and full decode. The generated footage begins at 720p; Motion composites the graphics and exports at 1080p. This draft is separate from the live course. The long teaching edits remain unfinished following the style changes.
